## Google Play

**Short description (≤80):**
A gentle companion for faith, doubt, and hope — stories, reflection, real talk.

**Full description (≤4000):**
Milk Before Meat is a quiet place to think — for anyone curious about faith, carrying something heavy, or just looking for something gentler than the endless scroll.

It doesn't open with a survey. It opens with a story — a short, beautifully told moment from the life of Jesus — and one honest question. However you answer, the app listens and meets you exactly where you are. No pressure. No shame. No hoops to clear before you belong.

What you'll find inside:

• Short, scripture-grounded stories you can actually see yourself in
• A warm companion to talk through faith, doubt, grief, or hope — any time, day or night
• A private space to reflect and to keep what you discover about yourself
• A real person always one tap away — never buried, never gated

Milk Before Meat will never argue with you, pressure you, or treat you like a number. It's built on a simple conviction: the people who ministered best met others with patience, attention, and love — one person, one story, one honest question at a time.

Free to use. No account required. Come as you are.
