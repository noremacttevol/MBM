# Peter Walks on Water — Codex Test Prompt Set

## Shared direction

Upright 9:16 frame from a reverent biblical epic photographed on location on
35mm film. Photoreal actors, real skin, wet woven wool, weathered first-century
wood, restrained grain, natural depth of field and directional light. Fourth
watch of the night on the deep Sea of Galilee. Cold moonlight through broken
storm cloud; moonlight is the only visible light source. Do not show a lantern,
glass lamp, oil lamp, flame, torch, or artificial orange glow anywhere on the
boat. Nobody poses or looks into the camera. Jesus uses the locked Jesus
references and is the only person in cream. Peter uses the locked Peter
references and wears a soaked blue-grey tunic. No halo, glow, divine beam, text,
border, painting, cartoon, glossy CGI, modern objects, duplicate people, fused
anatomy, or body/object intersections.

## Shot prompts

### 01 — Sent them ahead

Dusk on the pebble shore. Jesus stands securely in three-quarter profile,
calmly sending a first-century fishing boat away. Peter and several disciples
are visibly aboard as it pushes into the lake. Show correct feet on shore,
hands, mast, rigging, and hull. Only Jesus wears cream.

### 02 — Alone praying

Jesus is completely alone on a high rocky hillside, kneeling naturally with
both hands resting together before him. The rough lake and a tiny distant boat
lie far below in broken moonlight. His body has real contact and weight on the
ground.

### 03 — Wind contrary

A first-century fishing boat fights a deep-water gale, bow climbing a steep
black swell as spray bursts over six drenched disciples working ropes and oars.
The mast is secure and the sail tightly furled. Moonlight is the only light;
there is no lantern, lamp, flame, or artificial glow. Jesus is absent.

### 04 — Something on the water

From inside the pitching boat, look past Peter and terrified disciples toward
Jesus far across several black swells. Jesus is a small, non-glowing human
figure walking upright on the surface. Keep the crew, gunwale, rigging, hands,
and bodies physically distinct.

### 05 — “Be not afraid”

Intimate chest-up Jesus standing on the sea and speaking toward the unseen
boat. Wind drives his hair and cream robe sideways. Cold moonlight models one
side of his locked face; calm compassion matters more than spectacle.

### 06 — Peter asks

Close Peter gripping the wet gunwale with both hands and leaning toward Jesus
off-frame. Rain and spray cover his locked face. Longing, fear, and sudden hope
compete in his expression; frightened disciples fall out of focus behind him.

### 07 — “Come”

Close Jesus on the night sea, eyes fixed toward Peter off-frame, one
anatomically correct open hand extended in invitation. He does not look at the
viewer. Wind moves his hair and cream robe; no glow.

### 08 — Peter climbs out

Peter visibly straddles the solid gunwale. His hips are above the top rail, one
complete leg passes over it and hangs outside, and the other remains inside.
Both hands grip the rail. No foot, cloth, or body part passes through the hull.
Four disciples react behind him.

### 09 — Peter walks

Strict side view over visibly deep water. Peter at frame-left moves toward
Jesus at frame-right; the mast-up boat is far behind. Peter is in a long stride
with one bare foot planted entirely on a wave crest and the other lifted clear.
Jesus remains several paces ahead. Water crosses no foot, ankle, or clothing.

### 10 — The wind

Medium-full side view of Peter still above the water when a violent gust
strikes. His torso twists, tunic and hair whip sideways, arms spread for
balance, and he looks down in fear. One bare foot is entirely on a crest and
the other is lifted. He has not begun sinking.

### 11 — Beginning to sink

Peter drops vertically through the surface to his upper thighs or waist. One
clear waterline crosses his tunic; water erupts around his hips. Both complete
arms fling upward and outward. His legs remain naturally beneath the water.
Jesus is off-frame.

### 12 — “Lord, save me”

Very close Peter chest-deep in black water. One complete arm reaches straight
up with five open fingers while his mouth forms the cry. Cold moonlight catches
the wave rising around him. Jesus has not entered the frame.

### 13 — Jesus catches Peter

Medium side-action frame at the instant of rescue. Jesus is braced on the
surface and locks Peter’s forearm wrist-to-wrist, hauling him upward with real
body weight as water sheets from Peter. Jesus acts immediately; no posing or
anatomical ambiguity.

### 14 — “Why did you doubt?”

Close two-shot after the rescue. Jesus holds soaked Peter upright. Their faces
are close; Jesus is gentle rather than angry, and Peter cannot meet his eyes.
Feet and waterline remain outside the crop to avoid a false wading depiction.

### 15 — Returning to the boat

Medium rear three-quarter tracking frame from thighs upward, with feet and
waterline outside the bottom. Jesus supports exhausted Peter firmly across the
upper back as both move toward the waiting boat and reaching disciples. Their
forward angle and windblown clothing show motion.

### 16 — Into the boat

From inside the boat, face the solid gunwale. Peter’s hips are above the top
rail as one complete leg passes over it and disciples support his forearms and
shoulder. No limb intersects the wood. Jesus remains outside beside him with
one hand on the rail, preparing to board; crop before Jesus’s feet.

### 17 — The wind ceased

Wide view of a single first-century boat resting motionless on a black glass
sea. One moon and a field of stars reflect in perfectly calm water; clouds
peel back. Moonlight is the only light; the boat has no lantern, lamp, flame,
or artificial glow. The small boat sits low beneath an enormous quiet sky.

### 18 — Worshipped him

Inside the still boat, Jesus stands with both feet visibly grounded on wet deck
boards. Peter kneels before him and five distinct disciples kneel or bow among
nets and oars. Jesus looks down toward Peter. Calm water lies beyond the
gunwale; every knee, hand, foot, and body has clear physical contact.
