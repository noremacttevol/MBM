This clause exists because 'no buckle' was already written into this block as one word in a prohibition list and the model put a brass roller buckle on a servant's sandals anyway (row 35, the servant anchor) — the geometry of how the strap fastens has to be stated, not just the fitting forbidden. STORY MUST SHOW: the closing image — the swap held as emblem: the two diverging figures small in the cold morning — one walking free into the light, one led away bound and calm; the gospel's shape in one frame.
