# Codex visual recovery pilot — Peter walks on water

Purpose: prove the revised V2 visual direction before any mass generation resumes.
These six images are isolated tests. They do not replace existing V1 or V2 assets.

## Shared visual standard

- Upright vertical frame composed to survive a center 9:16 crop.
- Photoreal historical scene: real on-location actors photographed on 35mm film.
- Fourth watch of the night on the Sea of Galilee.
- Natural cold moonlight through broken storm cloud, with a practical oil lamp only
  when the boat is present.
- Directional light, organic grain, real skin and fabric, natural depth of field.
- Wind and spray affect hair, cloth, bodies, boat, and water together.
- Nobody poses or looks into the camera.
- Jesus is the exact locked man, wearing the only cream robe.
- Peter is the exact locked man, wearing a soaked blue-grey wool tunic and barefoot.
- No halo, glow, divine beam, glossy CGI, painted look, text, border, panel, logo,
  watermark, sunrise, or warm horizon.

## Six test frames

### 01 — “Be not afraid”

An intimate chest-up frame of Jesus standing on the sea, speaking toward the unseen
boat. His calm is the point. He looks just past the lens toward the disciples, never
at the viewer. Wind drives his hair and cream robe sideways; cold moonlight models
one side of his face while the other falls into natural shadow.

### 02 — Peter asks to come

Close on Peter gripping the wet gunwale with both hands, leaning toward Jesus
off-frame. Rain and spray cover his weathered face. Longing, fear, and sudden hope
fight visibly in his eyes. The boat and frightened disciples fall out of focus behind
him.

### 03 — Peter climbs out

A medium, physically legible action frame from inside the boat. Peter is halfway over
the gunwale under his own strength: both hands locked to the rail, one bare foot
descending toward the black water, the other still braced inside on the deck. The
disciples recoil and reach instinctively but do not lift him.

### 04 — Peter walks toward Jesus

A necessary side-view wide. Peter is on frame-left moving right toward Jesus on
frame-right; the mast-up boat remains far behind Peter. Both men have both bare feet
resting on top of unbroken water, with small ripple rings under their soles. The sea
is enormous, but the men remain readable.

### 05 — “Lord, save me”

Very close on Peter chest-deep in the black water. One arm reaches straight upward,
fingers open, his mouth caught in the cry and raw terror on his face. Cold moonlight
hits the wave rising around him. Jesus has not entered the frame yet.

### 06 — Jesus catches Peter

A medium side-action frame at the instant of rescue. Jesus stands braced on top of
the water, both feet visibly supported by the intact surface. One strong hand locks
wrist-to-wrist around Peter’s forearm and hauls him upward. Peter is emerging from
the sea with real body weight and water sheeting from his tunic. Jesus acts
immediately; strength and focused compassion replace posing.
