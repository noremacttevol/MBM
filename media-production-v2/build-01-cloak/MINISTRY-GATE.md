# MINISTRY GATE — row 1, build-01-cloak (V2)

PRODUCTION-BIBLE §5. Answered honestly, in writing, before the cut goes to Cameron.
Target viewer for rows 1–100: **a stranger with no faith background.** Viewed once,
straight through, as that person.

---

### 1. What does this video teach about Jesus, in one sentence?

**He stopped for one person nobody else would have stopped for — while he was
already on his way to save somebody more important — and instead of taking the
healing back, he gave her a name.**

### 2. Does every claim come from the scripture on screen?

Yes. Everything the video asserts is in Mark 5:25-34, and everything quoted is
verbatim KJV in the voice of whoever actually said it:

- `w28` — "If I may touch but his clothes, I shall be whole." Mark 5:28, her own
  words, in a woman's voice. Mark records this and V1 gave it to the narrator as
  paraphrase; V2 gives it back to her and, for the first time, gives it a picture.
- `j0` — "Who touched my clothes?" Mark 5:30.
- `s31` — "Thou seest the multitude thronging thee…" Mark 5:31, the disciples.
- `j1` — "Daughter, thy faith hath made thee whole; go in peace, and be whole of
  thy plague." Mark 5:34.

Nothing smuggled in: no Restoration content, no Book of Mormon, no Church
vocabulary — correct for a row-1 video whose viewer has shown no signals at all.
Nothing watered down either: she is called untouchable because the text's own
detail (twelve years, an issue of blood) made her so, and the video says it plainly
without depicting it.

The one narrator line that goes beyond quotation — "be free of what has been
hurting you" — is a translation of "be whole of thy plague" into modern English,
not an added claim.

### 3. Did the story get its full weight?

Yes, and this is where V2 differs most from V1. Nothing was compressed to close a
row; the coverage went UP where the story needed it:

- **20 pictures against V1's 11**, on identical audio. No beat was shortened; the
  narration, timing and captions are the shipped V1 assets untouched.
- Her one spoken line in the whole chapter had **no picture of its own** in V1 — it
  shared a still with the narration before it. It has its own frame now.
- "Reached out to touch the edge of his cloak" — the hinge of the entire story —
  shared a still with the pushing-through in V1. It has its own frame now.
- The beats that carry the meaning each got their own moment: he halts mid-stride;
  twelve years leave her face; the disciples throw their hands up; he searches face
  by face without hurrying; their eyes meet; he crouches to her level to speak.

### 4. Would this cut, alone, make its target viewer want the next video?

Yes. It works on someone with no faith background because it never asks them for
anything. It is a story about being invisible and chronically unwell and out of
money and out of options — and being seen anyway. The closing card asks nothing and
claims nothing: "Whatever you have carried for years — reach for him. He stops for
the one. He calls you his own."

The specific hook for a stranger is that he was **on his way somewhere more
important** and stopped anyway. That is the thing a person who feels like an
inconvenience will notice, and it is what would make them watch a second one.
