## 1. What MBM is (so the pitch is accurate)
MBM ("Milk Before Meat") is a mobile faith app patterned after how Jesus ministered — meeting a
person exactly where they are, paying attention, and gently guiding them from foundational truth
("milk") toward the restored gospel of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints ("meat").
It never pressures, shames, or argues doctrine. It opens with a story, listens, reflects the
person back to themselves, and always keeps a real human one tap away. Active members get a
separate, deeper discipleship track. The public-facing one-liner already used in the stores:
*"A gentle companion for faith, doubt, and hope — stories, reflection, real talk."*
