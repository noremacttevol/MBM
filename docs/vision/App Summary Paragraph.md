App Summary Paragraph
Milk Before Meat is a revolutionary, AI-driven spiritual exercise platform designed to cut through the noise of modern addictive entertainment and meet users exactly where they are in their faith journey. Operating through a dynamic prototype, the app features four core integrated spaces: a Feed tailored to the user’s spiritual background that prompts thought-provoking exercises; a Journal for deep personal reflection; an AI Chat where interactions from across the app are populated for deeper, personalized gospel ministry; and a Personal Profile. This profile compiles the user’s interactions to generate a "spiritual traits score," offering a Christ-centered assessment of their virtues. As the AI continuously learns and the user’s faith background evolves, the app acts as a bridge. Once a user is converted or identifies as a Latter-day Saint, the app unlocks its "Meat" tier—seamlessly transitioning them from foundational truths to deeper theological mysteries, all while restoring their view of a good God.
