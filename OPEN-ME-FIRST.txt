========================================================
  MILK BEFORE MEAT  —  WHAT EVERYTHING IS, IN PLAIN ENGLISH
  (Cameron's map. Read this, ignore the rest.)
  Last tidied: June 29, 2026
========================================================

There are 14 folders here. Out of all of them, only THREE
groups matter to you. Everything else is machinery that
makes the app run — you never have to open it.


----------------------------------------
  GROUP 1 — THINGS YOU PRINT AND HAND OUT
----------------------------------------

  pitch-book/        Your brochure and books.
                     The one you print is:
                     "Milk-Before-Meat-Come-and-See.pdf"
                     (now has your phone + email on the back)

  church-launch-kit/ The short brochure for church leaders.

  store-assets/      Icons and pictures for the app store
                     listing (you rarely touch these).


----------------------------------------
  GROUP 2 — THE APP AND THE WEBSITE
----------------------------------------

  mobile/    THE ACTUAL APP people download. Thousands of
             code files. You never open this by hand.

  site/      Your public website (milkb4meat.org).

  admin/     Your private "ministry console" — where you
             read messages from people who tap
             "talk to a real person."


----------------------------------------
  GROUP 3 — YOUR NOTES AND PLANS
----------------------------------------

  docs/      Every plan, roadmap, and review, sorted into
             folders. If you ever want to read where the
             project stands, open docs/00-PROJECT-MAP.md.


----------------------------------------
  EVERYTHING BELOW = MACHINERY. IGNORE IT.
----------------------------------------

  server/        The secure middleman that holds the AI key
                 so it never sits on people's phones.
  firebase/      Settings for the "talk to a real person" inbox.
  ministry-sim/  A practice tool that role-plays conversations
                 to test the app. Not part of the real app.
  content/       Raw text the app uses.
  tools/         Small helper scripts.
  scripts/       One helper script.
  archive/       OLD STUFF. Nothing in here is live. Safe to
                 leave alone forever. (Old screenshots, old
                 drafts, old app builds all got swept in here
                 on June 29 so they'd stop cluttering this view.)


----------------------------------------
  THE LOOSE FILES (the ones not in a folder)
----------------------------------------

  These are the project's rulebook and a few pieces the app
  needs. The important ones for you:

  README.md        Quick overview of the whole project.
  START-HERE.md    The "what's true right now" file.

  The rest (CLAUDE.md, AGENT-RULES.md, SESSION-LOG.md, the
  .py files, firebase.json, etc.) are instructions for the
  AI helper and bits the app needs. Leave them be.
