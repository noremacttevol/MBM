How to Get the App

Milk Before Meat — free, no ads, nothing sold.

Get it on your phone in about a minute

Find your kind of phone below and follow the steps. Or, if you’d rather see everything in one place, scan the code — or open milkb4meat.org in your browser.

Scan for the website

If you have an iPhone or iPad

  1. It’s on the App Store now — no invites, no extra apps.
  2. Scan the code with your phone’s camera — or open the App Store and search Milk Before Meat.
  3. Tap Get. Milk Before Meat appears on your home screen like any other app.
Scan to install on iPhone

Free, no account or payment needed.

If you have an Android phone

Android testing is invite-only for now, so there is one small step first:

  1. Email admin@milkb4meat.org and include the Google account email you use on your phone (that’s how Android invites work).
  2. We add you to the tester list and email back your personal install link.
  3. Tap that link on your phone, tap to become a tester, then install it from Google Play like any normal app.

If a link ever says you don’t have access, it usually means the email we added doesn’t match the Google account your phone is signed into — just let us know and we’ll fix it.

Questions, or want help getting it on your phone?
