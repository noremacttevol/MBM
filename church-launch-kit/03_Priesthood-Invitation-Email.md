# The Priesthood-Holder Invitation Email

*Send this ONLY after the Bishop has given his go-ahead — and send the Bishop this exact email first, so he sees every word before anyone else does. Two versions are below: a primary one, and a shorter one if you'd rather keep it brief. Fill in the brackets. The install instructions referenced at the bottom are in the Install Guide document, which you can paste in or attach.*

## VERSION A — The primary email (warm, complete, honest)

**Subject:** A small favor, brethren — testing a faith app I built (with the Bishop's blessing)

Brethren,

I'm writing to ask a small favor, and I want to be upfront from the first line: this is a personal project of mine, not a Church program, and I'm reaching out with Bishop [Last Name]'s blessing to do exactly this.

Over the past while I've built a faith app called **Milk Before Meat**. It's patterned on the way the Savior actually ministered to people — meeting them right where they are, pointing them to the goodness of God, never pressuring anyone, and always keeping a real person within reach. It's built to be honest about what it is: it tells every user, every time it opens, that it is not God and cannot answer a prayer, and that they should take their prayers and their worship straight to Heavenly Father. It is not an official Church product, and it says so plainly.

What I need is honest priesthood feedback before it goes any further. You're the men I trust to tell me the truth — if something feels off, if the tone is wrong, if anything could lead a person astray, I want to hear it. That's the whole point of this test.

Here's what I'm asking, and it's genuinely small:

- Install it and use it for about **two weeks** (the steps are below / attached — it takes about two minutes to set up on either an iPhone or an Android phone).
- Poke at it honestly. Try it the way a seeker might, or a skeptic, or a friend who's hurting.
- Tell me what you think — what works, what worries you, what you'd change. A text or a quick note is plenty.

There's no pressure to say yes, and no hard feelings if the timing isn't right for you. If you're willing, it would mean a great deal to me, and it genuinely makes the app better for the people it's meant to reach.

If you have any questions at all — including "wait, is this really okay?" — ask me, or ask the Bishop. I've walked this through him and through [Rich / the Elders Quorum] before sending a single word of this.

Thank you, brethren. I'm grateful for you.

[Phone number]
