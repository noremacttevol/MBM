Milk Before Meat

A quiet way to feed your own faith — and to share it without pushing.

Milk Before Meat is a faith app built by a member, patterned on how the Savior ministered: meet a person exactly where they are, point them to the goodness of God in Jesus Christ, and keep a real human within reach the whole way. For you, it is a place to be fed. For a friend who is searching, it is a gentle, no-pressure invitation to come and see.

“I have fed you with milk, and not with meat: for hitherto ye were not able to bear it.” — 1 Corinthians 3:2

For you, as a member

It feeds you, not the scroll. When you tell the app, in your own words, that you are a member, it quietly shifts to deeper discipleship content — the scriptures, words of the living prophet, and reflections that nourish instead of numb.
A private companion. Talk through doubt, grief, scripture, or hope any hour of the day. It answers honestly and never debates you.
It reminds you what it is. A lantern, not the Light. It cannot answer a prayer or replace the Spirit — it only points you toward Christ and toward the people who love you.

To share with a friend

It meets them where they are. No login, no account, and no survey about their religion before they are even welcomed. It opens with a story, not a sales pitch.
It never pressures. It begins with what every Christian shares — that God is good, and that they are seen — and goes deeper only if and when they ask. It lets anyone walk away free.
It is honest about who made it. Asked, it says plainly that it was built by members of the Church — while making clear it is not an official Church product.

How to share it

  1. Point them to the website: milkb4meat.org — everything starts there, including how to get the app on iPhone or Android.
  2. Or just say the honest thing: “There’s a quiet little app I use. No login, no pressure — it just tells you a story and listens. Want to come and see?”
  3. Let it do the ministering. You don’t have to have all the answers. The app meets them gently, and a real person is always one tap away.

“A person who leaves unconverted but truly met, unpressured, and free is a success.” — from the app's own guiding instructions

This app is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any church. It is an independent space, made by members, to explore who Jesus is.
