"A different gospel — which is really no gospel at all." That's not me being mean, that's just where I stand doctrinally.

So here's where I land. I'm glad this exists as a place where people can talk about Jesus. I genuinely am. And you've been more honest than I expected, which I respect. You clearly love Jesus and you clearly think hard about what you believe. Keep doing that. Keep asking the hard questions. Keep being the kind of person who won't accept easy answers or slipperiness, even from a place that claims to follow Christ.
