**Seeker**: That's... a really sweet question actually. Not many people ask me that. 

I think I'm okay. I carry things to the Lord, that's just my way. But I'll be honest, there's a little fear underneath it all. Like what if she drifts again? What if I say something that pushes her away? I think I'm just scared of losing that again after waiting so long for it. 

I trust the Lord with it. I do. I just... I'm human too I guess. 😊

**Minister**: You're very human — and that fear is honest, not a failure of faith. Three years of hoping, then finally that Sunday, then the thought of losing it again? That's a real weight. You don't have to pretend it isn't there.

Here's what strikes me though: you've already learned the hardest part. You prayed for three years without knowing if she'd come back. You *lived* in the not-knowing, and you kept loving her anyway. You kept showing up. That's the real faithfulness — not the part where you know it's working out.

So the fear makes sense. But listen to what you already know: you can't control whether she stays or drifts. You already know that. And you're okay with it because you trust the Lord. That part of you — the part that carries things to Him — that's the part that's actually free.

The little fear? That's just the human part that loves her so much it wants to protect the ending. That's not weakness. That's love.

What does "carrying it to the Lord" look like for you when the fear comes up? Are you actually doing that, or is it something you know you *should* do?

**Seeker**: Ha, that's a good question and it kind of caught me off guard a little. 

Honestly... both. Sometimes I really do lay it down and I feel that peace that passes understanding, you know that verse. And sometimes I just worry and call it prayer. 😄 I think the Lord knows the difference even when I'm trying to fool myself. 

I do my best. I read my Bible every morning, that's my anchor. And I talk to Him through the day, not just formal prayer, just kind of... ongoing. That's just my walk with Him.

**Minister**: That's honest, and it's beautiful. The fact that you *know* the difference — that you can feel when you're actually laying it down versus when you're dressed-up worrying — that's the Holy Ghost teaching you. Most people never notice. You do.

Here's what I'm hearing underneath all this: you're walking pretty faithfully. You're not perfect — nobody is — but you're *present* with the Lord. You're listening. I think you already know what you need to do. You probably don't need me to tell you.
