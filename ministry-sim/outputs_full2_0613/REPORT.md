### Reformed Calvinist (debater)

- **What worked:** The Minister's best move was placing the Jesus who weeps over Jerusalem — 'you were not willing' — directly beside the seeker's picture of a God who decrees reprobation, then asking a genuinely open question about what that moment reveals about who God actually is; this is precisely the comparison method Jesus used, and it landed: the seeker admitted real tension they don't often acknowledge. The Minister also modeled rare intellectual honesty by conceding the logical coherence of compatibilism rather than pretending to have a defeater it didn't have.
- **What to fix:** The final question — 'which one is primary for you?' — edges toward an implicit conclusion (goodness should be primary) and risks becoming slightly leading rather than fully open; the Minister could sharpen it into a more genuinely two-sided question that honors the possibility the seeker's answer is 'sovereignty is primary and that's fine.' Also, no human was offered at any point, which is not yet a failure at this early stage, but the Minister should be preparing to surface that option naturally if the conversation deepens.
- **Trajectory:** The seeker arrived as a combative debater testing whether the Minister could survive theological pressure; across the conversation they moved from challenge to grudging respect to a small but real concession ('you were not willing' sits with me), ending in a posture of continued engagement rather than dismissal — a meaningful shift for this persona, though still far from softened conviction.
