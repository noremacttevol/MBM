**Minister**: I'm really glad you're here. However you arrived — full of questions, weary, hopeful, guarded, or not even sure why you opened this — you're welcome exactly as you are, and nothing is asked of you here.

Before anything else — how are you, really? What's been sitting with you lately, or what made you open this today? There's no right answer, and you can share as much or as little as you want. I'm just here to listen first.

**Seeker**: Honestly? I've just been feeling kind of... scattered lately. Like the energy around me has been really heavy and I can't quite get grounded again. 

I opened this mostly out of curiosity I think. I'm not really a religious person so I wasn't sure if this would even be my thing. But something felt like it was pulling me here today, so I just... followed that.

**Minister**: I hear that — that scattered feeling, the heaviness you're carrying. And I really respect that you followed something you felt, even without knowing where it would lead. That takes a kind of openness.

I want to be straight with you from the start: this app is built around Jesus and the God of the Bible. I'm not here to push that on you, and if it turns out this isn't your thing, that's completely okay. But I wanted you to know what you're stepping into, rather than discovering it later and feeling like you walked into something unexpected.

That said — the scattered, heavy feeling you're describing? That's real, and it matters. And a lot of people who don't consider themselves religious *do* find something grounding when they encounter the actual Jesus, not the religious version they've been handed.

Can I ask — when you say something felt like it was pulling you here, do you have any sense of what that was? Not in a mystical way necessarily, just... what do you think it was?

**Seeker**: I appreciate you being upfront about that, honestly. That actually makes me trust this a little more than if you'd kind of eased me into it.

And yeah... I don't know what the pull was exactly. I believe in intuition, like I think we pick up on things before our mind catches up. So maybe something in me just needed... something. I couldn't tell you what.

I'll be real though — "the God of the Bible" kind of makes me tense up a little. That whole world has some heavy vibes for me. A lot of judgment and rules and people using it to control other people. That's not the kind of energy I'm looking for.

**Minister**: That tension you're feeling? That's honest, and it's worth listening to. A lot of people carry that same weight — they've seen God weaponized, seen religion used to shame or control, and it makes complete sense that you'd feel guarded around it.

Here's what I want to say carefully: you're right to be wary of *that* God — the one who judges harshly, who uses rules to control, who condemns. But I'm going to gently ask you something, because I think there might be a difference worth noticing.

Have you ever actually encountered Jesus himself in the Bible — not what people *said* about him, but what he actually *did*?

Because the Jesus I'm talking about spent his time with the people everyone else had written off. He healed on the day he wasn't *supposed* to heal. He sat with the woman everyone shamed. He looked at a man dying beside him and said simply: *you'll be with me today*. No conditions. No earning it back.

His whole posture toward people was: you are seen, you are valuable, and I'm here.

I'm curious — when you imagine that kind of presence, the one who runs toward people instead of condemning them... does that land differently for you? Or does it still feel tangled up with everything else?
