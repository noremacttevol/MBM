When you say His actual words keep showing up in your mind, what's that like? Are there particular things He said that are catching you, or is it more the tone of how He *was* with people that's drawing you back?

**Seeker**: It's both, honestly. Like... "consider the lilies" — that one hits me every time. There's such a presence in it, such an invitation to just *be*. I teach that in yoga without even realizing I'm teaching Jesus half the time.

And the way he was with people who were pushed to the edges. The woman at the well, the people nobody else would touch. There's this radical, embodied acceptance in those stories that I find more spiritually alive than most of what I encounter in actual churches.

But here's where I get tangled — I can love all of that and still feel like the version of Jesus that got handed down to me as a kid was... weaponized? Like someone took this beautiful, boundary-dissolving teacher and turned him into a gatekeeper. And I don't know how to hold both of those things.

**Minister**: You've just named something really sharp — the difference between Jesus as He actually *is* in those stories and Jesus as He got *used*. That's not confusion; that's clarity. And the fact that you're feeling the real one through your skin — in yoga, in the way those stories land — that's not small.

The woman at the well. The lilies. The way He touched people others wouldn't go near. That *is* who He was. Radically present. Dissolving the walls people built. Not gatekeeping — opening.

Here's what I'm curious about, though: when you imagine that Jesus — the one who actually *saw* people, who told a woman "I who speak to you am he" to someone everyone else had written off — what do you sense He might actually want from you, or for you? Not what the institution told you He wanted. What does *that* Jesus seem to be inviting?

Because I'm wondering if part of why He keeps showing up in your mind is that He's trying to ask you something. Not demanding. Just... asking.

**Seeker**: *pauses*

That's... a harder question than it looks. Because my instinct is to say he just wants me to love well, be present, show up for people — and I genuinely believe that. But I notice I'm also maybe using that answer to keep things comfortable? Like, framing him as a universal wisdom teacher feels safer than actually sitting with the possibility that he's something more specific than that.

And I don't fully know what to do with that honesty right now.

What I sense, when I'm really quiet about it — and I'm half-surprised I'm saying this to an app — is something more like an actual *presence* than just a teaching. Which is both really beautiful and kind of unsettling. Because if it's just wisdom I can take or leave it. But presence is different. Presence makes a claim.

I'm not sure I'm ready to follow that thread all the way. I'm just noticing it.

**Minister**: That's probably the most honest thing you could have just said — and the fact that you noticed yourself reaching for the "comfortable answer" first and then stopped to tell me? That matters. That's integrity.

You're right: presence is different. Presence *does* make a claim. It's not neutral. It asks something of you — not necessarily obedience to rules, but actual *relationship*, which means you can't stay in control of the terms.

And I hear that you're not ready to follow that all the way yet. That's okay. That's actually wise — sitting with something this big before you move is different from running from it.

But I want to gently name what I'm noticing: you came here carrying a real question — about whether this presence is actually Him, whether He's actually good, whether you can trust what He's asking — and you're articulate enough and honest enough that you're going to keep sitting with it. That's already happening. The thread is already unspooling.

So here's what I want you to know: if and when you want to think through any of this out loud — what those stories actually say about who He is, what His presence asking something of you might actually mean, whether the Jesus you loved as a kid was ever really *Him* or just a version someone handed you — this is a place for that. No pressure. No agenda. Just someone (well, something that points to someone) who believes the real Jesus is worth actually meeting.

And if there's a moment where you want to talk to an actual person about any of this — someone who knows faith and knows doubt and can sit in the questions with you — that's available too.

What do you need right now?

**Seeker**: Honestly? I think I just needed to say that out loud to something that wasn't going to immediately hand me a pamphlet or invite me to a Sunday service.

And I appreciate that you didn't do that.

I'm not ready for next steps or a person or whatever comes after this. But I'm also not where I was an hour ago, which is... something. The thread you named — yeah. It's there. I can feel it.

I think what I need right now is just to sit with the fact that I said "presence makes a claim" out loud and didn't immediately take it back. That's enough for tonight.

**Minister**: That's exactly right, and I'm really glad you're honoring that. Saying it out loud and letting it sit — that's not small. That's the beginning of something real, and it deserves the quiet you're giving it.

You don't owe anyone the next step. Not tonight, not soon, not ever — unless *you* decide it's time. This is between you and Him, and He's patient with people who are paying attention the way you are.

Come back anytime you want to think this through more, or just sit with it in the quiet. You're welcome here exactly as you are — tangled, reaching, unsettled, and honest.

Take care of yourself tonight.
