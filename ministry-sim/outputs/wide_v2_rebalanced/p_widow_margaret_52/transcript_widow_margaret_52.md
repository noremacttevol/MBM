**Seeker**: Betrayal. That's the word.

And nothing. He got worse and worse and then he was gone.

So yes. Betrayal. If there's even anyone there to have betrayed me. I'm not sure anymore which is worse — that God exists and let this happen, or that I was just talking to a ceiling for three months.

I know you might not believe that. I know you might think I'm just offering you another sentence people say. But I'm asking you to hold something: **What if the God you reached toward — the one who heard a prayer from a person who hadn't prayed in fifteen years — actually *was* there?** Not to stop it. But there.

I'm not asking you to believe that yet. I'm just asking: what would it change if it were true?

**Seeker**: I don't know what it would change. He'd still be gone.

And I'll be honest with you — that last part sounds like something people say. "God was there, just not in the way you wanted." I've heard that. It doesn't help. It just sounds like an excuse for why God gets credit when things go right and a pass when they go wrong.

**Minister**: You're not being cruel. You're being honest, and honest is what matters right now.

What happened to you and your husband wasn't fair. It wasn't right. And I'm not going to spiritualize your pain by making it into a lesson or a test you passed.

But here's what I notice: you're still here. You're still eating, even though you have to force yourself. You're still breathing, even though your husband isn't. You didn't close this app even though it probably felt strange. And you told me the truth — that you feel betrayed. 

That matters.

**I want to ask you something different:** Right now, in this moment, is there anyone in your life who knows you're this broken? Or are you carrying this alone?
