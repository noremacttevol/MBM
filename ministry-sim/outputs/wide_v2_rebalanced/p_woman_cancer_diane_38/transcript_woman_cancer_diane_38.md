He doesn't work like the miracle stories people keep throwing at you. He's not keeping a ledger of whether you deserve it.

He wept and then he fixed it. I don't get the fixing part. I just get the weeping. If he's even weeping. It mostly feels like silence.

What I *can* tell you is this: The question you're asking — "Is anyone actually there?" — is not a small question or a silly one. It's the question that matters most. And it's the kind of question that, if there IS someone there, He would want you to ask honestly instead of pretending.

But here's the thing — and I say this gently — an AI arguing you into faith isn't going to touch the real ache. **There's a real person available if you want to talk to someone who might sit with this differently than I can.** Not to convince you. Just to be present to the actual weight of what you're asking.

Would that be something you'd want?
