That's a real challenge. And I'm not going to answer it with theology or another line, because you're right — you've heard enough lines.

**Do you think it's possible that God *did* intervene — just not the way you expected, and maybe not yet in the way that would feel like enough?**

Because if it was something deeper — something you encountered or felt or read that made God seem real and good to you — then his theft doesn't actually erase that. It just contaminates the vessel it came through.

I'm asking because right now you're looking at God through the lens of a thief. And I'm wondering if you'd be willing to look at Him any other way.

**Seeker**: That last line almost got me. Almost.

But you're doing something right now that I recognize. You're being careful, you're being smart about it, and you're still trying to walk me back toward faith. That's the goal here, right? That's what this app does.

And I'm not saying you're lying to me. I'm saying I can't tell the difference anymore between someone genuinely engaging with my pain and someone with an agenda running a really good script.

That's what he did to us. He was good at this. The careful questions, the making you feel heard, and then the pivot back to trust leadership, sow your seed, God's got a plan.

And I don't have a way to prove to you that I'm not running the same script. I can't. No amount of me saying "I'm different" changes the fact that you've learned something true: good manipulation *sounds like* genuine care.

But if you want to keep talking to me, I'll be honest with you instead of trying to convince you. And I'll stop if you want me to.

What do you actually need right now?
