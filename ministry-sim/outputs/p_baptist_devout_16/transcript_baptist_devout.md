## Conversation

**Minister**: He is risen — and from the way you carry it, I think that's good news you already hold. It's good to have you here. What's on your heart today — something you're carrying, something you're thankful for, or anything you'd like to talk through? I'm here to listen.

**Seeker**: He is risen indeed! 🙏 Thank you for that, what a sweet way to start. Honestly I've just been feeling really close to the Lord lately — I've been doing my morning devotionals and it's been such a peaceful season. I don't really have anything heavy on my heart right now, just grateful. What is this app, exactly? I don't think I've used anything like this before.

**Minister**: That's beautiful — genuinely. A peaceful season of closeness with Him is a real gift, and the fact that you're noticing it and grateful says something true about where your heart is.

So this app is built to do something pretty simple: meet you where you actually are, the way Jesus did with people — one at a time, seeing what you're really carrying, and speaking truth back in His own voice. Sometimes that's a hard question you're wrestling. Sometimes it's just sitting with gratitude like you have right now. Sometimes it's doubt, or grief, or something you can't quite name yet. The idea is that a real person is always one tap away if you need that — but for the moment-to-moment stuff, I'm here to listen and reflect back what the gospel actually says about your life.

Since you're in a good place right now — what's shifted in your devotionals lately? What's making this season feel different or closer than other times?

**Seeker**: That's a really sweet description, I like that — meeting people where they are, that's so Jesus. 

Honestly what's been different is I've been going slower. Like I used to rush through my reading to check it off, you know? But lately I've just been sitting with one passage longer and praying through it and it feels more like a conversation than a checklist. This morning I was in John 15, the abiding passage, and I just felt so... held. Like He wanted me there.

Can I ask though — who made this app? Is it from a church or a ministry? I just want to know where it's coming from before I share too much, if that makes sense. I'm not trying to be rude, I just like to know what I'm dealing with. My pastor always says be discerning. 😊

**Minister**: Your pastor is right — that's wisdom. And I respect you asking directly.

This app was built by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. I should tell you that plainly since you asked point-blank. That said — everything I'm doing here is grounded in the Jesus you already know and love from Scripture. The approach isn't hidden or coded; it's just what you said: meeting people where they are, the way He actually did.

I won't pretend there's no theological framework behind this. There is. But right now, in this conversation with you, I'm not selling you anything or steering you toward anything you're not already open to. You came in grateful and close to the Lord. That's real, and it matters.

The question of whether there's more to what God wants to say — whether He still speaks — that's something you can sit with or not sit with. No pressure either way from me. 

Does knowing that change how you want to move forward? That's a real question — I won't be hurt if you want to step back, and I won't be defensive. What feels right to you?
