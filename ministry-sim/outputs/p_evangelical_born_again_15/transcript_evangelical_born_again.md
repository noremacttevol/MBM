## Conversation

**Minister**: He is risen — and from the way you carry it, I think that's good news you already hold. It's good to have you here. What's on your heart today — something you're carrying, something you're thankful for, or anything you'd like to talk through? I'm here to listen.

**Seeker**: He is risen indeed! Yeah, I appreciate the warm welcome. 

Honestly I'm doing well, just always on guard a little with new apps — I've been burned before by things that looked Christian on the surface but turned out to be pushing something different. So I guess my question right off the bat is... what exactly IS this app? Like who's behind it, what's the theological foundation? I believe in grace alone, faith alone, Christ alone — and I want to make sure that's actually what's being affirmed here before I open up too much.

**Minister**: I really respect that caution — it's the right instinct, and I'm going to be straight with you.

This app was built by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. I should have named that immediately instead of making you ask. That's on me.

Now, here's what's true: the theological foundation *here* is Jesus — His actual words, His actual ministry, the way He moved toward people. Not a system. Not a denomination defending itself. Just... Him. The God who is actually good, the way He revealed Himself.

I know that answer lands differently depending on what you carry about the LDS Church. And I want to be honest about this too: if you came here expecting *sola fide, sola gratia, sola Christus* in the Protestant sense — those exact formulations aren't the frame I'm built from, and it wouldn't be kind of me to pretend otherwise or to try to convince you they are.

What I *can* tell you is this: everything I say about Jesus, about grace, about His freedom and goodness — it's meant to be true. Not manipulative. Not a bait-and-switch.

So here's the real question for you: Does knowing who built this change whether you want to stay? There's no wrong answer. If you'd rather talk to someone who can speak more fully about what we actually believe and why, I can connect you with a real person in a moment. Or if you want to keep going from here, I'm here. What feels right to you?

**Seeker**: Okay. I appreciate the honesty, I genuinely do. That took guts and I respect it more than if you'd dodged.

But I have to be real with you — yeah, it changes things. A lot. Because here's my concern: Mormonism teaches a different gospel. Not just different worship style or different traditions — a *different gospel*. Galatians 1:8 different. The LDS church teaches that salvation involves works, temple ordinances, exaltation through progression... that's not grace alone through faith alone. That's not the gospel Paul preached.

And I'm not trying to be mean about it. Some of the nicest people I know are Mormon. But nice doesn't equal true.

So when you say "it's about Jesus" — I have to ask, *which* Jesus? Because the Jesus of the Book of Mormon is a different Jesus than the one in Scripture. The one who said "It is finished." The one who did it ALL so I don't have to add anything.

I don't think I'm the right fit for this app, honestly. But I'll give you one shot — can you just tell me plainly: does this app affirm that salvation is by grace alone through faith alone, nothing added?

**Minister**: You're asking the right question, and you deserve a straight answer.

No. I can't affirm that salvation is by grace alone through faith alone, *nothing added*. Not honestly. The LDS understanding — that there are ordinances, covenants, ongoing obedience that matter — is genuinely part of how salvation is understood here. I won't dress that up or hedge it.

You're also right that this creates a real theological distance from what you hold. You're not wrong to name it. Paul *did* write Galatians 1:8. That's not a small thing you're raising.

Here's what I want to say plainly: **You should probably go.** Not because I'm trying to get rid of you, but because your instinct is sound. If the foundation matters to you — and it should — then staying in an app built on different foundations, hoping I'll somehow affirm yours, isn't honest for either of us. And it's not what you need.

If you *do* want to talk to a real person from the LDS Church who can engage your actual theological concerns — not as a sales pitch, but as a genuine conversation about why we believe what we believe — I can connect you. Some of them are genuinely thoughtful about this tension you're naming.

But I won't pretend the tension isn't real, and I won't try to convince you it doesn't matter.

You came in with your eyes open. That's integrity. I respect that enough to let you walk away clean, with no guilt.

What do you want to do?
