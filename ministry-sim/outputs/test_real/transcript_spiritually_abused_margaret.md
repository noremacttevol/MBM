That's a different weight than anger at him or even at the church. That one lands on you.

I want to say something gently, though: the fact that you didn't see it sooner — that doesn't make you foolish or weak. You were in a system *designed* to keep you from seeing. That's literally how those systems work. They use authority and belonging and spiritual language to make you stop trusting your own mind. That's not a character flaw in you; that's the mechanism.

But here's what I'm actually wondering: underneath the anger at yourself — is there also some part of you that's grieving what you genuinely *believed* was real at the time? Because those two things can sit together. You can be furious at yourself *and* recognize that you weren't foolish — you were faithful to what seemed true then.

Does that distinction land at all, or does it feel like I'm trying to let you off the hook?
