I'm noticing something, though: you're *in* the deconstruction, not done with it. Still grieving, still figuring out what holds and what doesn't. And you said you're tired of doing it alone.

Here's what I want to ask gently — not to push you toward anything, but because it matters: **What would it look like to not be alone in this part?** Not to be talked back into the load-bearing wall. Because that exists. A real person. Not an app, not a recruiter — just someone trained to meet people exactly where they are in grief and doubt.

Would that be something you'd ever want?
