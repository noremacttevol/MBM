And the question "if I was wrong about this, what else?" is the right question to ask. It's also the terrifying one, which is probably why it's sitting so heavily.

But here's the thing I want to gently push back on: you're treating "I was taught a false picture of God" and "I can't trust any community or guide again" as the same conclusion. And I don't think they have to be.

Being taught a *distorted* God doesn't mean God Himself is distorted. And being burned by one institution doesn't mean the only safe move is to untangle alone.

So here's what I'm actually asking: When you read about Jesus with the people around him—the way he moved, what he actually valued, how he treated doubt and questions—does *that* feel true to you? Not what you were told about him. But what you actually see in the text when you read it?
