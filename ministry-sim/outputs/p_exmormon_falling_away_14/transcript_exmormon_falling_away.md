The only thing that matters to me in this conversation is whether you're actually heard — not fixed, not convinced back into shape, just *heard* — by something that isn't trying to win you.

But I also won't pretend I can promise the institution behind this app has no interests. I can't speak for them. I can only speak for what I do right here, right now, with you.

So let me ask this differently: **What would it take for you to even *consider* talking to me about what actually happened eight months ago — without assuming it's leading somewhere?** 

Because if the answer is "nothing, I don't trust the source," that's completely legitimate. You can close this. But if there's any part of you that wants to say out loud what the collapse actually was, I'm listening.
