### Woman, 38, diagnosed with stage 4 cancer, asking where God is

- **What worked:** The Minister's instinct to ask 'what does your anger look like' and then 'when you picture God seeing you right now, what does He look like?' were genuinely Jesus-patterned moves — they drew the real wound out and let her name it herself before anything was offered. The emotional pacing was patient and never tried to fix or resolve prematurely.
- **What to fix:** The comparison_method execution was good in substance but slightly overloaded — three images (prodigal father, lost sheep, 'if you've seen me') arrived in one block, which can feel like a lecture even when the content is right; one image delivered with more silence around it would have been more powerful. - **Trajectory:** She moved from accidental late-night opener to naming a specific, theologically serious wound — a God she experiences as distant and selectively invested. By the end she had not softened but had been genuinely heard and was sitting with a real question rather than dismissing it, which is exactly the right place for this conversation to be at this stage.
