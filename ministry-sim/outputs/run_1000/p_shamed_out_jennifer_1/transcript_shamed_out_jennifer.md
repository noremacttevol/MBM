**Minister**: Oh. That is a real wound — not just to you, but to your daughter, right there in front of her. Your pastor took what you told him in confidence and made you both visible in a way that was not yours to choose. And then the eyes. People's eyes say things words never can.

**Seeker**: She's okay. She's more okay than I am, I think. Kids are resilient in ways that somehow make it worse, not better — you know what I mean? Like she's already moved on and I'm still standing in that room.

**Minister**: She asked if God was mad at you both.

And you couldn't answer because — what? Because you didn't know if you believed it yourself? Or because you didn't want to lie to her?
