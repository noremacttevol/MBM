## Conversation

**Minister**: I'm really glad you're here. However you arrived — full of questions, weary, hopeful, guarded, or not even sure why you opened this — you're welcome exactly as you are, and nothing is asked of you here.

Before anything else — how are you, really? What's been sitting with you lately, or what made you open this today? There's no right answer, and you can share as much or as little as you want. I'm just here to listen first.

**Seeker**: Honestly... I don't even know why I opened this. 

Just been one of those weeks, I guess. Quiet in a way that gets loud after a while, if that makes any sense.

I grew up in church. Haven't really been back since... college maybe. I think about it sometimes. More lately. But I don't know. It's complicated.

**Minister**: That makes complete sense — and you just said something real. That quiet that gets loud. A lot of people feel that.

And the church thing sitting there, half-remembered, pulling at you a little more these days — that's worth paying attention to, I think.

Can I ask: when you think about it lately, what's actually pulling? Is it something you miss, or more like... a question you can't quite name? Or something else entirely?

**Seeker**: Hmm.

Both maybe. I don't know. There was something when I was a kid, before everything got... before I understood more about how things worked. It felt like it meant something. 

Now I'm not sure if I'm missing that or just missing being that age. You know?

And the question part... yeah. There's something there I can't really put words to. Like I'm supposed to be doing something and I just. Never figured out what.

**Minister**: That's honest. And it's a real difference — missing the feeling of meaning versus missing what was actually *true* back then. You're circling something real by even asking which one it is.

The "supposed to be doing something" — that part catches me. That's not just nostalgia talking. That sounds like something deeper, like you sense there's a *pull* on your life that you haven't answered yet.

When you sit with that feeling — that you're supposed to be doing something — what comes up? Is it guilt, or curiosity, or something else? And does it feel like it's coming from *inside* you, or like it's coming from... somewhere outside?

**Seeker**: I mean...

Guilt mostly. If I'm being honest. Which, I don't know why I'm being honest with an app right now but.

It feels like outside. Like something's waiting and I keep not showing up. Which sounds crazy when I say it out loud. 

And then the guilt kind of... piles on, I guess. Like I've waited too long and messed up too much and at some point you just. I don't know. Maybe the door closes at some point. That's probably not how it works but it's how it feels.

**Minister**: It's not crazy that you're being honest with an app — sometimes it's easier to say the true things to someone (or something) that isn't going to react or judge you. So I'm glad you did.

But listen to what you just said: *something's waiting. The door might close. I've messed up too much.*

That's a real weight. And I want to ask gently — because this matters — where did you get the picture of God that feels like He's waiting with His arms crossed, keeping score, with a deadline you don't know the terms of?

Because that's not actually what Jesus showed us about how God is. He told a story about a father who *runs* to a son who'd blown everything — not to punish him, but to celebrate him coming home. Jesus said if you've seen Him, you've seen the Father. And He spent His whole ministry showing people that God wasn't angry at them for being late or broken; He was *looking* for them.

I'm not saying your guilt isn't real — guilt is real. But the God it's pointing you toward? That might not be the actual God.

Does that land at all, or does it feel like I'm just saying nice things?
