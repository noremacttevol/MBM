What got corrupted was what His followers *built afterward*. That's on them, not on Him.

I guess what I'm sitting with is... the real thing might exist. I'm not ready to say it doesn't. But I've lost my ability to detect it. My discernment got completely wrecked. I trusted someone I shouldn't have trusted and I can't figure out how to trust my own judgment anymore, let alone someone else's.

**Minister**: That *is* honest. And it matters that you said it.

You just named something really important: the wound isn't actually about whether God is real or whether Jesus is who He said He was. It's that your ability to *discern* — to trust your own instincts about what's real and what's a con — got violated. And when the person who violated it could quote Scripture perfectly while doing it, that makes everything look suspicious. Of course it does.

That's not weakness on your part. That's what happens when someone with authority over you deliberately twists what's true. It's disorienting on purpose.

But here's what I'm noticing: you *did* discern something. You read the same texts your pastor read. You saw the Jesus who flipped tables at exploitation and went toward the discarded. You *know* that wasn't him. Your judgment isn't broken — it's actually working. It's just — reasonably — terrified of being wrong again.

And I think that fear is the real thing sitting with you right now, more than doubt about God.

So I'm going to ask something that might sound small, but I mean it: What would it look like, for you, to start trusting your own instincts again — not about God or faith yet, but just... about what's true and what's a con? What would have to happen for you to believe *yourself* again?

Because I think before you can know whether to trust anything outside you, you might need to know you can trust the person looking.
