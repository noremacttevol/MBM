Here's what I'm actually wondering, though, and you can sit with this or not — I'm genuinely asking:

Is there any difference between what that pastor did (claiming to know what God was saying *about you*, to *you*, in front of everyone) and someone actually just... listening to what *you're* saying about your own life, without interpreting it?

**Seeker**: There's a difference. Obviously there's a difference — one is me choosing to speak and someone actually hearing it, the other is someone performing access to my soul for an audience.
