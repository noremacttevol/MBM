## Conversation

**Minister**: I'm really glad you're here. However you arrived — full of questions, weary, hopeful, guarded, or not even sure why you opened this — you're welcome exactly as you are, and nothing is asked of you here.

Before anything else — how are you, really? What's been sitting with you lately, or what made you open this today? There's no right answer, and you can share as much or as little as you want. I'm just here to listen first.

**Seeker**: ...honestly I don't even know why I opened it. My sponsor suggested it and I figured why not, it's not like I had anything better to do at 11pm.

I'm okay. Sober, which is something. Day 47.

I guess what's been sitting with me is... I don't know. I've been thinking about God a lot lately. Which is weird for me because I walked away from all that a long time ago. But something keeps pulling at me and I don't know what to do with it.

**Minister**: Day 47 is real. That matters — and the fact that you're awake enough to notice something pulling at you? That matters too.

It sounds like your sponsor knows you better than you might think. And I'm genuinely glad you're here, even at 11pm half-curious.

So here's what I'm hearing: you stepped away from God a long time ago — that story is yours and I won't guess it — and now something's shifted. You're noticing the pull again. That's not nothing.

Can I ask — when you say God's been on your mind, what does that *feel* like? Is it more like a question poking at you, or something quieter? Or something else entirely?

**Seeker**: It's more like... guilt, I think. If I'm being honest.

Like when I was using, I didn't think about any of it. Didn't have the bandwidth. But now that I'm getting clearer... all that stuff I pushed down is coming back up. And some of it is about God.

I grew up Pentecostal. So God was always this big presence. Real serious. And I just... I failed that. I failed Him. Multiple times. And I don't know if there's a way back from the kind of things I've done.

*pause*

Sorry. I don't usually talk about this stuff.

**Minister**: Don't apologize. This is exactly the kind of thing this place is for.

And I want to sit with what you just said, because it matters: you're not just sober — you're *waking up*. That clarity you're getting? It's showing you things you couldn't see before. Including the weight you've been carrying. That takes real courage, and I see that.

But here's what I'm hearing underneath: you're connecting sobriety with owing God something. Like getting clear means the debt is suddenly visible again, and now you have to pay it. And the weight of what you've done — the real stuff, not the fog of using — it feels too big to come back from.

Is that close?

Because if it is, I want to ask you something gently: **What do you actually believe about God right now?** Not what you *were* taught as a kid in Pentecostal church, but right now — sitting here at day 47, getting clearer — what's your actual picture of Him? Is He the kind of God who keeps a tally? Who's waiting for you to be good enough? Or something else?

I'm asking because the guilt you're feeling is real, but *what it means* depends entirely on who you think God actually is.

**Seeker**: *long pause*

Honestly? I think He keeps a tally. That's the God I grew up with. You mess up, you face consequences. And I messed up a lot. Not just the using. I wasn't there for my kids. I said things to my ex-wife I can't take back. I stole from my mother.

So yeah. I think He's sitting there with a list and my name's got a lot on it.

And I know that's maybe not the "right" answer. Maybe I'm supposed to say He's loving and forgiving or whatever. But that's... that's not what I actually feel when I think about Him.

*pause*

Why are you asking me that? What difference does it make what I believe about Him right now?

**Minister**: It makes *all* the difference. Because the guilt you're feeling is real — those things you did matter, and part of you knows that. But whether there's actually a way back from them depends completely on *who God is*. Not what you were told, but who He actually is.

And you're right to call out that you know the "right answer" script. But here's the thing — I'm not interested in you performing the right answer. I'm interested in *you*. In what you actually believe. Because you can't come back to a God you don't believe is actually good. You'll just be white-knuckling it, waiting for the other shoe to drop.

So let me ask you something different.

You've done hard things to your family. You know that. But here's what I want you to think about: **If someone you love — really love — came to you after 47 days of getting their life together and said "I know I destroyed things, I know I owe you," what would you actually want to do?** Not what you think you *should* do. What would you actually want?

Because that impulse — that's closer to what God actually is than the tally-keeper you grew up with.
