Honestly? I just came off leading three services this weekend and I'm running on about four hours of sleep and a lukewarm cup of coffee. *laughs* The Lord is faithful, even when your feet hurt.

Can I ask — when that emptiness shows up, what does it feel like? Is it a letdown, or is it more like something's *missing*, or something else entirely?

**Seeker**: *pauses*

That's... a more specific question than I was expecting.

It's not really a letdown. Letdown implies you were high and came back down, you know? This is more like — I pour everything out up there, every single thing I have, and then I come home and the house is quiet and I just sit there and wonder... did any of that actually *reach* Him? Or was I just performing?

And then I feel terrible for even thinking that. Because that's not faith-filled language, right? You're supposed to know. You're supposed to feel it.

Give me a minute. I don't usually say that out loud.

**Minister**: Take all the time you need. And — I want to say this clearly: what you just named is not unfaith. It's honesty. It's the sound of someone who actually *cares* whether they're real or performing, and that caring itself is faithful.

Here's what I'm hearing underneath: you gave everything in those three services — poured it out, like you said — and then you're sitting in the quiet wondering if *He* heard you, if *He* was there in it. Not whether the people were moved. Whether *He* was.

Is that close?

**Seeker**: *quiet for a second*

Yeah. Yeah, that's close.

It's funny because I can tell you every testimony. I've seen healings, I've felt the Spirit move so thick in a room you could barely stand up. I *know* He's real. That's not my question.

My question is more like... does He hear *me*? Specifically. Not the worship leader with the microphone. Me.

And I know the right answer. I do. I've preached the right answer. But knowing it up here — *taps head* — and feeling it down here are two very different things lately, and I don't know when that gap opened up or why it won't close.

**Minister**: You just said something I want to sit with you on, because it's the real thing.

You said: "I know the right answer. I've preached it." And then you said the gap between knowing and *feeling* it won't close.

Here's what I'm wondering — and I'm genuinely asking, not leading you anywhere: **When you preach that answer to other people, what are you actually telling them about whether God hears them specifically?** Not the theological version. The real one — the God you're pointing them toward.

Because I'm sensing something: you can articulate it for them, but *you* don't quite believe it about yourself yet. And I don't think that's a head-heart problem. I think it might be something else.

What do you tell people when they ask if God hears them?
