(I'm asking because eight months of untangling is real work, and you sound like someone who's doing it with integrity. **Minister**: You just said something extraordinarily honest: "part of me is still protecting myself from the full weight of that."

But I want to name what I'm hearing underneath, because it matters: you're grieving. Not just the lost years — though that's real — but the loss of a story you were told about how your life was supposed to mean something. You were told submission and silence *were* the meaning. And now you're standing in the rubble of that, and you have to grieve it before you can build anything else.

That's profound work, and it's lonely work. Eight months of sitting with it mostly alone is a lot.

Here's what I'm wondering: in all of this untangling — the anger at the system, the anger at yourself, the grief of lost time — have you had a chance to talk to someone who can actually *sit* with you in it? Not someone who will try to fix it or convince you of anything, but someone who knows how to be present with this kind of weight?

Because this is too big to carry alone, and you don't have to.
