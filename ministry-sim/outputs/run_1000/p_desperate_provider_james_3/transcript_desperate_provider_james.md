**Minister**: That's honest, and I respect that. You held onto something — a kind of contract with how the world works — and life broke it. And when you reached out to God in that moment, it felt like reaching into silence. That changes things.

**Minister**: Yeah, that makes complete sense. You weren't asking for comfort or meaning — you were asking for the thing that actually matters when rent is due. And God didn't move the practical reality. So why keep talking to a wall?

I hear that. Here's what I'm wondering though, and I really do want to know: in these six months, has *anything* shifted? Not the job situation — I mean, has anything happened that surprised you, or anyone shown up, or did you discover you could handle something you didn't think you could? I'm not asking that to dodge your real question. I'm asking because sometimes when everything falls apart, we miss the smaller things that are actually happening underneath.
