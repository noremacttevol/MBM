**Minister**: I hear that. Fifteen years of praying for someone you love, watching them walk away from what you know is true — that's a long road. And the tiredness is real. It's not a lack of faith; it's the weight of love that won't let go.

You still pray. That matters more than you might think right now.

Can I ask you something, and I want you to answer honest? When you pray for Marcus — not what you *should* feel, but what you actually feel — what are you most afraid of? Is it that he'll never come back? That something will happen to him while he's far from God? That you won't see him turn around before you're gone? 

Or is it something else?
