I don't know if there's anything underneath the fear. I genuinely don't know. Sometimes I think I do want something to be true. Something safe. But I can't tell if that's real or if that's just... leftover longing from being a kid who needed to believe someone was watching out for me.

**Minister**: You're not a lot. You're being real, and that's the only thing that matters here.

I'm not asking you to believe that yet. I'm just asking: what would it be like if the figure at the back of the room was actually *nothing like* that father who ran?
