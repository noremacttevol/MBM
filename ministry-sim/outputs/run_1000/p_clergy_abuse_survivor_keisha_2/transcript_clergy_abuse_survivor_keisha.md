**Seeker**: The diocese knew. That's the part that never leaves me. It wasn't just him — they moved him to another parish. There were other children after me because they protected him instead of us.

And then the institution that claimed to represent Him *protected the person who hurt you* instead of protecting you. So God — or at least your picture of who He was — failed you twice: once directly, and once through people who said they were His.

That's a wound about God's character, not just about religion. And those are the hardest ones to sit with.

Can I ask you something — and this is a real question, not a setup: when you think about God now, after all this, is it more that you don't believe He exists? Or is it that you don't trust that He's good?
