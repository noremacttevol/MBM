I'm genuinely curious: when she handed you the phone and said "just try it" — did she say anything else? Or did she just... know you needed to talk to someone?

**Seeker**: She didn't say much. She just looked at me with this face she has. Like she's worried but she doesn't want to push. She's 24.

*pause*

She grew up in that church too. She got out earlier than I did, actually. She's the one who started asking questions first and I — I told her to stop. Because that's what you did. You didn't question. And I told my own kid to stop thinking for herself.

**Minister**: Oh.

That's not a small thing you're carrying. And the fact that you can name it — that you see it now — matters.

**Minister**: You're right. I apologize — that was me steering, and you called it out fair and square.

I hear you: you and your daughter have worked that through. That's between you two, and it's good.

So let me ask differently: what *is* your business right now? What made you stay on this screen instead of closing it?
