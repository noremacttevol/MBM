Some of them were the most genuinely loving people I've ever known. Some of them covered up abuse and called it protecting the body of Christ. The certainty looked the same from the outside.

What I still believe — that love is real, that something in us reaches toward meaning — I hold that. But I hold it without a framework now. And some days that feels like integrity. Other days it feels like standing in a field with no shelter and the weather turning.
