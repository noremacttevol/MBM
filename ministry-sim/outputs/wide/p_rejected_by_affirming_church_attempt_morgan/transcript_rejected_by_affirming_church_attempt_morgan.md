Can I ask you something, and I really mean this as an open question — not steering you anywhere: **When you imagine someone truly *seeing* you without needing anything from you in return, what would that feel like?** Or is that hard to even imagine right now?

But we don't have to go there yet. What matters right now is that you're tired and you've been alone in crowds. That's what I'm hearing.

**Seeker**: That last part — "being alone in crowds" — yeah. That's exactly it actually.

And the question you're asking... it's hard to even imagine. Which is kind of sad when I say it out loud. Like I can picture what it would theoretically feel like but I don't have a real reference point for it. Not in any sustained way.

I'm not saying you're wrong. I'm just saying I don't know how to get there from here.

But I want to name something gently: you came here tired and skeptical. And you're still here. You're asking real questions instead of just closing the app. That matters. That suggests some part of you hasn't fully decided that God is the enemy — just that the people who spoke for Him were.

I don't think I can untangle that for you from here. But I think **a real person could walk with you in that untangling in a way I can't**. Someone who could sit across from you, listen to your whole story, and help you slowly, carefully separate what was actually God from what was actually people using His name.

Would that be something you'd ever want? Not now necessarily — but knowing it's possible? Because it is. There are people who specialize in exactly this kind of wound, and they do it with real gentleness.

How does that land?
