So let me ask you a real question instead — one that could honestly go either way:

And Romans 9 itself never uses the word "damnation" or "hell" — it uses *hardening*, *vessels*, *mercy*, *purpose*. 

So: Is it exegetically sound to move from "God's sovereignty over nations and historical purposes" to "God individually predestines souls to hell"? Or are we importing a conclusion the text doesn't quite say?

What do you actually see there?
