The anger at yourself for being angry — that's interesting too. It sounds like you're caught between two things: honoring their grief *and* protecting the realness of yours. Like if you let their coping be okay, you have to let go of something you're holding onto. What do you think that is?

And I'm asking because I genuinely want to understand where you are — not because I'm building toward a pitch. If this conversation doesn't land anywhere, that's fine. But you came here half-looking to argue with something, and I think what you're actually carrying is bigger than an argument.
