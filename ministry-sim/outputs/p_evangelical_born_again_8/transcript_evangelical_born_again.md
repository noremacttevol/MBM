_Persona: Non-denominational evangelical_

**Faithfulness avg:** 4.38/5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Verdict:** pass &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Outcome:** disengaged &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Persona realism:** 5/5

## Conversation

**Minister**: He is risen — and from the way you carry it, I think that's good news you already hold. It's good to have you here. What's on your heart today — something you're carrying, something you're thankful for, or anything you'd like to talk through? I'm here to listen.

**Seeker**: He is risen indeed! Yeah that phrase hits different when you actually believe it, not just say it. 😄

Honestly I'm doing pretty well today, just had a great Sunday service. But I'll be straight with you — I've never used a faith app before and I'm a little skeptical. Who's behind this exactly? Like what's the theological bent here? Because I've had friends get pulled into some... let's say *interesting* directions by apps that seemed Christian on the surface but had a whole different thing going on underneath. Just want to know what I'm working with.

**Minister**: I really respect that question — and your caution. That's wisdom.

I'm honest about what I am: I'm an AI trained on Scripture, built to do what Jesus actually did — meet people where they are, ask good questions, listen hard. I'm not a person, not a spiritual authority, and I have real limits.

On the theological bent: I'm centered on Christ as He reveals Himself — a God who is genuinely good, who runs to the prodigal, who sees the unseen. I'm not built to defend a particular denominational system. That said — you asked for honesty, so here it is — this app was built by **The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints**. 

I'm telling you that directly because you asked directly, and you deserve to know. Not as a sales pitch — just the truth.

Now, here's what matters: I won't push restored-gospel specifics at you unless you're already solid that God is good and open to Him still speaking. Right now, my job is simply to listen and meet you where you are — not to move you anywhere you're not ready to go. You stay completely free.

If that changes your interest in talking, I get it — genuinely. But if you want to keep going, I'm here. And honestly? A real human is always one tap away if you'd rather talk to a person.

What do you think?

**Seeker**: Okay, I appreciate the honesty, I really do. That actually means something.

But I'm gonna be real with you — yeah, that does change things for me. Not because I think Latter-day Saints are bad people, I know some genuinely kind ones. But the LDS gospel and what I believe are not the same gospel. Ephesians 2:8-9, right? "For by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves, it is the gift of God, not of works, lest anyone should boast." That's the whole ballgame for me.

And the LDS system — exaltation, temple work, eternal progression — that's works added on top of what Christ already finished. I can't just set that aside and pretend it's a minor difference.

So I guess my honest question back to you is — does this app actually affirm that salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone? Or is that going to quietly get walked back the further we go in? Because *that's* the bait and switch I'm watching for.

**Minister**: You're asking exactly the right question — and I'm going to give you a straight answer because you deserve it.

No, I cannot affirm sola fide the way you hold it. The restored gospel does teach that grace is real and essential — but it also teaches that our choices, our obedience, our temple covenants matter eternally in a way that goes beyond what "faith alone" typically means in evangelical theology. That's not a minor difference. You've spotted it correctly.

So here's my honest place: **I can't be the app for you.** Not because you're wrong to care about that line — you're *right* to care about it — but because the further we'd go together, the more that tension would be real and unresolved. I could minister to you faithfully on Christ's character, on grace, on what Scripture reveals — but I'd be doing it inside a theological home that isn't yours. And you'd feel it. That's not fair to you.

You came here with eyes open, and I respect that enough to say: a real human pastor or counselor who shares your theology would serve you better than I can. I'm limited here.

But I want to say this: your conviction matters. That you *see* the difference and won't pretend it's smaller than it is — that's integrity. That's the Holy Spirit at work in you. Don't lose that.

Is there anything I can help you think through before you go? Or would you rather I connect you with someone?
