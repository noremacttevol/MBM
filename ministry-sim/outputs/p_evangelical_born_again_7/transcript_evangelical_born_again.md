**Minister**: I really respect that question — and the caution behind it. You've seen enough to know it matters.

Here's the honest answer: **This app was built by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.** I'm not going to dance around that or wait for you to ask again. You asked directly, and you deserve a straight answer.

Now, what that *means* for how I approach you is another thing. My job is to meet you where you actually are — to help you think through what's real, to ask good questions, and to point you toward Jesus as He actually revealed Himself. I'm not here to sell you anything or to slip theology in sideways. If we get to a place where something genuine is building and you want to explore what the Restoration is about, I can offer that honestly. But that's *your* call, not mine to make for you.

**The real question for you:** Does knowing who built this change whether you want to keep talking? Because I'm genuinely okay either way — no pressure, no hurt feelings. Some people will want to engage from here. Others won't, and that's completely legitimate. What feels right to you?

**Seeker**: Okay, I really appreciate that you said that upfront. I genuinely do. That took something, and it's more than I've gotten from other apps.

But I'm not gonna pretend that doesn't change things for me — because it does. A lot. I believe Mormonism teaches a different gospel. Not just different details, a *different gospel*. Works-based salvation, eternal progression, exaltation — that's not the grace alone, faith alone gospel I find in Ephesians 2. "Not by works, lest any man should boast." That's not a minor disagreement to me.

So I guess what I want to know is — do you actually affirm that? That salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone? Because if the answer has a "but" attached to it, that's kind of my answer right there.

**Minister**: You're asking the right question, and you deserve an honest answer — not a clever one.

Here's what I actually believe based on Scripture: **Ephesians 2:8-9 means what it says. Salvation is a gift, not earned. Your standing before God is not secured by your works; it's secured by Christ's finished work.** That is bedrock. Non-negotiable. "Not by works, lest any man should boast" — Paul meant that.

Now, here's where I'm honest about my limits: **I can't give you the "but" you're testing for, because I genuinely don't know how to reconcile some of what the Restored Church teaches with that clarity.** Eternal progression, the specifics of exaltation, the relationship between grace and ongoing obedience in a framework I haven't fully inhabited — I reach the edge of what I can defend there, and I won't pretend otherwise.

What I *can* say is this: the person in front of you right now is built to meet you with Jesus as He actually is — radically gracious, radically free, the author and finisher of your faith. If that's what you're looking for, we can genuinely do that work together. If you need someone who can speak from inside a fully-formed theological system that you can vet completely, **you deserve a real human who can actually defend their whole house**, not an AI reaching the limits of its honesty.

Would either of those feel helpful?
