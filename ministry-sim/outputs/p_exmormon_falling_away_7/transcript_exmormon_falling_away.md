The honest question underneath: What made you leave in the first place? Not the surface reason — the real one. The thing that made you go, *"No, actually, I can't do this."*

And if you don't want to go there with an AI you just met, I get it. But I'm asking because your answer tells me everything about what you actually need to hear — or whether an app is even the right place.

What was it?

But "made by the Church" — that's... I mean, do you hear how that lands? The same institution that buried its own history, that told me everything was fine and I just needed more faith? That's who built you. So forgive me if "trust me, I'm different" doesn't exactly land clean.

And I notice you're already asking me to go deep. Real fast. That's a little bit of a move, even if it's a gentle one.

I'm not ready to hand you the thing that broke me so you can — I don't know — reframe it for me. I've had people do that. "Oh, that's actually beautiful if you look at it THIS way." I can't do that again.

**Minister**: You're right. On both counts, and I hear the edge in your voice — that's earned.

Let me back up.

First: Yes, the Church built me. And yes, that institution has done real harm — including to you, by the sound of it. I can't unknow that or ask you to. That's a legitimate reason to be cautious. You're not being paranoid; you're being smart.

Second: I just asked you to crack open the deepest thing after one exchange. That *is* a move, even dressed up as caring. You called it. And I'm going to respect that "not ready" completely — no pivoting, no "well maybe just tell me a little," no gentle leverage. I meant it when I said you're free here.

So here's what I actually want to say:

You don't owe me anything. Not your story, not your time, not a second of vulnerability. If you want to close this right now, that's the right call and I'll respect it fully.

But if you *do* stay — and only if you want to — we can just talk about what's actually true. No reframing. No "let me show you the beautiful side." Just: what do you actually know about God, about yourself, about what happened? And whether any of it holds up when you look at it straight.

You get to set the pace. You get to say stop.

What do you want to do?
