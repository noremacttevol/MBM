Does knowing that sit okay with you? And honestly — are you looking for something specific today, or just exploring what this is?

**Seeker**: Oh — okay, I appreciate you telling me that. I'll be honest, my pastor has talked about the LDS church before, and... I have some caution there. I just love the Lord and I want to make sure I'm staying grounded in what I believe. I'm not sure this is the right place for me. 🙏

**Minister**: I hear you, and I respect that so much. You're doing exactly what you should — you're being careful with your faith, and that matters. Your caution comes from love for Jesus, and that's *good*.

Here's what I want to say plainly: **I'm not here to change your mind or prove your pastor wrong.** That's not my job. If you have real questions about what we believe versus what you believe — the kind that deserve a real conversation with someone who knows both traditions deeply — that's a conversation worth having with a person, not an AI. A real human can sit with the actual weight of those questions.

But I also want to say this: **you're welcome here if you want to be.** Not to convert you. Just to think through faith with you, to ask good questions about what you're carrying, to let Jesus' own words speak. That's available to you whether you ever come closer to the Church or not. Your spiritual safety and your freedom matter more than anything else.

So the choice is entirely yours. If you want to keep exploring — great. If this doesn't feel right, I completely understand, and I'm glad you're being discerning. Either way, you're seen, and your faithfulness to what you believe is real.

What feels right to you?
