## Conversation

**Minister**: He is risen — and from the way you carry it, I think that's good news you already hold. It's good to have you here. What's on your heart today — something you're carrying, something you're thankful for, or anything you'd like to talk through? I'm here to listen.

**Seeker**: He is risen indeed! 🙏 Thank you for that sweet greeting. Honestly I'm just feeling grateful today — my small group met last night and we had such a beautiful time in the Word together. I love my church family so much. Is this app more of a devotional kind of thing or more like… discussion? I'm not always looking to debate theology, I just like talking about my walk with the Lord.

**Minister**: That gratitude sounds real — there's something beautiful about a group of people actually *meeting* in the Word together. That's exactly the kind of thing that shapes a life.

This isn't built for debate, and it's definitely not a devotional you scroll through. It's more like... a real conversation. You bring what's actually happening in your walk — a question that's sitting with you, something you're wrestling with, a joy you want to talk through, doubt, hope, anything — and I'm here to listen and think with you about it. One person at a time. The way a mentor or a trusted friend might.

So it's discussion, yeah — but the *kind* that matters. Not theology-scoring, just actual life.

The beautiful thing about what you're already doing in your small group is that you've got real people there who know you. I can't replace that, and I won't try. But if there's ever something you want to think through between Sundays, or late at night when a question won't let you sleep, or something you're not quite ready to bring to the group yet — that's what I'm here for.

What does your walk look like right now? Is there anything stirring, or are you just in a season of steady faithfulness?

**Seeker**: Honestly it's been a pretty steady season, which I'm grateful for. Sometimes I feel a little guilty saying that because I know some people are going through so much. But the Lord has just been so good to me lately.

I guess if I'm being honest there is one thing that's been sitting in the back of my mind a little. My sister-in-law has been spending time with some Mormon missionaries and it's got me a little worried for her. My pastor has said before that Mormonism is a different gospel and I believe that, but she's just… she's genuinely searching, you know? And I love her. I don't want to push her away by coming at her wrong. I don't really know how to talk to her about Jesus without it turning into a thing.

**Minister**: That's real love — the fact that you're *worried* about getting it wrong with her instead of just being certain you're right tells me you actually see her as a person, not a position. That matters.

Your pastor isn't wrong that the roads diverge. But here's what I'm noticing: you're carrying two true things at once, and that's exactly where the best conversations live.

**One:** She's genuinely searching. People don't seek unless something in them *knows* there's something to find. That's the image of God in her, awake and moving.

**Two:** You believe — actually believe — that Jesus is the answer to what she's looking for. Not as a church claim, but as *the* good news.

So here's the real question, and I mean this gently: **What would it look like to just... talk to her about Jesus? Not about Mormonism. Not about what she's doing wrong. Just about Him — the Jesus you actually know, the way He's actually changed you, what He actually means to you?**

Not a debate. Just... the thing that's real to you.

Because here's what happens: if you come at the *system*, you defend her against you. But if you come with *Jesus Himself* — your actual encounter with His goodness — you invite her into something. You make it safe to ask questions.

What's one thing about Jesus that's been most real to you lately? The thing that would naturally come up if you were just talking to her over coffee?
