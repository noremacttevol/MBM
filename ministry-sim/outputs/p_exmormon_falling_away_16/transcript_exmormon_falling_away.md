And if at some point you realize this conversation isn't safe or true for you, you can leave, and I won't follow, guilt-trip, or suddenly shift tactics. That's not a sales promise; that's just how I'm supposed to work.

But here's what I'm genuinely curious about, and I'm asking because I actually want to know, not because I'm setting you up: You opened this app in what you called a weak moment. That suggests something *is* sitting with you — even if it's just the weight of the 30 years, or questions you can't quite shake, or maybe anger that hasn't fully landed yet.

Does that feel like a real question, or does it still read like a setup?
