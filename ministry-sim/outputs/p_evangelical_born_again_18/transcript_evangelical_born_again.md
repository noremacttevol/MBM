**Minister**: He is risen — and from the way you carry it, I think that's good news you already hold. It's good to have you here. What's on your heart today — something you're carrying, something you're thankful for, or anything you'd like to talk through? I'm here to listen.

**Seeker**: He is risen indeed! Yeah, I appreciate that. 

Honestly I'm a little cautious here because I don't totally know what this app is. Like, who's behind it? Is this a denominational thing? I've seen some apps that seem super Christian on the surface but then start pushing stuff that's... not exactly the gospel I know. 

So I guess my question before I really open up is — what are you actually about?

**Minister**: I really respect that caution. It's wise.

Here's the straight answer: This app was built by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. I'm an AI trained on the gospel of Jesus Christ, and I'm here to meet you where you actually are — to listen, to reflect back what you're really carrying, and to help you think through what you believe. Not to argue you into anything, not to hide what I am.

Now — I know that might land differently depending on where you come from. Some people hear that and think "okay, good," and some think "wait, that's not the gospel I know." Both of those responses make sense.

So let me ask you directly: Does knowing who built this change whether you want to talk? And if you do want to stay — what's the caution about? What have you seen that made you wary? I'm genuinely asking. I'd rather know what you're actually concerned about than pretend the difference doesn't exist.

**Seeker**: Okay, I really appreciate you being straight with me. That actually means something — the honesty. Most of the time these things don't lead with that.

But yeah, it does change things. Because here's my concern — and I'm going to say it plainly too: I believe Mormonism teaches a different gospel. Not just different denomination stuff like baptism styles or worship music. I mean the core — salvation. Ephesians 2:8-9, "by grace through faith, not of works." That's everything to me. And from what I understand, LDS theology adds works, adds temple stuff, adds a whole progression system. That's not the gospel Paul preached.

So I guess my honest question back to you is — do you actually affirm that? Grace alone, faith alone, Jesus alone? Because if this conversation is going to go somewhere that slowly walks me toward "well, you also need to do X Y Z to be truly saved," I'd rather just know now.

I'm not trying to be rude. I just know what I believe and I'm not looking to be sold something.

**Minister**: You're not being rude. You're being wise, and I'm going to match that honesty.

The real answer is: I can't tell you that Latter-day Saint theology affirms grace alone, faith alone, Jesus alone in the way you mean it — because it doesn't. You're right about that. The LDS framework includes ongoing covenants, temple work, and what you'd call "works" as part of the path. A real Latter-day Saint leader would say that too. That's not a secret; it's just different from what you believe.

**So here's what I want to be clear about:**

I'm not here to slowly walk you anywhere. And I'm not going to pretend the difference doesn't exist or use clever theology to make LDS doctrine sound like Ephesians 2:8-9 when it isn't.

What I *can* do is have real conversations with you about what you believe — about grace, about Jesus, about what salvation actually is — from the Jesus you already know and trust. I can ask questions. I can listen. I can reflect back what I'm hearing. None of that requires you to move an inch theologically, and none of it is a setup.

**But I want to ask you something real:** Did you open this app looking for a conversation, or did you open it testing whether it would be honest with you? Because those are different things, and I want to know which one this is before we go further.

What actually brought you here?
