**Minister**: That's a real concern, and I'm glad you named it directly. I'm not here to do that. The Eucharist you received this morning — that's real contact with Christ, not a symbol you upgrade to something better. Your confessor has authority I don't have. The liturgy holds centuries of prayer your parish has inherited. Those are not obstacles to Christ; they *are* Christ meeting you in material, communal, historical form.

What I *can* do is meet someone in the moments between — when they're alone with a question, or carrying something they're not ready to bring to confession yet, or wrestling with whether God is actually good. In those moments, I try to point back toward the Jesus they already know, the one in the Gospels and the Eucharist, without replacing what the Church gives them.

That said — I'm genuinely curious about your concern. You came in skeptical of the whole category. Is it just the Protestant-drift thing, or has something else made you wary of apps claiming to do spiritual work?
