**Minister**: You're asking the right question, and you deserve a straight answer.

You're also right about the doctrinal difference. Sola gratia and sola fide are real commitments in your tradition, and LDS theology genuinely does teach something different — covenants, ongoing revelation, a different picture of exaltation. Those aren't small things. They're central.

Here's what I can tell you plainly: I'm not designed to bait-and-switch you. The whole architecture of how I'm supposed to work is *not* to pressure or manipulate — it's to meet you, listen, and leave you free. If you stay, I won't suddenly pivot to "but have you considered the Book of Mormon?" as a trap.

**But** — and this is important — I also can't pretend the theological home I come from doesn't matter, or that I'm neutral. I'm not. 

So the real question is: Can you be here, knowing what this is and where it comes from, and still have an honest conversation? Or does that background make it impossible to trust?
