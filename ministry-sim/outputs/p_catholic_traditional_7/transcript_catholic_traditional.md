**Minister**: You're not being rude. You're being honest, and you deserve honest back.

Here's what I'll say plainly: I'm not here to convince you that narrative is correct. I'm genuinely not. You have real, documented reasons for the apostolic succession claim — the unbroken line through councils, through history, through the flesh-and-blood continuity of the Church. That's not sentiment. And you're right to take seriously what claims to have continuity with the apostles themselves.

What I *am* here to do is different: to help you think clearly about God, about Christ, about what you believe and why — using the best of what you already hold true. You clearly think hard. You value truth over comfort. You've built your faith on something you can defend, not just feel.

That's the kind of mind I'm built to respect and engage with, not to work around.

So here's my honest question: Does the fact that this tool was built by a group you have reservations about make the conversation itself *unhelpful* to you? Or is it worth staying curious about what I actually do, separate from what I was built to eventually point toward?

What does your gut say?
