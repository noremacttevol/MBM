Mormonism doesn't teach you work *for* salvation in the sense of earning it. But it does teach that your choices, repentance, and growth *matter* to God and are part of the journey He's inviting you into.

**Here's where I have to be honest about what I can't do:** I can't defend the LDS theological system against the Protestant "sola gratia" reading of Galatians and Ephesians. That's a real, substantive disagreement — not a detail, like you said. And if I tried to do it here, I'd either be bluffing or dragging you into something that deserves a real conversation with someone who can think it through *with* you, not *at* you.

So here's my actual honest position: **You might be right that these are incompatible. You might discover they're not. But that conversation is too important for an AI to finalize.** A real person from the Church — someone who actually understands both traditions — is what you deserve.

Do you want me to connect you with someone like that? No pressure. But if you're going to genuinely wrestle this, that's where the real thinking happens.
