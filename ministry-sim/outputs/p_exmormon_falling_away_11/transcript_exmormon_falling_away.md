That would be dishonest.

**But here's what I actually mean by "free":** If you want to talk about why you left, what you're carrying, what doesn't add up for you — I'm here for that conversation on *your* terms. Not to argue you back in. Not to find the emotional crack and exploit it when the logic gets hard. Not to wait for you to be vulnerable and then pivot to a testimony.

If this feels like a trap, you should leave. Seriously. There's no shame in that, and nothing changes about you if you do.

**If you want to stay:** I'm genuinely curious what brought you to open it, even in a weak moment. Not to convert you — but because weak moments sometimes tell us something true about what we're actually missing or questioning. We don't have to talk about the Church at all if you don't want to. We could talk about God, doubt, faith, why you left, what didn't work — whatever's actually real for you.

But that choice is entirely yours. What do you want to do?
