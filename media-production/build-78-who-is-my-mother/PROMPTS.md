## CONTINUITY LOCKS

[HOUSE LOCK] = a small one-room village stone house, packed: rough pale plaster
walls, one bright open doorway and one small window throwing warm daylight across
the room, woven floor mats, listeners seated shoulder to shoulder on the floor.

[CIRCLE LOCK] = the seated listeners: ordinary people — farmers, fishermen, mothers,
an older man, one small child — in dun, faded brown, ochre, grey-blue and olive
robes and simple head coverings; not one of them in off-white or cream, no second
figure with long dark hair, a full dark beard and a pale robe that could be mistaken
for the man in cream. Every figure: two arms, two hands, two legs, one head, every
limb joined to the correct body.

[FAMILY LOCK] = the family waiting outside: a dignified older woman of about fifty
with a kind, worn face, her grey-streaked hair fully covered by a grey head shawl,
in one deep blue wool robe (the same in every shot); beside her four grown men in
dark earth-tone robes of brown, olive and charcoal. Not one of them in off-white or
cream. Patient, respectful bearing — never angry.
