### MUST SHOW (facts the text states)
- Jesus SENT the disciples ahead by boat while he dismissed the crowd (v22)
- Jesus alone on a mountain at evening, praying (v23)
- The boat in the MIDST of the sea, tossed by waves, wind contrary (v24)
- FOURTH WATCH of the night (3-6am) — Jesus comes walking ON the sea (v25)
- The disciples' terror: "It is a spirit" — they cried out for FEAR (v26)
- Jesus speaks STRAIGHTWAY (immediately) to calm them (v27 — KJV j1)
- Peter asks to come; Jesus says "Come" (v28-29 — KJV j2)
- Peter walks on the water TOWARD JESUS — he actually does it (v29)
- He sees the wind boisterous, fears, BEGINS to sink, cries "Lord, save me" (v30)
- IMMEDIATELY Jesus stretches forth his hand and catches him (v31 — KJV j3)
- The wind CEASES when they come into the ship (v32)
- The men in the ship WORSHIP him: "Of a truth thou art the Son of God" (v33)

### MUST NEVER SHOW
- Jesus's face — NEVER, in any frame, at any distance where a face would read
- Jesus's hands — NEVER (PRODUCTION-BIBLE outranks the old pack's shot 6;
  the catch is staged so the point of contact is hidden by spray/water and
  the catching figure is silhouette/luminous shape from behind)
- Jesus in the boat at the start (he was on the mountain — v23)
- A calm sea before v32 (wind is contrary the whole night)
- Daylight during the storm (fourth watch = deep night before dawn)
- Peter fully submerged/drowned (he BEGINS to sink — waist-deep, not under)
- Modern objects, text in image, photorealism (locked look)

## FULL-STORY check (the pack vs. the scripture)

  This is the WHY: he sent them into the storm, and he saw them from the

## WHY-LAW / STUDY-GEMS woven into narration
- WHY was Jesus not in the boat? He had just fed the five thousand and sent
  everyone away so he could be alone with his Father and pray. (v22-23)
- GEM: "the fourth watch" = between three and six in the morning. They had
  been fighting that sea most of the night before help came. God's timing
  gem: he let them row long — but he was watching, and he came.
- GEM: "It is a spirit" — grown fishermen, men who had worked this lake all
  their lives, screamed in fear. Nobody walks on water. That's the point.
- GEM: "straightway"/"immediately" = at once, no gap. Twice in this story:
  he speaks at once to the frightened (v27), he catches at once the sinking
  (v31). The catch came BEFORE the sermon — grip first, question second.
- TRANSLATION (after j3): "wherefore didst thou doubt" = "why did you
  doubt?" — asked by the hand already holding him. A real question, not a
  scolding.
- GEM (v33): the same men who cried "It is a spirit" now say "you really
  are the Son of God." The storm taught them who he was.
