## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the moment his eyes moved off Jesus he began to sink — and the hand was there before the prayer was even finished. The story remembers that he walked, and that he was caught.
- **The turn:** Peter looking down at the waves instead of ahead at Jesus, and dropping — immediately met by the hand.
- **Character shown:** he catches the sinking one at once, no lesson first; his question comes from the grip already holding him.
- **Whose face carries it:** PETER — his daring, his fear, his cry, his face in the grip. Jesus only via master ref / the hand at the catch.
- **The card question falls out of:** "when your storm gets loud and your faith slips, look back up — the same hand is already reaching for you." — no change needed.
