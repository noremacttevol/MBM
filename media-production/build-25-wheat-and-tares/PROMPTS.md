# FLOW PROMPTS — Story Video #25: The Wheat and the Tares (matthew-13_wheat-and-tares)

Matthew 13:24-30, 36-43. STILLS-ONLY (Law E). Parable the Lord tells: he is kept
FULLY OFF-SCREEN and never shown in any still — only his narrating voice + KJV
lines. Because no Lord figure is ever shown, the gate is trivially safe
(`jesus_face_gate.py` exits 0). Every character IN the story (the farmer, his
workers, the enemy) is shown fully, faces and all, kept consistent. Nano Banana
2, 9:16, x2, 2K download. Hand-painted 2D storybook style.

**CHARACTER LOCKS (kept consistent across every still they appear in):**
- **The farmer (s1, s4, s5, s6)** — a warm, weathered Judean landholder: an older
  man, dark hair going gray, a neat gray-streaked beard, a good sturdy earth-brown
  robe with a simple cream mantle, a rope belt, a seed-bag at his side in the
  sowing shot. His face IS shown — steady, patient, kind.
- **The enemy (s2 only)** — a lean, furtive man wrapped in a dark hooded travel
  cloak, sneaking low through the young field at night scattering weed seed; kept
  shadowy and sinister but human (a man, not a monster). His face half-shadowed.
- **The workers / servants (s4)** — two or three younger field-hands in plain
  undyed work tunics, alarmed and earnest.
- **The reapers (s7)** — harvest workers with sickles, binding bundles.
- **The field** — terraced Judean farmland of standing grain; young green wheat in
  the early shots, ripe golden wheat at harvest. Darnel weeds (tares) look like
  the wheat but coarser and darker, mixed all through it.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or
captions in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen
in undyed earth colors. No modern objects.

## s1-sowing — Shot 1: a man sowed good seed in his field
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Broad daylight over open Judean farmland freshly tilled in warm
brown furrows. A weathered older farmer in an earth-brown robe with a cream mantle
and a seed-bag at his side strides through his field casting good wheat seed from
his hand in a wide golden arc, his face steady and hopeful. Rolling hills and a
bright morning sky behind. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge,
9:16 vertical.

## s2-enemy-night — Shot 2: his enemy came and sowed tares
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same field at deep night under a thin moon and cold stars,
the young wheat just a low green haze on the dark soil. A lean, furtive man wrapped
in a dark hooded travel cloak creeps low along the rows, one hand flicking weed
seed into the sleeping wheat, his face half-shadowed and secretive as he glances
back toward a sleeping farmhouse. Cold blue moonlight, long shadows, a sense of
quiet theft. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s3-both-appear — Shot 3: then appeared the tares also
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The field by day, grown up now: rows of green-gold wheat coming
into head — and threaded right through them, unmistakable, the coarser darker
darnel weeds standing among the good stalks. A close storybook view down the rows
so the viewer can see the two kinds of plant tangled together everywhere. Warm
daylight. No people. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s4-servants-ask — Shot 4: wilt thou that we gather them up
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] At the edge of the weed-mixed field, two or three earnest young
field-hands in plain undyed work tunics have hurried to the older farmer, one
pointing back at the weeds among the wheat, their faces alarmed and questioning as
they ask him what to do. The farmer listens, calm and thoughtful, his hand lifting
gently. Warm daylight over the rows. One single continuous scene painted edge to
edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s5-let-grow — Shot 5: let both grow together until the harvest
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same farmer, close now, answers the workers with quiet
authority, one open hand raised in a calm, restraining, unhurried gesture — not
angry, only wise and patient — as he tells them to leave the field alone. Behind
him the wheat and the weeds grow tangled together, their roots interwoven in the
same soil. His face is steady and merciful. Warm light. One single continuous
scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s6-grow-together — Shot 6: patience through the whole season
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A wide, peaceful view of the whole field ripening across a long
warm day, wheat and darnel standing side by side all the way to the hills, the sun
arcing warm and golden over it. The farmer stands small at the field's edge,
watching over it, letting it grow. A patient, hopeful, unhurried scene. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s7-harvest — Shot 7: gather the wheat into my barn
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Harvest day, the field now fully ripe and golden. Reapers with
sickles work the rows: to one side they bind the pulled darnel weeds into bundles
set apart, while the good golden wheat is gathered in armloads and carried toward
an open stone barn. The sorting is finally easy; nothing good is left behind. Warm
harvest light, faces intent and glad. One single continuous scene painted edge to
edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s8-shine — Shot 8: the righteous shine forth as the sun
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The gathered golden wheat sheaves stacked and glowing before an
open barn at radiant sunrise, the whole field bathed in warm light that seems to
pour out of the grain itself, bright and hopeful and clean, the darnel gone. Rays
of sun break over the hills. A pure, uplifting image of good harvest come home. No
people needed. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.
