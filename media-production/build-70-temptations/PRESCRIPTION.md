# PRESCRIPTION — build-70-temptations

**Row:** 70   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Matthew 4:1–11 (+ Hebrews 4:15)
**Tier:** T3 Full Arc   **Target length:** 120–165s   **Pictures:** 16–24 (planned: 16)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the Son of God fought hunger, loneliness and every shortcut as a
  man — with the same scripture on your shelf — so he knows how to stand next to you
  when you're tempted.
- **The turn:** the third offer — all the kingdoms of the world for one bow — the crown
  without the cross, refused. Everything he came to win, offered as a shortcut, and he
  named it and ended it.
- **Character shown:** he did not argue, did not use his own power, did not haggle with
  the offer — he answered as a man, standing on the word, and trusted the Father through
  the silence.
- **Whose face carries it:** JESUS alone throughout (there is no other human) — via
  master ref, gaunt and human. The tempter is presence/shadow, never given a literal
  face. Let the wilderness carry the weight, Jesus over-the-shoulder at the answers.
- **The card question falls out of:** "he has stood where you stand and held — ask him,
  he knows how to help you hold." No change needed.
