# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #166: "Baptized again, properly" (acts-19_baptized-again-properly)

Acts 19:1-6. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no
motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Baptism** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk. The note is holy ORDER: sincere disciples at Ephesus
had only John's preparatory baptism and had not even heard of the Holy Ghost. Paul teaches
them, they are baptized again — properly, by authority — and when he lays hands on them they
receive the Holy Ghost. Warm, reverent, glad. Study gem: sincerity did not replace authority;
the ordinance had to be done right, by one sent.

CHRIST / GOD IS NEVER DEPICTED as a figure — no face, no body, no shape of a person. The Holy
Ghost and heaven are shown ONLY as warm radiant light from above — never a dove, never a
figure, never a face. (This keeps the shot blocks free of any divine proper noun, so the
face-gate passes.)

THE POINT (Why-Law): Paul comes to Ephesus and finds a small group of sincere disciples (s1);
he asks whether they have received the Holy Ghost — and they have never even heard of it (s2 —
Acts 19:2); they had known only John's baptism of repentance (s3); Paul teaches them that
John's baptism pointed forward, and something more is needed (s4); so they are baptized again,
properly, by authority (s5 — Acts 19:5, the named verse); Paul lays his hands upon them (s6);
and they receive the Holy Ghost, and speak with tongues and prophesy (s7 — Acts 19:6); and the
same right pattern is offered to you (s8).

PEOPLE LOCK (s1-s8): the EPHESIAN DISCIPLES — a small group of about a dozen sincere ordinary
first-century men in undyed DARKER earth-tone wool (dun, faded brown, olive, ochre, russet,
grey), faces shown, earnest and teachable; never in cream or off-white.
PAUL LOCK (s1, s2, s4, s6): PAUL — a grave, earnest first-century apostle, balding on top with
a short dark beard streaked with grey, in a dark earth-tone travelling robe and mantle (deep
brown, olive, charcoal); plainly a man of authority and calling; never in cream, no insignia,
no bright ring around the head.
THE HEAVENLY LIGHT (s6, s7, s8): warm radiant golden light from above — only light, never a
face, never a figure, never a dove, never a body or hand within it.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or body of God or the risen Lord anywhere — only warm
light; no dove; no modern object; no cross, steeple, church-building-with-a-spire, or insignia
of any group; no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no baked-in text, letters or
numbers; no human dressed in cream or off-white; no nudity — everyone modestly robed even in
the water. Every image is ONE single unbroken full-frame scene — no panels, no comic strip, no
dividing lines.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.
