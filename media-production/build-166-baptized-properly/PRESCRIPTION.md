## The impact read
- **The one thing:** sincere, believing people can still be missing the real ordinance — and the fix is not more sincerity but baptism and the gift done properly, by one who was sent. Sincerity did not replace authority; it was completed by it.
- **The turn:** the searching question and its startling answer (s2) — *"Have ye received the Holy Ghost?" "We have not so much as heard whether there be any Holy Ghost."* That gap is the hinge; everything after is Paul setting it right.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** proper baptism by authority, followed by the gift of the Holy Ghost by the laying on of hands. Hands off to **Gospel Library → Baptism.**
- **Whose face carries it:** the Ephesian disciples — earnest, then startled, then filled. Not Paul's face at the climax; the disciples' faces when the gift comes.
- **The card question falls out of:** "When that pattern is offered to you, will you follow it in?" — lands straight on the one thing.
