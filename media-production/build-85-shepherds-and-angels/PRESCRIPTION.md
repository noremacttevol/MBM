# PRESCRIPTION — build-85-shepherds-and-angels

**Row:** 85   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Luke 2:8–20
**Tier:** T3 Full Arc   **Target length:** 120–165s   **Pictures:** 16–24 (planned: 15 — see Deviations)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** heaven's first birth announcement in history went to the
  lowest men on the night shift — the good news of great joy is for *all* people,
  and heaven went to the bottom of the list first.
- **The turn:** [j1] the angel's "unto you is born this day... a Saviour." Terror
  flips to good news; the announcement is aimed straight at the men in the field.
- **Character shown:** a God whose glory shows up first for nobodies, and whose
  sky says *peace* and *good will* over a sheep field.
- **Whose face carries it:** the shepherds — their terror, their awe, their joy —
  and Mary at the close. Jesus is a silent newborn (master ref).
- **The card question falls out of:** "Good news of great joy, for all people. Including you." No change.
