## CONTINUITY LOCKS

[SHEPHERDS LOCK] = the SAME three shepherds in every shot they appear in, exactly
THREE, no more no fewer: (1) an OLD shepherd with a deep-lined face, a FULL head of
grey-white hair and a long grey-white beard, in a dun-brown mantle, leaning on a tall
staff; (2) a BROAD middle-aged shepherd with a short black beard and a receding
hairline, in an olive-green wool cloak; (3) a YOUNG shepherd of about sixteen,
beardless with thick curly dark hair, in an ochre tunic, who carries a small lamb.
None of them wears cream or off-white. Each has two arms, two hands, two legs, one head.

[MARY LOCK] = the SAME young mother in every shot she appears in — a Jewish girl of
about eighteen, gentle tired face, olive skin, dark hair fully covered by a muted
BLUE head veil, a plain ochre-brown dress under the blue mantle — never cream, never
off-white. Two arms, two hands, one head.

[JOSEPH LOCK] = the SAME man in every shot he appears in — a sturdy Jewish carpenter
of about thirty, short-cropped dark hair and a short dark beard, in a dun-brown tunic
with a grey-brown mantle — never cream, never off-white. Two arms, two hands, one head.

[STABLE-SETTING LOCK] = a small limestone grotto-stable behind a Bethlehem inn at
night: a low rock ceiling, a wooden feeding-manger filled with clean hay, one small
clay oil lamp, a donkey and an ox resting quietly in the warm shadow, straw on the
floor.

[NEWBORN LOCK] = the newborn child is TINY and completely swaddled in plain undyed
cloth bands, only a small peaceful sleeping face showing, lying in the hay of the
manger — painted with utter tenderness, no brightening or ring of light around his
head, the lamp's warm light on him the same as on everything else.
