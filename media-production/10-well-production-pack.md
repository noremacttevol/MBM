# Story Video #10 — The Woman at the Well (John 4:1–42)

Runtime 75–90s. Prepend the STYLE BLOCK from 00-MASTER-PLAN.md. Jesus: seated
traveler seen from behind / hands with the water cup / voice — never his
face. The woman carries every close-up; this is her film.

## Narration script (app's exact words)

> A woman came to draw water at noon — the hottest hour of the day, when no
> one else would be there. She had reasons to avoid the crowd. Five
> marriages behind her, and the town knew all of it.
>
> A tired traveler was sitting by the well. A Jewish man, in Samaria —
> someone who by every custom of the day should not have spoken to her at
> all.
>
> He asked her for a drink. Then he offered her one: "Whoever drinks the
> water I give will never thirst again." *(held beat)*
>
> She came for water and ended up talking about her whole life. He already
> knew it — all of it — and he did not turn away. He stayed in the
> conversation. *(peak — music cuts to silence on "he did not turn away")*
>
> She left her water jar at the well and ran to tell the whole town she had
> been avoiding: "Come see a man who told me everything I ever did."

Closing card (hold 6s): *"She came for one thing and found out she was
thirsty for something much deeper. Have you ever been searching for
something and realized later it was deeper than what you thought you
wanted?"*

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — Noon, alone on purpose
```
Brutal midday sun, heat shimmer on a dusty path: a woman carries a large
clay water jar on her shoulder toward a stone well on empty ground far
outside a hillside town. Wide shot emphasizing how alone she is, and that
the aloneness is chosen. Her head is wrapped; her eyes are down.
```

### Shot 2 — The traveler
```
At the stone well, a road-worn traveler sits resting in the thin shade,
seen from behind and in silhouette against the glare — dusty feet, tired
shoulders, an empty hands-open posture. The woman stops short at the sight
of him. Everything in her body says: turn around.
```

### Shot 3 — He asked HER for a drink
```
The woman's face, close up, under the noon light: pure disbelief as she is
spoken to — looking around to confirm he means her, then wary, then almost
amused. Her guard is a lifetime thick and something just knocked on it. Her
hands stay busy with the rope and jar.
```

### Shot 4 — Living water
```
Close up: water is drawn up and poured — a clay cup filling, sunlight
turning the pouring water to white gold. The traveler's weathered hands
offer the cup across the well. The woman's hand hesitates in frame, then
takes it. Faces out of frame: hands, water, light.
```

### Shot 5 — Her whole life, and he stayed (the money shot — extra takes)
```
Long conversation framed against the well: the woman sits now, veil loosened,
face wide open — moving through startled, ashamed, defended, and finally
undone-in-a-good-way as she talks, tears rising without falling. Across
frame, the traveler's still, unmoving silhouette: he is not leaving. The sun
softens past noon. Nothing about his posture judges her.
```

### Shot 6 — The jar left behind
```
Iconic insert: the big clay water jar standing ALONE beside the well, rope
still swaying — while beyond it, out of focus, the woman RUNS toward the
town she has been avoiding, veil flying, holding her skirts, fast as joy.
```

### Shot 7 — Come and see
```
In the town square, the woman — the one who walked with her eyes down —
stands calling out to gathering, astonished townspeople, arms wide, face
blazing, pointing back up the road toward the well. People start to walk,
then hurry, in the direction she points.
```

### Closing card
Text on cream, serif. No narration.

## The Seed (what this video must leave behind)

He already knew everything she had done — all of it — and he STAYED, and offered living water to a Samaritan heretic the approved religion despised. God speaks outside the approved channel, to the disqualified. Quiet question: *what if being fully known and being loved are the same moment?* Carried by shot 5.
