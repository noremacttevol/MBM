## CONTINUITY LOCKS

[COURT LOCK] = a broad temple court in warm daylight: pale worn limestone pavement,
tall pillars and a colonnade, soft gold light, small knots of listeners here and
there.

[OFFICIALS LOCK] = the officials (Pharisees and Herodians together): six men in rich
dark robes — deep blue, wine-red, and charcoal with embroidered trim — broad fringed
shawls, rings, practiced smiles that never reach their sharp eyes; not one of them
in off-white or cream, none with long dark hair and a full dark beard that could be
mistaken for the man in cream. Every figure: two arms, two hands, two legs, one
head, every limb joined to the correct body.

[COIN LOCK] = the coin: ONE small silver Roman denarius bearing a stamped profile of
an emperor's head, with faint worn lettering around the rim that is blurred and
ILLEGIBLE — no readable letters, no legible text anywhere on it. The SAME coin in
every shot it appears in.

[LISTENERS LOCK] = the listening bystanders: ordinary people in dun, brown, ochre
and grey-blue — not one in off-white or cream.
