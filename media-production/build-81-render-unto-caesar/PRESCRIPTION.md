## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he answered a bigger question than the trap — the coin bears
  Caesar's face, but *you* bear God's, so the real question is what you owe the One
  whose image you carry.
- **The turn:** [j2] "Whose is this image and superscription?" — the question flips
  from taxes to identity, and the trap swings shut on the men who set it.
- **Character shown:** wisdom that refuses every trap and hands the attack back as
  an invitation.
- **Whose face carries it:** the officials (the trap closing on them) and the coin
  itself. Jesus via master ref on his spoken lines.
- **The card question falls out of:** "you bear God's face — what does that make you worth to him?" No change.
