# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #162: "The keys of the kingdom" (matthew-16_keys-of-the-kingdom)

Matthew 16:18-19. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no
motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Priesthood Keys** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk. The note is reassurance: Jesus did not leave his
church leaderless; he built it on rock and gave real authority. Warmth, solemn gladness.

FACE LAW: Jesus IS shown — the SAME master face in every shot where he appears (s1, s2, s3,
s4, s6, s7). Each of those shots carries the byte-identical JESUS LOCK v3 paragraph and a
"REF: jesus-master-ref" line. ONLY Jesus wears cream/off-white; Peter, the disciples, and
everyone else are in darker earth colors, in every prompt. The foundation shot (s5) and the
closing keys shot (s8) have NO divine figure.

PETER LOCK (s2, s3, s6, s8): Simon Peter, a strong Galilean fisherman of about forty-five,
weathered olive-brown sun-darkened skin, a short dark beard lightly greying, dark tousled
hair, in a plain undyed BROWN wool tunic and cloak with a rope belt — never cream or
off-white. His face is shown, earnest and moved, consistent in every shot.
DISCIPLES (s1, s2): a few other ordinary Galilean men in earth-tone wool (dun, olive,
russet, grey), faces shown; never in cream.
THE KEYS (s6, s7, s8): two large old golden keys on a simple ring, warm and gleaming.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no modern object; no baked-in text, letters or numbers; no bright ring or
aura around anyone's head; no second PERSON in cream or off-white (only Jesus); no gore, no
fear; every image is ONE single unbroken full-frame scene — no panels, no comic strip.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.
