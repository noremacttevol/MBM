## The impact read
- **The one thing:** Jesus did not leave his church leaderless — he built it on rock and
  gave real keys, authority binding on earth and in heaven, into human hands.
- **The turn:** kv19 — "I will give unto thee the keys of the kingdom of heaven." The
  promise moves from a founded church to a led church.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** priesthood keys — authority to bind on earth and in heaven.
  **GL handoff: Priesthood Keys.**
- **Whose face carries it:** Peter — his confession, and his face as the keys are given.
  Jesus only via master ref, over-the-shoulder onto Peter.
- **The card question falls out of:** "When you find it, will you trust it?" — lands on
  the one thing.
