# FLOW PROMPTS — Story Video #20: The Good Samaritan (luke-10_samaritan)

Luke 10:25-37. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) + Jesus face-never (#18): the Lord is shown
only from behind, his face turned away and never shown. Nano Banana 2, 9:16, x2,
2K download. Hand-painted 2D storybook style (NOT the stale master-plan
photorealistic block).

This is a PARABLE. Every character IN the story — the beaten traveler, the
priest, the Levite, the Samaritan, the innkeeper, the scholar — is shown fully,
faces and all, kept consistent. Jesus is only the storyteller telling it: he
appears ONLY in the bookend shots (1, 7, 8) as a seated teacher seen from
behind, his face turned away and never shown. Passes `jesus_face_gate.py`.

**CHARACTER LOCKS (keep consistent across every still they appear in):**
- **The scholar (shots 1, 7, 8)** — an older man in fine pale cream scholar's
  robes with a broad blue-fringed prayer-shawl border, a neat gray-streaked
  beard, chin held high. His face IS shown.
- **The traveler / beaten man (shots 2, 4, 5, 6)** — a middle-aged Judean man,
  dark hair and short dark beard, stripped to a torn undyed undertunic, a
  bloodied brow, dust on his skin. His face IS shown.
- **The priest (shot 3)** — fine white temple garments, an ornate sash; groomed.
- **The Levite / temple assistant (shot 3)** — a younger man with a leather

- **The Samaritan (shots 4, 5, 6)** — a dusty foreign merchant, sun-darkened
  skin, a short black beard, a DISTINCTLY foreign striped travel cloak in ochre

- **The brown pack donkey (shots 5, 6)** — same animal, loaded with the
  merchant's goods in shot 5.
- **The innkeeper (shot 6)** — a heavyset man in a work tunic at a lamplit inn
  doorway. His face IS shown.
- **The Lord / the storyteller (shots 1, 7, 8 ONLY)** — THE JESUS LOOK STANDARD:
  one plain undyed off-white / cream wool robe, LONG dark hair past the
  shoulders, warm Middle-Eastern; shown only from behind, back-of-head or
  back-only, his face turned away and never shown. No halo, no glow.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke

First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or

## s1-question — Shot 1: a question with limits in it
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Late golden light through an olive grove. In a seated,
listening crowd an older scholar in fine pale cream robes with a broad
blue-fringed shawl border stands out, chin held high, one hand lifted as he asks
a question with the smooth confidence of a man quietly setting a trap. The
ordinary people around him are all turned toward a seated storyteller — the Lord
— who sits on a low stone in a plain undyed cream wool robe, long dark hair
falling past the shoulders, warm Middle-Eastern, seen only from behind with his
back fully to us and his face turned away, never shown; no halo, no glow. Every
person in the crowd looks past the camera straight at him. One single continuous
scene painted edge to edge.

## s2-half-dead — Shot 2: left half dead
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The Jericho road: a savage rocky descent between pale cliffs
under harsh noon light. In the roadside dust lies a stripped, bloodied traveler
— a middle-aged Judean man, dark hair and short beard, a torn undyed undertunic,
a bloodied brow — unmoving except for shallow breath, one hand fallen open
toward the empty road. Torn belongings scattered in the dirt. Heat shimmer,
utter stillness, no other soul in sight. The aftermath only, no violence shown.
One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s3-crossed-over — Shot 3: the religious men cross to the other side
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same rocky Jericho road, the beaten man small in the
lower foreground. Two travelers pass on the FAR side of the road, giving him a
wide berth: nearest, a priest in fine white temple garments with an ornate sash,
eyes fixed forward, gathering his robe close as he hurries by; behind him a
younger temple assistant with a leather scroll satchel, glancing once at the
body and then quickening his pace away. Both keep to the far edge, faces turned
from the man in the dust. Harsh daylight, long hard shadows. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s4-compassion — Shot 4: moved with compassion
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The rocky roadside. A dusty foreign merchant — sun-darkened,
short black beard, a distinctly foreign striped travel cloak in ochre and deep
rust-red, leading a laden brown pack donkey — has rounded the bend and STOPPED.
He is dropping to his knees in the dust beside the stripped, bloodied traveler,
his weathered face open with compassion overruling fear, already reaching for
the wineskin at his belt. The wounded man lies just as before, dark hair and
bloodied brow. Warm low light breaking across the pale cliffs. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s5-own-animal — Shot 5: on his own animal
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Dusk falling on the brutal downhill road. The Samaritan in
his striped ochre-and-rust cloak walks on foot, leading the brown donkey by its
rope; slumped across the saddle, bandaged and half-conscious, rides the rescued
traveler in his torn tunic, where the merchant's own bundled goods used to ride
— the goods now strapped awkwardly on the Samaritan's own back. A long view down
the empty road, the slowest, most costly walk of his journey. Fading gold light.
One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s6-the-inn — Shot 6: whatever more it costs
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A lamplit inn courtyard at night. The Samaritan, still in
his striped ochre-and-rust cloak, presses two small silver coins into the open
palm of a heavyset innkeeper in a work tunic, his other hand gesturing back
toward a doorway where the bandaged traveler rests on a mat within. The
innkeeper looks from the coins to this foreigner, recalibrating everything he
assumed. Warm lamp glow, the brown donkey resting at the courtyard edge. One
single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s7-turned-around — Shot 7: which of these was a neighbor
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The olive grove at last light. The older scholar in his fine
pale robes stands among the crowd, and the confidence has drained from the
scholar's face — the trap he set has been turned back on him and his shoulders
have dropped. Low in the frame sits the seated storyteller — the Lord — in the
same plain cream wool robe, long dark hair past the shoulders, warm
Middle-Eastern, seen only from behind, his back to the camera and his face
turned away, never shown; no glow. The crowd around them leans in. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s8-go-do-likewise — Shot 8: go, and do thou likewise
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The olive grove against a deep sunset. The seated storyteller
— the Lord — has risen to his feet, a tall figure in the plain cream wool robe
with long dark hair past the shoulders, warm Middle-Eastern, seen only from
behind, his back to the camera and his face turned away, never shown, dark
against the sunset; no halo, no glow. All around him the crowd is rising too,
the fine-robed scholar among them, every face lifted toward the storyteller and
the open road beyond. The road home stretches away downhill into the last gold
light. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.
