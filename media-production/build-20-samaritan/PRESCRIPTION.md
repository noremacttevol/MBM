**Tier:** T3 Full Arc   **Target length:** 120–165s   **Pictures:** 16–24 (planned: 17)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the man you were raised to despise is the one who acts like God — and Jesus flips the whole question so you can't draw a line around who you're allowed to walk past.
- **The turn:** the Samaritan, not the priest or the Levite, being the one moved with compassion (n7→n9). The second hinge is Jesus turning the scholar's own question back — "which of them was neighbour?" (j1).
- **Character shown:** mercy has no borders, and he will not let you keep score of who counts.
- **Whose face carries it:** the BEATEN TRAVELER (the wounded stranger) and the SAMARITAN's compassion; the scholar's face at the end, unable to even say the word "Samaritan." Jesus is the teller — his face only via master ref in the framing beats (1–3, 14, 16).
- **The card question falls out of:** "Everyone finds themselves somewhere on that road. Where are you on it right now?" — falls straight out of the one thing. No change needed.
