# PRESCRIPTION — build-68-multitudes-mountain

**Row:** 68   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Matthew 15:29–32
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 13)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he healed thousands whose names nobody wrote down — a God who
  counts the days you have been carrying something and will not lose *your* name.
- **The turn:** four quiet words with no fanfare — "and he healed them" — the whole
  pain of a region set down at his feet and lifted in one sentence.
- **Character shown:** compassion that scales to a hillside and still notices one
  empty stomach — he remade their bodies and was thinking about their lunch.
- **Whose face carries it:** the CARRIERS and the HEALED — the ones who hauled family
  up the slope, the woman speaking her husband's name, the crowd glorifying God.
  Jesus's face only via master ref at the red line.
- **The card question falls out of:** "he remembers every one of them — he will not
  lose yours." No change needed.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 13 stills
1. [n0] Jesus climbing partway up the mountain and sitting down where he can be found — agrees: "climbed partway up a mountain, and sat down"
2. [n1] the crowds climbing, carrying the sick up the rocky slope — a father on a plank, a brother on a back, a blind neighbor led by the hand — "every step of that climb was somebody's love for somebody, written in sweat"
3. [n2/s30] the lame, blind, dumb, maimed laid down at Jesus' feet (blue) — "cast them down at Jesus' feet"
4. [n3] Jesus moving among them, healing — the four quiet words (master ref) — "and he healed them"
5. [n4] a healed woman speaking her husband's name for the first time — "a woman who had never spoken said her husband's name for the first time"
6. [n4] an old man's newly-opened eyes on his grandchild's face — "an old man's eyes came open on his grandchild's face"
7. [n5/s31] the crowd wondering — dumb speaking, maimed whole, lame walking, blind seeing (blue) — "the multitude wondered, when they saw the dumb to speak"
8. [n5b] the amazed faces glorifying the God of Israel, many of them outsiders — "they glorified the God of Israel"
9. [n6] the crowd staying — camped on the hillside three days, nobody going home — "they stayed. Three days, on a mountainside... nobody wanted to go home"
10. [j1] Jesus turning to the disciples, compassion over the crowd (red, master ref) — "I have compassion on the multitude, because they continue with me now three days"
11. [n6b] retell — hold on Jesus looking over the hungry crowd — "I am not sending them home hungry, in case they collapse on the way"
12. [n7] the ordinary tenderness — a God who counts your days, noticing their stomachs — "he noticed their stomachs... thinking about their lunch"
13. [card] warm hold on the healed crowd for the closing question — the card line
