## NARRATION (numbered segments — narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)

1. NARRATOR: He came back to Nazareth — the town that raised him — and on the Sabbath he walked into the synagogue like he always had.
2. NARRATOR: They handed him the scroll of Isaiah. He found the place, and read it out loud.
3. JESUS (KJV, Luke 4:18–19): "The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised, to preach the acceptable year of the Lord."
4. NARRATOR: Then he rolled the scroll up, handed it back, and sat down. Every eye in the room was fixed on him — this was the boy who grew up on their street. *(sacred-silence beat — narration cuts to silence here as the room waits)*
5. JESUS (KJV, Luke 4:21): "This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears."
6. NARRATOR: The promise Israel had waited on for centuries — the healing, the freedom, the good news for the poor — he said it was standing in front of them. Not someday. Today.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He still reads it as today. What would it mean if that promise were meant for you, right now?"
