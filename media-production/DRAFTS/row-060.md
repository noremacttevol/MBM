## NARRATION — numbered segments

Two-Voice Law: narrator = plain modern English, never echoes KJV wording. Jesus = exact KJV only. Non-Jesus speakers (the demon, Legion) are paraphrased by the narrator in modern English.

**n1** — NARRATOR:
"The night before this story, Jesus and his disciples crossed the sea of Galilee through a storm so violent that seasoned fishermen thought they were going to die. Jesus stilled it with a word. And when the boat touched the far shore — Gentile country, the region of the Gadarenes — it turned out there was a reason he had come: one man. A man everyone else had given up on."

**n2** — NARRATOR:
"He lived in the graveyard, among the tombs cut into the hillside. The town had tried everything to restrain him. Ropes, shackles, chains — he tore through all of them. No one was strong enough to hold him down, and by now, no one tried."

**n3** — NARRATOR:
"Night and day he wandered the tombs and the hills, screaming into the dark and turning the sharp edges of the rocks against his own skin. He was a man at war with himself — tormented, alone, and as good as dead to everyone who once knew him. That is who was waiting on the shore."

**n4** — NARRATOR:
"But when he saw Jesus step out of the boat — still far down the shore — something in him ran. Of all the directions that man could have run, he ran toward Jesus, and fell on his knees in front of him."

**n5** — NARRATOR:
"Then a voice came out of him that was not his own, shrieking: What do you want with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? Swear to God you won't torture me! — because Jesus was already commanding the thing to leave."

**j1** — JESUS (exact KJV, Mark 5:8):
"Come out of the man, thou unclean spirit."

**n6** — NARRATOR:
"Jesus asked it one question."

**j2** — JESUS (exact KJV, Mark 5:9):
"What is thy name?"

**n7** — NARRATOR:
"The answer came back: Legion, it said — there were many of them. In that occupied country, everyone knew the word. A legion was a Roman army unit — thousands of soldiers. It wasn't one darkness living in that man. It was an occupation."

**n8** — NARRATOR:
"The spirits begged Jesus not to send them out of the region. On the hillside above them, a herd of about two thousand pigs was feeding — this was Gentile land — and the demons begged to be sent into the herd instead. Jesus gave the word."

**n9** — NARRATOR:
"The whole herd broke at once — down the steep bank, into the sea, gone. To the men watching, it was a price tag: everything those spirits had been doing invisibly inside one human being, made visible in a single terrible minute. That is what had been living in him. That is what Jesus threw out."

**n10** — NARRATOR:
"The herdsmen ran and told the town, and the town came out to see for themselves. And there he was — the wild man of the tombs — sitting down, dressed, calm, clear-eyed, himself again, at the feet of Jesus."

**n11** — NARRATOR:
"And the town was afraid. Strange, isn't it — they had learned to live with the man being broken; they did not know how to live with him healed, or with the kind of power that healed him. They added up the cost of the pigs, looked at Jesus, and begged him to leave. And Jesus — who never forces himself on anyone — turned back to the boat."

**n12** — NARRATOR:
"As he was boarding, the healed man begged for the one thing he wanted: to come with him. And Jesus told him no. Out of everyone in the gospels who asked to follow Jesus, this may be the only man who was turned down — because Jesus had a different mission for him."

**j3** — JESUS (exact KJV, Mark 5:19):
"Go home to thy friends, and tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee, and hath had compassion on thee."

**n13** — NARRATOR:
"So the man with the worst story in the region became its first missionary. He walked back into the towns that knew his past — ten Gentile cities' worth of them, the Decapolis — and told them everything. And people were amazed. He had no training and no credentials. He had a before, and an after, and the name of the one who made the difference. That is all a witness has ever been. If God has done anything in your life, you already have the whole job description: go back to your people, and tell them your story."

**card** — NARRATOR (read aloud over closing question card):
"Everyone had given up on the man in the tombs. Jesus crossed a storm to reach him. What is the one thing in your life you've decided is too far gone — and what if it isn't?"
