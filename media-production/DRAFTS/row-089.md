# DRAFT row-089 — The last supper — Luke 22:14–20

> Row 89 · Ref: Luke 22:14–20 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: "shed" clean. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: On the night before he died, Jesus sat down to a final Passover meal with the twelve men closest to him.
2. NARRATOR: He told them he had longed to share this meal with them — he knew what was coming, and still he wanted to be with them. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: He took bread, gave thanks, broke it, and handed it to them.
4. JESUS (KJV, Luke 22:19): "This is my body which is given for you: this do in remembrance of me."
5. NARRATOR: Then he took the cup and gave it to them the same way.
6. JESUS (KJV, Luke 22:20): "This cup is the new testament in my blood, which is shed for you."
7. NARRATOR: Everything he was about to suffer, he handed to them first as a gift — bread and a cup, so they'd never forget what his love cost.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He gave himself for you before you ever asked. Come to his table."

## STORYBOARD (8–14 beats)
1. Interior night, warm lamplight: a low table set for Passover, thirteen men reclining, Jesus at center. Face SHOWN, cream robe.
2. Close on Jesus's face, tender and heavy with knowing, warm brown eyes moving over his friends. Master-locked.
3. Jesus's hands taking a flat loaf of bread, giving thanks, breaking it. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the breaking bread)*
4. Jesus passing broken bread to a disciple's cupped hands across the lamplit table.
5. Jesus lifting a simple cup of red wine, speaking the words of the new testament.
6. The disciples receiving the cup, faces solemn and moved, not yet understanding.
7. Wide: the whole table in warm glow, intimate and quiet, the weight of the night around them.
8. Closing still: the broken bread and the cup resting together on the table in soft light — remembrance.
