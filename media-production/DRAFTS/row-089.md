# DRAFT row-089 — The Last Supper — Luke 22:14–20

> Row 89 · Ref: Luke 22:14–20 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (intimate meal, no violence).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: Clean. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: The Passover had come. Jesus gathered his closest friends around one table in an upper room, knowing the night would change everything.
2. NARRATOR: He told them plainly: he had wanted this meal with them before the hard thing happened. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: Then he took the bread, gave thanks, broke it, and gave it to them — and said it was his body, given for them. A gift to remember him by.
4. JESUS (KJV, Luke 22:19): "This is my body which is given for you: this do in remembrance of me."
5. NARRATOR: After the meal he took the cup, and called it the new promise written in his own blood, poured out for them.
6. JESUS (KJV, Luke 22:20): "This cup is the new testament in my blood, which is shed for you."
7. NARRATOR: He said he would not drink the fruit of the vine again until the kingdom of God arrives. Then he and his friends sang together and walked out into the night.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He left a simple meal to remember him by. Come to his table — he is still the host."

## STORYBOARD (9 beats — CONTENT-CARE: intimate, faces not dread)
1. Exterior, dusk: a quiet upper-room window glowing warm over a sleeping city; the focus is the light, not the streets below.
2. Interior, evening lamplight: the small group around a low table, Jesus at the center, faces turned toward him. Jesus FACE-SHOWN — master-locked, Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
3. Jesus breaking the bread, hands open and gentle; disciples watching, a few leaning in. Master face visible, calm and tender.
4. Close on the cup lifted in Jesus's hands; the room soft-lit, faces reflecting the moment. *(sacred-silence beat — still holds on the lifted cup and his face)*
5. A disciple resting near Jesus, listening; warmth of friendship, no tension shown.
6. The group, turned to Jesus as he speaks the promise; intimate, unhurried.
7. Jesus's face, full master-locked close, speaking the words of remembrance — love in his eyes, not sorrow.
8. The group rising, singing softly, moving toward the door as night falls; hope, not dread.
9. Closing still: the empty table with two items left — bread and cup in the lamplight, an open invitation. Reverent, inviting.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE deep — intimate Passover meal, no violence/gore (betrayal only implied, never shown). Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked adult face. Mercy and love spoken (J-story law). ~55-65s. Narrator Andrew; Jesus Christopher exact KJV.
