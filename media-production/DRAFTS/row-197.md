# DRAFT row-197 — Your sons and your daughters shall prophesy — Joel 2:28–29 (Acts 2:17–18)

> Row 197 · Ref: Joel 2:28–29 (quoted Acts 2:17–18) · Jesus NOT present bodily (OT prophecy + Acts fulfillment after ascension). CONTENT-CARE: symbolic only.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: Clean. No bow / wound / wind / tears / lead / sow / live. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: The prophet Joel looked down the long corridor of time and described a day the LORD promised — not for a few insiders, but for all.
2. NARRATOR: God would pour out His Spirit on every kind of person. Sons and daughters would speak His words. Old men would dream. Young men would see visions.
3. NARRATOR: And it would not stop at age or status — even servants, men and women alike, would be filled with the Spirit. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: Decades later, on the day of Pentecost, the apostle Peter stood and said this promise had arrived. The Spirit fell, and ordinary people told of the mighty works of God.
5. NARRATOR: The promise was never meant to be locked in one building or one office. It was poured out — freely, widely, for whoever calls on the name of the LORD.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The promise was 'all flesh.' That includes you. The Spirit is offered — receive Him."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — Jesus absent bodily, no adult master face)
1. Exterior, open field at dawn: the prophet Joel standing, hand out toward the horizon — a future being named.
2. Sweeping still: people of every age and station — son, daughter, elder, servant — each touched by a shared light.
3. The upper room at Pentecost, gathered believers, wind-and-flame motif kept symbolic and soft (no CGI fire on faces).
4. Peter rising to speak to the crowd, joy and boldness on his face. *(sacred-silence beat — the crowd's intake, the promise landing)*
5. Diverse faces across the city, each hearing in their own tongue — the Spirit poured out.
6. Closing still: light spilling across all people, no boundary — "all flesh."
