# DRAFT row-193 — The Comforter shall teach you all things — John 14:26

> Row 193 · Ref: John 14:26 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN (upper room, the night before the cross). CONTENT-CARE: teaching, comfort, no gore.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: On the night before He died, Jesus sat with His disciples and told them they would not be left alone.
2. NARRATOR: He promised another Helper would come — the Holy Ghost, sent by the Father in His name. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: This Helper would do two things: teach them everything, and bring every word Jesus had spoken back to their minds.
4. JESUS (KJV, John 14:26): "But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you."
5. NARRATOR: The promise still stands — the Spirit who taught them then teaches everyone who listens now.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "You are not left to remember alone. The Comforter is here — let Him teach you."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: upper room, comfort; Jesus FACE-SHOWN)
1. Interior, lamp-lit upper room at night: Jesus with the twelve around a low table — calm, intimate. Jesus FACE-SHOWN (adult, master ref: Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo).
2. Jesus's face turned toward the disciples as He speaks the promise — master-locked, tender.
3. The disciples listening, faces lifted, some leaning in — receiving. *(sacred-silence beat — still holds on Jesus's face and the quiet room)*
4. A soft wisp of light at the window — the promised Comforter, hinted, never personified as a man.
5. Close on Jesus's hands open on the table — invitation, not farewell dread.
6. Exterior, pre-dawn: a single lamp still burning in the upper room — His word held in memory.
7. Closing still: Jesus's face meeting the viewer, calm — the Comforter's promise. Hopeful.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — teaching, comfort, no gore. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked (adult, not child). The Holy Ghost shown only as gentle light, never a face. ~50–60s. Narrator Andrew; Jesus Christopher exact KJV.
