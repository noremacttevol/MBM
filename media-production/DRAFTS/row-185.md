# DRAFT row-185 — In my Father's house are many mansions — John 14:2–3

> Row 185 · Ref: John 14:2–3 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN (upper-room discourse). CONTENT-CARE (comfort, no violence).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: On the night before everything changed, Jesus sat with his disciples and told them not to let their hearts be troubled.
2. NARRATOR: He was going somewhere — but not to leave them orphaned. He was going to get a place ready.
3. JESUS (KJV, John 14:2): "In my Father's house are many mansions: if it were not so, I would have told you. I go to prepare a place for you."
4. NARRATOR: A house with room for everyone. He said it plainly — if it were not true, he would have told them. *(sacred-silence beat)*
5. JESUS (KJV, John 14:3): "And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and receive you unto myself; that where I am, there ye may be also."
6. NARRATOR: He was not describing a far-off maybe. He was promising to come back and carry them home himself.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He went to prepare a place — and he's coming back for you. You are not forgotten."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked)
1. Upper room at night, lamp-lit, the disciples reclining; quiet, intimate. Jesus FACE-SHOWN (master ref: Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo).
2. Close on Jesus's face as he says "let not your heart be troubled" — calm, steady.
3. A soft beat: the room holds still, the promise landing. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. Jesus speaks of the Father's house — cut to a gentle image of many rooms of light, warm and welcoming (suggestive, not literal).
5. Close on Jesus's face again, "I will come again" — hope in his eyes, master-locked.
6. The disciples' faces softening, reassured.
7. Closing still: dawn light at the window — the promise of return.
