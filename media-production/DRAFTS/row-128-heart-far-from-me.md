## FRAMING (Jesus Method): milk, Christ-defending-the-heart. Jesus answers men who cared
more about outward ritual than about God — and he says plainly what God actually wants:
the heart, not the performance. It lands as comfort and honesty (God sees past the show),
never as an attack on any group today. Ends on the warm invitation to come to God with a
real heart, not a polished one.

## NARRATION
1. NARRATOR: Some of the religious leaders came to Jesus with a complaint. They had noticed his disciples eating with unwashed hands — breaking not God's law, but a tradition the elders had added on top of it. To them, that was a scandal.
2. NARRATOR: Jesus was not impressed by the show. He answered them with words the prophet Isaiah had written long before.
3. JESUS (KJV Mark 7:6–7): This people honoureth me with their lips, but their heart is far from me. Howbeit in vain do they worship me, teaching for doctrines the commandments of men. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: Lips close, heart far away. You can say all the right words, keep every custom, look perfectly religious — and God can still feel a thousand miles from you, because he was never looking at the hands. He was looking at the heart.
5. JESUS (KJV Mark 7:8): For laying aside the commandment of God, ye hold the tradition of men.
6. NARRATOR: Then Jesus gave them an example that exposed the whole game. God commanded, Honour thy father and thy mother. But their tradition let a man declare his money "Corban" — a gift set apart for the temple — and then use that word as an excuse to give nothing to his own aging parents.
7. JESUS (KJV Mark 7:13): Making the word of God of none effect through your tradition, which ye have delivered.
8. NARRATOR: They had used a religious rule to cancel a command of love. That is what he was warning against — letting the outside of faith grow so loud that it drowns out the heart of it. God would always rather have your honest heart than your flawless performance.
9. CLOSING CARD (invitation): "He was never looking at your hands. Come to him with your real heart — that is the worship he asked for."

   table — dark scholarly robes (only Jesus wears cream). Their eyes converge on Jesus.
2. The disciples' simple meal, ordinary hands, bread on a plain table — nothing wrong here.
3. Jesus answering, composed, the leaders listening — Jesus the clear center of the frame.
   *(Mark 7:6–7 lands — sacred hold)*
4. A close, quiet image of a human heart-level gesture: a hand over the chest vs. hands
   raised in show — the contrast of "lips near, heart far."
5. The "Corban" example staged simply: a bag of coins labeled/set toward the temple in the
   foreground, while an aged mother and father wait, unhelped, in the background.
6. Jesus's steady face as he names it plainly. *(Mark 7:13)*
7. Closing still: an ordinary person, alone, quietly sincere before God — no audience, no
   performance — the honest heart he actually wants.
