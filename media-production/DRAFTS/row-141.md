# DRAFT row-141 — "I am the bread of life" — John 6:35, 48, 51

> Row 141 · Ref: John 6:35, 48, 51 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (gentle, no violence, no shame).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: live(/liv/) — appears in KJV John 6:51 "he shall live for ever" (say /liv/, not /laiv/). No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: A crowd had followed Jesus after He fed thousands with a few loaves. They wanted more bread. He gave them something deeper.
2. JESUS (KJV, John 6:35): "I am the bread of life: he that cometh to me shall never hunger; and he that believeth on me shall never thirst."
3. NARRATOR: Not the kind you chew. The kind that satisfies the part of you food can't reach. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. JESUS (KJV, John 6:48): "I am that bread of life."
5. NARRATOR: The bread their ancestors ate in the wilderness didn't keep them alive forever. But the bread Jesus gives does.
6. JESUS (KJV, John 6:51): "I am the living bread which came down from heaven: if any man eat of this bread, he shall live for ever: and the bread that I will give is my flesh, which I will give for the life of the world."
7. NARRATOR: He was speaking of Himself — given, like bread broken, so the world could have life.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "Come hungry. He is the bread that actually fills you."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: gentle, faces and light carry it)
1. Exterior, daytime, lakeshore: crowd gathered, Jesus teaching, loaves in a basket beside Him. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
2. Close on a few loaves and fish, the miracle's remnant, as Jesus speaks of bread.
3. Faces in the crowd, leaning in, still hungry for more than food. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds a listening face)*
4. Close on Jesus's face as He says "I am the bread of life" — steady, gentle.
5. Illustrative: a single loaf broken, light within it, the wilderness manna fading behind.
6. Close on Jesus's face as He speaks of giving His flesh for the world's life — solemn, loving.
7. Closing still: an open hand offering bread, light spilling — invitation.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — gentle, no violence, no gore. "Flesh" reference kept reverent, not graphic. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked. ~50-60s. Jesus lines exact KJV John 6:35, 48, 51.
