# DRAFT row-143 — "I am the door" — John 10:7, 9

> Row 143 · Ref: John 10:7, 9 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (gentle, no violence, no shame).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live/lives/lived. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Jesus used a picture the crowd knew well — a sheepfold with one opening, and a shepherd who guards it.
2. JESUS (KJV, John 10:7): "Verily, verily, I say unto you, I am the door of the sheep."
3. NARRATOR: Anyone who climbs in some other way is there to take, not to care. But the door? The door lets the flock in safe. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. JESUS (KJV, John 10:9): "I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in and out, and find pasture."
5. NARRATOR: Through Him you come in and you go out — free, fed, not trapped. He's the way in, and the way to everything good.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "There's one door. Knock, and walk through."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: gentle, faces and the open fold carry it)
1. Exterior, daytime, hillside pasture: sheep, a stone sheepfold with one opening, Jesus standing at the threshold. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
2. Close on Jesus's face as He says "I am the door of the sheep" — protective, gentle.
3. Illustrative: a figure trying to climb the wall elsewhere, contrasted with the open gate. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds the open door)*
4. The sheep moving safely through the single opening, Jesus at the threshold.
5. Wide: sheep grazing in green pasture, peaceful, free — "go in and out, and find pasture."
6. Close on Jesus's face as He repeats "I am the door" — open, inviting.
7. Closing still: the open door of the fold, light beyond it — invitation.
