# DRAFT row-126 — By their fruits — Matthew 7:15–20

> Row 126 · Ref: Matthew 7:15–20 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN (adult, master ref). CONTENT-CARE (teaching — gentle warning, no fear framing).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: None of bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Jesus taught how to tell what is true — look at what grows from it.
2. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 7:15): "Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves."
3. NARRATOR: A tree shows what it is by what it bears. You don't guess at a tree by its bark. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 7:16–17): "Ye shall know them by their fruits. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles? Even so every good tree bringeth forth good fruit; but a corrupt tree bringeth forth evil fruit."
5. NARRATOR: The good and the rotten cannot switch places — what is inside comes out in the open.
6. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 7:19–20): "Every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire. Wherefore by their fruits ye shall know them."
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "What grows from a life with him is good fruit — love, kindness, truth. Abide in him, and let it show."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: teaching, nature imagery, no fear close-up)
1. Exterior, mountainside, daytime: Jesus teaching a gathered crowd — FACE-SHOWN, master-locked, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
2. Close on Jesus's face as he warns of wolves in sheep's clothing — calm, clear, protective not frightening.
3. A field beat: a wolf disguised near sheep, then revealed — gentle illustration, not graphic. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the contrast)*
4. A healthy tree heavy with good fruit; sunlight on it — the good tree.
5. A barren, twisted tree — the corrupt one, shown plainly, no gore.
6. Hands gathering fruit from the good tree — what is inside made visible.
7. Closing still: Jesus's face, open and teaching — know them by their fruits, and grow good ones.
