## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Jesus was preparing His disciples for a hard goodbye. His words could have brought fear — instead they brought comfort.
2. NARRATOR: Don't let your heart be troubled, He said. Trust God. Trust Me.
3. NARRATOR: He described a home they couldn't see yet — a place He was going to make ready for them, room for each one. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. JESUS (KJV, John 14:1–3): "Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me. In my Father's house are many mansions: if it were not so, I would have told you. I go to prepare a place for you."
5. NARRATOR: And then the promise that undoes every fear: I'm coming back. I'll bring you to Myself, so you're where I am.
6. NARRATOR: Not a vague hope. A prepared place. A return. A reunion.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He's preparing a place — and He's coming back for you. Let not your heart be troubled."
