# DRAFT row-184 — Caught up to the third heaven — 2 Corinthians 12:2–4

> Row 184 · Ref: 2 Corinthians 12:2–4 · Jesus NOT in frame (Paul's heavenly vision). CONTENT-CARE (reverent vision).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Paul wrote of a man he knew — caught up, in a single moment, far beyond the everyday world.
2. NARRATOR: Whether in the body or out of it, Paul could not say; God alone knew. The man was lifted to the third heaven. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: And from there, to paradise itself — where he heard words no human tongue is permitted to repeat.
4. NARRATOR: He could have boasted. He had the credentials. Instead he pointed to his weakness, so that Christ's strength could rest on him.
5. NARRATOR (paraphrasing Paul, 2 Cor 12:9): when Paul was shown more than words can carry, he still pointed back to grace — "my strength is made perfect in weakness."
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "Paul was shown more than words can hold — and still pointed back to grace. There is more ahead than you can imagine."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — Jesus NOT in frame)
1. Paul alone, eyes lifted, a quiet room at night; the world around him still and ordinary.
2. A soft upward motion suggested — light gathering, the boundary of the seen world thinning. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. The third heaven suggested as layered light, never a literal figure — reverent distance; no face of Christ here.
4. Paradise as warmth and stillness, a place of rest, not spectacle.
5. Paul returned, humbled, pen in hand — the letter being written.
6. Close on Paul's face, peace rather than pride.
7. Closing still: the everyday world glowing with the hint of what lies beyond.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — no violence, reverent vision. Jesus does NOT appear; this is Paul's account (2 Cor 12). No master face. ~50-60s. Narrator Andrew; keep any KJV as narrator paraphrased (no Jesus lines here).
