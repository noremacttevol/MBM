## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Jesus was inside a packed house, teaching, when word came in from the edge of the crowd.
2. NARRATOR: His mother and his brothers were standing outside, asking for him.
3. NARRATOR: The people near him passed it forward: your family's here, they want you. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: He looked around at the ordinary people sitting in a circle right in front of him — and asked a question nobody expected.
5. JESUS (KJV, Mark 3:33–34): "Who is my mother, or my brethren?... Behold my mother and my brethren!"
6. JESUS (KJV, Mark 3:35): "For whosoever shall do the will of God, the same is my brother, and my sister, and mother."
7. NARRATOR: He wasn't pushing his family away. He was opening the circle — telling a room full of nobodies they could belong to him like blood.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He drew the family line around whoever would come. That door is open to you too."

## STORYBOARD (8–16 beats)
1. Interior day, warm light through a doorway: a small stone house crammed with seated listeners, Jesus at the center teaching. Face SHOWN, cream robe.
2. Exterior of the same house: a dignified older woman (Mary) and several men waiting at the edge of the crowd, trying to send word in. Warm daylight.
3. A listener near the door leaning to relay the message inward, hand cupped; heads turning. 
4. Jesus pausing, looking slowly around the circle of common faces seated close around him — farmers, fishermen, women, a child. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on his gaze sweeping the room)*
5. Close on Jesus's face, warm brown eyes, a gentle almost-smile, asking the question. Master-locked.
6. Jesus with an open hand gesturing to the whole seated circle — "behold my mother and my brethren" — the people looking at each other, surprised and moved.
7. Wide: the circle of ordinary people bathed in warm light, drawn in close around him like a family.
8. Closing still: an open doorway of the house with warm light spilling out into the street, room for anyone to step in.
