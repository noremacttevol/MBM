## Narration Script (plain text, Aloud ready)

Hey Cameron. Row 003. Sinners and taxgatherers drew near to hear Jesus. The Pharisees and scribes murmured, saying "This man receiveth sinners, and eateth with them." Jesus told them a parable: "What man of you having a hundred sheep, and having lost one of them, doth not leave the ninety and nine in the wilderness, and go after that which is lost, until he find it? And when he hath found it, he layeth it on his shoulders, rejoicing. And when he cometh home, he calleth together his friends and neighbors, saying unto them Rejoice with me; for I have found my sheep which was lost." I say unto you, like wise there shall be more joy in heaven over one sinner that repenteth than over ninety and nine just persons which need no repentance.

This parable is about where God's heart is. Not the ninety and nine who are safe. Not the comfortable majority. God's heart is on the one lost one. He leaves them all to go find that one. He carries that one home rejoicing. He celebrates that one more than all the rest. You are the one sheep. God left to find you.
