## Narration Script (plain text, Aloud ready)

Hey Cameron. Row 007. Jesus stood by the lake of Gennesaret. The people pressed upon him to hear the word of God. He saw two ships standing by the brink of the lake, but the fishermen were washing their nets. He entered into one of the ships, which was Simon's, and prayed him that he would thrust out a little from the land. He sat down and taught the people out of the ship. When he had left speaking, he said unto Simon "Put out into the deep, and let down your nets for a draught." Simon answered and said "Master, we have toiled all the night, and have taken nothing. Nevertheless at thy word I will let down my net." When they had this done, they enclosed a great multitude of fishes, and their net brake. They beckoned unto their partners, which were in the other ship, that they should come and help them. They came and filled both the ships, so that they began to sink. When Simon Peter saw it, he fell down at Jesus' knees, saying "Depart from me; for I am a sinful man, O Lord." For he was astonished, and all that were with him, at the take of the fishes which they had taken. And so were also James and John, the sons of Zebedee, which were partners with Simon. And Jesus said unto Simon "Fear not; from henceforth thou shalt catch men." They brought their ships to land, forsook all, and followed him.

This story is about how discipleship starts. Not with grand promises. Not with power. With one command: "Follow me." Peter had failed all night. He went out at Jesus' word anyway. He caught more than he ever imagined. And when he saw who he was, Peter didn't say "Hire me." He said "Depart from me, I am sinful." And Jesus said "No. Follow me."
