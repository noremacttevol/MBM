# DRAFT row-086 — The wise men — Matthew 2:1–12

> Row 86 · Ref: Matthew 2:1–12 · Jesus is a CHILD (young child), NOT the adult master face. Nativity/Epiphany.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: Clean. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Some time after Jesus was born, travelers came from the east — scholars who had read the skies. They had seen his star, and they followed it.
2. NARRATOR: They arrived in Jerusalem asking a dangerous question.
3. WISE MEN (KJV, Matthew 2:2): "Where is he that is born King of the Jews? for we have seen his star in the east, and are come to worship him."
4. NARRATOR: They were led at last to a house in Bethlehem. And there he was — not in a palace, but with his mother. *(sacred-silence beat)*
5. NARRATOR: They fell down and worshipped the child, and opened their treasures — gold, frankincense, and myrrh.
6. WISE MEN (KJV, Matthew 2:11): "And when they were come into the house, they saw the young child with Mary his mother, and fell down, and worshipped him: and when they had opened their treasures, they presented unto him gifts; gold, and frankincense, and myrrh."
7. NARRATOR: Warned in a dream, they went home another way. The nations had come to bow to the King.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "Strangers from far away recognized him first. The door is open — you're invited to worship too."

## STORYBOARD (8–11 beats — Jesus is a CHILD, no adult master face)
1. Exterior night, desert road: three travelers on camels, a brilliant star leading them eastward.
2. Interior, King Herod's hall: the wise men before a troubled court, asking their question.
3. Exterior, the star moving ahead of them toward a small house.
4. Interior humble house: Mary seated, the young child Jesus on her lap — CHILD, not adult master face.
5. The wise men kneeling, faces of reverence, presenting opened gifts. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the kneeling figures and the child)*
6. Close on the gifts: gold, frankincense, myrrh, laid before the child.
7. The travelers departing by a different road under the star, peace on their faces.
8. Closing still: the star above the little house — the nations have found their King.

## NOTES: Jesus is a YOUNG CHILD here — never attach the adult master face ref. Warm, reverent, no fear framing. Gifts shown with dignity. ~55-65s. Narrator Andrew; wise-men lines Christopher (KJV Matthew 2:2, 2:11).
