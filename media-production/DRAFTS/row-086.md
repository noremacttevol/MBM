## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only — no Jesus speech here, he is a child)
1. NARRATOR: After Jesus was born, wise men from the east came to Jerusalem, following a star they'd watched rise.
2. NARRATOR: They were asking one question that shook the whole city: where is the one born King of the Jews? We've come to worship him.
3. NARRATOR: King Herod heard it and was troubled — a rival king was the last thing he wanted. He sent them on to Bethlehem, pretending he wanted to worship too. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: The star went ahead of them and stopped over the place where the child was. When they saw it stop, their joy overflowed.
5. NARRATOR: They came in, and there he was with Mary his mother. They fell down and worshipped him, and opened their treasures — gold, frankincense, and myrrh.
6. NARRATOR: Warned in a dream not to go back to Herod, they went home another way — quietly guarding the child they'd crossed the world to find.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "They followed a light a long way to find him, and gave him their best. He's just as findable now. What would you bring?"

## STORYBOARD (8–16 beats)
1. Night sky, deep blue: a single bright star hanging over a distant desert horizon; a small caravan of robed travelers on camels moving toward it. Warm lantern light.
2. Jerusalem by day: three richly-dressed foreign wise men asking their question in a busy street, city folk turning to listen. 
3. Interior palace, cooler light: King Herod on a throne, face tight with unease, leaning toward an advisor — trouble behind a thin smile.
4. Night again: the star ahead of the travelers, low over a small town (Bethlehem), its light pooling on the road. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the star stopped over the house)*
5. A humble house doorway, warm glow within: the wise men arriving, dismounting, treasures in hand.
6. Interior, warm: Mary seated with the child Jesus; the wise men kneeling, bowing low in worship. Reverent, tender. (Child Jesus — no adult face-law here; painted as a young child.)
7. Close: three opened gifts in the lamplight — a gleam of gold, a curl of frankincense smoke, a vial of myrrh.
8. Closing still: the caravan leaving by a different road under the fading star at dawn, the town safe behind them.
