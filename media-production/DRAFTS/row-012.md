## Narration Script (plain text, Aloud ready)

Hey Cameron. Row 012. On that day, when the even was come, Jesus said unto his disciples "Let us pass over unto the other side." And when they had sent away the multitude, they took even so him as he was in the ship. And there were also with him other little ships. And there arose a great storm of wind, and the waves beat into the ship. Jesus was in the hinder part of the ship, asleep on a pillow. The disciples woke him, saying "Master, carest thou not that we perish?" He arose and rebuked the wind, and said unto the sea "Peace, be still." And the waves ceased instantly. He said unto them "Why are ye so fearful? How is it that ye have no faith?" They were afraid with great fear, asking "Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?"

Jesus slept in the same boat they were in. The same boat that sank every day. The same boat that had nothing over God's control. But Jesus slept. He had no fear. When they woke him in panic, he didn't panic back. He rebuked the wind. He rebuked the sea. Peace, be still. And the sea stopped. The disciples didn't wonder how Jesus calmed the storm. They wondered WHO Jesus was.
