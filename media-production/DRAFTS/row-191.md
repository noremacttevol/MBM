# DRAFT row-191 — The windows of heaven — Malachi 3:10

> Row 191 · Ref: Malachi 3:10 · Jesus does NOT appear (Old Testament; prophet Malachi speaks for the LORD). CONTENT-CARE (blessing, hope).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Through the prophet Malachi, the LORD made a strange offer to a people holding back — bring everything in, and watch what I do.
2. NARRATOR: The storehouse was the place set apart for the Lord's house, and the tithe was the part meant for him.
3. NARRATOR: Then came the dare no king could make — test me, and see if I don't open the windows. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: Not a trickle. A pouring out, more than there is room to hold.
5. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He invites you to test his goodness. Bring it all, and watch the windows open."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: blessing, no lack-shame)
1. Exterior, temple storehouse at dawn, quiet and dignified — the "house of the LORD" shown with reverence. Jesus does NOT appear (Old Testament prophet speaks).
2. Hands placing grain and firstfruits into the storehouse — the tithe given willingly, no shame framing of poverty.
3. The prophet Malachi, robed, speaking to a gathered crowd — faces turned toward him, listening.
4. A sky-scene: clouds parting, light streaming down like opened windows — the promise made visible. *(sacred-silence beat — still holds on the light pouring)*
5. A household overflowing with blessing, baskets full, room running out — joy, not greed.
6. Close on a face lifted toward the opened sky, wonder not worry.
7. Closing still: the storehouse door open to morning light, an invitation. Hopeful, generous.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — no lack-shame, blessing spoken, no violence. Jesus does NOT appear; this is Malachi's prophecy of the LORD's promise. No wounds. ~50-60s. Narrator Andrew.
