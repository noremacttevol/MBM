# DRAFT row-175 — The mountain of the LORD's house — Isaiah 2:2–3

> Row 175 · Ref: Isaiah 2:2–3 · Jesus NOT appearing (Isaiah's prophecy of the last days).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: none. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live present. Clean.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: In the last days, a mountain would rise above all mountains — not by height, but by drawing every people to it.
2. NARRATOR: The house of the Lord would be established, and nations would stream toward it, saying: come, let us walk in his ways. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: From Zion would go the law, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem.
4. NARRATOR: Not by force, but by invitation — the mountain everyone chooses to climb.
5. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The mountain is open to you. Come, and walk in his paths."

## STORYBOARD (7 beats — CONTENT-CARE: no war/violence; focus on peace and invitation only)
1. Exterior, sweeping vista, dawn: a luminous mountain rising above lesser hills — symbolic, not literal geography.
2. Close: people of many kinds beginning to walk toward it — daylight, hopeful.
3. A still of the mountain's base, a gathering, faces upward — invitation, not fear. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. The path winding upward, warm light along it.
5. A frame of the city of peace on the heights — Jerusalem as Zion, gentle.
6. Many peoples together at the summit, listening — unity.
7. Closing still: first light on the mountain, the word going out — peaceful, grand, never grim.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE deep — though Isaiah's context includes war, this beat shows only peace and invitation. Jesus does NOT appear (Isaiah prophecy, pre-incarnate) — storyboard carries no master face. Mercy spoken (J-story law). ~55s. Narrator Andrew.
