# DRAFT row-150 — The Shepherd Psalm — Psalm 23:1–6

> Row 150 · Ref: Psalm 23:1–6 · Jesus does NOT appear as a character (Old Testament; the Shepherd is the LORD). CONTENT-CARE: valley of shadow reverent, no gore.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: lead(/leed/, rhymes with bead) — "He leads me"; spoken "leed," captions KJV. live(/liv/) — "all the days of my life"; no other homographs.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, paraphrasing the psalm, NEVER quoting KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only — none here)
1. NARRATOR: The LORD is my shepherd, the psalm says — so I have everything I need.
2. NARRATOR: He leads me to quiet water and green places to rest. He puts my life back together.
3. NARRATOR: He guides me down the right paths, for His name's sake. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: Even in the darkest valley, I'm not afraid. You are with me — Your rod and staff steady me.
5. NARRATOR: You set a table for me right in front of my enemies. You honor me; my cup runs over.
6. NARRATOR: Surely Your goodness and mercy will follow me every day of my life, and I'll dwell with the LORD forever.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The Shepherd who leads, restores, and walks the dark valley with you is the same One who invites you home. Let Him lead."

## STORYBOARD (8–11 beats — CONTENT-CARE: reverent; Jesus does NOT appear)
1. Exterior, morning: a shepherd and a small flock on green hills beside still water — peace.
2. Close on the shepherd's weathered hand guiding a lamb — restoration.
3. The flock moving along a narrow right-hand path, soft light ahead.
4. Valley at dusk, long shadows but a figure walking unfearful beside the sheep — presence, not terror. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the calm figure)*
5. A table set in open country, cup overflowing, enemies' silhouettes far off and small.
6. The shepherd anointing the sheep's head, oil glistening — honor.
7. The flock following toward a glowing dwelling on the horizon — home.
8. Closing still: the shepherd standing at the edge of light, staff in hand, facing the viewer — invitation.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE deep — valley of the shadow of death rendered as reverent low light, never graphic. Jesus does NOT appear as a character; the Shepherd is the LORD (Yahweh). Keep the psalm's comfort, no fear. ~50–60s. Narrator Andrew; no Jesus voice.
