## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Jesus went back to Nazareth, the town that watched Him grow up. He taught in the synagogue.
2. NARRATOR: But the people who'd known Him as a boy couldn't believe. They took offense at Him — and their unbelief became a wall.
3. NARRATOR: He could do only a few mighty works there. Not because His power ran out, but because they wouldn't receive it.
4. JESUS (context, KJV, Mark 6:5): "And he could there do no mighty work, save that he laid his hands upon a few sick folk, and healed them. And he marvelled because of their unbelief."
5. NARRATOR: He still healed the few who came. Even amazement at their doubt didn't stop His mercy. *(sacred-silence beat)*
6. NARRATOR: Faith opens the door. Where people believed, even a little, He worked.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He healed the few who reached out. The door is the same today — believe, and let Him work."
