# DRAFT row-095 — The thief on the cross — Luke 23:39–43

> Row 95 · Ref: Luke 23:39–43 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (crucifixion, reverent).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: "paradise" clean. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Two criminals were crucified with Jesus, one on each side. One of them sneered — if you're really the Christ, save yourself and us.
2. NARRATOR: But the other one stopped him. We're getting what we deserve, he said. This man has done nothing wrong. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: Then he turned his head toward Jesus and asked for the smallest thing — just to be remembered.
4. NARRATOR: Lord, remember me when you come into your kingdom. No good deeds to offer. No time left to fix his life. Just a dying man asking.
5. JESUS (KJV, Luke 23:43): "To day shalt thou be with me in paradise."
6. NARRATOR: Today. Not someday, not after you've earned it. Today. The last-minute faith of a criminal was enough.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He saved a man who had nothing to give but a request. It's never too late to ask."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: reverent, faces not wounds)
1. Exterior, heavy sky: three crosses on the hill in dignified silhouette/mid-distance; the focus is faces and sky, never suffering detail.
2. The mocking thief, face twisted, turned away — bitterness. (Composed, no gore.)
3. The second thief, face turning toward the center cross, something softening — recognition dawning.
4. Close two-shot: the repentant thief's face turned to Jesus, asking; Jesus's face turned toward him. Both master-composed, warm brown eyes meeting. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the two faces)*
5. Close on Jesus's face, tender even here, speaking the promise of paradise. Master-locked.
6. A soft break of warm light on the center and the repentant thief — grace in the darkest place.
7. Closing still: light breaking through the clouds onto the hill — today, paradise. Reverent, hopeful.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE deep — no wounds/blood/nails in focus, reverent distance and faces. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked. Mercy spoken (J-story law). ~55-65s. Narrator Andrew; Jesus Christopher exact KJV.
