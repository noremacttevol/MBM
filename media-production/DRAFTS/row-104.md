## Narration Script (plain text, Aloud ready)

Hey Cameron. Row 104. Samuel was a young boy serving God under the priest Eli. The word of the Lord was rare in those days, visions were not widespread. One night, Samuel lay down to sleep in the temple where the ark of God was. The Lord called "Samuel," and Samuel ran to Eli and said, "Here I am; thou calledst me." Eli told him he didn't call and to go back. This happened three times. The third time, Eli realized "the Lord calls thee" and told Samuel to go lie down, and if he is called, say "Speak; for thy servant heareth." Samuel did that. The Lord came and stood and said "Samuel, Samuel. He said, Speak; for thy servant heareth." Then the Lord revealed a prophecy to Samuel about Eli's house. In the morning, Samuel opened the doors of the house of the Lord and was afraid to tell Eli. When they both knew that the Lord had called the boy, Samuel told him all the words and hid nothing.

This story shows us that hearing God is learned, not automatic. Samuel hadn't heard the Lord's voice before. Eli had to teach him to respond. Three times Samuel thought it was Eli calling, but the third time Eli realized and taught him what to say: "Speak, Lord, for Thy servant heareth." That's the pattern. God speaks, we learn to listen, we respond, we obey.
