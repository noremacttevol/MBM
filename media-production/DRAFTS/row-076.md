1. NARRATOR: Parents were bringing their little kids to Jesus, just so he could put his hands on them and bless them.
2. NARRATOR: The disciples tried to wave them off — the Teacher's busy, this is grown-up work, not the place for children.

6. NARRATOR: Then he gathered them up in his arms, put his hands on them, and blessed them — one at a time, unhurried, like there was nowhere else he needed to be.
