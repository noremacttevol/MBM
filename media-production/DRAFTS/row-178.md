# DRAFT row-178 — Let us make man in our image, after our likeness — Genesis 1:26

> Row 178 · Ref: Genesis 1:26 · Jesus NOT shown (creation, OT). CONTENT-CARE (reverent, no gore).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: Genesis 1:26 — no bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live/lives/lived. Clean. (Narrator avoids live/lives/lived forms; KJV below is verbatim.)

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; JESUS lines = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: At the start of all things, before any person drew breath, a counsel happened in the Godhead — let us make man in our image.
2. NARRATOR: Not in the shape of any creature, but bearing something of God himself: the capacity to know him, to choose him, to reflect him.
3. NARRATOR: The plan included dominion — over fish, birds, cattle, and all the earth. Stewards, not owners. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: Then the act: God formed man from the dust and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and man became a living soul.
5. NARRATOR: Every person since carries that original dignity — made in the image, loved into being.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "You are made in his image. That is worth more than you know."

## JESUS LINES: none — creation narrative; Jesus not depicted in frame (the "us/our" hints the Trinity, but he is not shown). (Per spec: for passages where Jesus doesn't appear, say so.)

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: reverent, creation, no figure of Christ)
1. Exterior, formless void at first light, waters and darkness, awe.
2. The Spirit hovering over the waters — soft luminous presence, no figure face.
3. God's hand (symbolic, light) tracing the shape of a man in the dust — reverence.
4. The first man, Adam, rising, eyes opening to the world — dawn, wonder. *(sacred-silence beat — still holds on the first breath)*
5. Adam beholding the creatures, gentle, stewardship mood.
6. Eve beside him, the two together, image-bearers, garden light.
7. A wide shot of the finished creation, ordered and good, golden hour.
8. Closing still: a single human face, serene, reflecting light — image of God.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — no violence, no figure of Christ (OT creation). Reverent, dignity-of-man focus. ~45s. Narrator Andrew. Jesus NOT shown; Genesis 1 is pre-incarnate creation.
