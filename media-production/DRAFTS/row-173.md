# DRAFT row-173 — The dead shall hear the voice of the Son of God — John 5:25

> Row 173 · Ref: John 5:25 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN (adult, master-locked).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: "live" appears in the JESUS line ("they that hear shall live") — spoken as /liv/, verb. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow. Clean otherwise.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Jesus said a time was coming — and had already begun — when something impossible would happen.
2. NARRATOR: The dead would hear a voice. Not a rumor of a voice. His voice. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. JESUS (KJV, John 5:25): "Verily, verily, I say unto you, The hour is coming, and now is, when the dead shall hear the voice of the Son of God: and they that hear shall live."
4. NARRATOR: The One who made life is the One who calls it back. To hear him is to live.
5. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He speaks, and death loses its grip. Lean in and listen — and live."

## STORYBOARD (7 beats — CONTENT-CARE: reverent, no corpses/gore; grave implied by light only)
1. Exterior, open teaching crowd, daytime: Jesus standing, speaking — master-composed face, warm brown eyes, Middle Eastern, only-he-cream, no halo.
2. Close on Jesus's face as he says "Verily, verily" — authority and tenderness. Master-locked.
3. The crowd, faces turning, some amazed — daylight.
4. A still suggesting the unseen: a quiet grave, light touching it as if summoned. *(sacred-silence beat — the voice implied)*
5. Close on Jesus's hand, open, calling — master face partly shown, reverent.
6. Two-shot: Jesus and a listener whose face changes at the words — hope dawning. Master-locked.
7. Closing still: light breaking, the promise of life — hopeful, never grim.
