# DRAFT row-146 — The vine and the branches — John 15:5

> Row 146 · Ref: John 15:5 (15:1–8) · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (gentle, no violence, no shame).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live/lives/lived. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Jesus used a picture His friends would know — a vine, and the branches that grow from it.
2. JESUS (KJV, John 15:5): "I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing."
3. NARRATOR: A branch cut off from the vine doesn't dry up because it's weak. It dries up because it's disconnected. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: Stay joined to Him, and the life flows. Try to bear fruit on your own, and there's nothing there.
5. NARRATOR: He wasn't asking for effort. He was offering connection.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "Stay connected to the Vine. Let His life flow through you."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: gentle, faces and growth carry it)
1. Exterior, daytime, vineyard: a strong vine with healthy branches, grapes forming. Jesus FACE-SHOWN nearby, master-locked, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
2. Close on a branch heavy with fruit, joined to the trunk — living.
3. Close on Jesus's face as He says "I am the vine, ye are the branches" — teaching, warm. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on His face)*
4. Illustrative: a severed branch, withered and grey, apart from the vine.
5. Illustrative: the same kind of branch, this time joined, green and fruiting — contrast, not gore.
6. Close on Jesus's face as He speaks of abiding — gentle, inviting.
7. Wide: the whole vineyard under soft light, branches full and green.
8. Closing still: hands resting on a living branch, light through leaves — invitation.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — gentle, no violence, no gore. The withered branch is illustrative contrast only, never graphic. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked. ~50-60s. Jesus line exact KJV John 15:5.
