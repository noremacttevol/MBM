## NARRATION
1. NARRATOR: Before Jesus went back to heaven, he told his followers to wait in Jerusalem — he was going to send them help. They did not fully understand what he meant. So they waited, and they prayed. Then one morning it came.
2. SCRIPTURE (KJV Acts 2:2–3): And suddenly there came a sound from heaven as of a rushing mighty wind, and it filled all the house where they were sitting. And there appeared unto them cloven tongues like as of fire, and it sat upon each of them.
3. NARRATOR: The Holy Ghost filled every one of them. And ordinary people from Galilee began to speak in languages they had never learned. Jerusalem was crowded that day with visitors from every nation — and each one heard the wonders of God in his own language.
4. NARRATOR: A crowd gathered, amazed and confused. Some mocked and said they were drunk. So Peter — the same man who had denied Jesus weeks before — stood up in front of everyone, unafraid now, and told them the truth.
5. SCRIPTURE (KJV Acts 2:22–24, abridged): Jesus of Nazareth, a man approved of God among you by miracles and wonders and signs... ye have taken, and by wicked hands have crucified and slain: whom God hath raised up, having loosed the pains of death.
6. NARRATOR: This was his whole message. Not himself. Not the wind or the fire. Just Jesus — the one they had crucified, whom God had raised from the dead. And Peter said they had all seen him alive.
7. SCRIPTURE (KJV Acts 2:36): Therefore let all the house of Israel know assuredly, that God hath made that same Jesus, whom ye have crucified, both Lord and Christ.
8. NARRATOR: The words went straight to their hearts. They had helped kill him — and he had risen anyway. Cut to the heart, they asked the only question that mattered.
9. SCRIPTURE (KJV Acts 2:37b): Men and brethren, what shall we do?
10. SCRIPTURE (KJV Acts 2:38): Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.
11. NARRATOR: That day about three thousand people were baptized. The Church of Jesus Christ began — not with an army or a building, but with the Spirit poured out and a crowd who finally heard who Jesus really was.
12. CLOSING CARD (invitation): "He kept his promise and sent the Comforter. The same invitation still stands — repent, be baptized, and receive the gift of the Holy Ghost."
