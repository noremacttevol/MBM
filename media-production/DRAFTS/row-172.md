> Row 172 · Ref: 1 Peter 4:6 · Jesus NOT appearing (New Testament teaching; Peter's letter).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: "live" appears in the KJV ref ("but live according to God in the spirit") — spoken as /liv/, verb, not /laiv/. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow. Clean otherwise.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Some who heard the good news had already died before they could finish their lives in the body.
2. NARRATOR: The gospel was preached to them too — not in vain, not too late. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: So they might be judged by men's measure in the flesh, yet live by God's measure in the spirit.
4. NARRATOR: Death did not close the door. The message crossed over.
5. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The gospel reaches beyond the grave. No one is outside the reach of his mercy."

2. Close on the reader's face, peace in speaking the hope — evening.
