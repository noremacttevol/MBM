## Narration Script (plain text, Aloud ready)

Hey Cameron. Row 102. Jacob left Beersheba and went toward Haran. He came upon a certain place and stayed there because the sun had set. He took one of the stones of the place and put it under his head. Jacob lay down in that place. He dreamed of a ladder set up on the earth and the top of it reached to heaven. Angels of God were ascending and descending on it. The Lord stood above it and said, "I am the Lord God of Abraham thy father, and the God of Isaac. The land upon which thou liest I will give to thee and to thy seed. And thy seed shall be as the dust of the earth. I will be with thee and keep thee in all whithersoever thou goest." Jacob awoke and said, "Surely the Lord is in this place; and I knew it not." He was afraid and said, "How dreadful is this place! This is none other but the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven." He rose up early in the morning and took the stone which he had put for his pillow and set it up for a pillar and poured oil upon the top of it and called the name of that place Bethel.

This story shows us that heaven is not far away or closed off. It's open and trafficked — angels going up and down, God right there at the top. Even while Jacob was running away from his problems, God met him where he was, just a young man on the ground with a stone for a pillow. God promised to be with him, to keep him, to bring him back. Jacob woke up and realized this was holy ground, the house of God, the gate of heaven.
