# DRAFT row-094 — "Father, forgive them" — Luke 23:33–34

> Row 94 · Ref: Luke 23:33–34 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. HANDLE WITH DEEP CARE (crucifixion).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live in the KJV line. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: They brought him to a place called Calvary, and there they crucified him — between two criminals, at the hands of soldiers just following orders.
2. NARRATOR: He had every reason to curse them. Every right to call down judgment. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: Instead, the first words out of his mouth on the cross were a prayer — for the very people driving the nails.
4. JESUS (KJV, Luke 23:34): "Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do."
5. NARRATOR: Not "forgive them later, if they're sorry." Forgive them now — while it's still happening.
6. NARRATOR: That is how far his mercy reaches. If it covered the ones killing him, there is no one, and nothing you've done, it can't cover.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "His first words on the cross were mercy for the ones who put him there. That mercy has your name in it too."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: NO gore, NO wounds in focus, reverent distance)
1. Exterior, a darkening sky over a distant hill with three crosses in silhouette against heavy clouds — reverent long distance, no detail of suffering.
2. Closer but composed: Jesus on the cross from a respectful low angle, face SHOWN, eyes lifted to heaven — the focus is his face and the sky, never wounds. Master-locked.
3. Below, soldiers casting lots over a garment, not looking up — ordinary men unaware.
4. Close on Jesus's face, warm brown eyes toward the sky, lips parted in prayer — peace and pain held together, NO blood emphasized. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on his face)*
5. The faces of a few below — a centurion beginning to wonder, a mourner — caught by the words.
6. Wide: the hill under a bruised sky, the three crosses small, a shaft of light breaking through toward the center cross.
7. Closing still: the broken clouds with light spilling through onto the hill — mercy reaching down.
