# DRAFT row-125 — "I never knew you" — Matthew 7:21–23

> Row 125 · Ref: Matthew 7:21–23 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN (adult, master ref). CONTENT-CARE (judgment — mercy spoken aloud, no shame framing, no fear-mongering).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: None of bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Jesus warned that saying the right words is not the same as knowing him. The door is not opened by a name we repeat.
2. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 7:21): "Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven."
3. NARRATOR: He described a day when many will point to what they did — prophecies, miracles, works done in his name. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 7:22): "Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in thy name? and in thy name have cast out devils? and in thy name done many wonderful works?"
5. NARRATOR: And then the honest part — knowing him matters more than sounding like we do.
6. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 7:23): "And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity."
7. NARRATOR: This is not a threat to scare you. It is an invitation to be known — to walk with him, not just speak his name.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He wants to know you, not just be named by you. Come close, and let him know you."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: serious but merciful, faces not fire)
1. Exterior, mountainside, daytime: Jesus teaching, seated among listeners — FACE-SHOWN, master-locked, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
2. Close on Jesus's face as he speaks the warning — tender, not angry; weight in the words.
3. A listener's face, thinking hard — the call to real knowing. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the listening face)*
4. Imagined scene: people recounting deeds done in his name; faces earnest, searching.
5. Jesus's face again, speaking the solemn line — gravity held with mercy.
6. A contrasting beat: a hand in quiet friendship with another — knowing shown by love, not words.
7. Closing still: Jesus's face, open and inviting — the door is knowing, and it is offered.
