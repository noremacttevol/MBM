## Narration Script (plain text, Aloud ready)

Hey Cameron. Row 002. Some days later, Jesus entered Capernaum again. News spread and many gathered, so they had no room even about the door. He preached the word unto them. They came to him bringing a paralytic, carried of four men. They could not come nigh him for the crowd. They uncovered the roof where he was and when they had broken it up, let down the bed whereon the sick of the palsy lay. When Jesus saw their faith, he said unto the sick of the palsy "Son, thy sins be forgiven thee." Some scribes sat there and reasoned in themselves "Why doth this man thus speak blasphemies? Who can forgive sins but God only?" Jesus immediately perceived in his spirit that they so reasoned and said "Why reason ye these things in your hearts? Whether is it easier to say to the sick of the palsy Thy sins be forgiven thee, or to say Arise and take up thy bed and walk? But that ye may know that the Son of man hath power on earth to forgive sins, I say unto thee Arise take up thy bed and go thy way to thy house." He arose, took up the bed and went forth before them all. They were all amazed and glorified God and said We never saw on this fashion.

This story is the first miracle, the whole gospel in one moment. Jesus doesn't just make people comfortable in their misery. He forgives sin first. Then heals. Because the deepest sickness is not the body. It's the spirit. The four friends didn't just bring their friend. They broke through the roof. They moved mountains. They carried hope itself to Jesus.
