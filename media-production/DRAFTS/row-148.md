# DRAFT row-148 — Ruth and the redeemer — Ruth 1:16–17; 4:13–17

> Row 148 · Ref: Ruth 1:16–17; 4:13–17 · Jesus does NOT appear (Old Testament). CONTENT-CARE: no violence, reverent, one teardrop max.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: tears(/teerz/) — Naomi weeps at the loss of her family; spoken as "teerz," captions keep KJV. live(/liv/) — "thy people shall be my people"; no other homographs.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only — none here, OT)
1. NARRATOR: A widow named Naomi lost everything in a foreign land — her husband and both sons buried there.
2. NARRATOR: She told her two daughters-in-law to go home. One kissed her and left. But Ruth clung to her.
3. NARRATOR: "Where you go, I'll go," Ruth said. "Your people will be my people, and your God my God." *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: Back in Bethlehem, Ruth gleaned grain in the fields to keep Naomi fed — and the field belonged to a man named Boaz.
5. NARRATOR: Boaz noticed her. He protected her, fed her, and spoke kindly. He was a near kinsman — a redeemer by the law.
6. NARRATOR: At the threshing floor, Ruth asked him to cover her with his cloak, the sign of a kinsman's duty. He promised to redeem her.
7. NARRATOR: Before the town gate, Boaz bought the right to marry Ruth. Naomi's emptiness was filled; a son was born.
8. NARRATOR: That boy became the grandfather of King David — and part of the line that leads to the greater Redeemer still to come.
9. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "Ruth gave up everything to follow the God she'd come to love. He never let her go. Neither will He let you go."

## STORYBOARD (8–11 beats — CONTENT-CARE: reverent, no violence; Jesus does NOT appear)
1. Exterior, dawn: Naomi and her two daughters-in-law on a dusty road leaving Moab; long shot, quiet grief.
2. Close on Naomi's face, wet-eyed, releasing one daughter-in-law who turns away. One painted teardrop max.
3. Ruth's face turned toward Naomi, resolute, hand clasping hers — loyalty.
4. Bethlehem fields at harvest, golden light: Ruth bending to glean behind the reapers.
5. Boaz observing from the field edge, kind expression, gesturing protection to his workers.
6. Threshing floor at night, soft lamplight: Ruth at Boaz's feet, cloak-edge drawn over her — dignity, not shame.
7. Town gate at morning: Boaz before the elders, a sandal exchanged, the agreement sealed.
8. Naomi cradling the newborn, face lifted in relief; warm domestic light.
9. Closing still: a line of distant figures walking toward a rising sun — the lineage stretching forward. Hopeful.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE deep — no violence, no shame framing (Ruth's poverty shown with dignity). Jesus does NOT appear; this is the OT lineage story pointing to the Redeemer. Mercy/loyalty spoken aloud. ~55–65s. Narrator Andrew; no Jesus voice.
