# DRAFT row-145 — "I am the way, the truth, and the life" — John 14:6

> Row 145 · Ref: John 14:6 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (gentle, no violence, no shame).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live/lives/lived. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: In the upper room, the night before the cross, Jesus told His disciples He was going to prepare a place for them.
2. NARRATOR: Thomas spoke up — Lord, we don't know where You're going, so how can we know the way? *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. JESUS (KJV, John 14:6): "I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me."
4. NARRATOR: Not one way among many. The way itself. The truth itself. The life itself — standing right there in the room with them.
5. NARRATOR: The door to the Father isn't a map to find. It's a person to follow.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "Stop looking for the road. Walk with Him."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: gentle, faces and candlelight carry it)
1. Interior, evening, upper room: Jesus and the disciples at a low table, lamplight. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
2. Close on Thomas's face, puzzled, asking his question.
3. Close on Jesus's face as He answers — calm, certain, kind. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on His face)*
4. Two-shot: Jesus and Thomas, the question met with a promise.
5. Illustrative: a single road fading into mist, then a figure walking it — the Way made personal.
6. Close on Jesus's face as He says "no man cometh... but by me" — inclusive invitation, no condemnation.
7. Wide: the upper room at peace, lamplight warm on the gathered men.
8. Closing still: an open doorway glowing with light, Jesus standing in it — invitation.
