# Row 103: Peter's Confession — Matt 16:13-20

**Story:** Jesus asks His disciples who people say He is, then asks who they say He is. Simon Peter confesses Jesus as the Christ, the Son of the living God. Jesus declares this truth came by divine revelation, not human teaching.

**Key Point:** True testimony comes by revelation from God, not human teaching.

---

## NARRATION SCRIPT

**Opening**
In the regions of Caesarea Philippi, Jesus leads His disciples away from the crowds. Here, in this quiet place, He poses a question that will change everything.

**Scene 1-2**
Jesus turns to His disciples and asks, "Whom do men say that I am?" The question hangs in the air. Who is this Teacher from Nazareth? What do the crowds say?

**Scene 3-5**
The disciples exchange glances and respond with what they've heard: "Some say John the Baptist, some say Elijah, and others say one of the prophets." Respectful answers... but incomplete.

**Scene 6-7**
Then Jesus presses deeper, piercing through to the heart of the matter: "But whom say ye that I am?" Now the question is personal. Not what the world thinks. Not what the crowd says. But what do *you* believe?

**Scene 8**
Simon Peter steps forward, and his words are clear, bold, and certain: "Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God." This is not guesswork. This is not human reasoning.

**Scene 9**
Jesus smiles warmly and confirms Peter's confession with a staggering truth: "Flesh and blood hath not revealed this unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven." How could Peter know? Divine revelation. Not human teaching.

**Scene 10**
Continuing with weighty words, Jesus declares: "Thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church." The confession becomes the cornerstone. Faith revealed by God becomes the foundation of something eternal.

**Closing**
What is your confession? Do you know who Jesus is by revelation from the Father? This is the testimony that changes everything.
