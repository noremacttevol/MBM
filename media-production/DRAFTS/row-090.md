# DRAFT row-090 — Washing the disciples' feet — John 13:1–17

> Row 90 · Ref: John 13:1–17 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (intimate service, no violence).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: Clean. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: It was just before the Passover feast. Jesus knew his time had come, and he loved his own to the very end.
2. NARRATOR: So he did something no teacher should do. He tied a towel around himself, poured water into a basin, and began to wash his friends' feet. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: When he came to Peter, Peter objected — Lord, you're not going to wash my feet. But Jesus told him: if I don't, you have no part with me.
4. NARRATOR: Then Jesus finished the task, wiped their feet, and sat back down as their servant.
5. JESUS (KJV, John 13:14): "If I then, your Lord and Master, have washed your feet; ye also ought to wash one another's feet."
6. NARRATOR: He had given them more than clean feet. He had given them an example — that the greatest among them would bend low for the least.
7. JESUS (KJV, John 13:15): "For I have given you an example, that ye should do as I have done to you."
8. NARRATOR: The King of heaven on his knees. That's the kind of leader he calls us to be.
9. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He knelt to serve the ones he loved. Come, let him show you how to love like that."

## STORYBOARD (9 beats — CONTENT-CARE: humble service, faces not spectacle)
1. Exterior, evening: a quiet courtyard house, warm lamplight from an upper room over a still street; focus on the light.
2. Interior, evening lamplight: the disciples gathered, sandals at the door, Jesus rising and tying a towel. Jesus FACE-SHOWN — master-locked, Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
3. Close on the basin and Jesus's hands pouring water — service, not spectacle.
4. Jesus kneeling at a disciple's feet, gentle and unhurried; the disciple's face softened in surprise. Master face visible, calm. *(sacred-silence beat — still holds on the kneeling figure)*
5. Peter's face, conflicted, then yielding as Jesus speaks; warmth, no shame.
6. Jesus wiping a foot, tender; disciples watching, something shifting in them.
7. Jesus seated again, towel still on, explaining with love in his eyes. Master-locked close, no halo.
8. The group, quieter now, looking at one another differently.
9. Closing still: the empty basin and towel in the lamplight — an open invitation to serve. Reverent, warm.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE deep — intimate, humble service, no violence/gore. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked adult face. Mercy and love spoken (J-story law). ~55-65s. Narrator Andrew; Jesus Christopher exact KJV.
