# DRAFT row-090 — Washing the disciples' feet — John 13:1–17

> Row 90 · Ref: John 13:1–17 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: At that last supper, knowing he was about to leave the world, Jesus did something no master would ever do for his servants.
2. NARRATOR: He got up from the table, wrapped a towel around his waist, poured water into a basin, and began to wash his disciples' feet — one by one. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: When he came to Peter, Peter pulled back — Lord, you'll never wash MY feet. Jesus answered him gently but firmly.
4. JESUS (KJV, John 13:8): "If I wash thee not, thou hast no part with me."
5. NARRATOR: When he'd finished, he sat back down and asked if they understood what he'd just done.
6. JESUS (KJV, John 13:14): "If I then, your Lord and Master, have washed your feet; ye also ought to wash one another's feet."
7. NARRATOR: The greatest one in the room knelt at the dirtiest job in the house. That's the kind of king he is.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He kneels to serve the ones he loves. Will you let him near enough to do it for you?"

## STORYBOARD (8–14 beats)
1. Interior night, warm lamplight: the supper table, Jesus rising and setting aside his outer robe, taking up a towel. Face SHOWN, cream robe/tunic.
2. Jesus kneeling on the floor, pouring water from a jug into a clay basin. Humble, deliberate.
3. Jesus washing a disciple's feet in the basin, his hands careful, the disciple watching in stunned silence. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the washing)*
4. Peter half-recoiling, hand raised in protest, face conflicted; Jesus looking up at him with calm warmth. Master-locked.
5. Close on Jesus's face from below, gentle and certain, speaking to Peter.
6. Jesus drying a disciple's feet with the towel around his waist.
7. Wide: Jesus back at the table addressing them all, the basin and towel still on the floor — the lesson landing.
8. Closing still: the basin, the water, and the folded towel in warm lamplight — greatness on its knees.

## NOTES: Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master ref on 1,4,5,7. Only-he-cream. Tender, no conflict beyond Peter's protest. ~60-70s. Narrator Andrew; Jesus Christopher exact KJV.
