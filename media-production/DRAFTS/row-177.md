# DRAFT row-177 — Make me a sanctuary; that I may dwell among them — Exodus 25:8

> Row 177 · Ref: Exodus 25:8 · Jesus NOT shown (OT, Moses receives instruction). CONTENT-CARE (reverent, building/tent, no gore).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: Exodus 25 — no bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live/lives/lived. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; JESUS lines = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: While Israel camped in the wilderness, the LORD gave Moses a strange instruction — have the people build me a sanctuary.
2. NARRATOR: Not for his sake. For theirs. So that he could dwell among them in the middle of their ordinary days.
3. NARRATOR: He told them exactly how — the ark, the table, the lampstand — every detail meant to say: I am near. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: The pattern was carried by a people on the move, yet the promise was fixed — God with his people.
5. NARRATOR: Centuries later that promise would take a face. But here it begins as a tent in the desert, God pitching his tent beside them.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He wanted to live among his people then. He wants to live among you now."
