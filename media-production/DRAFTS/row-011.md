## Narration Script (plain text, Aloud ready)

Hey Cameron. Row 011. Immediately after feeding the five thousand, Jesus constrained his disciples to enter into the ship and to go to the other side before he could send the multitude away. So he parted them and sent them away. After sending the multitude away, he came into the ship, his disciples with him. Then came he to them walking on the sea to the ship, intending to pass them by. When they saw him walking on the sea, they supposed it had been a spirit and cried out; for they all saw him, and were troubled. But straightway Jesus talked with them and said unto them "Be of good cheer, it is I; be not afraid." He got into the ship with them, and the wind ceased. Then were they in the ship exceeding astonished, for they considered not the miracle of the loaves, for their heart was hardened.

Jesus didn't walk on the shore to the boat. He didn't wait for a safer path. He walked on the water to his disciples who were in the middle of a storm. They saw him and thought he was a ghost. They were terrified. Jesus didn't scold them. He said "It is I. Be not afraid." That's the name of God. I AM. Jesus is I AM. In the storm, the boat, the dark, Jesus walked on the water to say "Be not afraid."
