## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)

1. NARRATOR: Jesus sat down across from the temple treasury and just watched people give.
2. NARRATOR: The rich came through and put in large amounts — you could hear the coins, and everyone noticed.
3. NARRATOR: Then a poor widow came. She put in two tiny copper coins — worth almost nothing. *(sacred-silence beat — narration cuts to silence on the two small coins)*
4. NARRATOR: Jesus called his disciples over, like he'd just seen the most important thing all day.
5. JESUS (KJV, Mark 12:43–44): "This poor widow hath cast more in, than all they which have cast into the treasury: for all they did cast in of their abundance; but she of her want did cast in all that she had, even all her living."
6. NARRATOR: Everyone else gave from what they had left over. She gave from what she needed. Heaven does the math differently than we do.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He noticed the gift no one else did. What might he already see in you that others overlook?"
