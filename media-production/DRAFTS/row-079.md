## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Jesus picked seventy of his followers and sent them out two by two, ahead of him, into every town he was about to visit.
2. NARRATOR: He didn't send them with much — no extra bag, no spare sandals, no backup plan. Just each other and a message.
3. JESUS (KJV, Luke 10:2): "The harvest truly is great, but the labourers are few; pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he would send forth labourers into his harvest."
4. NARRATOR: Wherever they were welcomed, they were to heal the sick and say the same simple thing. *(sacred-silence beat)*
5. JESUS (KJV, Luke 10:9): "The kingdom of God is come nigh unto you."
6. NARRATOR: They came back amazed — even the hard things had listened to them. And Jesus told them what to actually celebrate.
7. JESUS (KJV, Luke 10:20): "Rejoice not, that the spirits are subject unto you; but rather rejoice, because your names are written in heaven."
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "Your name, written in heaven — that's the joy he wanted them to keep. It's meant for you as well."

## STORYBOARD (8–16 beats)
1. Exterior dawn, warm gold: Jesus standing before a gathered group of followers on a hillside, sending them out, arm raised toward the horizon. Face SHOWN, cream robe.
2. Pairs of ordinary men walking off down different roads into the morning, small packs, staffs — two by two, growing distant. 
3. A pair arriving at a village gate, greeted warmly by families; simple travel clothes, dusty feet.
4. One of the pair laying a hand on a sick person resting outside a house, the household watching with hope. Gentle, no gore.
5. A pair speaking to a small courtyard of listeners — "the kingdom of God is come nigh" — faces lighting. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the listening faces)*
6. Evening: the seventy streaming back to Jesus on the hillside, animated, joyful, telling their stories.
7. Close on Jesus's face, glad but steadying them, warm brown eyes, speaking about names written in heaven. Master-locked.
8. Closing still: a wide night sky over the quiet hills, warm and vast — names written where they can't be erased.
