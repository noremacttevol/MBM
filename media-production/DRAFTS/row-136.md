# DRAFT row-136 — Healed in two touches — Mark 8:22–26

> Row 136 · Ref: Mark 8:22–26 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (healing, reverent, no gore).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: "led" (lead, past tense) = /led/, NOT /leed/ — "He led him out of the town." Caption keeps KJV "led"; spoken audio respell to /led/. Also "saw" (/saw/) in "he saw men as trees" — keep /saw/, not /so/. No bow/wound/wind/tears/sow/live.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Some healings happened in a single word. This one took two touches — and Jesus was unhurried about it.
2. NARRATOR: They brought a blind man to Him outside the village. Jesus took his hand and led him away from the crowd.
3. NARRATOR: He put His hands on the man's eyes, and asked — what do you see?
4. NARRATOR: The man looked up: I see people, but they look like trees, walking. Not yet clear. *(sacred-silence beat)*
5. NARRATOR: So Jesus put His hands on his eyes again. And this time, the man saw everything — every face, sharp and whole.
6. NARRATOR: The healing was complete, done at the pace of kindness, not speed.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He does not rush your healing. One touch more, and you will see clearly."

## STORYBOARD (7–9 beats — CONTENT-CARE: healing touch reverent, no graphic detail)
1. Exterior, edge of Bethsaida, daytime: Jesus taking the blind man by the hand, leading him apart. Jesus FACE-SHOWN (master ref, Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo). Blind man is an adult, NOT a child.
2. The blind man, eyes closed, trusting, being led by the hand.
3. Close on Jesus's hands on the man's eyes, gentle; no graphic spittle, only the tenderness of the touch. Reverent, no gore.
4. The man looking up, unsure, describing blurry figures — "men as trees walking." *(sacred-silence beat — still holds on the man's unsure face)*
5. Jesus's second touch, hands again on the eyes, patient and kind. Master-locked face.
6. The man's face breaking into wonder as sight clears — first clear sight of Jesus's face.
7. Closing still: the man looking out at the world, sharp and grateful, warm daylight.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — no blood/gore; healing shown reverently. Jesus speaks no recorded KJV speech in this passage (His words are actions), so JESUS lines are omitted; narrator carries it. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked. ~50-60s. Narrator Andrew; Jesus Christopher exact KJV where spoken.
