## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: It was the custom at the feast to release one prisoner to the crowd. Pilate offered them a choice between Jesus — called the King of the Jews — and a man named Barabbas, a known rebel.
2. NARRATOR: The crowd was stirred up to ask for Barabbas instead. Pilate asked again what he should do with Jesus, and they cried out: crucify him. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: So the governor released the guilty man. And he handed Jesus over — to be crucified.
4. NARRATOR: The innocent was bound, and the one who had done harm walked free. A trade no one deserved, but one that would open the door for every one of us.
5. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 27:11): "Thou sayest."
6. NARRATOR: When Pilate asked if he was the King of the Jews, Jesus answered only: Thou sayest. He did not fight for his own release. He stood silent so that Barabbas — and we — could go free.
7. NARRATOR: The swap at the governor's seat was the picture of the whole thing: our guilt on him, his freedom offered to us.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He took the place of the guilty so you could walk free. Come, receive what he bought for you."
