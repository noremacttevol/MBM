# DRAFT row-100 — The ascension — Acts 1:6–11

> Row 100 · Ref: Acts 1:6–11 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN (risen, ascending); angels as amber-gold figures.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: "power" clean. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: In his last moments with them, the disciples asked Jesus if he was going to restore the kingdom right then. He turned them toward something bigger.
2. JESUS (KJV, Acts 1:8): "Ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me... unto the uttermost part of the earth."
3. NARRATOR: He was handing them the mission — go tell everyone, everywhere. And then something happened they would never forget. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: While they watched, he was lifted up, and a cloud received him out of their sight. They stood there staring into the sky, stunned.
5. NARRATOR: Then two figures in white stood beside them with a promise:
6. NARRATOR (as the angels, plain voice): This same Jesus, who was taken up from you into heaven, will come back the same way you've seen him go.
7. NARRATOR: He didn't leave them orphans. He left them a mission, a promise, and the sure word that he's coming again.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He's coming back the same way he left. Until then, you're not alone — and you're not without purpose."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats)
1. Exterior day, luminous sky: Jesus standing with his disciples on the Mount of Olives, addressing them, arm lifted. Face SHOWN, cream robe, radiant but no head-ring.
2. Close on Jesus's face, warm and commissioning, brown eyes, speaking the mission. Master-locked.
3. The disciples' upturned faces, attentive, being sent.
4. Jesus beginning to rise, lifted upward, robe and light gathering — awe, not fear. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the ascending figure)*
5. A bright cloud receiving him, his form haloed in warm light as he disappears upward.
6. The disciples gazing up into the empty sky, hands shielding eyes, wonder on every face.
7. Two amber-gold/white angelic figures beside them (no head-rings, gentle glow) delivering the promise of his return.
8. Closing still: the wide bright sky over the mount, a single break of radiant cloud — he will come again.

## NOTES: Jesus FACE-SHOWN, risen and ascending (radiant, but NO head-ring/halo per face law). Angels amber-gold/white, gentle. Triumphant, hopeful close to the whole 1-100 shelf. ~65-75s. Narrator Andrew (voices angels plainly); Jesus Christopher exact KJV (Acts 1:8).
