## Narration Script (plain text, Aloud ready)

Hey Cameron. Row 006. Afterward there was a feast of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem. Now there is at Jerusalem by the sheep market a pool, which is called in the Hebrew tongue Bethesda, having five porches. In these lay a great multitude of impotent folk, of blind, halt, withered, waiting for the moving of the water. An angel went down at a certain season into the pool and stirred it. Whosoever then first after the stirring of the water stepped in was made whole of whatsoever disease he had. There was a certain man lying there who had been sick 38 years. When Jesus saw him lying, and understood that he had been now a long time in that case, he saith unto him "Wilt thou be made whole?" The infirm man answered "Sir, I have no man, when the water is troubled, to put me into the pool. While I am coming, another steppeth down before me." Jesus said unto him "Rise, take up thy bed, and walk." Immediately the man was made whole, took up his bed, and walked. And on that day was the Sabbath. The Jews therefore said unto him that was cured "It is the Sabbath day. It is not lawful for thee to carry thy bed." He answered them "He that made me whole, the same said unto me, Take up thy bed, and walk." They asked "Who is the man that spake unto thee, Take up thy bed, and walk?" Now the man who was healed wist not who it was: for Jesus had conveyed himself away, a multitude being in that place. Afterward Jesus findeth him in the temple and said unto him "Behold, thou art made whole: sin no more, lest a worse thing come unto thee." The man departed and told the Jews that it was Jesus which had made him whole. For this cause the Jews persecuted Jesus, and sought to kill him, because he had done these things on the Sabbath. Jesus answered them "My Father worketh hitherto, and I work." Therefore the Jews sought the more to kill him, because he not only had broken the Sabbath, but said also that God was his Father, making himself equal with God. Jesus said "The Son can do nothing of himself, but what he seeth the Father do. For the hour cometh, in the which all that are in the graves shall hear his voice and shall come forth."

They hated him for mercy. They made it a crime to heal someone. They said God doesn't work on the Sabbath. God heals every day. Mercy never rests.
