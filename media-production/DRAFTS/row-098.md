# DRAFT row-098 — Mary at the tomb: her name — John 20:11–18

> Row 98 · Ref: John 20:11–18 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN (risen). 

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: "wept"/"weeping" clean. "tears" NOT used but "weeping" is — clean. No bow/wound/wind/lead/sow/live. Ear-check "wept".

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Mary Magdalene stayed behind at the tomb, weeping. She thought someone had taken his body, and grief was all she had left.
2. NARRATOR: She turned and saw a man standing there. Thinking he was the gardener, she begged — please, if you've moved him, tell me where. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: He said one word. Her name.
4. JESUS (KJV, John 20:16): "Mary."
5. NARRATOR: And instantly she knew. Grief flipped to joy in a single heartbeat — he was alive, and he had come looking for HER, by name.
6. NARRATOR: He sent her to tell the others. The first person to see the risen Lord, and the first preacher of the resurrection, was a weeping woman he called by name.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He knows your name — and he speaks it even in your grief. Listen for it."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats)
1. Exterior early morning, soft gold: Mary alone outside the open tomb, bent with grief, one painted teardrop (painted-teardrop law).
2. Mary turning to a figure standing in the garden light, not yet recognizing him — she sees a gardener. Jesus present, face softly lit but unrecognized.
3. Close on Mary's face, tear-streaked, pleading, asking where the body was taken. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on her grief)*
4. Close on Jesus's face, warm and alive, brown eyes full of tenderness, about to speak her name. Face SHOWN, master-locked (risen — whole, no wounds emphasized).
5. Mary's face transformed — the instant of recognition, joy breaking through tears.
6. Mary reaching toward him in wonder; Jesus gently, warmly gesturing — go and tell.
7. Mary running through the bright garden toward the city to tell the others, joy in every line of her.
8. Closing still: the sunlit garden and the open tomb behind — grief turned to morning.

## NOTES: Jesus FACE-SHOWN, risen and whole (no wound focus), master-locked, warmth. Mary's grief handled with dignity, ONE teardrop. ~60-70s. Narrator Andrew; Jesus Christopher — the single word "Mary" exact KJV, delivered tenderly.
