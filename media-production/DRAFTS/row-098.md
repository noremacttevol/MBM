# DRAFT row-098 — Mary at the tomb: her name — John 20:11–16

> Row 98 · Ref: John 20:11–16 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (one painted teardrop max, never wet CGI).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No flagged word appears as a literal token (script uses "wept"/"weeping"/"crying" for Mary's grief). If "tears" is later added, spoken audio says /teerz/. Clean otherwise.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Mary stood outside the tomb and wept. Everyone she had followed was gone, and the stone was rolled away on top of her grief.
2. NARRATOR: She bent to look inside and saw two angels in white, sitting where the body had been — one at the head, one at the feet.
3. NARRATOR: They asked why she was crying. She said they had taken her Lord, and she didn't know where. Then she turned around.
4. NARRATOR: Someone was standing there. She thought it was the gardener. He asked the same question — why are you weeping? Who are you looking for? *(sacred-silence beat)*
5. NARRATOR: She started to ask Him to tell her where He'd put the body. And then He said one word.
6. JESUS (KJV, John 20:16): "Mary."
7. NARRATOR: She knew that voice before she knew His face. She turned and cried out, "Rabboni" — my Teacher. He had called her by name, and the mourner became a witness.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He knows your name too. Turn, and hear Him call it."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: one painted teardrop, no wet CGI)
1. Exterior, early morning: the tomb garden, soft light, Mary alone weeping. One painted teardrop on her cheek — never wet CGI.
2. Mary bending to the tomb opening; two angels within, seated, clothed in white, calm.
3. The angels' quiet question; Mary's downcast face, grief plain but not overwrought.
4. Mary turning away from the tomb — a figure standing behind her in the garden light.
5. The "gardener" unseen-face beat: we see Him from behind / at distance; Mary mistaking Him. *(sacred-silence beat — hold on the two figures, the question hanging)*
6. Close on Jesus's face as He says "Mary" — recognition, tenderness. FACE-SHOWN, master-locked, Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
7. Mary's face breaking into joy, turning fully toward Him, one teardrop drying.
8. Closing still: the garden at sunrise, Mary and the risen Lord, hope restored.
