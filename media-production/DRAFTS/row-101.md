## Narration Script (plain text, Aloud ready)

Hey Cameron. Row 101. A certain ruler comes to Jesus and asks, Good teacher, what good thing shall I do that I may have eternal life? Jesus says why do you call me good? No one is good except one, that is God. But if you want to enter into life, keep the commandments. The man says I have kept all these since my youth. What do I lack yet? Jesus answers, If you want to be perfect, go sell what you have, give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come and follow me. When the man heard that, he went away sorrowful, for he had great possessions.

Jesus then tells his disciples that it is very hard for one who has wealth to enter the kingdom of heaven. It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter the kingdom. The disciples are greatly astonished and say, Who then can be saved? Jesus looks at them and says, With people this is impossible, but with God all things are possible.

This story is about honest seeking meeting an honest cost. The young ruler wanted heaven. He kept the rules. But Jesus asked him for the one thing he would not give: everything. He chose his money over Jesus, and that's why he walked away sad.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Ruler Approaches
**Visuals:** A wealthy young man in fine clothing approaches Jesus. He has rings on his fingers, expensive robes. Jesus sits among a crowd of ordinary people in simple dress.

**Caption:** "Teacher, what good thing shall I do, that I may have eternal life?"

### Scene 2 — Jesus Responds
**Visuals:** Jesus looks directly at the young man with kind but serious expression.

**Caption:** "Why do you call me good? No one is good but One, that is, God."

### Scene 3 — The Commandments
**Visuals:** Jesus standing, speaking clearly: "If you want to enter life, keep the commandments."

**Caption:** "Do not commit adultery; Do not murder; Do not steal; Do not bear false witness; Honor your father and mother; You shall love your neighbor as yourself."

### Scene 4 — The Man's Confidence
**Visuals:** The young man stands straight, looking pleased and confident.

**Caption:** "I have kept all these since my youth. What do I still lack?"

### Scene 5 — The Hard Choice
**Visuals:** Jesus' expression softens. He gives the invitation.

**Caption:** "If you want to be perfect, go, sell what you have, and give to the poor; and you will have treasure in heaven. And come, follow me."

### Scene 6 — The Choice Made
**Visuals:** The young man's face falls. He looks down at his fine clothes, then at Jesus. Turns and walks away, shoulders slumped.

**Caption:** "When the young man heard this, he went away sorrowful, for he had great possessions."

### Scene 7 — Jesus and the Disciples
**Visuals:** The disciples look surprised. Jesus teaches about wealth and heaven.

**Caption:** "It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle, than for a rich man to enter the kingdom of God."

### Scene 8 — The Disciples' Awe
**Visuals:** The disciples look at each other in amazement.

**Caption:** "Who then can be saved?"

### Scene 9 — Jesus' Answer
**Visuals:** Jesus looks at each disciple with a reassuring smile.

**Caption:** "With man this is impossible, but with God all things are possible."
