# DRAFT row-097 — The empty tomb — Matthew 28:1–7, 9–10

> Row 97 · Refs: Matthew 28:1–7, 9–10 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN (resurrection appearance, final beats). CONTENT-CARE (reverent, joyful).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live present. Clean.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Before the sun was fully up, two women walked to the place where He had been laid. They came carrying grief, not hope.
2. NARRATOR: The stone that had sealed the grave was already rolled away — not by them, and not by any man. An angel sat on it, bright as lightning.
3. NARRATOR: The guards shook like dead men and the angel spoke to the women: don't be afraid. You're looking for Jesus who was crucified. He isn't here. He's risen, just like He said.
4. NARRATOR: Come and see the place where He lay — the linen cloths, empty and undisturbed. The tomb had nothing left to hold. *(sacred-silence beat)*
5. NARRATOR: Then, on the road as they ran to tell the others, He met them. Not a ghost. Not a memory. Alive.
6. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 28:10): "Be not afraid: go tell my brethren that they go into Galilee, and there shall they see me."
7. NARRATOR: They fell at His feet and worshiped. Death had let go. The grave was empty, and so was their fear.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The tomb is empty. Death lost its hold. He is risen — and He is looking for you too."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: reverent, no gore)
1. Exterior, first light: the garden tomb, a carved stone doorway, quiet and still.
2. The two women approaching at dawn, faces heavy with sorrow, carrying spices.
3. The great stone rolled aside; an angel seated on it, clothed in white, calm and radiant.
4. The guards fallen back, overwhelmed — composed, no violence shown.
5. Interior of the tomb: empty linen wrappings laid neatly, the slab bare. Focus on emptiness, not death. *(sacred-silence beat — hold on the empty cloth)*
6. The women turning from the tomb, wonder beginning to replace grief.
7. The road at morning: Jesus appearing to them, alive and warm. FACE-SHOWN, master-locked, Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
8. Close on Jesus's face as He speaks "Be not afraid" — gentle, joyful. Master-locked.
9. Closing still: the empty tomb at full sunrise, light pouring in — risen.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — empty tomb, no suffering detail, joy and reverence. Jesus appears only in the final resurrection beats, FACE-SHOWN master-locked. ~50-60s. Narrator Andrew; Jesus Christopher exact KJV.
