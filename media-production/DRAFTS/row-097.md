# DRAFT row-097 — The empty tomb — Luke 24:1–8

> Row 97 · Ref: Luke 24:1–8 · Jesus NOT shown (he is risen/absent); angels as gentle amber-gold figures.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: "risen" clean. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; the angels' line may be spoken plainly by narrator, NOT as KJV-Jesus voice)
1. NARRATOR: Very early on the first day of the week, while it was still dark, the women who loved him came to the tomb carrying spices to anoint his body.
2. NARRATOR: But when they arrived, the huge stone that had sealed the tomb was rolled away. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: They stepped inside — and the body was gone. They stood there, confused and afraid.
4. NARRATOR: Then two figures in dazzling clothing stood beside them, and asked a question that has echoed for two thousand years:
5. NARRATOR (as the angels, plain voice): Why do you look for the living among the dead? He is not here — he is risen.
6. NARRATOR: Remember, they said, what he told you back in Galilee — that he would be crucified, and on the third day rise again. And they remembered.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The tomb couldn't hold him. Whatever feels dead and sealed shut in your life — he's the God of the rolled-away stone."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats)
1. Exterior pre-dawn, deep blue easing to first light: women in shawls walking a garden path toward a rock tomb, small clay jars of spices in hand.
2. The tomb entrance: the enormous round sealing-stone rolled fully aside, a dark opening beyond. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the open tomb mouth)*
3. Interior tomb, dim: an empty stone ledge with folded grave-cloths lying where a body had been — no body. Soft light from the doorway.
4. The women's faces in the doorway — fear, awe, confusion — lit by growing dawn.
5. Two gentle amber-gold angelic figures standing near the ledge (human form, warm glow, NO blinding white, no head-rings) speaking to the women.
6. Close on a woman's face shifting from fear to dawning wonder as she remembers his words.
7. Exterior: the women hurrying back out into full sunrise, the garden blazing with new light.
8. Closing still: the empty tomb with the stone rolled away and the sun rising fully behind it — death undone.

## NOTES: Jesus NOT depicted (he is risen/gone — the empty tomb IS the point). Angels = amber-gold per build-91 precedent, no head-rings, no blinding wings. Joy/awe tone. ~60-70s. Narrator Andrew (voices the angels plainly — do NOT use the Jesus KJV voice for angel lines).
