## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Paul wrote to the believers in Rome about who they really were — not strangers, not outsiders.
2. NARRATOR: The Spirit himself, he said, agrees with our own spirit: we are children of God.
3. NARRATOR (plain, Rom 8:17): and if we are children, then we are heirs — heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ himself.
4. NARRATOR: The terms were honest: if we share in his suffering, we will also share in his glory. *(sacred-silence beat)*
5. NARRATOR: Not earned by effort. Received by belonging. Co-heirs with the Son.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "You're not a visitor at the table — you're family. Heir with Christ. Come home."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — Jesus NOT in frame)
1. Paul at a table, letter unfolding, a quiet Roman morning; no Christ figure — this is written teaching.
2. A hand sealing the scroll; the words "children of God" lifted as soft text-light.
3. An open doorway of warm light suggesting inheritance — symbolic, no face. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. Two figures side by side (believer + Christ suggested by light, not a master face) walking toward that light — co-heirs.
5. A gentle family-table image: many seats, room for all.
6. Close on the scroll's closing line, peace.
7. Closing still: dawn over the city — belonging, not exclusion.
