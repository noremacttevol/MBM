# DRAFT row-188 — Be ye therefore perfect — Matthew 5:48

> Row 188 · Ref: Matthew 5:44–48 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (teaching, gentle).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Jesus sat on the hillside and taught the crowds a kind of love they had never heard before — not just for friends, but for enemies too.
2. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 5:44): "But I say unto you, Love your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you, and pray for them which despitefully use you, and persecute you;"
3. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 5:45): "That ye may be the children of your Father which is in heaven: for he maketh his sun to rise on the evil and on the good, and sendeth rain on the just and on the unjust."
4. NARRATOR: The point was simple and hard at once — God's kindness falls on everyone, the grateful and the cruel alike. *(sacred-silence beat)*
5. NARRATOR: Then he set the bar that no one could reach alone, and meant for us to run toward it.
6. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 5:48): "Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect."
7. NARRATOR: Not perfect by comparison with each other. Perfect by the measure of a Father whose love has no edge, no favor, no limit.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He asks for a love without limits — and he gives the grace to grow into it. Come and learn it from him."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: gentle, faces not conflict)
1. Exterior, morning: Jesus seated on a grassy hillside, crowds gathered below, soft light. Jesus FACE-SHOWN — master ref, Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
2. Close on Jesus's face as he speaks of loving enemies — calm, unhurried, no anger. Master-locked.
3. A wide shot of the mixed crowd: some listening hard, some unsure — ordinary people.
4. A small beat: sun breaking over the hill onto both near and far listeners — the "sun rises on evil and good" idea shown without words. *(sacred-silence beat — still holds on the light)*
5. Jesus's hand open, gesturing toward the sky — invitation, not command. Master-locked.
6. Close on a listener's face softening — the hard teaching landing as hope.
7. Closing still: Jesus on the hillside at golden hour, face turned to the viewer, warm. Reverent, inviting.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — no conflict/violence, no shame framing. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked. No wounds. ~55-65s. Narrator Andrew; Jesus Christopher exact KJV.
