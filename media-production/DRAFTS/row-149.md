# DRAFT row-149 — Hannah is heard — 1 Samuel 1:9–20

> Row 149 · Ref: 1 Samuel 1:10–11, 1:20 · Jesus does NOT appear (Old Testament). CONTENT-CARE: no shame framing for barrenness, one teardrop max, mercy spoken.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: tears(/teerz/) — Hannah wept in her prayer; spoken "teerz," captions KJV. live(/liv/) — "all the days of his life"; no other homographs.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only — none here, OT)
1. NARRATOR: Year after year, Hannah went to the house of the LORD and came away with an empty lap. The other wife mocked her for it.
2. NARRATOR: One year at Shiloh, Hannah slipped to the door of the tabernacle and prayed with a voice no one could hear — only her lips moved.
3. NARRATOR: "Lord of hosts," she whispered, "give me a son, and I'll give him back to You for all his life." *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: Eli the priest thought she was drunk. She told him no — she was pouring out her soul in deep sorrow.
5. NARRATOR: Eli blessed her. She went away, and her face was no longer sad. The LORD remembered her.
6. NARRATOR: A boy was born. She named him Samuel — "because I have asked him of the LORD."
7. NARRATOR: She kept her word. When he was weaned, she brought him to the house of the LORD and left him there to serve.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "Hannah prayed the prayer no one else could hear, and God answered. Your quiet prayers are heard too."

## STORYBOARD (8–11 beats — CONTENT-CARE: reverent, no shame; Jesus does NOT appear)
1. Interior tabernacle court, dim: Hannah apart from the crowd, lips moving, eyes closed — silent prayer.
2. Close on Hannah's face, one painted teardrop, not weeping openly — sorrow held, not displayed.
3. Peninnah at a distance, turned away; composition shows contrast, not cruelty in focus.
4. Eli seated near the door, watching, initially mistaken — brow furrowed.
5. Hannah explaining, calm; Eli's expression softening into blessing.
6. Exterior, daylight: Hannah's face lifted, lighter, walking out of Shiloh — relief.
7. Domestic still: infant Samuel in Hannah's arms, tender.
8. Hannah leading the small boy toward the tabernacle, handing him to Eli — devotion, not loss.
9. Closing still: a single lamp burning in the house of the LORD at dusk — a life given back. Quiet hope.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE deep — no shame framing of barrenness; present Hannah's sorrow with compassion, not judgment. Jesus does NOT appear (OT). Mercy/answer spoken aloud. ~55–65s. Narrator Andrew; no Jesus voice.
