## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: To a crowd of people worn down by work, by rules, by just getting through the day, Jesus made one of the gentlest offers ever spoken.
2. JESUS (KJV, Matt 11:28): "Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."
3. NARRATOR: A yoke was the wooden beam that let two oxen pull a load together, so no single animal carried it alone. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. JESUS (KJV, Matt 11:29): "Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls."
5. JESUS (KJV, Matt 11:30): "For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light."
6. NARRATOR: He wasn't promising a life with nothing to carry. He was promising to get under the load with you, so it never has to be carried alone again.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "Whatever you've been carrying by yourself — he's offering to take the other side of it. Come."

## STORYBOARD (8–16 beats)
1. Exterior late afternoon, warm gold: a tired crowd — a stooped laborer, a weary mother, an old man — gathered on a hillside as Jesus speaks. Face SHOWN, cream robe.
2. Close on worn hands and bent shoulders in the crowd — the weight people carry, painted with dignity, not misery.
3. Jesus with an open, welcoming gesture, warm brown eyes, speaking the "come unto me" line — invitation in his whole posture. Master-locked.
4. A visual of a wooden ox-yoke resting on a fence in the golden field beyond — two curved bows, made for two. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the empty yoke)*
5. Two oxen in the distance pulling a plough together under one yoke, the load shared, moving easily through soft light.
6. A weary person in the crowd straightening, face easing, as if a weight just lifted — relief, not drama.
7. Wide: the crowd settled and restful around Jesus in the warm evening light.
8. Closing still: the wooden yoke in warm light with room on the empty side — an open place to step in beside him.
