# DRAFT row-087 — The boy in the temple — Luke 2:41–52

> Row 87 · Ref: Luke 2:41–52 · Jesus is a CHILD (twelve years old), NOT the adult master face.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: Clean. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Every year Jesus's family went to Jerusalem for the Passover. When he was twelve, they made the trip as usual — and on the way home, they realized he wasn't with them.
2. NARRATOR: They turned back, searching for three days. And they found him in the temple — sitting among the teachers, listening and asking questions that amazed everyone. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: His mother, relieved and worried, asked why he'd done this to them. He answered with a question of his own.
4. JESUS (KJV, Luke 2:49): "And he said unto them, How is it that ye sought me? wist ye not that I must be about my Father's business?"
5. NARRATOR: They didn't fully understand. But he went home with them and obeyed them — and he kept growing in wisdom, in stature, and in favor with God and people.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "Even as a boy he knew where he belonged. You were made for the Father's house too — come find your place."

## STORYBOARD (8–11 beats — Jesus is a CHILD, no adult master face)
1. Exterior day, Jerusalem streets: a Passover caravan leaving the city, families walking home.
2. Interior, Mary and Joseph discovering Jesus is missing, faces turning anxious.
3. Exterior, three days of searching — the temple courts in view.
4. Interior temple: the twelve-year-old Jesus seated among robed teachers, listening intently — CHILD, not adult master face.
5. Close on Jesus's face, calm and clear, asking a question that silences the room. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the boy and the teachers)*
6. Mary and Joseph arriving, relief and wonder as they find him.
7. Jesus speaking his answer, gentle but certain of who his Father is.
8. Closing still: the boy walking home with his parents, the temple behind him — growing in wisdom.
