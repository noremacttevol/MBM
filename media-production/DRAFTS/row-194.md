# DRAFT row-194 — The fruit of the Spirit — Galatians 5:22–23

> Row 194 · Ref: Galatians 5:22–23 · Jesus does NOT appear as a character (Paul's epistle to the Galatians). CONTENT-CARE: gentle, no gore.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, paraphrasing, NEVER quoting KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only — none here)
1. NARRATOR: Paul wrote that when God's Spirit lives in a person, a harvest grows — not crops, but character.
2. NARRATOR: First, love. Then joy. Then peace — the kind that doesn't depend on circumstances. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: Longsuffering next — patience that doesn't quit. Then gentleness, and goodness, and faith that holds.
4. NARRATOR: Meekness, and temperance — self-control that masters the storms inside.
5. NARRATOR: Against these things, Paul says, there is no law. They can't be overdone.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "This fruit isn't grown by strain. Ask the Spirit — and let it ripen in you."

## STORYBOARD (8–11 beats — CONTENT-CARE: gentle, no gore; Jesus does NOT appear)
1. Exterior, garden morning: a single tree heavy with varied fruit — the Spirit's harvest, symbolic. Jesus does NOT appear (Paul's teaching).
2. Close on a hand offering love — a kindness to a stranger, no shame framing.
3. A face lit with quiet joy, eyes lifted — joy made visible.
4. Two people at peace beside still water — peace. *(sacred-silence beat — still holds on the calm)*
5. An elder patient with a child's mistake — longsuffering, gentle.
6. A steady hand setting down anger — temperance, self-mastery.
7. Closing still: the tree's fruit glowing softly toward the viewer — invitation, gentle.
