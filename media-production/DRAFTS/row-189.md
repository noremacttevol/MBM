# DRAFT row-189 — To him that overcometh — Revelation 3:21

> Row 189 · Ref: Revelation 3:20–21 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (hope, throne, gentle).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: To a church that had grown comfortable and half-asleep, Jesus sent a knock at the door — not a storm, just a knock.
2. JESUS (KJV, Revelation 3:20): "Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me."
3. NARRATOR: He does not break the door. He waits to be invited in. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: And to the one who opens, who keeps opening, he promises a seat no empire can give.
5. JESUS (KJV, Revelation 3:21): "To him that overcometh will I grant to sit with me in my throne, even as I also overcame, and am set down with my Father in his throne."
6. NARRATOR: The one who overcomes doesn't earn a throne by being flawless. He shares the one Christ already won.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He is knocking right now. Open, and you will eat with him — and reign with him."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: hope, no gloom)
1. Exterior night, lamplit: a simple wooden door, a faint glow at the threshold, no figure forced into frame yet. Quiet.
2. Close on the door — a gentle knock implied by a small shift of light; reverent, not frightening.
3. Interior, warm: a person turning toward the door, face lit with recognition. No fear.
4. Jesus at the threshold, FACE-SHOWN — master ref, Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo, standing in soft light, never looming. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the open door and his face)*
5. Two at a table, bread between them — the "sup with him" idea, intimate and calm. Master-locked on Jesus's face.
6. A lifted view: a throne of light, not heavy or gold-heavy, shared seat implied — hope, not threat.
7. Closing still: Jesus's face turned to the viewer at the door, hand near the latch, invitation in his eyes. Reverent, warm.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — no judgment gloom, mercy/invitation spoken. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked. No wounds. ~55-65s. Narrator Andrew; Jesus Christopher exact KJV.
