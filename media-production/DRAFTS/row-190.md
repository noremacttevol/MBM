## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: James wrote plainly to the early church: a faith that stays only in the head is already dead.
2. NARRATOR: He asked the sharp question — if a brother or sister has no clothes and no food, and you wish them well but give nothing, what good is that?
3. NARRATOR: Then he pointed to Abraham, who showed his faith by what he did — offering his son on the altar. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: And to Rahab, who hid the spies and was counted righteous by her action, not just her words.
5. NARRATOR: The line James lands on: faith by itself, if it has no works, is dead. Belief and life belong together.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "Real faith reaches out a hand. Let yours move — he sees every small, true thing you do."
