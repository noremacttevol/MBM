# DRAFT row-099 — Flesh and bone; Thomas's hands — Luke 24:36–43 / John 20:24–29

> Row 99 · Ref: Luke 24:36–43, John 20:24–29 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN (risen).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. "wounds"/"prints" appear — but as RISEN, healed marks, not gore. Ear-check nothing unusual; the word "handle" (v39) clean.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: The disciples were hiding behind locked doors, afraid, when Jesus suddenly stood among them. They thought they were seeing a ghost.
2. NARRATOR: He calmed them and showed them it was really him — flesh and bone, not a spirit.
3. JESUS (KJV, Luke 24:39): "Behold my hands and my feet, that it is I myself: handle me, and see; for a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as ye see me have."
4. NARRATOR: But Thomas wasn't there. And when they told him, he refused to believe it — unless he could see and touch for himself. *(sacred-silence beat)*
5. NARRATOR: Eight days later, Jesus appeared again — and turned straight to Thomas, offering exactly what he'd demanded.
6. JESUS (KJV, John 20:27): "Reach hither thy finger, and behold my hands... and be not faithless, but believing."
7. NARRATOR: He didn't scold Thomas for doubting. He met the doubt with his own hands. That's what he does with honest doubt — he steps toward it.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He met a doubter with open hands, not anger. Bring him your doubt. He can handle it."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats)
1. Interior night, lamplit locked room: startled disciples recoiling as Jesus stands suddenly among them. Face SHOWN, cream robe, calm.
2. Close on Jesus, warm brown eyes, showing his open hands — healed marks visible but gentle, NOT bloody/gory. Master-locked.
3. Disciples' faces turning from terror to astonished joy.
4. A shared meal moment — Jesus eating a piece of fish to prove he's real flesh, disciples marveling. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the quiet proof)*
5. Later: Thomas, arms crossed, jaw set, shaking his head among the others — honest, stubborn doubt.
6. Jesus returned, turning directly to Thomas, extending his hand in invitation — no rebuke, only welcome. Master-locked.
7. Thomas's face collapsing into belief and awe, reaching toward Jesus.
8. Closing still: an open hand extended in warm lamplight — doubt met with an invitation.

## NOTES: Jesus FACE-SHOWN, risen. The nail-marks are HEALED marks, shown gently — NO gore, no fresh wounds (content-care). Master-locked. Doubt treated with dignity. ~65-75s. Narrator Andrew; Jesus Christopher exact KJV (two lines).
