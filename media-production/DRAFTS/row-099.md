# DRAFT row-099 — Flesh and bone; Thomas's hands — John 20:24–29

> Row 99 · Ref: John 20:24–29 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (reverent, no wounds in focus).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live present. Clean.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Thomas wasn't there the first time Jesus came. When the others said they'd seen the Lord, he drew a line.
2. NARRATOR: Unless I see the marks in His hands, he said, and put my own finger where the nails were, and my hand into His side — I will not believe.
3. NARRATOR: Eight days later they were gathered again, and this time Thomas was with them. The doors were shut. And Jesus stood among them. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: He didn't scold the doubt. He walked straight to it. He told Thomas to reach out his finger, to see His hands, to put his hand into His side — and to stop doubting and believe.
5. JESUS (KJV, John 20:27): "Reach hither thy finger, and behold my hands; and reach hither thy hand, and thrust it into my side: and be not faithless, but believing."
6. NARRATOR: Thomas didn't need to touch. One look at the living Lord and the wall came down. "My Lord and my God," he said.
7. JESUS (KJV, John 20:29): "Thomas, because thou hast seen me, thou hast believed: blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed."
8. NARRATOR: The blessing reached past that room to everyone who would believe without seeing — including you.
9. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "You don't have to see to believe. He's real, and He's enough."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: reverent, no wounds in focus)
1. Interior, evening: the locked upper room, the disciples gathered, Thomas doubtful, arms crossed.
2. The earlier appearance implied (doors shut, Jesus among them) — handled as a soft glow beat, not shown in detail.
3. Eight days later: the same room, Thomas now present, tension in his posture.
4. Jesus appearing among them, light around Him — reverent, no suffering detail. *(sacred-silence beat — hold on Thomas meeting His gaze)*
5. Close on Jesus's face as He speaks the invitation to touch — calm, open, unhurried. FACE-SHOWN, master-locked, Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
6. Jesus's hands extended gently toward Thomas — shown reverently, no nail wounds in focus (faces and gesture carry it).
7. Thomas's face softening from doubt to awe; "My Lord and my God."
8. Closing still: the room at peace, Jesus's face luminous, the doubt answered by presence.
