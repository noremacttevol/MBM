## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)

1. NARRATOR: A Pharisee named Simon invited Jesus to dinner. It was a careful, respectable house — and it was about to be interrupted.
2. NARRATOR: A woman from the town, a woman everyone there knew by her reputation, came in uninvited. She carried an alabaster jar of costly perfume.
3. NARRATOR: She stood behind him at his feet, weeping — and her tears fell on them. She wiped them with her hair, kissed them, and poured out the perfume. *(sacred-silence beat — narration drops to silence on the pouring)*
4. NARRATOR: Simon thought to himself: if this man were really a prophet, he'd know what kind of woman is touching him.
5. NARRATOR: Jesus answered the thought Simon never said out loud — with a small story. Two people owed a debt; one owed ten times the other; the lender forgave them both. Which one, he asked, will love him more?
6. JESUS (KJV, Luke 7:47): "Her sins, which are many, are forgiven; for she loved much: but to whom little is forgiven, the same loveth little."
7. JESUS (KJV, Luke 7:48, 50): "Thy sins are forgiven... Thy faith hath saved thee; go in peace."
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "She was known for the worst thing she'd done. He knew her for her love. Which name would you rather answer to?"
