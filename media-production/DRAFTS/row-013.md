## Narration Script (plain text, Aloud ready)

Hey Cameron. Row 013. Jesus came into a house, and the people gathered together again so that they could not so much as eat bread. When his friends heard of it, they went out with the intent to lay hold on him, for they said he was beside himself. The scribes which came down from Jerusalem said "He hath Beelzebul, and by the prince of devils he casteth out devils." Jesus called them and said in parables "How can Satan cast out Satan? If a kingdom be divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand. And if a house be divided against itself, that house cannot stand. And if Satan rise up against himself, and be divided, he cannot stand, but hath an end. No man can enter into a strong man's house and spoil his goods, except he first bind the strong man; and then he spoileth his house. But he that shall do the will of God, he is my brother and sister and mother." Then they come to his mother and brother, and stood without. And they sent unto him, calling him. Many sat around him. They said unto him "Behold, thy mother and thy brother without seek for thee." He answered and said unto them "Who is my mother, or my brethren?" He looked round about on them which sat about him, and said "Behold my mother and my brethren. For whosoever shall do the will of God, the same is my brother, and my sister, and mother." And the people were astonished at his teaching, because he taught them as one that had authority, and not as the scribes.

The religious leaders couldn't explain his power. So they called it evil. By what power does this man cast out devils? By Beelzebul. They couldn't recognize God's power when it didn't wear the right robe. But Jesus said whoever does God's will is his family. Not blood. Not position. Obedience to God's will. That's what makes family.
