## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)

1. NARRATOR: Early morning at the temple, Jesus was teaching a crowd, when a knot of religious leaders shoved their way through — dragging a woman with them.
2. NARRATOR: They stood her in the middle of everyone. They said she'd been caught in adultery, and the law said stone her. What did he say?
3. NARRATOR: It was a trap. Say let her go, and he breaks the law. Say stone her, and he's just another man with a rock. *(sacred-silence beat — narration cuts to silence)*
4. NARRATOR: Jesus said nothing at first. He bent down and wrote in the dust with his finger. Then he straightened up.
5. JESUS (KJV, John 8:7): "He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her."
6. NARRATOR: And he bent down and wrote again. One by one — the oldest first — they dropped their stones and walked away. Until it was only the two of them.
7. JESUS (KJV, John 8:10–11): "Woman, where are those thine accusers? hath no man condemned thee?... Neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more."
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The only one with the right to condemn her, wouldn't. What would it change to hear him say that to you?"

## STORYBOARD (8–16 beats — what the still shows, time of day, who in frame)

1. Exterior temple court, early morning: broad pale-stone steps and pillars, soft gold light, a seated crowd around a teacher. Jesus seated, face SHOWN, cream robe.
2. A cluster of stern robed men pushing through the crowd, a frightened woman pulled along by the arm — she clutches her shawl closed. No exposure, no violence; fear read only in her face and posture.
3. The woman standing alone in the open center, head down, arms wrapped around herself; a ring of accusers around her, a few holding stones low at their sides. Dust and long morning shadows.
4. Close on an accuser's hand gripping a rough stone — tension, not action. (Content-care: the stone is never thrown, never raised high.)
5. Jesus bent low, one finger drawing in the fine temple dust; the crowd leaning in, unsure. His face calm, downcast, unbothered by the pressure. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the writing hand)*
6. Jesus straightened to full height, meeting the accusers' eyes as he speaks the one line — quiet, level, immovable.
7. The accusers turning away in sequence: an old man's hand opening, a stone falling to the dust, backs receding into the morning light — matching frame, oldest in front.
8. Wide two-shot: the emptied court, the woman still standing, Jesus alone facing her across the swept dust — everyone else gone.
9. Close: Jesus's face, warm brown eyes, speaking "neither do I condemn thee" — her face lifting from shame toward relief. Face-SHOWN, master-locked.
10. Closing still: a single unthrown stone lying in the dust where a footprint leads away, morning light warming the empty court — go, and sin no more.
