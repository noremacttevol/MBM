# DRAFT row-130 — "Ye know not what manner of spirit ye are of" — Luke 9:51–56

> Row 130 · Ref: Luke 9:51–56 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE: none.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: "save" clean. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check: none.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: As Jesus traveled toward Jerusalem, a Samaritan village refused to welcome Him. The disciples James and John were hot with anger.
2. NARRATOR: They asked if they should call down fire from heaven to burn the place. They wanted judgment.
3. NARRATOR: Jesus turned and rebuked them — not the village, but His own disciples' hearts.
4. JESUS (KJV, Luke 9:55–56): "Ye know not what manner of spirit ye are of. For the Son of man is not come to destroy men's lives, but to save them."
5. NARRATOR: He didn't come to burn. He came to rescue. The fire in the disciples' hearts was the wrong kind. *(sacred-silence beat)*
6. NARRATOR: Anger that wants to destroy isn't from Him. The spirit He brings saves.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He came to save, not to burn. Let His Spirit — the saving one — shape yours."

## STORYBOARD (7 beats — no fire shown on people, only symbolic contrast)
1. Exterior, late afternoon, a Samaritan village gate closed, villagers turning away; disciples James and John tense, indignant.
2. Jesus arriving, calm, unhurried, looking at the closed gate with sorrow, not wrath. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked, Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
3. Close on James and John's faces — flushed, ready for fire.
4. Jesus's face turned to His disciples, gentle but firm rebuke — love correcting love. Master-locked.
5. A visual contrast: a flame of destruction vs. a warm hearth light — the two spirits side by side, the hearth chosen. *(sacred-silence beat)*
6. Jesus walking on, the village behind, peace around Him.
7. Closing still: an open hand offering warmth, not fire — the saving spirit. Hopeful.
