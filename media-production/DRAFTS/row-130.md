# DRAFT row-130 — Ye know not what manner of spirit ye are of — Luke 9:51–56

> Row 130 · Ref: Luke 9:51–56 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (rebuke, gentle/mercy).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: none of bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live present. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Jesus was on His way to Jerusalem, the time drawing near. He sent helpers ahead into a Samaritan village to get things ready.
2. NARRATOR: But the village shut its doors. They would not welcome Him, because His face was set toward Jerusalem.
3. NARRATOR: Two of His followers, James and John, were hot with it. Lord, should we call down fire from heaven and burn them up, like Elijah did? *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: Jesus turned and looked at them — not with anger at the villagers, but at His own. That is not the spirit you are made of.
5. JESUS (KJV, Luke 9:55–56): "Ye know not what manner of spirit ye are of. For the Son of man is not come to destroy men's lives, but to save them."
6. NARRATOR: He did not come to burn. He came to rescue. And they walked on to the next village.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "He could have called fire. He chose mercy. That same mercy is for you."

## STORYBOARD (8–10 beats — CONTENT-CARE: no fire/scorch in focus, mercy spoken)
1. Exterior, late afternoon: Jesus walking the road toward Jerusalem, resolute, cloak catching the wind. Jesus FACE-SHOWN (master ref, Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo).
2. A Samaritan village gate, closed; villagers turned away in the background, mid-distance, no hostility staged.
3. James and John, faces flushed, stepping forward angry — the heat of the moment.
4. Close on James and John asking, hands gesturing toward the sky — indignation.
5. Jesus turning to them, calm; the rebuke in His eyes is gentle, not harsh. Master-locked face.
6. Three-shot: Jesus and the two disciples, the silence after the question. *(sacred-silence beat — still holds on the three faces)*
7. Jesus speaking the words of mercy; the village gate still closed but the light softens.
8. Exterior dusk: the small group walking on to the next village, no fire, only the road.
9. Closing still: open road at golden hour, peace where anger might have been.
