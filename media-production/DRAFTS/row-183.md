# DRAFT row-183 — Sun, moon, and stars — Genesis 1:14–19

> Row 183 · Ref: Genesis 1:14–19 · Jesus does NOT appear (creation, Old Testament). CONTENT-CARE (order and awe, no fear).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live/lives/lived present. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; God speaks EXACT KJV)
1. NARRATOR: On the fourth day, God turned to the empty sky and filled it. He set lights to mark the days, the seasons, and the years.
2. NARRATOR: Two great lights — the greater to rule the day, the lesser to rule the night — and the stars besides, more than could be counted.
3. GOD (KJV, Genesis 1:16): "And God made two great lights; the greater light to rule the day, and the lesser light to rule the night: he made the stars also."
4. NARRATOR: He set them in the sky to shine on the earth, to divide the light from the dark — and He looked, and called it good. *(sacred-silence beat)*
5. NARRATOR: From the very beginning, the lights above were a gift — ordered, generous, and good.
6. NARRATOR: The same hands that hung the sun, moon, and stars are the hands that made you — on purpose, and for good.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The God who set the sun, moon, and stars in place made you with the same care. You are under His light."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: order and awe; Jesus does NOT appear, no human figure)
1. Vast empty sky at the threshold of day four, calm and waiting.
2. The sun blazing into place — the greater light, warm and golden, steady.
3. The moon rising soft and silver — the lesser light, quiet and calm.
4. The stars pouring across the heavens by the thousands — generous, uncounted. *(sacred-silence beat — hold on the starfield)*
5. A view from the earth: day and night divided, ordered and beautiful, never chaotic.
6. The lights seen as a canopy of care over a silent, resting earth.
7. Closing still: the full heavens over a small quiet world — "and God saw that it was good."

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — no violence; awe, order, and generosity, never terror. Jesus does NOT appear; keep creation cosmic with no human or divine face. God's words are exact KJV (Genesis 1:16). ~45-55s. Narrator Andrew.
