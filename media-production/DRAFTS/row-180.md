## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; the LORD speaks EXACT KJV)
1. NARRATOR: A young man named Jeremiah felt far too small for the job. God was calling him to speak to nations, and he was certain he could not do it.
2. NARRATOR: But the call did not begin the day he heard it. Long before he was born, before he ever drew a breath, the plan was already set.
3. NARRATOR: The God who made him had already chosen him — and blessing, not pressure, was the shape of it. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. THE LORD (KJV, Jeremiah 1:5): "Before I formed thee in the belly I knew thee; and before thou camest forth out of the womb I sanctified thee, and I ordained thee a prophet unto the nations."
5. NARRATOR: Jeremiah answered that he was only a child. The LORD replied — go where I send you, speak what I command, and do not be afraid, for I am with you to deliver you.
6. THE LORD (KJV, Jeremiah 1:8): "Be not afraid of their faces: for I am with thee to deliver thee, saith the LORD."
7. NARRATOR: The same God who knew you before you were born is the one who walks with you now. The calling is His; the courage is His gift.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "You were known before you were born. You are not too small for what He has for you."
