# DRAFT row-067 — The Transfiguration — Mark 9:2–8

> Row 67 · Ref: Mark 9:2–8 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN, glorified (adult master face, radiant — the SAME face, caught in light). CONTENT-CARE: reverence and glory, no fear/dread; the cloud and the voice are comfort, not terror.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: Clean. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Jesus took three of his friends up a high, quiet mountain — Peter, James, and John. And there, something happened that words could barely hold.
2. NARRATOR: His face and his clothes began to shine with a light that had no source in the sun. Two men from the old stories stood with him — Moses and Elijah. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: The friends had only wanted to build shelters, overwhelmed. Then a cloud wrapped them, and out of it came a voice.
4. VOICE FROM THE CLOUD (KJV, Mark 9:7): "This is my beloved Son: hear him."
5. NARRATOR: When they looked again, the glory had drawn back, and only Jesus was there — standing with them, the same friend they had climbed the mountain with.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The Father said it plainly: hear him. Lean in, and listen — the same voice speaks to you."

## STORYBOARD (8–10 beats — Jesus FACE-SHOWN, glorified; reverence, not spectacle)
1. Exterior day, a high lonely mountain: Jesus leading the three disciples up a quiet trail.
2. The summit, Jesus alone in prayer — and the light begins to rise from within him.
3. Jesus transfigured: face and robe shining with unbearable gentle light; Moses and Elijah at his sides, dimmer, honored witnesses. Face SHOWN, master-locked and radiant — the same man, caught in glory.
4. Close on Jesus's shining face, kind and unafraid; the light is warmth, not terror. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on his glorified face)*
5. Peter, James, and John overwhelmed, shading their eyes, awestruck — ordinary men before the light.
6. A soft cloud enveloping the peak; the three disciples small within it, reverent.
7. The voice from the cloud — rendered as light and stillness, no frightening imagery; the Father's words heard.
8. The glory receding: Jesus alone again with the three, hand perhaps on a shoulder, the same friend.
9. Closing still: the mountain in calm evening light, the three walking down with him — they have seen, and are changed. Peaceful.

## NOTES: Jesus FACE-SHOWN, adult, master-locked AND glorified (Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, cream robe, NO halo/head-ring — the radiance rises from him, it is not a manufactured halo). Moses and Elijah are honored figures, not Jesus. CONTENT-CARE: glory handled as tender light, never as awe-terror; the cloud is comfort. ~55-65s.
