# DRAFT row-182 — The spirit returns to God — Ecclesiastes 12:7

> Row 182 · Ref: Ecclesiastes 12:1–7 · Jesus does NOT appear (Old Testament wisdom). CONTENT-CARE (death, reverent distance, hopeful).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live/lives/lived present. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; the Preacher speaks EXACT KJV)
1. NARRATOR: The Teacher, Solomon, wrote honestly about the end of life. The body grows old, the days grow dim, and then — the breath leaves.
2. NARRATOR: He did not leave it there in the dark. He pointed plainly to where the breath goes.
3. THE PREACHER (KJV, Ecclesiastes 12:7): "Then shall the dust return to the earth as it was: and the spirit shall return unto God who gave it."
4. NARRATOR: The body, made from dust, goes back to the ground. But the part of you that is from God goes home to Him. *(sacred-silence beat)*
5. NARRATOR: Death is not the end of the story. It is the spirit's quiet return to the One who lent it.
6. NARRATOR: And the Giver who receives it back is the same Giver who first breathed it into you — with mercy, not anger.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "You were given breath by God. He is ready to receive it — and ready to give you more."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: death, reverent distance, no grim detail; Jesus does NOT appear)
1. Exterior, evening: an aged figure seated quietly, the long warm light of late day around him.
2. Close on weathered, gentle hands at rest — a life lived, not lamented.
3. The body settling; dust-to-dust rendered as soft earth tones converging — reverent, never graphic.
4. A wisp of light rising from the figure toward the sky — the spirit's return. *(sacred-silence beat — hold on the rising light)*
5. Heavenward still: warm light gathering, the Giver receiving what He gave.
6. A glimpse of peace, not fear, on the face as the light lifts away.
7. Closing still: open sky at dusk, one light ascending — rest, not terror.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — death handled with reverent distance, no gore or grim close-up; mercy/hope spoken aloud (J-story law). Jesus does NOT appear; no divine face — convey God through received light. The Preacher's words are exact KJV (Ecclesiastes 12:7). ~50-60s. Narrator Andrew.
