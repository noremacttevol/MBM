# DRAFT row-088 — The triumphal entry — Matthew 21:1–11

> Row 88 · Ref: Matthew 21:1–11 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN (adult, master ref). CONTENT-CARE (reverent, joyful, no conflict focus).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: None of bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: As Jesus came near Jerusalem, he sent two disciples ahead into the next village with a simple instruction.
2. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 21:2–3): "Go into the village over against you, and straightway ye shall find an ass tied, and a colt with her: loose them, and bring them unto me. And if any man say ought unto you, ye shall say, The Lord hath need of them; and straightway he will send them."
3. NARRATOR: They brought the colt, laid their cloaks across its back, and Jesus sat on it — a quiet king, riding in low, not high. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: The crowd spread their own cloaks and cut branches from the trees, lining the road before him.
5. NARRATOR: And the people shouted as he passed — Hosanna to the Son of David! Blessed is he that comes in the name of the Lord!
6. NARRATOR: The whole city stirred. Who is this? they asked. And the answer went ahead of him: this is the prophet, Jesus, from Nazareth.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The King came gentle, on a colt, welcomed by ordinary people. He comes the same way to you — open the road."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: joyful, faces not crowd chaos)
1. Exterior, morning: Jesus speaking quietly to two disciples who turn to go; calm, purposeful.
2. The disciples untying a donkey and a colt at a village; simple, unhurried.
3. Jesus seated on the colt, cloaks beneath him, riding toward the city gates — FACE-SHOWN, master-locked, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on his face)*
4. The road lined with cloaks and fresh branches; people's faces turned toward him, glad.
5. Close on Jesus's face as the crowd shouts — steady, peaceful, kingly without pride.
6. Hands laying palm branches; a child's face among the crowd, smiling up.
7. Wide: the city wall ahead, the procession small but bright against it.
8. Closing still: Jesus entering, the crowd's welcome like light before him — humble kingship.
