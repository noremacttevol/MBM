# DRAFT row-088 — The triumphal entry — Matthew 21:1–11

> Row 88 · Ref: Matthew 21:1–11 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN (adult, mounted). Adult master face applies.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: Clean. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: As Jesus came near Jerusalem, he sent two disciples ahead to bring him a donkey and her colt — exactly as the old prophecy said.
2. NARRATOR: The crowds spread their cloaks on the road, and cut branches from the trees, and lined his path. The King was coming — but not the kind they expected. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: And the people shouted as he rode in:
4. CROWD (KJV, Matthew 21:9): "Hosanna to the son of David: Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord; Hosanna in the highest."
5. NARRATOR: The whole city was stirred. When they asked who this was, the crowd answered plainly.
6. CROWD (KJV, Matthew 21:10–11): "And when he was come into Jerusalem, all the city was moved, saying, Who is this? And the multitude said, This is Jesus the prophet of Nazareth of Galilee."
7. NARRATOR: He came not with swords but with peace — riding on a donkey, the humble King the scriptures had promised.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The King rode in on a donkey, not a warhorse. His kingdom is peace. You're welcome in it."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — Jesus FACE-SHOWN, adult master face)
1. Exterior day, road to Jerusalem: Jesus sending two disciples to fetch a donkey and colt.
2. The disciples returning with the animals, cloaks laid across the colt's back.
3. Jesus mounting the colt, riding toward the city gate — Face SHOWN, cream robe, Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, no head-ring. Master-locked.
4. The crowd spreading cloaks and palm branches on the road before him. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the path being laid before the King)*
5. Close on Jesus's face, calm and gentle, riding in peace. Master-locked.
6. The multitude waving branches, faces of joy, shouting Hosanna.
7. Jesus entering Jerusalem, the city walls behind him, onlookers amazed.
8. A citizen asking "Who is this?" and the crowd answering with his name.
9. Closing still: Jesus on the colt at the city gate, light warm on his face — the humble King has come.
