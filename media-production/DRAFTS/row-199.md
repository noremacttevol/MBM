# DRAFT row-199 — Fishers and hunters — Jeremiah 16:16

> Row 199 · Ref: Jeremiah 16:16 · Jesus NOT present (Jeremiah prophecy, OT). CONTENT-CARE: judgment framed with mercy spoken aloud.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: Clean. No bow / wound / wind / tears / lead / sow / live. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: The prophet Jeremiah spoke a hard word to a people who would not turn. The LORD said He would send fishers to draw them out, then hunters to chase them from every mountain, hill, and rocky hole.
2. NARRATOR: It is a picture of judgment — no one escapes, no hiding place far enough. The nets and the hunt find everyone.
3. NARRATOR: But hear the mercy underneath: the same God who sends the search is the one who wants them found, not lost. Judgment is the last call, not the first. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: The point was never the catching — it was that a holy God would not let His people slip away unnamed. He seeks, even when seeking means discipline.
5. NARRATOR: For us the picture flips into hope: the Fisher of men searches still, not to condemn, but to bring home whoever will be found.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The hunt was a wake-up, not a goodbye. He's still searching for you — let yourself be found."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — Jesus absent, no face ref needed)
1. Exterior, shoreline at grey dawn: fishers casting wide nets from small boats — the first search.
2. Rugged hillside, hunters moving across mountains and hills, searching rocks and clefts — the second search.
3. A hidden figure in a rocky crevice, discovered — no escape, but the finder's hand is open, not a fist. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the open hand)*
4. Jeremiah delivering the word, solemn but not cruel, the weight of love in the warning.
5. Closing still: the same nets and hills at sunrise, reframed as a search to gather, not only to catch.
