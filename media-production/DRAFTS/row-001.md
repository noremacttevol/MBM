## Narration Script (plain text, Aloud ready)

Hey Cameron. Row 001. Jesus came back to the other side of the sea, and a great crowd gathered. Jairus, a ruler of the synagogue, comes. His little daughter is dying. He begs Jesus, "Come and lay thy hands upon her, that she may be healed, that she may live." Jesus went with him, and a great crowd followed. A woman had an issue of blood for twelve years, had suffered many things of many physicians, and spent all that she had and was nothing bettered but rather grew worse. She heard of Jesus and came in the press behind him and touched his garment, saying "If I may touch but his clothes, I shall be made whole." Immediately her fountain of blood was dried, and she felt in her body that she was healed of her plague. Jesus perceived in himself that virtue went out of him, turned about and said "Who touched my clothes?" The disciples say "Thou seest the multitude thronging thee, and sayest thou Who touched me?" He looked round and saw the woman. The woman came in fear and trembling, fell down before him, told all the truth. Jesus said "Daughter, thy faith hath made thee whole; go in peace." While he yet spake, there come from the ruler of the synagogue's house saying "Thy daughter is dead. Trouble not the Master." Jesus overheard, said unto Jairus "Fear not, believe only." No man followed him except Peter, James, and John. They come to the bed where they saw the damsel sitting, and said "Maid, arise." Her spirit came again, and she arose straightway. Jesus commanded that something should be given her to eat.

This story shows us what faith looks like. Not just believing words. Not just being in the crowd. One woman reached out when everyone else just followed. She was healed the instant she touched his garment. Her faith made her whole.
