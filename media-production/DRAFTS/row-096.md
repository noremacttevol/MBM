## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: At the very end, Jesus knew everything had now been accomplished. He wasn't a victim losing a fight — he was finishing a work.
2. NARRATOR: With his last breath he spoke three words that weren't a cry of defeat, but a declaration of completion. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. JESUS (KJV, John 19:30): "It is finished."
4. NARRATOR: And he bowed his head and gave up the spirit — on his own terms, not theirs.
5. NARRATOR: At that moment, in the temple across the city, the great veil that walled off the holiest place was torn in two, from the top down — as if torn by a hand from above.
6. NARRATOR: That curtain had kept people out of God's presence for centuries. The instant he died, it was ripped open. The way in was thrown wide — for everyone.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The curtain that kept you out was torn open the moment he died. The way to God is open. Walk in."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: reverent, no gore)
1. Exterior, dark heavy sky over the hill, the center cross in dignified silhouette against a last shaft of failing light — long reverent distance.
2. Close on Jesus's face lifted, eyes toward heaven, peace overtaking pain — the focus his face only, never wounds. Master-locked.
3. Close on his face as he speaks "it is finished" — resolve, completion, not defeat. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on his face)*
4. His head bowing gently — composed, reverent, no graphic detail.
5. Interior temple, gold and shadow: the massive embroidered veil beginning to split at the very top.
6. The veil torn fully in two, top to bottom, light pouring through the opening into the once-hidden holy place. Dramatic, awe not fear.
7. Wide: the empty torn veil, the way standing open, warm light flooding in.
8. Closing still: light streaming through the torn curtain into darkness — the open way.
