# DRAFT row-096 — "It is finished"; the veil torn — John 19:30; Matthew 27:51

> Row 96 · Refs: John 19:30, Matthew 27:51 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (crucifixion, reverent distance).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live present. Clean.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: The work was done. Every promise, every prophecy, every weight He had carried — finished. He lifted His voice one last time.
2. JESUS (KJV, John 19:30): "It is finished."
3. NARRATOR: And He bowed His head and gave up His breath. The King laid down His life; no one took it from Him. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: At that same moment, far away in the temple, a curtain hung between the people and the holiest place — a barrier no one could cross.
5. NARRATOR: It tore. From the top to the bottom, in a single ripping sound. The divide was gone. The way was open.
6. NARRATOR: The earth itself seemed to lean in. Stone split. The old separation ended the moment He spoke.
7. NARRATOR: "It is finished" was not a cry of defeat. It was the sentence that ended the sentence — the debt marked paid.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The barrier is torn. The way back to Him is open. Come."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: reverent, faces not wounds)
1. Exterior, dim afternoon: the cross on the hill in dignified mid-distance; sky heavy, focus on light and form, never suffering detail.
2. Close on Jesus's face as He speaks "It is finished" — tender, resolved. Master-locked, Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo. Reverent framing.
3. The head bows; a quiet stillness falls over the hill. *(sacred-silence beat — hold on the bowed face)*
4. Cut to interior, the temple: the great veil, a thick woven wall, filling the frame in soft lamplight.
5. The veil tears from top to bottom — a clean, solemn rip; no gore, only the barrier falling away.
6. Wide of the temple court: watchers turning as the earth quakes gently; dust from stone.
7. A beam of light through the torn veil — the holy place now visible, inviting.
8. Closing still: the hill at dusk with the empty cross silhouetted and light breaking the clouds — finished, and open.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE deep — crucifixion kept at reverent distance, no nails/blood/wounds in focus; only the face and the torn veil carry the moment. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked. ~50-60s. Narrator Andrew; Jesus Christopher exact KJV.
