# DRAFT row-192 — The fast God has chosen — Isaiah 58:6–12

> Row 192 · Ref: Isaiah 58:6–12 · Jesus does NOT appear (Old Testament; Isaiah prophesies the LORD's fast). CONTENT-CARE: mercy, no gore, no shame.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, paraphrasing, NEVER quoting KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only — none here)
1. NARRATOR: Isaiah told God's people what kind of fast the LORD actually wants — not just going hungry to look holy.
2. NARRATOR: He said: loose the chains of injustice. Untie the ropes that weigh people down. Let the oppressed go free. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: Share your bread with the hungry. Bring the poor, the ones with nowhere to go, into your home.
4. NARRATOR: When you see someone with no clothes, cover them. Don't turn away from your own family.
5. NARRATOR: Do that, and your light breaks out like the dawn. Your healing springs up fast.
6. NARRATOR: The LORD promises — call, and He answers. Help others, and your own darkness becomes noonday.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The fast God chooses is a hand opened to the hurting. Open yours — He meets you there."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: mercy, no gore; Jesus does NOT appear)
1. Exterior, dawn: a man untying the ropes from a burdened worker's shoulders — justice made visible. Jesus does NOT appear (Isaiah's prophecy).
2. A heavy yoke lifted and set down — oppression loosed, shown with dignity, no shame framing.
3. Interior, warm: bread broken and pressed into a hungry person's hands — mercy spoken aloud.
4. A doorway opening to receive a homeless family, lamp glowing — welcome. *(sacred-silence beat — still holds on the open door)*
5. A cloak laid over bare shoulders — covering, never exposure.
6. Exterior: first light spilling over a hillside as the giver walks among the poor — light breaking like dawn.
7. Closing still: an open hand reaching toward the viewer, morning behind it — invitation, hopeful.
