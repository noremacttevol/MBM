## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: John came to Jesus with a complaint. They'd seen someone driving out demons using Jesus's name — and the man wasn't one of their group. So they told him to stop.
2. NARRATOR: John thought he was protecting the cause. The man wasn't with them, so he shouldn't be using the name.
3. NARRATOR: Jesus corrected the instinct. Don't stop him. Anyone doing good in My name isn't against you. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. JESUS (KJV, Mark 9:39–40): "Forbid him not: for there is no man which shall do a miracle in my name, that can lightly speak evil of me. For he that is not against us is on our part."
5. NARRATOR: Whoever gives a cup of water in His name won't lose his reward. The work isn't about belonging to a team — it's about Him.
6. NARRATOR: John learned a lesson that day: the kingdom is bigger than the inner circle.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "Don't draw the circle too small. Whoever works in His name is on your side."
