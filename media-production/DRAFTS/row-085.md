# DRAFT row-085 — Shepherds and angels — Luke 2:8–20

> Row 85 · Ref: Luke 2:8–20 · Jesus is a CHILD (infant), NOT the adult master face. Nativity.

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: Clean. No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: That same night, out on the hills around Bethlehem, shepherds were keeping watch over their flocks in the dark.
2. NARRATOR: Suddenly the sky lit up. An angel of the Lord stood among them, and the glory of the Lord shone around — and they were terrified. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. NARRATOR: But the angel spoke the words the whole world had been waiting for.
4. JESUS-STORY ANGEL (KJV, Luke 2:10–11): "Fear not: for, behold, I bring you good tidings of great joy, which shall be to all people. For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord."
5. NARRATOR: And then the sky filled — a great company of heaven, praising God together:
6. HEAVENLY HOST (KJV, Luke 2:14): "Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward men."
7. NARRATOR: The shepherds didn't wait. They hurried to the town and found the baby lying in a manger, just as they'd been told. The King of heaven had come as a child.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The good news came first to ordinary people keeping watch in the dark. It comes to you too. Come and see."

## STORYBOARD (8–11 beats — Jesus is an INFANT, no adult master face)
1. Exterior night, open hillside: shepherds seated by a small fire, sheep resting, stars overhead.
2. The sky bursts with light; an angel figure stands radiant among them (no head-ring, gentle glow).
3. Shepherds' faces, awe and fear, shielding their eyes from the light. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the lit sky)*
4. The heavenly host filling the sky, a chorus of light, praising.
5. Shepherds rising, urgent, leaving the flock to go.
6. Interior humble stable, warm lamplight: Mary and Joseph beside a manger.
7. The infant Jesus in the manger, tiny, wrapped in swaddling clothes — CHILD, not adult face.
8. Shepherds gathered at the manger, kneeling, wonder on every face.
9. Closing still: the stable under the night sky, a single bright star above — peace on earth.

## NOTES: Jesus is an INFANT here — never attach the adult master face ref. Reverent, tender, joyful. No gore, no fear framing beyond the shepherds' natural startle. ~55-65s. Narrator Andrew; angel lines Christopher (KJV Luke 2:10–11, 2:14).
