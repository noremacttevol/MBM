# DRAFT row-085 — Shepherds and angels — Luke 2:8–20

> Row 85 · Ref: Luke 2:8–20 · Jesus is the NEWBORN CHILD, NOT the adult master face. CONTENT-CARE (nativity, gentle).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: None of bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; ANGEL = EXACT KJV — Jesus is a baby and does not speak here)
1. NARRATOR: That night, near Bethlehem, some shepherds were out in the fields watching their flocks in the dark.
2. NARRATOR: Suddenly the sky lit up. An angel of the Lord stood among them, and the glory of God shone around — and they were terrified. *(sacred-silence beat)*
3. ANGEL (KJV, Luke 2:10–11): "Fear not: for, behold, I bring you good tidings of great joy, which shall be to all people. For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord."
4. NARRATOR: Then the sky filled — a great crowd of heaven's host, praising God together.
5. ANGEL HOST (KJV, Luke 2:14): "Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward men."
6. NARRATOR: The shepherds didn't wait. They hurried into town and found the baby lying in a manger, just as they'd been told.
7. NARRATOR: They saw him with their own eyes — and then they went out telling everyone what they had seen and heard.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "The first news of the Savior went to ordinary people keeping watch in the night. He came for you too. Come and see."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: warm, reverent, no fear framing)
1. Exterior, deep night: shepherds and a quiet flock under a star field; still, calm.
2. The sky breaks open with soft light — an angel standing in gentle radiance; shepherds' faces lifted, awe not terror. *(sacred-silence beat — the light holds)*
3. Wide shot: the heavenly host across the sky, a chorus of light, praising.
4. The shepherds walking quickly toward Bethlehem under the stars, hope on their faces.
5. Interior, humble stable: Mary and Joseph beside a manger; the newborn CHILD wrapped in swaddling clothes — Jesus shown as a baby, soft and small, NOT the adult master face.
6. Close on the shepherds' faces as they look down at the child — wonder, peace.
7. The shepherds leaving, telling others; warm dawn beginning to break.
8. Closing still: a single star over the stable — the good news for all people.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — gentle nativity, no fear/shame. Jesus is the infant child; do not apply the adult master face reference here. Angel words are exact KJV. ~55–65s. Narrator Andrew; angel Christopher (exact KJV).
