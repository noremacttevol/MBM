## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: The weight of leading a whole nation was crushing Moses. He told the LORD he could not carry the people alone, not one more day.
2. NARRATOR: So God told Moses to gather seventy trusted men. He would take some of the Spirit that rested on Moses and share it with them, and together they would help bear the load.
3. NARRATOR: When the Spirit came down on those seventy, they began to speak God's words — they prophesied. A one-time gift for a crushing day.
4. NARRATOR: But two men, Eldad and Medad, had stayed back in the camp. The Spirit came on them too, right there among the tents, and they prophesied.
5. NARRATOR: A runner hurried to Moses with the news. Joshua, Moses's right-hand man, was worried — stop them, he said, that is not how it is supposed to work. *(sacred-silence beat)*
6. NARRATOR: Moses's answer is one of the most generous in all of Scripture. Do not be jealous for my sake. I wish every one of the LORD's people had the Spirit and could prophesy.
7. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "Moses did not guard the gift — he wished it for everyone. The same Spirit is offered to you. Ask, and receive."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — Jesus absent, no face ref needed)
1. Exterior, wilderness camp at dusk: Moses alone on a rise, shoulders bent under the weight, the sprawling camp below.
2. The LORD's presence at the tabernacle, warm light — God instructing Moses to gather the seventy elders.
3. Seventy men standing around the tabernacle, light resting on each — the Spirit shared, faces lifted.
4. Interior camp scene, midday: Eldad and Medad among the tents, caught by the Spirit, speaking God's words in wonder.
5. A young runner hurrying to Moses, urgency — news of the two prophesying in the camp.
6. Joshua at Moses's side, protective, hand raised — "forbid them."
7. Moses's face, calm and unthreatened, turning toward the camp — the generous answer forming. *(sacred-silence beat — hold on Moses's untroubled face)*
8. Closing still: the whole camp under open sky, light hinting over every tent — the wish that all would prophesy.
