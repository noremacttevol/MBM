## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: The prophet Isaiah pointed far ahead to a figure he called the root of Jesse — David's family tree, springing up fresh after it looked cut down.
2. NARRATOR: This one would stand as a banner, a signal, lifted up so the nations could find their way to Him. Not hidden. Raised for all to see.
3. NARRATOR: And Isaiah said the LORD would act a second time — reaching out again to gather His scattered people from the four corners of the earth. *(sacred-silence beat)*
4. NARRATOR: Exiles in every direction, lifted out of every nation, brought home. The ensign is the invitation; the gathering is the answer.
5. NARRATOR: Read as the Messiah drawing Gentiles and Jews alike — the banner is raised, and the seeking ones come home.
6. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "A banner was raised so you could find your way. Seek Him, and you'll be gathered in."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — Jesus absent in frame, ensign symbolic, NO adult master face)
1. Exterior, distant hill at sunrise: a lone banner / ensign planted and lifted, catching the first light — symbolic, not a person.
2. The stump of Jesse's tree with a fresh green shoot — the root springing up, hope from what looked dead.
3. Peoples from the four corners — East, West, North, South — turning toward the raised ensign, journeys converging.
4. A wide still: scattered exiles gathered, the nations seeking, faces hopeful. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds on the converging crowd)*
5. Closing still: the banner against an open sky, all peoples beneath it — home.
