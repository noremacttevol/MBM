# DRAFT row-139 — The lamp on a stand — Matthew 5:14–16

> Row 139 · Ref: Matthew 5:14–16 · Jesus FACE-SHOWN. CONTENT-CARE (gentle, no violence, no shame).

## HOMOGRAPH FLAGS: No bow/wound/wind/tears/lead/sow/live/lives/lived. Clean. Ear-check anyway.

## NARRATION (narrator = plain modern English, NEVER KJV; Jesus = EXACT KJV only)
1. NARRATOR: Jesus was teaching a crowd on a mountainside. He told them who they were before He told them what to do.
2. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 5:14): "Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid."
3. NARRATOR: Nobody lights a lamp and then hides it under a bowl. You put it up where it can light the whole room.
4. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 5:15): "Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick; and it giveth light unto all that are in the house."
5. NARRATOR: So let your light shine, He said — not to show off, but so people see the good and look up to your Father. *(sacred-silence beat)*
6. JESUS (KJV, Matthew 5:16): "Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven."
7. NARRATOR: You were lit on purpose. You were put on the stand on purpose.
8. CLOSING CARD (invitation, never fear): "You were made to be seen for good. Let it shine."

## STORYBOARD (8–12 beats — CONTENT-CARE: gentle, faces and light carry it)
1. Exterior, daytime, hillside: Jesus seated teaching, crowd gathered, open sky. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked, warm brown eyes, only-he-cream, no halo.
2. A small lamp in a dark room, then a hand placing it on a stand — light spreading. (illustrative beat)
3. A city on a hill at dusk, glowing, impossible to hide.
4. Close on Jesus's face as He teaches "ye are the light" — warm, inviting. *(sacred-silence beat — the still holds His face)*
5. A candle lifted from under a basket onto a candlestick, light filling the frame.
6. Faces in the crowd, listening, something kindling in them.
7. Closing still: a single lamp on a stand, light reaching every corner — gentle, hopeful.

## NOTES: CONTENT-CARE — gentle, no violence, no shame. Jesus FACE-SHOWN, master-locked. ~50-60s. Jesus lines exact KJV Matthew 5:14-16.
