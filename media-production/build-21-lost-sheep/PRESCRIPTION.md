# PRESCRIPTION — build-21-lost-sheep

**Row:** 21   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Luke 15:1–7
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 10)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he doesn't stand there counting what he still has — he goes out after the one that's gone, and heaven throws a party when it turns back, not a lecture.
- **The turn:** the shepherd finding the one and laying it on his shoulders, rejoicing (j2) — the story pivots from the search in the dark to joy carried home.
- **Character shown:** the outsider is not a loss he can shrug off; the one who wandered was his the whole time it was missing.
- **Whose face carries it:** the FOUND SHEEP and the SHEPHERD's joy (the shepherd is Jesus's stand-in in the parable, so the shepherd's face is fine); and the muttering religious men at the open, whose complaint the whole parable answers.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Have you ever felt like the one who wandered too far to be worth coming after?" — falls straight out of the one thing. No change needed.
