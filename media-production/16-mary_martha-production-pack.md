# Story Video #16 — Mary and Martha (Luke 10:38–42)

Runtime 60s. Prepend the STYLE BLOCK from 00-MASTER-PLAN.md. Jesus: a seated
presence at the edge of warm lamplight, from behind — never his face. This
is a small domestic film: one house, one evening, two sisters. Its power is
gentleness; nothing in it should be loud.

## Narration script (app's exact words)

> Jesus came to the home of two sisters. Martha threw herself into the
> work — the meal, the serving, everything a guest like this deserved.
>
> Her sister Mary sat down at Jesus's feet and just listened.
>
> Martha, worn thin, finally said what she was feeling: "Lord, don't you
> care that my sister has left me to do the work alone? Tell her to help
> me!"
>
> He answered with her name, twice — gently: "Martha, Martha. You are
> worried and troubled about many things. But only one thing is needed."
> *(peak — music cuts to silence on the second "Martha")*
>
> "Mary has chosen the better part, and it will not be taken away from her."
> He never scolded the serving. He worried about the worry.

Closing card (hold 6s): *"Worried and troubled about many things — or
sitting down long enough to listen. Which one sounds more like your life
right now?"*

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — The guest arrives
```
Golden late afternoon: a modest stone house; a woman flings the door wide
with real joy, wiping her hands on her apron, waving the honored guest and
his companions in. Her younger sister stands behind her in the doorway
light, hands still, face quietly lit up.
```

### Shot 2 — Martha at full speed
```
Interior, evening lamplight: one woman in constant motion — stirring pots,
slicing bread, catching a tipping jug, carrying dishes, hair escaping its
tie, sweat at her temple. Beautiful food piling up; her face tightening
notch by notch. The camera pans with her and never gets to rest either.
```

### Shot 3 — Mary at his feet
```
In the warm main room, guests seated in lamplight around a figure seen only
from behind at the head of the room: the younger sister sits on the floor
at the edge of the lamp's glow, knees drawn up, face turned up, completely
still — the only stillness in the house. Dust motes hang gold in the
lamplight over her.
```

### Shot 4 — Worn thin
```
The kitchen doorway: the working sister stops, flour on her hands, and
watches her sister just SITTING — and her face does the honest human thing:
hurt first, then anger. She wipes her hands hard, steps into the main room,
and says it out loud in front of everyone, pointing. The room goes quiet.
```

### Shot 5 — Her name, twice, gently (the money shot — extra takes)
```
Close up on the standing sister's flushed, defensive face as words we don't
hear land on her — and her name said kindly does what nothing else could:
the anger drains, her shoulders drop from her ears, tears surprise her
eyes, and she lets go of the cloth twisted in her hands. Warm lamplight.
No one is laughing at her. She is being SEEN, not scolded.
```

### Shot 6 — Two sisters in the lamplight
```
Later: the food finally served and forgotten on the table, BOTH sisters now
sit side by side on the floor at the edge of the lamp's glow, the older one
still in her apron, listening — unpracticed at stillness, choosing it. The
lamp burns steady. Slow pull back through the doorway into the dark.
```

### Closing card
Text on cream, serif. No narration.

## The Seed (what this video must leave behind)

He never scolded the serving — he worried about the WORRY. Relationship with him outranks performance for him. This quietly undoes every anxious, checklist version of Christianity. Quiet question: *is your faith mostly work you're doing for God, or time you're spending with him?* Carried by shots 5–6.
