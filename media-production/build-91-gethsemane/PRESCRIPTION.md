## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he asked, out loud, for another way — and then handed the
  answer to his Father anyway. Asking is allowed; trusting the answer is the faith.
  He faced his darkest night so you'd never face yours alone.
- **The turn:** [jv42] "nevertheless not my will, but thine, be done." The surrender
  is the hinge — the whole video bends around it.
- **Character shown:** honesty and trust together — real agony (sweat like drops of
  blood), real tenderness toward the friends who fell asleep, real resolve.
- **Whose face carries it:** this is his own agony, so handle it via **master ref,
  reverent — prefer the bowed figure, the hands, over-the-shoulder, the ground —
  not a full dramatic close-up on his face.** The three sleeping disciples carry the
  human/witness side.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Where is he asking you to trust him, even now?" No change.
