# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #124: Gethsemane (luke-22_gethsemane)

Luke 22:39-46, with the fuller witness of Matthew 26:36-46. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) —
twelve painted stills, no motion clips.

CARE FLAGS: **ARC, R, G** (CONTENT-CARE.md #124 — "real agony, restrained blood; the
angel sent; drop by drop"). This is Passion-arc material, made with the camera's
restraint:
- **R — RESTRAINT.** The agony is real but the wound is never the picture. His sweat "as
  it were great drops of blood" is shown as a FEW dark drops fallen on the pale night
  stones and olive roots (s7) — muted, moonlit, not red-bright, never a bleeding face,
  never gore. The weight lands on his bowed shoulders and the ground, not on a wound. QC
  question for every frame: "would a parent let a 10-year-old see this?" — yes.
- **G — GRIEF honored, not rushed.** His sorrow "even unto death" is real, and God's
  answer is PRESENCE: the angel strengthening him (s8) is the hope-beat of the whole
  video, warm gold light breaking the blue night. The narration lands there, and the
  closing card is an open invitation, never a fear-question.
- **ARC.** Reverent restraint, exactly like the face law: what we withhold is what makes
  it worship instead of spectacle. The band coming to arrest him (s12) is only a distant
  line of torches far across the valley — no soldiers' faces, no weapons, no violence.

TIME OF DAY: **NIGHT throughout** (TIME-OF-DAY LAW) — Passover night on the Mount of
Olives. Deep blue night, a bright full Passover moon, stars, cool silver light with a
few small warm lantern points. There is NO sunrise, NO sunset, NO golden-hour sky
anywhere in this video. Any warm gold light is local: a carried lantern, or the angel's
radiance in s8.

SETTING: the garden of Gethsemane — an ancient olive grove of gnarled, twisted olive
trees and a low stone olive-press, rocky moonlit ground, on the western slope of the
Mount of Olives. Across the dark Kidron valley, the lamp-lit walls of Jerusalem and the
temple sit small and far in the wide shots. ONE garden only, one continuous place.

Jesus appears (face never shown) in s1, s2, s3, s4, s5, s6, s7, s8, s9, s10, s11 and s12
— he is the center of this story, so in EVERY frame the camera is BEHIND him, OVER his
shoulder, or at DISTANCE: the back of his head and his long dark hair to us, his face
never shown, never visible, at any angle. He kneels and prays; heaven answers. Hide him
by ANGLE only — never by glow, halo, or rim-light.

JESUS LOCK (identical wording wherever he appears; the project-wide look standard): a
first-century Middle-Eastern man, warm olive-brown skin, LONG dark brown-black hair
falling well past the shoulders, one plain undyed off-white cream wool robe (a simple
Judean tunic with a plain mantle, the same garment in every shot), bare-headed, no hood;
no glow, no halo, no rim-light of any kind; an ordinary solid man in the moonlight. The
camera is always behind him or over his shoulder and his face is never shown, never
visible, at any angle.

ONLY JESUS WEARS CREAM (build law, carried forward from #40). In every frame he is the
ONLY figure in an off-white or cream robe. Every other person — the eleven disciples —
wears a clearly darker earth colour (dun, faded brown, olive, grey-blue, maroon, deep
indigo) and each prompt says so explicitly. AND NOBODY ELSE HAS LONG HAIR: every other
man has SHORT hair or his head covered by a head-cloth; not one other man has long hair
falling past his shoulders, and no one else is in cream. The signature is the two things
TOGETHER — long dark hair past the shoulders AND a cream robe — so both are reserved for
him. Never leave the moonlit ground behind him as an empty stage for the model to fill
with a lone pale-robed long-haired figure: the olive trees and the disciples fill it.
Check this on every generated frame, not just the prompt.

THE THREE LOCK (Peter, James, John — s3, s4, s9, s10, s11, s12): the three fishermen he
takes deeper into the grove. Their faces ARE shown and stay the same men throughout;
they carry the human feeling of the video.
  PETER — about fifty, broad and weathered, a short grey-streaked dark beard, SHORT dark
  hair, a DUN-BROWN wool tunic under a faded OLIVE mantle. Earnest, heavy-hearted.
  JAMES — about thirty-five, a short black beard, SHORT dark hair, a dark MAROON wool
  tunic and mantle.
  JOHN — the youngest, about twenty-five, only a light beard, SHORT dark curls, a
  GREY-BLUE wool tunic. Tender, troubled.
Not one of the three wears off-white or cream; not one has long hair past his shoulders.

THE EIGHT LOCK (s1, s2): the other eight disciples who stay near the grove's edge —
ordinary first-century Galilean men, weathered, SHORT hair or head-cloths, in dun, faded
brown, ochre, olive, grey-blue and deep indigo wool. None in off-white or cream, none
with long hair past the shoulders. Faces shown, tired.

THE ANGEL LOCK (s8 only): an angel from heaven, "strengthening him." A tall, calm robed
figure of warm light standing among the dark olive trees and bending toward the kneeling
man, one steadying hand laid on his shoulder. Rendered with reverence, not spectacle:
soft warm golden radiance breaking the cool blue night, a plain pale robe, no gaudy
wings, no horror, no drama — a gentle heavenly presence. The angel's own face is soft and
gentle, turned down toward the kneeling man, not toward the camera. This warm radiance is
the ONLY strong warm light in the whole video and it belongs to the angel, not to Jesus — who is always seen only from behind, his face never shown, never visible, at any angle.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single continuous scene painted edge to edge." Skip any
comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.
