# PRESCRIPTION — build-90-washing-feet

**Row:** 90   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** John 13:1–17
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 10)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God took the job nobody in the room would touch — and then
  told Peter that being served by him isn't optional. You can't be his until you
  let him do something for you.
- **The turn:** [j1] "If I wash thee not, thou hast no part with me." Peter's refusal
  meets Jesus's insistence, and the stakes turn out to be everything. The hinge.
- **Character shown:** a King who kneels — the greatest one in the room at the
  dirtiest job in the house.
- **Whose face carries it:** **Peter** — the man refusing to be loved (the hinge)
  and the other disciples. Jesus via master ref / his hands at the feet.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Will you let him near enough to do it for you?" No change.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 10 stills
1. [n0] the supper table, Jesus rising with a purpose no master would take — agrees: "did something no master would ever do for his servants"
2. [n1a] Jesus wrapping a towel around his waist, pouring water into a basin — "wrapped a towel around his waist, and poured water into a basin"
3. [n1b] Jesus kneeling, washing a disciple's feet, one by one — "began to wash his disciples' feet — one by one"
4. [n2] Jesus reaching Peter, Peter pulling his foot back — "when he came to Peter, Peter pulled back"
5. [s8] Peter protesting — "Thou shalt never wash my feet"
6. [j1] Jesus answering gently but firmly, his hands at Peter's feet (over-shoulder) — "If I wash thee not, thou hast no part with me"
7. [n2c] close on Peter's face — the man refusing to be loved — "refusing to be loved"
8. [n3] Jesus finished, drying their feet, sitting back down — "dried their feet with the towel... sat back down"
9. [j2] Jesus teaching them to do the same for each other — "ye also ought to wash one another's feet"
10. [n4 + card] the greatest one kneeling at the dirtiest job — the kind of king he is — closing card — "The greatest one in the room knelt at the dirtiest job"
