## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the God you thought was unknown and far off is your Father — nearer than
  any idol, and you are his own offspring.
- **The turn:** p1 — Acts 17:28, "in him we live, and move, and have our being… we are also
  his offspring." The unknown God on the altar is named as the Father who made you.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** *the distant, unknown, indifferent God* — the inherited picture
  of a God far off and unnamed. This cut answers it: he "made the whole world… is not far from
  any of us," and he "had been near the whole time." The unknown God was never unknown to him.
- **Whose face carries it:** PAUL (conviction and gentleness) and the Athenian listeners. No
  divine figure appears — this is Paul at Athens; Jesus does not appear or speak.
- **The card question falls out of:** "You were made to be His. Reach out — He is not far." No
  change needed.
