## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God never promised he would keep them out of the fire — he
  promised something better, that he would be *in* it with them.
- **The turn:** the king's own eyewitness cry — "Lo, I see four men loose... and the
  form of the fourth is like the Son of God." The man who lit the furnace becomes the
  witness that God got in it.
- **Wound answered (silent / absent heaven in suffering):** the fear that God stays
  safely outside your hard thing and leaves you alone in it. Here he meets them inside
  the furnace — "he met them inside it."
- **Whose face carries it:** the KING — his dare, then his astonishment, then his
  confession — and the three men. The fourth figure is the premortal Christ: a radiant
  form among the flames, over-the-shoulder / silhouette, **never a clear face**.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what would change if you believed God meets you
  inside the hard thing, not only on the far side of it?" Lands on the one thing. No change.
