# PRESCRIPTION — build-151-ask-of-god

**Row:** 151   **Shelf:** Member   **Scripture:** James 1:5–6
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 9)

> A member verse with no action — one seeker, one door, one open-handed God. The
> beats are the seeker's journey from asking the crowd to asking God, so each frame
> is a stage of that turn, not a scene of spectacle. God is never a face here — he
> is the light that pours over the kneeling man.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** you do not have to stay stuck with a question too big for you —
  you can go straight to the God who made you, and he gives generously and never scolds.
- **The turn:** the open door (kv5) — the moment the seeker stops asking the tangled
  crowd and asks God himself, directly.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the Restoration began exactly here — a young man took James
  1:5 at its word, asked God, and heaven answered. **GL handoff: Restoration.** (The
  video keeps him unnamed on purpose — milk before meat.)
- **Whose face carries it:** the young seeker — his face at the fork, kneeling, and
  lit from above. God is shown only as light/glory, never a face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "What would you ask him?" — lands straight on
  the one thing.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 9 stills
1. [n1] a person stopped at a fork in the road, weighed down by a question too big — agrees: "a fork in the road, a choice we cannot reason our way through"
2. [n2] the same person surrounded by many talking faces — friends, teachers, a loud crowd — looking more tangled, not less — "most of us ask everyone around us... come away more tangled"
3. [n3] a door standing open, light spilling through it; the person turning toward it — "another door, and it has been standing open the whole time"
4. [kv5] the young man leaving the noise behind, walking into a quiet place apart — "ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally"
5. [n4] the young man kneeling, face upturned, asking aloud like a child to a glad father — "he kneels down, and he asks. Honestly, out loud"
6. [n5,n6] generous light pouring down over his upturned, unashamed face — given openly, not scolded (held through both lines) — "openly and generously, more than the person even came for... he does not scold"
7. [kv6] the man steady, hands clasped, unwavering in faith — "let him ask in faith, nothing wavering"
8. [s6b] a wave of the open sea, driven and tossed by the wind — the wavering the verse names — "like a wave of the sea driven with the wind and tossed"
9. [n8,card] the man risen, resolved, the question handed over — warm hold for the closing question — "it shall be given him... What would you ask him?"
