# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #151: "If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God" (james-1_ask-of-god)

James 1:5-6. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no
motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Restoration** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk (CONTENT-CARE.md). Nothing violent or fearful. Milk
framing: this is GOOD news — when you do not know, you are not stuck. God gives wisdom to
ANYONE who asks, generously and without scolding. The tone is reassurance and warmth, an
open invitation, never pressure or shame.

CHRIST IS NEVER DEPICTED in any frame. God is shown ONLY as warm light from the open sky —
never a figure, never a face, never a shape, never a hand. No divine person is drawn. The
seeker prays and light is given; no one is ever pictured above him.

THE POINT (Why-Law): every one of us hits a question too big for us (s1); asking the crowd
only tangles us in disagreeing answers (s2); but there is another door — go apart and ask
God himself (s3); so the young man kneels and asks plainly (s4, James 1:5); God gives to
ALL who ask, liberally, more than they came for (s5); and He does not upbraid — no shame,
no scolding, only kindness (s6); the one thing asked back is faith, nothing wavering (s7,
James 1:6); and the promise on the end is not "maybe" but "it shall be given" — he rises
with new understanding (s8).

SEEKER LOCK (every shot — the same young man throughout): an earnest young Middle-Eastern
man of about twenty-five, warm olive-brown skin, short dark brown hair and a short dark
beard, honest open face (shown), wearing a plain undyed DUN-and-TAN coarse wool tunic to
the knee with a simple rope or leather belt and worn sandals — earth-toned, never off-white
or cream, no ornament. Consistent face, hair and dress in every frame.

VOICES-OF-THE-CROWD LOCK (s2 only): several ordinary older village men — teachers and
elders — in varied DARKER earth-tone wool robes (deep brown, faded indigo, olive, dun),
beards, faces shown, each gesturing a different direction as if giving conflicting advice.
None in off-white or cream; none resembles the seeker.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or shape of God or Christ anywhere — God is only
warm light; no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no church buildings, steeples, crosses,
logos, or any modern object; no baked-in text, letters or numbers; no gore, no fear, no one
shamed or in peril; no second person dressed in off-white or cream.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.
