## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the ones who found him first were foreigners with no scripture,
  no covenant, and no invitation — and they came to worship. The door was open to
  the nations.
- **The turn:** [j2 / n3] the worship on the dirt floor — kings from the far side of
  the world fall down before a peasant child. That's the hinge.
- **Character shown / wound answered:** a King reachable by outsiders. Herod's false
  "that I may come and worship him also" is the foil that shows how real their
  worship was.
- **Whose face carries it:** the wise men (the seekers) and Herod (the cold contrast).
  Mary and the child via master ref.
- **The card question falls out of:** "The door is open — you're invited to worship too." No change.
