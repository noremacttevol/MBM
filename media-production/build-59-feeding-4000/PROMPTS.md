edge of the Sea of Galilee just visible in the far distance. One continuous open place.
Warm daylight; no night, no lurid sunset.

carried in a simple basket by a disciple (there is no named boy in Mark 8). Bread and fish
shown as plain, wholesome food.

grey-blue wool, short hair and beards, clearly NONE in cream. Faces shown — weary and
doubtful at first, then amazed as they carry the endless food.

THE CROWD (about four thousand): a vast multitude of ordinary weary travellers — men, women
and children in dun, faded brown, ochre, olive, grey-blue, deep indigo and muted red wool,
head-coverings and short hair, footsore and hungry, then satisfied. NONE in off-white or
cream, none with long hair past the shoulders.

darker earth colour with short or covered hair. Check this on every generated frame.
