# Story Video #18 — The Road to Emmaus (Luke 24:13–35)

Runtime 75–90s. Prepend the STYLE BLOCK from 00-MASTER-PLAN.md. Jesus: THE
STRANGER — walking beside them, always framed from behind, over-shoulder, or
just out of focus at frame edge; at the table, only his hands breaking the
bread. The film's trick mirrors the story's: the audience also never quite
sees him until the bread breaks.

## Narration script (app's exact words)

> Two of Jesus's followers walked the road to Emmaus on the worst weekend of
> their lives. Their teacher had been executed. Their hope had died with
> him.
>
> A stranger fell in step beside them and asked what they were discussing.
> They stopped, faces downcast: "Are you the only one who doesn't know what
> happened?"
>
> It was Jesus. Risen. Walking beside them. And they were kept from
> recognizing him — so he simply walked with them, mile after mile, and let
> them pour out the whole story of their broken hope. *(held beat)*
>
> He listened first. Then, starting from what they already knew, he showed
> them what it all meant — and their hearts burned within them, though they
> still didn't know who he was.
>
> Only at the table, when he broke the bread, were their eyes opened. And he
> had been there the entire road. *(peak — music cuts to silence on "were
> their eyes opened")*

Closing card (hold 6s): *"They only recognized him afterward — looking back
at the road. Have you ever looked back at a hard stretch of your life and
wondered if something was walking with you through it?"*

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — The worst weekend
```
Late afternoon: two men walk a long road through open hill country, heads
down, gait heavy, a grief you can read from fifty meters. Long wide shot:
two small figures under a vast indifferent sky. They barely speak. One
kicks a stone.
```

### Shot 2 — A stranger falls in step
```
Over-the-shoulder from behind a third figure who has drawn alongside the
two men — camera stays behind his shoulder, his face never in view. The two
men stop, incredulous at his question; one spreads his hands: how can you
not know? Their grief pours out at this stranger who walks like he has
nowhere else to be.
```

### Shot 3 — Mile after mile, he listened
```
Walking shots through changing golden light: the two men talk, gesture,
break, go silent, resume — unloading everything — while the stranger walks
between and slightly ahead, always from behind, head inclined toward
whoever is speaking. The road unspools. The light lowers and warms.
```

### Shot 4 — Hearts burning
```
Close on the two men's faces as the stranger speaks (his voice, their
faces): confusion softening into attention, attention catching fire into
something they don't have a name for yet — grief physically loosening its
grip on both faces in the sunset light. One presses a hand flat to his own
chest without noticing he's done it.
```

### Shot 5 — Stay with us
```
Blue dusk at a village edge: the stranger's silhouette continues up the
road as if going farther, and the two men call after him, beckoning toward
a lit doorway — urgent, almost pleading, not ready to lose him. He turns
back toward their light.
```

### Shot 6 — The breaking of the bread (the money shot — extra takes)
```
Lamplit table, extreme close up: weathered hands take a flat loaf, bless
it, and BREAK it — and at the exact crack of the crust, warm light swells
in the room. Cut to the two men's faces: recognition detonates on both at
once — eyes flying wide, one knocking over his cup as he stands. Cut back
to the head of the table: the seat is empty, bread still warm, torn open.
```

### Shot 7 — Running back in the dark
```
Night road under stars: the two men RUN back the way they came — miles be
damned — laughing and shouting to each other as they go, retracing in the
dark the same road they'd walked in despair, now the fastest either has
moved in years.
```

### Closing card
Text on cream, serif. No narration.

## The Seed (what this video must leave behind)

He fell in step beside them, unrecognized, listened first, and their hearts BURNED as he spoke. He walked with them the entire road. Quiet question — the biggest seed in the whole set: *if he did that then, does he still? Does God still walk, still speak — or did he stop?* Carried by shots 3, 4, and 6. The burning heart is the recognition his voice leaves behind.

## Red-letter lines (KJV) — the Jesus voice speaks ONLY these

This film's trick is that the stranger's voice IS the Jesus voice — the
audience hears it all along without being told. That is the payoff of one
consistent Jesus voice across every video: viewers who know the other
stories will recognize him before the two disciples do.

1. **Where:** shot 4 (hearts burning), as the stranger's teaching on the
   road.
   **KJV (Luke 24:26):** "Ought not Christ to have suffered these things,
   and to enter into his glory?"
   **Narrator bridge (after, modern):** "Starting from the scriptures they
   already knew, he showed them the suffering wasn't the failure of the
   plan. It WAS the plan — love paying what love costs."
2. The disciples' later line — "Did not our heart burn within us, while he
   talked with us by the way?" — is theirs, not red letter: the narrator
   carries it, modern, over shot 7.
