## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the King they were shouting for arrived quietly, on a borrowed
  farm animal — his kingdom comes in peace, and you're welcome in it.
- **The turn:** [n2 / j1] the crowd naming him King out loud as he rides in on a
  colt. The mismatch — King / donkey — is the whole point.
- **Character shown:** a King who comes "meek," fulfilling the old promise, with
  peace instead of swords.
- **Whose face carries it:** the crowd (their shouting, their hope) and the city
  (its "who is this?"). Jesus on the colt via master ref.
- **The card question falls out of:** "His kingdom is peace. You're welcome in it." No change.
