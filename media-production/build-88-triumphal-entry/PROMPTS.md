STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or
captions in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and
linen in undyed earth colors. No modern objects.

## CONTINUITY LOCKS

[COLT LOCK] = one same small gentle gray donkey colt in every picture it appears
in, dark mane, soft eyes, folded cloaks in earth colors laid across its back as a
saddle; its mother, a slightly larger gray donkey, sometimes walks near. Four legs
and one head per animal, every leg joined correctly.

[ROAD LOCK] = the dusty road down the Mount of Olives toward Jerusalem: olive
trees, low stone walls, the golden city walls and gate ahead; the road strewn with
cloaks in earth reds, browns and blues and with cut green palm branches.

[CROWD LOCK] = the festival crowd: ordinary Judean families of every age in earth
colors, waving cut green palm branches, laying cloaks on the road, faces of open
joy — celebration, never anger, no weapons anywhere.
