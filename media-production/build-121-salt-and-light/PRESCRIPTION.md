# PRESCRIPTION — build-121-salt-and-light

**Row:** 121   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Matthew 5:13–16
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 9)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** you — plain and ordinary as you are — already *are* salt and
  light; you don't have to become something you're not, just don't lose your savour
  and don't hide your lamp.
- **The turn:** the reason Jesus gives for the light — "that they may see your good
  works, and glorify your Father." The point flips from *look at you* to *they'll
  look past you to God*.
- **Wound answered (worth / "I'm nobody"):** the quiet belief that you have nothing
  to offer God or the world. To fishermen, mothers and farmers he says "you matter
  that much" — you are what the world needs kept bright.
- **Whose face carries it:** the CROWD — the plain, unremarkable faces told who they
  are. Jesus is shown teaching, over-the-shoulder onto the listeners.
- **The card question falls out of:** "where could your light give someone else a
  little more of God today?" Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
