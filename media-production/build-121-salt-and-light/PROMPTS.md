# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #121: Salt and Light (matthew-5_salt-and-light)

Matthew 5:13-16 (the Sermon on the Mount). STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — ten painted stills,
no motion clips.

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk. Nothing violent, no judgment framing. This is pure
encouragement: YOU are the salt of the earth and the light of the world — kept bright,
you are exactly what the world needs. The one warning (salt that loses its savour) is
gentle and about usefulness, never fear; the closing card is an invitation to shine, not
a threat.

TIME OF DAY: mostly BRIGHT DAY on a green Galilean hillside (the Sermon on the Mount is
daytime) — the teaching frames (s1, s4, s8, s9) are warm sunlit day. The LIGHT
illustrations are lit so the light can be seen: the city on a hill (s5) is dusk/evening
with its lamps glowing; the two lamp frames (s6, s7) are warm lamplit interiors in a dim
house; the closing (s10) is warm GOLDEN EVENING. State the time of day in every prompt.
No night-black scenes anywhere.

SETTING: a green, flowering hillside above the Sea of Galilee in springtime, the blue
lake and distant hills below; and, for the illustrations, a first-century Galilean home
and a hilltop town.

Jesus appears (face never shown) in s1, s4 and s10 only — the TEACHING frames. In each
the camera sits BEHIND him, over his shoulder, the crowd on the far side facing past the
camera toward him; the back of his head and his long dark hair to us, his face never
shown, never visible, at any angle. Hide him by ANGLE only — never by glow, halo, or
rim-light. The illustration frames (s2, s3, s5, s6, s7, s8, s9) carry NO divine figure —
the salt, the city, the lamps and the ordinary people are painted fully, faces shown.

JESUS LOCK (identical wording in s1, s4, s10; the project-wide look standard): a
first-century Middle-Eastern man, warm olive-brown skin, LONG dark brown-black hair
falling well past the shoulders, one plain undyed off-white cream wool robe (the same
garment every video, a simple Judean tunic with a plain mantle), bare-headed, no hood; no
glow, no halo, no rim-light of any kind. The camera is always behind him or over his
shoulder and his face is never shown, never visible, at any angle.

ONLY JESUS WEARS CREAM (build law, from #40). Jesus is always seen only from behind, his
face never shown; in every frame he appears he is the ONLY figure in an off-white or
cream robe. Every other person wears a clearly darker earth colour, and NOBODY ELSE HAS
LONG HAIR past the shoulders — every other man has SHORT hair or a head-cloth. Both
signatures (long dark hair past the shoulders AND a cream robe) are reserved for him.

THE CROWD LOCK (s1, s4): a warm mixed crowd of ordinary first-century Galileans seated on
the grass listening — men, women, a few children, weathered working people in dun, faded
brown, ochre, olive, grey-blue and maroon wool, many women in soft head-scarves, faces
open and attentive. Not one of them in off-white or cream; not one man with long hair past
the shoulders.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). No writing,
letters or numbers on any object (the model paints gibberish text; keep every surface
blank). Every prompt ends with "One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, no
panels." Skip any comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.

## s1-teaching-hillside — Shot 1: he taught them, saying, Ye are the salt of the earth
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A warm, sunlit green hillside above the blue Sea of Galilee on a clear spring morning, wildflowers in the grass, distant hills soft in the haze. The camera sits DIRECTLY BEHIND a man in a cream robe, low and close to his back, so only the back of his head, his long dark brown-black hair and his shoulders fill the near lower corner of the frame; the back of his head is to us and his face is never shown, never visible, at any angle. He is a Middle-Eastern man with warm olive-brown skin and LONG dark brown-black hair past his shoulders, in one plain undyed off-white cream wool robe, bare-headed, no hood, no glow, no halo, no rim-light, an ordinary solid man seen only from behind, one hand lifted gently as he teaches. Spread on the grass before him, facing past the camera toward him, sits a warm mixed crowd of ordinary Galileans — men, women and a few children in dun, faded brown, ochre, olive, grey-blue and maroon wool, many women in soft head-scarves — their faces painted fully and clearly, open and attentive, drinking in his words. Not one of them wears off-white or cream and not one man has long hair past his shoulders. Bright warm morning sunlight, green hill, blue lake below, no night anywhere. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, no panels.

## s2-salt-of-the-earth — Shot 2: the salt of the earth, good and precious
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A close, warm still-life on a simple wooden table in a sunlit first-century kitchen, soft daylight from a window. A small shallow clay dish holds clean, bright white SALT, and a work-worn hand is sprinkling a pinch of the white salt over a loaf of fresh flatbread and a bowl of simple food. Everything is homely and precious — salt was costly and it preserved food and gave it flavour. The hand belongs to an ordinary Galilean woman in a dun sleeve; her hand and forearm are joined properly, no spare hand appears. No people's faces needed, just the hand, the salt, the bread. Warm domestic daylight, the white salt clean and bright against the earthy table. No writing or letters on anything. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, no panels.

## s3-salt-trodden — Shot 3: if the salt lose its savour, it is trodden under foot
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A plain outdoor doorstep and stone path in flat daylight. A little heap of dull, greyish, spoiled SALT that has lost its savour has been swept out of the house and scattered across the worn stone path, where it is being trodden under the sandalled feet of passers-by — good for nothing now, just grit underfoot. We see the pale dull salt on the grey stones and a sandalled foot stepping over it; the salt is clearly tired and colourless, not the bright clean salt from before. The mood is quiet and matter-of-fact, not frightening — only the picture of something useful that has gone flat and been set aside. Soft flat daylight, grey stone, dull pale salt. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, no panels.

## s4-light-of-the-world — Shot 4: Ye are the light of the world
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The sunlit green hillside again, warm spring daylight, the blue lake and hills wide below. The camera sits OVER THE SHOULDER of the man in the cream robe, the back of his head and his long dark brown-black hair and one shoulder in the near foreground, his face never shown, never visible, at any angle; he has opened one hand toward the wide bright land spread out below, as if to say, all of that — and you. He is the Middle-Eastern man with warm olive-brown skin and LONG dark brown-black hair past his shoulders, in the one plain undyed off-white cream wool robe, bare-headed, no hood, no glow, no halo, no rim-light, seen only from behind. On the far side the crowd of ordinary Galileans in dun, brown, ochre, olive, grey-blue and maroon wool follow his gesture out toward the sunlit valley, their faces painted fully and clearly, lifted and wondering. Not one of them wears cream and not one man has long hair past his shoulders. Bright warm daylight, green hill, blue lake, no night. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, no panels.

## s5-city-on-a-hill — Shot 5: a city set on a hill cannot be hid
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A wide evening view across a darkening valley to a first-century town built high on a distant hilltop, its pale stone houses catching the last warm light and its many small windows and streets beginning to glow with golden lamplight against the deep blue dusk. The town stands out plainly for miles — set up high, it simply cannot be hidden; anyone in the whole valley can see its lights. Rolling hills and a few olive trees in the foreground, the first stars in the deep blue evening sky above. No people needed. Warm golden lamplight of the hilltop town against cool blue dusk, glowing and unmistakable. No writing or letters anywhere. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, no panels.

## s6-lamp-under-bushel — Shot 6: no man lights a candle to put it under a bushel
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The inside of a small, dim first-century Galilean house, the room mostly in shadow. A single small clay oil lamp with a little flame has been set down on the floor and covered over with an upturned earthenware BUSHEL-BASKET, so its light is trapped and smothered underneath — only a thin, sad glow leaks out at the rim, and the whole room stays dark and useless. A hand has just set the basket down over it. The picture makes the mistake obvious: a good light, hidden, helping no one. Dim shadowed interior, one small trapped glow under the basket. No writing or letters anywhere. Two arms, two hands where the figure shows. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, no panels.

## s7-lamp-on-a-stand — Shot 7: but on a candlestick, and it gives light to all in the house
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same small first-century house, now warm and glowing. The single clay oil lamp has been lifted up and set high on a wooden LAMPSTAND, and from up there its flame throws warm golden light into every corner of the room, filling the whole home. A small family — an ordinary Galilean father, mother and two children in dun, olive and maroon wool, faces painted fully and clearly, warm and glad — sit together within the good light, eating and talking. Not one of them wears cream. The contrast with the hidden lamp is the whole point: lifted up, the same little flame lights everyone. Warm golden lamplight filling the room, cosy and bright. No writing or letters anywhere. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, no panels.

## s8-let-your-light-shine — Shot 8: let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A warm, sunlit village lane in the daytime, ordinary first-century Galileans doing plain acts of kindness out in the open where others can see: a young man in dun wool helping an old woman in a grey head-scarf carry a heavy water jar, another sharing a loaf of bread with a ragged traveller, a woman lifting a fallen child to its feet. Their faces are painted fully and clearly, kind and unshowy — good works done gladly, in the daylight, visible to all. Every one of them wears an earth colour, none in off-white or cream, none of the men with long hair past the shoulders. Bright warm daylight, a plain village lane, the good deeds plainly in view. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, no panels.

## s9-glorify-your-father — Shot 9: that they may glorify your Father which is in heaven
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Warm late-afternoon light on the village, the people who received and did those kindnesses now pausing together, some looking up toward the bright open sky in quiet gratitude and worship — their good works have turned everyone's eyes not to themselves but upward, to God. Ordinary Galileans in dun, ochre, olive and maroon wool, a mother with a child, an old man, a young couple, their faces painted fully and clearly, moved and thankful, faces turned up to the warm light of the sky. None in off-white or cream, none of the men with long hair past the shoulders. The mood is glad and reverent, the light pouring down warmly from above. Warm golden afternoon light, faces lifted, open bright sky. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, no panels.

## s10-carry-it-out — Shot 10: go and be salt and light in the world
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The green hillside at warm golden evening, the teaching finished, the crowd rising and starting down the slope toward their villages in the valley, carrying what they have heard out into the world. The camera sits BEHIND the man in the cream robe and a little to the side, only the back of his head, his long dark brown-black hair and his cream-robed shoulders showing, his face never shown, never visible, at any angle, one hand lifted in gentle blessing as they go. He is a Middle-Eastern man with warm olive-brown skin and LONG dark brown-black hair past his shoulders, in the one plain undyed off-white cream wool robe, bare-headed, no hood, no glow, no halo, no rim-light, seen only from behind. The ordinary Galileans move away down the golden hillside toward the little towns below where evening lamps are beginning to glow, in dun, brown, ochre, olive, grey-blue and maroon wool, short hair or head-scarves, none in cream, none of the men with long hair past the shoulders, their faces warm and resolved. Warm golden evening light, green hill, the valley towns glowing softly below, no night-black anywhere. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, no panels.
