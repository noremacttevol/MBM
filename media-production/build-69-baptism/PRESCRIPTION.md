# PRESCRIPTION — build-69-baptism

**Row:** 69   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Matthew 3:13–17
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 13)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he walked through the door first — baptized though he had nothing
  to wash away — so no one who followed would ever be asked to do what he had not done;
  and the Father approved him *before* he had preached or healed a thing.
- **The turn:** the heavens breaking open and the Father's voice — identity first,
  approval before achievement.
- **Character shown:** he never leads from behind; and he is the Beloved Son the Father
  delights in before any work is done — grace, not earning.
- **Whose face carries it:** JOHN at the refusal (the wild man who feared nobody, humbled),
  and the CROWD on the bank watching the sky open. Jesus at the water via master ref,
  face lifted; his face is not the emotional carrier of the voice — the witnesses are.
- **The card question falls out of:** "he walked through the door first, so you'd never
  face one he hadn't — the way in is still open." No change needed.
