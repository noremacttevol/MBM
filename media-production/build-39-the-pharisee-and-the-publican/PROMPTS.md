# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #39: The Pharisee and the Publican (luke-18_pharisee-and-publican)

Luke 18:9-14. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — fourteen painted stills, no motion clips.

Time of day is the SAME in every shot: a bright, clear first-century MORNING — the
hour of prayer and sacrifice at the temple. Never night, never sunset, never
golden-hour drama.

Verses 10-13 are a PARABLE Jesus is telling. Inside the parable there is no divine
figure of any kind: the Pharisee, the publican, and the temple crowd are ordinary
people, drawn fully, faces shown. Jesus himself appears in only TWO shots — s1 (he
tells the parable) and s9 (he gives the verdict) — and in both the camera stands
directly behind him, the back of his head to us, never any view of his face.

JESUS LOCK (identical wording in s1 and s9; the project-wide look standard):
a first-century Middle-Eastern man, warm olive-brown skin, LONG dark brown-black hair
falling well past the shoulders, one plain undyed off-white cream wool robe (a simple
Judean tunic with a plain mantle, the same garment in every shot), bare-headed with no
hood; no glow, no bright ring, no outlining light of any kind; an ordinary solid man in ordinary
daylight. The camera is always directly behind him and his face is never shown.

PHARISEE LOCK (s2, s3, s5, s5b, s8, s11) — a dignified, well-fed Middle-Eastern man of about
fifty, upright and calm, with a neatly trimmed grey-streaked beard, wearing a fine pale
cream-and-ivory robe under a wide blue-and-white striped prayer shawl with long knotted
tassels at its corners, and a broad dark leather prayer band bound across the forehead.
He is genuinely devout and widely respected, dignified and self-assured — never a
sneering cartoon villain, never comic. His face IS shown, consistent in every shot.

PUBLICAN LOCK (s2, s4, s5b, s6, s7, s7b, s8, s10) — a stocky Middle-Eastern man of about forty,
short dark hair, close-cropped dark beard, wearing a good-quality plain rust-brown
terracotta tunic with a dark leather belt and a worn leather money pouch at his hip. He
is a well-off man, not a beggar — clean, decently dressed, and utterly wrecked with
shame. His face IS shown, consistent in every shot. He never lifts his eyes upward in
any temple shot; he looks down.

THE CERTAIN MEN LOCK (s1, s9) — six well-dressed religious men in fine pale robes with
long tasselled prayer shawls and broad dark leather prayer bands bound across their
foreheads, the same six men in both shots. Their faces ARE shown.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single continuous scene painted edge to edge." Skip any
comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.

## s1-the-certain-men — Shot 1: he spake this parable unto certain which trusted in themselves
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A sunlit first-century Judean street beside the great temple wall, bright clear morning. The camera stands DIRECTLY BEHIND Jesus and close to his back, so that only the back of his head, his long dark hair and his shoulders fill the lower near edge of the frame; the back of his head is to us and his face is never shown, never visible, at no angle, from no direction. He is a Middle-Eastern man with warm olive-brown skin and LONG dark brown-black hair falling well past his shoulders, wearing one plain undyed off-white cream wool robe, bare-headed, with no hood, no glow, no bright ring and no outlining light of any kind — an ordinary solid man standing in ordinary morning daylight, seen only from behind. Filling the frame beyond him, and looking past the camera straight toward him, stand six well-dressed religious men in fine pale robes with long tasselled prayer shawls and broad dark leather prayer bands bound across their foreheads. The six men are painted fully and clearly, every one of them turned toward the figure in the foreground: chins lifted, arms folded, calm and satisfied and faintly amused — the look of men who are certain they are the good ones and that other people are not. Warm gold morning light, dust in the air. Every figure has two arms and two hands. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s2-two-men-go-up — Shot 2: two men went up into the temple to pray
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The great stone temple steps in bright clear morning light, seen from the TOP of the staircase looking back and down along it, so the whole flight of steps falls away below us toward the city rooftops far beneath. Two men are CLIMBING UP the steps toward us, ascending, both still far below the top and far apart from one another, with other worshippers also making their way up. Every figure on the stairs is moving upward, away from the city below and toward the temple behind the viewer. Climbing the open central stair, mounting the steps tall and unhurried with his head high, is a dignified well-fed Middle-Eastern man of about fifty with a neatly trimmed grey-streaked beard, in a fine pale cream-and-ivory robe under a wide blue-and-white striped prayer shawl with long knotted tassels, a broad dark leather prayer band bound across the forehead; people give him room and glance at him with respect. Far below and over at the outer edge of the steps, hugging the wall, climbing slowly upward with his shoulders hunched and his head down, is a stocky Middle-Eastern man of about forty with short dark hair and a close-cropped beard, in a good-quality plain rust-brown terracotta tunic with a dark leather belt and a worn leather money pouch at his hip; the people near him lean away from him. Both faces are fully painted and clear. The wide empty distance between the two men is the heart of the composition. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s5-pharisee-prays — Shot 5: the Pharisee stood and prayed thus with himself
