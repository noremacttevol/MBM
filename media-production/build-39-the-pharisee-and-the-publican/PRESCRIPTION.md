# PRESCRIPTION — build-39-the-pharisee-and-the-publican

**Row:** 39   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Luke 18:9–14
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 13)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the despised man "went down to his house justified" — the door is
  not closed to people who have done wrong; it only closes from the inside, by people
  sure they don't need it.
- **The turn:** "this man went down to his house justified rather than the other." The
  hinge is Jesus's verdict reversing everything the watchers assumed.
- **Character shown / wound answered:** answers both the "I'm too far gone" fear and
  the "I'm one of the good ones" blindness — one man came holding a list, the other
  holding nothing, and only one was actually asking.
- **Whose face carries it:** the PUBLICAN (broken, head down, striking his chest) and
  the PHARISEE (self-satisfied, standing tall). No Jesus face except the teller frame.
- **The card question falls out of:** "If you stopped performing for a moment, what
  would you actually say to him?" No change needed.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 13 stills
1. [s9/n1] Jesus teaching the self-assured — those sure they were righteous and looked down on others — agrees: "certain which trusted in themselves that they were righteous, and despised others"
2. [jv10/n2] two men climbing the temple steps to pray — a Pharisee and a tax collector — "Two men went up into the temple to pray"
3. [n3] the Pharisee — genuinely devout: fasting twice a week, tithing everything, the city's "best man" — "every finger would have pointed at him"
4. [n4] the tax collector — a traitor with a money box, collecting for Rome from his own neighbors, and he knew it — "A traitor with a money box. And he knew it"
5. [n5/j1] the Pharisee praying out in the open where he can be seen — "God, I thank thee, that I am not as other men are"
6. [n6] close on his self-satisfied face — a list, not a plea; even glancing down at the publican — "he says the word I five times... stepping on the man behind him"
7. [jv13a/n7/j2] the tax collector at the very back, head down, striking his chest — "God be merciful to me a sinner"
8. [n8a] behind him, the lamb of sacrifice at the temple; he asks that mercy fall on him, personally — "a lamb is being killed for the sins of the whole nation, and he is asking God to let that mercy fall on him"
9. [n9] the two men side by side — one doing all the talking, the other barely able to speak — "Two men. The same temple. The same God"
10. [j3/n10] Jesus's verdict — "this man went down to his house justified rather than the other"
11. [n11] the publican walking home a different man — nothing to offer, and that was enough — "he had not done one thing to earn it"
12. [n12] the Pharisee still standing there — certain, fine, alone — the saddest part — "The only thing keeping that man out was how sure he was that he was already in"
13. [n13/card] the door only closes from the inside — Jesus letting the good men in too; warm hold — "It only closes from the inside, by people convinced they do not need it"
