## The impact read
- **The one thing:** you were handed an ocean of mercy you could never repay — so choking someone over a cupful of it is the one thing this King cannot bear.
- **The turn:** the king's heart breaking with compassion and cancelling the whole impossible debt (n7→n8) — the reversal every viewer must feel, so the servant's cruelty a moment later lands. The mirror hinge is "O thou wicked servant" (j5).
- **Character shown (in the King who stands for the Father):** mercy that wipes out what could never be paid, and grief when the forgiven refuse to forgive.
- **Whose face carries it:** the FORGIVEN SERVANT — his begging face before the king, then his cruelty at the throat of a fellow servant; the fellow servant begging with the very same words; the king's compassion and then his anger. (Parable — the faces are the characters, not literal Jesus.)
- **The card question falls out of:** "Who is holding a small one against you — that you could let go of today?" — falls straight out of the one thing. No change needed.
