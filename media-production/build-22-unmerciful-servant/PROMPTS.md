# FLOW PROMPTS — Story Video #22: The Unmerciful Servant (matthew-18_unmerciful-servant)

Matthew 18:21-35. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) + Jesus face-never (the Face Law): the
Lord is shown only from behind, his back to the camera and his face turned away,
never shown. Nano Banana 2, tall vertical 9:16, x2, download 1K. Hand-painted 2D
storybook style (NOT photorealistic).

This is a PARABLE Jesus tells. The story's characters are a KING and his
SERVANTS — ordinary people, their faces ARE shown and kept consistent so the
viewer can follow who is who. Jesus himself is only the storyteller who frames
it: he appears in ONE bookend opening shot (s1) seated among the disciples,
shown only from directly behind, his back to the camera and his face turned
away, never shown. Every other still (s2-s8) has NO Jesus figure at all — the
simplest, safest reading of the Face Law. Passes `jesus_face_gate.py`.

**CHARACTER LOCKS (keep consistent across every still they appear in):**
- **The King (s2, s4, s8)** — a stern, dignified Near-Eastern king in a deep
  royal-purple robe with gold-embroidered trim, a broad gold collar and a heavy
  gold ring, a greying dark beard, seated on a carved cedar-and-gold seat on a
  raised stone dais. His face IS shown; keep it the same man each time.
- **The first servant / the unmerciful one (s2, s3, s4, s5, s6, s8)** — the man
  we follow. A lean man of about forty in a belted RUST-BROWN wool tunic, close-
  cropped dark hair, short dark stubble, worn leather sandals. His face IS shown
  and stays the same across every shot — desperate when he begs (s3), overwhelmed
  when forgiven (s4), then hard and cruel in the street (s5, s6), then cowering
  (s8).
- **The fellow servant / the poorer man (s5, s6)** — a younger, thinner man in a
  plain undyed pale-grey flax tunic, bare-headed, barefoot, gentle worn face.
  His face IS shown.
- **The other household servants (s2, s7)** — several servants in muted plain
  brown and grey tunics carrying account scrolls and clay tablets.
- **The guards (s8)** — two hard men in leather jerkins with plain wooden staves.
- **The Lord / the storyteller (s1 ONLY)** — one plain undyed cream wool robe,
  long dark hair past the shoulders, warm Middle-Eastern; shown only from
  directly behind, his back to the camera and his face turned away, never shown.
  No halo, no glow.

**STILL STYLE BLOCK** (prepend to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, warm golden light, earth-toned palette of first-century Judea. Sacred,
gentle tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions in the image. Historically
modest rough-woven wool and linen robes in undyed earth colors. No modern
objects.

**ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE** (append to every prompt, exactly — Nano Banana tiles
without it):
ONE SINGLE CONTINUOUS SCENE painted edge to edge as one unbroken composition
filling the entire tall vertical 9:16 frame — NOT divided into panels, NOT a
comic strip, just one single picture.

## s1 — Shot 1: Peter's question (the only Jesus beat, from behind)
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A warm first-century Galilean courtyard in soft golden
afternoon light. Peter — a broad Galilean fisherman with thick dark curly hair and a
full dark beard, in his locked BLUE-GREY tunic (never cream), the same man as the
attached peter reference — leans in earnestly, one hand open, mid-question. A small
knot of other plainly dressed disciples gathers close around him, quietly
listening. They are all turned toward a seated teacher, the Lord, resting on a
low stone step at the near edge of the frame, shown only from directly behind
with his back fully to the camera and his face turned away, never shown — one
plain undyed cream wool robe, long dark hair past the shoulders, warm
Middle-Eastern, no bright ring and no glow. The whole gathering leans toward him in the
honeyed light. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s2 — Shot 2: the king settles accounts
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A grand torch-lit palace hall of pale stone columns and
hanging oil lamps. A stern, dignified Near-Eastern king in a deep royal-purple
robe with gold-embroidered trim, a broad gold collar and a greying dark beard,
sits on a carved cedar-and-gold seat on a raised stone dais, a long table of
account scrolls and clay tablets before him. Household servants in muted brown
and grey tunics bring ledgers forward. In the center, guards lead one lean man of
about forty in a belted rust-brown tunic — the first servant — forward to stand
before the king to answer for his debt. Warm lamplight, deep shadows, a hush of
reckoning. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s3 — Shot 3: he falls down and begs
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same palace hall, closer now. The first servant — the
lean man in the belted rust-brown tunic, close-cropped dark hair — has thrown
himself down on his knees on the stone floor before the raised dais, both hands
lifted in desperate pleading, his whole body bent low in fear and begging. Around
him the scattered account scrolls of an impossible debt. His face is stricken,
desperate. The tall dais and the king's seat loom above him in warm lamplight.
[ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s4 — Shot 4: the king's compassion — the debt forgiven
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The palace hall bathed in a soft golden glow. The king in his
deep royal-purple robe with gold trim and greying dark beard leans forward from
his carved seat, his face moved with warm compassion, one hand extended in
mercy — the other hand letting a large account scroll fall away, released, torn
and forgiven. Below him the first servant in the rust-brown tunic kneels
overwhelmed, hands pressed to his chest, disbelief and relief flooding his face.
The whole hall feels lifted, warm, unburdened. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s5 — Shot 5: outside, he seizes his fellow servant by the throat
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A narrow dusty sunlit street of stone and mud-brick just
outside the palace gate. The same first servant — the lean man in the belted
rust-brown tunic, his face now hard and furious — has seized a younger, thinner
fellow servant in a plain pale-grey flax tunic by the throat with both hands,
shoving him back against a wall, snarling and demanding payment. The poorer man
claws helplessly at the gripping hands, frightened and choking. Harsh bright
daylight, dust, cruelty where mercy should have been. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s6 — Shot 6: the same plea — refused
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same dusty street. The younger fellow servant in the
plain pale-grey flax tunic has fallen to his knees in the dirt, both hands lifted
in the very same desperate plea for patience — a mirror of the first servant's
own begging. But the first servant, still in his rust-brown tunic, stands over
him unmoved, arms folded, face cold and pointing toward a heavy iron-barred
prison door in a stone wall behind them. No mercy in him at all. Hard midday
light. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s7 — Shot 7: the other servants grieve and go tell the king
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A shadowed stone doorway and colonnade near the street. A
small group of the other household servants in muted brown and grey tunics stand
half-hidden, having witnessed the cruelty — their faces stricken with grief and
dismay, some clutching each other, two of them already turning to hurry back
toward the palace to tell their lord what they saw. Behind them, softly out of
focus, the first servant walks away unbothered. Somber, heavy light. [ANTI-PANEL
CLAUSE]

## s8 — Shot 8: the king's judgment
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The grand palace hall again, now cold and severe. The king in
his deep royal-purple robe with gold trim and greying dark beard has risen to his
full height on the dais, his face stern and grieved with righteous anger, one arm
outstretched in judgment. Below him the first servant in the rust-brown tunic
cowers small and terrified on the stone floor, and two hard guards in leather
jerkins with plain wooden staves close in to take him away. Long shadows, a cold
blue light cutting the warm lamplight. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]
