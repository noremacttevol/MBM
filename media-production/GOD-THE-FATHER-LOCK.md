# GOD THE FATHER — CHARACTER LOCK

**Locked by Cameron, 2026-07-18.** Companion to the JESUS LOCK. Paste the lock
paragraph byte-identical into EVERY prompt where the Father appears, exactly the
way `JESUS LOCK v3` is pasted, so his look never drifts between videos.

---

## Why this exists

Cameron, denial #113: *"God has a body, weve bren through this and hopwfully you
have created a character for him as well so his look doesnt change much like Jesus
and other famous characters that will be ilustrated multiple times."*

Several builds carried a rule saying the Father is **never** depicted — that he is
"a voice and a warm seeking PRESENCE", shown only as formless golden light, with
"no figure, no face and no shape". Build #179 said the same and additionally
ended its vision shot with "One figure only", which is why Stephen's vision could
not show what Stephen actually saw. **That rule is wrong and is removed.** God the
Father has a body of flesh and bone, and he is painted as a person.

Because he recurs across videos, he needs one fixed look — the same reason Jesus
has a master lock. Without it every video invents a different Father.

## THE LOCK (paste verbatim, never reworded)

> GOD THE FATHER LOCK: the SAME glorified man in every picture — a majestic
> embodied man of about sixty, warm tan olive-brown skin, full silver-white hair
> to his shoulders and a full silver-white beard, calm deep-set eyes, serene and
> kingly and full of love, wearing a plain flowing pure-white robe (only the
> Father wears pure white; Jesus alone wears cream). No crown, no throne-props, no
> halo ring, no glow around the head. He is a real painted person, never a cloud,
> never formless light, never a shape in the brightness.

---

## Rules of use

1. **He has a body.** Never "light only", never "no figure", never "unformed
   brightness standing in for him".
2. **Wardrobe separates the persons.** Father = pure white. Jesus = cream. Nobody
   else wears either.
3. **When both appear** (Acts 7:55-56, the First Vision pattern), they are TWO
   distinct separate persons with clear space between them, and Jesus stands on
   the Father's OWN right hand — since both face the viewer, that puts Jesus on
   the LEFT of the picture and the Father on the RIGHT.
4. **Never** a dove, triangle, all-seeing eye or any Trinitarian symbol.
5. **Old Testament caution — ask before assuming.** In Latter-day Saint teaching
   the God who walks and speaks in the Old Testament is usually **Jehovah, the
   premortal Christ**, not the Father. So an embodied divine figure in Eden or on
   Sinai should normally use the JESUS lock, not this one. Where a build is
   unclear, ask Cameron which member of the Godhead the scene shows rather than
   guessing.
