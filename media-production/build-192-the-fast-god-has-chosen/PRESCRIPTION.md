> Isaiah 58 is one unbroken divine oracle that redefines fasting. The two quoted
> verses each walk through several concrete mercies — loosing chains, feeding the
> hungry, taking the poor home, covering the naked — every one a distinct thing the
> words make you see, switched mid-segment. The card stays out of BEATS.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the fast God actually wants isn't an empty stomach to look
  holy — it's a hand opened to someone hurting, and it's the doer whose own light rises.
- **The turn:** "Is not THIS the fast that I have chosen?" — God takes fasting away
  from self-denial and hands it back as mercy.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the true fast is an offering that reaches the poor and
  hurting. Hands off to **Gospel Library: Fasting**.
- **Whose face carries it:** the hungry and oppressed being helped, then the doer's
  own face as their light breaks. No face for God.
- **The card question falls out of:** "open your hand to the hurting — He meets you
  there." No change needed.
