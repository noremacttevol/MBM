# PRESCRIPTION — build-146-vine-and-branches

**Row:** 146   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** John 15
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Length:** pace to the story (T1 band 45–75s)

> Bridge wound: **the exhausting religion of performance — a God who demands you
> produce.** The inherited picture is a treadmill: bear fruit or you're cut off, and
> it's all on you. The video answers it with his own image: a branch doesn't strain,
> it stays attached, and the life of the vine does the rest. Connection, not effort.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he isn't asking you to produce for him — he's offering you the
  connection that produces. Stay attached; the life flows through.
- **The turn:** "without me ye can do nothing" set against "abide in me, and I in you"
  — the whole weight shifts off your effort and onto staying joined to him.
- **Wound answered:** the dead-religion / performance wound — a cut branch doesn't dry
  up because it's weak, but because it's disconnected. He offers connection, not a quota.
- **Whose face carries it:** the BRANCHES — his friends, part of the same living thing;
  the fruit and the withered branch carry the contrast. Jesus only via master ref.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Stay connected to the Vine. Let His life flow
  through you." Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
