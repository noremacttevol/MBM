## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God does not run low on generosity when he spends it on someone who came late — there is a full day's welcome waiting no matter what hour you finally come in.
- **The turn:** the last-hired men, who worked one hour, each handed a full day's pay (n8) — the moment that exposes the dawn crew's real grievance: grace someone else didn't earn.
- **Character shown:** goodness that is never used up, and that the first men mistook for unfairness — "Is thine eye evil, because I am good?"
- **Whose face carries it:** the LAST-HIRED LABORERS (never picked, standing idle) and the grumbling DAWN CREW; the landowner's face when he calls the grumbler "friend." (Parable — the landowner is Jesus's stand-in.)
- **The card question falls out of:** "You have not missed your chance by coming late. Will you come into the vineyard now?" — falls straight out of the one thing. No change needed.
