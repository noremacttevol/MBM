# FLOW PROMPTS — Story Video #26: The Mustard Seed (matthew-13_mustard-seed)

Matthew 13:31-32. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) + the Face Law: this is a parable Jesus
tells, and NO Jesus figure appears on screen at all — his face is never shown
because he is simply not depicted here; only the parable's own characters and the
growing tree are painted. Nano Banana 2, tall vertical 9:16, x2, download 1K.
Hand-painted 2D storybook style (NOT photorealistic). Passes `jesus_face_gate.py`.

**CHARACTER / PROP LOCKS (keep consistent across every still they appear in):**
- **The farmer / sower (s1, s2)** — an ordinary Judean farmer of about forty, warm
  brown skin, dark hair and a short dark beard, a simple undyed sandy-brown rough
  wool tunic with a rope belt, worn sandals. His face IS shown; keep him the same
  man. He is the only human figure in the video.
- **The mustard seed (s1, s2)** — a single tiny round pale seed, small as a speck,
  the smallest of seeds.
- **The mustard tree (s5, s6)** — a large, tall, broadly spreading mustard tree with
  masses of small green leaves and sprays of tiny yellow blossoms, wide sheltering
  branches. It grows in the field, dwarfing a low dry-stone wall.
- **The birds (s6)** — several small brown wild songbirds perching and nesting in
  the tree's broad branches.
- **The field** — a modest terraced Judean field of tilled brown soil bordered by a
  low dry-stone wall, soft hills and a warm sky beyond.

**STILL STYLE BLOCK** (prepend to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, warm golden light, earth-toned palette of first-century Judea. Sacred,
gentle tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions in the image. Historically
modest rough-woven wool and linen robes in undyed earth colors. No modern objects.

**ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE** (append to every prompt, exactly):
ONE SINGLE CONTINUOUS SCENE painted edge to edge as one unbroken composition
filling the entire tall vertical 9:16 frame — NOT divided into panels, NOT a comic
strip, just one single picture.

## s1 — Shot 1: the tiny seed in his palm
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A close, tender view of an ordinary Judean farmer's open,
weathered palm — warm brown skin — cupped and holding a single tiny round pale
mustard seed, small as a speck, catching the warm sunlight. Behind the softly
blurred hand, a hint of his sandy-brown tunic and the earth-toned field. The whole
image says how astonishingly small this one seed is. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s2 — Shot 2: he sows it in the field
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A modest terraced Judean field of freshly tilled brown soil
bordered by a low dry-stone wall, soft hills beyond. The same farmer — warm brown
skin, dark hair and short dark beard, sandy-brown rough wool tunic with a rope belt
— kneels and presses the single tiny mustard seed down into a small hole in the
dark soil with one finger, careful and hopeful. Just one man, one seed, one patch
of open ground. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s3 — Shot 3: the first tiny sprout
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A very close view of the dark tilled soil of the same field,
where a single tiny, tender green sprout — just two small new leaves on a slender
pale stem — has pushed up through the earth into the morning light. Dew on the
young leaf, crumbs of dark soil around it. New life, impossibly small, beginning.
[ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s4 — Shot 4: the young shrub grows
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same field, later. The mustard plant has grown into a
healthy waist-high young shrub, leafing out full and green with the first sprays of
tiny yellow blossoms, standing where the seed was sown, the low dry-stone wall and
hills behind it in warm daylight. It is clearly growing far beyond what such a small
seed should become. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s5 — Shot 5: the great tree
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same field at golden hour. Where the tiny seed was planted
now stands a great, tall, broadly spreading mustard tree — masses of small green
leaves and sprays of tiny yellow blossoms, wide sheltering branches — glowing in
the warm evening light, dwarfing the low dry-stone wall and rising against the soft
hills and golden sky. The largest plant in the whole field, grown from the smallest
of seeds. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s6 — Shot 6: the birds lodge in its branches
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A warm, close view up into the broad, leafy branches of the
great mustard tree, dappled with golden light. Several small brown wild songbirds
perch and settle into little nests cradled among the wide sheltering branches and
yellow blossoms, at home and safe. Tender and full of quiet life. [ANTI-PANEL
CLAUSE]
