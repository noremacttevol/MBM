- **Character shown:** he does enormous things from the smallest of starts; the kingdom almost never begins big.
- **Whose face carries it:** no human witness — the SEED in the palm and the grown TREE carry it; the picture is the seed and the growth.
- **The card question falls out of:** "What small seed is he asking you to plant today?" — falls straight out of the one thing. No change needed.
