# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #153: "The times of restitution of all things" (acts-3_restitution-of-all-things)

Acts 3:19-21. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no
motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Restoration** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk (CONTENT-CARE.md). Nothing violent or fearful. Milk
framing: this is GOOD news — the world is not just winding down into ruin. God has
promised to set ALL things right, a great restoration foretold by every prophet. Warmth,
relief, and hope; an open invitation to turn and be refreshed, never dread.

CHRIST IS NEVER DEPICTED in any frame. Heaven and God are shown ONLY as warm light from
the open sky — never a figure, never a face, never a shape, never a hand. No divine person
is drawn.

THE POINT (Why-Law): after the healing a crowd gathers and Peter stands to preach (s1);
he calls them to turn back to God (s2); turning brings times of refreshing, like rain on
dry ground (s3, s4 — Acts 3:19); but a far larger promise stands — a day when all things
are set right (s5); heaven keeps that promise until the appointed time (s5); every prophet
since the beginning foretold this restoring (s6); the restitution of all things: broken
made whole, made new (s7 — Acts 3:21); so turn, and be part of the refreshing to come (s8).

PETER LOCK (s1, s2, s4 — the same apostle): the apostle Peter, a sturdy Middle-Eastern
man of about fifty, weathered sun-browned face, short grey-and-brown beard, short greying
hair, strong kind features, wearing a simple fisherman's tunic and cloak of undyed DUN and
dark BROWN wool with a leather belt — dark earth tones, never white, cream, off-white or
pale. His face is always shown, earnest and warm. Consistent in every shot he appears.

CROWD LOCK (s1, s2, s3, s7, s8 where people appear): ordinary first-century Israelites of
every age — men, women and children in undyed DARKER earth-tone wool (dun, faded brown,
olive, ochre, grey), faces shown, honest and moved; never in off-white or cream, never
resembling Peter.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or shape of God or Christ anywhere — heaven is only
warm light; no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no church buildings, steeples,
crosses, logos, or any modern object; no baked-in text, letters or numbers; no gore, no
fear, no one shamed or in peril; no second person dressed in off-white or cream.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.
