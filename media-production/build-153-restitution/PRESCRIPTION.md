## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the world is not sliding into ruin — God has planned a restitution
  of all things, and turning back to him brings the refreshing now.
- **The turn:** kv21 / n6 — "the times of restitution of all things... spoken by all his
  holy prophets since the world began." The story lifts from one man's fresh start to
  the whole world set right.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the restoration of all things, foretold by every prophet. **GL
  handoff: Restoration.**
- **Whose face carries it:** Peter (fisherman turned preacher) and the healed man; no
  Jesus's face — he is named in the command, not pictured.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Will you be ready for the restoring?" — lands on
  the one thing.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 10 stills
1. [n1] a crowd running up amazed at the temple gate; Peter rising among them to speak — agrees: "a crowd came running, amazed... Peter... stood up among them"
2. [s6] Peter reaching his hand down to the lame beggar at the gate, commanding in Jesus' name — "In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth rise up and walk"
3. [n1b] the man who had never walked rising to his feet; Peter's empty hands — "a man who had never walked to stand up"
4. [n2] Peter preaching to the crowd: turn back to God, change your direction — "Turn back to God... Change your direction"
5. [n3,kv19] the refreshing — cool rain falling on cracked ground, sins blotted out — "Like cool rain on cracked ground... your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come"
6. [n4] a larger vision — the whole broken world being set right, a healing of all things — "everything that has gone wrong with the world would finally be set right"
7. [n5] heaven holding the promise safe, like the best gift kept back for exactly the right moment — "heaven itself is keeping the promise safe"
8. [n6,kv21] a long line of prophets since the beginning, all promising the same restoring — "spoken by the mouth of all his holy prophets since the world began"
9. [n7] something broken made whole and new — restitution, the way it was always meant to be — "made whole, made new, every part of it"
10. [n8,card] a face turned toward God, ready to be part of the refreshing — warm hold for the closing question — "Will you be ready for the restoring?"
