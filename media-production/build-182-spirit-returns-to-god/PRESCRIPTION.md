## The impact read
- **The one thing:** death is not an ending but a return — your spirit goes home to the
  God who lent it, and he receives it with mercy.
- **The turn:** Ecclesiastes 12:7 — "the spirit shall return unto God who gave it." The
  chapter turns from a lament about aging into the round trip home. That is the hinge.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the plan of salvation — the spirit's round trip, from God and
  back to God. Hands off to **Gospel Library → Plan of Salvation**.
- **Whose face carries it:** the ORDINARY person — the aging one, and the young one urged
  to remember now. God is shown as receiving light (s0, s1 are SCRIPTURE-blue —
  Solomon writing *about* God, never God speaking), never a face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "You were given breath by God. He is ready to
  receive it — and ready to give you more." Lands on the one thing. No change.
