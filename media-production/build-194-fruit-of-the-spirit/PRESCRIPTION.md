## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the Spirit's evidence isn't strain or effort — it's character
  quietly ripening, and no law anywhere stands against it.
- **The turn:** Galatians 5:22–23 — the nine named, landing on "against such there is
  no law." The point is these are the things that can't be overdone.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** you recognize the Holy Ghost working in you by its fruit, not
  by feelings alone. Hands off to **Gospel Library: Holy Ghost**.
- **Whose face carries it:** a person in whom the fruit is ripening — the witness, not Jesus.
- **The card question falls out of:** "ask the Spirit — and let it ripen in you." No change needed.
