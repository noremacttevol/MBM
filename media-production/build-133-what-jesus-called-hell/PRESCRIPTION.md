## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the hell Jesus warned about was a real burning valley he used as a
  picture — and the dungeon-of-endless-torture image never came from him.
- **The turn:** "notice what he did NOT say" — the pivot from the inherited horror
  picture to the shepherd/father God who warns the way a parent grabs a child off a cliff.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** "one heaven, one chance, one size" / the God-is-a-jailer
  fear. Answers it with Jesus's own words and the real geography of Gehenna.
- **Whose face carries it:** the LISTENERS hearing him — recognition, then relief.
  Jesus only via master ref / over-the-shoulder; never his face over the fire imagery.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Who taught you to fear him more than to trust him?"
