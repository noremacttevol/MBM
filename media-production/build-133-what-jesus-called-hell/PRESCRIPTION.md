## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the hell Jesus warned about was a real burning valley he used as a
  picture — and the dungeon-of-endless-torture image never came from him.
- **The turn:** "notice what he did NOT say" — the pivot from the inherited horror
  picture to the shepherd/father God who warns the way a parent grabs a child off a cliff.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** "one heaven, one chance, one size" / the God-is-a-jailer
  fear. Answers it with Jesus's own words and the real geography of Gehenna.
- **Whose face carries it:** the LISTENERS hearing him — recognition, then relief.
  Jesus only via master ref / over-the-shoulder; never his face over the fire imagery.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Who taught you to fear him more than to trust him?"

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 11 stills
1. [n0] a shadowed medieval-style torment picture dissolving — the inherited image being set down — "hell is a torture chamber"
2. [n1] Jesus teaching a crowd near the city wall, gesturing beyond it — "the word... was Gehenna"
3. [n1/n2] the valley of Hinnom itself: a smoking refuse-fire ravine outside Jerusalem's wall — "the city's burning garbage dump"

8. [n4] a hand releasing/cutting loose something clutched — sin worth dropping — "sin is worth cutting loose from"

10. [n6] the shepherd going after one lost sheep at the cliff edge / the father on the road — "a shepherd who leaves ninety-nine to chase one"
11. [card] a warm hold — a hand reaching down toward someone near the fire's edge — for the closing question
