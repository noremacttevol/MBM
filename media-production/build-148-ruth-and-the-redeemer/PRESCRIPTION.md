## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the God of Israel doesn't keep the outsider at the edge — he
  grafts her into the center, all the way into the line that leads to Christ.
- **The turn:** Ruth's vow on the road — "thy people shall be my people, and thy God my
  God" — a Moabite choosing Israel's God, and being received, not turned away.
- **Wound answered:** the one-size / exclusion wound — the outsider is grafted in, her
  emptiness filled, her name written into the redeemer's line.
- **Whose face carries it:** RUTH the outsider and NAOMI the bitter widow — the
  clinging on the road, the empty lap filled. Boaz the redeemer. No Jesus (Old Testament).
- **The card question falls out of:** "He never let her go. Neither will He let you
  go." Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
