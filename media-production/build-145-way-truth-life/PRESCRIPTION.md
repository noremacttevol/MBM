## The impact read
- **The one thing:** you're not handed a map and sent off alone — the way is a person,
  and he walks it with you.
- **The turn:** Thomas asks "how can we know the way?" and Jesus answers not with
  directions but with himself — "I am the way." The road becomes a companion.
- **Wound answered:** the "God gives you a map and leaves" wound — a way implies
  walking, and he walks it beside you.
- **Whose face carries it:** THOMAS and the disciples — the honest "we don't know the
  way" on their faces, then following. Jesus only via master ref.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Stop looking for the road. Walk with Him."
  Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
