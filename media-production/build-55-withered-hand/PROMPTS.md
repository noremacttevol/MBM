hand — never gruesome, no wound, no gore. The hope-beat is the restored hand (s7). The
closing card is an open invitation.

ANTI-PANEL: every prompt asks for ONE single full-frame illustration of a single frozen
instant — no comic strip, no panels, no stacked or side-by-side frames, no dividing lines.

TIME/SETTING: **sabbath DAY.** A modest first-century synagogue — plastered stone walls,
simple wooden benches, a curtained niche holding the scrolls, warm daylight through high
small windows. One continuous room for s1-s7 and s9; s8 steps just outside the doorway into
the bright street. No night, no sunset.

THE MAN (withered hand): an ordinary first-century Galilean man of middle years in a plain
dun / faded brown tunic, clearly NOT cream, short dark hair and beard. One hand is withered
— thin, weakened and slightly curled inward, drawn gently and never gruesome. His face is
shown: quiet and ashamed at first, then hopeful, then full of wonder and joy once his hand
is restored whole (s7, s9).

THE LEADERS (Pharisees, s2, s5, s8): a few sincere but hard-hearted religious men — NOT
cartoon villains — in fuller formal wool robes of deep indigo-blue and dark maroon with a
woven prayer-fringe at the hem, well-kept dark beards, clearly NOT cream. Faces shown:
watchful and cold at first, then hardened and grim, never sneering caricatures.

THE CONGREGATION: ordinary first-century Galilean worshippers in dun, faded brown, ochre,
olive, grey-blue and deep indigo wool, head-coverings or covered heads, short hair. Faces
shown. NONE in off-white or cream, none with long hair past the shoulders.

ONLY JESUS WEARS CREAM (build law). In every frame he is the ONLY figure in an off-white or
cream robe, and the only man with long hair past the shoulders. Everyone else is in a
darker earth colour with short or covered hair. Check this on every generated frame.
