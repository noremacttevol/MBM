# PRESCRIPTION — build-36-shrewd-steward

**Row:** 36   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Luke 16:1–13
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 10)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he is not after your money — he is after you; be as urgent about
  loving people and the things that last as the world is about money.
- **The turn:** the steward's panic → his frantic dealing with the debtors. The hinge
  is the urgency Jesus points at (not the cheating) — worldly people are so quick about
  their own future.
- **Character shown:** God wants your heart free of a cruel little master and your
  hands open to spend your one short life on what lasts forever.
- **Whose face carries it:** the STEWARD (panic, then shrewd scheming) and the DEBTORS
  (amazement). The steward is a worldly manager — see the face flag below.
- **The card question falls out of:** "What are you being urgent about?" No change.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 10 stills
1. [s1/j0/n1] the rich man and his steward — word comes the steward has wasted his goods — agrees: "the same was accused unto him that he had wasted his goods"
2. [n2] the rich man calls him in — "give account; you cannot be my manager any longer" — "he was about to lose his job and his home"
3. [j3/n3] the steward alone, panicking — "I cannot dig; to beg I am ashamed"
4. [n4] the steward quickly summoning his master's debtors, one after another — "he quickly called in every person who owed his employer money"
5. [j6/n5] the first debtor — a hundred jugs of oil; "write fifty" — his disbelief — "the farmer could hardly believe it"
6. [n6] the second debtor — a hundred measures of wheat; "write eighty" — "One after another he made friends"
7. [j1/n7] even the master admires the shrewdness — the urgency, not the cheating — "the children of this world are... wiser than the children of light"
8. [j2/n8] Jesus' point — use what you have to love people now; those friendships outlast you — "when ye fail, they may receive you into everlasting habitations"
9. [j13/n9] "Ye cannot serve God and mammon" — one master will be loved, the other despised — "money is a hard, hungry thing to serve"
10. [n10/card] he is not after your money — he is after you; warm hold for the card — "He is not after your money. He is after you"
