## s1-accused — Shot 1: give an account of thy stewardship
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A prosperous first-century estate courtyard in warm daylight,
storerooms and jars behind. A wealthy, well-dressed estate owner with a
grey-streaked beard stands holding an unrolled account scroll, his expression

plain belted tunic with keys and ledgers, caught out and worried. Both are
ordinary men, faces clearly shown. One single continuous scene painted edge to
edge, 9:16 vertical.

[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The steward alone in a storeroom doorway at dusk, ledgers

will do. A single ordinary figure, face clearly shown, low warm lamplight, quiet

[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A lamplit room at evening. The steward, a new resolve on his

single continuous scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s4-oil-bill — Shot 4: take thy bill, and write fifty
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Close on the lamplit table. The steward hands a debt-scroll
