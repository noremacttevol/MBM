## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God's work isn't a franchise — anyone acting in his name is on your
  side, and the kingdom is far bigger than the inner circle assumed.
- **The turn:** j1 — "Forbid him not… he that is not against us is on our part." Jesus
  refuses to take his own disciple's side against a stranger doing good in his name.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** *the tribal / franchise God* — the inherited fear that you
  must belong to the right group to count, that God only works through your circle. This cut
  answers it: the name is doing the work, not the membership; John drew the circle too small
  and expected thanks, and Jesus widened it.
- **Whose face carries it:** JOHN (drew the circle small, then understands). Jesus face-shown
  via master ref correcting him, gently.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Don't draw the circle too small. Whoever works in His
  name is on your side." No change needed.
