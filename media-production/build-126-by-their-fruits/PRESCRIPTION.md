## The impact read
- **The one thing:** you can tell what is true by what grows from it — not the clothes,
  not the confidence, not the words, but the fruit; and anybody can run that test.
- **The turn:** "A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit... Cannot. Not will not."
  What is inside comes out in the open, given long enough — so watch the fruit.
- **Wound answered (dead religion / being deceived):** the fear of not knowing who or
  what to trust in religion — wolves in sheep's clothing. Jesus hands over a test
  anyone can use: "taste the fruit."
- **Whose face carries it:** the images carry it — the wolf in the sheep's coat, the
  trees and their fruit. Jesus speaks (red), shown teaching, over-the-shoulder onto
  the listeners.
- **The card question falls out of:** "abide in him, and let it show." The good fruit
  of a life with him is where the card lands. No change needed.
