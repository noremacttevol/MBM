# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #164: The Rainbow Covenant (genesis-9_the-rainbow-covenant)

Genesis 8:20-9:17. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — twelve painted stills, no motion clips.

CARE FLAGS: none — #164 is unlisted in CONTENT-CARE.md, GREEN. The flood itself is
NEVER shown: no storm-drowning, no wreckage, no bodies, no one in peril anywhere.
Every frame is AFTER the flood — a washed, quiet, new world. Milk framing: this is
GOOD news — God binds HIMSELF with a one-way promise Noah never asked for and never
earned, and hangs the reminder in the sky where GOD will see it. The WHY the video
carries: to eight flood survivors a dark cloud meant terror, and God answered that
fear — not with a scolding but with a sign of peace.

NO DIVINE FIGURE ANYWHERE. God is a voice in this story and is never depicted:
no figure in the sky, no shape in the clouds or the light, no hand from heaven.
Where God speaks, the picture is the family and the light — the light is empty of
any figure or shape. (No Jesus appears in this video — he is never shown, never
visible, in no frame at all; nobody wears cream or off-white in any frame —
every garment is a clearly darker earth color.)

NOAH LOCK (every shot he appears in: s2, s3, s4, s5, s6, s9): a hale, white-bearded
elder with weathered warm olive-tan skin and deep kind lines, LONG white-grey beard,
in one ANKLE-LENGTH rust-brown wool robe falling to his sandals, LONG sleeves to the
wrists, a soft dark-brown cloth sash, and a faded grey wool mantle over his
shoulders — never off-white, never cream. His face IS shown, kind and weathered,
consistent in every shot.

FAMILY LOCK (s2, s4, s5, s6, s9): Noah's wife, an older woman with silver hair
under a dun-brown headscarf in an ankle-length deep-olive wool dress with long
sleeves; his three grown sons, dark-bearded men in ankle-length wool robes with
long sleeves and cloth sashes — one faded indigo-blue, one ochre-brown, one dark
maroon; and their three wives in ankle-length dresses with headscarves — one muted
sage-green, one grey-brown, one soft terracotta. Not one of the eight wears
off-white or cream. Their faces ARE shown and stay consistent across shots.

ANIMAL LOCK: the animals from the ark appear in calm pairs — two doves, sheep,
goats, a pair of oxen — gentle painted storybook creatures, never frightened,
never in peril.

RAINBOW LOCK (s8, s10, s11, s12): ONE single rainbow arc, its colors soft luminous
watercolor bands of gentle rose, gold, green and blue — never neon, never
electric, no light rays, no double arc — the same soft painted bow every time it
appears.

MUST NEVER SHOW: any depiction of God; flood water destroying anything; wreckage,
drowned animals or people; anyone in peril or terror beyond solemn watchfulness;
a neon or glowing rainbow; any modern object; any text baked into the image.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single continuous scene painted edge to edge." Skip any
comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.

## s1-the-ark-at-rest — Shot 1: the morning the world was new
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Very early morning, soft silver-gold mist lifting off a green mountainside. The great weathered wooden ark rests aground on the high slope, an enormous plain-timbered hull with a single door standing open, streaked and darkened by long months of rain, now still and quiet. Below it the washed earth rolls away in soft green terraces under a clearing pale-gold sky, small streams of leftover water catching the light as they drain away peacefully into the valley. Two white doves fly across the brightening sky. No people are in the frame, nothing is broken or wrecked anywhere — the world is clean, hushed and utterly new. Gentle mist, warm gold morning light on wet grass. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s2-first-steps — Shot 2: eight people and a new earth
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Clear fresh morning light on the mountainside below the great weathered wooden ark, its ramp and open door behind. Noah, a hale white-bearded elder with weathered warm olive-tan skin, in his ankle-length rust-brown wool robe with long sleeves to the wrists, a dark-brown cloth sash and a faded grey wool mantle, steps carefully onto the wet green grass. Around him come his family: his wife, an older silver-haired woman in a dun-brown headscarf and a deep-olive ankle-length dress; his three dark-bearded grown sons in ankle-length long-sleeved wool robes of faded indigo-blue, ochre-brown and dark maroon; and their three wives in ankle-length dresses of muted sage-green, grey-brown and soft terracotta with headscarves — eight people in all, and not one of them wears off-white or cream. Calm animals come out in pairs around them: two sheep, two goats, a pair of oxen. Their faces are painted fully and clearly, quiet with awe at the wide washed world. Warm morning light, wet grass, soft mist in the valley. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s3-building-the-altar — Shot 3: the first thing Noah built
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Bright midday light on a grassy shelf of the mountainside. Noah, the hale white-bearded elder with weathered warm olive-tan skin, in his ankle-length rust-brown wool robe with long sleeves, dark-brown cloth sash and faded grey wool mantle, kneels at a low altar of rough uncut grey stones, carefully setting one more stone in place. His right hand sets the stone on the top course and his left hand steadies the course beneath it; every hand is joined to the correct body by a visible arm, and no spare or floating hand appears anywhere. His face is painted fully and clearly, kind, weathered and intent, full of quiet gratitude. Around the altar the green slope is scattered with small white wildflowers; the great wooden ark stands far behind him up the slope. Clear midday light, clean washed air. Two arms, two hands, two legs, one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s4-the-offering — Shot 4: the smoke goes up, and the promise comes down
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Late golden afternoon on the mountainside. The low altar of rough grey stones now carries a small burnt offering, and one slender column of pale smoke rises straight and peaceful into a soft gold-and-blue sky. Noah, the white-bearded elder in his ankle-length rust-brown wool robe, long sleeves, dark-brown cloth sash and faded grey mantle, stands with his head bowed before the altar, his hands folded together at his chest, both hands joined to his own arms, no spare hand anywhere. Behind him his family stands gathered in a loose half-circle — the silver-haired wife in deep olive and a dun-brown headscarf, the three dark-bearded sons in indigo-blue, ochre-brown and dark maroon, the three wives in sage-green, grey-brown and terracotta — every garment clearly darker than cream, not one person in off-white. Their faces are painted fully and clearly, reverent and still. The thin smoke is the only thing that moves. Warm golden light, long soft shadows. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s5-the-gathering-clouds — Shot 5: when the sky darkened again
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Late afternoon turning grey: heavy rain clouds are gathering over the valley for the first time since the flood, deep blue-grey and rolling, though no rain falls yet. In the foreground Noah, the white-bearded elder in his ankle-length rust-brown wool robe and faded grey mantle, stands looking up at the darkening sky, one hand shading his brow and the other resting at his sash, each hand joined to its own visible arm. Close beside him stand his silver-haired wife in deep olive with her dun-brown headscarf and one dark-bearded son in faded indigo-blue, all three faces painted fully and clearly, solemn and watchful — people who remember what rain once meant — yet standing steady together, no panic, no terror. Their cheeks are completely dry and clean: no tears, no teardrops, no wet streaks; all the feeling is carried in the eyes, brows and mouth. Not one of them wears off-white or cream. Grey-blue cloud light with the last warm gold at the horizon. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s6-god-speaks — Shot 6: the covenant is spoken
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same grey sky, but now it is parting: broad shafts of warm gold light break through the blue-grey clouds and pour down onto the mountainside where the whole family stands very still among their calm animals, every upturned garment edge catching the light. The light is empty of any figure or shape — only clean warm light through parted cloud, nothing standing in it, no form within it. Noah in rust-brown with his faded grey mantle stands at the front, his family behind him — deep olive, indigo-blue, ochre-brown, dark maroon, sage-green, grey-brown, terracotta, none in off-white or cream — their faces painted fully and clearly, lifted, listening, wondering. Two sheep and a pair of oxen stand quietly among them. The mood is hushed and holy, a family hearing something enormous and kind. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s7-never-again — Shot 7: the washed world, promised safe
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A wide, high view over the whole valley in clearing late-afternoon light: green slopes freshly washed, silver streams finding their beds again, groves of young trees shining wet, the floodwaters fully receded and the land whole and at peace. Far below, tiny in the distance, the family's camp with its thin thread of altar smoke; the great wooden ark rests far up on the ridge. The blue-grey rain clouds are breaking apart and drifting off the edges of the sky, and calm gold light spreads across the land. No water threatens anything; nothing is broken; the earth simply lies open and safe and new. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s8-the-bow-in-the-cloud — Shot 8: I do set my bow in the cloud
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The moment after rain: the air still silver with clearing mist, low warm sun breaking through, and across the whole deep blue-grey retreating cloud bank stands ONE single enormous rainbow arc — soft luminous watercolor bands of gentle rose, gold, green and blue, never neon, no light rays, no second arc — spanning the entire valley from ridge to ridge. Beneath it, small at the bottom of the frame, the family stands together on the green slope looking up at it, eight tiny figures in dark earth-toned robes of rust-brown, deep olive, indigo-blue, ochre-brown, dark maroon, sage-green, grey-brown and terracotta, none in off-white or cream, their calm animals beside them. The bow is the whole picture — vast, gentle and utterly peaceful over the washed world. Soft after-rain gold light. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s9-faces-lifted — Shot 9: the warrior hangs up his bow

Noah, the white-bearded elder in rust-brown with his faded grey mantle, stands at the center with his head lifted, and around him his silver-haired wife in deep olive and dun-brown headscarf, one dark-bearded son in faded indigo-blue and one son's wife in muted sage-green also gaze upward. ## s12-the-promise-keeper — Shot 12: the God who binds himself
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Calm early evening over a peaceful valley: the soft watercolor rainbow — gentle rose, gold, green and blue bands, never neon, one single arc — fading gracefully into a quiet lavender-and-gold sky as the day settles. Warm dusk light, deep peace. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.
