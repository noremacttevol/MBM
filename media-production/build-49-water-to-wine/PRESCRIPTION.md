# PRESCRIPTION — build-49-water-to-wine

**Row:** 49   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** John 2:1–11
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 13)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** his very first move in the whole world was to walk into a
  family's worst moment of the day and quietly turn it into more joy than they started
  with — for people who never asked and never knew who paid.
- **The turn:** [jv8/n9] "Draw out now" — no lightning, no words over the jars; the
  water quietly becomes the finest wine somewhere between the jar and the cup.
- **Character shown:** a God whose first miracle protects joy, not settles a score —
  and who saves the best for last.
- **Whose face carries it:** MARY (noticing, trusting), the SERVANTS hauling water,
  and the STEWARD's baffled delight. Jesus is present but not the emotional face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what would you bring a God who saves the best
  for last?"

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 13 stills
1. [n1] the very first miracle — a village wedding about to fall apart — "He saved a village wedding from falling apart"
2. [n2] Jesus a guest at the days-long Cana feast, in the middle of ordinary joy — "he was right in the middle of it"
3. [n3] the quiet disaster: the wine running out, the family's shame looming — "a public shame they would carry for years"
4. [n4/w3/n4b] his mother noticing first, going straight to him — "They have no wine"
5. [jv4/n5] Jesus answering gently, wondering aloud whether the hour has come — "Woman, what have I to do with thee? mine hour is not yet come"
6. [n6/w5/n6b] Mary turning to the servants, trusting, and stepping back — "Whatsoever he saith unto you, do it"
7. [n7] the six big empty stone washing-jars standing nearby — "six big stone jars... Empty jars, meant for making things clean"
8. [jv7/n8] Jesus telling the servants to fill the jars with water; they haul it to the very top — "Fill the waterpots with water"
9. [jv8/n9] "Draw out now" — no show; the water quietly becoming wine between jar and cup — "somewhere between the jar and the cup, the water quietly became something else"
10. [n10/s10/n10b] the steward tasting, pulling the bridegroom aside, half laughing — "thou hast kept the good wine until now"
11. [n11] the best wine, ~150 gallons, poured out on people who would never know who paid — "Something like a hundred and fifty gallons of the finest wine"
12. [n12] his friends seeing it and believing — the God who turns a family's worst moment into more joy — "His friends saw it, and they believed him"
13. [card] hold for the closing question about a God who saves the best for last
