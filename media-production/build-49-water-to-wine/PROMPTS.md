# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #49: Water to Wine at Cana (john-2_water-to-wine)

John 2:1-11. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — twelve painted stills, no motion clips.

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk. Framed around JOY, generosity and abundance, never
around drink or intoxication: the wine is the sign of gladness and of God saving the best
for last (verse card, John 2:10). No one is shown drunk; the feast is warm, family-friendly
celebration.

Jesus is a GUEST at the wedding and He speaks, so He appears in several shots — but as in
every video, His face is NEVER shown: He is staged from directly BEHIND or over-the-shoulder
in every shot He is in (s2, s5, s8, s9, s12), the back of His head to us, His face never
visible at any angle. Everyone else is shown in full, and NOBODY but Jesus wears cream or
off-white.

JESUS LOCK (s2, s5, s8, s9, s12): a first-century Middle-Eastern man, warm olive-brown skin,
LONG dark brown-black hair past the shoulders, one plain undyed off-white cream wool robe,
bare-headed, no hood; no glow, no halo, no rim-light; an ordinary solid man. The camera is
always directly behind Him or over His shoulder and His face is never shown, never visible,
at any angle.

MARY LOCK (s4, s5, s6) — Mary the mother: a gracious middle-aged woman of about
forty-eight, warm and calm, in a soft dusty-blue wool robe and a matching dusty-blue
headscarf, clearly NOT cream. Her face IS shown, kind, steady and trusting.

SERVANTS LOCK (s6, s8, s9) — the wedding servants: ordinary working men and youths in plain
dun and faded-brown tunics with cord belts, clearly NOT cream, faces shown, willing and
attentive.

GOVERNOR LOCK (s10) — the ruler / governor of the feast: a dignified older steward in a
richer robe of deep russet-red with a woven gold-ochre border, a trimmed grey beard, clearly
NOT cream. His face IS shown, surprised and delighted as he tastes.

BRIDEGROOM LOCK (s10) — the young bridegroom: a glad young man in a festive deep-blue
embroidered wool robe with a bright woven red-and-gold sash and a simple flower garland,
clearly NOT cream. His face IS shown, happy.

WATERPOTS LOCK (s7, s8, s9) — six large stone water jars, pale tan-grey carved stone, each
big enough to hold twenty or thirty gallons, plain and heavy, of the kind used for the
Jewish washing rituals.

WEDDING FEAST (s1, s2, s3, s10, s11, s12) — a joyful village wedding in a lamp-lit stone
courtyard at warm golden evening: long low tables with bread, figs and cups, clay oil lamps,
flower garlands, and ordinary Galilean guests of every age in warm festal reds, ochres,
greens and blues — none of them in cream or off-white.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single continuous scene painted edge to edge." Skip any
comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns. Every image is ONE single
unbroken scene filling the whole vertical frame, never split into panels or bands.

## s1-the-wedding-feast — Shot 1: a marriage in Cana of Galilee

The whole room glows with warm gold lamplight and simple happiness. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.
