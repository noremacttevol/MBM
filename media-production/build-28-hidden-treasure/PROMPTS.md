# FLOW PROMPTS — Story Video #28: Hidden Treasure (matthew-13_hidden-treasure)

Matthew 13:44. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) + the Face Law: this is a parable Jesus tells,
and NO Jesus figure appears on screen at all — his face is never shown because he
is simply not depicted here; only the parable's own characters are painted.
Nano Banana 2, tall vertical 9:16, x2, download 1K. Hand-painted 2D storybook
style (NOT photorealistic). Passes `jesus_face_gate.py`.

**CHARACTER LOCKS (keep consistent across every still they appear in):**
- **The man / the finder (s1–s7)** — an ordinary hired field laborer of about
  thirty-five, warm brown skin, dark hair and a short dark beard, a simple undyed
  dusty-tan rough wool tunic with a rope belt, worn leather sandals. He carries a
  wooden-handled iron mattock (hoe) in the field shots. His face IS shown and stays
  the same man in every shot — weary at work, astonished at the find, then flooded
  with joy.
- **The treasure (s2, s3)** — an old half-buried earthenware jar and a small
  wooden chest brimming with gold coins and a few gold objects, dulled with age and
  soil. Warm gold glow.
- **The field owner / landowner (s6)** — an older, better-dressed man in a fuller
  dyed robe (deep ochre) and head-cloth, a landowner; face shown.
- **The field** — a modest terraced Judean field of dry pale-brown soil bordered
  by a low dry-stone wall, with a few olive trees at the edge and hills beyond.

**STILL STYLE BLOCK** (prepend to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, warm golden light, earth-toned palette of first-century Judea. Sacred,
gentle tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions in the image. Historically
modest rough-woven wool and linen robes in undyed earth colors. No modern objects.

**ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE** (append to every prompt, exactly):
ONE SINGLE CONTINUOUS SCENE painted edge to edge as one unbroken composition
filling the entire tall vertical 9:16 frame — NOT divided into panels, NOT a comic
strip, just one single picture.

## s1 — Shot 1: the laborer digging in the field
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A modest terraced Judean field of dry pale-brown soil bordered
by a low dry-stone wall, olive trees at the edge and soft hills beyond, under a
warm midday sun. An ordinary hired field laborer of about thirty-five — warm brown
skin, dark hair and a short dark beard, a simple undyed dusty-tan rough wool tunic
with a rope belt, worn sandals — swings a wooden-handled iron mattock, breaking the
hard ground, sweat on his brow, the honest tiredness of a working day. Just an
ordinary man at ordinary work. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s2 — Shot 2: the mattock strikes something
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same field, close now. The laborer has stopped mid-swing,
leaning on his mattock and staring down at the ground, where the blade has struck
something hard and scraped the soil away from the curved lip of an old half-buried
earthenware jar and the corner of a small aged wooden chest. Loose earth all
around, his hand reaching down to brush the dirt away, curiosity and dawning
disbelief on his face. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s3 — Shot 3: the treasure revealed
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Kneeling in the dug soil, the laborer has lifted the lid of the
small aged wooden chest and tipped the old earthenware jar — and out spills a
treasure: gold coins and a few gold objects, dulled with age but glowing warm in
the sunlight. His face is lit with pure astonishment and joy, one hand pressed to
his chest, the other cupping the gold. A hidden fortune, found. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s4 — Shot 4: he hides it again
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same field. The laborer is on his knees pushing the soil
back over the jar and chest with both hands, carefully reburying the treasure and
smoothing the dirt to hide any sign of it, while glancing back over his shoulder to
be sure no one is watching. A secret, breathless, hopeful joy on his face — he
knows exactly what he is going to do. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s5 — Shot 5: he sells everything he owns
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A busy first-century village market of stone and mud-brick.
The same laborer stands before his small stone house, handing over all that he owns
— clay pots, tools, blankets, a goat on a rope, bundles of goods — to buyers and a
merchant who count coins into his hand. His modest possessions are being carried
away, and yet his face is glad, not grieving, letting go of everything without
regret. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s6 — Shot 6: he buys the field
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] At the edge of the same terraced field with its low dry-stone
wall. The laborer presses a heavy purse and a stack of gold coins into the hands of
an older, better-dressed landowner in a deep-ochre dyed robe and head-cloth, the two
men clasping hands to seal the sale. The laborer gives away every coin he has, his
face quietly beaming; the landowner, pleased, has no idea what lies buried in his
soil. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s7 — Shot 7: the field is his
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same field at golden hour, long warm light across the pale
soil, olive trees and hills glowing behind. The laborer stands alone in the middle
of the field that is now his own, one hand pressed to his chest and the other open
at his side, his head lifted in overwhelmed, tearless joy — a man who has given up
everything and gained far more. He knows the treasure is his now, waiting beneath
his feet. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]
