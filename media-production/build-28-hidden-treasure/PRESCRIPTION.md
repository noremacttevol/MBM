## The impact read
- **The one thing:** when you truly see who Jesus is, giving up everything else isn't
  a sad sacrifice — it's the joy-filled trade of a lifetime.
- **The turn:** the spade striking something hard → the treasure uncovered. The hinge
  is the moment his face changes from labor to joy.
- **Character shown / wound answered:** the "faith costs you everything, grimly"
  picture is answered — "for joy thereof" he sold all. Joy, not fear, drives it.
- **Whose face carries it:** the HIRED MAN — his face at the find, and his joy at the
  trade. No Jesus face needed (the treasure IS the point; keep it a real buried find).
- **The card question falls out of:** "If you have found the treasure, what is he
  worth to you?" No change needed.
