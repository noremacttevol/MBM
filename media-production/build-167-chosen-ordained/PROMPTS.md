# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #167: "I have chosen you, and ordained you" (john-15_chosen-and-ordained)

John 15:16. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no motion
clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Callings** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk. The note is CALLING: no one appoints himself to God's
work — God chooses and ordains, by name, for a purpose (to bring forth fruit that lasts), and
backs the calling with power. Warm, humbling, hopeful. Study gem: called of God, by name — not
self-chosen.

CHRIST / GOD IS NEVER DEPICTED as a figure — no face, no body, no shape of a person. The one
who calls, chooses and ordains is shown ONLY as warm radiant light from heaven — never a
figure, never a face. (This keeps the shot blocks free of any divine proper noun, so the
face-gate passes.)

THE POINT (Why-Law): ordinary people were living their own lives, not seeking to appoint
themselves to anything (s1); but the light of heaven reached down and CHOSE them, calling them
by name (s2 — "I have chosen you"); they were ordained and set apart for the work (s3); and
sent out to go (s4); to bring forth fruit — real, good labour and lives changed (s5); fruit
that would last and remain (s6 — the named verse's promise); and the calling came with power,
so that what they asked of heaven was given (s7); and the same call, by name, is offered to
you (s8).

PEOPLE LOCK (s1-s8): ordinary first-century people — men, women (a few) of mixed age in undyed
DARKER earth-tone wool (dun, faded brown, olive, ochre, russet, grey), faces shown, humble and
willing; never in cream or off-white.
THE HEAVENLY LIGHT (s2, s3, s6, s7, s8): warm radiant golden light from heaven, sometimes as a
single clear shaft that singles out one person — only light, never a face, never a figure,
never a body, hand or dove within it.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or body of God or the risen Lord anywhere — only warm
light; no dove; no modern object; no cross, steeple, church-building-with-a-spire, or insignia
of any group; no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no baked-in text, letters or
numbers; no human dressed in cream or off-white. Every image is ONE single unbroken full-frame
scene — no panels, no comic strip, no dividing lines.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.
