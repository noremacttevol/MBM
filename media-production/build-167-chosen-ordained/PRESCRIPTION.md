## The impact read
- **The one thing:** you do not appoint yourself to God's work — he chooses and ordains, by name, and sends you to bear fruit that lasts. A calling is a gift received, not a badge taken.
- **The turn:** the direction of the choosing (n2) — *it runs from heaven down to us, not the other way.* "Ye have not chosen me, but I have chosen you" is the hinge the whole verse pivots on.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** authority and callings — men are called of God, by name, and ordained for his work. Hands off to **Gospel Library → Callings.**
- **Whose face carries it:** the ordinary person at ordinary work, singled out by name. Jesus speaks (red) via master ref / over-the-shoulder; the called one's face receives it.
- **The card question falls out of:** "When heaven calls you by your own name, will you look up, and answer?" — lands straight on the one thing.
