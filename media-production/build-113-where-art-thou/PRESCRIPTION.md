# PRESCRIPTION — build-113-where-art-thou

**Row:** 113   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Genesis 3
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 12)

> Bridge shelf. The wound is **"God is angry / after I failed he turned away."** The answer
> is the very first thing God says to hiding people — not a scolding but "where art thou?"
> — the cry of a Father looking for a child, and then he covers the shame they could not
> cover themselves.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** when people first failed and hid, God did not wait for them to clean
  themselves up — he came walking through the garden looking for them, and he clothed the
  shame they could not fix, because he wanted them back.
- **The turn:** "Where art thou?" — the first word God speaks after the fall is not "how
  dare you" but a Father searching for a hiding child. That is the hinge.
- **Wound answered:** "God is angry / turns away when I fail." The answer — he came
  *looking*, and covered them with better coverings than the leaves they grabbed.
- **Whose face carries it:** the MAN and the WOMAN — hiding among the trees, answering from
  behind the tree in fear, standing ashamed and waiting for the end, then clothed and sent
  out not alone. God is presence/light/hands moving through the garden, never a rendered
  face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Where are you hiding, that he is gently calling you
  out of?" — lands on the one thing. No change needed.
