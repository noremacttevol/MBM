Genesis 3. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — ten painted stills. BRIDGE/MILK.
CARE FLAGS (self-assessed): the Fall — shame and consequence. Handle TENDERLY: God comes
SEEKING, not to destroy; the heart is a grieving Father looking for his hiding children and
covering their shame. NO nudity (Adam and Eve are modestly clothed in simple leaf/skin
garments and shown from a respectful distance or behind). NO serpent/devil figure shown at
all. NO violence, no blood, no menace. The tone is sorrowful-yet-merciful, never frightening.

attach CHARACTERS/god-the-father refs. The old "formless light" law is DEAD for the Father.
(No Jesus is shown in any frame; only the Father wears pure white — Adam and Eve are in
earth-toned leaf and skin garments.)

ADAM & EVE LOCK (s2-s10): a young man and young woman with warm olive-tan skin and dark
hair, modestly clothed — first in simple woven fig-leaf garments, later in plain undyed
soft brown animal-skin garments; shown with dignity, faces sorrowful and tender, never
exposed. Their faces ARE shown, consistent.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single tall upright vertical scene painted edge to edge, no panels."
Skip any comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.

MUST NEVER SHOW: God as a person, face, figure or hand; a serpent, snake or devil figure;
any nudity or exposure; violence or blood; anyone in cream or off-white; any modern object;
any baked-in text; a multi-panel or comic-strip layout.
