# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #155: "A falling away first" (2-thessalonians-2_a-falling-away)

2 Thessalonians 2:1-3. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted
stills, no motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Apostasy** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: handle with care — the theme is a "falling away," but the treatment is HOPE,
never accusation. NEVER depict or blame any specific church, people, or era; NEVER show a
villain, a "man of sin," gore, or anyone shamed. The apostasy is shown ONLY as gently
fading light — a lamp burning low, a garden going quiet, dusk — always with a hint that
morning follows. Milk framing: the dimming was foretold, so it never meant God failed; and
what is foretold as lost can be restored.

CHRIST IS NEVER DEPICTED in any frame; God and hope are shown ONLY as warm light — never a
figure, never a face, never a shape. No divine person is drawn.

THE POINT (Why-Law): frightened believers are steadied by Paul's letter (s1, s2, s3); do
not be shaken by rumor (s3, s4 — 2 Thes 2:2); Paul foretells a falling away will come
first (s4, s5); the light dims as many drift from the truth (s5); but foretold is not
failed (s6); a falling away first, plainly predicted (s7 — 2 Thes 2:3); a night warned of
implies a morning to come (s7); and the light returns — take hold (s8).

PAUL LOCK (s1, s3, s6 — the same apostle): the apostle Paul, a lean intense older
Middle-Eastern man of about fifty-five, high forehead with receding dark-grey hair, a
dark-grey pointed beard, sharp earnest features, wearing a plain tunic and cloak of undyed
DUN and dark BROWN wool with a leather belt — dark earth tones, never white, cream,
off-white or pale. His face is always shown, earnest and kind. Consistent every shot.

BELIEVERS LOCK (s1, s2, s3 — the Thessalonians): ordinary first-century believers, men and
women of mixed age in undyed DARKER earth-tone wool (dun, faded brown, olive, ochre, grey),
faces shown, at first anxious then steadied; never in cream or off-white, never resembling
Paul.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or shape of God or Christ anywhere — hope is only
warm light; no villain, no "man of sin," no monster, no specific church, cathedral, temple,
steeple, cross, robe of office, or religious insignia of any group; no bright ring or aura
around anyone's head; no modern object; no baked-in text, letters or numbers; no gore, no
one shamed or in peril; no human dressed in cream or off-white.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

## s1-paul-writing — Shot 1: Paul writes a steadying letter
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Warm lamplight in a plain stone room at night. The apostle Paul, a lean intense older Middle-Eastern man of about fifty-five, high forehead with receding dark-grey hair, a dark-grey pointed beard, sharp earnest features, wearing a plain tunic and cloak of undyed dun and dark brown wool with a leather belt (dark earth tones, never white, cream or pale), sits at a low wooden table writing on a scroll with a reed pen, his face thoughtful and caring. A small oil lamp glows beside him. He has one head, two arms and two hands. No other person present. Quiet, intimate. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s2-rumors-and-fear — Shot 2: frightened believers, rattled by rumor
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Warm daytime light in a simple stone dwelling. A small group of ordinary first-century believers — men and women of mixed age in darker earth-tone wool (dun, faded brown, olive, grey), faces shown — stand close together looking anxious and unsettled, some whispering, some with a worried hand raised, troubled by frightening rumors. No danger is actually present; the fear is only in their faces. Every figure whole and correctly formed, one head each. No one in cream or off-white. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s3-paul-steadies-them — Shot 3: do not be shaken
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The apostle Paul, lean intense older Middle-Eastern man of about fifty-five, receding dark-grey hair, dark-grey pointed beard, in his dun-and-brown wool tunic and cloak (dark earth tones, never cream or pale), stands calmly among the same believers with an open, steadying hand raised, his expression reassuring, as their anxious faces begin to settle into calm and trust. Warm even light. Every figure whole and correctly formed, faces shown. No one in cream or off-white. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s4-anchored — Shot 4: not tossed by every wind (2 Thes 2:2)
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A single ordinary first-century believer in dun-and-brown earth-tone wool (dark earth tones, never cream or pale) stands firm and calm on solid rock beside a great steadfast anchor and a mooring rope, while a brisk wind bends the grass and clouds around; he is unshaken, steady, at peace. Symbolic of a mind anchored and not troubled. Warm daylight breaking through moving cloud. One figure, whole and correctly formed, one head, face shown, calm. No one in cream or off-white. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s5-the-light-dimming — Shot 5: a falling away — the light grows dim
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A once-bright, once-tended courtyard at deep dusk, now quiet and emptying: a single oil lamp on a stone ledge burning very low, its small flame guttering down to a faint ember, long soft shadows gathering, a well-kept garden at the edges beginning to grow wild and untended. Nobody is shown; the mood is gentle loss and fading, not menace — a light going quiet. No people, no villain. Warm dwindling glow against deepening blue dusk. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s6-foretold-not-failed — Shot 6: it was foretold, not a failure
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Warm steady lamplight. The apostle Paul, lean intense older Middle-Eastern man of about fifty-five, receding dark-grey hair, dark-grey pointed beard, in his dun-and-brown wool tunic and cloak (dark earth tones, never cream or pale), looks directly out with quiet assurance and hope, one hand resting calmly on his scroll — the steadiness of a man who knows the ending in advance. His face is shown, peaceful and certain. One head, two arms, two hands. No other figure. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s7-night-before-morning — Shot 7: the night that a morning must follow (2 Thes 2:3)
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A wide, still landscape in the last of deep night — dark hills under a fading star-scattered sky — but along the far horizon the very first faint warm band of coming dawn is just beginning to glow, promising morning. Quiet, hushed, hopeful; the darkness is ending, not deepening. No people, no figure, no shape in the light — only the land, the night sky, and the first thin line of dawn. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s8-the-light-returns — Shot 8: the light returns — take hold
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Full warm golden dawn breaking over open country, the darkness gone. A single ordinary young first-century believer in dun-and-brown earth-tone wool (dark earth tones, never cream or pale) stands on a rise and reaches out a hand into the warm returning light, face lifted in hope and gladness, ready to take hold. Warm gold light across the whole landscape, open sky, a sense of gentle invitation. Only one figure, whole and correctly formed, one head, two arms, two legs, face shown. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.
