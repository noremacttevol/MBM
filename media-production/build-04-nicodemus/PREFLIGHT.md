## FULL-STORY check — FINDING (caught on paper, zero cost)

The production pack (60s, written before the 2026-07-09 laws) ends with an
invented beat ("he walks home changed") and stops at John 3. Scripture gives
Nicodemus a complete arc that IS the point:

- John tags him "he that came to Jesus by night" at every later mention —
  deliberately — so the reader watches what the night visit became.
- **John 7:50-51** — months later he defends Jesus before the hostile council:
  "Doth our law judge any man, before it hear him?" First daylight courage.
  They turn on him for it (7:52).
- **John 19:39** — on the day Jesus died, when even the apostles hid, Nicodemus
  came openly with "a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about an hundred pound
  weight" — a royal burial quantity. The man who came at night gave Jesus a
  king's burial in daylight, when belief could no longer profit anyone.
- **John 3:19-21** — Jesus's light/darkness words were spoken TO the man who
  crept in under cover of dark: "he that doeth truth cometh to the light."
  Not a jab — an invitation, which Nicodemus eventually lived. This is the
  video's spine.

The build includes the whole arc. Runtime grows from the pack's 60s to a full
telling (~230s), per the Zacchaeus V2 precedent.
