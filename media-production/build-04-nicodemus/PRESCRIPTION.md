## The impact read
- **The one thing:** Jesus never shamed the fear and never shamed the night — he answered the real question underneath and let the courage grow on its own, until the man who crept in by dark gave him a king's burial in daylight.
- **The turn:** John 3:16, said quietly at night to one scared man — and, across the whole arc, the man's guard finally falling at "How can these things be?"
- **Character shown:** patience with the fearful; he meets an honest seeker with kindness, not correction.
- **Whose face carries it:** NICODEMUS — the fear in the lamplight, the honest question, the growing courage in the council and at the tomb. Jesus only via master ref / over-the-shoulder at j1, j2, j3.
- **The card question falls out of:** "have you ever felt drawn toward something, and been afraid to let anyone else see it?" — no change needed.
