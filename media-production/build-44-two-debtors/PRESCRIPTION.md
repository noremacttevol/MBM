## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the more you know you have been forgiven, the more you love —
  being "forgiven much" was never the punishment, it is the gift.
- **The turn:** [j4/n9] "Her sins, which are many, are forgiven; for she loved much" —
  and the correction that she loved *because* she was already forgiven, not the other
  way around. The tears are the sign, not the payment.
- **Character shown:** he receives and publicly clears the one woman the whole town
  had written off — welcome of the outsider, at a table full of people judging her.
- **Whose face carries it:** the WOMAN at his feet, and SIMON's watching, judging
  face. Not Jesus's face in the emotional beats — over-the-shoulder / on her.
- **The card question falls out of:** "how much do you believe you have been forgiven?"

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 12 stills
1. [n1] Simon the Pharisee's respectable house, Jesus taking his place at the low table with the guests — "took his place at the low table with the other guests"
2. [n2] the door opening — the woman entering where she does not belong, alabaster jar in hand — "a small alabaster jar of costly perfume"
3. [n3] the woman at his feet, weeping, wiping with her hair, kissing, pouring the perfume — "she wept until her tears fell on his feet"
4. [n4] Simon watching, saying nothing, judging inwardly — "inside, he had already made up his mind"
5. [j1/s40/n4b] Jesus turning to Simon — "Simon, I have somewhat to say unto thee" / "Master, say on"
6. [j2/n5/n6] the parable: a creditor and his two debtors, one owing much, one little — "the one owed five hundred pence, and the other fifty"
7. [j3/s43/j3b/n7] both debts frankly torn up; Simon answering which will love most — "which of them will love him most?"
8. [jv44/n8] Jesus turning to look at the woman while still speaking to Simon, setting them side by side — "Seest thou this woman?"
9. [j4/n9] THE TURN — "Her sins, which are many, are forgiven; for she loved much" — her tears the sign, not the payment
10. [jv48/n9b] "Thy sins are forgiven" — said out loud at the full table, over the one everyone had written off
11. [j5/n10] "Thy faith hath saved thee; go in peace" — the woman leaving saved, at peace, loved — "She walked out saved, and at peace, and loved"
12. [card] hold on the woman's face for the closing question
