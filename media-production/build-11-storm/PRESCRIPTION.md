## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he never said the storm wasn't real and never scolded them for waking him — he came through it in the boat with them and was simply bigger than it.
- **The turn:** he stands in the sinking boat and speaks to the storm, not the men — "Peace, be still" — and the sea obeys.
- **Character shown:** authority the wind and sea obey, joined to a presence in the boat that never left them alone in it.
- **Whose face carries it:** the DISCIPLES — the veteran fishermen's terror, then their greater awe after the calm. Jesus only via master ref (asleep in the stern, standing in the stern).
- **The card question falls out of:** "whatever storm you are in tonight, the same Jesus is still in the boat with you." — no change needed.
