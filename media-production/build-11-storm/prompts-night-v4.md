## NIGHT VARIANT of the locked style block (Correction #15a)
The locked block's "glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold
highlights" is what keeps forcing sunset skies. For night scenes the lighting
clause becomes: "lit only by cold moonlight, starlight, and small warm lamplight,
deep night palette of blue-blacks and muted earth tones". Everything else in the
block is unchanged. This variant is used for every prompt below.

STYLE-N = "Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and hushed, like a
classic illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly
brushstroke textures, lit only by cold moonlight, starlight, and small warm
lamplight, deep night palette of blue-blacks and muted earth tones. First-century
Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions in the image.
Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed earth colors.
No modern objects. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge as one
unbroken composition filling the entire tall frame. It is fully NIGHT: the sky is
deep blue-black with stars and cloud, and there is absolutely no sunset, no
sunrise, no golden-hour glow, and no warm bright horizon anywhere in the image."

## s1 REROLL — EVENING SHORE DEPARTURE (fail: sunset sky)

men, women, and children in earth-toned robes stands along the dark stony
waterline, a few of them holding small clay oil lamps and one raised torch whose
warm light catches nearby faces. Just offshore, a broad-beamed wooden Galilee
fishing boat with weathered gray-brown planks and a single bare mast sits low in
the black moonlit water while several fishermen wade beside it and climb aboard,
pushing it out. Seated in the boat's high curved stern is a man in an undyed cream
wool robe with a hood drawn up, seen from far behind so that only the back of his
hooded head and shoulders are visible — no part of his face and no skin of his
visible anywhere, at this distance he is only robe and hood. Every person in the
crowd gazes toward the boat. Each person has exactly two arms, two hands, and two
legs. The scene is one unbroken painting, not divided into panels. Moonlight lays
a cold silver path on the water; the hills behind are black shapes against the
starry sky.

## Clip A REGEN v2 (from new s3) — CYCLICAL BAILING (Correction #15c: the pots must MOVE through the scoop-lift-fling cycle, never a static pour)

Animate this hand-painted scene. The boat heaves violently on huge dark night
waves lit by cold lightning flickers; sheets of rain lash across the frame; cold
spray bursts over the gunwale. The two bailing fishermen work in a clear repeating
cycle: each man bends DOWN and dips his clay pot into the floodwater at his feet,
lifts the filled pot up across his body, and SWINGS it out over the side rail to
hurl the water into the sea in one flung sheet, then swings back down empty to
scoop again — the pots themselves visibly travel down, up, and out with each
throw, and the water leaves the pots only during the outward fling. The third
fisherman keeps hauling downward, hand over hand, on the rigging rope that stays
entirely inside the boat. The furled sail and rigging whip in the wind. No water
is ever poured into the boat, no rope crosses the side rail, and no pot pours a
continuous stream. The painted brushstroke look is preserved exactly. No new
people enter the frame. No text.
