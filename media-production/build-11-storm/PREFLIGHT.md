### MUST SHOW (facts the text states)
- Evening, the same day he had taught the crowds (v35) — he is worn out;
  "even as he was" (v36) means no rest between the teaching and the boat.
- Other little ships went with them (v36) — others were in that storm too.
- A great storm of wind; waves beat INTO the ship; it was NOW FULL (v37) —
  real sinking, not drama. The fishermen aboard were professionals; their
  fear is the measure of the storm.
- Jesus asleep in the HINDER PART (stern), on a PILLOW/cushion (v38) —
  both details are in the text.
- They wake him with an accusation of the heart: "carest thou not that we
  perish?" (v38) — they doubted his CARE, not his power.
- He arose, rebuked the wind, spoke to the sea (v39). Wind ceased AND a
  great calm — instant, both at once (sailors know swell outlives wind;
  this didn't).
- His question comes AFTER the rescue, gently (v40).
- They feared EXCEEDINGLY after the calm (v41) — awe grew, and the story
  ends on their question: what manner of man is this.

### MUST NEVER SHOW
- Jesus's face — NEVER, at any distance where a face would read. Hidden
  by ANGLE only (Correction #12): asleep = face turned away into the
  cloak's shadow; standing = seen from BEHIND or over-the-shoulder.
- NO rim-light, halo, glow, or lightning edge-light outlining his head or
  face shape (Correction #12) — CRITICAL in this video: lightning + a
  silhouetted figure is exactly the "glowing outline" trap. Every prompt
  for a Jesus beat states positively: "no light or glow on or around his
  head; his hooded head reads dark against the sky." High-zoom audit of
  every Jesus frame for edge-glow.
- Jesus's hands — NEVER. The pack's shot 4 says "one hand rising toward
  the storm" — RESTAGED per the blanket hands-never law (Correction #2's
  hand allowance is for physical rescue/touch only; speaking to a storm
  is not touch). Staging: one arm lifted toward the storm with the long
  wool sleeve fallen fully over the hand, seen from behind at
  half-distance.
- A small detached rear-view Jesus away from where the men look
  (Correction #11) — in every "men responding to him" beat the gazes
  visibly converge on him; the v40 beat is a textbook OVER-THE-SHOULDER
  frame (camera behind him, his soaked shoulder soft in a lower corner,
  the drenched men filling the frame, every eye locked past camera AT him).
- The storm as God's punishment/lesson — the Seed says the opposite: he
  did not send the storm; he STILLED it. No visual or line implies
  authorship.
- Painted tear beads (Correction #3); modern objects; text in image;
  photorealism; canvas/fabric texture (anti-texture sentence available).

## WHY-LAW / STUDY-GEMS woven into narration
- WHY the storm was terrifying: Galilee sits ~700 ft below sea level in a
  bowl of hills — cold downslope wind turns it violent in minutes; these
  men were career fishermen and THEY panicked. Their fear calibrates it.
- WHY asleep matters: exhausted from teaching all day ("even as he was"),
  and unafraid. The cushion and the stern are the text's own details.
- WHY the accusation stings: "don't you care" doubts his HEART, not his
  power — the question people still ask in storms.
- GEM: wind ceased AND great calm at once — swell normally outlives wind
  for hours; the lake didn't wind down, it OBEYED.
- GEM: Psalm 107:29 — their scriptures said it is GOD who "maketh the
  storm a calm." They watched a man in dripping clothes do it. That is
  why the fear grew AFTER the danger passed (v41).
- TRANSLATION bridge after v40 (pack's, kept): he asked it soaked,
  standing in their boat, having come through the storm WITH them — not
  from the shore.
- THE SEED: he never denied the storm was real — he was just bigger than
  it. He didn't send it; he stilled it. Card = pack card verbatim.

## Locks (written into EVERY prompt where the figure/prop appears)

**FISHERMAN LOCK (the one who wakes him):** a broad-shouldered fisherman
in his mid-forties with a weathered sun-browned face, thick dark beard
streaked with gray, heavy brows, wearing a dark walnut-brown wool tunic
with a rope belt, soaked through and clinging — exactly two arms, two
hands, two legs. (Dark tunic reads against storm-gray and firelight both.)

**JESUS LOCK (updated per Correction #13, Cameron 2026-07-10):** an undyed
cream wool robe, always. Asleep = a sleeping form under the cream cloak on
the stern cushion, staged so the BACK of his hooded head faces the camera
and his face is on the far side, completely away from view — NEVER the hood
opening toward camera (void-face banned), NEVER any profile or partial
face, hands hidden beneath the cloak. Standing = seen only from DIRECTLY
BEHIND, the back of his hooded head dark against the sky, no light or glow
on or around his head, sleeves fallen fully past his hands with no skin
visible, robe soaked dark and storm-dimmed so he reads as a dark shape
among dark shapes. No solid-black featureless cutout may ever stand in for
him. Every render audited at full zoom for any facial feature before
banking.

**PROP LOCK — THE BOAT:** one broad-beamed wooden Galilee fishing boat,
weathered gray-brown planks, a single mast with furled rigging, a high
curved stern with a small steersman's deck. Same boat every beat.

**PROP LOCK — THE CUSHION:** a plain rough leather steersman's cushion in
the stern (v38's pillow). Present in s4, s5.
