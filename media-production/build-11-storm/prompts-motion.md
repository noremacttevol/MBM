# Motion prompts — Story #11 Calming the Storm (2 Veo clips, ~20 credits)

Both clips are image-to-video FROM the banked, QC-passed 2K stills so the
painted style, staging, and Jesus-framing carry over exactly. Still plays
BEFORE its motion clip in the cut (Correction #10).

## Clip A — THE STORM (from s3-the-storm.jpeg, asset dbd4000f-5d32-4c75-b286-57fa892e3308)

Animate this hand-painted scene. The boat heaves violently as huge dark
waves lift and drop it; cold spray bursts over the gunwale; sheets of rain
lash across the frame; the three drenched fishermen keep bailing and
hauling in desperate rhythm; the furled sail and rigging whip in the wind;
the sky flickers with distant storm light. The painted brushstroke look is
preserved exactly. No new people enter the frame. No text.

QC per second: limb counts on all three men, no new figures, no face
distortion, stern stays empty.

## Clip B — PEACE, BE STILL (from s6-peace-be-still.jpeg, asset 8718659b-c91c-45a9-a2ec-bcc880e95edc)

Animate this hand-painted scene. The robed figure stands braced and
UNMOVING with his back to the camera, one sleeve-covered arm raised toward
the storm; he does not turn, and the back of his hooded head stays toward
the camera for the entire clip with no light or glow appearing on or
around his head. In front of him the towering waves collapse and flatten,
the rain thins and stops, the churning water smooths into stillness, and
the black clouds break apart to reveal calm sky. The painted brushstroke
look is preserved exactly. No new people enter the frame. No text.

QC per second (CRITICAL): he must NEVER turn or show any face/profile; no
rim-light or halo forming on his head as the sky brightens; sleeve stays
over the hand; no new figures.
