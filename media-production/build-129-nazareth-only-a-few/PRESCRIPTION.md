# PRESCRIPTION — build-129-nazareth-only-a-few

**Row:** 129   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Mark 6:1–6
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 7 — see deviation)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** his power didn't run out in Nazareth — their willingness did — and
  even there he laid hands on the few who came, mercy never shrinking to match the unbelief.
- **The turn:** jv4 — "A prophet is not without honour, but in his own country." The moment
  the hometown's familiarity is named as the wall his power ran into.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** *the God who withholds / won't show up* — the inherited
  fear that God's power is arbitrary, that he has given up on you or your town. This cut
  answers it: nothing failed on his side; the closed, offended heart was the wall — and he
  still healed every one of the few who reached out.
- **Whose face carries it:** the TOWNSPEOPLE (offended familiarity — "isn't this the
  carpenter?") and then the healed few. Jesus face-shown via master ref laying on hands
  (s4) — not a close Jesus face in the grief of the rejection.
- **The card question falls out of:** "He healed the few who reached out. The door is the
  same today — believe, and let Him work." No change needed.
