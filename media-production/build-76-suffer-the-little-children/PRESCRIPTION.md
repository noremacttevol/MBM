## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he was displeased that anyone would block a child from him — he has
  time and arms for the smallest, least important person in the room, and that includes you.
- **The turn:** the disciples wave the children off, and he overrules them —
  "Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not."
- **Character shown:** he receives the small and unqualified; the kingdom is a gift you
  take with open hands like a child, not a thing you earn.
- **Whose face carries it:** the CHILDREN and their PARENTS (hopeful, then blocked, then
  gathered in), and the DISCIPLES at the rebuke. Jesus holding the children via master ref.
- **The card question falls out of:** "he had time for the smallest and least important
  person in the room — that includes you." No change needed.
