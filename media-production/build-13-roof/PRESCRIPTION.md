**Row:** 13   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Mark 2:1–12
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 15)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** four friends tore a roof open because their friend could not get there alone — and heaven counted *their* faith for the man on the mat, and went to his deepest wound first.
- **The turn:** "when Jesus saw their faith" — theirs, not his — and the first word he gives the man is *Son*, and forgiveness, before a single leg has moved.
- **Character shown:** he honors the carrying; he heals the shame before the body, and proves the unseen forgiveness with a visible healing.
- **Whose face carries it:** the FOUR FRIENDS ringing the hole in the roof, and the paralyzed man braced for words about his legs. Jesus only via master ref at j1, j2, j3.
- **The card question falls out of:** "where do you find yourself in that room — on the mat, or holding a rope?" — no change needed.
