## Scripture card (KJV, fetched via bible-api.com 2026-07-11)

Mark 2:1-12 read END-TO-END (full text in qc/mark2-kjv.txt). Twelve verses,
ALL twelve in the storyboard — including the scribes (v6-10), which the

### MUST SHOW (facts the text states)
- Capernaum, in a house; word spread that he was home (v1).
- Crowd packed so tight there was no room "not so much as about the door"
  (v2) — he was PREACHING when the ceiling opened.
- The man had palsy and was carried BY FOUR (v3) — four bearers exactly.
- They could not get near "for the press," so they UNCOVERED the roof and
  BROKE IT UP, then let the BED down (v4) — real digging, real debris,
  daylight breaking into a dark packed room from above.
- "When Jesus saw THEIR faith" (v5) — the friends' faith, plural. The
  camera must make the four visible at the moment of the word.
- FIRST words: "Son, thy sins be forgiven thee" (v5) — forgiveness BEFORE
  healing. The legs come later. This order is the whole Seed.
- Scribes sitting there, reasoning IN THEIR HEARTS — not aloud (v6-7).
  Jesus perceived it "in his spirit" (v8) — he answered thoughts.
- The "whether is it easier" question (v9) and the stated reason for the
  miracle (v10): proof of power to forgive.
- "Arise, and take up thy bed, and go thy way into thine house" (v11).
- He arose IMMEDIATELY, took the bed, went out BEFORE THEM ALL; all
  amazed, glorified God: "We never saw it on this fashion" (v12).

### JESUS — HOW TO SHOW HIM (updated to Correction #16, 2026-07-11)
- He IS shown — a real Middle Eastern man in the painterly storybook style,
  warm tan/olive-brown skin, dark hair, and hands visible. NOT a hooded
  void, NOT a black cutout, NOT robe-and-posture-only. The old zero-skin /
  hood-required / no-hands rules are DEAD.
- NEVER white / Caucasian-pale / European — this is the one hard racial
  rule and is non-negotiable. Warm modern Middle Eastern tan.
- Kept omniscient / non-specific: NO sharp photoreal portrait that fixes
  his exact features, and never a clear side profile. Keep the face soft —
  three-quarter, from behind or beside, at distance, downcast, or in gentle
  focus. The cartoon style itself keeps him non-specific; do not push his
  face toward realism.
- NO rim-light/halo/glow outlining his head (Correction #12, still in
  force) — CRITICAL here: the light-shaft from the roof is the film's
  signature; the LIGHT belongs to the man on the mat, not to Jesus. Do not
  stage Jesus inside/against the shaft in a way that traces a glow on his
  head.
- Hands MAY show and rest calmly (no touch happens in this text — he only
  speaks — so keep them at rest, not reaching).
- TIME-OF-DAY (Correction #15a): the text states no night; staged as DAY.
  Interior = dark packed room lit by lamps + the roof shaft once opened;
  street/roof exteriors = honest daylight. No sunset/sunrise drama skies.
- Action logic (#14b/#15b): the bed is lowered on ropes at FOUR corners
  held by the four men above — ropes go UP through the hole to their
  hands, never to nowhere. Diggers dig DOWN through clay from the roof
  side. The lowered man lands INSIDE the room, floor visible under the
  crowd's feet. No figure floats.
- Cyclical motion (#15c) for the digging/lowering clips: hands must
  visibly repeat tear-lift-toss cycles; the mat must visibly DESCEND
  across the clip, not hover.
- Painted tear beads (Correction #3) — the man's undoing at v5 lives in
  his brows/eyes/mouth, wet shining eyes at most.
- Anatomy counts (§4b): crowd + tangled roof scenes are the highest-risk
  frames in this story — count limbs on EVERY figure in every frame.
- No modern objects, no text in images, no photorealism, no canvas
  texture (anti-panel + anti-texture sentences mandatory in wide
  multi-figure stills).

## WHY-LAW / STUDY-GEMS woven into narration
- WHY the house was packed: Capernaum was his home base (v1, "in the
  house" — likely Peter's); fame from the healings had made him
  unreachable by ordinary means. That's the obstacle the friends beat.
- WHY the roof worked: first-century Galilean roofs were flat packed
  clay over reed thatch on beams, reached by an outside stair — you
  could literally dig through one in minutes and repair it in a day.
  (Study-gem so the roof-digging doesn't read as vandalism fantasy.)
- WHY "their faith" matters: the text credits the CARRIERS. One person
  can be carried by the faith of others, and heaven honors it — the Seed.
- WHY forgiveness first: he went to the deepest wound first. In that
  world the man's paralysis was assumed to BE his guilt made visible;
  Jesus cut the assumed root before touching the symptom.
- WHY the scribes gasped: "who can forgive sins but God only?" (v7) —
  their logic was right; the conclusion they refused was the point. The
  healing was staged as EVIDENCE (v10): the visible miracle proved the
  invisible one.
- GEM: he answered thoughts they never spoke (v8) — the scariest moment
  in the room wasn't the ceiling.
- THE SEED: "When Jesus saw THEIR faith." Card = pack card verbatim:
  "Four people tore a roof open because their friend could not get there
  alone. Where do you find yourself in that room?"

## Locks (written into EVERY prompt where the figure/prop appears)

**MAT-MAN LOCK (the paralyzed man):** a thin man in his early thirties
with short dark curly hair and a sparse young beard, olive-skinned,
hollow-cheeked, wearing an undyed flax-linen tunic, his motionless legs
covered by a faded ochre wool blanket — exactly two arms, two hands, two
legs. His face carries embarrassment and fragile hope until v5, then
undone relief. No tear beads ever.

**FOUR-FRIENDS LOCK:** exactly four working men, distinct and repeatable:
(1) a big broad man with a black beard in a walnut-brown wool tunic,
(2) a lean younger man, clean-shaven with cropped dark hair, in a tan
tunic, (3) a wiry gray-bearded older man in a gray-brown tunic,
(4) a round-faced man with a short brown beard in a dark olive tunic.
All dusty, sweat-streaked, rope belts. Exactly four, every roof scene.

**JESUS LOCK (per Correction #16):** a real Middle Eastern man in an
undyed cream wool robe — warm tan/olive skin, dark wavy hair, and hands
visible; NEVER white. Kept omniscient: seen three-quarter, from behind or
beside, at distance, or in soft focus so his exact features are never
fixed, and NEVER a clear front or side portrait. No rim-light, halo, or
glow on or around his head. When present he is never centered, never the
compositional star, never staged inside the light shaft (that light
belongs to the man on the mat); in beats where the text keeps him back he
may sit at the frame's edge or off-frame, but he is no longer a hooded
void.

**PROP LOCK — THE MAT/BED:** a woven reed sleeping mat with a rolled
edge, a rope tied at each of its four corners for the lowering. Same mat
from street to shoulder to ropes to under his arm at the end.

**PROP LOCK — THE HOUSE:** a small flat-roofed Capernaum house of black
basalt stone and pale clay plaster, one low doorway, an outside stone
stair to the roof. Interior: a single packed room, low wooden beams,
small oil lamps. Same house every beat.

**PROP LOCK — THE ROOF HOLE:** ragged-edged opening of broken clay and
reed thatch, blazing daylight pouring through as a visible dust-filled
shaft. Consistent size/position once opened (s4 onward).
