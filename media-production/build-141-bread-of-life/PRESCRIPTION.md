  in you — it's the doorway he built to be filled by.
- **The turn:** "I am the bread of life... shall never hunger" — the crowd came back
  wanting more loaves, and he turns their appetite toward the thing that actually fills.
- **Wound answered:** the "why am I still hungry / still empty" wound — the ache is an
  invitation, and he is what it's for.
- **Whose face carries it:** a HUNGRY PERSON in the crowd — the deeper longing on a
  face, not Jesus's face. Jesus only via master ref / over-the-shoulder as he speaks.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Come hungry. He is the bread that actually
  fills you." Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
