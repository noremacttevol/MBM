## The impact read
- **The one thing:** no one hands himself the authority of God — even Christ was called by
  the Father; that authority is conferred by a call and the laying-on of hands, not assumed.
- **The turn:** kv4 — "no man taketh this honour unto himself, but he that is called of
  God, as was Aaron."
- **Doctrine + GL page:** priesthood authority is conferred, not assumed — called of God,
  as was Aaron. **GL handoff: Priesthood.**
- **Whose face carries it:** Aaron with his bowed head being ordained, and Moses; the high
  priest. Christ only via master ref, over-the-shoulder, at the Father's call. The Father
  is voice/light (green), no face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Where is God calling you?" — lands on the one thing.
