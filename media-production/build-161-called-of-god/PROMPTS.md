Hebrews 5:1-6. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no
motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Priesthood** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: none — #161 is unlisted in CONTENT-CARE.md, GREEN. Milk framing: this is GOOD
news — the right to act for God is not something a person seizes or performs their way into;
it is GIVEN, by God, through a call and the laying-on of hands, the same gentle way it came to
Aaron. No one is shamed; the tone is reverence and reassurance, not gatekeeping.

CHRIST IS NEVER DEPICTED in any frame. Hebrews 5:5 speaks of Christ, but he is never shown,

THE POINT (Why-Law): a high priest is an ordinary man who stands between the people and God
(s1-s2); even a good man cannot simply take that honour for himself (s3); Aaron did not appoint
himself — God called him and he was ordained and anointed by the laying-on of hands (s4-s6);
even Christ (never shown, never depicted) did not make himself a high priest — the Father
called him (s7); and that same pattern, a call and hands laid on, is how the authority is
still given (s8).

AARON LOCK (s1, s2, s4, s5, s6): the high priest — a dignified older man, warm Middle-Eastern
olive-brown skin, a well-kept grey-streaked dark beard, his face shown and kind. His garments
are the scriptural high-priestly vestments and are his signature: a long woven BLUE robe to the
ankles, its hem trimmed with small blue-purple-scarlet pomegranates and little gold bells; over
it the EPHOD woven of gold, blue, purple and scarlet with two onyx shoulder-clasps; on his chest
a square BREASTPLATE set with twelve gemstones in gold settings; a plain white linen tunic shows
at the wrists and ankles beneath; on his head a white linen turban (mitre) with a small gold
plate across the front. Consistent in every shot. He is clearly robed in blue-and-gold, never in
a plain cream robe alone.

MOSES LOCK (s4, s5): an old prophet with a long flowing white beard, weathered olive-brown skin,
in a plain deep-brown wool robe with a faded ochre mantle and a wooden staff; face shown, grave
and kind.

PEOPLE LOCK (s2, s3, s8): ordinary Israelites of every age in undyed earth-tone wool — dun,
faded brown, olive, ochre, grey; faces shown, honest and reverent; never in blue-and-gold priestly
vestments and never in off-white or cream.

MUST NEVER SHOW: Christ is never depicted and no face for the Lord is ever drawn or shown; no
church logos, buildings, or any modern object; no baked-in text or lettering; no gore or
judgment; no one shamed or in peril.
