# PRESCRIPTION — build-110-lords-prayer

**Row:** 110   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Matthew 6:7–13 (Luke 11:1)
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 11)

> Bridge shelf. The wound is **"God is angry / a distant judge you must perform for."** The
> answer is the very first word Jesus gives when asked how to pray — not a technique or a
> ritual, but a family word: *Father*. You talk to God the way a child talks to a good dad.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God is not a distant judge or a force to be impressed — he is a Father
  you belong to, and prayer is a child climbing into his lap and simply talking; you do
  not need the right words, only to begin.
- **The turn:** they expected a technique; instead Jesus hands them "Our Father." The
  family word, in place of a ritual, is the hinge.
- **Wound answered:** "God is angry / has to be performed for." The answer — the prayer God
  loves most may be the simplest one a child ever whispered; no vain repetitions, no
  corner to be admired on.
- **Whose face carries it:** the CHILD / the one praying — the plainness of it, climbing
  into a good Father's lap. Jesus teaching is shown via master ref / over-the-shoulder.
- **The card question falls out of:** "What would you say to him, if you began today?" —
  lands on the one thing. No change needed.
