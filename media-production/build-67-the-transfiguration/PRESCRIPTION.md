## The impact read
- **The one thing:** for one moment three ordinary friends saw who Jesus really is —
  and heaven's whole instruction about him was one sentence: *hear him.*
- **The turn:** the shining — "his clothes turned a blinding white... they saw him
  shining with who he really is." That is the hinge and the most important frame.
- **Character shown:** glory, and then instantly the same gentle friend reaching down
  to lift the terrified men — the majesty and the tenderness are the same person.
- **Whose face carries it:** PETER, JAMES and JOHN — their awe, their fear, their faces
  under the voice. Jesus's transfigured face only via master ref; the emotional beats
  ride the three disciples.
- **The card question falls out of:** "heaven said, hear him — that invitation still
  stands." No change needed.
