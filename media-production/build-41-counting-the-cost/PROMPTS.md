# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #41: Counting the Cost (luke-14_counting-the-cost)

Luke 14:25-35. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — sixteen painted stills, no motion clips.

TIME OF DAY: ONE CONTINUOUS DAY, all of it daylight, and it is stated inside every single
prompt. The road beats (s1-s5) are bright, dusty, hot LATE MORNING. The two parables
(s6-s9) and the salt (s12) are FULL DAYLIGHT. From s10 on the sun sinks: s10-s11 are late
afternoon, and s13-s16 are warm GOLDEN EVENING light. There is no night frame anywhere in
this video and no lamp-lit interior except the finished tower's small window in s14.

WHAT IS NEVER PAINTED IN THIS VIDEO: no cross, no crucifixion, no condemned man, no beam
carried by anyone, no execution of any kind. Luke 14:27 says "bear his cross" and the
narration explains what that meant to people who watched Rome use them — but not one frame
shows it. No battle, no blood, no fighting, no dead men, no weapons held ready: the whole
point of the king parable is the war that does NOT happen, so the opposing army is only a
distant cloud of dust on a far horizon. No writing, letters, numbers or symbols on any prop
anywhere (the builder's tablet, the king's map): every one of them is blank, because the
model paints gibberish text and the style block forbids text in the frame.

Jesus appears in FIVE shots only: s1, s2, s10, s13 and s15. In every one of them the camera
stands behind him, the back of his head is to us, and his face is never shown. Verses 28-32 are PARABLES he is TELLING, so no divine figure of any kind appears in
s6, s7, s8, s9 or s12 — the builder, the king, the envoys and the old woman are ordinary
people, drawn fully, faces shown.

JESUS LOCK (identical wording in s1, s2, s10, s13 and s15; the project-wide look standard):
a first-century Middle-Eastern man, warm olive-brown skin, LONG dark brown-black hair
falling well past the shoulders, one plain undyed off-white cream wool robe (a simple
Judean tunic with a plain mantle, the same garment in every shot), bare-headed with no
hood; no glow, no bright ring, no outlining light of any kind; an ordinary solid man in ordinary
daylight. The camera is always behind him and his face is never shown.

ONLY JESUS WEARS CREAM (build law, carried forward from #40). In every frame where he
appears he is the ONLY figure in an off-white or cream robe. Every other person in the
picture wears a clearly darker earth colour — dun, faded brown, ochre, olive, grey-blue or
maroon — and each of those prompts says so explicitly. Why: in #40 the model dressed a
bearded disciple in cream and centred him, which reads to a viewer as a second Jesus with a
fully painted face. One cream robe per frame, and it is always the man seen from behind.

AND NOBODY ELSE HAS LONG HAIR (added to this build after the FIRST pass of s1, s2, s10 and
s11 — the model did it again, four frames out of four). Colour alone was not enough: told
only "nobody else wears cream," the model painted, in the middle of the empty road behind
him, a bearded man with LONG dark hair past his shoulders in a pale cream robe with a
draped mantle — walking alone, centred, face fully rendered. That is a second Jesus with an
invented face, which is the exact thing the Face Law exists to prevent, and no word of the
prompt was illegal. The signature is TWO things together — long dark hair past the
shoulders AND a cream robe — so BOTH are now reserved for him. Every crowd prompt states
positively: every other man has SHORT hair or his head covered by a head-cloth, not one
other man has long hair falling past his shoulders, and no one else is in cream. And the
open road behind him is never left empty as a stage for the model to fill with a lone
pale-robed figure — the crowd fills it. Check it on every generated frame, not just the
prompt.
