# PRESCRIPTION — build-41-counting-the-cost

**Row:** 41   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Luke 14:25–35
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 13 — see deviation)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he refuses to lie to you about the cost — he tells the whole
  price up front, before you say yes, because he wants the tower standing, not a
  half-built life.
- **The turn:** [n11] the crowd thins, people go home, "and he did not lower the
  price." A man with the biggest crowd he ever had deliberately makes it smaller.
- **Character shown:** honesty that will not manipulate; a leader who counts his own
  cost first ([n15] — he was walking to Jerusalem while he said it).
- **Whose face carries it:** the CROWD on the road (the thousands, then the ones
  turning home) and the builder/king in the two parables. Jesus turned to face them
  ([s25]) — not his face in the parable beats.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what would you ask a man who refuses to lie to
  you about the cost?"

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 13 stills
1. [n1] the enormous crowd filling the road, more joining at every village — "Thousands, filling the road"
2. [s25/n2] Jesus turning around to face the crowd — "he turned, and said unto them"

6. [jv29/n7] a half-built tower — three courses of stone, weeds growing through, onlookers mocking — "a foundation with three courses of stone and weeds growing through it"
7. [j5/n8] the king parable: a king seated, reckoning ten thousand against twenty thousand — "with ten thousand to meet him that cometh against him with twenty thousand"
8. [j6/n9] the wise king sending envoys for terms while the other army is still far off — "he sendeth an ambassage, and desireth conditions of peace"
