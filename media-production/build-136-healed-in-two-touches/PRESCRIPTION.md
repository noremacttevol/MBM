# PRESCRIPTION — build-136-healed-in-two-touches

**Row:** 136   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Mark 8:22–26
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 7 — see deviation)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** healing came in stages, not all at once — half-sight wasn't failure, it
  was the middle of the work — and he is patient enough to touch you twice.
- **The turn:** the SECOND touch — "Jesus put His hands on the man's eyes a second time." The
  moment that says the first touch was never the whole of it.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** *the all-or-nothing God* — the inherited fear that if your
  faith isn't instant and complete, you've failed, and God is disappointed. This cut answers
  it: God works line upon line; the man saw men "as trees walking" and Jesus wasn't done — he
  finished what he had started.
- **Whose face carries it:** the BLIND MAN (half-seeing, blinking, then clear). Jesus
  face-shown via master ref for the two touches — the wonder is on the man's face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Maybe your faith came in stages too. He is patient
  enough to touch you twice." No change needed.
