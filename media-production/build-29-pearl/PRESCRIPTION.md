**Row:** 29   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Matthew 13:45–46
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 7)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** read it one way, one pearl is worth everything you own; read it
  the other way, YOU are the pearl Jesus gave everything to buy — and both are true.
- **The turn:** "some have read it the other way around" — the merchant becomes Jesus
  and the pearl becomes you. That reversal is the hinge and the most important beat.
- **Character shown:** how much you are worth to him — he went looking, found you, and
  gave his own life gladly.
- **Whose face carries it:** the MERCHANT through the search and the find; at the turn,
  the pearl stands for a soul and Jesus is shown only via master ref / over-shoulder.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Have you seen how much you are worth to him?"
  No change needed.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 7 stills
1. [n1/j1] a merchant among the pearl stalls, searching — a life spent seeking — agrees: "a merchant man, seeking goodly pearls"
2. [n2/n3] he examines fine, costly pearls and sets them down, unsatisfied — none is the one — "he kept looking, because not one of them was the one"
3. [n4/n5] he finds THE pearl — a single, perfect pearl held up; his face knows instantly — "a single pearl, more perfect... than anything he had ever held"
4. [j2/n6] home — selling everything: his house, his goods, every other pearl he owns — "went and sold all that he had, and bought it"
5. [n7/n8] joy, not grief — the easiest trade he'll ever make — "he did not do it grieving... the easiest trade you will ever make"
6. [n9/n10] THE TURN — Jesus is the merchant, and YOU are the pearl he sold all to buy (Jesus via master ref; the pearl/soul carries the frame) — "to Jesus, you are the pearl... gave up everything he had, his own life, gladly, to buy you back"
7. [card] warm hold — you were worth all of it to him — "you were the pearl worth everything"
