# FLOW PROMPTS — Story Video #29: The Pearl of Great Price (matthew-13_pearl-of-great-price)

Matthew 13:45-46. STILLS-ONLY (Law E). Short parable the Lord tells: he is kept
FULLY OFF-SCREEN and never shown in any still — only his narrating voice + KJV
lines. Because no Lord figure is ever shown, the gate is trivially safe
(`jesus_face_gate.py` exits 0). The MERCHANT is a character in the story, shown
fully and kept consistent. Nano Banana 2, 9:16, x2, 2K download. Hand-painted 2D
storybook style.

**CHARACTER LOCKS (kept consistent across every still they appear in):**
- **The merchant (s1, s2, s3, s4, s5)** — a dignified, prosperous pearl trader:
  a middle-aged man with a neat dark beard and discerning eyes, in a fine but
  not gaudy deep teal-blue merchant's robe with a warm ochre sash and a small
  leather traveler's satchel at his side. His face IS shown — thoughtful, then
  awestruck, then joyful.
- **The pearl of great price (s3, s5, s6)** — ONE single luminous, perfect
  round white pearl that glows with soft inner light; clearly the most beautiful
  pearl in the story. Always exactly one.
- **Setting** — a first-century eastern port / market town of pearl traders:
  stone stalls, draped cloth awnings, small scales and trays of lesser pearls,
  the sea or harbor glimpsed beyond.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or
captions in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen
in undyed earth colors. No modern objects.

## s1-merchant — Shot 1: a merchant seeking goodly pearls
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A sunlit first-century port market of pearl traders, stone
stalls with draped awnings and the harbor glinting beyond. A dignified pearl
merchant in a fine deep teal-blue robe with an ochre sash and a leather satchel
holds a small pearl up to the light between finger and thumb, examining it with
the practiced, discerning eye of a man who has spent his life seeking the finest
pearls. Other traders and trays of lesser pearls around him. His face is
thoughtful and searching. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge,
9:16 vertical.

## s2-searching — Shot 2: he had seen many, but not THE one
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] At a trader's cloth-draped table spread with trays of good
pearls, the same teal-robed merchant sets a handful of fine pearls back down,
unsatisfied, his brow gently furrowed — none of them is the one he is looking
for. Warm market light, small brass scales beside the trays, the sea beyond.
Still seeking. His face is patient but unfulfilled. One single continuous scene
painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s3-the-pearl — Shot 3: he found one pearl of great price
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same merchant, alone now in a quiet corner of the market
in a shaft of warm light, holds up a single luminous perfect white pearl that
glows with soft inner light, far more beautiful than any other. His face is lit
with awe and recognition — this is the pearl he has searched for his whole life.
Everything else in the frame falls into soft focus around the glowing pearl. One
single continuous scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s4-sells-all — Shot 4: he went and sold all that he had
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The merchant's home courtyard, where he is gladly giving up
everything he owns: buyers carry away his chests, fine cloth, furniture, and his
trays of other pearls, while he hands over the last of it with an open,
unhesitating hand. His wealth is emptying out around him, and there is no grief
in his face, only resolve and a rising joy. Warm daylight. One single continuous
scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s5-buys-it — Shot 5: and bought it, with joy
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The teal-robed merchant, everything else now given away,
stands cradling the single glowing pearl in his two open cupped hands, holding it
close to his heart with profound, quiet joy on his face — utterly content, richer
than he has ever been, though he owns nothing else. Soft warm light gathers
around him and the pearl. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, 9:16
vertical.

## s6-pearl-radiant — Shot 6: the one pearl, radiant
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A close, tender image of the single perfect white pearl held
up in two gentle hands, radiant with soft warm inner light against a softly
glowing background, luminous and priceless and beautiful — the one thing worth
everything. Pure, hushed, uplifting. One single continuous scene painted edge to
edge, 9:16 vertical.
