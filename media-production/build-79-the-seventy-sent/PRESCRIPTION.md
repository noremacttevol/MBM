## The impact read
- **The one thing:** ordinary followers were handed real power — and the joy he
  told them to keep wasn't the power, it was that heaven already knew their names.
- **The turn:** [j3] the redirect — "rejoice not, that the spirits are subject unto
  you; but rather rejoice, because your names are written in heaven." The whole
  video pivots there from *what we can do* to *whose we are*.
- **Character shown:** he trusts small people with big things, then gently pulls
  their eyes off the thrill and onto the thing that lasts.
- **Whose face carries it:** the seventy — their empty-handed setting out and their
  joy coming back. Jesus's face only via master ref on his spoken lines.
- **The card question falls out of:** "your name, written in heaven." No change.
