## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the father *ran* — God does not wait at the door for the one coming home; he runs out to meet him before a word is said, and then goes out a second time for the angry son too.
- **The turn:** "He was still a long way off... when his father saw him. The father ran." Old men in that world did not run. That is the hinge, and the most important picture.
- **Character shown:** a Father who runs to the wasteful son and walks out to the resentful one — welcome before the speech, mercy for both sons.
- **Whose face carries it:** the FATHER's face at the run and the embrace, the younger SON's face (shame breaking into being held), the elder SON's face at the end. This is a parable — the characters are not Jesus, so their faces carry it freely.
- **The card question falls out of:** "which one feels closest to where you are — the son who left, the father who ran, or the brother who stayed and felt unseen?" — lands on the one thing. No change needed.
