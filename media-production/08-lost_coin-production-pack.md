# Story Video #8 — The Lost Coin (Luke 15:8–10)

Runtime 50–60s (shortest story — let it breathe, don't pad). Prepend the
STYLE BLOCK from 00-MASTER-PLAN.md. A parable: the woman may be shown fully;
Jesus is only the narrating voice. Intimate, single-location, lamplit — the
easiest of all 20 to generate well.

## Narration script (app's exact words)

> A woman has ten coins. She loses one.
>
> She lights a lamp. She sweeps the whole house. She searches carefully —
> not casually, carefully — until she finds it.
>
> Then she calls her neighbors and friends to celebrate.
>
> One coin. Out of ten. The joy is disproportionate to the value of the
> coin. *(beat)*
>
> Jesus said the angels celebrate like that over one person who comes home.
> Not a crowd. One. *(peak — music cuts to silence on "One.")*

Closing card (hold 6s): *"Have you ever felt like the thing that got lost
rather than the one doing the searching?"*

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — Ten, then nine
```
Close up, warm evening light: a woman's work-worn hands count small silver
coins from a cloth into her palm — and stop. She counts again, faster. One
short. Her breath catches. The coins tremble slightly in her hand.
```

### Shot 2 — She lights a lamp
```
In a darkening one-room stone house, the woman kneels and lights a clay oil
lamp. The flame catches and blooms, pushing warm gold light into every
corner of the dark room. Her face, lit from below, is set with quiet
determination.
```

### Shot 3 — Not casually — carefully
```
The woman sweeps the packed-earth floor on her knees, lamp beside her,
moving the broom slowly along the base of the walls, pulling baskets out,
reaching into the dark seams of the room. Dust motes swirl gold in the
lamplight. She will not stop.
```

### Shot 4 — Found (the money shot — extra takes)
```
Extreme close up: in a dark crack between floor stones, the broom's straw
passes — and a tiny disc of silver catches the lamplight and FLASHES. The
woman's hand flies to it. She lifts the small coin into the light, holds it
against her chest, and closes her eyes. The lamp flame reflects in the
silver.
```

### Shot 5 — Calling the neighbors
```
Night: the woman stands in her lit doorway calling out joyfully into the
dark street, holding the coin high. Neighbor women come hurrying with their
own lamps, laughing, until the little house doorway is crowded with light
and embracing figures — a disproportionate, wonderful celebration.
```

### Shot 6 — Like that, over one
```
Slow pull back and up from the one small glowing house in a dark hillside
village, its doorway spilling gold light and joy into the night — rising
until the house is a single bright point among the dark hills, under an
immense field of stars that seem to lean toward it.
```

### Closing card
Text on cream, serif. No narration.

## The Seed (what this video must leave behind)

Heaven's joy is over ONE — and the woman searches carefully until she FINDS. Not until she gets tired. Not until it's too late. A God like that does not shrug and accept losing most of what is his. Quiet question: *how far would a search like that go — and would it stop at a grave?* Carried by shots 4 and 6.

## Red-letter lines (KJV) — the Jesus voice speaks ONLY these

A parable: the whole story is his. The narrator carries the telling; the
Jesus voice delivers the two heart-lines in exact KJV.

1. **Where:** over shot 5 (calling the neighbors).
   **KJV (Luke 15:9):** "Rejoice with me; for I have found the piece which
   I had lost."
2. **Where:** over shot 6 (the pull-back to the stars), replacing the
   narrator's angels line — the last spoken words of the video.
   **KJV (Luke 15:10):** "Likewise, I say unto you, there is joy in the
   presence of the angels of God over one sinner that repenteth."
   **Narrator bridge (after, modern):** "Over one. Not a crowd. One."
