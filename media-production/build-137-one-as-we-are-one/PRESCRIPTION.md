## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the night before he died, Jesus prayed that you would be drawn into the
  same oneness he shares with the Father — two distinct persons, one heart — which means you
  were never meant to be outside the circle.
- **The turn:** j2 — "that they may be one, even as we are one… made perfect in one." The
  prayer that would ask believers to melt into one being if "one" meant one person — and
  instead asks for perfect unity between distinct persons, with you brought in.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** *the solitary, unrelatable God — and the loneliness of not
  belonging.* The inherited picture of a single, distant being you can't be near. This cut
  answers it: Father and Son are distinct persons, perfectly one, and Jesus prayed you into
  that same oneness. (No church named; the build references GL: Godhead but no Member handoff
  — this is a Bridge row.)
- **Whose face carries it:** the praying Jesus — face-shown via master ref, behind /
  over-the-shoulder ONLY (the side-profile v1 of s2 was killed for this reason). The Father is
  addressed, never depicted. The two-flames → one-light visual carries the doctrine, not a
  divine figure.
- **The card question falls out of:** "he prayed for you — that you would be one with them,
  as they are one with each other." No change needed.
