# PRESCRIPTION — build-82-anointing-at-bethany

**Row:** 82   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Mark 14:3–9
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 12)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** what you give him is never wasted, whatever the room says —
  he receives your best as worship and promises the whole world will remember it.
- **The turn:** [j1] "Let her alone... she hath wrought a good work on me." The room
  calls it waste; he stops them cold and calls it beautiful. Scorn flips to honor.
- **Character shown:** he defends the one everyone else is scolding, and reads her
  extravagance as the only person in the house who understood what was coming.
- **Whose face carries it:** the woman — silent through the whole scene, scorned,
  then honored. Jesus via master ref when he turns to defend her.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what would it mean to stop holding back from him?" No change.
