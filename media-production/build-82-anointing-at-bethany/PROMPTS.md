[BETHANY-HOUSE LOCK] = a modest house in Bethany at evening: rough warm plaster
walls, a low wooden table set with bread and cups, guests reclining on cushions
around it, oil lamps in wall niches throwing warm pools of light.

[WOMAN LOCK] = the woman: about thirty-five, olive skin, dark hair braided beneath a
soft olive-green head covering (the same in every shot), one modest deep
olive-green robe with an umber shawl — never off-white, never cream, fully and
modestly covered, her bearing quiet resolve and devotion, never staged for
spectacle.

[JAR LOCK] = the alabaster jar: ONE slender-necked flask of pale veined alabaster,
palm-sized, with a narrow sealed neck — the SAME jar (and later the same broken
pieces) in every shot it appears in.

[GUESTS LOCK] = the other guests: grown men in dun, faded brown, grey and maroon
robes — not one of them in off-white or cream, no second man with long dark hair and
a full dark beard who could be mistaken for the man in cream. Every figure: two
arms, two hands, two legs, one head, every limb joined to the correct body.
