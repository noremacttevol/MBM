## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the risen Christ spent his first afternoon not with kings or crowds but walking at the pace of two heartbroken people who had already quit — and he is still in the habit of walking with the ones who have lost hope.
- **The turn:** the breaking of the bread (n6→n7) — the taking, blessing, breaking motion opens their eyes, they know him, and in that instant he is gone. The realization "did not our heart burn within us" (s32) is the aftershock.
- **Character shown:** patience and nearness — he lets himself go unrecognized, listens to all their heartbreak, and opens the scriptures to them the long way before he lets them see.
- **Whose face carries it:** the TWO DISCIPLES (Cleopas especially) — stricken on the road, then the recognition, then the burning-heart wonder. Keep Jesus deliberately unreadable — hooded / from behind / over-the-shoulder — until beat 11; the whole point is that they do not know him.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Is it possible he has been walking with you — in a season you thought you were walking alone?" — falls straight out of the one thing. No change needed.

## The fix
s32 — "did not our heart burn within us" — is the line the whole video is built around and it was not in the old cut. All four blue lines and the recognition sequence need their own frames. The one hard direction: Jesus stays visually unrecognized (hooded / from behind / over-shoulder) through beats 2–11, so the reveal at beat 11–12 actually lands; a picture that shows his face early contradicts the words "their eyes were held" (the Emmaus failure STORY-COVERAGE-LAW warns about). Let beats 11 (the breaking) and 12 (recognition) hold and breathe.
