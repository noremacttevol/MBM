## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he walked past every respectable man in town to call the one person
  everyone had written off — you don't qualify your way in; the door is already open that wide.
- **The turn:** "Follow me" — two words with no conditions attached — and Matthew rising,
  leaving the coins on the table.
- **Character shown:** he seeks out the isolated and the disqualified; the only thing that
  keeps anyone from his table is believing they're already fine.
- **Whose face carries it:** MATTHEW at the booth (rich and alone → called), and the
  OUTCASTS at the table (used to being turned away, for once welcome). Jesus via master
  ref at the call and the red lines.
- **The card question falls out of:** "if the door was open that wide for the man the
  whole town wrote off, what makes you think it's closed to you?" No change needed.
