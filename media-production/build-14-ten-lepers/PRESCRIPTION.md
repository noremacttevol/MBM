# PRESCRIPTION — build-14-ten-lepers

**Row:** 14   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Luke 17:11–19
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 17 — see Deviations)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he heals you in the middle of obeying — before there is one thing to see — and the outsider everyone counted out is the only one who comes back to the Giver.
- **The turn:** on the road, mid-step, before any proof, they were made clean (n6) — the pivot from "obey a bare word" to "it already happened." The second hinge is the one man stopping when he sees his skin and turning back (n7→n8).
- **Character shown:** his mercy lands on all ten, the grateful and the nine alike; and his question at the empty road is grief, not wounded pride — "they got the gift and kept walking, and they missed the giver."
- **Whose face carries it:** the SAMARITAN leper — his face the instant he sees new skin, and face-down in the dust at Jesus's feet. Jesus's face only via master ref when he reaches down to lift him.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Have you ever had to move forward on nothing but a word — before there was any proof?" — lands directly on the one thing (the healing came while they walked). No change needed.

1. [n0] Jesus on the road at the Samaria–Galilee seam, between two peoples who despise each other — agrees: "walked the borderland between Samaria and Galilee"
2. [n1] the ten lepers halted far off across a gap of empty ground, strung along a rise, bandaged, untouched for years — "they stopped far off, strung along a rise, and came no closer... ten men no one had touched in years"
3. [s13/n2] the ten lifting their voices together, calling across the gap — "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us"
4. [n3/j1] Jesus answering across the distance without closing it — "Go shew yourselves unto the priests"

6. [n5] one by one they turn and start walking anyway — "one by one, they turned and started walking anyway"
7. [n6] on the road, mid-step, in the middle of obeying, they are made clean — "on the road, mid-step... they were made clean"
8. [n7] the linen loosening and falling, new skin underneath — "The linen loosened and fell. New skin underneath."
9. [n7] nine of them running on toward the city and their old lives — "Nine of them ran on toward the city"
10. [n7] one of them stopping in the road when he sees what happened — "one of them, when he saw what had happened to him, stopped in the road"
11. [n8] the one turning around, running back, praising God at the top of his voice — "running, praising God at the top of his voice"
12. [n9] the man face-down in the dust at Jesus's feet, pouring out thanks — "threw himself face-down in the dust at Jesus's feet"
13. [n9] the reveal — he is a Samaritan, the outsider the day's religion said did not belong — "he was a Samaritan. The outsider."
14. [n10/j2] Jesus looking at the empty road where the nine had gone — "where are the nine?... save this stranger"

16. [n14] the man rising and walking home a whole man, old wrappings left behind in the road — "walked home a whole man, the old wrappings left behind him in the road"
17. [card/n15] warm hold on the road / the man made whole for the closing question — the card line

## Deviations from the band
Stepped to 17 pictures (band tops at 15) because the narration walks through many distinct actions a lazy cut collapses — the mid-road cleansing, the linen falling, the nine running on, the one stopping, the turn-back, the face-down thanks, the Samaritan reveal, the lifting. Each is a new thing the words make you see. Trust the beats over the band (STORY-BLUEPRINT-SYSTEM Part 3).
