# PRESCRIPTION — build-200-gospel-to-all-the-world

**Row:** 200   **Shelf:** Member   **Scripture:** Matthew 24:14
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Length:** 80–120s band   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 8)

> The catalog's final row. On the Mount of Olives, Jesus turns from the frightening
> road ahead to the one thing that outlasts it — the gospel reaching every person.
> The build is designed as eight beats, with a sacred-silence hold after n1b. Every
> video in this app is verse one of this sentence. Card out of BEATS.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** past every war and hardship, the one thing Jesus said would
  outlast it all is the good news reaching every person — including you — and this
  whole app is verse one of that sentence.
- **The turn:** n1a → j1 — Jesus turns from the long, hard road ahead to the single
  promise that outlasts it: the gospel preached in all the world.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the gospel preached to all nations before the end. Hands off
  to **Gospel Library: Second Coming**.
- **Whose face carries it:** the disciples on the Mount of Olives; Jesus only via
  master ref, over-the-shoulder for j1; then the viewer, handed the task to carry on.
- **The card question falls out of:** "the good news was meant... for you. Come, hear
  it, and carry it on." No change needed.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 8 stills
1. [n0a] Jesus on the Mount of Olives with his disciples, looking out over the long road ahead — agrees: "telling them what the long road ahead would hold"
2. [n0b] the hard road pictured — wars, rumors, hardship, people enduring under strain — "wars, rumors, hardship, and patience under strain"
3. [n1a] Jesus turning from all of that toward the one thing that outlasts it — "the one thing that would outlast all of it"
4. [n1b] a message going out to every corner of the earth (sacred-silence hold follows — let it breathe) — "a message carried to every corner of the earth"
5. [n2a] not a secret kept for a few — "Not a secret kept for a few"
6. [n2b] a witness to all nations, the good news going out until the very end — "A witness given to all nations... going out until the very end"
7. [j1] Jesus speaking the promise, over-the-shoulder onto the listening disciples — "this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world... and then shall the end come"
8. [n3] the task handed to us now — tell it, show it, live it — "The task he gave is still the task we carry"
