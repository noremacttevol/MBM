## The impact read
- **The one thing:** real trust in God always becomes a moving hand — belief that
  never reaches out isn't belief yet.
- **The turn:** James 2:17 — "faith, if it hath not works, is dead, being alone."
  The hollow good-wish (n1) is answered by two people who acted (Abraham, Rahab).
- **Doctrine + GL page:** covenant faith is a principle of action, not a feeling —
  it moves. Hands off to **Gospel Library: Faith**.
- **Whose face carries it:** the neighbor in need, then the doers — Abraham on the
  mountain, Rahab at the wall. Never an abstract "James" face in the key beat.
- **The card question falls out of:** "let your faith move — he sees every small,
  true thing you do." No change needed.
