## The impact read
- **The one thing:** you don't earn your place near him by working yourself ragged — being with him is the good part, and he will not let anyone take it from you.
- **The turn:** Martha's breaking-point outburst → Jesus answering with her own name, said twice and gently, instead of a scolding (w40 → j1). The scene pivots from her frantic serving to his tenderness.
- **Character shown:** he is not troubled by her serving but by the worry underneath it; he defends the woman who simply wanted to be near him, and gives another woman a student's place no one expected her to take.
- **Whose face carries it:** MARTHA — her face tightening as she serves, her face at the outburst, her face as Jesus says her name. Shoot the j1 line over Jesus's shoulder onto Martha. Mary is silent all through (nothing is put in her mouth).
- **The card question falls out of:** "Worried and troubled about many things, or sitting down long enough to listen. Which one sounds more like your life right now?" — falls straight out of the one thing. No change needed.
