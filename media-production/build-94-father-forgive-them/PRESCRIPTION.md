## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the first words out of his mouth on the cross were mercy for
  the men driving the nails — and he said it while it was still happening, not
  after, not on condition of their sorrow.
- **The turn:** instead of a curse, he prays "Father, forgive them." That prayer,
  spoken over the soldiers mid-crime, is the hinge.
- **Character shown:** mercy so wide it covers the ones killing him — which means
  there is no one, and nothing, it can't cover.
- **Whose face carries it:** the SOLDIERS below — the recipients of the mercy,
  oblivious, gambling for his clothes. Jesus's face only via master ref /
  over-the-shoulder, never carrying the beat.
- **The card question falls out of:** "His first words on the cross were mercy for
  the ones who put him there. That mercy has your name in it too." Lands on the one thing.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 10 stills
1. [s33/n0] arrival at Calvary — Jesus raised on the cross between the two criminals — agrees: "which is called Calvary, there they crucified him... one on the right hand, and the other on the left"
2. [n0] the Roman soldiers at their work below, just following orders — "at the hands of soldiers just following orders"
3. [n1] Jesus above them (face via master ref) — every reason to curse, every right to judgment — "He had every reason to curse them. Every right to call down judgment."
4. [s34b] the soldiers parting his garments, casting lots at the foot of the cross — "they parted his raiment, and cast lots"
5. [n2b] close on the gambling hands / dice in the dirt beneath him — what was going on underneath him — "throwing dice for them, right at the foot of the cross"
6. [n2] Jesus drawing breath — not a curse but a prayer forming — "the first words out of his mouth on the cross were a prayer"
7. [j1] the prayer spoken (over-the-shoulder down onto the soldiers) — "Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do"
8. [n3] the soldiers' faces, oblivious, being forgiven mid-crime — "Forgive them now — while it's still happening"
9. [n4a] widen: the mercy reaching the very ones killing him — "If it covered the ones killing him,"
10. [n4b/card] hold on the cross against the sky — nothing you've done it can't cover — "there is no one, and nothing you've done, it can't cover"
