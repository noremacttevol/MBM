# PRESCRIPTION — build-43-the-wedding-garment

**Row:** 43   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Matthew 22:1–14
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 14)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the King fills his hall with people straight off the road and
  hands each one clean clothes at the door — the only way to be shut out is to refuse
  the robe he already bought for you.
- **The turn:** [n10→n11] every guest was handed a festival robe at the door; the one
  man cast out is not too poor, he is the one who said no to the robe. That reframes
  the whole story from judgment to invitation.
- **Character shown:** a King whose welcome reaches the beggars and day-laborers, who
  clothes people who could never make themselves presentable.
- **Whose face carries it:** the ROADSIDE GUESTS being brought in, and the one
  UNROBED MAN with nothing to say. The King is seen, but the guests' faces carry it.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what has he been holding out for you to put on,
  that you keep walking past?"

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 14 stills
1. [s1/n1] Jesus teaching in the temple, the men hunting to arrest him standing in front of him — "the men who were hunting for a way to arrest him were standing right in front of him"
2. [jv2/n2] the king's wedding hall for his son — tables loaded, hall full of light — "the tables were loaded, the hall was full of light"

7. [jv8_9/n7/n8] the king sending servants out to the open roads instead of the guest list — "Go ye therefore into the highways"
8. [jv10/n9] servants gathering everyone off the road, bad and good, the hall filling up — "gathered together all as many as they found, both bad and good"
9. [n10] each road-guest handed a clean festival robe at the door by the king's servants — "the clean festival robe was the king's to give, handed to every guest at the door"
10. [n11/jv12] the king entering, finding one man still in his own dusty road clothes — "how camest thou in hither not having a wedding garment?"
11. [n12] "Friend" — the king's word — the man with nothing to say — "Friend... and the man had nothing to say"
12. [n13/jv13] the man put back outside, into the dark, away from the open light — "Back outside, in the dark, away from a light that had been standing wide open for him"
13. [jv14/n14/n15] the ones who came and let the King clothe them, filling the table — "the ones who came, and who let the King put the clean clothes on them"
14. [n16/card] the clean clothes bought, folded, waiting inside the open door — "the clean clothes are already bought and folded and waiting inside" — card
