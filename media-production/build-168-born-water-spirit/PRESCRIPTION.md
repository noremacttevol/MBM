- **The turn:** "Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter" (kv5b) — Jesus turns "born again" from a puzzle into a plain, non-negotiable gate. That is the hinge.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the ordinance-gate of baptism and the gift of the Holy Ghost, required of all. Hands off to **Gospel Library → Baptism.**

- **The card question falls out of:** "When you stand before it, will you go through?" — lands straight on the one thing.
