# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #168: "Born of water and of the Spirit" (john-3_born-of-water-and-spirit)

John 3:5 (with 3:1-8). MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills,
no motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Baptism** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk. The note is the GATE: Nicodemus, a respected ruler and
teacher, comes by night and learns that to enter the kingdom of God a person must be born
again — born of water (baptism) AND of the Spirit (the gift of the Holy Ghost). Warm,
searching, hopeful. Study gem: the gate is the gate — required for everyone, even the wise and
good.

CHRIST / GOD / THE SPIRIT IS NEVER DEPICTED as a figure — no face, no body, no shape of a
person. The one Nicodemus comes to, and the Spirit, are shown ONLY as warm radiant light —
never a figure, never a face, never a dove. (This keeps the shot blocks free of any divine
proper noun, so the face-gate passes.)

THE POINT (Why-Law): Nicodemus, a distinguished teacher, comes secretly by night with his
questions (s1); he comes to the warm light and listens (s2); and hears the astonishing thing —
a person must be born again to see the kingdom (s3 — John 3:3); he is baffled, how can a grown
man be born again? (s4 — John 3:4); the answer is not physical: born of WATER, the baptism (s5);
and of the SPIRIT, the gift from above (s6); for only so can anyone enter the kingdom of God
(s7 — John 3:5, the named verse); and the same one gate is set before you (s8).

NICODEMUS LOCK (s1, s2, s4): NICODEMUS — a dignified older first-century ruler and teacher,
grey-bearded, in fine but DARK rich earth-tone robes (deep brown, dark olive, deep russet, with
a dark mantle) — clearly a man of standing, yet humble and searching; never in cream or
off-white, no bright ring around the head.
THE HEAVENLY LIGHT (s2, s3, s6, s7, s8): warm radiant golden light — only light, never a face,
never a figure, never a body, hand or dove within it.
NIGHT (s1, s2): deep blue night, lit by warm lamplight and the soft glow the seeker comes toward.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or body of God, the risen Lord, or the Spirit anywhere —
only warm light; no dove; no modern object; no cross, steeple, church-building-with-a-spire, or
insignia of any group; no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no baked-in text, letters or
numbers; no human dressed in cream or off-white; no nudity — modestly robed even in the water.
Every image is ONE single unbroken full-frame scene — no panels, no comic strip, no dividing
lines, no painted border or torn-paper edge; the artwork fills the whole frame.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.
