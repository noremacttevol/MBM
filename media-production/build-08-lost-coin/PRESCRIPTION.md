# PRESCRIPTION — build-08-lost-coin

**Row:** 8   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Luke 15:8–10
**Tier:** T2 Encounter (reads T1 — see deviation)   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 → **planned 8 (see deviation)**

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** heaven's joy is out of all proportion to the odds — one coin out of ten, one sinner out of a crowd, and all of heaven throws a party.
- **The turn:** she finds it, and the story stops counting value and starts counting joy — she calls in the neighbors over a single coin.
- **Character shown:** God searches diligently for the one, and rejoices louder than the loss ever hurt.
- **Whose face carries it:** the WOMAN in the parable — her worry as she sweeps, her joy when she finds it. This is Jesus's parable; her lines are his, so her face carries it freely.
- **The card question falls out of:** "have you ever felt like the thing that got lost, rather than the one doing the searching?" — no change needed.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 8 stills
1. [n0] Jesus choosing this story to show how God feels about one lost soul (over-shoulder / listeners) — "he didn't talk about crowds. He told this story"
2. [jv8/n1] a woman with ten silver coins, one now missing (her face) — "having ten pieces of silver, if she lose one piece" / "She loses one"
3. [n2a] she lights a lamp and sweeps the whole house — "She lights a lamp. She sweeps the whole house"
4. [n2b] the woman searching carefully, low to the floor, until she finds it — "seek diligently till she find it"

6. [n4] the single coin held up — joy out of all proportion to its value — "The joy is disproportionate to the value of the coin"
7. [j2/n5] Jesus: likewise, joy among the angels of God over one sinner — "joy in the presence of the angels of God over one sinner that repenteth"
8. [card] warm hold on the found coin / the rejoicing woman, for the closing question — the card line

## Deviations from the band
Stepped to 8 pictures because this is a three-verse parable with one image and one small arc — light the lamp, sweep, search, find, rejoice. That is 8 distinct moments; padding to 10 would repeat the sweep/search image. It reads as a T1 Saying/Verse (6–9 band), and the beats decide the count.
