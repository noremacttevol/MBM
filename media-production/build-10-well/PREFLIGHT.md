## Scripture card (KJV, fetched via bible-api.com 2026-07-10)

John 4:4-30 read END-TO-END (full text in qc/john4-kjv.txt); vv.39-42

### MUST SHOW (facts the text states)
- Jesus wearied by the journey, sitting on the well; about the sixth hour —
  NOON (v6). The heat and the hour are the story's front door.
- The woman comes to draw water ALONE at noon (v7) — the social exile is
  the WHY behind the hour; the town knew her story (v18, v29).
- Jesus speaks FIRST and asks HER for a drink (v7) — a Jew asking a
  Samaritan woman, breaking every custom (v9 says so in the text).
- The living-water offer (vv13-14) — Jesus's exact KJV words.
- He knows about the five husbands and the man now — and he SAYS it
  without turning away (vv17-18); she calls him a prophet (v19).
- "I that speak unto thee am he" (v26) — the first plain "I am the
  Messiah" in the gospel, said to HER.
- She LEAVES THE WATERPOT (v28) — the jar left behind is in the text.
- She runs to the town SHE AVOIDED and says "Come, see a man, which told
  me all things that ever I did" (v29) — and they come out to him (v30).

### MUST NEVER SHOW
- Jesus's face — NEVER, at any distance where a face would read.
- Jesus's hands — NEVER (PRODUCTION-BIBLE outranks the pack's shot 4
  "weathered hands offer the cup." Restaged: the cup sits ON the well rim
  in sunlight, already poured, sleeve edge at most at frame edge — the
  offer reads through the cup and the light, not through his hands).
- Jesus touching her — the text has no touch; none is staged.
- Full-back centered Jesus filling a frame (Correction #1) — he is a
  seated traveler at frame EDGE, in thin shade / silhouetted by glare,
  or an over-the-shoulder foreground shape per the approved #09 pattern.
- Painted tear beads on cheeks (Correction #3) — shot 5's "tears rising
  without falling" = wet shining EYES only; every close-up zoom-checked.
- Modern objects, text in image, photorealism (locked look).

## WHY-LAW / STUDY-GEMS woven into narration
- WHY noon: women drew water in the cool morning, together. Noon alone
  means she was avoiding them — or they had made it plain she wasn't
  welcome.
- WHY the shock (v9): Jews and Samaritans despised each other for 700
  years — and a rabbi did not speak to an unknown woman in public. He
  broke both walls with one sentence, and he was the one asking for help.
- GEM: Jacob's well — the well itself was a patriarch's gift (v12), deep
  and famous; she thought he was claiming to out-give Jacob. He was.
- GEM (v26): the first person Jesus ever told plainly "I am the Messiah"
  was not a king, not a priest, not even one of the twelve — a Samaritan
  woman with five marriages behind her, at the bottom of every list her
  world kept.
- TRANSLATION bridge after vv13-14: a well INSIDE you — he wasn't talking
  about the water in the jar; he was talking about the thirst underneath
  the thirst.
- THE SEED: he already knew everything she had done — and he stayed in
  the conversation. Being fully known and being loved were the same
  moment. Closing question from the pack, verbatim.

## Locks (written into EVERY prompt where the figure appears)

**WOMAN LOCK:** a Samaritan woman in her late thirties with tired,
guarded dark eyes that later come alive, olive skin, dark hair covered by
a dusty pale-blue headscarf, wearing a simple ochre-brown wool robe with
a woven striped sash, leather sandals — exactly two arms, two hands, two
feet. (Blue scarf + ochre robe chosen to read instantly against the gold
noon light and hold between beats — Correction #4.)

**JESUS LOCK:** a road-worn traveler seen only as a seated shape at the
edge of frame — undyed cream wool robe, dusty feet in worn sandals,
shoulders tired, face always fully hidden by angle, glare, or shade; his
hands never visible, sleeves fall past them.

**PROP LOCK — THE JAR:** one large round-bellied clay water jar,
terracotta orange-brown with a rope sling, big enough to need a shoulder.
Same jar in every beat it appears (shots 1, 3, 6).

**PROP LOCK — THE WELL:** a low round stone well with a wide flat rim of
pale sun-bleached stones, a wooden crossbeam, and a rope. Same well
throughout.
