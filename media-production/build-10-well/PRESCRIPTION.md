# PRESCRIPTION — build-10-well

**Row:** 10   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** John 4:1–42
**Tier:** T3 Full Arc   **Target length:** 120–165s   **Pictures:** 16–24 (planned: 19)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he fully knew her — every chapter the town whispered about — and still spoke to her with respect, and told her plainly what he told no one else: that he is the Messiah.
- **The turn:** "I that speak unto thee am he." The first person he ever tells plainly is a Samaritan woman at the bottom of every list her world kept.
- **Character shown:** he crosses every wall (Jew/Samaritan, man/woman, clean/shamed) by *needing her help*, and honors the outsider with the biggest secret.
- **Whose face carries it:** the WOMAN — her guardedness, her exposure, her wonder, her run to the town. Jesus only via master ref at j1, j2.
- **The card question falls out of:** "have you ever been searching for something — and realized later it was deeper than what you thought you wanted?" — no change needed.
