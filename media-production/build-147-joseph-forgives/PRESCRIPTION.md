> Bridge wound: **the silent heaven of the wasted years — "God wasn't in it, and the
> harm done to me was just harm."** The inherited picture is a God absent during
> betrayal and loss. Joseph names the exact evil done to him, refuses to soften it,
> and still sees God's hand turning it toward rescue: "ye thought evil... but God
> meant it unto good."

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the years you count as wasted, and the harm done on purpose, are
  not outside God's reach — he can mean for good what others meant for evil.
- **The turn:** "I am Joseph your brother, whom ye sold into Egypt" — the powerful
  stranger reveals himself to the men who betrayed him, and instead of revenge, gives
  them the words that close the wound.
- **Wound answered:** the silent-heaven wound — God was working through the betrayal
  the whole time, to preserve life; the wasted years get redeemed, not erased.
- **Whose face carries it:** the BROTHERS — guilt and terror turning to relief — and
  JOSEPH's tears. Old Testament: Joseph and the brothers on master-ref sheets; no Jesus.
- **The card question falls out of:** "What others meant for harm, God can mean for
  good. Let it go." Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
