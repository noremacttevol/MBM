# PRESCRIPTION — build-06-two-sons

**Row:** 6   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Matthew 21:28–32
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 11)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the yes that matters is the one your feet make — the son who said no and went beat the son who said yes and stayed home.
- **The turn:** the first son, all morning, getting up and going to the vineyard he had refused — the no that would not leave him alone.
- **Character shown (told to leaders):** Jesus lets the confident religious men convict themselves; the door is open to whoever actually turns and goes.
- **Whose face carries it:** the two SONS — the first son's refusal then his wrestling then his work; the leaders' faces when the story turns on them. This is a parable; the characters carry it freely.
- **The card question falls out of:** "have you ever said no — maybe loudly — and found yourself moving toward it anyway?" — no change needed.
