## The impact read
- **The one thing:** at Peter's very worst moment — mid-denial, caught in a lie —
  Jesus looks at him not with scorn but with a love that already knew and chose him
  anyway.
- **The turn:** the rooster crows and Jesus turns and looks straight across the
  courtyard at Peter. That silent look is the hinge — the story pivots from Peter's
  hiding to Peter's breaking.
- **Character shown:** mercy that meets betrayal with a look of love, not
  condemnation. He knew Peter would fall and kept him anyway.
- **Whose face carries it:** PETER throughout — the hiding, the denials, the memory
  landing, the weeping. Jesus's face appears only in the LOOK; even there shoot
  over Jesus's shoulder onto Peter's face receiving it. Never Jesus's face carrying
  the emotional beat.
- **The card question falls out of:** "He looked at Peter in his worst moment — and
  still chose him. He looks at you the same way." Lands directly on the one thing.

with limited distinct movement, and its power is two held-silence beats — the LOOK

frames to reach 16 would pad the sacred pause the story is built on. Trust the beats.
