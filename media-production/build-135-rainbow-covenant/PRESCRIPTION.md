# PRESCRIPTION — build-135-rainbow-covenant

**Row:** 135   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Genesis 8:22; 9:1–16
**Tier:** table says T2 Encounter — **reads T3 Full Arc** (see deviation)   **Target length:** 120–165s   **Pictures:** 12

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God met his people's fear of the sky not with a warning but by binding
  himself — a one-way promise, forever — and he hung the reminder where he himself would see it.
- **The turn:** jv13 — "I do set my bow in the cloud… a token of a covenant." The war-bow
  goes up unstrung, aimed away from the earth: the warrior hangs up his weapon, the storm
  between heaven and earth is over.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** *the unpredictable, threatening God you brace against* — the
  inherited fear that God might turn on you again, that a dark sky means doom. This cut
  answers it: Noah signs nothing and earns nothing; God binds himself, one way, for free, and
  "answers fear with beauty instead of blame."
- **Whose face carries it:** NOAH and his family — the eight standing in the wreckage of a
  drowned world, braced against the first clouds, then faces lifted. God is NOT depicted as a
  figure (green voice from heaven); the covenant sign — the bow in the cloud — carries the
  divine beat. (Character-law: needs the Noah sheet; QUEUE flags #135 = Noah.)
- **The card question falls out of:** "The next rainbow you see is God remembering. What
  might change for you, if you believed he keeps his promises?" No change needed.

the returning clouds, and the covenant, to the bow. Honest beat read = 12 pictures, at the top
of the T2 count band (10–15) but at genuine T3 length (120–165s). Prescribing to the story, not
the table row.
