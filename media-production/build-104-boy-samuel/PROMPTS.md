# STILL PROMPTS — Video #104: The Boy Samuel (1samuel-3_the-boy-samuel)

1 Samuel 3:1-19. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — ten painted stills. BRIDGE/MILK.
CARE FLAGS: none shown — GREEN. The hard message God gives Samuel about Eli's house is
kept OFF-SCREEN; the video's heart is a child being called by name and learning to
listen. No violence, no fear.

NO DIVINE FIGURE ANYWHERE. God is a voice and a gentle radiant presence and is NEVER
depicted as a person — where the LORD speaks or "comes and stands," the picture is only
soft warm light and the listening boy; no figure, no face, no shape, no hand in the
light. (No Jesus is shown in any frame; nobody wears cream or off-white except the
boy's plain linen tunic is a pale natural linen — keep it muted oatmeal-linen, NOT
bright white, and it is a child's simple tunic, not a cream wool robe.)

SAMUEL LOCK (s2,s3,s4,s6,s7,s8,s9,s10): a young boy of about eleven or twelve, warm
olive-tan skin, dark curly hair, in a simple sleeveless natural oatmeal-linen tunic (a
plain child's ephod) belted at the waist — bare-armed, barefoot or in simple sandals,
gentle open face, shown clearly and kept consistent. As he grows in the last frame he
is a little older but plainly the same boy.

ELI LOCK (s4,s6,s7): a very old high priest, near ninety, heavy and slow with great age,
long white beard and white hair, dim tired eyes, warm olive skin deeply lined, in an
ankle-length priestly robe of deep indigo-blue with a wide dark sash — never cream or
off-white; kind and fatherly. His face is shown, consistent.

SETTING LOCK: the ancient tabernacle sanctuary at Shiloh — a dim stone-and-timber holy
place hung with heavy woven curtains, the golden seven-branched LAMP OF GOD burning low
with small warm flames, the curtained inner shrine at the far end. Night, warm lamplight
and deep shadow.

MUST NEVER SHOW: any depiction of God/the LORD as a person, face, figure or hand; any
figure or shape inside the light; anything frightening; any modern object; any baked-in
text; a bright-white cream wool robe on anyone; a multi-panel or comic-strip layout.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single tall upright vertical scene painted edge to edge, no panels."
Skip any comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.
