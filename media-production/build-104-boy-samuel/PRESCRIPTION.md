# PRESCRIPTION — build-104-boy-samuel

**Row:** 104   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** 1 Samuel 3
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 11)

> Bridge shelf. The wound is **"heaven is silent now."** The story opens by naming it —
> "the word of the LORD was rare" — and answers it with a child learning to stop running
> around and simply listen. God was speaking all along; hearing is what had to be learned.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God still speaks — patiently, by name, in the dark — and what he was
  waiting for was not a worthy man but a heart small enough and open enough to stop
  running and say, speak, I am listening.
- **The turn:** Samuel lying back down and answering the voice himself — "Speak; for thy
  servant heareth." The running stops; the listening begins. That is the hinge.
- **Wound answered:** "heaven is silent / the word of the LORD is rare." The answer is God
  calling a child by name in exactly that silent time — hearing was the thing missing, not
  his voice.
- **Whose face carries it:** SAMUEL — the boy sitting bolt upright, standing in the
  doorway ready to work, lying down to wait, answering into the dark; and old Eli's face
  at the moment he understands. God is a voice, never a face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "could you say it too — speak, I am listening?" —
  lands on the one thing. No change needed.
