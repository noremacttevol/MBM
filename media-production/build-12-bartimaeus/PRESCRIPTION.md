## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the one voice everyone tried to shut off is the one that stopped him in the road — Jesus, a week from the cross, halts a whole procession for a blind beggar and asks him what he wants.
- **The turn:** "Jesus stood still." The crowd's hushing meets a King who stops.
- **Character shown:** he asks the man's own voice instead of the crowd's guess; he honors faith and sets him free to go anywhere.
- **Whose face carries it:** BARTIMAEUS — listening at the roadside, shouting louder, throwing off his cloak, his new eyes on the road. Jesus only via master ref at j1, j2.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what is the thing you would shout for, if no one could tell you to be quiet?" — no change needed.
