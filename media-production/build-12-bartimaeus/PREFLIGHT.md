### MUST SHOW (facts the text states)
- Jericho, the highway side, a GREAT crowd leaving the city with him
  (v46) — daytime, dusty road, procession scale.
- Bartimaeus is BLIND and BEGGING (v46) — clouded unseeing eyes that
  read instantly (Relative-Physicality Lock) but stay dignified
  (Physicality Calibration — a grown man, never a caricature of misery).
- He HEARD it was Jesus (v47) — he lives by listening; head tilted,
  tracking sound. His shout uses the title SON OF DAVID — the blind man
  recognized the Messiah the seeing crowd only called "of Nazareth."
- MANY charged him to be quiet; he cried THE MORE A GREAT DEAL (v48) —
  the held beat of the whole video.
- Jesus STOOD STILL (v49) — the entire procession stops for one voice.
- The crowd's turnaround (v49b): the same voices that hushed him now say
  rise, he's calling you — narrator keeps this line, modern.
- He CAST AWAY his garment, ROSE, and CAME (v50) — the beggar's cloak
  (his coin-catcher, his everything) left in the dust.
- Jesus ASKS instead of assuming (v51) — j1, exact KJV.
- "Lord, that I might receive my sight" — his own voice, modernized by
  the narrator ("Rabbi — I want to see").
- IMMEDIATELY he received his sight and FOLLOWED HIM IN THE WAY (v52) —
  the road up to Jerusalem; free to go his way, he chose Jesus's way.

### MUST NEVER SHOW
- Jesus's face — NEVER, from ANY angle (Correction #13): no frontal, no
  profile, no three-quarter, no jaw/cheek/nose sliver. Camera directly
  behind, over-the-shoulder, or true distance ONLY. No void-hood
  openings, no black-cutout silhouettes.
- ZERO visible skin on the Jesus figure, anywhere, ever (Correction
  #14): no hands, wrists, neck, ear, nothing pale inside the hood
  shadow. Robe, hood, posture — period. Sleeves fallen past hands in
  every frame. Audited at high zoom AND phone scale.
- NO rim-light, halo, glow, or "shape in the light" outlining his head
  or figure (Correction #12). The production pack's shot 4 ("a distant
  robed figure — seen only as a shape in the light") and shot 6 ("warm
  out-of-focus light" as his stand-in) are RESTAGED: he is a whole,
  plainly-lit figure seen from behind (Correction #8 — a person in the
  scene, not light or a floating presence).
- Jesus's hands — never (no rescue-touch beat in this story; Correction
  #2's allowance does not apply — the healing is by WORD, v52).
- A small detached rear-view Jesus away from where people look
  (Correction #11): in s6 every stopped head visibly turns back toward
  the beggar ALONG the road that leads to the Jesus figure; s9 is the
  textbook over-the-shoulder frame.
- Jesus touching Bartimaeus's eyes — the text has NO touch; healing is
  spoken. (Other gospels' healings differ; we film MARK.)
- Painted tear-beads sitting on cheeks (Correction #3) — s10b allows wet
  shining eyes and tear TRACKS through road-dust, never beads.
- Bartimaeus's blindness exaggerated into grotesqueness — calibrated:
  milky/clouded irises, ordinary face otherwise.
- Time-of-Day Law: v46 is a daytime departure scene — warm DAYLIGHT,
  never night, and not staged as a sunset/sunrise drama.
- Modern objects; text in image; photorealism; canvas/fabric texture
  (anti-texture sentence on standby for drift rerolls).

- Pack runtime (60-75s) superseded by story-driven runtime (~4 min) like

- v47/v48 "Son of David" title — KEPT as the recognition gem.

## WHY-LAW / STUDY-GEMS woven into narration
- WHY he was there: Jericho was the last stop before the Passover road
  up to Jerusalem — huge pilgrim crowds, a beggar's best season. This is
  Jesus's FINAL journey; days later comes the cross. One blind beggar's
  voice stopped that procession.
- WHY "Son of David" matters: it is a Messiah title. The man who could
  not see recognized him; the crowd with working eyes said only "Jesus
  of Nazareth." Mark's quiet point: who here is really blind?
- WHY the crowd shushed him: a shouting beggar at a rabbi's procession
  was shameful noise — respectable religion telling a desperate man his
  need was an embarrassment.
- WHY the question (v51): Jesus asks what is obvious because he wants
  the man's OWN voice — not the crowd's guess about him. God honors a
  person saying what they actually want.
- GEM: the cloak (v50) — livelihood, bed, coin-catcher, everything;
  cast off BEFORE he was healed. He spent his faith in advance.
- GEM: "followed him in the way" (v52) — free to go his own way, the
  way he chose was the road Jesus was walking.
- THE SEED: the crowd said hush; Jesus stopped everything. When religion
  silences a desperate voice, God overrules religion. Card = pack card
  verbatim.

## Locks (written into EVERY prompt where the figure/prop appears)

**BARTIMAEUS LOCK:** a lean weathered grown man in his mid-forties,
sun-browned skin, tangled shoulder-length dark hair, short rough dark
beard, pale milky-gray clouded eyes that clearly do not see (dignified,
ordinary face otherwise — never grotesque), wearing a patched
oatmeal-colored rough wool tunic with a simple cord belt — exactly two
arms, two hands, two legs. AFTER the healing (s10b, s11): the SAME face,
hair, beard, tunic, but the eyes deep warm brown and clear.

**PROP LOCK — THE BEGGAR'S CLOAK:** one ragged sand-brown wool cloak with
frayed edges. s1-s5: draped over his shoulders and spread across his lap,
its hem flat on the dust in front of him with a few small dull coins on
it. s8: the same cloak mid-air behind him, coins scattering out of it
into the dust. s9 onward: GONE — he is in the tunic only, and stays that
way through s11 (Wardrobe-hold, Correction #4).

**JESUS LOCK (Corrections #13 + #14):** an undyed cream wool robe, hood
up, seen ONLY from directly behind or over-the-shoulder — the BACK of his
hooded head to camera, his face always on the far side, away from view.
Zero visible skin anywhere: sleeves fallen fully past his hands, no neck,
no ear, no pale tone inside the hood. No light or glow on or around his
head; he reads as a plainly lit robed figure among the crowd, never a
black cutout, never a shape made of light. Every render audited at full
zoom and phone scale for any skin or facial feature before banking.

**PROP LOCK — THE ROAD:** the dusty Jericho highway: pale packed earth,
low stone walls and scrubby olive trees at its edges, distant hills.
Same road every beat.
