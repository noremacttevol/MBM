## s1 — Beside the road (STILL)
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Bright warm midday light on the dusty highway leading
out of Jericho, pale packed earth with low stone walls and scrubby olive
trees at its edges. At the road's edge sits [BARTIMAEUS LOCK], cross-legged
in the dust, a single ragged sand-brown wool cloak with frayed edges draped
over his shoulders and spread across his lap, the cloak's hem lying flat on
the ground in front of him with three small dull bronze coins resting on
it. His head is tilted slightly, listening. Several travelers' sandaled
feet and robe hems pass him by in the foreground, every walker continuing
on without pausing. Full daylight, blue-white sky. One single continuous
scene painted edge to edge.

## CLIP A — The leap and the cloak (Veo 3.1 Fast, 8s, Frames-to-video from banked s8)
[CLIP STYLE BLOCK] A lean weathered man in his mid-forties with tangled
shoulder-length dark hair, a short rough dark beard, and pale milky-gray
clouded unseeing eyes, wearing a patched oatmeal-colored rough wool tunic
with a simple cord belt, exactly two arms, two hands, and two legs, leaps
up from sitting at the edge of a bright dusty daylight road and surges
forward with both hands reaching out ahead of him. As he rises, his single
ragged sand-brown wool cloak slides off his shoulders and flies backward
away from him through the air behind him, and three small bronze coins
tumble out of the cloak, scattering onto the dust of the road behind him
as the cloak lands crumpled in the dust. He keeps his tunic on and moves
forward away from the fallen cloak, joy breaking across his face. He is
the only person in the frame for the entire video. The cloak and coins
travel backward and land behind him; he travels forward.
