# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #84: Calling the Fishermen (matthew-4_calling-the-fishermen)

Matthew 4:18-22 (with Mark 1:16-20 in view). STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — twelve painted
stills, no motion clips.

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk (CONTENT-CARE.md). Nothing violent, nothing to
soften. Milk framing: this is GOOD news of the plainest kind — the Lord does not begin
his kingdom with priests or scholars but walks up to four working men at their nets, on
an ordinary morning, with wet rope in their hands, and calls them exactly as they are.
He does not ask them to become someone else first; he takes what they already are —
fishers — and says he will make them fishers of men. The WHY the whole video carries:
he came to THEM, in the middle of a normal workday, and the wonder of the story is not
that they were worthy but that he chose the ordinary. The cost is real and shown gently
(James and John leave their father Zebedee in the boat), but the weight of the story is
the KINDNESS and the authority of the invitation, never guilt. Closing card is an
invitation, never a fear-question.

Jesus appears in only FOUR shots — s3, s4, s8 and s10. In every one the camera is
BEHIND him or over his shoulder, the back of his head to us, his face never shown,
never visible, at any angle. The other stills (s1, s2, s5, s6, s7, s9, s11, s12) have no
divine figure: the fishermen, the boats, the nets and the old father are ordinary things
and ordinary people, faces shown.
