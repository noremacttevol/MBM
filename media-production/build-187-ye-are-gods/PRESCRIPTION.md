- **The one thing:** Jesus stood on their own scriptures to show that the God who calls
  men his children — even "gods" — is telling the truth about who they, and he, really are.
- **The turn:** John 10:36 — "because I said, I am the Son of God?" Jesus turns their
  own charge of blasphemy back on their own book. That is the hinge.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** divine potential — "Ye are gods; children of the most High,"
  Jesus quoted it and meant it. Hands off to **Gospel Library → Divine Potential**.
- **Whose face carries it:** the LEADERS — hostility hardening into being cornered.
  Jesus is shown via master ref / over-the-shoulder (j2 is JESUS-red), standing firm,
  never an emotional close-up.
- **The card question falls out of:** "He stood on the scriptures they claimed to love.
  Come know him as he truly is." Lands on the one thing. No change.
