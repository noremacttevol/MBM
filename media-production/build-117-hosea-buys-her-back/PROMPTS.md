# STILL PROMPTS — Video #117: Hosea Buys Her Back (hosea-1_hosea-buys-her-back)

Hosea 1–3. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — ten painted stills. MILK. CARE FLAGS: D, L —
redemption at the price paid, NEVER the scandal; scripture's own economy of words about what
she was; nothing explicit. God commanded the prophet Hosea to love a wife who had wandered
far from him — a living picture of God's own love for a people who strayed. Hosea goes and
BUYS HER BACK at a price, lifts her up, covers her, and brings her home; and God promises to
betroth her to himself forever in mercy. Warm, tender, redeeming. The whole video is about
being loved home, never about the sin she left.

NO DIVINE FIGURE ANYWHERE. Where God speaks or promises, the picture is warm radiant light —
no figure, no face, no shape, no hand-of-God-as-a-person. HOSEA the prophet is a HUMAN and
DOES show his face — a kind, gentle bearded man; keep him identical every frame: warm
Middle-Eastern skin, dark hair and a short dark beard, a deep russet-brown robe with a
rust-red sash. GOMER, his wife, is a young woman with dark hair; keep her identical: a dusty
ochre dress and a faded dull-blue headscarf. Nobody wears cream or off-white (there is no
Jesus figure in this video — Jesus is never shown, so cream never appears). Nothing about
her past is depicted — no seduction, no other man, no explicit anything; her straying is only
ever a lone figure walking away down a road. The story's meaning is carried by the price
paid, the hand that lifts her, and the road home.

illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
