# PRESCRIPTION — build-117-hosea-buys-her-back

**Row:** 117   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Hosea 1–3 (2:5–20; 3:1–2)
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 12)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** however far you wandered, he does not wait at the door with a
  list of your failures — he comes looking, pays the price himself, and calls you home.
- **The turn:** Hosea finding her low in a far marketplace and *buying her back* —
  "he did not lecture her. He paid for her." Redeeming love stops being a metaphor
  and becomes silver counted out.
- **Wound answered (too far gone / unwanted — come-remember):** the fear that you
  wandered too far to be wanted back. The whole marriage is God dramatizing that
  "you are not too far gone to be wanted."
- **Whose face carries it:** the WOMAN — her drifting, her being lifted off the ground
  and covered, her turning homeward — and HOSEA searching and paying. God is a voice,
  no face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what would it mean to be loved home like that?"
  Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
