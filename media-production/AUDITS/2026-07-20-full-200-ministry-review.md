# FULL 200 MINISTRY REVIEW — every row judged against its shelf's purpose (2026-07-20)

Cameron's standard, restated so there is no doubt what this review is measuring:
- **Rows 1–100 (EVERYONE):** would this story make a stranger with no faith background
  love Jesus and want the next one?
- **Rows 101–150 (BRIDGE):** does this answer a real wound left by inherited theology,
  and does it raise the better question — without naming the church?
- **Rows 151–200 (MEMBER):** does this prove, from the Bible alone, that a restoration
  was needed and happened — and point deeper?
- Across all: truth as the restored gospel teaches it, not as the creeds bent it.
  Nothing missing that obviously belongs.

Repeats were already purged (6 rows, see 2026-07-20-repeat-audit.md). This is the
judgment pass on everything that remains.

- **23 vineyard workers** — land on "I choose to give," the offense of generosity.

  Jesus's own picture has the dead conscious, in two conditions, before any final judgment.
- **43 wedding garment** — land on "everyone got invited," handle the outer-darkness line
  briefly and honestly.
- **45 wicked tenants · 32 talents** — accountability parables; keep the weight on the
  owner's patience (he sent messenger after messenger).
- **36 shrewd steward** — flagged as the WEAKEST milk row. Scholars argue about what it
  even means; a stranger will just be confused. It is approved, so it stays — but it is
  first in line for an add-on companion that makes its point ("use money for people,
  because people last") or for rotation out of the first 100 in a later corpus.
- **59 feeding 4000** — only earns its slot as a deliberate add-on to 58 ("he did it
  AGAIN — compassion is not a one-time event"). Script must say so, or it plays as a rerun.
- **68 multitudes healed** — thin as a story; works as a breather beat. Keep, low priority.
- **17 Lazarus (deferred redo)** — when rebuilt: the weeping and the raising. The "I am
  the resurrection" saying belongs to 144; do not double it.

**Milk gap check:** is anything missing a stranger needs? The annunciation and the
centurion-at-the-cross were cut for space — acceptable losses; the arc holds without
them. No change ordered.

## SHELF 2 — BRIDGE (101–150)

**Verdict: after the purge, every wound named in the spec has real answers.** Rulings:

- **Silent heaven (101–108): covered and strong.** Still small voice, Jacob's ladder,
  Samuel learning to hear, Moses face-to-face, Hebrews' "God spake," the Baptist's doubt
  answered kindly, sheep hear his voice. NOTE on **105 (Ex 33:11):** the script must
  anticipate the standard objection ("no man hath seen God") the way a real conversation
  would — the same Exodus chapter holds both verses; let the tension BE the better question.
- **Angry God (109–120): covered and strong.** Bread-not-stone, Our Father, sparrows,
  Beatitudes, God comes LOOKING in Eden, Abraham negotiating mercy, the ram provided,
  graven on palms, Hosea, Jonah, the fourth man in the fire, Job. This is the best
  section on the shelf.
- **Dead religion (121–132): covered.** NOTE on **125 "I never knew you":** aimed at
  wounded people, so the tone is diagnosis-of-religion, never threat-at-the-viewer —
  the point is knowing him vs. knowing about him. **128 (new, Mark 7 traditions of men)**
  is now the keystone of this section — Jesus himself saying tradition can void the word
  of God. That row must be built with extra care; it carries Cameron's core argument.
- **One-size heaven (133–138): transformed by the purge.** What Jesus called hell (133),
  paradise-not-yet-heaven (134), two touches / line upon line (136), one-as-we-are-one
  (137), offspring of God (138), many-rooms verse stays via live 185 on the member shelf.
  These four new rows are the shelf's sharpest better-questions. Build them before the
  other unbuilt rows — they are the reason this shelf exists.
- **Come remember (139–150): covered.** Lamp, Naaman (new — do the simple thing again),
  the I AMs, Joseph forgiving wasted years, Ruth grafted in, Hannah heard, Psalm 23.

**Bridge bench (wounds real people carry that the 50 doesn't yet answer — first
candidates for the weekly post-launch batches, NOT swapped in now):**
- **Hurt BY church people** → Ezekiel 34: shepherds who fed themselves, and God's answer —
  "I myself will search for my sheep." The single best text for the person burned by clergy.
- **Bitterness at what life took** → Ruth 1, "call me Mara" — Naomi came back bitter and
  was still gathered in.
- **Shame that keeps you out** → Peter restored (John 21) already lives at milk 19; a
  bridge add-on could frame it for the returner.

## SHELF 3 — MEMBER (151–200)

**Verdict: every row earns its slot; the restoration case builds in the right order.**
Foretold (151–160) → authority (161–167) → covenant path (168–174) → temples (175–177) →
nature of God (178–189) → discipleship (190–197) → gathering (198–200). Rulings:

- 151 James 1:5 first is exactly right — the verse that opened the dispensation opens
  the shelf. 200 (gospel to all the world) closing it is exactly right.
- **169 baptism of Jesus / 168 born of water** — same scenes as milk 69/4 by DESIGN:
  the member cut must open by building on the milk video (add-on law), never retell.
  Same for 162 (keys) over milk 103, and 170 (sacrament) over milk 89.
- **187 "ye are gods"** — doctrinally the boldest row. Keep: Jesus himself quoted Psalm 82
  and meant it (John 10:34). Script stays inside what the two texts say.
- **171 baptism for the dead / 172–173 gospel to the dead** — the strongest
  bible-verses-the-church-rarely-gets-credit-for section. Keep exactly as designed.

**Member gaps found (real holes, queued as the FIRST post-launch batch — the launch 200
is full and nothing on this shelf is weak enough to evict):**
1. **Eternal marriage and family** — the app's audience is LDS members and there is not
   one row on it. Matt 19:4–6 "what therefore God hath joined together" + 1 Cor 11:11
   "neither is the man without the woman, neither the woman without the man, in the Lord."
   Pairs with 174 (Elijah, hearts of the fathers). This is the biggest single gap in
   the whole 200.
2. **The Melchizedek Priesthood by name** — 161 (Heb 5:4, called as Aaron) proves
   authority must be conferred, but Heb 5–7 + Ps 110:4 ("a priest for ever after the
   order of Melchisedec") names the HIGHER priesthood the restoration restored. Completes
   the authority section.
3. **Daniel 1 (pulse and water)** — the Bible's own Word-of-Wisdom story: young men who
   refused the king's fare and stood healthier and wiser. Discipleship section has
   tithing and fasting but nothing on the body as a stewardship.
