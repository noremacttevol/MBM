## CONTINUITY LOCKS

[UPPER-ROOM LOCK] = the same upper room every shot: warm stone walls, low wooden
table with flat bread, simple clay cups and one shared clay pitcher, floor cushions,
two hanging oil lamps giving even amber light, one small window showing deep blue
night.

[DISCIPLES LOCK] = the disciples: ordinary Middle Eastern men in earth-toned wool —
browns, grays, olive greens, dark blues — varied ages and beards, none in off-white
or cream, none resembling the man in cream. Faces warm and attentive, never afraid.
