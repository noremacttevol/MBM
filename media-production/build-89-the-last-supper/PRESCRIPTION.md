## The impact read
- **The one thing:** on the last night he had, he gave them something to hold — the
  sacrament isn't a ritual he left behind, it's the table he still hosts.
- **The turn:** [j1] "This is my body... this do in remembrance of me." The Passover
  meal becomes the memorial he hands over, piece by piece. That's the hinge.
- **Character shown:** tenderness — "with desire I have desired to eat this passover
  with you" — a God whose last wish was to sit and eat with his friends.
- **Whose face carries it:** the disciples receiving the bread and cup — their hands,
  their faces. Jesus via master ref through the sacrament actions (his hands
  breaking bread, offering the cup; hands / over-the-shoulder).
- **The card question falls out of:** "Come to his table — he is still the host." No change.

## Deviations from the band
Landed at **13 pictures**, under the T3 band (16–24). Two reasons, both deliberate:
(1) the betrayal (Luke 22:21–22, "the hand of him that betrayeth me is with me on
the table") is kept OUT — it turns a video about the gift into a video about the
betrayal and needs meat-level context on Judas; reverence over completeness. (2)
What's left is an intimate scene whose weight sits in held sacrament moments (the
bread, the cup) rather than many cuts — the bread gets three beats and the cup
three, which is the honest read. Trusting the beats over the range.
