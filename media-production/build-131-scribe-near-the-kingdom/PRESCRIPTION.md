## The impact read
- **The one thing:** honest questions don't push you away from God — a man who thinks
  clearly and out loud is told, to his face, that he is not far from the kingdom.
- **The turn:** j1 — "Thou art not far from the kingdom of God." The verdict on an honest
  questioner is nearness, not suspicion.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** *dead religion / the God who rejects the questioner* — the
  inherited fear that doubt and hard questions disqualify you, that thinking pushes you
  further out. This cut answers it: the scribe came with "a real question, not a trap,"
  agreed out loud and went further than he was asked — and Jesus told him honest thinking
  had put him a step from the door.
- **Whose face carries it:** the SCRIBE (the honest questioner — sincerity, then agreement,
  then recognition). Jesus face-shown via master ref answering.
- **The card question falls out of:** "You may be closer than you think. Love God, love your
  neighbor — and step through the door." No change needed.
