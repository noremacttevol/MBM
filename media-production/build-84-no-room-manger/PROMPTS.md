# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #84: No Room — the Manger (luke-2_no-room-the-manger)

Luke 2:1-7. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — twelve painted stills, no motion clips.

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk (CONTENT-CARE.md). The birth is shown with complete
modesty — never graphic, never a labour scene; the moment of delivery is only ever implied
by Mary resting and the swaddled child. Milk framing: the God who made everything arrives
with nowhere to stay, laid in a feed-trough because the world was full — and that is the
GOOD news, that he came all the way down to the lowest room in the house, to the people the
world had no space for. The WHY the whole video carries: there was no room for him then, so
that there would always be room for us now. Closing card is an invitation, never a fear-question.

FACE LAW FOR THE INFANT: the newborn is the Christ, and his face is NEVER shown or
constructed — no infant face, no features. In every shot where the child appears (s6-s9) he
is a small bundle wrapped in plain swaddling linen, seen from BEHIND the parents, from ABOVE
at a distance, in soft shadow, or with his face turned away — never a face toward the camera.
The swaddling linen is the one off-white/cream cloth in the whole video, because it wraps
him; NOBODY else wears off-white or cream (Mary in blue, Joseph in brown, everyone else in
darker earth colours), so the eye is never pulled onto a made-up face of Christ.

MARY LOCK (s2-s9) — a young woman of about eighteen, warm olive-brown skin, a gentle
face, in a deep BLUE mantle over a soft dusk-rose tunic with a simple headscarf, clearly
NOT cream. Great with child in s2-s5; tender and radiant after the birth. Face shown.

JOSEPH LOCK (s2-s9) — a strong working man of about thirty-five, a short dark beard, a
kind weathered face, in a plain BROWN wool tunic and mantle, clearly NOT cream. Face shown,
protective and gentle throughout.

INNKEEPER LOCK (s4) — an ordinary Bethlehem householder, tired and apologetic, NOT cruel,
in a dun-grey tunic, clearly NOT cream. Face shown, genuinely regretful that he is full.

STABLE LOCK (s5-s9) — a humble stone stable / cave lit by one small oil lamp: straw, a
rough wooden manger (a feed-trough), a patient ox and a grey donkey, warm shadows.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt body adds "flat clean illustrated storybook look, not a heavy textured oil
painting" and "fills the entire frame edge to edge with no border, no frame and no cream
margin" (a border and an oil-paint drift both leaked in build-72). Every prompt ends with
"One single continuous scene painted edge to edge." Skip any comic-strip or multi-panel
variant the model returns.
