# PRESCRIPTION — build-84-no-room-manger

**Row:** 84   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Luke 2:1–7
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 13)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the King everyone was too busy to make room for came all the
  way to the bottom, on purpose — so that no one who comes to him would ever be
  turned away.
- **The turn:** [v7] the baby laid in the manger. The lowest room in the town
  becomes the one with space, and the feed-trough becomes a throne. That's the hinge.
- **Character shown:** a God who comes small, poor, and reachable — who did not
  force his way in.
- **Whose face carries it:** Mary and Joseph gazing at the child. The newborn's
  face is shown via master ref (nativity, not an emotional-witness beat).
- **The card question falls out of:** "what makes you think there is no room for you near him?" No change.
