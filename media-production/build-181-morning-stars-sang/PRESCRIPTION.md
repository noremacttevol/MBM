## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God answers your suffering not with an explanation but by showing
  you he was there before the beginning — and that the beginning was joy.
- **The turn:** Job 38:7 — "the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of God
  shouted for joy." God's answer to *why* is a memory of the first morning; that singing
  sky is the hinge.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the Council in Heaven — the sons of God present and rejoicing
  at creation, before mortal birth. Hands off to **Gospel Library → Council in Heaven**.
- **Whose face carries it:** JOB — his grief, his upward look, his comfort. God is the
  whirlwind / the voice (g4, s1 are GOD-green), never a face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "The stars sang when the world began. Your story
  is still being written by the same hand." Lands on the one thing. No change.
