# PRESCRIPTION — build-160-stone-cut

**Row:** 160   **Shelf:** Member   **Scripture:** Daniel 2:44–45
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 10)

> Nebuchadnezzar's dream, read by Daniel — the statue of empires and the stone cut
> without hands that shatters them and fills the earth. The beats follow the vision
> image by image, then turn on God's everlasting kingdom. No Jesus face; the vision
> carries it.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** every human kingdom rises and falls, but God is setting up one
  kingdom — cut from the mountain by no human hand — that will never be destroyed and
  will fill the whole earth.
- **The turn:** kv44 / n5 — the stone growing to fill the earth: "the God of heaven set
  up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed."
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the kingdom of God restored in the last days, never to be
  destroyed. **GL handoff: Kingdom of God.**
- **Whose face carries it:** Daniel the interpreter and the troubled king; the stone,
  statue, and mountain carry the vision. No Jesus's face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "will you belong to it?" — lands on the one thing.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 10 stills
1. [n1] the troubled king, his wise men unable; young Daniel brought in to explain — agrees: "a young exile named Daniel... was brought in to explain it"
2. [n2] the towering statue — gold head, silver chest, bronze belly, iron legs, iron-and-clay feet — the great kingdoms — "a head of gold, chest of silver, belly of bronze, legs of iron, and feet of iron mixed with crumbling clay"
3. [n3,kv45] a single stone cut out of the mountain by no human hand, breaking free on its own — "the stone was cut out of the mountain without hands"
4. [n4,kv45] the stone striking the statue at its feet; the whole towering thing shattering — "struck the great statue at its feet — the whole towering thing came crashing down"
5. [n4] the shards blown away like chaff on the wind, not a trace left where it stood — "blew away on the wind like chaff, until not a trace was left"
6. [n5] the stone growing into a mountain — "It became a mountain"
7. [n5] the mountain filling the whole earth, from one end to the other — "until it filled the whole earth, from one end of it to the other"
8. [n6] Daniel interpreting to the king — empires rise, fall, and are forgotten — "Every human empire, however golden, would rise and then fall"
9. [kv44,n7] the God of heaven sets up a kingdom that shall never be destroyed and shall stand for ever — "shall set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed... it shall stand for ever"
10. [n8,card] the everlasting kingdom offered to you; a face turned toward it — warm hold for the closing question — "When that kingdom is offered to you, will you belong to it?"
