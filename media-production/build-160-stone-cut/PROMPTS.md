# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #160: "The stone cut without hands" (daniel-2_stone-cut-without-hands)

Daniel 2:44-45. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no
motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Kingdom of God** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: handle with care. The great statue is a DREAM VISION shown in the king's mind —
render it as a shimmering, translucent, dreamlike apparition, NEVER a solid idol being
worshipped, NEVER bowed to. No real person is harmed when it shatters (it is a dream image
crumbling). Milk framing: hope and permanence — human empires fall, but God sets up a
kingdom that never ends. Awe and reassurance, not dread.

CHRIST IS NEVER DEPICTED in any frame; God and his kingdom are shown ONLY as warm light —
never a figure, never a face, never a shape, never a hand-of-God.

THE POINT (Why-Law): Daniel interprets the king's troubling dream (s1); the dream statue of
four metals stands for the empires of men (s2); a stone is cut from a mountain by no human
hand (s3 — Dan 2:45); the stone strikes and shatters the whole image (s4, s5); the stone
grows into a mountain filling the earth (s6); God sets up a kingdom that shall never be
destroyed (s7 — Dan 2:44); a kingdom that stands for ever, offered to all (s8).

DANIEL LOCK (s1 — the prophet): the prophet Daniel, a wise young Middle-Eastern man of about
thirty-five, dark hair and a short dark beard, calm and dignified, wearing the modest robes
of a Babylonian-court exile in deep earth tones — dark brown, olive, muted indigo (never
white, cream, off-white or pale). Face shown, steady and kind.

KING LOCK (s1): King Nebuchadnezzar, an older Middle-Eastern king seated on a throne in
rich but DARK royal robes of deep purple and dark red with a simple gold circlet (rich dark
colors, never cream or pale), troubled and attentive. Attendants in dark earth tones.

THE DREAM STATUE (s2, s4, s5): a colossal shimmering translucent DREAM apparition of a
standing statue — a head of gold, chest and arms of silver, belly of bronze, legs of iron,
feet of iron mixed with grey clay — glowing faintly and semi-transparent like a vision, set
against a dark dreamlike void, clearly not a solid real object, never worshipped.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or shape of God or Christ anywhere — the kingdom is
only warm light; no one bowing to or worshipping the statue; no gore, no real person harmed;
no modern object; no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no baked-in text, letters or
numbers; no human dressed in cream or off-white. Every image is ONE single unbroken
full-frame scene — no panels, no comic strip, no dividing lines.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A great rugged mountainside at dawn. High on the rock, a single large smooth stone breaks free and comes loose from the mountain entirely on its own — with no workman, no chisel, no tool and no hand anywhere near it — a soft warm light resting on it as it separates, cut out without hands.
