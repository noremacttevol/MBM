## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he saw the woman no one saw — bent, silent, at the back for eighteen years — called her first before she asked, and gave her back her name as a daughter of the covenant.
- **The turn:** he stops teaching, looks past every face to the back wall, and calls her over — worth first, healing second.
- **Character shown:** he sees the overlooked; he defends her freedom over the rules that would make it wait.
- **Whose face carries it:** the WOMAN — her face at the ground, then lifting straight for the first time in eighteen years. Jesus only via master ref at j1, j2.
- **The card question falls out of:** "is there something you have been carrying so long you have stopped expecting it to change? He called her first." — no change needed.
