## The impact read
- **The one thing:** a love that will not force you — Jesus looked at the sincere young man, loved him, named the one thing between them, and then let him walk away without chasing him, loving him the whole time.
- **The turn:** "Jesus, looking at him, loved him" — and out of that love comes the hardest sentence; the hinge is the loved look, not the sad ending.
- **Character shown:** he does not lower the bar to keep a follower, and he does not stop loving the one who cannot pay it.
- **Whose face carries it:** the young RULER — running, kneeling, his face falling, his back as he walks away. Jesus only via master ref, over-the-shoulder onto the man for the loved look.
- **The card question falls out of:** "is there something you already know — quietly — that stands between you and fully following what you believe?" — no change needed.
