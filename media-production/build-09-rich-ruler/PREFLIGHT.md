Built per PRODUCTION-BIBLE §4b. Checked ON PAPER before any credit is spent.

## Scripture card (KJV, fetched via bible-api.com 2026-07-09)

Mark 10:17-27, read END-TO-END. Full text banked in this session.

### MUST SHOW (facts the text states)
- Jesus setting out on a journey; the man comes RUNNING and KNEELS (v17)
- He asks the eternal-life question sincerely (v17)
- He has kept the commandments from his youth — and means it (v20)
- Jesus BEHOLDING him LOVED him (v21a) — the money beat; carried by light
  and the young man's face, never by showing Jesus
- The one thing: sell, give to the poor, treasure in heaven, come follow (v21b)
- He was SAD at that saying and went away GRIEVED (v22)
- WHY: "for he had great possessions" (v22)
- Jesus lets him go — no chasing, no lowered bar (the whole point)

### MUST NEVER SHOW
- Jesus's face — NEVER, at any distance where a face would read
- Jesus's hands — NEVER. The pack's shot 6 says "hands open at his sides";
  PRODUCTION-BIBLE outranks the pack: the watching figure is staged from
  behind at middle distance with long sleeves fully covering the hands,
  arms relaxed — the OPEN posture reads from the shoulders and stance.
- A softened ending. NO reconciliation shot, no return, no smile at the end.
  The pack is explicit: "it ends in sorrow, and stays there."
- The young man looking back (v22 — he went away; the text gives no look back)
- Modern objects, text in image, photorealism (locked look)

## FULL-STORY check (the pack vs. the scripture)
- Pack quotes Mark 10:21 as "take up THY cross" — the actual KJV reads
  "take up THE cross." CORRECTED. Two-Voice Law demands exact KJV.
- Pack omits v18 ("Why callest thou me good?") and the naming of the
  commandments (v19). Narrator carries both in modern words (Translation
  Law) — they explain WHY the man can answer "all these have I observed."
- vv23-27 (camel and needle's eye, "with God all things are possible")
  are CONSCIOUSLY EXCLUDED — not an accidental omission. The pack's
  directive is explicit and theological: the young man's story ends at
  v22, and the app's whole theology of agency lives in that unsoftened
  ending. vv23-27 shift to the disciples and would soften the close.
  This is a documented pack directive, not a FULL-STORY violation.
- Runtime story-driven (~2.5-3 min), superseding the pack's 60s line.

## WHY-LAW / STUDY-GEMS woven into narration
- WHY the running matters: wealthy men in fine robes did NOT run in public —
  it was undignified. He ran anyway, and knelt in the dust. He wanted the
  answer that badly.
- GEM (v21): "Jesus beholding him loved him" — Mark says it straight out,
  of this one man, right before the hard sentence. The hardest thing Jesus
  says in this story is said with love in it.
- GEM: "come, follow me" — the same call given to Peter, Andrew, James,
  John. This was an invitation into the inner circle. It came wrapped in
  the one thing this man couldn't put down.
- TRANSLATION (before j1): "One thing thou lackest" = "you're missing one
  thing." Not one more rule — one thing standing between him and God.
- GEM (v22): the text tells us why he grieved: "for he had great
  possessions." He didn't walk away because he stopped believing. He
  walked away because the price was the thing he loved most.
- THE SEED: he loved him AND let him walk away. No coercion, no lowered
  bar, no chasing. A love that will not force you.

## Two-Voice Law — Jesus lines (exact KJV, Jesus voice ONLY)

## Character / wardrobe locks (written into EVERY prompt where they appear)
- YOUNG MAN LOCK: a young man in his mid-twenties with short dark hair and
  a clean-shaven earnest face, wearing fine rich robes of deep blue and
  cream linen with gold embroidered trim, long loose sleeves that cover
  both arms all the way down to his wrists, gold rings on his fingers, a
  heavy leather money purse at his belt, fine leather sandals. His wealth
  reads instantly against everyone else's undyed wool.
- JESUS LOCK: a robed figure in plain undyed wool seen ONLY from behind or
  at middle distance, never close enough for a face, long sleeves fully
  covering the hands at all times. NOT luminous in this story (an ordinary
  dusty road in daylight) — his presence is carried by the light that
  falls on the young man's face, not by glow.
- ROAD LOCK: a dusty dirt road winding through dry golden-brown Judean
  hills, a fine walled estate visible on a far hill. Morning gold light
  s1-s5; heavy amber dusk s6-s8.

## Production findings (during asset generation)
- s2 (kneeling) first take was missing the gold rings and money purse —
  fixed with an in-place edit rather than a regen; wardrobe locks held.
- Face consistency: s3 generated FIRST and banked as the reference face
  (per the build-07 lesson); s2/s4/s5 all verified against s3 at zoom.
  The same face holds across every close-up in the finished video.
- THE RUN CLIP SAGA (s1) — the big lesson of this build:
  1. Original Veo clip passed browser-side scrubs, but post-assembly
     frame extraction caught a bare HAND with fingers on the foreground
     Jesus figure. Browser scrubs are NOT sufficient QC.
  2. Direct Veo regen with hardened positive wording ("only draped wool
     visible where hands would be, no skin visible anywhere on the
     foreground figure") STILL produced a bare hand at 0.5-4.5s —
     confirmed by local frame crops. That was failure #2, triggering the
     build-07 rule: go straight to still-anchor + Frames-to-video.
  3. Still-anchor attempt #1: Nano Banana rendered the near-foreground
     "from behind, cropped at the chest" figure as a side PROFILE with
     visible beard and nose — a face on the Jesus figure. Rejected.
  4. Restaged composition: near-foreground figure ELIMINATED; the robed
     figure made small and DISTANT down the road, fully from behind —
     "only a small simple shape of undyed wool cloth and the back of a
     head of dark hair, no facial features and no hands visible at that
     distance." Anchor PASSED zoom QC, banked as s1-run-anchor.jpeg (2K).
  5. Frames-to-video (Start frame + motion prompt, 10 credits): output
     clip PASSED full local frame QC at every second 0.5-7.5s, including
     dedicated crops of the distant-figure region at closest approach.
     Banked as assets/s1-the-run.mp4; video rebuilt.
- LESSONS (carry to every future build):
  - The hands-NEVER wording must explicitly cover EVERY figure in frame
    that represents Jesus, not just "the figure."
  - Near-foreground Jesus figures are high-risk for both faces and
    hands. Prefer distant-from-behind staging whenever the beat allows.
  - Definitive clip QC is LOCAL: download first, extract frames every
    second with ffmpeg, view full frames PLUS dedicated crop zooms of
    any Jesus-figure region at its closest approach — BEFORE banking.
    Never bank a clip on browser scrubs alone.
- Both clip downloads produced "(1)" duplicates in ~/Downloads; deleted.
