## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the God you fear has forgotten you has carved you into his own
  hands — you are the thing he cannot help but see, always in front of him.
- **The turn:** God's open palms with your name graven into them — "I have graven
  thee upon the palms of my hands." The fear of being overlooked breaks against a
  permanent engraving.
- **Wound answered (silent heaven / forgotten):** "the LORD hath forgotten me... I
  never really mattered enough to be remembered." The whole verse is God's tender
  answer to exactly that fear.
- **Whose face carries it:** the FORGOTTEN PERSON (Zion pictured as one weary,
  overlooked individual), and the MOTHER with her newborn. God is the graven palms —
  hands, never a face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "where do you most need to hear that you have
  not been forgotten?" Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
