## The impact read
- **The one thing:** even at his own arrest, on the worst night of his life, he heals
  the enemy sent to seize him — there is nobody on the wrong side of the sword he is
  unwilling to reach toward.
- **The turn:** he stops Peter, then turns *away* from his own rescue and toward the
  wounded enemy — the last miracle he does as a free man is on the man arresting him.
- **Character shown:** mercy toward the enemy, and a refusal to let the moment become
  about violence — he lays his life down on purpose and won't spill one drop to save it.
- **Whose face carries it:** PETER through the swing (loyal, brave, wrong), then MALCHUS
  at the healing (the enemy walking home whole). Jesus's face only via master ref /
  over-the-shoulder at the red lines and the touch.
- **The card question falls out of:** "no enemy too far for his mercy — including the
  one you're afraid is in you." No change needed.
