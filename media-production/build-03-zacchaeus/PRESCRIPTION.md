## The impact read
- **The one thing:** Jesus moves first — he invites himself to the outcast's table *before* the man changes a single thing, and being wanted is what changes him.
- **The turn:** Jesus stopping under that exact tree, looking up, and calling him by name. The seeker in the tree is found by the one he was trying to glimpse.
- **Character shown:** the Son of man came to seek and save the lost — he chooses the worst-thought-of man's house on purpose.
- **Whose face carries it:** ZACCHAEUS — up the tree straining for a glimpse, then his face at the table when he is named a son of Abraham. Jesus only via master ref, looking up.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what would it mean to be wanted like that, exactly as you are right now?" — no change needed.
