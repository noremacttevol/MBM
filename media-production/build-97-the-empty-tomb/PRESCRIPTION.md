## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the tomb couldn't hold him, and the very stone the women were
  dreading was already gone before they arrived — whatever feels dead and sealed
  shut, he's the God of the rolled-away stone.
- **The turn:** the angels' question, "Why seek ye the living among the dead? He is
  not here, but is risen." That sentence is the hinge — grief flips to impossible news.
- **Character shown:** power over death, and a God who removes the obstacle you're
  still worrying about before you even reach it.
- **Whose face carries it:** the WOMEN throughout — the worry on the road, the fear
  inside the empty tomb, the recognition dawning. Jesus does not appear in this scene.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Whatever feels dead and sealed shut in your
  life — he's the God of the rolled-away stone." Lands on the one thing.
