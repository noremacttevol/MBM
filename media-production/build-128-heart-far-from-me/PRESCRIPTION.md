## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God was never impressed by the performance of religion — he
  looks straight past the custom for the honest heart behind it, and he would always
  rather have the real one than a flawless show.
- **The turn:** the Isaiah quote (j1) — "their heart is far from me." The leaders came
  to accuse the disciples; in one line the complaint is turned back onto the accusers.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** *dead religion* — the inherited picture of a God who
  wants the right words, the right washing, the right customs. This cut answers it: he
  "was never counting the handwashing," he wanted "what stood behind it," and he would
  rather have an honest heart than a perfect performance.
- **Whose face carries it:** the RELIGIOUS LEADERS' faces (the accusers being answered),
  then the ordinary honest face at the close. Jesus is face-shown via master ref while
  answering (s3) — not a close emotional Jesus beat.
- **The card question falls out of:** "He would always rather have an honest heart than
  a flawless performance. Come to him with the real one." No change needed.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 7 stills
1. [n1a] s1-the-complaint — the religious leaders arriving, faces set, leveling a
   complaint at Jesus — agrees: "religious leaders came to Jesus with a complaint"
2. [n1b] s2-the-simple-meal — cutaway: the disciples eating simply with unwashed hands,
   an ordinary meal (n1 splits mid-segment here) — "eating with unwashed hands — breaking
   not God's law, but a tradition the elders had added"
3. [n2] s3-he-answers — Jesus (master ref) unmoved, answering, about to quote Isaiah —
   "He answered them with words the prophet Isaiah had written long before"
4. [j1 → n3 → j2] s4-lips-near-heart-far — the image of lip-service: honoring with the
   mouth while the heart stands far off (n3 retells, j2 states the same indictment —
   commandment of God laid aside for tradition of men — over the held image) — "This
   people honoureth me with their lips, but their heart is far from me"
5. [n4] s5-the-corban-excuse — the vivid example: a man labeling his money a gift to the
   temple and using the label to give nothing to his aging parents — "use that label as
   an excuse to give nothing to his own aging parents"
6. [j3] s6-he-names-it — Jesus naming it plainly: the word of God cancelled by their own
   tradition — "Making the word of God of none effect through your tradition"
7. [n5] s7-the-honest-heart — the close: an ordinary, honest heart turned toward him;
   warm hold for the closing question — "He would always rather have an honest heart…
   Come to him with the real one"

## Deviations from the band

spare: one confrontation, no scene changes, no travel — it reads at the T1/low-T2
boundary and the honest beat read gives 7 distinct moments. Not starved: 7 stills carry
7 beats, one per moment. Padding to 10+ would invent scenes Mark 7 does not paint.
