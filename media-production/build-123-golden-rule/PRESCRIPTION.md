## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the whole law of how to treat each other folds into one line you
  can start living today — treat others the way you long to be treated, and you've
  kept nearly all of it at once.
- **The turn:** Jesus turning the longing outward — "the bread you wish someone would
  hand you when you are empty — hand it to the next person at your door." Your own
  ache becomes the measuring line for how you treat everyone else.
- **Wound answered (dead religion / law as burden):** the picture of God's law as a
  heavy list of rules. This shows the whole law as one act of imagination — "not a
  weight Jesus laid on us... the shortest way to everything the law was trying to teach."
- **Whose face carries it:** the ORDINARY PEOPLE giving and receiving — the doer and
  the one lifted. Jesus is shown teaching, over-the-shoulder onto the listeners.
- **The card question falls out of:** "who is the one person, this week, you could
  treat the way you have always wished someone would treat you?" Lands on the one
  thing. No change needed.
