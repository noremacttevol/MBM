# STILL PROMPTS — Video #123: The Golden Rule (matthew-7_golden-rule)

Matthew 7:12 (with Luke 6:38 as the companion promise). STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — ten painted
stills. MILK. CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN. Jesus's teaching that the whole law folds into one line:
treat others the way you would want to be treated. The video is told as a warm VISUAL
ILLUSTRATION of one ordinary man living the rule through a single day — he knows the longing to be
helped, so he becomes the one who helps, and the kindness returns to him "good measure."

NO JESUS FIGURE IS SHOWN. Jesus is the TEACHER whose exact words are heard (the cream scripture
voice), but he is never depicted in any frame — the pictures are the illustration itself (ordinary
villagers living the rule), and where a teaching crowd appears the unseen teacher is off-frame
(never a small figure at the edge). No divine figure, no face, no cream robe appears.

ONE RECURRING MAN (call him THE MAN) carries the whole illustration: a middle-aged man with a
SHORT BLACK BEARD and dark hair, in an OLIVE-GREEN wool tunic with a TAN headscarf/mantle. Keep
him identical in every frame he appears (s3 through s10). The people he helps are different each
scene and all show their faces, kept internally consistent within their own shot. NOBODY wears
cream or off-white (that is reserved for the Jesus who is never shown here).

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single tall upright vertical scene painted edge to edge, no panels."
Skip any comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.

MUST NEVER SHOW: Jesus or any divine figure/face/hand; anyone in cream or off-white; any baked-in
text or written letters; any modern object; a multi-panel or comic-strip layout.
