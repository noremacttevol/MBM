# Story Video #9 — The Rich Young Ruler (Mark 10:17–22)

Runtime 60s. Prepend the STYLE BLOCK from 00-MASTER-PLAN.md. Jesus: from
behind / silhouette only — the LOOK of love in shot 3 is carried entirely by
light and the young man's reaction, not by showing Jesus's face. This video's
tone is unique in the set: it ends in sorrow, and stays there. Do not
soften the ending — the app's whole theology of agency lives in this story.

## Narration script (app's exact words)

> A young man ran to Jesus. He knelt. He asked the right question: "What
> must I do to have eternal life?"
>
> He had kept all the commandments since childhood. He was serious about it.
>
> The text says: Jesus looked at him and loved him. *(held beat)*
>
> Then Jesus gave him one thing he couldn't do. The man went away sad.
>
> Jesus let him go. He didn't lower the bar. He didn't chase him. He loved
> him and let him walk away. *(peak — music cuts to full silence on "let him
> walk away," and does not return)*

Closing card (hold 6s): *"Is there something you already know — quietly —
that stands between you and fully following what you believe?"*

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — He ran
```
A young man in expensive, immaculate robes RUNS down a dusty road — actually
runs, undignified, urgent — and drops to his knees in the dust before a
robed figure seen only from behind. His fine clothes pool in the dirt. His
face is open, earnest, completely sincere.
```

### Shot 2 — Serious about it
```
Close on the kneeling young man's face as he speaks earnestly, counting on
his fingers — a lifetime of kept commandments — his eyes hopeful, like a
student who has done all the homework and wants to know it was enough.
```

### Shot 3 — Looked at him and loved him (the money shot — extra takes)
```
The kneeling young man's face, close up: warm golden light falls across it
and his earnest expression slowly changes under a gaze we never see — first
warmed, truly seen, almost tearful — then, as unheard words land, the light
seems to grow heavier on him. Hope and grief cross his face in the same
breath.
```

### Shot 4 — The one thing
```
Insert shots, quiet and simple: the young man's hand with its gold rings;
the fine embroidered hem of his robe against the dirt; a leather purse heavy
at his belt. Each object beautiful, each suddenly weighing something. No
music. Ambient wind.
```

### Shot 5 — He went away sad
```
Wide shot, dusk: the young man walks away alone down the long road, back
toward his estate on the hill, shoulders slowly losing their squareness as
he goes. He does not look back. The camera does not follow him. The dust of
his passing hangs and settles.
```

### Shot 6 — He let him go
```
The robed figure, from behind, stands motionless in the road watching the
small figure recede — not moving, not calling out, hands open at his sides.
Hold the stillness a long time. The sun drops. The road empties. Fade down
slowly to deep warm dark.
```

### Closing card
Text on cream, serif. No narration. (After the silence of shot 6, the
question lands with full weight.)

## The Seed (what this video must leave behind)

He loved him AND let him walk away. No coercion, no lowered bar, no chasing — love that honors agency completely. This is the opposite of irresistible grace and forced salvation. Quiet question: *a love that will not force you — is that weakness, or the deepest respect you have ever been shown?* Carried by shots 3 and 6.
