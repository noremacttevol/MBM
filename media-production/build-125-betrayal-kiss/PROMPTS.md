# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #125: The Betrayal Kiss (matthew-26_betrayal-kiss)

Matthew 26:47-56, with the fuller witness of Luke 22:47-53 and John 18:1-12. STILLS-ONLY
(Law E) — twelve painted stills, no motion clips. This is the same night as #124, in and
around the garden of Gethsemane, immediately after the prayer.

CARE FLAGS: **ARC** (CONTENT-CARE #125 — "betrayal kiss — 'Friend'"), and the RESTRAINT
LAW binds the arrest even though only ARC is tabled:
- The swords and staves the band carries are REAL and present (scripture), but the
  violence is never the picture: no blow lands, no wound is shown, no blood. Peter's
  sword is shown DRAWN and RAISED (s6) — never the stroke connecting.
- The servant's ear (Malchus): we paint the HEALING, not the harm. Jesus (always seen
  only from behind, his face never shown) reaches out his hand and touches the man's ear
  and the ear is whole and healed, the man astonished (s8) — never a severed ear, never
  blood. "He touched his ear, and healed him" is the picture.
- Reverent restraint, exactly like the face law: what we withhold is what makes it
  worship instead of spectacle. The guards are ordinary men on a grim errand, not
  horror-villains; no cruelty is dwelt on.

TIME OF DAY: **NIGHT throughout** (continuous with #124) — deep blue night, a bright
Passover moon, and now the warm orange of the band's LANTERNS and TORCHES (John 18:3).
There is NO sunrise, NO sunset anywhere. Warm light is local: torches and lanterns.

SETTING: the edge of the olive grove of Gethsemane on the Mount of Olives, gnarled olive
trees, rocky moonlit ground, Jerusalem's walls small and far across the dark Kidron
valley in the wide shots. ONE place, one continuous night.

Jesus appears (face never shown) in s3, s4, s5, s6, s7, s8, s9, s10, s11 — in EVERY frame
the camera is BEHIND him, OVER his shoulder, or at DISTANCE: the back of his head and his
long dark hair to us, his face never shown, never visible, at any angle. Hide him by
ANGLE only — never by glow, halo, or rim-light. (s1, s2 and s12 are the band, Judas, and
the fleeing disciples — no Jesus figure, or only his cream-robed back at the edge.)

JESUS LOCK (identical wording wherever he appears; the project-wide look standard): a
first-century Middle-Eastern man, warm olive-brown skin, LONG dark brown-black hair
falling well past the shoulders, one plain undyed off-white cream wool robe (the same
garment as every video, a simple Judean tunic with a plain mantle), bare-headed, no hood;
no glow, no halo, no rim-light of any kind. The camera is always behind him or over his
shoulder and his face is never shown, never visible, at any angle.

ONLY JESUS WEARS CREAM (build law, from #40). Jesus is always seen only from behind, his
face never shown; in every frame he is the ONLY figure in an off-white or cream robe.
Every other person wears a clearly darker colour, and NOBODY ELSE HAS LONG HAIR past the
shoulders — every other man has SHORT hair or a head-cloth. Both signatures (long dark
hair past the shoulders AND a cream robe) are reserved for him.

JUDAS LOCK (s1, s2, s4, s5): the betrayer — an ordinary Galilean man of about forty, warm
olive-brown skin, a SHORT dark beard and SHORT dark hair (never long past the shoulders,
never cream), in a dun-brown wool tunic with a faded grey mantle. His face IS shown and
stays the same man: outwardly calm but hollow and troubled, a false friendliness that does
not reach his eyes. Never a cartoon villain — a grieved, hardened man doing a terrible thing.

THE BAND LOCK (s1, s2, s3, s6, s10): a large crowd sent from the chief priests — temple
guards and servants, ordinary hard-faced men, NOT monsters, carrying LANTERNS and lit
TORCHES and a few plain SWORDS and wooden STAVES (clubs). They wear dun, brown, grey,
deep indigo and dark red wool, short hair or head-cloths, none in cream, none with long
hair past the shoulders. Faces shown, grim and intent. No blood, no cruelty dwelt on.

PETER LOCK (s6, s7): the fisherman from #124 — about fifty, broad, a short grey-streaked
dark beard and short dark hair, in a dun-brown tunic and faded olive mantle. In s6 he has
drawn a short plain fisherman's sword and raised it in alarm — the blade is up, mid-air,
never shown striking anyone, no blood anywhere.

MALCHUS LOCK (s8): the high priest's young servant, about twenty-five, short dark hair, in
a plain grey servant's tunic, not cream. In s8 his ear is WHOLE and healed and he is
astonished; there is no wound, no blood, nothing severed — only the touch and the healing.

THE ELEVEN (s12): the other disciples — ordinary Galilean men in dun, brown, olive,
grey-blue and maroon, short hair or head-cloths, none in cream, none with long hair past
the shoulders — turning and fleeing into the dark trees.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, no panels." Skip any
comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.
