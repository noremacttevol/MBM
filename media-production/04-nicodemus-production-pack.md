# Story Video #4 — Nicodemus at Night (John 3:1–21)

Runtime 60s. Prepend the STYLE BLOCK from 00-MASTER-PLAN.md. Jesus is present
only as warm lamplight and a listening silhouette — never his face. This
whole video is a nocturne: lamps, stars, doorways, shadow.

## Narration script (app's exact words)

> Nicodemus was a Pharisee — a religious leader, educated, respected. He had
> everything to lose by being seen with Jesus.
>
> He came at night. *(give this line its own held beat)*
>
> He said: "No one could do what you do unless God were with him." Which
> meant he had already been thinking about it. He just couldn't say it in
> daylight.
>
> Jesus didn't turn him away for coming at night. He didn't point out the
> cowardice. He just answered the real question underneath the question.
> *(peak — music cuts to silence on "He just answered.")*

Closing card (hold 6s): *"Have you ever felt drawn toward something and been
afraid to let anyone else see it?"*

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — Everything to lose
```
A distinguished older man in fine religious robes and head covering stands in
a grand lamplit stone chamber among other robed scholars, respected,
listened to — but his eyes are elsewhere, fixed on a window and the night
beyond it. Rich warm interior light, deep shadows.
```

### Shot 2 — He came at night (the money shot — generate extra takes)
```
A lone figure in fine robes moves through narrow empty Jerusalem streets at
night, keeping to the shadows, hood drawn, checking over his shoulder. Cold
blue moonlight on stone walls; a single warm lit doorway waits at the end of
the street. He hesitates at the edge of its light. Extra takes on the
hesitation.
```

### Shot 3 — Crossing the threshold
```
Close on a weathered scholarly hand hovering at a rough wooden doorpost, half
in cold moonlight, half in warm lamplight spilling from inside. The hand
tightens once, then knocks softly. The door opens; warm light floods across
his lined, frightened, hopeful face.
```

### Shot 4 — The conversation
```
Interior at night: two figures sit across a low table by a single oil lamp.
The older robed scholar leans forward, speaking earnestly, hands open. The
other figure is only a warm silhouette on the edge of the lamplight,
listening completely — face never visible. Moths at the flame. Stillness.
```

### Shot 5 — The real question underneath
```
Slow push-in on the old scholar's face in lamplight as his prepared,
formal expression loosens into something younger — the face of a man finally
asking what he actually wants to know. His eyes shine. The lamp flame
steadies. Silence around him.
```

### Shot 6 — Leaving changed
```
The scholar steps out of the doorway into the night — but now he walks down
the center of the street, hood back, unhurried, looking up at an immense sky
of stars. The cold blue night feels different: vast instead of frightening.
He almost smiles.
```

### Closing card
Text on cream, serif. No narration.
