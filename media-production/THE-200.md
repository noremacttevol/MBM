## The one standard (never changes, any shelf)

Every feed card, for every user, is the same four things:
**story video + one verse + one question + one invitation.**

## The three shelves (cumulative — nobody loses content moving up)

| Shelf | Who (by their own words) | Pool |
|---|---|---|
| **EVERYONE** (rows 1–100) | Unknown, agnostic, atheist, any faith — no signals needed | 1–100 |

- EVERYONE = the whole life of Jesus, birth to ascension, plus his parables, miracles
  and encounters. Pure Bible. (v1's flaw is fixed: the nativity, Gethsemane, the cross
  and the resurrection are now INSIDE the first 100 — a stranger meets the whole Jesus.)
- BRIDGE = still 100% Bible, but chosen to minister to the person whose inherited
  theology hurt them — each video answers a real wound (silent heaven, angry God,
  one-size heaven, dead religion) with a Bible scene showing the good God the
  restored gospel actually teaches. Also serves returning/inactive members: the tone
  is "come remember," never "you failed."
- MEMBER = the restoration proven from the Bible itself — the verses the church's own
  missionaries and teachers use — plus discipleship. Every member video ends with a
  pointer to official church material (Gospel Library topic/chapter). We make the
  video the church rarely makes; we send them to the church for everything else.
- Old Section IX (Book of Mormon / PoGP scenes) is ARCHIVED intact in
  THE-200-PHASE2.md — built later, possibly after church contact. Not deleted.
