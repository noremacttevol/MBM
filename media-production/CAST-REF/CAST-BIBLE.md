# THE CAST BIBLE — the Twelve, locked (2026-07-22, Machine C)

> The same discipline we used for Jesus, now for his disciples. Jesus is locked by an
> approved reference IMAGE (`JESUS-MASTER-REF/jesus-face.jpeg`, attached REF to every
> shot). The Twelve are locked the same way: an approved reference image per man in
> `CAST-REF/`, attached as REF whenever that disciple appears, PLUS the byte-identical
> text lock below dropped into the shot. Picture first, words second — the reference
> image IS the face; the prose only keeps wardrobe/behaviour straight.
>
> **Goal:** Peter, John, Andrew — every one of the Twelve — looks like the SAME man in
> all 200 videos, and the twelve are visibly distinct from one another so viewers learn
> their faces the way they learn Jesus's voice.

## ROSTER CORRECTIONS (Cameron, 2026-07-22) — for the character catalog / characters.html
- **Adult Jesus is required** as his own character (not only the boy-at-twelve). The
  master adult face is `JESUS-MASTER-REF/jesus-face.jpeg`.
- **Elias and Elijah are TWO DISTINCT persons** (Latter-day Saint doctrine — the
  Transfiguration / D&C 110 restoration). The catalog must list BOTH: **Elijah** (the OT
  prophet, sealing keys) AND **Elias** (a separate personage, the Abrahamic/gospel
  restoration) — never merged into one.
- **All TWELVE apostles** must be present. The public sheet was missing five —
  **Philip, Bartholomew (Nathanael), James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus (Jude), Simon the
  Zealot** — all five are locked here in CAST-BIBLE and have portraits in `CAST-REF/`.

## FACE-LOCK METHOD NOTE (2026-07-22)
A single attached portrait on the FREE model only loosely matches (drifts in age/greying).
For a reliable lock: use the PAID Pro image model AND attach MORE than one angle of the
character (front + three-quarter), the way the character-sheet catalog stores each person
in three views. Single-portrait + free-model is not tight enough for final art.

## HOW TO USE (per shot)
1. In `PROMPTS.md`, any shot with a named disciple carries that disciple's LOCK
   paragraph below, byte-identical.
2. Add a `REF:` line naming the disciple's reference file(s), e.g. `REF: cast/peter`
   (and `jesus-master-ref` too when Jesus is in the shot).
3. Wardrobe colours are FIXED per man (below) and never clash with Jesus's cream —
   **only Jesus wears cream**; no disciple ever wears cream/off-white/white.
4. GATE SAFETY: disciple LOCKs never use the tokens *Jesus / the Lord / Christ /
   Saviour* or the banned drift words (caucasian/pale/blue-eyed/blond/halo/glow) —
   so `jesus_face_gate.py` stays clean. When Jesus shares the shot, HIS JESUS LOCK v3 +
   `REF: jesus-master-ref` are added exactly as always.
5. All twelve are first-century Galilean Jews (Judas the lone Judean): warm tan to deep
   olive-brown Middle Eastern skin, dark eyes, rough-woven undyed wool. Weathered,
   real, working men — never modern, never European.

## THE TWELVE — fixed looks

**1. SIMON PETER** — the lead. Established look, unchanged.
> PETER LOCK: Peter is the same man in every shot — a sturdy Galilean fisherman in his
> late thirties, broad and strong, thick dark curly hair going a little wild, a full
> dark beard, weathered warm-olive skin, deep brown eyes, heavy honest features. He
> wears a soaked or dusty BLUE-GREY wool tunic with a plain rope belt (never cream). His
> face is shown clearly.

**2. ANDREW** — Peter's brother; gentler, seeker.
> ANDREW LOCK: Andrew is the same man in every shot — Peter's younger brother, early
> thirties, similar sturdy build but leaner, short dark curly hair, a shorter rounded
> dark beard, warm-olive skin, open kind eyes. He wears a RUST-BROWN wool tunic with a
> cord belt (never cream). His face is shown clearly.

**3. JAMES son of Zebedee** — tall, strong, quick-tempered ("son of thunder").
> JAMES-Z LOCK: James is the same man in every shot — tall and strong, mid thirties,
> dark hair pulled back off the face, a thick full black beard, deep-olive skin, a
> steady bold gaze. He wears a DEEP-OLIVE (forest brown-green) wool tunic with a
> leather belt (never cream). His face is shown clearly.

**4. JOHN son of Zebedee** — the youngest, the beloved; James's brother.
> JOHN LOCK: John is the same man in every shot — the youngest disciple, early twenties,
> smooth-featured and gentle, wavy chestnut-brown hair to the jaw, only a soft light
> beard, warm tan skin, large calm dark eyes. He wears a SAND / warm-tan wool tunic with
> a woven sash (never cream). His face is shown clearly.

**5. PHILIP** — practical, literal-minded.
> PHILIP LOCK: Philip is the same man in every shot — mid thirties, lean and thoughtful,
> straight dark hair parted at the side, a neatly trimmed dark beard, olive skin, level
> brows. He wears a DUSTY-GREEN wool tunic with a cord belt (never cream). His face is
> shown clearly.

**6. BARTHOLOMEW (Nathanael)** — older, honest, "no guile."
> BARTHOLOMEW LOCK: Bartholomew is the same man in every shot — the eldest of the
> younger set, late forties, broad and kind, a greying dark beard, a high receding
> hairline with grey at the temples, sun-browned olive skin, laugh lines. He wears an
> EARTH-BROWN wool tunic with a rope belt (never cream). His face is shown clearly.

**7. THOMAS (Didymus)** — wiry, intense, questioning.
> THOMAS LOCK: Thomas is the same man in every shot — mid thirties, wiry and restless,
> dark deep-set eyes under strong brows, medium-length dark hair, a medium dark beard,
> olive skin, an earnest searching look. He wears a SLATE-GREY (blue-charcoal) wool
> tunic with a cord belt (never cream). His face is shown clearly.

**8. MATTHEW (Levi)** — the former tax collector; a little neater than the fishermen.
> MATTHEW LOCK: Matthew is the same man in every shot — late thirties, a former tax
> collector so his hands and dress are a shade neater than the fishermen, dark hair
> combed back, a short well-kept dark beard, warm-olive skin, quiet observant eyes. He
> wears a DEEP OXBLOOD-MAROON wool tunic with a plain belt (never cream). His face is
> shown clearly.

**9. JAMES son of Alphaeus ("James the Less")** — smaller, quiet, younger-seeming.
> JAMES-A LOCK: James son of Alphaeus is the same man in every shot — small and slight,
> late twenties, a thin light dark beard, short dark hair, warm tan skin, a mild modest
> expression. He wears an ASH-GREY / dun wool tunic with a cord belt (never cream). His
> face is shown clearly.

**10. THADDAEUS (Jude / Lebbaeus)** — stocky, warm-hearted.
> THADDAEUS LOCK: Thaddaeus is the same man in every shot — stocky and warm, early
> forties, curly dark hair going grey, a rounded greying beard, ruddy-olive skin, a
> ready gentle smile. He wears an OCHRE / muted-mustard wool tunic with a rope belt
> (never cream). His face is shown clearly.

**11. SIMON the Zealot** — fiery, lean, sharp.
> SIMON-Z LOCK: Simon the Zealot is the same man in every shot — mid thirties, lean and
> intense, sharp features, black hair and a black beard trimmed close, deep-olive skin,
> quick alert eyes. He wears a DARK BRICK / red-brown wool tunic with a leather belt
> (never cream). His face is shown clearly.

**12. JUDAS ISCARIOT** — the lone Judean; kept dignified until the betrayal, never a
> cartoon villain. A money-bag is the only tell.
> JUDAS LOCK: Judas Iscariot is the same man in every shot — the one Judean among
> Galileans, mid thirties, smoother and better-kept than the others, sleek dark hair and
> a neat dark beard, olive skin, watchful guarded eyes. He wears a DARK TEAL-GREEN wool
> tunic with a plain belt and a small leather money-pouch at his side (never cream). His
> face is shown clearly. (Depict him as an ordinary man; the sorrow of the story is
> carried by the scripture, not by making him look evil.)
