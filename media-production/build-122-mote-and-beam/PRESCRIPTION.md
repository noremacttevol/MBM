**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 9)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** Jesus wasn't telling us to stop seeing clearly — he was telling
  us where to start: with the beam in our own eye. Deal with that, and our eyes for
  other people fill with mercy instead of judgment.
- **The turn:** the moment we finally catch sight of the beam — "Not his. Ours."
  Judgment turns inward, and everything changes.
- **Wound answered (dead / judgmental religion):** the inherited picture of religion
  as people standing superior, sentence-passing, eager to point at your speck. This
  turns judgment inward first and ends in mercy.
- **Whose face carries it:** the TWO BROTHERS — the one eager to point, then humbled;
  the absurd man with a plank in his own eye carries the hinge. Jesus is shown teaching,
  over-the-shoulder onto the listeners.
- **The card question falls out of:** "whose speck have you been watching, while a
  beam waited in your own eye?" Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
