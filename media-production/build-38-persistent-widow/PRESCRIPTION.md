# PRESCRIPTION — build-38-persistent-widow

**Row:** 38   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Luke 18:1–8
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 9 — see deviation)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God is NOT the reluctant judge — he doesn't have to be worn down
  into caring; he already loves you and has been waiting to hear from you all along.
- **The turn:** "Now think about God." The hinge is the flip from the cold judge who
  gives in to be rid of her, to the Father who already wants to hear you.
- **Character shown / wound answered:** answers the "God is annoyed by my asking / I
  have to nag him" wound — the parable argues from opposite to opposite.
- **Whose face carries it:** the WIDOW — her worn face, her steady persistence at the
  judge's door. The judge is the parable's judge; God is not depicted (the turn is
  spoken, not shown as a figure).
- **The card question falls out of:** "What have you stopped asking him for, because
  you assumed he wasn't listening?" No change needed.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 9 stills
1. [s1/n1b] Jesus teaching the reason up front — keep praying, don't lose heart (brief teller frame, master ref) — agrees: "men ought always to pray, and not to faint"
2. [n1] the widow — alone, no husband, no protector, wronged, with no power — "She had lost her husband and had no one to protect her"
3. [jv2/n2] the widow standing before the hard judge — "Avenge me of mine adversary"
4. [n3] the judge waving her away — she has no bribe, no rank, nothing to offer — "He waved her away. She had nothing to offer him"
5. [n4] the widow returning, day after day — the same worn face and steady voice at his door — "Every day the same worn face at his door"
6. [j1/n5] the judge finally relenting — "she wearies me; I'll give her justice to be rid of her" — "because this widow troubleth me, I will avenge her"
7. [j2/n6] the turn — "shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry day and night unto him?"
8. [jv8/n7b] "he will avenge them speedily" — and the quiet question, will he find faith on the earth? — "when the Son of man cometh, shall he find faith on the earth?"
9. [n7/n8/card] God is not that judge — already loving, already waiting to hear you; warm hold — "he is the very opposite of that judge, and he has been waiting to hear from you"
