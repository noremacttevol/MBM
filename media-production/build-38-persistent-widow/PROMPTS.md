# FLOW PROMPTS — Story Video #38: The Persistent Widow (luke-18_persistent-widow)

Luke 18:1-8. STILLS-ONLY (Law E). This is a PARABLE Jesus tells — there is NO
Jesus figure in any shot, only his narrating KJV voice. The JUDGE is an ordinary
parable character (a cold, corrupt magistrate), NOT the Lord; the widow is an
ordinary wronged woman. The face gate is trivial: no shot below names Jesus /
the Lord / the master at all. (Gate gotcha: the God-figure of the parable is
never drawn — the pivot to God is shown only as an ordinary person praying and a
warm dawn; nothing is called "the master" or "the lord" in this file.)

Nano Banana 2, 9:16, x2, 2K download. Hand-painted 2D storybook style (NOT the
stale master-plan photorealistic block).

**CHARACTER LOCKS (keep consistent across every still they appear in):**
- **The widow (shots 1, 2, 4)** — a weary, dignified older Middle-Eastern woman
  in a dark charcoal-grey mourning shawl over a faded dusty-blue robe, a lined
  kind face worn with grief but steady and unbroken, empty work-worn hands. Poor
  but not filthy. Her face IS shown — she is an ordinary widow, not a holy figure.
- **The judge (shots 2, 3, 5)** — a well-fed, richly dressed Middle-Eastern
  magistrate in a deep-maroon robe with a heavy gold-threaded sash, seated on a
  raised carved judgment seat, a proud cold face with a trimmed oiled beard,
  bored and dismissive. His face IS shown — an ordinary corrupt official, not a
  divine figure.
- **The one who prays (shots 6, 7)** — a different ordinary humble person (a
  young working man in a plain undyed tunic) kneeling in prayer at home in warm
  light, at peace, heard. NOT the widow, and no divine figure appears — just an
  ordinary man. Face shown, calm and hopeful.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or
captions in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen
in undyed earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms and two hands (anatomy guard). End each prompt with
"One single continuous scene painted edge to edge." Skip any comic-strip /
multi-panel variant Flow returns.

## s1-widow-alone — Shot 1: a widow, wronged and alone
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A poor Middle-Eastern widow stands alone at the edge of a
dim, empty stone courtyard at grey dawn. She wears a dark charcoal-grey mourning
shawl over a faded dusty-blue robe; her lined, kind face is worn with grief but
steady, her two work-worn hands empty at her sides. She has no husband beside
her, no wealth, no one to defend her — only her quiet, unbroken dignity. Cool
muted light, a single warm gleam on her face. One single continuous scene painted
edge to edge.

## s2-widow-pleads — Shot 2: give me justice against mine adversary
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Inside a cold, grand judgment hall of pale stone pillars. The
poor widow in her charcoal-grey shawl stands small before a raised carved
judgment seat, both hands lifted in earnest pleading. On the seat sits the richly
dressed judge in a deep-maroon robe with a heavy gold-threaded sash, a proud cold
face with a trimmed oiled beard, looking down at her with bored indifference. The
gulf between her poverty and his power fills the room. Shafts of hard daylight.
One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s3-judge-dismisses — Shot 3: for a while he would not
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same judgment hall. The richly dressed judge in his
deep-maroon gold-sashed robe waves the widow away with one hand, turning his
proud face aside in cold dismissal, already reaching for other matters. His
attendants ignore her. The small grey-shawled widow is being shown the door,
unheard. Hard, indifferent light, the judge's bored contempt plain on his face.
One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s4-again-and-again — Shot 4: by her continual coming
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A worn stone doorway of the courthouse seen across several
overlapping moments of the same day-after-day: the grey-shawled widow arriving
again at the threshold, the same weary steady face, the same lifted plea, her
two hands raised. Faint repeated impressions of her figure suggest she has come
many times and will come again — persistence without end. Dawn-grey light warming
slightly. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s5-judge-relents — Shot 5: lest by her continual coming she weary me
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The judgment hall again. The richly dressed judge in his
deep-maroon gold-sashed robe slumps back on his carved seat, one hand pressed
wearily to his brow, worn down at last, grudgingly waving the widow forward to
grant her justice at last — not out of kindness but only to be rid of her. His
face is annoyed and exhausted, not softened. The small grey-shawled widow stands
steady before him. Tired hard light. One single continuous scene painted edge to
edge.

## s6-praying-heard — Shot 6: shall not God avenge his own elect
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A complete change of place and mood: a humble home at warm
lamplit evening. An ordinary young working man in a plain undyed tunic kneels in
quiet prayer beside a low window, both hands open, his face lifted and calm, bathed
in a soft warm golden glow pouring gently over him — clearly heard, clearly loved,
not ignored. Tender, radiant, unhurried light, utterly unlike the cold hall. One
single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s7-the-good-father — Shot 7: he is nothing like that judge
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A warm golden dawn breaking wide over the quiet rooftops of a
first-century town, gentle light spilling across the whole scene, a single open
window glowing with welcome. The same humble young man in his plain undyed tunic
rises with peace on his face, unburdened, at the start of a new day — the picture
of a prayer already heard by a God who was never reluctant. Radiant, hopeful,
overflowing warm light. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.
