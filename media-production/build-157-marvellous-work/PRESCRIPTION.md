# PRESCRIPTION — build-157-marvellous-work

**Row:** 157   **Shelf:** Member   **Scripture:** Isaiah 29:11–14
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 10)

> Isaiah's sealed-book parable — a book no one can open, the learned and the simple
> both failing, and God stepping in himself. The beats walk the sealed book from hand
> to hand, then turn on God's promised wonder. The LORD speaks (green); no Jesus face.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** when human wisdom hits its limit and God's word sits sealed, God
  himself does a marvellous work to open it — the coming forth of scripture no scholar
  could unseal.
- **The turn:** kv14 — "I will proceed to do a marvellous work and a wonder." Human
  wisdom ends and God is only getting started.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the marvellous work — the coming forth of the Book of Mormon,
  the sealed book opened by an act of God. **GL handoff: Book of Mormon.**
- **Whose face carries it:** the respected scholar and the plain unschooled man — the two
  witnesses who both admit they cannot open it. The LORD is voice/light (green), no face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "When the wonder comes, will you receive it?" —
  lands on the one thing.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 10 stills
1. [n1] the truth of God as a book sealed shut, held in their hands yet closed — agrees: "like a book that is sealed shut... its meaning locked away"
2. [n2] a precious, clasped, sealed book among the people; all sense it matters, none can open it — "a precious book, clasped and sealed, that no one around can open"
3. [n3] the people carrying it to the respected scholar: "please, read this for us" — "they carry it to their most educated man... please, read this for us"
4. [kv11] the scholar turning it over and admitting he cannot — "he saith, I cannot; for it is sealed"
5. [n4] handing it to the plain, unschooled man; his kind, helpless head-shake — "Neither the wise nor the simple can open it on their own"
6. [kv13a,kv13b,n5] the people honouring God with their lips, hearts drifted far, worship shrunk to habits taught by men — "with their lips do honour me, but have removed their heart far from me"
7. [n6] the wisdom of the wise run to its very end; the experts with no key — "The wisdom of the wise had run to the very end of itself"
8. [kv14] the LORD's promise — a marvellous work and a wonder — light breaking (THE TURN) — "I will proceed to do a marvellous work... a marvellous work and a wonder"
9. [n7] the act of God opening what men had sealed — the seal breaking, light on the book — "an act of God that would open what men had sealed"
10. [n8,card] a humble face, the wonder offered — warm hold for the closing question — "When the wonder comes, will you receive it?"
