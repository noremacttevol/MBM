> Bridge wound: **come-remember / dead religion** — the drifted person who is sure
> the way back has to be some grand, dramatic act, and can't believe it could be
> this plain. Naaman almost rode away from his own healing because the cure sounded
> too small. The story answers the lie that returning to God must be earned with
> something great.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the way back to God looks almost insultingly simple — and it
  works anyway. Do the simple thing.
- **The turn:** the servants stopping the raging captain on the road — "wouldest
  thou not have done some great thing? how much rather... Wash, and be clean?" That
  reasoning is the hinge: his pride cracks and he goes down to the water.
- **Wound answered:** the come-remember wound — "I've drifted too far, and coming
  back can't be as plain as pray again, read again, come again." Naaman proves it is.
- **Whose face carries it:** NAAMAN — the proud captain's fury on the road, then his
  face over his own clean skin. And the captive GIRL whose small faith starts it all.
  No Jesus in this one (Old Testament); Naaman/the girl on master-ref sheets.
- **The card question falls out of:** "He almost rode away from his healing because
  it sounded too simple." Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
