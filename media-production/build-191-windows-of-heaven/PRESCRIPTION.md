> One verse, one dare. Malachi 3:10 is a single long divine sentence that moves
> through three images — the tithe brought in, the windows of heaven opened, the
> blessing poured out past holding — so it takes mid-segment switches, not one
> static still. The closing card stays out of BEATS, as the build has it.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God is the one being in all scripture who invites you to test
  him — put his generosity to the proof and watch heaven open.
- **The turn:** "prove me now herewith" — the dare no king could make. The people
  holding back are challenged to bring it all and see what he does.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** tithing is a covenant with a promise attached; the LORD
  binds himself to answer. Hands off to **Gospel Library: Tithing**.
- **Whose face carries it:** the people holding back, then their wonder as it pours.
  God's hand only through the opened window — no face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "bring it all, and watch the windows open."
  No change needed.
