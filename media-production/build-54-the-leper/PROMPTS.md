# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #54: The Leper Made Clean (mark-1_the-leper)

Mark 1:40-45. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — nine painted stills, no motion clips.

**FACE LAW v3 (face SHOWN, locked to master ref).** Jesus's face IS shown — the SAME face
as the attached JESUS-MASTER-REF portrait — in s2, s3, s4, s5, s6, s7, s9, staged
naturally. Each carries the byte-identical JESUS LOCK v3 paragraph and a
"REF: jesus-master-ref" line, and generation attaches jesus-face.jpeg as --ref. s1 (the
outcast, alone) and s8 (the healed man telling others) have NO divine figure. ONLY Jesus
wears cream/off-white.

CARE FLAGS: **R — RESTRAINT.** Leprosy is shown GENTLY: pale, greyish, dry patches on the
skin, wrapped hands and forearms, a gaunt and weary outcast — NEVER graphic open sores,
oozing wounds, blood or anything gruesome. QC question for every frame: "would a parent
let a 10-year-old see this?" — yes. The heart of the story is the TOUCH (s4): the
untouchable man is touched. The hope-beat is his clean, restored skin and his joy (s5-s6).
The closing card is an open invitation.

ANTI-PANEL: every prompt asks for ONE single full-frame illustration of a single frozen
instant — no comic strip, no panels, no stacked or side-by-side frames, no dividing lines.
(Nano Banana returns triptychs for action/emotion beats otherwise.)

TIME/SETTING: bright open DAY. The dusty edge of a first-century Galilean town and the
open country just outside it — pale stone, dry ground, low hills, a bright clear sky. A
few shots move to the town's edge and the road. Warm natural daylight throughout; no
night, no sunset.

THE MAN (the leper): a first-century Galilean man of middle years, gaunt and weary, in
ragged, dust-coloured dun and grey rags, clearly NOT cream, his hands and forearms wrapped
in cloth, pale greyish dry patches on the skin of his face and arms (restrained, never
gory). His face is shown: desperate and downcast at first (s1-s3), then in the wonder of
the touch (s4), then radiant, clear-skinned, healthy and overjoyed once healed (s5-s8).

THE PRIEST / TOWNSFOLK (s7-s9): ordinary first-century Judeans — a robed temple priest in
muted layered linen with a subtle woven border, and townsfolk in dun, faded brown, ochre,
olive, grey-blue and deep indigo wool, head-coverings and short hair. Faces shown. NONE in
off-white or cream, none with long hair past the shoulders.

ONLY JESUS WEARS CREAM (build law). In every frame he is the ONLY figure in an off-white
or cream robe, and the only man with long hair past the shoulders. Everyone else is in a
darker earth colour with short or covered hair. Check this on every generated frame.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single continuous scene painted edge to edge." Each prompt also
carries "The painting fills the entire frame edge to edge with no border, no frame and
no cream margin." Skip any comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.
