## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the urge to call down fire on the people who reject you is not his
  spirit — he came to save men's lives, not to destroy them, and he says so gently, to the
  disciples he loves.
- **The turn:** j1 — "Ye know not what manner of spirit ye are of… the Son of man is not
  come to destroy men's lives, but to save them." He rebukes the wanting-to-destroy, not
  the village.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** *the angry, vengeful God* — the fire-from-heaven God an
  inherited religion may have taught. This cut answers it plainly: the fire the disciples
  wanted "was the wrong kind," it "isn't from Him," and he simply walked on. Your religion's
  fire was never his.
- **Whose face carries it:** JAMES and JOHN (hot with anger, then rebuked and softened).
  Jesus face-shown via master ref turning to rebuke (s4), over-the-shoulder onto their faces.
- **The card question falls out of:** "He came to save, not to burn. Let His Spirit — the
  saving one — shape yours." No change needed.
