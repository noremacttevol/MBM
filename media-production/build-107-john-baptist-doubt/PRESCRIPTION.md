> Bridge shelf. The wound is **"heaven is silent when I doubt / God is angry at honest
> questions."** The answer is that the greatest prophet who ever lived sat in the dark and
> asked, and Jesus was not offended — he answered gently, with evidence of grace, and then
> turned and defended his doubting friend to the crowd.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God does not shame you for an honest question — even the strongest
  believer sits in the dark and wonders, and the answer to doubt is often not the rescue
  you asked for but "look at the love; it is real, and it is for you."
- **The turn:** Jesus does not send back an argument, he sends back a scene — "the blind
  receive their sight... the poor have the gospel preached to them." The answer is the
  works of love themselves. That is the hinge.
- **Wound answered:** "God is impatient / angry when I doubt." The answer — Jesus met
  John's doubt with tenderness, then praised him publicly. Doubt was met with kindness.
- **Whose face carries it:** JOHN — pointing to Jesus at the height of his ministry, then
  in the prison cell in the dark, then at peace at the end. Jesus's face only via master
  ref / over-the-shoulder in the answer beats.
- **The card question falls out of:** "would you dare to bring [your honest question] to
  him too?" — lands on the one thing. No change needed.
