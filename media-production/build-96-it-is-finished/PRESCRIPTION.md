## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he did not lose — he finished. The work of getting you back to
  God is not still in progress and not waiting on you; it is done, and the way in
  stands open.
- **The turn:** the veil of the temple rent in two, top to bottom, the instant he
  dies. That torn curtain is the hinge — the death becomes the door.
- **Character shown:** a Savior who lays his life down (nobody takes it) and whose
  finished work throws the barrier to God wide open for everyone.
- **Whose face carries it:** no human witness is named in the narration, so the
  emotional payload lands on an object — the torn veil, the barrier gone. Jesus's
  cross beats use his face only via master ref / over-the-shoulder; the picture is
  the finished work, not a grieving face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "The way to God is open. Walk in." Lands on
  the torn veil and the one thing.
