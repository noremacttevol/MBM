# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #165: "Laying on of hands for the Holy Ghost" (acts-8_laying-on-of-hands)

Acts 8:14-17. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no
motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Laying On of Hands** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk. The note is holy ORDER and the GIFT: Samaria believed
and was baptized in water, yet the Holy Ghost had not come — because the gift is conferred by
those with authority. The apostles sent Peter and John, who laid their hands on the believers,
and they received the Holy Ghost. Warm, reverent, glad. Study gem: the gift travels by
authority, not by sincerity alone.

CHRIST / GOD IS NEVER DEPICTED as a figure — no face, no body, no shape of a person. The Holy
Ghost and heaven are shown ONLY as warm radiant light descending from above — never a dove,
never a figure, never a face. (This keeps the shot blocks free of any divine proper noun, so
the face-gate passes.)

THE POINT (Why-Law): Samaria gladly received the word of God and believed (s1); the news
reached the apostles at Jerusalem (s2 — Acts 8:14); they sent Peter and John, men with
authority (s3); who came down and prayed for the new believers to receive the Spirit (s4 —
Acts 8:15); the believers had already been baptized in water, yet the gift had not yet come
(s5 — Acts 8:16); then Peter and John laid their hands on them and warm light came down — they
received the Holy Ghost (s6 — Acts 8:17, the named verse); the believers were filled, faces
alight with the gift (s7); and that same gift, by that same order, is offered to you (s8).

PEOPLE LOCK (s1, s4-s8): ordinary first-century Samaritan believers of mixed age — men, women
and children in undyed DARKER earth-tone wool (dun, faded brown, olive, ochre, russet, grey),
faces shown, earnest and glad; never in cream or off-white.
APOSTLES LOCK (s2, s3, s4, s6, s8): PETER and JOHN — two grave, kind first-century apostles in
dark earth-tone robes and mantles (deep brown, olive, charcoal, russet); ; plainly men of
authority and calling; never in cream, no insignia, no bright ring around the head.
THE HEAVENLY LIGHT (s4, s6, s7, s8): warm radiant golden light descending from above — only
light, never a face, never a figure, never a dove, never a body or hand within it.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or body of God or the risen Lord anywhere — only warm
descending light; no dove; no modern object; no cross, steeple, church-building-with-a-spire,
or insignia of any group; no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no baked-in text,
letters or numbers; no human dressed in cream or off-white. Every image is ONE single unbroken
full-frame scene — no panels, no comic strip, no dividing lines.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.
