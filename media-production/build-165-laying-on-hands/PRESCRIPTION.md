## The impact read
- **The one thing:** believing and being baptized was real, but the gift of the Holy Ghost still had to come one way — by the laying on of hands, under apostolic authority. Order and gift belong together.
- **The turn:** n3 — *even though they already believed and were baptized, the gift had not come to a single one of them.* The story pivots from "surely they're done" to "something authorized is still missing," and lands when the hands are laid.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the gift of the Holy Ghost is conferred by authority through the laying on of hands. Hands off to **Gospel Library → Laying On of Hands.**
- **Whose face carries it:** the Samaritan believers — especially the one whose face lights up under Peter's and John's hands. Never a face for the "apostolic authority" abstraction; show the hands and the receiving face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "When that gift is offered to you, will you receive it?" — lands straight on the one thing.
