# PRESCRIPTION — build-35-great-banquet

**Row:** 35   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Luke 14:16–24
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 10)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** when the ones who should have come turned him down, he didn't
  shrink his table — he opened the doors wider; God wants a FULL house.
- **The turn:** the servant returning alone → "go out quickly... bring in the poor."
  The hinge is the host refusing to cancel and widening the invitation instead.
- **Character shown / wound answered:** answers the "you're not the right kind to be
  invited" wound — the overlooked get a seat with their name on it.
- **Whose face carries it:** the SERVANT (going out) and the POOR being welcomed —
  their faces at being wanted. The host is the parable's host (God); keep him generic /
  master-ref, not a Jesus likeness.
- **The card question falls out of:** "What excuse have you been giving for not sitting
  down?" No change needed.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 10 stills
1. [s16/j16/n1] the host preparing a great feast — finest food, tables set, every place ready — agrees: "a great supper... He prepared everything"
2. [n2] the servant sent out to the invited guests — "come, for everything is ready now"
3. [j18/n3] the first guest's excuse — "I have bought a piece of ground... have me excused"

6. [j1/n6] "Go out into the streets" — the servant sent to the poor, the crippled, the blind — "bring in hither the poor, and the maimed, and the halt, and the blind"

8. [j22/n8] the servant reports — and there is still room — "it is done as thou hast commanded, and yet there is room"
9. [j2/n9] "Go to the highways and hedges" — the servant far out on the roads, bringing more — "compel them to come in, that my house may be filled"
10. [n10/card] the full table, doors opened wider; a place still kept for you — warm hold — "there has always been a place at it for you"
