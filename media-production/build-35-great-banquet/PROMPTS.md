# FLOW PROMPTS — Story Video #35: The Great Banquet (luke-14_great-banquet)

Luke 14:15-24. STILLS-ONLY (Law E). This is a PARABLE Jesus tells — there is NO
Jesus figure in any shot, only his narrating KJV voice. The HOST is an ordinary
parable character (a generous nobleman), NOT the Lord, so the face gate is
trivial: no shot below names Jesus / the Lord / the master at all.

Nano Banana 2, 9:16, x2, 2K download. Hand-painted 2D storybook style (NOT the
stale master-plan photorealistic block).

**CHARACTER LOCKS (keep consistent across every still they appear in):**
- **The host (shots 1, 4, 7)** — a warm, generous, wealthy Middle-Eastern
  nobleman, kind open face, neatly groomed dark beard lightly greyed, a rich
  warm-crimson robe with a gold-embroidered border. His face IS shown — he is
  an ordinary generous man, not a holy or divine figure.
- **The servant / messenger (shots 2, 3, 5, 6)** — an eager, kind younger man
  in a plain cream tunic with a brown sash, always carrying or gesturing with
  the invitation; the one who runs to fetch the guests. His face IS shown.
- **The invited guests who excuse themselves (shot 3)** — well-dressed
  comfortable townsmen who wave the servant off (one by a field, one with a
  yoke of oxen, one with his new wife). Faces shown, distracted and dismissive.
- **The poor and overlooked (shots 6, 7)** — ragged, joyful common folk: a blind
  man, a man on a crutch (lame), a beggar, a weary mother and child, the
  outcast — astonished and glad to be invited. Faces shown, lit with wonder.
- **The banquet** — a long low table laden with abundant food, bread, fruit,
  roast, wine, warm oil-lamp light; keep it the SAME feast through the story.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or
captions in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen
in undyed earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms and two hands (anatomy guard). End each prompt with
"One single continuous scene painted edge to edge." Skip any comic-strip /
multi-panel variant Flow returns.

## s1-feast-ready — Shot 1: a man made a great supper
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A warm stone banquet hall at dusk, oil lamps glowing. A long
low table stretches through the room, laden with an abundant feast: fresh bread,
roasted meat, bowls of fruit, jugs of wine, everything ready and waiting. The
generous host, a warm-faced Middle-Eastern nobleman with a neatly greyed beard,
in a rich warm-crimson robe with a gold border, stands looking over the ready
table with open, hopeful joy, everything prepared for his guests. Rich golden
lamplight, an air of glad anticipation. One single continuous scene painted edge
to edge.

## s2-servant-sent — Shot 2: come, for all things are now ready
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The doorway of the fine house opening onto a dusk street.
The host's eager young servant in a plain cream tunic and brown sash steps out
through the door with the rolled invitation in his hand, one arm lifted, hurrying
off to call the invited guests, the warm glow of the laden feast visible behind
him through the doorway. Warm interior light spilling out into the cool blue
street. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s3-excuses — Shot 3: they all with one consent began to make excuse
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A sunlit street outside a comfortable townhouse. The eager
young servant in his cream tunic holds out the invitation, but the well-dressed
invited guests wave him off, distracted and dismissive: one comfortable man
gestures proudly toward a field and a pair of yoked oxen behind him; another
turns away with his new wife on his arm; none of them will come. Their faces are
busy and unbothered. Clear daylight, the servant's hopeful arm meeting shrugs
and turned backs. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s4-servant-returns — Shot 4: the servant came back and told his lord
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Back inside the warm banquet hall by the ready, still-empty
table. The eager young servant in his cream tunic has returned alone and stands
before the generous host with empty hands and an apologetic look. The host, in
his warm-crimson gold-bordered robe, does not look crushed or bitter but roused
and resolute, his kind face already turning toward a better idea. The full
untouched feast waits behind them in the lamplight. One single continuous scene
painted edge to edge.

## s5-to-the-streets — Shot 5: bring in the poor, the maimed, the halt, the blind
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The doorway again, the host sending his young servant back
out with new urgency, one arm flung wide toward the poorer streets and lanes of
the town beyond. The eager servant in his cream tunic hurries out, and down the
dim lanes ahead the shapes of the overlooked can be seen, a beggar, a man on a
crutch, a blind man feeling the wall. Warm light behind, humble streets ahead.
One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s6-bringing-them-in — Shot 6: the servant found them and brought them in
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A humble street corner in warm afternoon light. The kind
young servant in his cream tunic gently gathers and welcomes the poor and
overlooked toward the feast: a blind man led by the hand, a lame man leaning on
a crutch, a ragged beggar, a weary mother with a child. Their worn faces are
lifting into astonished, disbelieving joy at being invited to a great feast.
Warm, tender light on their wonder. One single continuous scene painted edge to
edge.

## s7-table-full — Shot 7: that my house may be filled
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The banquet hall now FULL and glowing with joy. The long
table is crowded with the poor and the overlooked, the blind man, the man with
his crutch beside him, the beggar, the mother and child, all eating and laughing
together in the golden lamplight, the feast overflowing. At the head of the
table the generous host in his warm-crimson gold-bordered robe stands with both
arms open wide, his face full of delight, welcoming every unexpected guest.
Radiant warm light, a house completely filled with gladness. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge.
