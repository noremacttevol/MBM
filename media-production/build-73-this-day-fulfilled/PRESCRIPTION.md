## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the promise Israel waited on for centuries — good news to the poor,
  freedom for captives, sight for the blind — he said was standing in front of them,
  today, not someday.
- **The turn:** he rolls up the scroll, sits down, and says "This day is this scripture
  fulfilled in your ears" — the ancient promise becomes present tense in that room.
- **Character shown:** he claims Isaiah's rescue-list as his own job description — this
  is who he came to be for the poor, the broken, the captive, the blind, the bruised.
- **Whose face carries it:** the NAZARETH SYNAGOGUE — the townspeople who knew him as
  the boy on their street, every eye fastened on him, the room holding its breath.
  Jesus reading via master ref.
- **The card question falls out of:** "he still reads it as today — what would it mean
  if that promise were meant for you, right now?" No change needed.
