# PRESCRIPTION — build-156-famine-of-hearing

**Row:** 156   **Shelf:** Member   **Scripture:** Amos 8:11–12
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 8)

> Amos's strange famine — not of bread, but of hearing God's word. The beats move from
> the paradox (full tables, starving souls), through the fruitless searching, to the
> ache-before-the-harvest turn. The LORD speaks here (green), shown as voice, no face.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the deepest hunger is not for bread but for God's word — and the
  very ache proves that word is real and is coming back.
- **The turn:** n7 — "you do not ache for what was never real... it was always meant to
  be found." The famine flips into a promise.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** a famine of hearing the word — the apostasy, named this time.
  **GL handoff: Apostasy.**
- **Whose face carries it:** the hungry seeker and the wandering people; Amos as the
  warning prophet. The LORD is voice/light (green captions), never a face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "will you feed on it?" — lands on the one thing.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 8 stills
1. [n1] a strange famine — bread on the tables, water in the wells, and still the land starving — agrees: "There would be bread on the tables and water in the wells, and still the land would starve"
2. [n2] the real hunger — the word of God grown scarce, the heavens quiet, people aching — "a hunger for the word of God... the living voice from heaven grew scarce"
3. [n3] a person amid plenty with a hollow, starving soul no meal can reach — "eat your fill, and still feel a hollow place inside that no meal could reach"
4. [kv11] the LORD declaring the famine of hearing his words, over the barren, quiet land — "I will send a famine in the land, not a famine of bread... but of hearing the words of the LORD"
5. [n4,n5] people wandering, searching high and low, from one end of the world to the other — "wandering from one end of the world to the other, running here and there"
6. [n6,kv12] the search that fails — running to and fro, sea to sea, and not finding it — "they shall run to and fro to seek the word of the LORD, and shall not find it"
7. [n7] the turn — a face realizing the hunger itself proves the word is real — "you do not ache for what was never real"
8. [n8,card] a table spread, the word set before them again, sitting down to eat — warm hold for the closing question — "When the word is offered again, will you feed on it?"
