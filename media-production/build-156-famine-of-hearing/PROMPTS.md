# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #156: "A famine of hearing the words of the LORD" (amos-8_famine-of-hearing)

Amos 8:11-12. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no
motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Apostasy** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: handle with care — the theme is a spiritual "famine," but the treatment is
HOPE, never accusation. NEVER blame any specific church, people, or era; NEVER show a
villain, gore, or literal starvation/emaciation (the verse says it is NOT a famine of
bread). The famine is shown ONLY as inward hunger and searching for God's word — always
with a hint that a harvest follows. Milk framing: a hunger like this is a promise, because
you only ache for what is real.

CHRIST IS NEVER DEPICTED in any frame; God, the word, and hope are shown ONLY as warm
light or as bread/an open book — never a figure, never a face, never a shape.

THE POINT (Why-Law): Amos foretells a strange famine — not of bread but of God's word
(s1, s2); people are outwardly fed yet inwardly hollow (s3, s4 — Amos 8:11); so they
wander and search everywhere for it (s5, s6); running from sea to sea, seeking, not
finding (s7 — Amos 8:12); but the ache itself proves the word is real and meant to be
found (s7); and the word is set before them again (s8).

AMOS LOCK (s1 — the prophet): the prophet Amos, a weathered older Middle-Eastern herdsman
of about fifty-five, sun-browned skin, a full grey-streaked dark beard, deep-set kind
eyes, wearing a simple rough herdsman's tunic and cloak of undyed DUN and dark BROWN wool
with a leather belt and a plain wooden shepherd's staff — dark earth tones, never white,
cream, off-white or pale. Face shown, grave and kind.

SEEKERS LOCK (s2-s8 where people appear): ordinary first-century Israelites of every age —
men, women and children in undyed DARKER earth-tone wool (dun, faded brown, olive, ochre,
grey), faces shown, honest, hungry-hearted and searching (never gaunt or starving — they
are well-fed in body); never in cream or off-white.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or shape of God or Christ anywhere — hope is only
warm light or bread; no villain, no monster, no specific church, cathedral, temple,
steeple, cross, or religious insignia of any group; no literal starvation, emaciation,
gore, or anyone in peril; no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no modern object;
no baked-in text, letters or numbers; no human dressed in cream or off-white.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.
