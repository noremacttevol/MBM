## The impact read
- **The one thing:** prayer is not saying it just right or wearing God down — it is a child
  asking a good Father who is not reluctant but leaning in, glad to hear your voice, and
  gladder still to give good things.
- **The turn:** "how much more shall your Father which is in heaven give good things to
  them that ask him?" The bar leaps from a flawed human parent to a perfect one. That is
  the hinge.
- **Wound answered:** "God is angry / stingy / hard to reach." The answer — bread, not a
  stone; a fish, not a serpent; the Father delights to be asked.
- **Whose face carries it:** the CHILD / the person praying — open trusting hands, the
  searching, the knock, and the child receiving good from a father's hand. Jesus is
  teaching (over-the-shoulder / master ref); no Jesus emotional-face beat here.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Is there something you have been afraid to ask him
  for?" — lands on the one thing. No change needed.
