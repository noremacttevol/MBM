# FLOW PROMPTS — Story Video #30: The Net / The Dragnet (matthew-13_the-net)

Matthew 13:47-50. STILLS-ONLY (Law E). Parable the Lord tells: he is kept FULLY
OFF-SCREEN and never shown in any still — only his narrating voice + KJV lines.
Because no Lord figure is ever shown, the gate is trivially safe
(`jesus_face_gate.py` exits 0). The FISHERMEN are characters in the story, shown
fully and kept consistent. Nano Banana 2, 9:16, x2, 2K download. Hand-painted 2D
storybook style.

**CHARACTER LOCKS (kept consistent across every still they appear in):**
- **The fishermen (s1, s3, s4, s5)** — a small crew of weathered first-century
  Galilean fishermen: sun-browned, dark hair and beards, plain undyed rough-wool
  work tunics belted with rope, sleeves pushed up, bare or sandaled feet. Strong,
  glad, hard-working. Their faces are shown.
- **The great dragnet** — a large hand-knotted rope fishing net with cork/wood
  floats along its top edge and stone weights along the bottom.
- **The fish "of every kind"** — a wide variety of Mediterranean/Galilee fish:
  many different sizes, shapes, and silvery-and-colored kinds, all tangled
  together in the net (this variety IS the point — do not make them all the same).
- **Setting** — the Sea of Galilee: an open wooden fishing boat, blue-green water,
  a pebbled shore, gentle hills beyond, warm daylight.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or
captions in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen
in undyed earth colors. No modern objects.

## s1-cast — Shot 1: a net cast into the sea
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Morning light on the Sea of Galilee. From an open wooden
fishing boat, two or three weathered fishermen in plain undyed work tunics heave a
great hand-knotted dragnet out over the side, the net spreading wide in a graceful
arc as it drops toward the blue-green water, cork floats along its top edge.
Gentle hills on the far shore, calm sea, a hopeful working morning. Their faces
are shown, intent and strong. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge,
9:16 vertical.

## s2-every-kind — Shot 2: gathered of every kind
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] An underwater-edge view of the full dragnet as it fills, alive
with a great VARIETY of fish of every kind tangled together — many different
sizes, shapes, silvery and colored Galilee fish all swept in side by side, none
of them alike. The wide net gathers them all indiscriminately in shafts of
sunlit green water. A sense of abundance and welcome, nobody left out. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s3-draw-shore — Shot 3: drawn to shore, and they sat down
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The pebbled shore of the Sea of Galilee in warm daylight. The
fishermen have dragged the whole heavy, bulging dragnet up out of the water onto
the beach, its floats and stone weights trailing, and have sat down beside the
huge glittering catch to begin the work. The boat rests at the water's edge behind
them. Their faces are shown, tired and satisfied. One single continuous scene
painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s4-good-vessels — Shot 4: gathered the good into vessels
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Close on the shore, a fisherman kneels beside the opened net,
carefully lifting good, healthy fish and placing them into large woven baskets and
clay vessels, handling each one with care, keeping them, not losing one. Warm
light, the sea behind, other baskets already filling. His face is shown, focused
and glad. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s5-cast-bad — Shot 5: but cast the bad away
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same shore. A fisherman quietly sets a few unusable fish
aside onto the pebbles, away from the full baskets of good fish, the sorting done
plainly and without cruelty — simply keeping the good and setting the rest apart.
Warm daylight, the baskets of kept fish beside him, the calm sea beyond. His face
is shown, matter-of-fact and calm. Nothing grim or violent. One single continuous
scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s6-shore-close — Shot 6: the net was cast for the whole sea
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A wide, hopeful view of the Sea of Galilee at golden light, the
great dragnet spread and full along the shore, the vast open water stretching out
to the hills beyond — an image of a net cast wide enough to reach the whole sea.
Peaceful, generous, uplifting. No people needed. One single continuous scene
painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.
