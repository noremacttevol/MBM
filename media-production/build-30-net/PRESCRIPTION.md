# PRESCRIPTION — build-30-net

**Row:** 30   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Matthew 13:47–50
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 7)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the gathering comes first — the net is thrown over the whole
  ocean, of every kind, so no one who wants to be found is missed; the sorting is
  God's job, not ours, and it comes last.
- **The turn:** "the gathering came first" — the emphasis moves from a fearful sorting
  to the wide, indiscriminate mercy of the cast net. That reframe is the hinge.
- **Character shown / wound answered:** answers the "God is picky / you're probably
  not in" wound — the net is not fussy; nobody is too ordinary or too far gone.
- **Whose face carries it:** the FISHERMEN and the mixed catch; at the close, the net
  reaching a single person (you). No Jesus face except the brief teller frame.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Will you let yourself be caught up in his
  grace?" No change needed.
