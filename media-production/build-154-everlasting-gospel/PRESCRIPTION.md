## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the gospel was never lost for good — heaven deliberately sent it
  back to the whole earth, which means it was sent for you.
- **The turn:** kv6 — the angel flying in mid-heaven with the everlasting gospel: a
  restoration delivered from heaven, not worked up from earth.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** a restoration delivered from heaven — the everlasting gospel
  restored. **GL handoff: Restoration.**
- **Whose face carries it:** John the seer, and the faces of the peoples the angel is
  sent to reach. The angel is shown; no Jesus's face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Will you receive it?" — lands on the one thing.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 7 stills
1. [n1] peoples in the dark reaching up toward heaven, the good news buried and lost — agrees: "Men reached for heaven in the dark and could not quite find the way"
2. [n2] John of Patmos, heaven opening in vision, an angel appearing on an errand of mercy — "John of Patmos saw heaven open... an angel, sent on an errand of mercy"
3. [n3,kv6] the angel flying in the midst of heaven, holding out an open book, the whole earth below (held through "everlasting" in n4) — "fly in the midst of heaven, having the everlasting gospel to preach"
4. [n5] every nation, kindred, and tongue — faces of peoples across the earth being reached — "every nation, every family, every tongue. No one is too far away"
5. [n6,kv7] the angel's loud call: fear God, give glory, worship the Maker of heaven, earth, sea, and springs — "worship him that made heaven and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters"
6. [n7] creation itself — stars set in place, seas filled, the ground underfoot — coming home to the Maker — "the same God who set the stars in place and filled the seas"
7. [n8,card] a hand receiving the gospel, sent for you too — warm hold for the closing question — "sent for you, too... Will you receive it?"
