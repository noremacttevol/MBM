# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #154: "Another angel, the everlasting gospel" (revelation-14_everlasting-gospel)

Revelation 14:6-7. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills,
no motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Restoration** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk (CONTENT-CARE.md). Nothing violent or fearful (the
"hour of judgment" is spoken only in the KJV line; the pictures stay hopeful — a rescue,
not a threat). Milk framing: this is GOOD news — the gospel was never lost for good;
heaven sent it back for EVERY nation and person. Awe, warmth, and welcome.

CHRIST IS NEVER DEPICTED in any frame; God the Creator is shown ONLY as warm light from
heaven — never a figure, never a face, never a shape, never a hand. The ANGEL is a
clearly-winged heavenly messenger and must never look like the Lord: never a bearded man
in a plain cream robe, but a luminous winged being in gold-and-light raiment.

THE POINT (Why-Law): whole peoples once lived without the good news, reaching in the dark
(s1); in vision John sees an angel sent from heaven bearing it (s2, s3); the everlasting
gospel is held out to the earth (s4 — Rev 14:6); it goes to every nation, kindred and
tongue, no one left out (s5); the call is to honour and worship the Creator (s6, s7 — Rev
14:7); and it was sent for you, too (s8).

ANGEL LOCK (s2, s3, s4 — the same messenger): a majestic radiant angel, a noble heavenly
messenger with great outstretched feathered wings, clothed in shining raiment of warm GOLD
and amber light (NOT cream, NOT a plain white robe), a calm serene reverent face — clearly
an ANGEL, never a bearded man in a cream robe, never the Lord — cradling a single open
book, surrounded by soft warm radiance. Consistent in every shot he appears.

NATIONS LOCK (s1, s5, s6, s8 where people appear): ordinary people of MANY nations and
lands — varied skin tones and features, men, women and children in varied period earth-tone
robes, mantles and head-coverings (dun, brown, olive, ochre, faded red, muted indigo,
grey), faces shown, honest and hopeful; never in cream or off-white, never winged.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or shape of God or Christ anywhere — the Creator is
only warm light; no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no church buildings,
steeples, crosses, logos, or any modern object; no baked-in text, letters or numbers; no
gore, no terror, no one shamed or in peril; no human dressed in cream or off-white.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

## s1-the-world-waiting — Shot 1: peoples reaching in the dark
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A wide, dim landscape at dusk under a heavy grey-blue sky, the world in spiritual twilight. No angel and no light yet; a mood of quiet waiting and longing, not despair. Every figure whole and correctly formed, faces shown. No one in cream or off-white. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

Below, the dim earth begins to catch the first of the light. The angel is clearly a winged heavenly being, dignified and awe-inspiring, with one head, two arms and two great wings. The light around him is a soft, diffuse open radiance and bright sky only — there is no ring, no disc, no circle, no hoop and no crown of light around, behind, or above his head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.
