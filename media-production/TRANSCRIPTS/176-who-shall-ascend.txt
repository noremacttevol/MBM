[narrator] A question was put to Israel long ago — who gets to climb the hill of the LORD and stand in his holy place?

[scripture] Who shall ascend into the hill of the LORD? or who shall stand in his holy place?

[scripture] He that hath clean hands, and a pure heart; who hath not lifted up his soul unto vanity, nor sworn deceitfully.

[narrator] The answer was not about bloodline or rank.

[scripture] Lift up your heads, O ye gates; and be ye lift up, ye everlasting doors; and the King of glory shall come in.

[narrator] Then the call goes out to the gates themselves — lift up your heads, you ancient doors,

[narrator] and let the King of glory come in.

[scripture] Who is this King of glory? The LORD strong and mighty, the LORD mighty in battle.

[narrator] And who is this King of glory?

[narrator] The LORD himself — strong, mighty, and unbeatable. He is the one who comes in.

[scripture] He shall receive the blessing from the LORD, and righteousness from the God of his salvation.

[narrator] Such a one receives blessing from the LORD, and righteousness from the God of their salvation.

[narrator] The door is open to the one with a clean heart. Come and stand in his presence.
