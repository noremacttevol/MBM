[narrator] Jesus told one more short story, only two lines long.

[jesus] Again, the kingdom of heaven is like unto a merchant man, seeking goodly pearls:

[narrator] This was a man who knew pearls. He spent his whole life traveling and searching, handling the finest pearls in the world, always hunting for something better.

[narrator] He had seen a lot of beautiful pearls. Good ones. Costly ones. But he kept looking, because not one of them was the one.

[narrator] And then one day, he found it. A single pearl, more perfect and more precious than anything he had ever held. The pearl he had been looking for his whole life.

[narrator] And he knew, the moment he saw it, exactly what he was going to do.

[jesus] Who, when he had found one pearl of great price, went and sold all that he had, and bought it.

[narrator] He went home and sold everything. His house, his goods, every other pearl he owned. All of it, gone, to buy the one.

[narrator] And here is the thing. He did not do it grieving. He did not feel robbed. He gave up everything he had, gladly, because what he was getting was worth more than all of it put together.

[narrator] That is what finding the real thing does. When you finally find what your whole life was looking for, letting go of the rest is not a loss. It is the easiest trade you will ever make.

[narrator] And there is one more wonder hidden in this little story. Some have read it the other way around, and it is just as true. That to Jesus, you are the pearl.

[narrator] That he is the merchant who went looking, who found you, and who gave up everything he had, his own life, gladly, to buy you back. That is how good he is. You were worth all of it to him.

[narrator] To him, you were the pearl worth everything. Have you seen how much you are worth to him?
