[narrator] Jesus once told a very short story — about a man, and a field.

[narrator] He was a hired worker, out digging in a field that wasn't even his own.

[narrator] And on one ordinary day, his spade struck something hard, buried in the ground.

[narrator] He cleared away the dirt — and there it was. A treasure. Hidden, forgotten, and worth more than he had ever seen in his life.

[narrator] His heart pounded. Quickly, quietly, he covered it back over — and told no one.

[jesus] Again, the kingdom of heaven is like unto treasure hid in a field; the which when a man hath found, he hideth,

[jesus] and for joy thereof goeth and selleth all that he hath, and buyeth that field.

[narrator] Did you catch what he did? He went home and sold everything he owned.

[narrator] His house. His tools. All of it — gladly, without a second thought.

[narrator] And with every coin he had, he bought that one field for himself.

[narrator] Because he knew what was waiting under the soil. That field was worth more than everything else he owned, put together.

[narrator] That, Jesus said, is what God's kingdom is like.

[narrator] At first it can look like an ordinary field. But once you catch sight of the treasure in it — once you truly see who Jesus is — nothing else even compares.

[narrator] And you don't give everything up sadly. You do it out of pure joy — because you've found the one thing worth having everything else.

[narrator] The man sold all he had — and called it the best trade of his life. If you have found the treasure, what is he worth to you?
