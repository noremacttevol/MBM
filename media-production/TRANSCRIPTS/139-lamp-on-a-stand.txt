[narrator] Jesus was teaching a crowd on a mountainside. He told them who they were before He told them what to do.

[jesus] Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid.

[narrator] Nobody lights a lamp and then hides it under a bowl. You put it up where it can light the whole room.

[jesus] Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick; and it giveth light unto all that are in the house.

[narrator] So let your light shine, He said — not to show off, but so people see the good and look up to your Father.

[jesus] Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.

[narrator] You were lit on purpose. You were put on the stand on purpose.

[narrator] You were made to be seen for good. Let it shine.
