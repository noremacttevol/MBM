[narrator] Jesus stood among the temple courts and spoke into the noise of the crowd. He did not offer another rule for finding God. He offered himself.

[jesus] I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.

[narrator] Not a lamp that burns out. A light that walks with you, so you're never feeling your way blind. It does not pretend the night is harmless. It gives you someone to follow through it.

[narrator] Later, as he met a man who had never seen a face or a sunrise, he said it again.

[jesus] As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world.

[narrator] He did not leave the claim floating in the air. He opened the man's eyes, then stayed with him when the crowd pushed him away. The sign became an invitation: let Jesus show you what is true, then keep walking with him.

[narrator] You do not have to know the whole road to take the next honest step. Walk with the Light.
