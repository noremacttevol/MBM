[narrator] Some in Corinth were arguing as though resurrection might not be real. Paul pointed to a practice already familiar to them and pressed the contradiction.

[scripture] Else what shall they do which are baptized for the dead, if the dead rise not at all? why are they then baptized for the dead?

[narrator] The only reason to do such a thing is the quiet hope that the dead are not gone forever.

[narrator] Baptism stands for new life — a beginning, not an end.

[narrator] So the work done for those who've passed is built on one promise: that death is not the last word.

[scripture] But now is Christ risen from the dead, and become the firstfruits of them that slept.

[narrator] That is Paul's foundation — not a metaphor or a wish.

[narrator] And because He rose, the grave loses its grip — for Him first, and then for all who belong to Him.

[scripture] For as in Adam all die, even so in Christ shall all be made alive.

[narrator] The ordinance done in love reaches across the veil.

[narrator] Offering every soul the chance to choose.

[narrator] Death separates for a while, not forever. Because He lives, there is hope for every name on the other side of the veil.
