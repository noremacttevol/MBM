[narrator] Before the great day, a messenger would come first — Elijah, the prophet, sent ahead.

[god] Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the LORD:

[narrator] Listen to who is talking there. That is God himself, making a promise out loud: before the last great day arrives, I am sending Elijah back. Not an army. Not a warning shot. A prophet.

[god] And he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers, lest I come and smite the earth with a curse.

[narrator] His work was not to thunder, but to mend. To turn the hearts of fathers back to their children, and children back to their fathers.

[narrator] The same spirit would later rest on John, preparing the way.

[narrator] So that when the Lord came, families would be ready.

[narrator] Not divided, but whole.

[narrator] He cares about your family. Let the healing start with your own heart.
