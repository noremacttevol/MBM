[narrator] Jesus stood before the crowd and described two roads — two ways that every person gets to choose.

[jesus] Enter ye in at the strait gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction, and many there be which go in thereat:

[narrator] One road looks easy. The gate is wide, the path is broad, and a lot of people walk that way.

[jesus] Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it.

[narrator] The other road looks harder. The gate is narrow, the path is tight, and far fewer find it.

[narrator] He wasn't describing geography. He was describing a decision.

[narrator] A narrow gate that leads to life, found by the few who choose it.

[narrator] He never said the narrow way would be crowded. He said it leads to life. Both gates are standing open right now, and nobody walks through either one by accident.

[narrator] There are two gates, and one choice. He said the narrow way is worth finding. Step through.
