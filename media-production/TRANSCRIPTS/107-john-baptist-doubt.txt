[narrator] John the Baptist had spent his whole life preparing the way. He had pointed to Jesus and said, behold, the Lamb of God. And now he sat in a prison cell, waiting to die, and the doubts crept in.

[narrator] If Jesus really was the promised one, why was John still in chains? Where was the rescue? So he did something honest and brave. He sent two of his followers to ask Jesus directly.

[scripture] Art thou he that should come, or do we look for another?

[narrator] It is one of the most human questions in the whole Bible. Are you really who I hoped you were? And notice — Jesus was not offended. He did not scold John for asking.

[scripture] Jesus answered and said unto them,

[jesus] Go and shew John again those things which ye do hear and see: The blind receive their sight, and the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have the gospel preached to them.

[narrator] He did not send back an argument. He sent back a scene. Go and tell John what you see happening — right here, right now. The blind seeing. The broken mended. The poorest people being treated like they matter. This is what I am doing.

[narrator] Not overthrowing an empire. Not breaking open a prison. But healing, one by one, the people everyone else stepped over. That was the answer to give a doubting man. Look at the love. Look at what it is actually doing.

[jesus] And blessed is he, whosoever shall not be offended in me.

[narrator] It was tender, not sharp. Blessed is the one who does not give up on me when I do not look the way he expected. And then, the moment the messengers left, Jesus turned to the crowd and praised John — defending his doubting friend to his face.

[narrator] The answer came back to the cell, and John was at peace. Not rescued — but no longer alone in the dark, and no longer afraid that he had been wrong.

[narrator] Sometimes the answer to our doubt is not the thing we asked for. It is simply, quietly: look at the love. It is real, and it is for you. Do not be offended — only trust, and be at peace.

[narrator] Even the strongest believer sometimes sits in the dark and wonders. Jesus did not shame John for asking; he answered gently, with evidence of grace. If you have your own honest question, would you dare to bring it to him too?
