[narrator] At the start of all things, before any person drew breath, a counsel happened in the Godhead — let us make man in our image.

[scripture] And God said,

[god] Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.

[narrator] Hear the word us. God is not talking to himself there — he is talking with someone, and what they decide together is to make a creature that looks like them. Then he hands that creature the whole living world to take care of.

[scripture] So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.

[narrator] And then he did it. Moses says it three times in one sentence, like he cannot get over it — in his own image, in the image of God, male and female both. Not one of them closer to God than the other. Both of them bearing the likeness.

[scripture] And the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.

[narrator] Then the act:

[narrator] The plan included dominion — over fish, birds, cattle, and all the earth. Stewards, not owners.

[narrator] Not in the shape of any creature, but bearing something of God himself:

[narrator] the capacity to know him, to choose him, to reflect him.

[narrator] Every person since carries that original dignity — made in the image, loved into being.

[narrator] You are made in his image. That is worth more than you know.
