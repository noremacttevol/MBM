[narrator] One year at Shiloh, Hannah slipped to the door of the tabernacle and prayed with a voice no one could hear — only her lips moved. This is what she said.

[woman] O LORD of hosts, if thou wilt indeed look on the affliction of thine handmaid, and remember me, and not forget thine handmaid,
