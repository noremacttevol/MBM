[narrator] It was the custom at Passover for the governor to release one prisoner the crowd chose. Pilate had two men: Jesus, and a killer named Barabbas.

[narrator] Pilate could see Jesus had done nothing worth death. So he put the choice to the crowd himself.

[scripture] Will ye that I release unto you the King of the Jews?

[narrator] Do you want me to release the King of the Jews to you? He was almost offering it to them.

[narrator] He thought the crowd would surely pick the innocent one.

[narrator] But the chief priests worked the crowd, and they shouted for Barabbas instead. Pilate asked them what he should do with Jesus, then — and they answered with one word.

[scripture] Crucify him.

[narrator] Crucify him. That was it. That was the answer. And Pilate could not make sense of it.

[scripture] Why, what evil hath he done?

[narrator] Why? he asked them. What has he actually done? The man holding all the power in that courtyard said out loud that there was no case — and then handed him over anyway.

[narrator] So the guilty man walked free, and the innocent one was handed over in his place.

[narrator] Think about what Barabbas got: he was condemned, and then someone else took his exact sentence. He walked out free because Jesus took his cross.

[narrator] That's not just Barabbas's story. That's the whole gospel in one swap — the innocent for the guilty, so the guilty could go free.

[narrator] Barabbas walked free because the Son of God took his place. So can you. That's the whole point.
