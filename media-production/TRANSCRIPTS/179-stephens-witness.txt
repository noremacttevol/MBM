[narrator] Stephen, full of the Holy Spirit, was dragged before the council for speaking the truth. He told them the whole story of Israel — and they boiled with rage.

[narrator] But Stephen didn't look at his accusers. He looked up, and what he saw changed everything.

[narrator] He saw the glory of God — and Jesus, standing at the right hand of the Father. And he said so, out loud:

[scripture] Behold, I see the heavens opened, and the Son of man standing on the right hand of God.

[narrator] Look, he told them — heaven is open, and the Son of man is standing at God's right hand. They would not hear it. They rushed him out.

[narrator] But Stephen's face was the face of an angel — at peace, not afraid.

[narrator] Then he knelt down, with the stones still coming, and his last words asked mercy for the ones throwing them.

[scripture] Lord, lay not this sin to their charge.

[narrator] Don't hold this against them, he prayed. And then he fell asleep.

[narrator] He saw the Son of man standing to receive him. You can face your end with that same peace.
