[scripture] If thou be Christ, save thyself and us.

[narrator] But the other one stopped him. We're getting what we deserve, he said. This man has done nothing wrong.

[scripture] Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom.

[narrator] Lord, remember me when you come into your kingdom. No good deeds to offer. No time left to fix his life. Just a dying man asking.

[jesus] Verily I say unto thee, To day shalt thou be with me in paradise.

[narrator] Today. Not someday, not after you've earned it. Today. The last-minute faith of a criminal was enough.
