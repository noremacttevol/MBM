[narrator] Isaiah told God's people what kind of fast the LORD actually wants — not just going hungry to look holy.

[god] Is not this the fast that I have chosen? to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke?

[god] Is it not to deal thy bread to the hungry, and that thou bring the poor that are cast out to thy house? when thou seest the naked, that thou cover him; and that thou hide not thyself from thine own flesh?

[narrator] Share your bread with the hungry.

[narrator] Bring the poor, the ones with nowhere to go, into your home.

[narrator] When you see someone with no clothes, cover them. Don't turn away from your own family.

[god] Then shall thy light break forth as the morning, and thine health shall spring forth speedily:

[narrator] The LORD promises — call, and He answers. Help others, and your own darkness becomes noonday.

[narrator] The fast God chooses is a hand opened to the hurting. Open yours — He meets you there.
