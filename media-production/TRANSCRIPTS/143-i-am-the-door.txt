[narrator] Jesus used a picture the crowd knew well — a sheepfold with one opening, and a shepherd who guards it.

[jesus] Verily, verily, I say unto you, I am the door of the sheep.

[narrator] Anyone who climbs in some other way is there to take, not to care.

[narrator] But the door? The door lets the flock in safe.

[jesus] I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in and out, and find pasture.

[narrator] Through Him you come in and you go out — free, fed, not trapped. He's the way in, and the way to everything good.

[narrator] There's one door. Knock, and walk through.
