[narrator] Jesus went back to Nazareth, the town that watched Him grow up. He taught in the synagogue.

[scripture] Is not this the carpenter, the son of Mary, the brother of James, and Joses, and of Juda, and Simon? and are not his sisters here with us?

[narrator] But the people who'd known Him as a boy couldn't believe. They took offense at Him — and their unbelief became a wall.

[jesus] A prophet is not without honour, but in his own country, and among his own kin, and in his own house.

[narrator] A prophet gets honored everywhere, He said — everywhere except home. Except among his own relatives. Except in his own house.

[narrator] He could do only a few mighty works there. Not because His power ran out, but because they wouldn't receive it.

[scripture] And he could there do no mighty work, save that he laid his hands upon a few sick folk, and healed them. And he marvelled because of their unbelief.

[narrator] He still healed the few who came. Even amazement at their doubt didn't stop His mercy.

[narrator] A few sick folk. That is all Nazareth brought Him, and He laid His hands on every one of them. The mercy never shrank down to match the unbelief.

[narrator] Faith opens the door. Where people believed, even a little, He worked.

[narrator] He healed the few who reached out. The door is the same today — believe, and let Him work.
