[narrator] Lazarus had been in the tomb four days. His sister Martha came to Jesus, heavy with grief but still full of faith.

[narrator] She said she believed her brother would rise again at the last day.

[narrator] Jesus answered with a promise bigger than the last day.

[jesus] I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live:

[narrator] Not a theory about life after death. He said He Himself is the life — and that trusting Him beats the grave.

[jesus] And whosoever liveth and believeth in me shall never die. Believest thou this?

[narrator] Martha said yes.

[narrator] And moments later, her brother walked out of the tomb.

[narrator] Death is not the end of the story. He is the life.
