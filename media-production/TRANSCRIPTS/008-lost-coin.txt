[narrator] When Jesus wanted to show how God feels about one lost soul, he didn't talk about crowds. He told this story.

[jesus] Either what woman having ten pieces of silver, if she lose one piece, doth not light a candle, and sweep the house, and seek diligently till she find it?

[narrator] A woman has ten coins. She loses one.

[narrator] She lights a lamp. She sweeps the whole house.

[narrator] She searches carefully — not casually, carefully — until she finds it.

[jesus] And when she hath found it, she calleth her friends and her neighbours together, saying,

[jesus] Rejoice with me; for I have found the piece which I had lost.

[narrator] Then she calls her neighbors and friends to celebrate.

[narrator] One coin. Out of ten. The joy is disproportionate to the value of the coin.

[jesus] Likewise, I say unto you, there is joy in the presence of the angels of God over one sinner that repenteth.

[narrator] Over one. Not a crowd. One.

[narrator] Have you ever felt like the thing that got lost, rather than the one doing the searching?
