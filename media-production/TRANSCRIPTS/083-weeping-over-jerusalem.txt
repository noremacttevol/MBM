[narrator] If only you had known — even you, even today — what would have brought you peace. He is not scolding the city. He is talking to it the way you talk to someone you love who is about to walk past the one thing that would have saved them.

[narrator] His tears weren't anger. They were the grief of love that sees exactly what could have been.
