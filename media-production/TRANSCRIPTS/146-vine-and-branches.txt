[narrator] Jesus used a picture His friends would know —

[narrator] a vine, and the branches that grow from it.

[jesus] Abide in me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, except it abide in the vine; no more can ye, except ye abide in me.

[narrator] Stay in me, he said, and I will stay in you. A branch cannot make fruit on its own. It has never once done it. All it does is stay attached, and the life of the vine does the rest.

[jesus] I am the vine, ye are the branches:

[narrator] I am the vine, he said. You are the branches. Not visitors. Not hired hands. Branches — part of the same living thing.

[jesus] He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing.

[narrator] A branch cut off from the vine doesn't dry up because it's weak. It dries up because it's disconnected.

[narrator] Stay joined to Him, and the life flows. Try to bear fruit on your own, and there's nothing there.

[narrator] He wasn't asking for effort.

[narrator] He was offering connection.

[narrator] Stay connected to the Vine. Let His life flow through you.
