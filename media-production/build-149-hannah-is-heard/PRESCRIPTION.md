> Bridge wound: **the silent heaven — "my prayers go nowhere, and I'm misjudged even
> when I pray."** The inherited picture is a God who doesn't hear, over a life no one
> understands. Hannah prays a prayer so quiet no one can hear it, is accused of being
> drunk while she prays — and God hears it exactly.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the prayer no one else can hear, prayed by someone the world has
  written off, is heard exactly — your quiet prayers reach him.
- **The turn:** "the LORD remembered her" — the silent, misjudged prayer at the
  doorpost is answered; heaven was not silent after all.
- **Wound answered:** the silent-heaven wound — she prays with only her lips moving,
  is misread as drunk, and is heard precisely; God remembered her.
- **Whose face carries it:** HANNAH — the empty lap, the silent moving lips, the tears,
  the face no longer sad. Eli's misjudgment. No Jesus (Old Testament).
- **The card question falls out of:** "Your quiet prayers are heard too." Lands on the
  one thing. No change needed.
