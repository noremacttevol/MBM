# STILL PROMPTS — Video #102: Jacob's Ladder (genesis-28_jacobs-ladder)

Genesis 28:10-22. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — ten painted stills, no motion clips.
BRIDGE/MILK. CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN. No violence: Jacob is a lone traveller; the
dream is bright and holy, never frightening.

NO DIVINE FIGURE ANYWHERE. God is a voice and a radiant presence in this story and is
NEVER depicted as a person: at the top of the ladder there is only warm radiant golden
light pouring from an opening in heaven — no figure, no face, no shape, no hand within
it. (No Jesus is shown in any frame; nobody wears cream or off-white in any frame —
every garment is a clearly darker earth color.) The ANGELS on the ladder ARE shown:
graceful shining messengers in soft pale robes of light, ascending and descending —
they are angels, not the Lord. Because no shot depicts Jesus or the Lord as a figure,
no JESUS LOCK / REF line is required and the face-gate stays green.

JACOB LOCK (every shot he appears in — s1,s2,s6,s7,s8,s9,s10): a young Hebrew man of
about forty, warm olive-brown sun-weathered skin, a short-cropped dark brown beard and
dark brown hair, in one ankle-length travelling robe of dusty russet-brown wool with a
worn dark leather girdle, a coarse grey-brown mantle over his shoulders, leather
sandals, a wooden staff and a small travelling bundle — road-worn and alone. His face
IS shown, tired then awed, consistent in every shot. Every figure has two arms, two
hands, two legs and one head.

ANGEL LOCK (s3, s4, s5): tall graceful winged messengers in flowing pale luminous
robes, calm and beautiful, moving up and down the great stair — clearly angels, faces
serene; they never wear a hood and are never dark voids. They are the only shining
figures; the Lord himself is never a figure, only light.

MUST NEVER SHOW: any depiction of God or the Lord as a person/figure/face/hand; any
figure or shape inside the light at the top; anything frightening; any modern object;
any text baked into the image; anyone in cream or off-white; a multi-panel or
comic-strip layout.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single tall upright vertical scene painted edge to edge, no panels."
Skip any comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.
