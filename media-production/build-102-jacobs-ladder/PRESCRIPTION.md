> Bridge shelf. The wound is **"heaven is silent now / heaven is closed and far off."**
> The answer is a literal open stairway between heaven and the exact patch of dirt where
> a runaway lay down. God meets the man who ran, at his lowest, and promises to go with
> him.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** heaven is not far off and God is not done with you — he comes to a
  liar and a runaway at his lowest and promises to go with him and never let go, and the
  man had done nothing to earn it.
- **The turn:** the dream — a great stairway rising from the very ground into an opening
  in heaven, angels moving up and down over the lonely dirt where he sleeps. That image
  is the hinge: heaven was open right above him the whole time.
- **Wound answered:** "heaven is silent / closed." The answer is heaven trafficked and
  open, right over the head of the one who ran.
- **Whose face carries it:** JACOB — fleeing and looking back, sleeping under the stone,
  waking with a start, then a changed man on the road. God is a presence/light above the
  stairway, not a rendered face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what might God be wanting to say to you, right
  where you are?" — lands on the one thing. No change needed.
