## CONTINUITY LOCKS

[TREASURY LOCK] = the temple treasury court, warm daylight through tall openings:
pale stone walls and pillars, and along one wall a row of large bronze offering
chests with wide trumpet-shaped metal mouths, their metal catching the light. The
SAME chests, in the same row, in every shot they appear in.

[WIDOW LOCK] = the poor widow: an elderly woman with a small thin frame, a deeply
lined kind face, her grey hair fully covered by a worn dark charcoal-grey head
shawl, one patched dark grey-brown robe (the same in every shot) — never off-white,
never cream; her bearing quiet and dignified, never staged for pity.

[RICH-GIVERS LOCK] = the affluent worshippers: men in rich saturated robes of deep
blue, wine-red and embroidered ochre with fine sashes and rings — confident and
human, never sneering caricatures; not one of them in off-white or cream, none
resembling the man in cream. Every figure: two arms, two hands, two legs, one head.

[DISCIPLES LOCK] = the disciples: grown men in oatmeal, dun, faded brown and grey
wool robes — not one in off-white or cream. Every figure: two arms, two hands, two
legs, one head.
