  costs you, not its size, and he noticed the one gift no one else in the courtyard did.
- **The turn:** Jesus calls his disciples over, as if he'd just seen the most important

  unnoticed are exactly who he is watching.
- **Whose face carries it:** the WIDOW — small, unnoticed, giving everything she had.
  Jesus watching from across the treasury via master ref.
- **The card question falls out of:** "he noticed the gift no one else did — what might
  he already see in you that others overlook?" No change needed.
