## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he is not the barrier standing between you and safety — he is the
  way in. A door, not a wall.
- **The turn:** "I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and
  shall go in and out, and find pasture" — the thing you feared was closed is an opening.
- **Wound answered:** the God-as-gatekeeper wound — the door isn't locked against you,
  it's how you come in, and how you're free to come and go, fed.
- **Whose face carries it:** the FLOCK / a person at the threshold — the ones going in
  safe and out free. Jesus only via master ref as he teaches.
- **The card question falls out of:** "There's one door. Knock, and walk through."
  Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
