## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he knows his own by name, and he speaks it while you are still
  crying — what opened Mary's eyes wasn't an argument or a proof, it was her name.
- **The turn:** "Mary." / "Rabboni." — one word each, red straight into pink,
  nothing between. That exchange is the hinge; grief flips to joy in a heartbeat.
- **Character shown:** a risen Lord who comes looking for the one weeping woman, by
  name, before she can even see him through her tears.
- **Whose face carries it:** MARY throughout — the weeping, the mistaken plea, the
  recognition. Jesus's face only via master ref / over-the-shoulder at his lines;
  the camera stays on Mary hearing her name.
- **The card question falls out of:** "He knows your name — and he speaks it even in
  your grief. Listen for it." Lands directly on the one thing.
