# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #163: "Built on apostles and prophets" (ephesians-2_apostles-and-prophets)

Ephesians 2:19-20. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no
motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Church Organization** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk. The note is belonging and holy order: once-strangers
are made family, and the church has a real design. Warmth and welcome.

CHRIST IS NEVER DEPICTED as a figure. He is shown ONLY as the glowing chief CORNERSTONE of
the building, and as warm light — never a face, never a body, never a shape of a person.
God is likewise only warm light.

THE POINT (Why-Law): once outsiders and strangers (s1), those far off are brought near and
welcomed (s2); made family, the household of God (s3, s4 — Eph 2:19); a household needs a
house, so the church is a building (s4-s5); its foundation is the apostles and prophets (s5);
its chief cornerstone, that keeps it square, is Christ — shown as a glowing stone (s6); the
whole house rises, built on that foundation (s7 — Eph 2:20); and you have a fitted place in
it (s8).

PEOPLE LOCK (s1-s4, s8): ordinary first-century people of mixed age and nation — men, women
and children in undyed DARKER earth-tone wool (dun, faded brown, olive, ochre, russet, grey),
faces shown, at first uncertain then welcomed and glad; never in cream or off-white.
BUILDERS (s5): ordinary first-century stone-masons and elders in earth-tone work clothes,
laying great foundation stones; never in cream.
THE CORNERSTONE (s6, s7): a single large, beautiful, perfectly-cut foundation cornerstone of
warm honey-gold stone set at the corner, glowing softly with a warm inner light — clearly a
STONE, never a person.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or body of God or Christ anywhere — Christ appears
only as the glowing cornerstone, God only as light; no modern object; no cross, steeple,
church-building-with-a-spire, or insignia of any group (the "house of God" is an ancient
stone temple/house, timeless); no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no baked-in text,
letters or numbers; no human dressed in cream or off-white. Every image is ONE single
unbroken full-frame scene — no panels, no comic strip, no dividing lines.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.
