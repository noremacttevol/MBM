# PRESCRIPTION — build-112-beatitudes

**Row:** 112   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Matthew 5:1–10
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 12)

> Bridge shelf. The wound is **"God is angry / blesses the winners and religion overlooks
> me."** The answer is the Beatitudes, where Jesus turns it upside down: every blessing
> goes to exactly the person the world and religion walk past. Here "the picture is the
> people" — each blessing gets a real human face.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God's kingdom is upside down and good — the blessings all go to the
  overlooked, so if you have ever felt small, unseen, or passed over, you are not at the
  back of the line, you are exactly the one he came for.
- **The turn:** it opens where no one expects — "Blessed are the poor in spirit" — the ones
  with nothing to offer God but empty hands get the kingdom first. That reversal, held
  across every blessing, is the hinge.
- **Wound answered:** "religion overlooks me / God blesses the strong." The answer — every
  blessing is written for the person the world would call a loser.
- **Whose face carries it:** the OVERLOOKED ONES — the poor, the grieving, the meek, the
  merciful, the pure, the peacemakers, each on its own real human face. Jesus teaching is
  shown via master ref / over-the-shoulder.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Which of these blessings sounds like it was written
  for you?" — lands on the one thing. No change needed.
