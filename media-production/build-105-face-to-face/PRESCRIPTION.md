# PRESCRIPTION — build-105-face-to-face

**Row:** 105   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Exodus 33:7–23
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 13)

> Bridge shelf. The wound is **"heaven is silent / God is distant — a master to obey from
> far off, not someone you could know."** The answer is how God actually talked with
> Moses: face to face, as a man speaks to his friend, and when Moses asks to see his
> glory God chooses to show him his *goodness*.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God does not want to be feared from far off — he wants to be known up
  close, as a friend, and of everything he could show a friend he chooses to show his
  goodness.
- **The turn:** "shew me thy glory" → "I will make all my goodness pass before thee." Of
  all he could have shown, he chose goodness. That answer is the hinge, and the cleft in
  the rock is its picture.
- **Wound answered:** "God is distant / cold." The answer — he spoke with a man as with a
  friend, and hid that friend in a rock to keep him safe from too much glory. Care, not
  distance.
- **Whose face carries it:** MOSES — walking out to the tent, bold enough to ask, tucked
  into the cleft under the covering hand, coming down with a shining face he doesn't even
  know he has. God is presence/cloud/light and a covering hand; the text itself says no
  man sees his face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Would you let him be that kind of friend to you?" —
  lands on the one thing. No change needed.
