# PRESCRIPTION — build-46-seed-growing

**Row:** 46   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Mark 4:26–29
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 9)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the growing was never on your shoulders — you do your small
  faithful part and God does the part you were never strong enough to do; you can
  actually rest.
- **The turn:** [n4] "Notice what he is not doing" — the farmer is not out at midnight
  pulling on the shoots. He planted; now he trusts. The story hands you permission to
  stop straining.
- **Character shown:** a God who makes things grow quietly, underground, on a
  timetable you did not set — the restful side of his goodness.
- **Whose face carries it:** the FARMER (scattering, then sleeping, then reaping with
  joy). No Jesus figure in the parable; it is a field and a man.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what could you finally set down, if you believed
  him?"

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 9 stills
1. [n1/jv26] a farmer scattering seed by hand across his field — "as if a man should cast seed into the ground"
2. [n2] the farmer finishing and walking home — "he does it by hand, and then... he goes home"
3. [jv27/n3] the farmer sleeping and rising, night and day, ordinary weeks passing — "should sleep, and rise night and day"
4. [n3/n4] under the dark soil where he cannot see or help, the seed quietly growing — "the seed is doing the one thing he could never make it do. It is growing"
5. [jv28/n6] the field coming in its own order — first the blade, then the ear, then the full corn — "first the blade, then the ear, after that the full corn in the ear"
6. [n7/n8] the field ripening on a clock he did not set — hidden is not dead, slow is not stopped — "Under the surface, where you cannot watch it, it is already on its way up"
7. [jv29/n9] one morning the grain is ready — the farmer going out with the sickle to bring it in with joy — "immediately he putteth in the sickle, because the harvest is come"
8. [n10/n11] the farmer able to rest, the growing God's part not his — "you can actually sleep tonight"
9. [card] hold on the ripe golden field for the closing question
