# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #46: The Seed Growing Secretly (mark-4_seed-growing)

Mark 4:26-29. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eleven painted stills, no motion clips.

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk. Nothing violent, nothing frightening. The most
restful parable in the set: one field, followed from sowing to harvest, and the quiet
point that the growing is never on the farmer's shoulders. Milk framing: hidden is not
dead, slow is not stopped, the harvest always comes; the closing card is an invitation to
set down what was never yours to carry.

Jesus appears in only TWO shots — s1 and s11, the FRAME where he teaches the parable to a
small crowd. In both the camera is directly behind him, the back of his head to us, his
face never shown, never visible, at any angle. The parable pictures (s2-s10) have NO divine
figure: they follow one ordinary Galilean farmer and his one grain field through time.

JESUS LOCK (identical wording in s1 and s11): a first-century Middle-Eastern man, warm
olive-brown skin, LONG dark brown-black hair past the shoulders, one plain undyed off-white
cream wool robe, bare-headed, no hood; no glow, no bright ring, no outlining light; an ordinary solid
man. The camera is always directly behind him and his face is never shown, never visible,
at any angle.

CROWD LOCK (s1, s11) — ordinary Galilean listeners of every age, men, women and a few
children, in plain earth-tone wool of dun, faded brown, olive and muted blue, all clearly
darker than the one cream robe of the figure in the foreground; not one wears off-white or
cream. Faces shown, attentive and at ease.

FARMER LOCK (s2, s3, s4, s5, s9, s10) — one ordinary Galilean farmer of about forty,
weathered and kind, a short dark beard, in a plain russet-brown wool work tunic to the knee
with a cord belt and a simple dun head-cloth, clearly NOT cream. His face IS shown, calm,
patient and content — a man at rest in his work, never anxious.

FIELD LOCK (s2-s10) — one gentle grain field on a low Judean hillside, followed through
time: freshly plowed brown furrows at sowing (s2); the same field dark and still under a
starry night (s3); the first faint green haze of sprouting (s4-s5); rows of young green
wheat (s6); taller wheat forming pale green-gold heads (s7); a full field of heavy golden
ripe wheat (s8-s9). Low stone walls and soft hills frame it.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single continuous scene painted edge to edge." Skip any
comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.

## s1-the-telling — Shot 1: so is the kingdom of God
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A gentle green Galilean hillside beside a grain field in the warm gold light of morning. The camera stands DIRECTLY BEHIND the teacher and low, close to his back, so that only the back of his head, his long dark brown-black hair and his shoulders fill the lower near corner of the frame; the back of his head is to us and his face is never shown, never visible, at no angle. He is a Middle-Eastern man with warm olive-brown skin and LONG dark brown-black hair past his shoulders, in one plain undyed off-white cream wool robe, bare-headed, no hood, no glow, no bright ring, no outlining light, an ordinary solid man teaching, seen only from behind. Filling the frame beyond him and looking past the camera toward him sits and stands a small crowd of ordinary Galilean people of every age, men, women and a few children, in plain earth-tone wool of dun, faded brown, olive and muted blue. Their clothes are all clearly darker than the one cream robe of the figure in the foreground; not one of them wears off-white or cream, and no second man in this picture wears a cream robe. Their faces are painted fully and clearly, attentive and at ease. Warm gold morning light, green grass, a grain field beyond. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s2-casting-seed — Shot 2: as if a man should cast seed into the ground
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A freshly plowed grain field of open brown furrows on a low Judean hillside in the soft gold light of early morning. One ordinary Galilean farmer of about forty, weathered and kind with a short dark beard, in a plain russet-brown wool work tunic with a cord belt and a simple dun head-cloth, NOT cream, walks the field scattering seed by hand from a cloth seed-bag at his hip, his arm swept out in the wide arc of sowing, grains spraying from his open hand across the turned earth. His face is painted fully and clearly, calm and unhurried, doing good ordinary work. Soft gold morning light, brown furrows, low stone walls and hills beyond. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s3-he-sleeps — Shot 3: and should sleep, and rise night and day
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same hillside grain field at deep quiet night under a wide sky full of soft stars, the furrows dark and still, everything resting. In the near corner, the small stone farmhouse glows with one warm low lamp through its window, where the farmer is asleep for the night. No one is awake in the picture, nothing is being done — the whole scene is stillness and trust while, unseen beneath the dark soil, the work goes on. Deep blue night, gentle starlight, one warm window, soft silver on the furrows. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

His face is painted fully and clearly, easy and untroubled. Behind him the faint new green rises on its own across the furrows. Warm morning light, the first green on the dark earth, hills beyond. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s5-he-knows-not-how — Shot 5: he knoweth not how
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A closer, tender view of the farmer of about forty in his russet-brown work tunic and dun head-cloth, NOT cream, crouched at the edge of the field in warm daylight, gently cupping a hand near a cluster of small green wheat shoots pushing up out of the dark soil, marvelling at them. His face is painted fully and clearly, glad and quietly amazed, not understanding how it happens and not needing to. His hand is open and near the shoots but not pulling at them; every hand is joined to the correct arm and no spare hand appears. Warm daylight, tender green shoots, rich dark earth. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s6-the-blade — Shot 6: first the blade
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A wide, peaceful view of the hillside field now filled with rows of young green wheat, the first tender blades standing bright and fresh across the whole slope in soft morning light, gentle and full of new life. No people are in the frame. The painting rests quietly on the field of new green wheat, low stone walls and soft hills framing it. Fresh green blades, warm gold light, dark earth showing between the young rows. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s7-the-ear — Shot 7: then the ear

No people are in the frame. The painting lingers on the ripening heads of grain nodding on their stalks, the field fuller and heavier than before. Warm afternoon light, green-gold wheat forming its heads, hills beyond. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s8-the-full-corn — Shot 8: after that the full corn in the ear

No people are in the frame. The whole picture is warm, golden and complete, a field that has quietly arrived at its fullness.
