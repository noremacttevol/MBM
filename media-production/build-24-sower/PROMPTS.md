## s1-boat-teaching — Shot 1: he taught them from the boat
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A calm Galilee lakeshore in warm morning light. In the
foreground, seen only from behind with his back fully to the camera and his face
turned away, never shown, the seated storyteller — the Lord — sits in a small
wooden fishing boat drawn up just off the shore, in a plain undyed cream wool
robe with long dark hair past the shoulders, warm Middle-Eastern, one hand
lifted as he teaches; no halo, no glow. Rising up the grassy hillside beyond the
water, a large crowd of ordinary Galilean people sit and stand, every face
turned toward the boat, listening. Soft gold light on the water. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s2-sower-scatter — Shot 2: a sower went out to sow
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A wide open field on a hillside in warm daylight. A
weathered Middle-Eastern farmer with a short dark beard, in a cream tunic under a
worn brown mantle, strides across the freshly turned earth with a woven
seed-bag slung at his shoulder, his arm sweeping out in a broad arc as he flings
a spray of pale golden grain across the ground. The seed scatters wide through
the air. Open sky, rolling gold-and-green country behind him. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s3-wayside-birds — Shot 3: some fell by the wayside
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Close along a hard-packed dirt footpath at the edge of the
field. Scattered grains of pale wheat lie exposed on the beaten, sun-baked path
where they could not sink in, and several small black birds have dropped down and
are pecking the seed up from the bare ground. Beyond the path the farmer is a
smaller figure still sowing. Harsh clear daylight, dusty earth. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s4-stony-scorched — Shot 4: some fell on stony ground
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A patch of thin soil over flat shelves of pale rock under a
harsh high sun. A few thin green shoots have sprung up quickly between the
stones, but they are pale and wilting, drooping and scorched, their roots unable
to reach past the rock beneath. Heat shimmer, cracked dry ground, the hard rock
showing through the shallow soil. One single continuous scene painted edge to
edge.

## s5-thorns-choked — Shot 5: some fell among thorns
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A tangle of grey-green thorn bushes and dry thistles taking
over a corner of the field. A few young wheat stalks are struggling up in the
middle of it, but the thorns and weeds have grown thick and tall all around and
are crowding and choking them, smothering the pale stalks so they cannot bear
grain. Dull overcast light, a strangled, overgrown feel. One single continuous
scene painted edge to edge.

## s6-good-harvest — Shot 6: but other fell into good ground
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A glorious field of ripe golden wheat under warm late-day
light, heavy full heads of grain bowing on strong green stalks as far as the eye
can see, deep-rooted and thriving. The weathered farmer stands among the tall
wheat, one hand cupping a heavy head of grain, his face lit with quiet joy at the
abundance. Warm gold light pouring across the harvest. One single continuous
scene painted edge to edge.

## s7-boat-close — Shot 7: who hath ears to hear
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same Galilee lakeshore now in golden late-day light. In
the foreground, seen only from behind with his back fully to the camera and his
face turned away, never shown, the seated storyteller — the Lord — still sits in
the small wooden boat just off the shore, in his plain cream wool robe with long
dark hair past the shoulders, warm Middle-Eastern, both hands open in an
unhurried, inviting gesture toward the listening crowd; no halo, no glow. On the
grassy hillside beyond the water the large crowd leans in, every face turned
toward the boat. Warm gold light low on the lake. One single continuous scene
painted edge to edge.
