# PRESCRIPTION — build-24-sower

**Row:** 24   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Matthew 13:1–23
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 12)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he flings his word wide over every kind of heart, hoping — and no soil is fixed forever; a hard path can be broken up.
- **The turn:** the seed on the good ground bearing a harvest many times over (j8/j3) — after three failures the story lands on what grows; the final beat that ground can *change* (n12) is the invitation.
- **Character shown:** generosity — the farmer does not skip the hard path or the rocks; he keeps sowing and never stops hoping your heart is the good ground.
- **Whose face carries it:** no human witness — the SOWER and the four soils carry it; the picture is the ground and the seed. (Parable — the sower is Jesus's stand-in.)
- **The card question falls out of:** "None of these soils are fixed forever. What is the soil of your heart today?" — falls straight out of the one thing. No change needed.
