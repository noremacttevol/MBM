# Story Video #12 — Blind Bartimaeus (Mark 10:46–52)

Runtime 60–75s. Prepend the STYLE BLOCK from 00-MASTER-PLAN.md. Jesus: the
procession that stops, a voice, a presence framed as light — never his face.
Sound design carries this one: it is a story ABOUT hearing and shouting.

## Narration script (app's exact words)

> A blind man named Bartimaeus sat begging beside the road out of Jericho.
> When he heard Jesus was passing, he began to shout: "Jesus, have mercy on
> me!"
>
> The crowd told him to be quiet. Many voices, telling one desperate man
> that his need was an embarrassment.
>
> He shouted louder. *(held beat — this line alone)*
>
> Jesus stopped the whole procession and said: "Call him." And the man threw
> off his cloak — the coat a beggar spread to catch coins, everything he
> owned — jumped up, and came. *(peak — music cuts to silence on "stopped
> the whole procession")*
>
> Jesus asked him one question: "What do you want me to do for you?" As if
> it weren't obvious. He wanted the man to say it. "Rabbi, I want to see."
> And he saw.

Closing card (hold 6s): *"The crowd told him to keep quiet, and he shouted
louder. What is the thing you would shout for, if no one could tell you to
be quiet?"*

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — Beside the road
```
A blind beggar in a ragged cloak sits in the dust at the edge of the Jericho
road, clouded eyes open to nothing, his spread cloak before him catching a
few small coins. Feet and hems pass him without pausing. His head is
tilted — he lives by listening.
```

### Shot 2 — He hears the name
```
Close on the blind man's face as a growing procession noise approaches — his
head lifts, turns, tracking the sound, and at some word in the crowd-noise
his whole face IGNITES. He pulls in a huge breath and shouts with everything
in his body, cords standing out in his neck.
```

### Shot 3 — Shouted down, shouting louder
```
Hostile faces in the crowd turn on the shouting beggar — hands wave him
down, a man hisses at him, someone shoves his shoulder. The blind man grips
the dust with one hand, throws his head back, and shouts LOUDER, ragged and
absolutely unashamed, into the sky he cannot see.
```

### Shot 4 — The procession stops (the money shot — extra takes)
```
Wide shot down the crowded road: an entire moving procession halts at once,
dust rolling forward past stopped feet. A corridor opens in the crowd
between a distant robed figure — seen only as a shape in the light — and the
blind man frozen mid-shout at the roadside. Every head turns toward the
beggar.
```

### Shot 5 — Throwing off the cloak
```
Slow motion: the blind man LEAPS up, and his beggar's cloak — coins
scattering out of it into the dust — flies off behind him like a shed skin.
He stumbles forward fast through the corridor of people, hands out in front
of him, leaving everything he owned on the ground without a glance of his
body toward it.
```

### Shot 6 — "I want to see."
```
The blind man stands breathing hard before warm out-of-focus light, his
clouded eyes wide, and speaks four visible urgent words. Extreme close up on
his eyes: the cloudiness clears like silt settling out of water — and the
first thing the new eyes focus on is the light in front of them. Tears cut
tracks through road dust on his face.
```

### Shot 7 — He followed
```
The road out of Jericho, wide, golden: the procession moves on — and one
figure with a new unpracticed stride walks in the middle of it, no cloak, no
begging bowl, head up, looking at EVERYTHING: hills, faces, his own hands,
the sky.
```

### Closing card
Text on cream, serif. No narration.
