# PRESCRIPTION — build-93-barabbas-goes-free

**Row:** 93   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Mark 15:6–15
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s
**Pictures:** 10–15 band → **planned 13**

> Mark 15 is a shouting match, and the earlier cut summarized the trial instead of
> letting it be heard. The SPEAKER-LAW rebuild lifts the three lines that decide
> everything — Pilate's offer, the crowd's one-word answer, and Pilate saying out
> loud there was no case — into verbatim scripture (light blue). The video's spine
> is the SWAP: a guilty man walks free because the innocent one takes his sentence.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the whole gospel in a single swap — the innocent takes the
  exact sentence the guilty man had earned, and the guilty man walks out free
  having done nothing to deserve it.
- **The turn:** the crowd's one-word answer, "Crucify him." The choice locks and
  the innocent is condemned in the guilty man's place. That is the hinge.
- **Character shown / wound answered:** grace that is unearned and total — Barabbas
  did nothing to be freed; someone else's death did it for him.
- **Whose face carries it:** BARABBAS — the guilty man watching himself go free —
  and PILATE at the trial hinge. Jesus stays bound and silent; his face only via
  master ref / over-the-shoulder, never carrying the emotional beat.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Barabbas walked free because the Son of God
  took his place. So can you." Lands directly on the swap.
