# Story Video #3 — Zacchaeus (Luke 19:1–10)

Runtime 60–75s. Prepend the STYLE BLOCK from 00-MASTER-PLAN.md to every shot
prompt. Jesus appears in shots 5–6: silhouette / from behind / voice only —
never his face.

## Narration script (app's exact words)

> Zacchaeus was a tax collector — which in his time meant he worked for the
> occupying empire and got rich doing it. He was publicly despised.
>
> When Jesus came through town, Zacchaeus wanted to see him. But he was
> short. He couldn't see over the crowd.
>
> So he ran ahead and climbed a tree. A grown man. A man of status. He
> climbed a tree just to catch a glimpse from a distance.
>
> Jesus looked up, saw him, and said: "Zacchaeus, come down. I am coming to
> your house today." *(peak — music cuts to silence on "Zacchaeus, come
> down.")*
>
> Not after Zacchaeus changed. Not after he apologized. Before. He changed
> because Jesus came first.

Closing card (hold 6s): *"Have you ever done something — maybe something a
little embarrassing — just to get a look at something you thought might be
real?"*

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — Despised
```
A small, richly dressed man walks alone through a market street in Jericho,
fine rings on his fingers, while people turn their backs, pull children
aside, and spit in the dust as he passes. His chin is up but his eyes are
lonely. Midday sun, hard shadows.
```

### Shot 2 — He can't see
```
A dense excited crowd lines a street, everyone craning toward something
coming. Behind the wall of backs, the small rich man jumps, strains on his
toes, tries to push in — shoulders close ranks against him. Shot from his
low, blocked point of view: only backs and dust.
```

### Shot 3 — The run
```
The small rich man RUNS down a side alley, robes flapping, rings and dignity
forgotten, dodging baskets and chickens, breathless. Tracking shot, handheld
energy, warm sunlight strobing between buildings.
```

### Shot 4 — Up the tree
```
A grown man in expensive robes climbs a broad sycamore-fig tree — awkward,
scrambling, sandals slipping on bark — and settles onto a limb above the
road, hidden in leaves, peering down with the wide eyes of a child. Green
dappled light on his face.
```

### Shot 5 — Seen (the money shot — generate extra takes)
```
Low angle up through sycamore leaves: sunlight flares through the branches
around a man's silhouette below, seen only from behind and shoulders-down,
who stops walking, turns, and looks directly UP into the tree — into the
camera. The crowd's noise dies. The leaves glow gold. His face is never
visible, only warm light.
```

### Shot 6 — Come down
```
The small man half-falls, half-climbs down the sycamore in joyful disbelief,
landing in the dust in front of the silhouetted figure, laughing and
straightening his robes, gesturing eagerly up the street toward his own
house. The crowd stares. Warm afternoon gold.
```

### Shot 7 — Changed because he came first
```
Interior, warm lamplight: the small man stands at his own table pushing
stacks of coins and ledgers ACROSS the table toward astonished poor families
seated as guests, giving it away with tears on his face and joy in it.
Firelight, no trace of the loneliness from shot 1.
```

### Closing card
Text on cream, serif. No narration.

## The Seed (what this video must leave behind)

"I am coming to your house today" — BEFORE Zacchaeus changed, apologized, or repaid anyone. Grace comes first and change follows it, not the other way around. Quiet question: *what if God's coming to you isn't waiting on you being fixed first?* Carried by shots 5–7.

## Red-letter lines (KJV) — the Jesus voice speaks ONLY these

1. **Where:** replaces the narrator's quote "Zacchaeus, come down. I am
   coming to your house today." — spoken over shot 5 (the look up into the
   tree).
   **KJV (Luke 19:5):** "Zacchaeus, make haste, and come down; for to day I
   must abide at thy house."
   **Narrator bridge (after, modern):** "'I must' — not 'I might.' Out of
   everyone in that crowd, staying with the man everyone hated wasn't
   Jesus's detour. It was his plan."
2. **Optional second line, over shot 7:**
   **KJV (Luke 19:10):** "For the Son of man is come to seek and to save
   that which was lost."
