## The impact read
- **The one thing:** she was known by the worst thing she'd ever done — he knew her by
  her love, and said so out loud in the house of the man who despised her.
- **The turn:** "Seest thou this woman?" — he turns the whole room's gaze from her sin
  to her love, and reads Simon's unspoken thought back to him with a story.
- **Character shown:** he reads the heart no one voices, defends the outsider to her face,
  and measures a person by love, not reputation — much forgiven, much love.
- **Whose face carries it:** the WOMAN throughout — weeping at his feet, then named for
  her love, then "thy sins are forgiven," then "go in peace." SIMON carries the contrast
  (certain he was the clean one). Jesus via master ref.
- **The card question falls out of:** "she was known for the worst thing she'd done; he
  knew her for her love — which name would you rather answer to?" No change needed.
