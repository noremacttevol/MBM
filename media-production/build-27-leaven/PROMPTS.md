## s1-takes-leaven — Shot 1: a woman took leaven
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Warm morning light in a humble first-century home kitchen of
mud-brick and wood. A kind middle-aged Middle-Eastern woman in an undyed cream
tunic and rust-brown apron, her dark hair covered by a simple head-cloth, stands
at a large wooden kneading trough and holds up between her floury fingers a
small, plain lump of pale sourdough starter — the leaven — looking at how little
it is. Clay jars of flour and a low domed clay oven behind her. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s2-three-measures — Shot 2: three measures of meal
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same kitchen. The woman stands beside an enormous mound
of pale flour heaped in a great wide wooden trough, tipping the last of a large
clay jar of meal onto the pile — far more flour than one family could use, a
huge batch, dwarfing the tiny lump of leaven resting on the edge of the trough.
Warm daylight, dust of flour in the air. One single continuous scene painted
edge to edge.

## s3-hides-it-in — Shot 3: and hid it in the meal
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Close over the wooden trough. The woman has begun to work
water and the small piece of leaven down deep into the great mass of pale dough
with both floury hands, kneading and folding it in, hiding the leaven so
completely that it has vanished into the whole. Her face is calm and intent, her
forearms dusted with flour. Warm kitchen light. One single continuous scene
painted edge to edge.

## s4-covered-waiting — Shot 4: then she covers it and waits
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The great bowl of smooth pale dough now sits covered with a
clean undyed cloth on a low bench in the quiet, dim kitchen at dusk, a single
small oil lamp glowing nearby. The woman is stepping away, glancing back once —
nothing is visibly happening, just still, hidden, waiting time. Soft shadows,
one warm point of lamplight. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s5-risen — Shot 5: till the whole was leavened
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Soft morning light returning to the kitchen. The cloth has
been lifted away and the dough has RISEN — the whole heavy mass has swelled up
high and alive, doubled and billowing over the rim of the wide bowl, transformed
all the way through by the hidden leaven. The woman looks at it with quiet
wonder and joy. Fresh warm light. One single continuous scene painted edge to
edge.

## s6-baked-bread — Shot 6: it quietly changes everything
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The low domed clay oven glowing warm. The woman lifts a
wooden peel bearing several round, golden, freshly baked loaves of bread from the
oven, more loaves cooling on the bench beside her — the whole great batch of
dough now become warm, fragrant bread. Her face is lit with quiet satisfaction.
Rich golden light and rising steam. One single continuous scene painted edge to
edge.

## s7-bread-to-table — Shot 7: made into something that can feed people
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The woman carries a wide basket heaped with the warm golden
loaves out of the kitchen toward a long low table where a gathering of ordinary
villagers — men, women, and children — wait to be fed, their faces warm and
glad, hands reaching to receive the bread. Warm gold evening light, a sense of
one small hidden thing having become food for many. One single continuous scene
painted edge to edge.

## s1b-the-leaven-itself — NEW (split off S1 / Matthew 13:33, was 31.6s): the least impressive thing
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A CLOSE macro view: a woman's flour-dusted HANDS, plain earth-toned wool sleeves at the wrists, hold a SMALL torn piece of old GREY sourdough starter — dull, grey and unremarkable, no bigger than a walnut — poised over a WIDE wooden kneading trough heaped with PALE flour. The little grey lump of leaven is TINY in the frame; the vast expanse of pale flour fills most of the picture around and below it. Warm ordinary morning kitchen light, soft and plain. Nothing dramatic, nothing glowing — the whole point is that this scrap of leaven is the LEAST impressive thing in the room, easy to overlook. Only the hands, the tiny grey starter, and the great field of flour. No cream or off-white clothing anywhere. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge. 9:16 vertical.
