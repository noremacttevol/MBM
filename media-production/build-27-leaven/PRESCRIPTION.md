# PRESCRIPTION — build-27-leaven

**Row:** 27   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Matthew 13:33
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 8)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God does not overpower you — he works small, hidden, and
  patient, from the inside, until the whole of you is changed and can feed others.
- **The turn:** the covered bowl overnight → the risen, living dough by morning. The
  hinge is "nothing looks like it's happening" becoming "changed all the way through."
- **Character shown:** his gentleness and patience — quiet, hidden work, no spectacle.
- **Whose face carries it:** the WOMAN in her kitchen — her hands working the leaven in,
  her waiting. Jesus only in the brief teller frame, via master ref / over-the-shoulder.
- **The card question falls out of:** "God is often working quietest right where you
  cannot see it yet." No change needed.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 8 stills
1. [s33] Jesus teaching the crowd, opening another parable (teller frame, master ref) — agrees: "Another parable spake he unto them"
2. [n1/j1] a woman in her own kitchen taking a small lump of leaven in her hand — "something a woman does... with her own hands" / "leaven, which a woman took"
3. [n2] close on the small, plain lump of old dough / sourdough starter — "a little piece of old, living dough... small, plain, easy to overlook"

7. [n6/n7] morning light — the whole mass risen, alive, changed all the way through — "the whole heavy mass has risen, alive, changed all the way through"
8. [n8/card] the finished bread shared with people at a table — warmed, changed, feeding others; warm hold for the card — "made into something that can feed other people"
