# PRESCRIPTION — build-103-peters-confession

**Row:** 103   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Matthew 16:13–18
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 10)

> Bridge shelf. The wound is **"heaven is silent now / God doesn't reveal himself to
> ordinary people anymore."** The answer is the hinge line: Peter's answer did not come
> from clever thinking — "flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father."
> God still reveals himself, quietly, straight to a heart ready to receive it.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** knowing who Jesus really is is not something you argue your way into
  — it is given, quietly, from God, to a heart ready to receive it, and everything else
  is built on that one answer.
- **The turn:** "But whom say ye that I am?" The safe, second-hand answers stop; the
  question no one can borrow lands, and Peter says the thing out loud. That exchange is
  the hinge.
- **Wound answered:** "heaven is silent / God no longer speaks to a person." The answer —
  the Father revealed this to Peter directly; revelation still happens, to ordinary people.
- **Whose face carries it:** PETER and the disciples — the ease of the crowd-opinions, the
  caught silence at the real question, Peter stepping forward, the others' faces at what he
  dared. Jesus's face only via master ref / over-the-shoulder.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Who do YOU say that he is?" — the exact question in
  the story. No change needed.
