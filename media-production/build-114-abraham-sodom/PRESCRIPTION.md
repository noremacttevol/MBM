**Tier:** T3 Full Arc   **Target length:** 120–165s   **Pictures:** 16–24 (planned: 16)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the God you were taught is straining to condemn is actually
  one you can talk *toward* mercy, again and again — and he keeps saying yes.
- **The turn:** the bargaining montage — fifty, forty-five, forty, thirty… ten —
  where Abraham stops fearing God's anger and starts discovering how much God would
  rather spare than destroy. The hinge is the realization, not any single number.
- **Wound answered (angry God):** the inherited picture of a God itching to burn a
  city. This scene shows a God who lets an old man argue with him and answers mercy
  every single time. "Not one straining to condemn — one who could be talked toward mercy."
- **Whose face carries it:** ABRAHAM — his courage growing, his daring, his amazement
  walking home. God is the warm presence / light on the hill, never a face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "he welcomes the bold and the interceding —
  what would you dare ask him for?" Lands straight on the one thing. No change needed.
