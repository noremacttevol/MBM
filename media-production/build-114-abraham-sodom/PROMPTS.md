# STILL PROMPTS — Video #114: Abraham Pleads for Sodom (genesis-18_abraham-pleads-for-sodom)

Genesis 18. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — ten painted stills. BRIDGE/MILK.
CARE FLAGS (CONTENT-CARE #165, care J): **Sodom's destruction is OFF-SCREEN — NEVER show
people dying, fire falling, or the city burning.** The story is the NEGOTIATION and God's
YESES — Abraham boldly interceding and God mercifully agreeing again and again. God is a
warm PRESENCE with a voice, never a figure. Closing card is an INVITATION, never a fear-question.

NO DIVINE FIGURE ANYWHERE. Where God speaks or the LORD's presence is, the picture is warm
radiant light or a numinous glow — no figure, no face, no shape, no hand. (No Jesus is shown
in any frame; nobody wears cream or off-white — Abraham and the visitors are in earth colours.)
The three heavenly VISITORS who come to Abraham ARE shown as three dignified travelers /
messengers in plain robes, faces serene — they are guests, not the depicted LORD.

ABRAHAM LOCK (s1-s10 as present): a dignified elder of about ninety-nine, warm olive-brown
weathered skin, a long full white beard and white hair, in an ankle-length deep russet-brown
robe with a wide cloth girdle and a coarse tan mantle — humble, earnest, respectful. His
face IS shown, consistent. VISITORS LOCK (s1-s4): three calm travelers in plain dust-tan and
soft ochre robes, faces serene and kind; ordinary-looking wayfarers, no wings, no glow.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single tall upright vertical scene painted edge to edge, no panels."
Skip any comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.

MUST NEVER SHOW: God/the LORD as a figure, face or hand; Sodom burning, fire falling, or
anyone dying or harmed; anyone in cream or off-white; any modern object; any baked-in text;
a multi-panel or comic-strip layout.
