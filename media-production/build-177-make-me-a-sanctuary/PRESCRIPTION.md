# PRESCRIPTION — build-177-make-me-a-sanctuary

**Row:** 177   **Shelf:** Member   **Scripture:** Exodus 25:8, 25:22
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 10)

> The whole chapter is God asking for a tent in the middle of the camp so he can
> live where his people live. The narration walks from the strange instruction → the
> tent pitched among the tents → the furnishings → the mercy seat where he promises to
> meet them → "I am near" → the camp on the move → God pitching his tent beside them.
> Each is a distinct thing the words make you see; every one gets its frame.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God has always wanted to *live among* his people — nearness, not
  distance. He asked for a tent in the middle of the camp, not a fortress on a hill.
- **The turn:** Exodus 25:22 — "there I will meet with thee… from above the mercy
  seat." The chapter stops being blueprints and becomes an address: *here* is where I
  will meet you. That is the hinge.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** God dwelling with his people — the temple pattern begins here.
  Hands off to **Gospel Library → Temples**.
- **Whose face carries it:** the PEOPLE of the camp and Moses receiving the word.
  God's presence is shown as the tent / the light over the mercy seat — never a face
  (s1 and g22 are GOD-green, Jehovah's own voice).
- **The card question falls out of:** "He wanted to live among his people then — he
  wants to live among you now." Lands on the one thing. No change.
