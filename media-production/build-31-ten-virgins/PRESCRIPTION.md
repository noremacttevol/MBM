**Row:** 31   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Matthew 25:1–13
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 14)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the oil is the one thing you cannot borrow at the last minute —
  a readiness that has to be your own — and the good news is the door is still open now.
- **The turn:** the midnight cry → the two sets of lamps. The hinge is the wise lamps
  burning bright while the foolish lamps sputter out.
- **Character shown:** he is worth being ready for; and the closing beat turns it to
  hope — "the door is still open now," not fear.
- **Whose face carries it:** the TEN YOUNG WOMEN — the wise trimming bright lamps, the
  foolish staring at dying ones. No Jesus face except the brief teller frame at n14.
- **The card question falls out of:** "You cannot borrow someone else's oil." (Card
  wording flag below.)
