# STILL PROMPTS — Video #101: The Still Small Voice (1kings-19_the-still-small-voice)

1 Kings 19:1-18 (focus 9-18). STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — ten painted stills, no motion clips.
BRIDGE/MILK. CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN. No violence shown: the wind, earthquake and
fire are FORCES on an empty mountain, no person is harmed, no bodies, no gore.

NO DIVINE FIGURE ANYWHERE. God is a voice and a presence in this story and is NEVER
depicted: no figure in the wind, the quake, the fire, the light or the stillness; no
face, no shape, no hand from heaven. Where God acts, the picture is the mountain, the
elements and Elijah — the wind/quake/fire are empty of any figure, and the "still small
voice" is painted only as hushed, luminous calm. (No Jesus appears in this video — he
is never shown in any frame; nobody wears cream or off-white in any frame — every
garment is a clearly darker earth color.) Because no shot depicts Jesus or the Lord as
a figure, no JESUS LOCK / REF line is required; the face-gate stays green.

ELIJAH LOCK (every shot he appears in — s1,s2,s3,s4,s5,s6,s8,s9,s10): a weathered
Middle-Eastern prophet of about sixty, warm olive-brown sun-darkened skin, a long
grey-streaked dark beard and long grey-streaked dark hair, deep tired lines around the
eyes. He wears a coarse rough dark-brown haircloth mantle of camel hair over a plain
dun-grey wool tunic to the shins, a wide worn leather girdle at the waist, leather
sandals — rugged, poor, travel-worn, NEVER off-white or cream. His face IS shown,
gaunt and worn, consistent in every shot. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two
legs and one head.

MUST NEVER SHOW: any depiction of God or a divine figure; any figure or shape inside
the wind, quake, fire, light or stillness; any modern object; any text baked into the
image; anyone in cream or off-white; a multi-panel or comic-strip layout.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single tall upright vertical scene painted edge to edge, filling the frame from base to crown." Skip any
comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.
