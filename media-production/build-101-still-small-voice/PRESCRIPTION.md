# PRESCRIPTION — build-101-still-small-voice

**Row:** 101   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** 1 Kings 19:9–18
**Tier:** T3 Full Arc   **Target length:** 120–165s   **Pictures:** 16–24 (planned: 17)

> Bridge shelf. The wound this one answers is **"heaven is silent now."** The whole
> point of the picture plan is the contrast the narration builds and then breaks: the
> wind, the earthquake, the fire — all the noise a burned-out man assumes God must be
> in — and then the quiet. Let the three loud beats be genuinely loud, so the whisper
> lands.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** when you are worn out and sure you are the only one left, God does
  not come in thunder to shame you — he comes in a whisper, and tells you the truth that
  you were never as alone as you feared.
- **The turn:** after the fire, the still small voice. Everything goes quiet and the low
  gentle whisper is where God actually was. That silence is the hinge and the most
  important frame in the video — let it hold.
- **Wound answered:** "heaven is silent now / God has stopped speaking." The answer is
  that God still speaks — just not in the spectacle Elijah was braced for.
- **Whose face carries it:** ELIJAH throughout — the collapse under the bush, the pouring
  out of grief, the face wrapped in the cloak, the face easing when he hears he is not
  alone. God is a voice here, never a face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "could the voice you most need be the gentle one?" —
  lands directly on the one thing. No change needed.
