# PRESCRIPTION — build-120-job-from-whirlwind

**Row:** 120   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Job 1; 19; 38–42
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 16)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** Job's comfort was never a tidy explanation for his pain — it was
  a Person who showed up in it. God draws near to the broken.
- **The turn:** "I have heard of thee by the hearing of the ear: but now mine eye
  seeth thee." The ending isn't Job getting his answer — it's Job getting God himself.
- **Wound answered (silent heaven):** the fear that God abandons the suffering and
  owes them an explanation he never gives. God never explains why — instead he comes
  near, and "that nearness was the answer his grief actually needed."
- **Whose face carries it:** JOB — the torn coat, the ashes, the hand over the mouth,
  the seeing. God is the whirlwind and the wild creation vistas, no face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "if you are in the ashes right now... where do
  you most need him to show up?" Lands on the one thing. No change needed.

## Deviations from the band
Planned 16 stills, just above the T2 band. The whirlwind speech alone is several
distinct creation vistas (foundations, singing stars, Pleiades and Orion, the wild
world), each a new thing the words paint — so the honest beat read runs a touch long
(Part 3: trust the beats over the range).
