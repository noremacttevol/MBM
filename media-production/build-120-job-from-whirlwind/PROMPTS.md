# STILL PROMPTS — Video #120: Job Answered from the Whirlwind (job-38_job-from-whirlwind)

Job 38–42. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — ten painted stills. MILK / grief. CARE FLAGS: G, J —
the answer to Job's suffering was PRESENCE, not an explanation. The narration NEVER makes God
the tormentor-for-glory: it does not dwell on the heavenly wager, it does not frame God as
authoring pain to win a bet. Job's losses are shown as GRIEF and AFTERMATH — a broken man in
ashes, never gore, never a rendered dead child, never grotesque sores. God answers not with
reasons but by SHOWING UP — out of the whirlwind — and Job finds peace in the encounter
itself. The closing never promises the same restoration; the hope is what it reveals about God:
he came near.

NO DIVINE FIGURE ANYWHERE. God is shown ONLY as the whirlwind and warm radiant light — a great
storm of wind, cloud, and light — no figure, no face, no shape, no hand-of-God-as-a-person.
JOB is a HUMAN and DOES show his face — keep him identical: a dignified older man, warm
Middle-Eastern skin, a full grey beard and grey hair; in prosperity a deep burgundy-red robe,
in his suffering a torn dull ash-grey robe. His three friends and family show their faces too.
Nobody wears cream or off-white (there is no Jesus figure — Jesus is never shown, so cream
never appears).

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single tall upright vertical scene painted edge to edge, no panels."
Skip any comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.

MUST NEVER SHOW: God as a figure, face or hand-of-God-as-a-person; any dead body, dead child,
gore, or grotesque sores/wounds; anyone in cream or off-white; any baked-in text or written
letters; any modern object; a multi-panel or comic-strip layout.
