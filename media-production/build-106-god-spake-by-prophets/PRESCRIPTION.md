# PRESCRIPTION — build-106-god-spake-by-prophets

**Row:** 106   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Hebrews 1:1–5
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 10 — see deviation)

> Bridge shelf. The wound is **"heaven is silent now."** The answer is the whole shape of
> Hebrews 1: for ages God spoke in fragments through prophets, and then he said it all at
> once in a Son — a warm human face you can look full into and finally know what God is
> really like.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God's clearest and last word about himself is not a message but a
  person — a real human face; if you have ever wondered whether God is angry or distant or
  cold, the answer is not a guess, it is Jesus.
- **The turn:** "He stopped sending messages about himself — and came in person." The
  shift from scattered fragments to the Son is the hinge.
- **Wound answered:** "heaven is silent / God only ever spoke in bits, long ago." The
  answer — that was his *old* pattern, and it ended in a face you can look at.
- **Whose face carries it:** this is the rare one where the payoff lands ON Jesus's face
  (shown via master ref) — the "express image" of God is the whole point, so the final
  beats deliberately let you look at that face. The prophets are figures, not portraits.
- **The card question falls out of:** "If you want to know what God is really like, where
  might you start looking?" — lands on the one thing. No change needed.

## Deviations from the band
Stepped to **10 pictures** (band is 6–9) because the verse itself enumerates the "sundry
times and divers manners" God spoke — the bush, the fire, the scroll, the city gate — so
it honestly paints more distinct images than a single-image T1 saying. Each mode is its
own frame; collapsing them would flatten the whole point of "in many times and many ways."
