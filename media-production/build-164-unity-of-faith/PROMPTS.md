# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #164: "Till we all come in the unity of the faith" (ephesians-4_unity-of-the-faith)

Ephesians 4:11-14. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no
motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Apostles** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk. The note is holy ORDER and GROWTH: the risen Lord gave
his church living leaders — apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, teachers — to build his
people up until they all reach one faith and full maturity. Warm, hopeful, belonging. Study
gem: those offices were never meant to be temporary — they are given "till we all come" to
that unity, and the church has not yet arrived.

CHRIST IS NEVER DEPICTED as a figure — no face, no body, no shape of a person. Where the text
speaks of the risen Lord giving gifts, or of the Son of God and the fulness that the church
grows toward, he is shown ONLY as warm radiant light from heaven. God is likewise only warm
light. (This keeps the shot blocks free of any divine proper noun, so the face-gate passes.)

THE POINT (Why-Law): when the risen Lord ascended he did not leave his church leaderless — he
gave it living leaders as gifts (s1); apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors and teachers
(s2 — Eph 4:11); their work is to equip and mature the ordinary saints (s3 — Eph 4:12); so the
whole body is knit together and built up (s4); until the whole church comes together as one,
in one faith (s5); grown to full maturity, the measure of the fulness of heaven (s6 — Eph
4:13, the named verse); the alternative is to stay children, tossed about by every new wind of
teaching and easy prey for clever deceivers (s7 — Eph 4:14); so we are meant to grow up
together into that unity — come and take your place (s8).

PEOPLE LOCK (s1, s3-s8): ordinary first-century people of mixed age and nation — men, women
and children in undyed DARKER earth-tone wool (dun, faded brown, olive, ochre, russet, grey),
faces shown; never in cream or off-white.
LEADERS LOCK (s2, s3): a group of grave, kind first-century servant-leaders — men in dark
earth-tone robes and mantles (brown, olive, grey, russet), some bearing a shepherd's staff, some
an open scroll — clearly teachers and shepherds of the people; never in cream, no insignia.
THE HEAVENLY LIGHT (s1, s5, s6, s8): warm radiant golden light from heaven — only light,
never a face, never a figure, never a body or hand within it.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or body of God or the risen Lord anywhere — only warm
light; no modern object; no cross, steeple, church-building-with-a-spire, or insignia of any
group; no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no baked-in text, letters or numbers; no
human dressed in cream or off-white. Every image is ONE single unbroken full-frame scene — no
panels, no comic strip, no dividing lines.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.
