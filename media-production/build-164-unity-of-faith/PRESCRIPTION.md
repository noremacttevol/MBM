## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the risen Lord did not leave his church to run itself — he gave it living leaders, apostles and prophets, and he gave them *until we all arrive*, which means the church still needs them now.
- **The turn:** the "quiet study gem" (n6) — *given until we ALL come to unity, and we plainly have not arrived, so the gifts were never temporary.* That inference is the hinge the whole video exists to land.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the Restoration of living apostles and prophets — the offices were permanent, not first-century scaffolding. Hands off to **Gospel Library → Apostles.**
- **Whose face carries it:** the ordinary believers being built up, and the "children tossed to and fro" without leaders. Not the Lord's face — he ascends, seen from below / behind.
- **The card question falls out of:** "When he offers you a place in it, will you come and grow?" — lands straight on the one thing (grow up together, not alone).

1. [n1] the risen Lord ascending, light pouring gifts down onto his people below — agrees-with: "poured out gifts on his people"

8. [card] warm hold — a place offered in the settled, growing body — the closing question — "When he offers you a place in it, will you come and grow?"
