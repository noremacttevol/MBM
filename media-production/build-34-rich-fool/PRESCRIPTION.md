## The impact read
- **The one thing:** a life spent storing up only for yourself leaves the soul empty;
  the wealth that lasts is being rich toward God.
- **The turn:** "But there was one thing he had never planned for. And that very night,
  God spoke to him." The hinge is night falling on all his plans.
- **Character shown / wound answered:** answers the "get enough and you're set"
  assumption — every plan was about himself; he forgot the one certain thing.
- **Whose face carries it:** the RICH MAN — his self-satisfied planning, then his
  emptiness. God is a voice in the night, not a depicted figure (keep it darkness /
  light, no face).
- **The card question falls out of:** "What are you storing up for yourself — and what
  are you storing up with God?" No change needed.
