# FLOW PROMPTS — Story Video #34: The Rich Fool (luke-12_rich-fool)

Luke 12:16-21. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) + the Face Law: this is a parable Jesus tells,
and NO Jesus figure appears on screen at all — his face is never shown because he
is simply not depicted here; only the parable's own characters are painted. Nano
Banana 2, tall vertical 9:16, x2, download 1K. Hand-painted 2D storybook style
(NOT photorealistic). Passes `jesus_face_gate.py`.

**CHARACTER / PROP LOCKS (keep consistent across every still they appear in):**
- **The rich man (s1–s6)** — a prosperous, well-fed middle-aged Judean landowner,
  a trimmed greying dark beard, dressed in fine dyed robes: a deep-green tunic under
  a gold-bordered russet mantle, a gold ring on his hand. His face IS shown and
  stays the same man — proud and self-satisfied through s1–s4, then startled and
  hollow at night in s5. He is a parable character.
- **The harvest / goods** — golden wheat sheaves, woven baskets and big clay jars
  brimming with grain, jars of oil and wine.
- **The barns** — sturdy stone-and-mud-brick granaries.
- **The humble farmer (s7 ONLY)** — a different, ordinary poor farmer in a plain
  undyed tan wool tunic, open-handed and generous; a grateful poor mother and small
  child receiving from him.
- **The setting** — a prosperous first-century Judean farm and estate, golden
  fields, warm daylight; night for the solemn summons.

**STILL STYLE BLOCK** (prepend to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, warm golden light, earth-toned palette of first-century Judea. Sacred,
gentle tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions in the image. Historically
modest rough-woven wool and linen robes in undyed earth colors. No modern objects.

**ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE** (append to every prompt, exactly):
ONE SINGLE CONTINUOUS SCENE painted edge to edge as one unbroken composition
filling the entire tall vertical 9:16 frame — NOT divided into panels, NOT a comic
strip, just one single picture.

## s1 — Shot 1: the great harvest
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Golden late-afternoon light over a vast field of ripe wheat
stretching to soft Judean hills. The rich man — a prosperous, well-fed middle-aged
landowner with a trimmed greying dark beard, a deep-green tunic under a gold-bordered
russet mantle — stands at the edge of the field surveying an enormous, abundant
harvest with a satisfied smile, one hand shading his eyes. The heavy golden crop is
everywhere. A very good year. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s2 — Shot 2: no room to store it all
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The estate's granary yard, piled high with the harvest: woven
baskets and big clay jars brimming and overflowing with grain, sheaves stacked, jars
of oil and wine, far more than the sturdy stone barns can hold. The same rich man in
his green tunic and russet mantle stands among the overflowing plenty, one hand on
his chin, frowning thoughtfully — he has run out of room. Warm afternoon light on
mountains of grain. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s3 — Shot 3: pull down the barns, build greater
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same estate. The rich man in his green tunic and gold-bordered
russet mantle stands directing the work with a confident sweep of his arm, as laborers
tear down an old stone barn and begin raising much larger new granaries with fresh
timber and stone. Ambition and pride on his face; scaffolding, tools, and rising walls
around him. Bright, busy, prosperous daylight. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s4 — Shot 4: eat, drink, and be merry
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A warm lamplit hall in the rich man's fine house. The same rich
man reclines alone at a lavish, heavily laden table — bread, fruit, roasted food,
jars and a full cup of wine lifted in his hand — leaning back at ease with a
self-satisfied smile, surrounded by his wealth. Rich fabrics and abundance all
around him, and no one else at the table. Comfort and self-indulgence. [ANTI-PANEL
CLAUSE]

## s5 — Shot 5: this night thy soul required
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Deep night in the rich man's opulent house, lit by a single
failing oil-lamp. The same rich man sits alone, suddenly startled and solemn, one
hand pressed to his chest, his eyes lifted as if hearing a voice in the dark — all
his gold, jars and fine things around him now cold and hollow. A long shadow, a hush,
a sobering stillness. No graphic imagery, only a quiet dread. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s6 — Shot 6: then whose shall these things be?
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Pale morning light. The great new barns and the heaped baskets
and clay jars of grain and goods stand full and untouched, but the rich man is gone;
a few other people — servants and heirs in plain robes — stand among the piled
wealth looking at it uncertainly, one hand reaching toward a jar. The goods remain;
their owner does not. A quiet, hollow abundance. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s7 — Shot 7: rich toward God
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Warm golden dawn at the edge of a humble field. A different,
ordinary poor farmer in a plain undyed tan wool tunic kneels with open, giving hands,
sharing a basket of grain and a loaf of bread with a grateful poor mother and her
small child. Their faces are gentle and full of quiet joy; soft radiant morning light
falls over the simple, generous moment. A picture of a life that is truly rich.
[ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]
