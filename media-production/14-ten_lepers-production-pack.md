# Story Video #14 — The Ten Lepers (Luke 17:11–19)

Runtime 60–75s. Prepend the STYLE BLOCK from 00-MASTER-PLAN.md. Jesus: a
distant figure across the mandated gap, then a presence at whose feet the
one man falls — silhouette and light, never his face. Depict leprosy with
restraint: wrapped hands, covered faces, distance — never gore.

## Narration script (app's exact words)

> Ten men with leprosy stood at a distance — the law required it — and
> called out to Jesus for mercy.
>
> He did not touch them or announce a healing. He simply said: "Go, show
> yourselves to the priests." Which only made sense if they would be clean
> when they arrived.
>
> They went. And as they went — on the road, mid-obedience, before anything
> visible had changed — they were healed. *(peak — music cuts to silence on
> "as they went")*
>
> One of them, when he saw it, turned around. He came back praising God at
> the top of his voice and threw himself at Jesus's feet in thanks. He was a
> Samaritan — the outsider of the group.
>
> Jesus asked: "Were not all ten cleansed? Where are the other nine?" Then
> to the one: "Rise and go. Your faith has made you well."

Closing card (hold 6s): *"The healing happened while they walked, before
they could see it. Have you ever had to move forward on nothing but a word —
before there was any proof?"*

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — At a distance
```
Ten figures in ragged robes stand strung out along a rocky ridge at the
legal distance from the road, faces half-wrapped in cloth, hands bound in
strips of linen. Between them and a small group of travelers below lies a
wide gap of empty stony ground — the visual subject of the shot is the GAP.
```

### Shot 2 — Calling across the gap
```
The ten wrapped men cry out across the distance in unison, hands cupped at
their mouths, voices visibly desperate — asking from too far away to be
touched. Below, the traveling group stops. One robed figure at its front,
seen only as a shape against the light, turns toward the ridge.
```

### Shot 3 — Sent with nothing but a word
```
Close on the lepers' wrapped faces reacting: confusion — no healing, no
touch, only an instruction pointed toward the distant city. They look at
each other, at their still-bandaged hands, at the long road. Then, one by
one, they start walking anyway.
```

### Shot 4 — Healed mid-stride (the money shot — extra takes)
```
Tracking with ragged men walking a bright dusty road: one pulls at the
linen wrapping his hand as he walks — and the skin beneath is NEW, smooth,
whole. He stops dead. Around him the others tear at their wrappings,
strips of linen falling and blowing down the road like shed cocoons,
laughter starting somewhere and spreading. Sunlight everywhere.
```

### Shot 5 — Nine go on, one turns
```
Wide high shot of a fork in the bright road: nine unwrapped, jubilant men
run onward toward the distant city gates — and ONE man stands still at the
fork, looking back the way they came. The nine shrink one way; then he
RUNS the other, alone, robes flying.
```

### Shot 6 — At his feet
```
The healed man throws himself down on the road at the feet of a robed
figure — camera stays low with him, at ground level: his unwrapped new
hands flat in the dust, his face pressed toward the earth, shouting thanks
through tears. Above him, only the hem of a cloak and warm light. A hand
enters frame and rests on his head.
```

### Shot 7 — Rise and go
```
The healed Samaritan rises to his feet, face wet and shining, and walks
backward a few steps — unwilling to turn away yet — before finally turning
toward the city, taller than he has stood in years. The road ahead of him
is gold.
```

### Closing card
Text on cream, serif. No narration.

## The Seed (what this video must leave behind)

The healing happened mid-obedience, on the road, before any proof. God's pattern: the word comes first, you move, and the confirmation meets you walking. Quiet question: *have you been waiting for proof before you move — when the healing is on the road?* Carried by shot 4.

   religion of the day said didn't belong."
3. **Where:** shot 7, the last red-letter words.
   **KJV (Luke 17:19):** "Arise, go thy way: thy faith hath made thee
