## The impact read
- **The one thing:** a condemned man with nothing left to offer — no good deeds, no
  time to fix his life — asked to be remembered, and got paradise the same day.
- **The turn:** "Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom." The dying
  man turns his head toward Jesus and asks for the smallest thing. That plea is the
  hinge.
- **Character shown:** mercy that needs nothing brought to it — the last-minute
  faith of a criminal was enough; it is never too late to ask.
- **Whose face carries it:** the PENITENT THIEF throughout — the rebuke, the plea,
  the answer landing on him. Jesus's face only via over-the-shoulder at the promise.
- **The card question falls out of:** "He saved a man who had nothing to give but a
  request. It's never too late to ask." Lands directly on the one thing.
