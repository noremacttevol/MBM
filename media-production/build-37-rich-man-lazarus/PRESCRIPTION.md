# PRESCRIPTION — build-37-rich-man-lazarus

**Row:** 37   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Luke 16:19–31
**Tier:** T3 Full Arc   **Target length:** 120–165s   **Pictures:** 16–24 (planned: 17)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the gate you walk past matters — there is still someone waiting at
  it, and still time to stop, see them, and turn, while the day is yours.
- **The turn:** "there is still a gate in front of you today" — the story pivots from
  the two dead men to the living viewer, from warning to mercy.
- **Character shown / wound answered:** the warning is real, but underneath it is a
  mercy — Jesus gives it while there is still time to change.
- **Whose face carries it:** the RICH MAN (his daily indifference, then his anguish)
  and LAZARUS at the gate. Abraham speaks; keep him a distant figure. No Jesus face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Who are you walking past — and will you stop
  while there is still time?" No change needed.
