# FLOW PROMPTS — Story Video #37: The Rich Man and Lazarus (luke-16_rich-man-lazarus)

Luke 16:19-31. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) + the Face Law: this is a parable Jesus tells,
and NO Jesus figure appears on screen at all — his face is never shown because he
is simply not depicted here; only the parable's own characters are painted (the
rich man, Lazarus, Abraham, angels). Nano Banana 2, tall vertical 9:16, x2,
download 1K. Hand-painted 2D storybook style (NOT photorealistic). Passes
`jesus_face_gate.py`.

**CHARACTER / PROP LOCKS (keep consistent across every still they appear in):**
- **The rich man (s1–s7)** — a prosperous, well-fed middle-aged Judean man with a
  neatly trimmed dark beard, dressed in a deep royal-purple mantle over a fine
  bright-white linen tunic, a heavy gold ring and gold trim. Proud and comfortable
  in s1–s3. In s5–s6 he is the SAME man but now gaunt, ashen, and hollow-eyed in a
  shadowed place. His face IS shown — he is an ordinary parable character.
- **Lazarus (s2, s4, s6)** — a thin, gentle poor beggar in rough undyed torn wool
  rags, sores on his skin, a kind weary face. In s4 and s6 he is made restful,
  clean and whole, at peace in warm light. His face IS shown.
- **Abraham (s6)** — a serene, kindly white-bearded ancient patriarch in warm,
  flowing cream-and-amber robes, calm and full of gentle authority. His face IS
  shown.
- **The angels (s4)** — gentle, luminous winged figures in soft white-gold light.
- **The gate** — a fine ornate stone gateway into the rich man's estate; the
  boundary the rich man crosses daily and the beggar never does.
- **The setting** — first-century Judea: a lavish feasting hall and estate gate in
  warm lamplight for life on earth; then the world beyond — Abraham's side bathed
  in warm radiant light, and the rich man's side a desolate, dim, dry and shadowed
  expanse separated by a great dark divide. TASTEFUL throughout: NO hellfire, NO
  flames, NO horror, NO graphic suffering — only a quiet, dry, sobering desolation.

**STILL STYLE BLOCK** (prepend to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, warm golden light, earth-toned palette of first-century Judea. Sacred,
gentle tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions in the image. Historically
modest rough-woven wool and linen robes in undyed earth colors (except the rich
man's dyed purple). No modern objects.

**ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE** (append to every prompt, exactly):
ONE SINGLE CONTINUOUS SCENE painted edge to edge as one unbroken composition
filling the entire tall vertical 9:16 frame — NOT divided into panels, NOT a comic
strip, just one single picture.

## s1 — Shot 1: the rich man feasts every day
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A warm, lamplit feasting hall in a wealthy first-century Judean
house. The rich man — a well-fed middle-aged man with a neatly trimmed dark beard,
wrapped in a deep royal-purple mantle over a fine bright-white linen tunic, a gold
ring on his hand — reclines at a long table heaped with bread, fruit, roasted food
and full cups of wine, feasting at ease with a satisfied smile. Rich fabrics, gold,
and abundance all around him; an ornate stone gate glimpsed beyond. Every day a
feast. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s2 — Shot 2: Lazarus at the gate
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Just outside the rich man's ornate stone estate gate, in the
dusty street, a thin poor beggar named Lazarus lies on the ground in rough torn
undyed wool rags, his skin marked with sores, his weary gentle face turned up
longing toward the warm glow of the feast beyond the gate. A couple of stray street
dogs stand near him. He aches for the scraps from the rich man's table. Warm light
inside the gate, cold shadow where he lies. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s3 — Shot 3: the rich man walks past
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The rich man in his royal-purple mantle and white linen strides
out through his ornate stone gate, chin lifted, turning his body away and stepping
around the poor beggar Lazarus who lies in his rags on the ground at the gate. The
rich man does not look down; the suffering man reaches a thin hand toward him
unseen. He passes by, unmoved. Warm evening light on the wealthy man, grey shadow on
the beggar. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s4 — Shot 4: the angels carry Lazarus home
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The poor man Lazarus, now made restful, clean and whole, is
gently lifted upward by two luminous winged angels in soft white-gold radiance,
carried tenderly out of his suffering into a warm and glorious light above. His face
is peaceful at last, his rags become soft and clean. A rising column of gentle
golden light. Tender, glorious, comforting. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s5 — Shot 5: the rich man in torment
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same rich man — now gaunt, ashen and hollow-eyed, his purple
robe dulled and dusty — stands alone in a desolate, dim, dry and shadowed expanse of
cracked barren ground, parched and thirsty, one hand lifted and reaching toward a
distant warm light far across a great dark divide. His posture is longing and
sorrowful. A sobering, quiet desolation — NO fire, NO flames, NO horror, only a dry
grey shadowed emptiness and a vast dark gulf. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s6 — Shot 6: the great gulf fixed — Abraham and Lazarus in the light
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A wide view across a great dark chasm that splits the scene. On
the far side, bathed in warm radiant golden light, a serene white-bearded ancient
patriarch, Abraham, in flowing cream-and-amber robes stands with the restful, whole
Lazarus resting peacefully at his side. On the near side, small and dim in grey
shadow, the gaunt rich man reaches across the impassable divide toward the distant
light. A great fixed gulf between comfort and longing. Tasteful and reverent, no
fire. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s7 — Shot 7: the five brothers
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Back in the world of the living: five well-dressed prosperous
young men, brothers, feast and lounge heedlessly around a laden table in a fine
lamplit Judean house, laughing and at ease, entirely unaware — while just outside
their own open gateway, dim in the shadow, a poor ragged beggar sits unnoticed,
reaching out a hand none of them see. Warm careless abundance inside, quiet need at
the gate. [ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]

## s8 — Shot 8: the gate before you today (invitation)
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Warm, hopeful dawn light at a humble open gateway. An ordinary
kind person in a plain undyed wool tunic kneels down and stops, offering a loaf of
bread and an open, gentle hand to a poor ragged beggar seated at the gate; their
eyes meet with warmth and dignity. Soft radiant golden morning light falls over the
simple, merciful moment. A picture of someone who stopped, and saw, and turned.
[ANTI-PANEL CLAUSE]
