# FLOW PROMPTS — Story Video #33: The Sheep and the Goats (matthew-25_sheep-and-goats)

Matthew 25:31-46. STILLS-ONLY (Law E). The KING (the Son of man / the Lord) in
this scene is kept FULLY OFF-SCREEN and never shown in any still — only his
narrating voice + KJV lines. Because no Lord figure is ever shown, the gate is
trivially safe (`jesus_face_gate.py` exits 0). The art shows a SHEPHERD dividing
his flock (as a simile, seen only from behind / at a distance, face never shown)
and — above all — the ACTS OF MERCY and the ordinary people who do them, faces
shown and warm. Nano Banana 2, 9:16, x2, 2K. Hand-painted 2D storybook style.

**CHARACTER / IMAGERY NOTES:**
- **The shepherd (s1 only)** — a lone shepherd figure on a hillside dividing his
  flock, shown ONLY from behind or at a distance so his face is never seen (he is
  the simile for the King; keep him reverent and faceless, a dark warm figure).
- **The flock (s1)** — white woolly sheep gathering to the shepherd's right, and
  darker shaggy goats moving to his left.
- **The helpers and the needy (s2–s7)** — ordinary first-century people: kind
  helpers (men, women, children) and the ones they help (the hungry, a weary
  stranger, a cold poor person, a sick person, a prisoner, a small beggar). All
  their faces ARE shown, warm and human.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or
captions in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen
in undyed earth colors. No modern objects.

## s1-separate — Shot 1: as a shepherd divideth the sheep from the goats
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A wide hillside at golden evening light. A lone shepherd,
seen only from behind as a warm dark figure with his back to us, his face never
shown, stands between his flock and divides them: soft white woolly sheep gather
to his right, and darker shaggy goats move off to his left. A quiet, solemn,
peaceful separation under a wide dusk sky. One single continuous scene painted
edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s2-hungry-fed — Shot 2: I was an hungred, and ye gave me meat
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A humble village doorway in warm daylight. A kind woman and
a boy hold out a loaf of bread and a bowl of food to a thin, ragged hungry man
who receives it with grateful, wondering eyes. Warm ordinary human kindness,
faces gentle and clearly shown, no grandeur — just food freely given. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s3-stranger-drink — Shot 3: I was a stranger, and ye took me in
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A doorway at dusk. A family welcomes a weary, dusty traveler
— a stranger with a worn cloak and a walking staff — in out of the road, one of
them offering him a clay cup of water, another gesturing him warmly inside toward
the lamplight. Relief on the stranger's face, kindness on theirs, all faces
clearly shown. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s4-clothed-sick — Shot 4: naked, and ye clothed me; sick, and ye visited me
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Two tender acts of mercy in one warm scene: a young woman
gently wraps a warm woven cloak around the shoulders of a shivering, poorly
dressed old man; and nearby, another person sits at a sick person's bedside,
holding their hand and offering a cup, a small oil lamp glowing. Compassion on
every face, all clearly shown. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge,
9:16 vertical.

## s5-prison-visit — Shot 5: I was in prison, and ye came unto me
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Outside a rough stone prison wall in warm low light. A kind
visitor crouches at a small barred window, passing a piece of bread and a
comforting hand through to a gaunt prisoner within, whose weary face lifts toward
the kindness. Not grim or violent — a quiet act of remembering the forgotten. All
faces clearly shown. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge, 9:16
vertical.

## s6-least — Shot 6: unto one of the least of these
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A dusty street corner in warm golden light. An ordinary
passer-by kneels down in the dust to tenderly help a small, poor beggar child
sitting against a wall — offering food, meeting the child eye to eye with real
gentleness — while other people hurry past in the background, not noticing. Soft
warm light gathers around the two of them. Both faces clearly shown. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.

## s7-hands-close — Shot 7: ye have done it unto me
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A close, tender image of one weathered helping hand placing a
piece of bread and a small clay cup into the open, cupped hands of a needy person,
warm golden light glowing around the simple gift of mercy — the whole meaning of
the story held in one small kindness. Uplifting and hushed. One single continuous
scene painted edge to edge, 9:16 vertical.
