## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God hid himself in the people easiest to overlook, so plain,
  unscored kindness would always reach him — "ye have done it unto me."
- **The turn:** "Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these... ye have
  done it unto me." The hinge is the reveal that Christ was in the needy all along.
- **Character shown:** he did not hide behind something impressive; he hid in the
  hungry, the stranger, the sick, the imprisoned.
- **Whose face carries it:** the RIGHTEOUS (puzzled, "when did we see you?") and the
  needy people they served. The King is Christ; show him in the needy via master ref /
  over-the-shoulder onto the helper — never a throne-portrait as the emotional beat.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Will you show him a kindness today?" No change.
