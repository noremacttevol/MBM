# MASTER PAIRING LIST — Feed 2.0, Build-Order Step 1

Created 2026-07-07 per FEED-2.0-SPEC.md section 6. Three parts:

- **Part A** — the exact linked verse for each of the 200 video entries in
  THE-200.md (displayed beneath its video in the feed, honored separately).
- **Part B** — the verse-only pool (~100 items) that fills and varies pages.
- **Part C** — the BRIDGE sprinkle study set (signal-gated; scholar-grade
  care; never argued, only offered).

**Engineering law for verse text:** this file is the source of REFERENCES
only. The verse TEXT shown in the app is pulled at build time from a
verified public-domain source — KJV text from a standard KJV database, and
Book of Mormon / D&C / Pearl of Great Price text from the public-domain
scripture text (bare verses only, never the Church's copyrighted study
helps) — and verified by script against that source. Verse text is NEVER
typed by hand into the app. This makes misquotes impossible.

**Selection rule used throughout Part A:** the paired verse is the single
verse (occasionally two) that carries the video's Seed — the line a person
should sit with after watching — not merely the story's citation. Where the
video's KJV red-letter line and the best Seed verse differ, the Seed verse
wins, because the video already delivers the red-letter line out loud.

### C-1. 1 Corinthians 15:29 — baptism for the dead

- KJV: "Else what shall they do which are baptized for the dead, if the
  dead rise not at all? why are they then baptized for the dead?"
- The honest scholarly picture: Paul cites a practice his readers evidently
  knew — baptism on behalf of the dead — as evidence for the resurrection.
  Mainstream commentators have proposed dozens of alternate readings
  precisely because the plain reading is uncomfortable; a wide scholarly
  current concedes the plain reading (a vicarious practice existed in the
  Corinthian church) while holding Paul neither endorses nor condemns it.
- The restored-gospel resonance (never stated to Tier-2 users, only felt):
  a practice the rest of Christianity has no home for has a complete,
  loving home in the Restoration — the dead are not beyond the reach of
  ordinances or of God's fairness. (Pairs quietly with 1 Pet 3:18–19 and
  4:6 — Christ personally preached to the dead — and with feed video #132,
  the thief in "paradise.")
- Feed-item draft (subject to verification pass): show the bare verse.
  One-line prompt beneath: "Paul asked his readers a question that assumes
  they knew the answer. What do you think they were doing — and why?"
- VERIFY before shipping: current state of scholarship on the plain
  reading (Fee, Thiselton, and recent journal treatments), so the framing
  overclaims nothing.

### C-2. John 10:30 read with John 17 — what "one" means

- KJV John 10:30: "I and my Father are one."
- The Greek: "one" here is *hen* (neuter — one THING, one in unity), not
  *heis* (masculine — one PERSON). On its own grammar the verse asserts
  perfect unity, not identity of person.
- The interpretive key Jesus himself supplies: John 17:21–22 — "that they
  may be one; AS thou, Father, art in me... that they may be one, EVEN AS
  WE ARE ONE." Whatever oneness the Father and Son share, Jesus prays his
  disciples will share the same kind — which no one reads as the disciples
  merging into a single being. Add the scene evidence the feed already
  carries: the baptism (#82), the transfiguration (#78), Stephen's vision
  (Acts 7:55–56), "not mine to give... but my Father's" (#96).
- Feed-item draft (subject to verification pass): show John 10:30, then
  John 17:22 side by side. Prompt: "Same word, same Gospel, same speaker.
  What kind of 'one' did he pray his friends would become?"
- VERIFY before shipping: confirm the *hen/heis* grammar note against
  standard Greek references (BDAG; standard John commentaries) and phrase
  it so a Greek student would nod, not wince.

### C-3. Galatians 1:8 — "another gospel" (study-only; hardest of the three)

- KJV: "But though we, or an angel from heaven, preach any other gospel
  unto you than that which we have preached unto you, let him be
  accursed."
- Why it must be handled with total honesty: this is THE verse used
  against the Restoration (an angel, a new book). Any clever dodge would
  violate the app's soul. The real response is contextual, not defensive:
  (a) Paul's target is the Judaizers DISTORTING the gospel he had already
  preached — the curse falls on preaching a DIFFERENT gospel, not on
  heavenly messengers as such; (b) the New Testament itself continues to
  feature angels delivering true messages after this epistle (Rev 14:6:
  "another angel... having the everlasting gospel to preach"); (c) the
  Restoration's claim is precisely NOT "another gospel" — it is the same
  gospel Paul preached, restored: faith, repentance, baptism, the Holy
  Ghost, Christ crucified and risen. The question Galatians 1:8 actually
  poses is "is the content the same gospel?" — which is an invitation to
  compare content, and the app welcomes that comparison.
- Status: STUDY-ONLY. This one enters the feed last, if at all, and only
  after a dedicated research pass (LDS scholarly treatments and the
  strongest critical counter-readings side by side). It may serve better
  as chat-AI knowledge (for when a user raises it) than as a feed item —
  decide after research.
- VERIFY before shipping: everything.
