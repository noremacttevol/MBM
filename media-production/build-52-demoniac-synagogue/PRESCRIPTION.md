# PRESCRIPTION — build-52-demoniac-synagogue

**Row:** 52   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Mark 1:21–28
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 8 — see deviation)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** there is no darkness in you so deep that his word cannot reach it,
  and nothing holding you that has to stay when he says come out.
- **The turn:** [jv25/n5] "Hold thy peace, and come out of him" — a few short words, no
  battle of equals; the thing that had bound the man for so long simply has to go.
- **Character shown:** authority — the first one in a room full of religious people to
  say out loud who he was, was the darkness; and the darkness obeys him instantly.
- **Whose face carries it:** the BOUND MAN — the torment in his cry, then his face
  draining quiet and whole; and the astonished congregation. Jesus speaks the command
  without being the emotional face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what is he asking you to let him set free?"

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 8 stills
1. [n1/n2] Jesus standing to teach in the Capernaum synagogue on the sabbath, the room astonished at his authority — "He taught them as one who had authority of his own"
2. [n3] a man in the congregation held by an unclean spirit, stirred by Jesus' nearness, suddenly crying out — "the nearness of Jesus stirred it... he suddenly cried out"
3. [n4/s24/n4b] the voice not his own — the darkness naming him first, in a room full of religious people — "I know thee who thou art, the Holy One of God"
4. [jv25/n5] "Hold thy peace, and come out of him" — no battle; the spirit shaking the man one last time and leaving — "the spirit shook the man one last time... and came out of him"
5. [n6] the man free, torment drained from his face, standing quiet and whole — "himself again, like someone waking gently from a long and terrible dream"
6. [n7/s27/n7b] the people amazed, turning to one another — "What new doctrine is this? for with authority commandeth he even the unclean spirits, and they do obey him"
7. [n8] the news of him going out everywhere, through all Galilee — "the news of him went out everywhere, through all the country round about Galilee"
8. [card] hold for the closing question about what he can set free

## Deviations from the band
Landed at 8 pictures, just under the T2 10–15 band. This is a short, single-scene
encounter with little staged action — one room, one command, one deliverance — so the
distinct visual moments the words paint are few; adding beats would invent movement
the narration does not describe. Honest read ~8, sitting between T1 and T2. Trusting
the beats over the band.
