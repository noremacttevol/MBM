## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the third servant's real tragedy is that he misjudged his
  master's heart — God is not the hard man fear imagines; he trusts you with something
  real and is longing to say "well done."
- **The turn:** the third servant's excuse — "I knew thee that thou art an hard man...
  I was afraid." The hinge is the gap between what he believed about the master and
  who the master really was.
- **Character shown / wound answered:** answers the "God is harsh, so keep your head
  down" wound. The master's joy, not his severity, is the point.
- **Whose face carries it:** the THREE SERVANTS — the fearful third one especially —
  and the master's face (grieved, not cruel). The master is the parable's lord, not
  Jesus; do not draw a Jesus likeness.
- **The card question falls out of:** "What if he is kinder than you think?" No change.

## Deviations from the band
Planned 14 pictures, below the T3 band of 16–24. Reason: the narration **summarizes**
the two faithful servants' work ("went straight to work... doubled everything") rather
than dramatizing multiple trading scenes, so there are fewer distinct visual moments
than a full-arc dramatization would give. The 14 are every moment the words actually
paint — not padded up to hit the band, per the no-clock rule (the band is a mirror,
not a target).
