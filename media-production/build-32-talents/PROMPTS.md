# FLOW PROMPTS — Story Video #32: The Talents (matthew-25_talents)

Matthew 25:14-30. STILLS-ONLY (Law E). This is a PARABLE Jesus tells — there is
NO Jesus figure in any shot, only his narrating KJV voice. The MASTER is an
ordinary parable character shown fully (a wealthy nobleman, NOT the Lord), so
the face gate is trivial: no shot below names Jesus / the Lord at all.

Nano Banana 2, 9:16, x2, 2K download. Hand-painted 2D storybook style (NOT the
stale master-plan photorealistic block).

**CHARACTER LOCKS (keep consistent across every still they appear in):**
- **The nobleman (shots 1, 5, 6, 7)** — a dignified, warm-faced wealthy
  Middle-Eastern nobleman, grey-streaked dark hair and short beard, a rich deep
  indigo-blue robe with a gold-embroidered border and a wide sash. His face IS
  shown — he is an ordinary man in the story, generous and kind, not a holy or
  divine figure.
- **The five-talent servant (shots 1, 2, 5, 6)** — a capable, energetic younger
  man, short dark hair, a clean olive-green tunic. His face IS shown.
- **The two-talent servant (shots 1, 3, 5)** — a steady, earnest man, a warm
  ochre-tan tunic. His face IS shown.
- **The one-talent servant (shots 1, 4, 7)** — an anxious, careworn man, a drab
  grey-brown tunic, always tense and fearful. His face IS shown.
- **The talents** — heavy leather drawstring pouches bulging with silver coins.
  Five pouches / two pouches / one pouch to mark each servant.

## s1-entrusting — Shot 1: he entrusted his servants with his money
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The courtyard of a fine stone house, a laden travel-donkey
waiting by the gate at dawn. A dignified wealthy nobleman with a grey-streaked
beard, in a rich deep indigo-blue robe with a gold border, is handing out heavy
leather pouches of silver to three servants standing before him: to the
energetic younger man in an olive-green tunic he gives five bulging pouches; to
the earnest man in an ochre-tan tunic, two pouches; to the anxious man in a drab
grey-brown tunic, a single pouch. His face is open and trusting. Warm morning
light, a sense of great responsibility being handed over. One single continuous
scene painted edge to edge.

## s2-five-trades — Shot 2: the five-bag servant traded and gained five more
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A busy first-century marketplace in warm daylight. The
energetic five-talent servant in his olive-green tunic is in the thick of it,
striking a deal over stacked goods and baskets, coins and silver changing hands,
his face bright and industrious as his wealth clearly multiplies around him. His
open leather pouches spill bright silver. Bustling stalls, bolts of cloth, jars
of oil, a thriving trade. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s3-two-works — Shot 3: the two-bag servant also gained two more
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] A modest workshop courtyard in warm light. The earnest
two-talent servant in his ochre-tan tunic works diligently at an honest trade,
his hands busy, his two pouches of silver having clearly grown as goods and
coins accumulate neatly beside him. His face is steady and satisfied with good
work. Warm, humble, hard-working atmosphere. One single continuous scene painted
edge to edge.

## s4-one-buries — Shot 4: the one-bag servant hid it in the earth
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Night in a lonely field behind a house, cold blue moonlight.
The anxious one-talent servant in his drab grey-brown tunic kneels and buries a
single leather pouch of silver deep in a freshly dug hole in the ground,
patting the dirt over it, glancing fearfully over his shoulder. His face is
tight with fear and mistrust. A spade in the dirt beside him. Cold moonlit
night, secretive and small. One single continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s5-return-reckon — Shot 5: the nobleman returned and they showed him
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Back in the fine stone house in warm golden light. The
returned nobleman in his indigo-blue robe stands as the five-talent servant in
olive-green and the two-talent servant in ochre-tan proudly lay out before him
their doubled pouches of silver, coins gleaming in neat piles on a low table.
The two servants' faces are eager and glad; the nobleman leans in with delight
at what they have made. Warm, celebratory light. One single continuous scene painted
edge to edge.

## s6-well-done — Shot 6: well done, enter into the joy of thy lord
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] Close and warm in the golden-lit house. The kind nobleman in
his indigo-blue robe clasps the shoulder of the faithful servant with both hands,
his grey-bearded face lit with open, overflowing joy and pride, welcoming the
servant in as an equal, almost embracing him. The servant's face is moved to
grateful joy. Rich warm golden light, a moment of shared celebration. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge.

## s7-buried-returned — Shot 7: I was afraid, and hid thy talent in the earth
[STILL STYLE BLOCK] The same warm house, but a heavier mood. The anxious
one-talent servant in his drab grey-brown tunic stands with his head bowed,
holding out his single dirt-caked pouch of silver, just dug up and handed back
untouched. The nobleman in his indigo-blue robe looks at him not with rage but
with deep sorrow and disappointment, as if grieved that the servant had so badly
misjudged his heart. Warm light, a quiet ache of a wasted trust. One single
continuous scene painted edge to edge.
