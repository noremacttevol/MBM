## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the enemy outsider trusts Jesus's bare word more than his own people ever did — and Jesus flings the door of God's kingdom open to every nation on the strength of that trust.
- **The turn:** "only speak the word" (n10–n11) — the centurion refuses the house-visit and stakes everything on Jesus's word alone. That faith is what stops Jesus and pulls the "many from east and west" promise out of him.
- **Character shown:** he will walk into the home of the enemy for one dying servant nobody valued, and he measures a person by trust, not birth or nation.
- **Whose face carries it:** the CENTURION — the hardened officer humbling himself, and coming apart at the reunion. The dying servant's face at the healing. Jesus's face only via master ref at the spoken lines.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Is there something in your life you would place in Jesus's hands like that — simply on his word?" — falls straight out of the one thing. No change needed.
