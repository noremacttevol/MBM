> One truth told in a few strong images: a counsel in the Godhead → "let us make man
> in our image" → the living world handed over → man and woman made equal in the
> likeness → formed from dust → given breath → set as stewards → the dignity that runs
> to every person since. Each is a distinct moment the words paint; one still apiece.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** you are made in the image of a God who counseled together to make
  you and handed you the world — dignity is your origin, not something you earn.
- **The turn:** Genesis 1:26 — "Let us make man in our image." The plural *us* is the
  hinge: God is speaking *with* someone, and the doctrine of the Godhead sits right here.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the Godhead — "us / our" is more than one divine person in
  counsel. Hands off to **Gospel Library → Godhead**.
- **Whose face carries it:** the HUMAN face — the person bearing the likeness (beats
  5, 7, 8). Deity is never shown as a face: the council is light / figures, the forming
  is hands only (s1, s2, s3 are SCRIPTURE-blue, g26 is GOD-green).
- **The card question falls out of:** "You are made in his image. That is worth more
  than you know." Lands on the one thing. No change.
