# PRESCRIPTION — build-47-houses-on-rock-and-sand

**Row:** 47   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Matthew 7:24–29
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 12)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** both men heard the very same words — the only difference between
  a life that stands and one swept away is that one of them went home and *did*
  something about what he heard.
- **The turn:** [jv27/n9] the dry riverbed becomes a wall of water and the sand house
  is gone — the storm that finally "asks" what was underneath.
- **Character shown:** authority — the crowd astonished because he spoke as though the
  words belonged to him; and a plain, doable invitation, not a threat.
- **Whose face carries it:** the TWO BUILDERS (one digging to bedrock, one building
  fast on sand) and the astonished CROWD. Not Jesus's face in the parable beats.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what is one thing he said that you could
  actually go and do this week?"

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 12 stills
1. [n1/n2] Jesus finishing his teaching on the hillside; two men in the crowd, both hearing every word — "both men heard exactly the same thing"
2. [n3] the first man walking past the easy flat sand of the dry riverbed — "this man walks past the easy ground"
3. [n4] the wise man digging down through soil to bedrock, laying his foundation on stone — "all the way to bedrock, and he lays his foundation on the stone"
4. [jv24/n5] the finished rock house, looking no better than the rest in good weather — "it does not look one bit better than any other house on the plain"
5. [jv25/n6] the storm hitting the rock house full force — it does not even move — "the house did not even move. Because of what was underneath it"
6. [n7/jv26] the second man building fast on the smooth sand, skipping the digging — "straight down on the smooth sand, and he skips the digging altogether"
7. [n8] the sand house standing fine in the sunshine, indistinguishable from the first — "you could not have told the two houses apart"
8. [jv27/n9] the dry riverbed turned to a wall of water, tearing the sand out — the house gone — "it tore the sand out from under the house, and there was nothing left"
9. [n10] the two houses after the storm — one standing, one swept away — "One standing, one swept away"
10. [s28/n11a] the astonished crowd sitting there — he taught as one having authority — "the people were astonished at his doctrine"
11. [n11/n12] the real point: the wise man is the one who went home and did something — "went home and did something about what he heard"
12. [card] hold for the closing question about one thing to go do this week
