## What scripture tells us
- 2 Kgs 1:8 — "He was an hairy man, and girt with a girdle of leather about
  his loins" — scripture directly fixes the wild hair and the leather
  girdle; this overrides any tidier default.
- 1 Kgs 19:13 — "he wrapped his face in his mantle" — the rough mantle is
  his signature garment, later left to Elisha (2 Kgs 2:13).
- 1 Kgs 19:4-8 — worn to the bone in the wilderness, fed by the angel's
  bread — a lean, sun-scoured frame.
- 1 Kgs 18:46 — "he girded up his loins, and ran before Ahab" — wiry
  endurance, not bulk.
- The shipped #101 build locked his look; this sheet adopts it verbatim.

## Written description
(Adopted from the shipped #101 build.) A weathered Middle Eastern prophet of
about sixty with warm olive-brown sun-darkened skin, a long grey-streaked
dark beard and long grey-streaked dark hair — the hairy man of 2 Kgs 1:8 —
lean and wiry with desert endurance, deep-set burning eyes that can hold
both fire and utter weariness. A man of the open wilderness, never of
courts.

## Standard garments
(Locked by #101, adopted verbatim; mandated by 2 Kgs 1:8.) A coarse rough
DARK-BROWN camel-hair mantle over a plain DUN-GREY wool tunic to the shins,
girt with a wide worn LEATHER girdle; worn leather sandals; a plain wooden
staff when full length. No ornament of any kind, no cream, no off-white, no
halo, no glow.
