## What scripture tells us
- Gen 17:15 — "thou shalt not call her name Sarai, but Sarah shall her name
  be" — Sarah, princess: a noblewoman's name and bearing.
- Gen 12:11 — "thou art a fair woman to look upon"; Gen 12:14 — "the woman
  that she was very fair" — beautiful enough to turn a Pharaoh's court.
- Gen 18:11 — "Abraham and Sarah were old and well stricken in age" — OLD in
  her primary story; the sheet is this age. Beauty aged into dignity, not
  erased.
- Gen 18:12 — "Sarah laughed within herself" — wit and warmth live in her
  face.
- Gen 23:1 — she lived a hundred and twenty-seven years.
- Scripture gives no further features. Default: historically accurate
  ancient Near Eastern noblewoman of the patriarchal age.

## Written description
An old Hebrew noblewoman of about ninety who is still, unmistakably,
beautiful: warm olive-brown skin finely lined with age, silver-white hair
kept neatly beneath her headscarf, high graceful features, bright quick dark
eyes with laughter close behind them, an upright dignified carriage. A
princess of tents — refinement without royalty, warmth without frailty.

## Standard garments
An ankle-length long-sleeved wool dress in DEEP TEAL-BLUE with a warm ochre
mantle across the shoulders and a soft dove-grey headscarf draped over her
hair — finer weave than a servant's, still honest wool. Plain leather
sandals; at most one simple bracelet of small carnelian beads. No crown, no
cream, no off-white, no halo, no glow.
