## What scripture tells us
- John 1:45-46 — Philip fetches him; his first reaction is blunt and skeptical:
  "Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth?" A plain-spoken man.
- John 1:47 — Jesus's own description of him, and the anchor of this sheet:
  "Behold an Israelite indeed, in whom is no guile!" His face must be OPEN —
  nothing sly, nothing calculating.
- John 1:48-49 — seen "under the fig tree"; he yields at once: "Rabbi, thou art
  the Son of God." Skeptical, then wholehearted.
- John 21:2 — "Nathanael of Cana in Galilee."
- Scripture gives no physical description; default is historically accurate
  first-century Galilean Judean. Because he speaks with settled, blunt
  authority, this sheet makes him the OLDEST of the twelve.

## Written description
The eldest of the twelve, about forty-five: warm olive-brown Middle Eastern
skin weathered at the corners of the eyes, thick BLACK hair clearly streaked
with silver at the temples and worn to the collar, and a FULL black-and-silver
beard, the broadest beard among the apostles. Deep-set steady brown eyes and a
wide, open, guileless face — plain-spoken, entirely without slyness. A solid,
settled build.

## Standard garments
A DEEP FOREST-GREEN wool tunic — a true dark green, distinctly greener and
darker than Matthew's blue-toned teal and than Andrew's faded grey-olive — with
a woven brown sash, a charcoal-brown mantle when a cloak is needed, worn
leather sandals. Never cream, never off-white.
