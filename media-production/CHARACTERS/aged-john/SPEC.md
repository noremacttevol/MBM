**Appears in:** #154 (Rev 14:6 everlasting gospel), #174 (Mal 4 hearts of the
fathers / Revelation beats), #189 (Rev 3:21) — the old apostle who outlived the
others and wrote the Revelation on Patmos, ~60 years after the ministry.
**Variant of:** `john` (prime — the clean-shaven young fisherman of #51/#71).
**Stage key:** `aged` — see AGE-VARIANT KEY in `../../CHARACTER-LAW.md`.

## Continuity anchors (IDENTICAL to prime john — never change with age)
- Warm olive-brown Middle-Eastern skin tone (weathered now, but the same tone).
- Dark brown eyes, the same bright attentive gaze.
- The same underlying facial structure and nose as the young John — a viewer
  should read "that is John, sixty years on," not a stranger.

## Only these change with age
- Hair: now WHITE, thinner, still to about the jaw.
- Beard: the young John was clean-shaven; the old apostle now wears a SHORT
  WHITE beard (age, not a different man).
- Skin: deeply lined, the face of a man past eighty who has seen much.
- Build: leaner and slightly stooped; the wiry fisherman's frame gone spare.
- Garment: a plain undyed pale-grey/dun wool robe of an exiled elder — NEVER
  cream (only Jesus wears cream), no ornament.
