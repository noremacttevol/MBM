## What scripture tells us
- Gen 25:27 — "Jacob was a plain man, dwelling in tents" — no soldier, no
  wild man; a quiet keeper of tents and flocks.
- Gen 27:11 — "Esau my brother is a hairy man, and I am a smooth man" —
  smooth-skinned, in deliberate contrast to Esau (it took goat skins on his
  hands and neck to fool Isaac, Gen 27:16).
- Gen 28:10-11 — fleeing alone from Beersheba toward Haran, night catches
  him in the open, "he took of the stones of that place, and put them for
  his pillows" — a road-worn fugitive, this sheet's moment.
- Gen 28:12 — "behold a ladder set up on the earth" — the dream at Bethel.
- The shipped #102 build locked his look; this sheet adopts it verbatim.

## Written description
(Adopted from the shipped #102 build.) A young Hebrew man of about forty
with warm olive-brown sun-weathered skin, a short dark brown beard and dark
brown hair, smooth-handed rather than rugged — a plain man of tents, not a
hunter. On the road he is tired-shouldered and watchful, glancing back the
way he came; after Bethel the same face carries awe and a steadied hope. A
wooden staff and a small travel bundle are his signature props.

## Standard garments
(Locked by #102, adopted verbatim.) A dusty RUSSET-BROWN ankle-length
travelling robe with a worn leather girdle and a coarse GREY-BROWN mantle,
worn leather sandals; wooden staff and small shoulder bundle when full
length. No cream, no off-white, no ornament, no halo, no glow.
