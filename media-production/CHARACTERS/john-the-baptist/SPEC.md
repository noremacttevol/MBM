## What scripture tells us
- Matt 3:4 — "his raiment of camel's hair, and a leathern girdle about his
  loins; and his meat was locusts and wild honey." The one apostle-era figure
  whose wardrobe scripture LOCKS itself.
- Matt 3:1 — "preaching in the wilderness of Judaea" — a desert man, wind and
  sun in everything about him.
- Luke 1:15 — "shall drink neither wine nor strong drink" — an ascetic from
  birth, a Nazarite bearing.
- Matt 11:2 — "when John had heard in the prison the works of Christ, he sent
  two of his disciples" — the prison scenes of #107.
- Matt 11:11 — "Among them that are born of women there hath not risen a
  greater than John the Baptist."
- John 1:29 — "Behold the Lamb of God" — fire and certainty in the eyes.

## Written description
(Adopts the [JOHN LOCK] shipped in build #107 and repeated in #169 — already
on screen twice, must not drift.) A lean weathered man of about thirty with
sun-darkened olive skin, wild dark shoulder-length hair and a rough dark
beard — a lean desert ascetic, sinewy and spare from a diet of locusts and
wild honey, with burning earnest eyes and a dignified bearing even in chains.
Never bloodied or harmed on screen; the prison scenes keep him dignified and
thoughtful.

## Standard garments
A coarse CAMEL-HAIR garment — rough woven animal-brown, texture visible —
cinched with a wide LEATHER girdle (Matt 3:4; this wardrobe is scripture, not
a style choice, and never changes), bare weathered forearms and shins, worn
leather sandals or bare feet at the river.
