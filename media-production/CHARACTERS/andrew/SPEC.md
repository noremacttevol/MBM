## What scripture tells us
- Mark 1:16 — "Simon and Andrew his brother casting a net into the sea: for
  they were fishers." A working Galilean fisherman, Peter's brother.
- John 1:40-42 — first a disciple of John the Baptist; "He first findeth his
  own brother Simon" — the bringer, the introducer.
- John 6:8-9 — it is Andrew who finds the lad with five barley loaves and two
  small fishes — observant, practical, hopeful.
- John 12:22 — Greeks seeking Jesus come through Andrew — approachable.
- Scripture never describes his face or build; the sheet keeps him a
  historically accurate first-century Galilean fisherman and gives him the
  family resemblance to his brother Peter.

## Written description
(Adopts the [ANDREW LOCK] shipped in build #71 — already on screen, must not
drift.) A lean Galilean fisherman in his early-to-mid thirties, a little
younger and leaner than his brother Peter but plainly family: the same dark
curly hair (kept shorter than Peter's), a SHORT dark beard, weathered olive
skin, long wiry rope-hardened arms, an eager open face — the man who is
always bringing someone to see for himself.

## Standard garments
A faded OLIVE-DRAB fisherman's wool tunic (locked in #71 — never cream, and
never Peter's blue-grey, so the brothers never blur together), rope belt,
a dun-brown work mantle when a cloak is needed, bare wiry forearms, simple
leather sandals.
