## What scripture tells us
- Luke 8:2 — "Mary called Magdalene, out of whom went seven devils" — a woman
  delivered; the deliverance is in her past, and the face carries the peace
  that followed it, not the affliction.
- Luke 8:2-3 — among the women who "ministered unto him of their substance" —
  a devoted follower on the road.
- John 19:25 — she stands by the cross.
- John 20:1, 11-18 — first at the tomb while it is "yet dark"; weeps; and is
  the first witness of the risen Lord — "Rabboni."
- Mark 16:9 — "he appeared first to Mary Magdalene" — scripture itself makes
  her the first witness.
- Scripture never describes her face; the sheet keeps her a historically
  accurate first-century Judean woman.

## Written description
(Adopts the [MARY LOCK] shipped verbatim in build #98 — already on screen,
must not drift.) A Middle Eastern Jewish woman of about thirty, warm brown
skin, long dark wavy hair escaping a loosely draped deep madder-red shawl,
large dark expressive eyes — a face that can carry grief at the tomb and
astonished joy at the recognition, with the settled peace of a delivered
soul underneath both.

## Standard garments
A dark OLIVE-BROWN wool dress under a loosely draped DEEP MADDER-RED shawl
worn as her veil and mantle (locked in #98 — the madder-red is HER color
among the rostered women; never pale, never cream), the shawl draped in the
modest first-century manner but loosely, so the long dark wavy hair shows at
its edge, simple leather sandals.
