## What scripture tells us
- Matt 1:19 — "Joseph her husband, being a just man" — decency and restraint
  are the core of him.
- Matt 13:55 — "Is not this the carpenter's son?" — a carpenter; strong,
  careful working hands.
- Luke 2:4 — "of the house and lineage of David" — a poor man of a royal
  line, dignity without wealth.
- Matt 1:24 — obeys the angel immediately; Matt 2:14 — "took the young child
  and his mother by night" — the quiet protector, always watching over them.
- Luke 2:48 — present in the temple story, still guarding the family.
- Scripture never describes his face; the sheet keeps him a historically
  accurate first-century Judean workman.

## Written description
(Adopts the look shipped in build #84 — a strong man of about thirty-five
with a short dark beard and a kind weathered face — must not drift.) A
strong Galilean carpenter of about thirty-five: warm olive-brown skin
weathered by outdoor work, short dark hair, a short dark beard, a kind
watchful face that is always half-turned toward Mary and the child, broad
shoulders and a carpenter's strong careful hands. Protective, patient,
saying little.

## Standard garments
A plain WARM-BROWN wool tunic and matching brown mantle (locked in #84 —
never cream), a simple cord or leather belt, dusty leather sandals; a
walking staff and a donkey lead-rope are his recurring props on the road.
