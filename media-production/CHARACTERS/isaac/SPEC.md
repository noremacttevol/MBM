## What scripture tells us
- Gen 22:5 — "I and the lad will go yonder and worship" — a LAD, a youth,
  in his primary story; the sheet is this age.
- Gen 22:6 — "Abraham took the wood of the burnt offering, and laid it upon
  Isaac his son" — old enough and strong enough to carry the wood up the
  mountain.
- Gen 22:7 — "My father… behold the fire and the wood: but where is the
  lamb for a burnt offering?" — trusting, thoughtful, asking honest
  questions.
- Gen 21:3 — the promised son, born to Abraham at a hundred years old.
- Scripture is silent on his features. The shipped #115 build locked his
  look as a boy of about twelve; this sheet adopts it verbatim.

## Written description
(Adopted from the shipped #115 build.) A gentle boy of about twelve with
dark wavy hair: warm olive-brown skin, large trusting dark eyes, a soft
open beardless face — calm, willing and unafraid, the promised son who
walks beside his father carrying the wood. A boy sturdy enough for a
mountain climb, still plainly a child, never a grown man on this sheet.

## Standard garments
(Locked by #115, adopted verbatim.) A plain DUN (dusty grey-brown) wool
tunic to the knee with a simple cord belt, bare forearms, worn leather
sandals. No mantle needed; nothing fine. No cream, no off-white, no
ornament, no halo, no glow.
