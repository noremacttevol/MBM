## What scripture tells us
- Luke 10:39 — "Mary, which also sat at Jesus' feet, and heard his word" —
  the listener; stillness is her whole posture.
- Luke 10:42 — "Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken
  away from her" — quiet, and commended for it.
- John 11:32 — she falls down at his feet weeping — the feeling sister,
  where Martha is the speaking one.
- John 12:3 — "a pound of ointment of spikenard, very costly," anointing his
  feet and wiping them "with her hair" — so her hair is long and dark
  beneath the veil.
- Scripture never describes her face; the sheet keeps her a historically
  accurate first-century Judean woman, the younger quiet sister.

## Written description
(Adopts the look shipped in builds #16 and #17 — the younger, quiet sister
in dusty-blue — already on screen, must not drift.) A Middle Eastern Jewish
woman in her mid-twenties, younger than her sister Martha: warm brown skin,
a soft still attentive face with large gentle dark eyes that rest on whoever
is speaking, long dark hair covered loosely by her shawl, quiet unhurried
hands — the listener at the feet, feeling everything, saying little.

## Standard garments
A DUSTY-BLUE wool dress (locked in #17 — plainly softer and lighter than
Mary the mother's deep blue, and nothing like Martha's russet-red or
Magdalene's madder-red), a simple undyed natural grey-brown wool shawl worn
loosely over her hair in the modest first-century manner (#16 — never cream),
simple leather sandals.
