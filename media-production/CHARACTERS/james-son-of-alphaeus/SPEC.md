## What scripture tells us
- Matt 10:3, Mark 3:18, Luke 6:15, Acts 1:13 — "James the son of Alphaeus,"
  one of the Twelve in every apostle list.
- Mark 15:40 — "Mary the mother of James the less" — the Greek *mikros* means
  little or younger. Scripture itself distinguishes him by SIZE, so this sheet
  obeys it the way Zacchaeus's does (Luke 19:3): he is the SHORTEST of the
  twelve, compact and small-framed. This is the one physical fact scripture
  gives about him, so it is the sheet's defining feature.
- He speaks no recorded word — a quiet, faithful man who is simply always
  there. That reads as steadiness, never as timidity.

## Written description
The shortest and most compact of the twelve, about thirty: a small-framed but
sturdy Middle Eastern Jewish man, noticeably shorter than the other apostles
and standing a full head below Peter. Warm olive-brown skin, close-cropped
dark brown hair, a short neat dark beard, calm attentive dark eyes, a quiet
steady face. Plain, unremarkable, entirely faithful — the man at the edge of
the group who never leaves it.

## Standard garments
A plain NEUTRAL STONE-GREY wool tunic — a true warm grey with NO blue in it,
so he is never mistaken for Peter's blue-grey or John's grey-blue — with a
simple rope belt, a dun-brown mantle when a cloak is needed, worn leather
sandals. The plainest clothing of the twelve. Never cream, never off-white.
