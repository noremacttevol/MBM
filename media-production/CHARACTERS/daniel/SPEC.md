## Written description
A wise young Judean noble in Babylonian exile, about thirty-five in the
locked look: warm Middle-Eastern skin, dark hair kept neat, a short dark
beard, clear steady eyes, a handsome well-favoured face that is calm rather
than proud. Upright courtly posture — at ease before a throne without ever
grasping at one. His dignity comes from restraint: amid gold and purple he
is the composed man in modest earth tones. Never anxious, never servile.

## Standard garments
The modest robes of a Babylonian-court exile (his locked colors, from the
#160 lock): a dark brown ankle-length robe with an olive under-tunic and a
muted indigo sash or mantle — court-cut cloth, Babylonian in drape, but
deliberately plain against the palace's splendor. Simple leather sandals.
No gold, no jewels, no Babylonian idol motifs. Never white, cream, off-white
or pale.
