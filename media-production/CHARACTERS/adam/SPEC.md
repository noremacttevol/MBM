## What scripture tells us
- Gen 2:7 — "the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground" — the first
  man, made in full adult strength, never a child, never aged.
- Gen 2:15 — put into the garden "to dress it and to keep it" — a working
  man's build and hands.
- Gen 3:21 — "Unto Adam also and to his wife did the LORD God make coats of
  skins, and clothed them" — the sheet's locked garment. The sheet is ALWAYS
  the clothed state; never nudity, at any angle, ever.
- Scripture is silent on his features. Default: warm ancient Near Eastern
  features, adult and timeless — the shipped #113 build locked only "earth-
  toned leaf and skin garments, modest," which this sheet obeys.

## Written description
The first man, adult and timeless — he reads as somewhere in his early
thirties, in the full strength of unfallen creation: tall and strongly built
from garden labor, warm olive-brown skin, thick dark brown hair falling
almost to the shoulders, a full but neat dark beard, deep thoughtful dark
eyes that have looked on Eden. Upright, unhurried bearing; after Genesis 3,
a quiet sorrow-and-hope in the face, never despair.

## Standard garments
The coat of skins of Gen 3:21, and nothing else ever on this sheet: a simple
long garment of soft TAWNY-BROWN animal skins from the shoulders to
mid-calf, sleeveless at the forearm, bound at the waist with a plain twisted
hide cord. Bare feet. Completely modest — chest, torso and thighs always
covered. No cream, no off-white, no ornament, no halo, no glow.
