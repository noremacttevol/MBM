## Written description

grief, warm olive skin gone thin and soft with age, grey hair kept fully
covered by her mourning shawl, tired dark eyes that have wept much and still
know how to be kind. Slightly bent, moving carefully, carrying loss in her
shoulders. BEFORE state (Mara): hollowed, bitter-sad, empty-handed. AFTER
state: the same woman with Ruth's child in her arms, the lines of grief
still there but lit with quiet restored joy.
