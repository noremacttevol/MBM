## Why this variant exists (read before changing it)
Cameron clarified 2026-07-21 that by "older Jesus" he meant the **adult** Jesus,
not an aged one — and the adult ministry Jesus is already locked as
JESUS-MASTER-REF, so no new sheet was needed for that. This sheet covers the
one Jesus stage that still had nothing governing it: the Jesus **after** the
Resurrection. Same man, same age, now glorified. Jesus's four stages are
therefore: `infant` → `young` (twelve) → **prime** (adult ministry,
JESUS-MASTER-REF) → `risen`.

## What scripture tells us
- Luke 24:39 — "a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as ye see me have." The
  risen body is a real, tangible body. Not a ghost, not a mist, not a light.
- John 20:27 — Thomas is told to reach and thrust his hand into his side. The
  **wound marks in his hands, feet, and side remain**, and they are the proof.
- Luke 24:16, John 20:14-15 — he is not instantly recognized; he looks like an
  ordinary man walking around, not a blazing apparition.
- Acts 7:55-56 — Stephen sees him **standing** on the right hand of God, both
  in glory. Two distinct glorified personages (see `../../GOD-THE-FATHER-LOCK.md`).
- Rev 1:14-15 — in the full glorified vision: hair white as wool, eyes as a
  flame, feet like fine brass. **Use this only for explicit throne/vision
  beats**, never for the tomb-garden or upper-room beats.

## Continuity anchors (never change — this must read as the master ref)
Warm tan olive-brown skin; warm BROWN eyes; dark brown-black wavy shoulder-
length hair; the full dark beard; the exact facial structure of
JESUS-MASTER-REF; the CREAM robe (cream is his alone — the Father alone wears
pure white). Never caucasian, never pale, never blue-eyed, never blond, never
white-haired outside an explicit Rev 1 throne beat.

## Hard rules
1. **Wound marks are mandatory** in every risen shot — hands, feet, side.
   Reverent and non-gory: clean marks, no blood, no wet wounds.
2. **Never pure white.** If he is beside the Father, the Father's pure-white
   robe and Jesus's cream robe are what separate the persons at a glance.
3. **No halo ring, ever** — glory is light in the scene, not a disc behind the
   head.
4. In any two-personage beat, both figures are fully embodied, distinct, and
   the same height class. Jesus stands at the Father's right hand.
