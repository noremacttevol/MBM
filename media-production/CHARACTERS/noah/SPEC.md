## What scripture tells us
- Gen 6:9 — "Noah was a just man and perfect in his generations, and Noah
  walked with God."
- Gen 7:6 — "Noah was six hundred years old when the flood of waters was
  upon the earth" — an old patriarch, yet hale enough to build the ark and
  work the ground after it.
- Gen 8:20 — "Noah builded an altar unto the LORD" — the first act off the
  ark; a working man's hands.
- Gen 9:13 — "I do set my bow in the cloud" — the covenant story this sheet
  serves; his age on the sheet is the Gen 9 old patriarch.
- Gen 9:20 — "Noah began to be an husbandman" — a farmer's solid frame.
- Scripture is silent on his features beyond great age. The shipped #135
  build locked his look; this sheet adopts it verbatim.

## Written description
(Adopted from the shipped #135 build.) A hale white-bearded elder with
weathered warm olive-tan skin — great age carried with strength, not
frailty: a full white beard, white hair, a deep-lined kind face that has
watched the world drown and begin again, steady grateful eyes often lifted
toward the sky. The solid build of a man who built an ark with his own
hands and ploughed the new earth after it.

## Standard garments
(Locked by #135, adopted verbatim.) An ankle-length RUST-BROWN wool robe
with long sleeves to the wrists, a dark-brown cloth sash at the waist, and a
faded grey wool mantle over the shoulders. Worn leather sandals. No cream,
no off-white, no ornament, no halo, no glow.
