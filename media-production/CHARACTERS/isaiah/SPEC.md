## What scripture tells us
- Isa 1:1 — the vision "in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah,
  kings of Judah" — a long ministry in Jerusalem, eighth century BC.
- Isa 6:1, 6:5 — sees the Lord "in the year that king Uzziah died"; "I am a
  man of unclean lips" — a prophet marked by that vision.
- Isa 7:3 — sent out to meet King Ahaz face to face.
- Isa 37:2, 38:1 — King Hezekiah sends his elders to Isaiah; Isaiah comes to
  the king — a court prophet with direct access to kings, dignified enough
  to stand in palaces.
- 2 Kgs 19:2 — the elders of the priests are sent "to Isaiah the prophet the
  son of Amoz." (Isa 20:2's barefoot sign-act is a story moment, not the
  sheet.)
