## What scripture tells us (Luke 7:36-50)

  Pharisee host, well-off enough to give a banquet.

  have known" — correct on the outside, judging on the inside.
- 7:40 — "Simon, I have somewhat to say unto thee" — named by the Lord.
- 7:44-46 — gave no water, no kiss, no oil — a host whose courtesy was
  measured and cold.
