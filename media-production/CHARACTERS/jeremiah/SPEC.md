## What scripture tells us
- Jer 1:1 — "the son of Hilkiah, of the priests that were in Anathoth" — a
  priestly family, a village north of Jerusalem, late seventh century BC.
- Jer 1:6 — "Ah, Lord GOD! behold, I cannot speak: for I am a child" —
  young at his call. (Build-180 covers that moment; the SHEET shows his
  mature ministry years.)
- Jer 9:1 — "Oh that my head were waters, and mine eyes a fountain of
  tears" — the weeping prophet; grief lives in his face.
- Jer 16:2 — "Thou shalt not take thee a wife" — a solitary life.
- Jer 20:9 — the word "as a burning fire shut up in my bones" — he cannot
  stop, however much it costs.
- Jer 38:6 — cast into the dungeon, "and in the dungeon there was no water,
  but mire: so Jeremiah sunk in the mire" — a body that has known suffering.

## Written description
LOCKED by build-199, adopted verbatim: a Middle Eastern man of about
forty-five, a grave sorrowing face with deep kind eyes, a FULL hairline of
dark grey-streaked hair, a full grey-streaked beard. Weathered olive skin, a
frame gone lean from hardship, shoulders that carry a weight — sorrow and
kindness in the same face, never bitterness. The eyes of Jer 9:1.

**Young call variant (#180, already shipped):** the same man at about
twenty — slight earnest build, a FULL hairline of loose curly dark hair, the
first thin beginnings of a young beard, wide thoughtful dark eyes, dark
olive tunic with a rust-brown mantle. Same face, scaled young; condition any
future call-scene stills on the sheet plus that written variant.

## Standard garments

pale, never cream. Plain rope or leather belt, worn leather sandals. No
ornament of any kind.
