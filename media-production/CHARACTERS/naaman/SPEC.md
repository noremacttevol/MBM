## Written description
A powerfully built Aramean (Syrian) commander in his late forties: broad
shoulders and a soldier's upright bearing, bronzed olive skin weathered by
campaigns, strong jaw, dark eyes used to command, black hair to the nape
dressed in the Syrian court fashion, a full squared dark beard streaked with
the first grey (ninth-century-BC Aramean style). BEFORE state: pale patchy
leprous skin visible on his forearms and the backs of his hands — dignified,
never graphic, no open sores on the face. AFTER state: the same man, skin
clean and renewed. His face is identical in both.

## Standard garments
Aramean commander's dress, NOT Judean: a rich deep-red knee-length tunic with
a bronze-studded wide leather belt, a dark charcoal officer's cloak pinned at
one shoulder, leather bracers, hardened-leather greaves over calf-high boots.
No crown; a commander, not a king. (Era: ninth century BC Damascus — the
style block's era line is adjusted accordingly.)
