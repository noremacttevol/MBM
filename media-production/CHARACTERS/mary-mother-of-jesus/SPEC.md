## What scripture tells us
- Luke 1:27 — "a virgin espoused to a man whose name was Joseph" — very young
  at the annunciation, as betrothal age was in first-century Judea.
- Luke 1:38 — "Behold the handmaid of the Lord" — willing, humble, steady.
- Luke 2:7 — "brought forth her firstborn son, and wrapped him in swaddling
  clothes" — the young mother of the nativity.
- Luke 2:19 — "Mary kept all these things, and pondered them in her heart" —
  a quiet, inward, thoughtful face.
- Luke 2:48 — "thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing" — the temple
  story, still a young mother.
- John 2:5 — "Whatsoever he saith unto you, do it" — calm certainty at Cana.
- John 19:26-27 — "Woman, behold thy son!" — she stands at the cross.
- Scripture never describes her face; the sheet keeps her a historically
  accurate first-century Judean woman.
