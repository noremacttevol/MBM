## What scripture tells us
- John 3:1 — "a man of the Pharisees, named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews"
  — a member of the ruling council, a man of rank and learning.
- John 3:2 — "the same came to Jesus by night" — cautious, coming in secret.
- John 3:4 — "How can a man be born when he is old?" — he speaks of himself
  as an old man.
- John 3:10 — "Art thou a master of Israel?" — a recognized teacher.
- John 7:50-51 — defends Jesus before the council — courage growing.
- John 19:39 — brings "a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about an hundred pound
  weight" for the burial — a wealthy man, openly devoted at the end.
