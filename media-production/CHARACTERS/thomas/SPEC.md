## What scripture tells us
- John 11:16 — "Thomas, which is called Didymus" — the twin; and "Let us also
  go, that we may die with him" — brave loyalty under the doubt.
- John 14:5 — "Lord, we know not whither thou goest" — the one who asks the
  question everyone else is afraid to ask.
- John 20:25 — "Except I shall see in his hands the print of the nails… I
  will not believe" — honest, stubborn doubt with real grief underneath.
- John 20:28 — "My Lord and my God" — the doubter gives the plainest
  confession in the gospels.
- Scripture never describes his face; the sheet keeps him a first-century
  Judean and lets the earnest, questioning temperament carry the look.
