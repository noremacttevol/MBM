- Gen 39:6 — "Joseph was a goodly person, and well favoured" — scripture
  directly states he is handsome.

- Gen 41:42 — Pharaoh "arrayed him in vestures of fine linen, and put a gold

  the text.
- Gen 41:43 — "he made him to ride in the second chariot which he had" —
  second only to Pharaoh.
- Gen 41:46 — thirty years old when he stood before Pharaoh; about forty at
  the reconciliation with his brothers, this sheet's age.

## Written description
(Adopted verbatim from the shipped #147 JOSEPH LOCK.) A Middle Eastern man
of about forty made Egyptian by office — CLEAN-SHAVEN, straight black hair
cut in the plain Egyptian court manner, warm intelligent dark eyes, a goodly
and well-favoured face (Gen 39:6) that can hold power and tears in the same
moment. His dress is Egyptian court linen, deliberately nothing like
rough-woven wool — a Hebrew heart in Pharaoh's livery.

## Standard garments
(Locked by #147, adopted verbatim; mandated by Gen 41:42.) Fine WHITE
PLEATED EGYPTIAN LINEN with a broad GOLD-AND-LAPIS collar and an Egyptian
sash; a gold chain about his neck and Pharaoh's gold signet ring on his
hand; flat Egyptian sandals. This Egyptian court linen is scripture-mandated
dress and is NOT the cream wool robe (Jesus only) nor the pure-white glory
garment (the Father only) — it reads unmistakably Egyptian. No halo, no
glow.
