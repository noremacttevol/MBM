## What scripture tells us
- Ezek 1:1-3 — "in the thirtieth year… as I was among the captives by the
  river of Chebar… the word of the LORD came expressly unto Ezekiel the
  priest, the son of Buzi, in the land of the Chaldeans" — a priest, called
  at thirty, an exile in Babylonia, sixth century BC.
- Ezek 3:15 — "I came to them of the captivity at Tel-abib, that dwelt by
  the river of Chebar" — he lives among the exiles, not in a court.
- Ezek 5:1 — commanded to take a razor "upon thine head and upon thy beard"
  as a sign-act — scripture confirms he wore hair and a beard (the sign-act
  is a story moment, not the sheet).
- Ezek 24:16-18 — his wife dies and he is forbidden to mourn openly — a man
  of deep, contained feeling.

## Written description
LOCKED by build-158, adopted verbatim: a thoughtful Middle-Eastern man of
about forty-five, dark hair and a full dark beard lightly touched with grey,
earnest deep eyes, his face thoughtful and kind. (Ezek 1:1 marks his call at
thirty; the sheet shows him in his ministry years, as already shipped in
#158.) A priest's careful, deliberate manner in exile — hands used to sacred
things now inscribing signs on wood by a Babylonian river.

## Standard garments
LOCKED by build-158: a plain tunic and cloak of undyed DUN and dark BROWN
wool with a leather belt — dark earth tones, never white, cream, off-white
or pale. Worn leather sandals. No priestly vestments in exile; no ornament.
