## What scripture tells us
- Mark 1:19-20 — son of Zebedee, a fisherman mending nets with his father;
  the YOUNGER of the two brothers by tradition and framing.
- John 13:23 — "leaning on Jesus' bosom… whom Jesus loved" — the youngest
  apostle by long tradition; gentle closeness.
- Mark 3:17 — a "son of thunder" — youth with fire in it, not softness only.
- John 20:4 — outran Peter to the tomb — young legs.

## Written description
(Consistent with the SONS OF ZEBEDEE LOCK in builds #51/#71 — John is the
clean-shaven younger brother.) A young Galilean fisherman of about twenty:
warm olive-brown skin, dark wavy hair to the jaw, CLEAN-SHAVEN (his standing
distinguisher among the apostles), bright attentive dark eyes, a lean wiry
build with a fisherman's hard forearms, an open earnest young face.

## Standard garments
A faded grey-blue coarse wool tunic with a rope belt (locked in #51/#71),
bare forearms, an ochre-brown mantle when a cloak is needed, simple leather
sandals. Never cream.
