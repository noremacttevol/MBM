## What scripture tells us (Daniel 2–4)
- Dan 2:37 — "Thou, O king, art a king of kings: for the God of heaven hath
  given thee a kingdom, power, and strength, and glory" — the greatest monarch
  of his age.
- Dan 3:1 — sets up the golden image, threescore cubits high — grandeur and
  pride.
- Dan 3:13, 3:19 — "in his rage and fury"; "full of fury, and the form of his
  visage was changed" — a temper that shows on his face.
- Dan 3:24 — "astonied," rises up in haste at the fourth man in the fire.
- Dan 3:28 — "Blessed be the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego" — a
  proud man capable of being humbled.
- Dan 4:30 — "Is not this great Babylon, that I have built… by the might of
  my power, and for the honour of my majesty?" — pride is his defining trait.
  (Dan 4:33's beast-of-the-field state is a story moment, not the sheet.)
