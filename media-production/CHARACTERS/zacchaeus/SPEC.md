## What scripture tells us (Luke 19:1-10)
- 19:2 — "named Zacchaeus, which was the chief among the publicans, and he
  was rich" — a chief tax collector, a wealthy man.
- 19:3 — "he sought to see Jesus who he was; and could not for the press,
  because he was little of stature" — SHORT. Scripture says it plainly; the
  sheet obeys it. His shortness is the hinge of the whole story.
- 19:4 — "he ran before, and climbed up into a sycomore tree" — spry enough
  to run and climb despite his years.
- 19:8 — "the half of my goods I give to the poor" — a rich man whose fine
  clothes the story needs, so the giving means something.
