## What scripture tells us
- 1 Sam 1:9 — "Eli the priest sat upon a seat by a post of the temple of the
  LORD" — the presiding priest at Shiloh; his seat is his signature posture.
- 1 Sam 2:22 — "Now Eli was very old."
- 1 Sam 4:15 — "Eli was ninety and eight years old; and his eyes were dim,
  that he could not see" — scripture directly states DIM EYES. This overrides
  any default: his eyes are clouded, unfocused, kind.
- 1 Sam 4:18 — "he was an old man, and heavy" — scripture directly states
  HEAVY. A big, heavy-set frame, slow-moving with age.

## Written description
A very old Israelite high priest, near ninety in the Samuel story: heavy-set
and broad, a big frame gone soft and slow with great age, a full head of long
thin white hair and a long white beard, a broad soft deep-lined face, and
clouded, dim, unfocused eyes — he peers rather than looks, and his kindness
shows in his face because it can no longer show in his gaze. Gentle, weary,
fatherly; a good-hearted man grown old at his post. He is most often seated
or half-risen; he never moves quickly.

## Standard garments
The dark layered robes of the presiding priest at Shiloh: a deep indigo-blue
ankle-length priestly robe (his locked color, from #104) worn over a dark
under-tunic, with a woven sash at the waist (from the #149 lock). Plain worn
leather sandals. Dignified but aged and lived-in — nothing gleaming, no
ornament beyond the sash. Never cream, never pure white.
