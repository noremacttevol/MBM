  and for beauty" — the vestments are commanded in detail and the sheet

- Ex 28:6 — the ephod "of gold, of blue, and of purple, of scarlet, and
  fine twined linen."
- Ex 28:15-21 — the breastplate of judgment with FOUR ROWS of THREE stones,
  twelve stones for the twelve tribes, set in gold.
- Ex 28:31-34 — "the robe of the ephod all of blue," its hem ringed with

## Written description
(Adopted from the shipped #161 build.) A dignified older man of about
eighty-three with warm olive-brown skin and a well-kept grey-streaked dark
beard, calm and reverent, his face gentle with the compassion of Heb 5:2 —
a high priest taken from among men, able to feel for them. Priestly
composure, never regal pride; he is consecrated, not crowned.

## Standard garments
(Locked by #161, following Ex 28 precisely.) The high-priestly vestments: a
long woven BLUE robe to the ankles with a hem of small pomegranates in blue,
purple and scarlet and golden bells between them; over it the
gold-blue-purple-scarlet EPHOD; on his chest the twelve-stone BREASTPLATE in
four rows of three, set in gold; on his head the white linen turban (the
mitre) with its plate of pure gold bearing faint indistinct engraving, no
readable letters. Plain leather sandals. The white linen of the turban is
part of the commanded vestments, not a white garment — his robe is BLUE;
never cream, never a pure-white robe. No halo, no glow.
