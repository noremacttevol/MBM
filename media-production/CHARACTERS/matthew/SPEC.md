## What scripture tells us
- Matt 9:9 — "a man, named Matthew, sitting at the receipt of custom: and he
  saith unto him, Follow me. And he arose, and followed him." A tax collector
  at his booth — a desk man, not a net man.
- Matt 9:10 — the feast in his house with "many publicans and sinners" — a
  man of means, a householder who could set a big table.
- Matt 10:3 — "Matthew the publican" — the label follows him even in the
  apostle list; wealthy, and despised for how he got it.
- Scripture never describes his face; the sheet keeps him a first-century
  Judean, and his trade shows in the details: city softness, careful hands,
  better-kept clothes than the fishermen around him.

## Written description
(Adopts the look shipped throughout build #72 — already on screen, must not
drift.) A well-kept man of about forty with a neatly trimmed dark beard,
warm olive skin unweathered by outdoor labor, dark hair combed and orderly,
intelligent guarded eyes that learn to open, soft careful counting-house
hands — city softness next to the rope-worn fishermen. After his calling the
guardedness gives way to quiet, grateful purpose.

## Standard garments
A finer MUTED TEAL-GREEN wool tunic with a subtle darker woven border
(locked in #72 — never cream, and plainly better cloth than the fishermen's),
a leather belt. Pre-calling scenes only: a leather coin-purse at the belt;
after the calling the purse is gone (in #72's closing still a scroll takes
its place). A deep grey-brown mantle when a cloak is needed, leather sandals.
