# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #134: "Today shalt thou be with me in paradise" (luke-23_today-in-paradise)

THE POINT (Why-Law): two sentences of Jesus, read side by side, widen the two-doors picture.
On Friday He promised the dying thief "today... in paradise"; on Sunday He told Mary He had
"not yet ascended" to the Father. So the paradise He promised was real and good but not yet
the final home — a place of waiting, and the Shepherd is there too. Mercy reached a criminal
in his last minutes. That should change how we grieve.

REVERENCE RULES: the crucifixion is shown with restraint — no gore, no blood, no wounds in
close-up, no agony as spectacle. Prefer near-silhouette against sky, hands, and faces turned
toward one another. Jesus is shown warm and merciful; His face may be shown. The tomb-garden
is full of morning light. Every visible hand joined to a visible arm. No text, letters or
words anywhere in any image.
