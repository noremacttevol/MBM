> Two verses set side by side quietly widen the "one heaven, one chance" picture: the
> thief goes to paradise "today," yet three days later Jesus has "not yet ascended" —
> so paradise is real, good, and not the final home. Mercy has more room and more time.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the road past death is longer and kinder than you were taught —
  mercy reached a thief in his last minute, and paradise is not the last door.
- **The turn:** setting John 20:17 beside Luke 23:43 — "he has not yet gone up to his
  Father," so the paradise he promised was somewhere in between, not the end of the road.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** "one heaven, one chance, one size." Answers the fear that
  a loved one who died "without every box checked" fell through a trapdoor.
- **Whose face carries it:** the dying THIEF (hope breaking through pain), then MARY at
  the tomb. Jesus via master ref / over-the-shoulder; on the cross keep his face away.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Who have you already given up on that God has not?"
