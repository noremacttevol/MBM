**Row:** 124   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Matthew 5:43–46
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 10)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** loving an enemy isn't unnatural and it isn't about what they
  deserve — it's the family likeness; it's how you show whose child you are.
- **The turn:** Jesus's reason — "that ye may be the children of your Father... for he
  maketh his sun to rise on the evil and on the good." The ask stops being unreasonable
  the moment you see the Father already doing it every morning.
- **Wound answered (angry / tribal God who keeps score):** the picture of a God who
  loves his side and hates the enemy. This shows a Father whose sun rises on the cruel
  and the kind alike — "likeness is the point."
- **Whose face carries it:** the FARMER — lifting the enemy's fallen stones, praying
  for the man across the wall by lamplight. Jesus is shown teaching, over-the-shoulder
  onto the listeners.
- **The card question falls out of:** "who is the hardest person in your life to
  bless? What might change if, tonight, you prayed for them — by name?" Lands on the
  one thing. No change needed.
