## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the only one in that courtyard with the right to condemn her,
  wouldn't — and he cleared out every accuser first.
- **The turn:** "He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her" —
  the trap set for him turns back on the accusers, and the courtyard empties one by one.
- **Character shown:** he refuses to be the mob's weapon, exposes self-righteousness
  without a lecture, and gives a shamed woman back her feet and her future.
- **Whose face carries it:** the WOMAN throughout — exposed in the center, then as the
  courtyard empties, then her three words standing up, then uncondemned. The ACCUSERS
  for the drop. Jesus via master ref at the stooping and the red lines.
- **The card question falls out of:** "the only one with the right to condemn her,
  wouldn't — what would it change to hear him say that to you?" No change needed.
