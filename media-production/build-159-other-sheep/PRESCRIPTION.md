## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the Good Shepherd has other sheep the crowd never counted — another
  fold, another record — and they will hear his voice too.
- **The turn:** kv16 / n4 — "other sheep I have, which are not of this fold... one fold,
  and one shepherd." The saying turns from this flock to every scattered sheep.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** other sheep = another fold and another record — the Book of
  Mormon peoples who also heard him. **GL handoff: Book of Mormon.**
- **Whose face carries it:** the listeners' startled faces, and the far-off outsider
  sheep. Jesus only via master ref, over-the-shoulder — never his face in the turn.
- **The card question falls out of:** "When you hear his voice, will you follow?" — lands
  on the one thing.
