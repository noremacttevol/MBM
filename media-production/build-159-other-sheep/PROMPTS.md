# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #159: "Other sheep I have" (john-10_other-sheep)

John 10:14,16. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no
motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Book of Mormon** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk. The note is belonging and gathering: the Shepherd has
sheep the crowd never counted, and he will bring them all. Warmth, tenderness, welcome.

FACE LAW: Jesus IS shown — the SAME master face in every shot where he appears (s1, s2, s3,
s4, s7, s8). Each of those shots carries the byte-identical JESUS LOCK v3 paragraph and a
"REF: jesus-master-ref" line. ONLY Jesus wears cream/off-white; everyone else — and every
shepherd, villager, and distant people — is in darker earth colors, in every prompt. The
two "other fold" shots (s5, s6) have NO divine figure — only distant sheep and far-off
people; God's call is shown as warm light, never a figure.

FLOCK / PEOPLE LOCKS: NEAR LISTENERS (s1) — ordinary Judean villagers of mixed age in
earth-tone wool (brown, dun, olive, ochre, grey), faces shown, attentive; never in cream.
THE OTHER PEOPLE (s5, s6) — people of a distant land, different features and dress but still
ancient and modest, in their own earth-tone robes and mantles (never cream), among their
own flocks of sheep. SHEEP throughout: ordinary woolly sheep, warm off-white fleece (sheep
fleece is fine; the cream-robe rule is about PEOPLE only).

MUST NEVER SHOW: no modern object; no baked-in text, letters or numbers; no bright ring or
aura around anyone's head; no second PERSON in cream or off-white (only Jesus); no gore, no
fear; every image is ONE single unbroken full-frame scene — no panels, no comic strip.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.
