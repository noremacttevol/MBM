# STILL PROMPTS — Video #115: The Ram in the Thicket (genesis-22_the-ram-in-the-thicket)

Genesis 22. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — ten painted stills. BRIDGE/MILK.
CARE FLAGS (CONTENT-CARE §C THE CHILDREN LAW / #166 R,C,G,L): **A CHILD IN DANGER IS NEVER
THE RENDERED IMAGE.** Isaac is NEVER shown bound, on the altar, under a knife or blade, or
in peril of any kind. NO knife, NO raised blade, NO sacrificial threat is depicted or even
implied in an image. The pictures are: the WALK UP, the boy's QUESTION, the RAM, the RELEASE,
the EMBRACE. The whole story's point is "God himself provides the lamb" — GOD NEVER WANTED
THE CHILD. Isaac is safe and whole in every frame. Closing card is an INVITATION, never a threat.

NO DIVINE FIGURE ANYWHERE. Where God's voice stops Abraham or provides, the picture is warm
light from heaven — no figure, no face, no shape, no hand. (No Jesus is shown in any frame;
nobody wears cream or off-white — Abraham and Isaac are in earth colours.)

ABRAHAM LOCK: a dignified elder of about a hundred, warm olive-brown weathered skin, long
full white beard and white hair, in a deep russet-brown ankle-length robe with a tan mantle
— tender, resolute, deeply loving toward his son. ISAAC LOCK: his son, a gentle boy of about
twelve, warm olive-tan skin, dark wavy hair, in a simple dun-brown tunic — trusting, alive,
safe and unharmed in every frame, his face shown. Both faces shown, consistent.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single tall upright vertical scene painted edge to edge, no panels."
Skip any comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.

MUST NEVER SHOW: Isaac bound, on an altar, under a knife or blade, or in any peril; ANY
knife, dagger or raised blade anywhere; God as a figure/face/hand; anyone in cream or
off-white; any modern object; any baked-in text; a multi-panel or comic-strip layout.
