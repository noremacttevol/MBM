## The impact read
- **The one thing:** this was never a story about a God who takes — it is about a
  God who provides, who steps in at the last moment with what we could never provide
  ourselves. He never wanted the child.
- **The turn:** the hand stopped in mid-air and the ram caught in the thicket —
  "God's own provision, ready all along." The story flips from *God demands* to
  *God provides* in that one revealed animal.
- **Wound answered (the God who takes):** the fear of a God who would ask for the
  thing you love most and mean it. The whole cut exists to overturn it — "God never
  wanted the child. He never did."
- **Whose face carries it:** ABRAHAM and the BOY — the boy's trusting question on the
  climb, Abraham's ache and then his weeping relief. God is a voice from the sky, no face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "where do you most need to trust that the Lord
  will provide?" Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
