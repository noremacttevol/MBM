## The impact read
- **The one thing:** knowing *about* him and doing things in his name is not the same
  as knowing him — he wants to be known by you, not just named by you.
- **The turn:** "I never knew you." Not *I never heard of your works* — the whole
  weight lands on the word *knew*. He isn't measuring the size of a list; he's asking
  whether we ever walked with him.
- **Wound answered (dead / performance religion):** the picture of faith as saying the
  right words and racking up works. This reframes it as a relationship — "an invitation
  to be known — to walk with him, not just speak his name."
- **Whose face carries it:** the EARNEST PEOPLE holding up their works, and then the
  one invited to walk close. Jesus speaks (red) but is shown teaching / walking
  alongside, over-the-shoulder — no emotional face-carry needed.
- **The card question falls out of:** "come close, and let him know you." The card is
  the invitation the story lands on. No change needed.
