## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the whole book exists to answer one fear — God is not eager to
  destroy, he is eager to spare; he forgave a city of a hundred thousand strangers
  the moment they turned.
- **The turn:** God relenting — "he saw that they turned from their evil, and he
  relented. The judgment never fell." And the hinge behind the hinge is Jonah's
  confession that he ran because he was afraid God was *too good*.
- **Wound answered (angry God):** the picture of a God itching to burn the wicked.
  Jonah himself carries the wound — he ran from a God he thought "far too forgiving"
  — and God argues him out of it, out loud, to the last line.
- **Whose face carries it:** JONAH throughout — fleeing, in the fish, furious on the
  hill — and the repenting CITY at the turn. God is a voice, no face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what would it change to believe God is that
  quick to forgive?" Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
