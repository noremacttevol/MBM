> the Son of man STANDING at the Father's right hand → they rush him out → his face like

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** you can face the worst thing that will ever happen to you with your
  eyes on someone standing up for you — and with forgiveness still in your mouth.
- **The turn:** Acts 7:56 — "the Son of man standing on the right hand of God." Stephen
  looks up and sees it; the whole room falls away. That vision is the hinge.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the Godhead — the Son distinct from the Father, embodied,
  *standing* and seen at his right hand. Hands off to **Gospel Library → Godhead**.
- **Whose face carries it:** STEPHEN throughout — the calm inside the rage, the angel
  face, the dying prayer. Jesus appears only inside the vision (beat 5), standing at a
  glory distance via master ref — never an emotional close-up.
- **The card question falls out of:** "He saw the Son of man standing to receive him.
  You can face your end with that same peace." Lands on the one thing. No change.
