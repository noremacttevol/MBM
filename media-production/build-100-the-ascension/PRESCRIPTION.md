## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he didn't leave them staring at the sky — he left them a job, a
  companion (the Holy Ghost), and a promise to come back the same way he went.
- **The turn:** the ascension itself — lifted up, a cloud receiving him out of sight
  — answered immediately by the angels' promise that he'll return in like manner.
  That pairing is the hinge.
- **Character shown:** a Lord who redirects "when?" into "go," commissions his own,
  and does not abandon them.
- **Whose face carries it:** the ELEVEN DISCIPLES — receiving the charge, gazing up,
  stunned. Jesus's face via master ref before the lift; during the ascension shoot
  from below / over the disciples' shoulders onto the rising figure and cloud.
- **The card question falls out of:** "He's coming back the same way he left. Until
  then, you're not alone — and you're not without purpose." Lands on the one thing.
