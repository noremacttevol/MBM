## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God gathers what was scattered — two records, two peoples, made one
  in HIS hand; the second record was always part of the plan.
- **The turn:** kv17 / kv19 — "they shall be one in mine hand." The two separate records
  become one, and it is God's own hand that binds them.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** two records — the Bible and the Book of Mormon — one witness in
  God's hand. **GL handoff: Book of Mormon.**
- **Whose face carries it:** Ezekiel acting out the sign, and the onlookers who stop and
  stare. The LORD is hand/voice (green captions), never a face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "will you take them both up and read?" — lands on
  the one thing.
