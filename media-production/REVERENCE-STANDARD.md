# REVERENCE STANDARD — the first law above all other laws (2026-07-16)

> Cameron's charge: these videos are made FOR Jesus, to help people love Him and come
> to His gospel. They will be handed to seekers at the most tender, most controversial
> moments of His life. They must be 100% right — reverent and true — before they are
> ever "done." This standard outranks speed, volume, and every convenience. If a still
> or a video fails this, it does not ship, no matter how far along it is.

## THE ONE QUESTION (asked of every still, every video, before approval)
**"Does this honor Jesus and tell His story exactly right?"**
If the answer is not a clear YES, it is rejected and remade. "Probably fine" = rejected.

## THE FOUR TESTS (all must pass)
1. **REVERENT** — Jesus is depicted with dignity and warmth. No mockery, no cartoonish
   silliness, no anything that would make a believer wince. His face matches the master
   reference every time (Middle Eastern, warm brown eyes, only-He-cream, no halo).
2. **TRUE TO SCRIPTURE** — the scene matches what the Bible actually says. Jesus's
   spoken words are EXACT KJV. Nothing invented that contradicts the text. The miracle
   is the miracle (e.g. He walks ON the water, not in it).
3. **RIGHT IN SPIRIT** — the story lands the way Jesus would want: mercy is spoken out
   loud in every judgment story; the hurting are shown with dignity; the close is always
   an INVITATION, never fear or shame. Meets the seeker with the love that draws them in.
4. **CLEAN CRAFT** — no bad anatomy (extra hands/fingers/limbs, duplicated people), no
   baked-in text or gibberish letters, no wrong-era objects, correct 9:16 frame.

## CONTENT-CARE (the controversial moments — handle as holy ground)
The cross, the betrayal, the tomb: reverent distance, faces and light, NEVER wounds/
gore/violence in focus. The point is His love and victory, not His suffering on display.

## THE WORKFLOW THIS CHANGES
- Stills are painted (continuously, as now).
- BEFORE any video is built, every story's stills pass THIS standard — reviewed against
  the four tests, bad ones flagged for Cameron, remade until they pass.
- ONLY stills that pass become videos.
- Nothing marked "approved" or "done" unless it has genuinely passed — a machine may
  NOT self-approve past this standard. Final yes on the hardest stories is Cameron's.

This is why we build: so that people meet the real Jesus and come to love Him.
Everything above is in service of that. Honor it.
