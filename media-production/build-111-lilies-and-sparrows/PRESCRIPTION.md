# PRESCRIPTION — build-111-lilies-and-sparrows

**Row:** 111   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Matthew 6:26–34
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 10)

> Bridge shelf. The wound is **"God is angry / distant and has forgotten me."** The answer
> is what Jesus points at: the Father feeds the sparrows and clothes the wildflowers, and
> you are worth so much more to him than these — not one falls unnoticed.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the same Father who feeds every sparrow and dresses every wildflower
  has not forgotten you — he is not scolding your worry, he is gently loosening your grip
  on tomorrow and offering to carry it with you.
- **The turn:** "if God so clothe the grass of the field... shall he not much more clothe
  you?" The how-much-more that swings from birds and flowers to the anxious person is the
  hinge.
- **Wound answered:** "God is angry / has forgotten me." The answer — not one sparrow falls
  unnoticed, and you matter to him far more than a sparrow.
- **Whose face carries it:** the WORRIED LISTENER / the person — the anxious face, the grip
  loosening, the breath at the end. Jesus teaching is shown via master ref /
  over-the-shoulder.
- **The card question falls out of:** "What worry could you set down today, and trust him
  with instead?" — lands on the one thing. No change needed.
