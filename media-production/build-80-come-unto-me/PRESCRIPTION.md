# PRESCRIPTION — build-80-come-unto-me

**Row:** 80   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Matthew 11:28–30
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 8)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he doesn't promise to empty your arms — he promises to take
  the other side of what you're carrying, so it's never carried alone again.
- **The turn:** [j2] "Take my yoke upon you" — the offer shifts from *come rest* to
  *we pull together.* The yoke is built for two, and he steps into the empty half.
- **Character shown:** gentleness and humility — "I am meek and lowly in heart";
  a God who says come *while* you're still under the load, not once you've handled it.
- **Whose face carries it:** the worn-out person / the crowd. Jesus via master ref
  on the offer.
- **The card question falls out of:** "he's offering to take the other side of it." No change.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 8 stills
1. [n0a] a crowd of worn-out people, heads down, trudging through an ordinary day — agrees: "worn down by work, by rules, by just getting through the day"
2. [n0b + j1] Jesus turning to them with the gentle offer (his face via master ref) — "Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden"
3. [n0c] one of them still bent under the load, invited while still carrying it — "come while you are still under it"
4. [n1] a wooden yoke across two oxen pulling one load together — "the wooden beam that let two oxen pull a load together"
5. [j2] Jesus offering his yoke, gesturing to the empty side — "Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me"
6. [j3 + n1b] Jesus stepping into the empty side beside the person — "He is stepping into the empty side of the one you are already pulling"
7. [n2b] the two of them pulling the load together, the weight shared — "get under the load with you, so it never has to be carried alone again"
8. [card] warm hold on the shared yoke / the offered hand for the closing question — the card line
