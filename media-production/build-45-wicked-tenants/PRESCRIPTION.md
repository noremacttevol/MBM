# PRESCRIPTION — build-45-wicked-tenants

**Row:** 45   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Mark 12:1–11
**Tier:** T3 Full Arc   **Target length:** 120–165s   **Pictures:** 16–24 (planned: 15 — see deviation)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** an owner who keeps sending — after the first, the tenth, even the
  worst — is not looking for a reason to be done with you. This is a story about a
  patience that keeps reaching out long past the point anyone else would.
- **The turn:** [jv6/n8] the owner sending his one beloved son last — "They will
  reverence my son." Everything the patience had built rides on that hope.
- **Character shown:** a God who will not stop sending, told to the very men about to
  reject him, and still calling them in.
- **Whose face carries it:** the SERVANTS/MESSENGERS walking up the road, and the
  SON coming last; the OWNER's grief and hope. Jesus is the teller, not a face in the
  parable's staged beats.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what would you do with a patience like that?"
