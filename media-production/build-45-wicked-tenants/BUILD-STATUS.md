* **R — the violence is never the picture.** No blow is ever shown landing, no weapon is

  shaken with a torn cloak and an empty basket (s5); the son's death is carried entirely by

  faithful workers, with the old tenants only small distant figures walking away (s11). Every
  frame passes "would a parent let a ten-year-old see this?"

  reason (s5-s7) and finally sends the son he loves most (s8). The ending is pure hope: the
  rejected stone becomes the cornerstone (s12). Verse-card truth (PAIRING-LIST #45, Mark
  12:6): God keeps sending. The closing card is an invitation about patience, never a fear-
  question.
