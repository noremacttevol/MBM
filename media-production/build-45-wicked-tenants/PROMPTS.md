# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #45: The Wicked Tenants (mark-12_wicked-tenants)

Mark 12:1-11. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — thirteen painted stills, no motion clips.

CARE FLAGS: **R — restraint (violence) AND J — mercy-in-judgment (CONTENT-CARE.md).**
The violence in this parable (servants beaten, the son killed) is NEVER the picture.
No blow is ever shown landing, no weapon is ever raised to strike, no wound, no blood,
no body — the aftermath and the witnesses carry the weight instead: a messenger walking
home shaken with an empty basket (s5), the father's grief at the shut gate (s10). QC every
frame: **would a parent let a ten-year-old see this?** The mercy that carries the video is
the OWNER'S PATIENCE — he keeps sending long past reason, and finally sends the son he
loves most (s8) — and the ending is pure hope: the rejected stone becomes the cornerstone
(s12). The reckoning is the vineyard passing to people who WILL give its fruit (s11), never
a scene of the tenants being destroyed.

Jesus appears in only TWO shots — s1 and s13, the FRAME where he tells the parable in the
temple. In both the camera is directly behind him, the back of his head to us, his face
never shown, never visible, at any angle. The parable pictures (s2-s12) have NO divine
figure. **The vineyard owner is NEVER called "lord" or "master" in these prompts — he is
"the vineyard owner" — so nothing but the from-behind frame figure reads as the Lord.**

JESUS LOCK (identical wording in s1 and s13): a first-century Middle-Eastern man, warm
olive-brown skin, LONG dark brown-black hair past the shoulders, one plain undyed off-white
cream wool robe, bare-headed, no hood; no glow, no bright ring, no outlining light; an ordinary solid
man. The camera is always directly behind him and his face is never shown, never visible,
at any angle.

TEMPLE CROWD LOCK (s1, s13) — the listeners in the temple court: some chief priests, scribes
and elders in fuller formal wool robes of deep indigo-blue and dark maroon with woven
prayer-fringes and well-kept dark beards, and ordinary Jerusalem people around them in dun,
faded brown and olive. Every one of them is clearly darker than cream; not one wears
off-white or cream. Faces shown, listening.

VINEYARD OWNER LOCK (s2, s3, s5, s6, s7, s10, s11) — a dignified, prosperous older man of
about sixty, warm and patient, a full grey-white beard, in a fuller wool robe of deep
teal-blue with a woven deep-red border, clearly NOT cream. His face IS shown, kind and
long-suffering — a generous man, not a harsh one.

SON LOCK (s8, s9) — the vineyard owner's beloved son: an earnest, gentle young man of about
twenty-two, short dark hair, clean-shaven or the lightest young beard, in a good deep
forest-green wool robe with a simple woven sash, clearly NOT cream and NOT off-white. His
face IS shown, open, trusting and willing.

MESSENGER LOCK (s4, s5, s6) — the servants the owner sends: ordinary working messengers in
plain dun and faded-brown travel tunics with a cloth shoulder-bag and a woven collecting
basket, faces shown. In the aftermath shot they are weary and shaken but show NO wounds,
NO blood — only a dusty torn cloak, a bowed head, an empty basket.

TENANTS LOCK (s4, s6, s9, s11) — the husbandmen who work the vineyard: rough, weathered
field workers in dark grey-brown and muddy-olive work tunics, hard unfriendly faces, a
surly and menacing posture, clearly NOT cream. They are never shown striking anyone, never
raising a weapon to strike, never standing over a hurt person — their threat is carried only
by posture, folded arms, blocking the gate, and cold expressions. In s11 the wicked tenants
are only small downcast figures walking away in the background.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single continuous scene painted edge to edge." Skip any
comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.
