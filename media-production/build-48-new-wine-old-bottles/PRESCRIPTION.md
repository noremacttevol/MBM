# PRESCRIPTION — build-48-new-wine-old-bottles

**Row:** 48   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Mark 2:18–22
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 8)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God was not tinkering with the old rules — he was pouring out
  something brand new and full of joy, and the only thing he asks is a heart soft
  enough to hold it.
- **The turn:** [jv19/n3] "the groom is here" — under a question about fasting, the
  quiet, staggering claim that he himself is the bridegroom, and this is a wedding.
- **Character shown:** joy — the God who answers a rule with a feast, standing right
  there to give the new thing.
- **Whose face carries it:** the SINCERE RELIGIOUS MEN who came asking, and the
  everyday pictures (the mender, the wineskins). Jesus is seen answering, not staged
  inside the two object lessons.
- **The card question falls out of:** "where is he offering you something new, if you
  will only make room for it?"

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 8 stills
1. [n1/s18/n1b] sincere religious men coming to Jesus with an honest question about fasting — "Why do the disciples of John and of the Pharisees fast, but thy disciples fast not?"
2. [n2] good men going hungry to draw near God — and his disciples not fasting at all — "It looked careless"
3. [jv19/n3] the wedding picture — nobody starves while the groom is in the room; the groom is here — "the only fitting thing to do is celebrate"
4. [jv20/n4] Jesus looking past the moment to the day the groom is taken away — the cross — "He is looking straight past this moment to the cross"
5. [n5/jv21/n5b] the mending picture — a stiff new patch on a worn coat tearing the hole wider — "it tears the hole wider than it was before you started"
6. [n6/jv22/n7] the wineskins — new wine bursts a hard old skin; fresh wine in a fresh skin that can stretch — "new wine must be put into new bottles"
7. [n7/n8] the quiet claim — God pouring out something brand new and alive, standing right there to give it — "Not a patch on the old religion. A whole new thing"
8. [card] hold for the closing question about making room for something new
