## The impact read
- **The one thing:** who may ascend the hill of the Lord and stand in his holy place is not decided by bloodline or rank, but by clean hands and a pure heart.
- **The turn:** the answer to the opening question (s2/n1) — "He that hath clean hands, and a pure heart" — the psalm turns from *who is worthy?* to *this is who.* That answer is the hinge.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** temple worthiness — clean hands and a pure heart to enter the holy place. Hands off to **Gospel Library → Temple Worthiness.**
- **Whose face carries it:** the worthy worshipper ascending — hands held clean, heart set right. The King of glory is carried by the lifted gates and the glory/light, not a face-closeup.
- **The card question / close falls out of:** "The door is open to the one with a clean heart. Come and stand in his presence." — lands straight on the one thing.
