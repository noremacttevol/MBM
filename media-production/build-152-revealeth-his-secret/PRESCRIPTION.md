# PRESCRIPTION — build-152-revealeth-his-secret

**Row:** 152   **Shelf:** Member   **Scripture:** Amos 3:7–8
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 9)

> A verse about how God works, carried by one witness — the herdsman-prophet Amos.
> The beats follow the message from the hills, to the entrusting, to the town gate,
> to the warned people. God is shown as voice and light, never a face.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God never moves against his people in the dark — before he acts
  he tells a living prophet, so no one who listens is ever caught by surprise.
- **The turn:** kv7 — "he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets." The
  verse pivots from a private word on the hills to a public warning meant to save.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** God still calls living prophets and reveals his will through
  them — the pattern did not stop with Amos. **GL handoff: Prophets.**
- **Whose face carries it:** Amos, the plain herdsman-prophet — his face receiving the
  word, and the warned people's faces turning to listen. No face on the LORD.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Are you listening?" — lands straight on the one thing.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 9 stills
1. [n1] Amos out on the hills with his flock, a plain working man — agrees: "a herdsman, out with his flock on the hills"
2. [n2] the herdsman stilled, the word of the Lord coming to him on the wind — "the word of the Lord came to him... a voice carried on the wind"
3. [n3,kv7] the prophet entrusted, standing above the town he must warn — God confiding his plan first — "he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets"
4. [n4] storm clouds gathering on the horizon while the forewarned prophet hurries to warn — "warns his household before the storm arrives"
5. [n5] the prophet at the town gate and market, saying it out loud whether the crowd wants it or not — "goes down to... the gate and the market, and he says out loud"
6. [n6] people who heard beginning to turn, mend, and come home in time — mercy, not threat — "A people who are warned can still turn... come home in time"
7. [kv8] a lion on the hills, having roared; heads turning in fear — "The lion hath roared, who will not fear?"
8. [n7] the prophet unable to stay silent, compelled to speak the message — "the prophet cannot stay silent... a lion roaring close beside you"
9. [n8,card] a face turned upward, listening for God's word — warm hold for the closing question — "He still opens his plans to living prophets... Are you listening?"
