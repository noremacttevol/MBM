# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #152: "He revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets" (amos-3_revealeth-his-secret)

Amos 3:7-8. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no
motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Prophets** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: none — GREEN, plain milk (CONTENT-CARE.md). Nothing violent or fearful. Milk
framing: this is GOOD news — God is not silent or arbitrary. Before he acts, he warns his
people by revealing his plans to prophets, so no one who listens is ever caught in the
dark. Warmth and reassurance, an open invitation to listen, never dread.

CHRIST IS NEVER DEPICTED in any frame. God is shown ONLY as warm light from the open sky —
never a figure, never a face, never a shape, never a hand. No divine person is drawn.

THE POINT (Why-Law): God calls an ordinary herdsman, not a king, to carry his word (s1);
the word of the Lord comes to him, given not invented (s2); before God acts he opens his
plan to a trusted servant (s3, s4 — Amos 3:7); the prophet declares it aloud to the people
at the gate (s5); a warned people can still turn and be ready — this is mercy (s6); once
God has spoken the prophet cannot stay silent (s7, Amos 3:8); and God still speaks this
way, so anyone willing can listen (s8).

PROPHET LOCK (every shot — the same man throughout): a weathered older Middle-Eastern
prophet of about fifty-five, sun-browned skin, a full grey-streaked dark beard, deep-set
kind eyes, grave and gentle, wearing a simple rough herdsman's tunic and coarse cloak of
undyed DUN and dark BROWN wool with a leather belt and a plain wooden shepherd's staff —
dark earth tones, never white, cream, off-white or pale. His face is always shown.
Consistent face, beard and dress in every frame.

TOWNSFOLK LOCK (s5, s6 only): ordinary Israelite men, women and children of every age in
undyed DARKER earth-tone wool (dun, faded brown, olive, ochre, grey), faces shown, honest;
never in off-white or cream, never resembling the prophet.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or shape of God or Christ anywhere — God is only
warm light; no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no church buildings, steeples,
crosses, logos, or any modern object; no baked-in text, letters or numbers; no gore, no
fear, no one shamed or in peril; no second person dressed in off-white or cream.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.
