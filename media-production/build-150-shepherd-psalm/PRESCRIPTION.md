# PRESCRIPTION — build-150-shepherd-psalm

**Row:** 150   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Psalm 23
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Length:** pace to the story (T2 band 80–120s)
**Pictures:** ~11 beats (T2 band 10–15)

> Bridge wound: **the God you have to run from — absent in the dark, and only ever
> chasing you down to punish.** The inherited picture is a shepherd who leaves you
> alone in the valley and whose pursuit is a threat. The psalm answers it: it is
> *goodness and mercy* that follow you all your days, and in the darkest valley he
> gets closer, not further — "thou art with me."

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** what is chasing you all your days isn't judgment — it's goodness
  and mercy; and in the dark valley the Shepherd is nearer, not gone.
- **The turn:** the pronoun shift David makes in the valley — up to verse 4 he says
  *He*; in the shadow of death he starts saying *You*. He gets closer to the Shepherd
  in the dark, not further away. That's the hinge of the whole psalm.
- **Wound answered:** the angry/absent-God wound — the following isn't a hunter, it's
  mercy; the valley isn't abandonment, it's "thou art with me."
- **Whose face carries it:** the SHEEP / the TRAVELER in the valley, and DAVID the
  shepherd-king who wrote it. The Shepherd is shown as a shepherd (not Jesus's face —
  milk; a viewer who later learns who the Shepherd is finds the video was right).
- **The card question falls out of:** "Let Him lead." Lands on the one thing. No change needed.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — ~11 stills
1. [n0] David the shepherd-king who once kept sheep, writing a song about being shepherded — agrees: "a shepherd-king wrote a song about being shepherded himself"
2. [s1 / n0b] the Shepherd leading his flock — nothing lacking — "The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want"
3. [s2 / n1a] sheep lying down in green pastures beside still water — "He maketh me to lie down in green pastures... beside the still waters"
4. [s3a / n1b] a rescued lamb carried, restored — "He restoreth my soul"
5. [s3b] the Shepherd leading along a right path — "he leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for his name's sake"
6. [s4 / n3] the Shepherd and sheep walking through a dark ravine — rod and staff, close beside them (the HINGE — He becomes You here) — "though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death... thou art with me"
7. [s5a / n4a] a table spread in the open with enemies at the edges — "Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies"
8. [s5b / n4b] oil poured on the head, a cup running over — "thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over"
9. [s6a / n5a] the traveler walking on, goodness and mercy following close behind — "goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life"
10. [s6b / n5b] arriving home — the house of the LORD, forever — "and I will dwell in the house of the LORD for ever"
11. [card] warm hold on the Shepherd and the sheep for the closing line — the card line
