# PRESCRIPTION — build-127-the-strait-gate

**Row:** 127   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** Matthew 7:13–14
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 7)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the narrow gate is not God keeping people out — it's a real
  door standing open, worth finding, and nobody walks through either gate by accident.
- **The turn:** "He wasn't describing geography. He was describing a decision" (n3a) —
  the two roads stop being a threat and become a choice standing in front of the viewer.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** *the exclusionary / fear God* — the inherited picture
  of a God who rigged salvation so only a chosen few squeak through and everyone else
  is doomed. This cut answers it: "few find it" means the way must be *found*, not that
  most are shut out; both gates stand open right now and the narrow one leads to life.
- **Whose face carries it:** the everyman traveler at the fork (the one deciding).
  Jesus is face-shown via master ref only, framed over the crowd / at a distance —
  never a close emotional Jesus face. The human beat is the traveler choosing.
- **The card question falls out of:** "the narrow way is worth finding… Step through" —
  lands on the one thing. No change needed.
