## The impact read
- **The one thing:** honest doubt is not the opposite of faith — Thomas named the
  exact proof he needed, and Jesus walked in and offered him that exact proof, word
  for word, without one word of rebuke.
- **The turn:** eight days later Jesus turns straight to Thomas and offers the
  precise proof he'd demanded — "Reach hither thy finger." That is the hinge.
- **Character shown:** a Savior who meets doubt with open hands, not anger, and
  steps toward the doubter.
- **Whose face carries it:** THOMAS — his refusal, his demand, his confession.
  Jesus's wounded hands and side via master ref; his face over-the-shoulder onto
  Thomas.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Bring him your doubt. He can handle it."
  Lands directly on the one thing.
