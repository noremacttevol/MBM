# PRESCRIPTION — build-50-noblemans-son

**Row:** 50   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** John 4:46–54
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 12)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he healed a boy he never touched, with a word that was already
  true before anyone could prove it — his word can carry a whole day's journey.
- **The turn:** [jv50/n7] "Go thy way; thy son liveth" — no trip, no hand on the boy,
  just a word; and the man believes it and turns for home carrying nothing but that.
- **Character shown:** authority at a distance, and an invitation to trust before the
  proof arrives — "except ye see signs and wonders" answered by a father who doesn't
  need the show.
- **Whose face carries it:** the FATHER — the powerful man stripped of pride, then the
  long walk with no proof, then the servants running to meet him. Jesus speaks the
  word; the father's face carries the faith.
- **The card question falls out of:** "how far do you think his word can reach? what
  would you trust him with tonight?"

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 12 stills
1. [n1] Jesus back in Cana where he turned the water into wine; word of it spreading — "word of it was spreading fast"
2. [n2] a royal official at his son's bedside in Capernaum, powerless, the boy burning with fever — "his boy was burning with fever, and the doctors had run out of answers"
3. [n3] the powerful man dropping everything, hurrying a day's walk uphill on foot to Cana — "hurrying on foot to find a village carpenter's son"
4. [n4] the father finding Jesus, begging him to come down before it is too late — "A father with no pride left, only fear"
5. [jv48/n5] Jesus reaching past the man's panic — "Except ye see signs and wonders, ye will not believe"
6. [n6/s49/n6b] the father, not arguing, breaking — "Sir, come down ere my child die"
7. [jv50/n7] "Go thy way; thy son liveth" — no trip, no touch; the man believes, turns, and starts home — "the man believed him, turned, and started home"
8. [n8] the long day's walk home with no proof in his hands — "nothing to hold but a stranger's word that his son was already well"
9. [n9/s51/n9b] the next day, still on the road, his servants running to meet him — "Thy son liveth"
10. [n10/s52/n10b] the father asking when — "the seventh hour the fever left him" — the exact hour Jesus spoke — "It had already been done while he was still walking"
11. [n11] the man and his whole household believing; the word true the entire way home — "his whole household believed with him"
12. [card] hold for the closing question about how far his word can reach
