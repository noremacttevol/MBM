# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #40: The Friend at Midnight (luke-11_friend-at-midnight)

Luke 11:1-13. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — sixteen painted stills, no motion clips.

TIME OF DAY IS A HARD SPLIT, and it is stated inside every single prompt.
The FRAME of the story (s1, s2, s12, s13, s14, s15) is MORNING — s1/s2 in the pale
first light just after sunrise, s12/s13/s14/s15 in full warm morning. The PARABLE
(s3 through s11) and the closing image (s16) are DEEP MIDNIGHT: a black sky, a thin
sliver of moon, cold stars, a dark blue-black village fast asleep, and one single
source of warm light in the whole picture — the small orange flame of a clay oil
lamp. Luke 11:5 says midnight, so midnight is what gets painted. (Time-of-Day Law.)

Verses 5-8 are a PARABLE Jesus is telling. Inside the parable there is no divine
figure of any kind: the householder, the traveller, the sleeping neighbour and his
family are ordinary people, drawn fully, faces shown. Jesus himself appears in only
FOUR shots — s1, s2, s12 and s15 — and in every one of them the camera stands
directly behind him, the back of his head to us, never any view of his face.

JESUS LOCK (identical wording in s1, s2, s12 and s15; the project-wide look standard):
a first-century Middle-Eastern man, warm olive-brown skin, LONG dark brown-black hair
falling well past the shoulders, one plain undyed off-white cream wool robe (a simple
Judean tunic with a plain mantle, the same garment in every shot), bare-headed with no
hood; no glow, no bright ring, no outlining light of any kind; an ordinary solid man in ordinary
morning daylight. The camera is always directly behind him and his face is never shown.

DISCIPLES LOCK (s1, s2, s12, s15) — eight ordinary Galilean working men, weathered and
sunburnt, in plain wool tunics of DUN, FADED BROWN and OLIVE, each falling to mid-calf
with long sleeves and a soft folded cloth sash at the waist. Among them: one heavy-set
older fisherman with a thick grey-streaked beard, and one young man barely twenty with a
smooth chin and no beard at all. The same eight men in all four shots. Their faces ARE
shown, consistent throughout.

ONLY JESUS WEARS CREAM (build law, added after the first pass of s1). Every one of the
eight men wears a clearly DARKER earth-coloured tunic — dun, faded brown or olive —
plainly darker than the one off-white cream wool robe worn by the figure in the
foreground. No second man in the frame is dressed in off-white or cream. Why: the first
generation of s1 painted the disciple who steps forward in a near-white robe, bearded and
centred with one hand outstretched — a Jesus-shaped figure, which is exactly the
confusion the Face Law exists to prevent. One cream robe per frame, and it is always the
man seen from behind.

HOUSEHOLDER LOCK (s3, s4, s5, s6, s8, s9, s10, s11) — the man who goes out at midnight.
A lean Middle-Eastern man of about thirty-five, short dark curly hair, a close-cropped
black beard, wearing one plain undyed oatmeal wool tunic that falls to mid-calf with
LONG sleeves to the wrists and a soft folded cloth sash wound at the waist — no short
tunic, no bare knees, no buckled belt — and plain leather sandals. He carries a small
round clay oil lamp with one single orange flame. His face IS shown, consistent in
every shot. He is a decent, ordinary, working man, never comic.

TRAVELLER LOCK (s3, s4, s11) — a road-worn Middle-Eastern man of about forty, black
hair going grey, a full grey-streaked beard, wearing a dusty brown travelling cloak over
a dun-coloured ankle-length tunic with long sleeves, a worn leather satchel on a strap
across his body, and a wooden walking staff. His sandals are thick with road dust and he
is plainly exhausted. His face IS shown, consistent in every shot.

NEIGHBOUR LOCK (s7, s10) — a broad, heavy-set Middle-Eastern man of about forty-five,
thick black hair flattened on one side from sleeping on it, a heavy black beard, wearing
one plain undyed cream linen sleeping tunic that falls all the way to his ankles with
long sleeves to the wrists and no sash — the clothes he has slept in. He is a decent
tired man with a real reason to stay in bed, never a sneering villain and never comic.
His face IS shown, consistent in both shots.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.

Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head (anatomy guard). Every
prompt ends with "One single continuous scene painted edge to edge." Skip any
comic-strip or multi-panel variant the model returns.

Their faces are painted fully and clearly, hungry and hopeful. Cool pale dawn light, dew on the grass. Every figure has two arms, two hands, two legs and one head.
