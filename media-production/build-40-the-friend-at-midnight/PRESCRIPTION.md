# PRESCRIPTION — build-40-the-friend-at-midnight

**Row:** 40   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Luke 11:1–13
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 20 — see deviation)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** God is not the reluctant sleeper you have to wear down — your
  Father was never asleep at all, and the best thing he has to give was never bread,
  it is himself.
- **The turn:** [n14a→n14b] the reveal that the neighbor is *not* a picture of God —
  he is the contrast. "If even a worn-out man behind a barred door will get up for
  you, what will your Father do, who was never asleep?" That flip is the hinge.
- **Character shown:** an eager, always-awake, fatherly generosity — the God who
  answers.
- **Whose face carries it:** the MAN AT THE DOOR (begging in the dark) and the
  DISCIPLES listening. Jesus's face only as the teacher framing the parable — never
  in the parable's staged beats.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what would you ask God for tonight, if you
  knew for certain he was awake?" — lands straight on the one thing.

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 20 stills
1. [s1] Jesus praying alone in a certain place, a disciple watching from a distance — agrees: "as he was praying in a certain place"
2. [n1] the disciple stepping up to ask him — "they asked him for the thing they wanted most. Teach us how to do that"
3. [n2/j0/n3] Jesus teaching them the prayer, forming the first word — "When ye pray, say, Our Father which art in heaven"
4. [n3/n4] the parable opens: a village road at night, a weary friend arriving out of the dark — "a friend really could turn up at midnight, starving"
5. [n5] the host opening his door to the road-worn friend — "a man opens his door at midnight to a friend who has walked all day"
6. [n5] the host turning to his own shelf — bare, not a crumb — "there is nothing on it. Not a crumb"
7. [n6] the host lifting his little clay lamp, stepping into the sleeping village — "he picks up his little clay lamp and walks out into a sleeping village"
8. [jv5/j1] the man at the neighbor's door, calling up in the dark for three loaves — "Friend, lend me three loaves"
9. [n7] close on the man begging in the dark for bread he will not eat himself — "begging for bread he will not eat"
10. [n8] inside: the one-room house, the whole family asleep on one platform behind the heavy bar — "the whole family slept on one platform... behind a heavy bar"
11. [j2/n9] the neighbor's tired voice from the bed, refusing — "the door is now shut, and my children are with me in bed"
12. [n10] the man knocking again, louder — shutters banging open up the street, dogs barking — "Shutters bang open up the street. Dogs start barking. And he keeps knocking"
13. [n11] close on the man's shameless face — not too proud to look a fool — "He was not too proud to look like a fool"
14. [j3/n12] the neighbor risen at last, handing an armful of loaves out the door — "he will rise and give him as many as he needeth"
15. [n13] the weary stranger eating in the dark house, a meal he never knew was fought for — "a tired stranger ate a meal he never knew somebody had fought for"
16. [n14a] the WRONG picture: God imagined as the annoyed sleeper behind a barred door — "God is the man behind the door. Annoyed. Asleep"
17. [n14b/j4] THE TURN — the Father who was never asleep, his door already open, light spilling out — "what will your Father do, when he was never asleep at all?"
18. [jv11/n16b] a father and his child — the loaf-shaped river stone, the egg-shaped scorpion, refused — "a scorpion curled pale and round like an egg"
19. [j5/n17/n18] the Father holding out the best gift — himself, the Holy Spirit; the light already on inside — "the best thing he has to give... is himself"
20. [card] warm hold on the lit doorway for the closing question

## Deviations from the band
Stepped to 20 pictures because the narration is unusually long — it frames the
Lord's Prayer *and* the midnight-friend parable *and* the ask-seek-knock teaching
*and* the good-gifts (stone/serpent/scorpion) teaching in one runtime. The honest
beat read lands ~20, above the T2 10–15 band; by length this reads closer to a T3.
Trusting the beats over the band per the SYSTEM doc.
