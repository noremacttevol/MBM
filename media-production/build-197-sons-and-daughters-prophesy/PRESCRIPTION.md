## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the promised outpouring names exactly the people a listener
  assumes are left out — daughters, servants, the young, the old — and leaves nobody out.
- **The turn:** Joel 2:29 — "and ALSO upon the servants and upon the handmaids." The
  promise keeps reaching further than you'd expect, right down to the overlooked.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the Spirit poured out on all in the last days. Hands off to
  **Gospel Library: Revelation in the Last Days**.
- **Whose face carries it:** the ordinary, diverse crowd receiving the Spirit; Peter
  standing at Pentecost. Not God.
- **The card question falls out of:** "the promise was all flesh — that includes you."
  No change needed.
