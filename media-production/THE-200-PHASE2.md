## IX. Post-signal: the Restoration track (181–200)

**Never surfaced until a person's own words show (a) belief that God is good
and (b) openness to God still speaking — per the BOM law. Serves the member
track from day one.** These are the payoff videos: the same Jesus, the same
voice, on another shore.

181. Night without darkness — 3 Ne 1 — The sign of his birth reached the other hemisphere; no people were left out of the announcement.
182. The voice in the darkness — 3 Ne 9–10 — After the destruction: "how oft have I gathered you as a hen gathereth her chickens" — the SAME sentence he wept over Jerusalem (#100), same voice, other sheep.
183. "Behold my Beloved Son" — 3 Ne 11 — The Father's voice introduces him a third time (baptism, transfiguration, Bountiful): the pattern completes.
184. One by one — 3 Ne 11:14–15 — Twenty-five hundred people, and every single one touched the wounds personally. God does not do crowds; he does persons — at scale.
185. "Have ye any that are sick? Bring them hither" — 3 Ne 17 — He healed every one, and this time nobody had to press through a crowd or dig through a roof.
186. He blessed the children one by one — 3 Ne 17 — And angels descended and encircled them; the disciples who once blocked children (#89) redeemed forever.
187. "And behold, my joy is full" — and he wept — 3 Ne 17 — The Man of Sorrows weeping for JOY over his people. Pairs with Lazarus (#17): the God who feels.
188. The sacrament at Bountiful — 3 Ne 18 — The same emblems, the same remembrance, hemisphere two.
189. The prayer that could not be written — 3 Ne 19 — Words so great "they cannot be written"; some things exceed language.
190. "Ye are they of whom I said: other sheep I have" — 3 Ne 15 — The payoff of John 10:16 (#156), in his own voice. The seed becomes a tree.
191. The brother of Jared sees his finger — Ether 3 — The premortal Christ shows a body of spirit in the form of a man: he had a form before Bethlehem.
192. Alma the Younger — Mosiah 27 / Alma 36 — An angel for a persecutor; the worst enemy of the church became its greatest voice (Paul's story, other continent).
193. Enos's wrestle — Enos 1 — A hunter prays all day and into the night, and hears: "thy sins are forgiven thee... because of thy faith in Christ."
194. King Lamoni and Abish — Alma 19 — Light overwhelms a violent king; a servant woman who believed in secret becomes the pivotal witness.
195. They buried their weapons — Alma 24 — Converted killers who chose to die rather than kill again; the most radical repentance story in scripture.
196. Abinadi's fire — Mosiah 13–17 — "Ye have not power to slay me until I have delivered my message" — one voice, one convert, everything downstream.
197. Enoch sees God weep — Moses 7 — "How is it that THOU canst weep?" — the heavens weep over human suffering. The crown of everything the Lazarus video began: our God cries because he loves.
198. "This is my work and my glory" — Moses 1:39 — God's self-declared job description: "to bring to pass the immortality and eternal life of MAN." His glory is US. The direct answer to every theology where God's glory requires human loss.
199. Liberty Jail — D&C 121–122 — From the worst place: "My son, peace be unto thy soul; thine adversity shall be but a small moment." God still speaks to people in pits — by name.
200. The First Vision — JS–History 1 — A boy who read "ask of God" and did. The Father and the Son, two glorified beings, standing above him in the light — and the first word spoken was his name: "Joseph." Every seed in the previous 199 videos points here: God is good, God is embodied, God still speaks, and God calls people by name.
