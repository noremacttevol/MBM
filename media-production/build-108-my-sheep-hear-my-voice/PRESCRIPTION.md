# PRESCRIPTION — build-108-my-sheep-hear-my-voice

**Row:** 108   **Shelf:** Bridge   **Scripture:** John 10:3, 11, 27–28
**Tier:** T1 Saying / Verse   **Target length:** 45–75s   **Pictures:** 6–9 (planned: 11 — see deviation)

> Bridge shelf. The wound is **"heaven is silent now / God no longer speaks to me."** The
> answer is the shepherd metaphor in the present tense: "My sheep hear my voice" — hearing
> him is meant to be normal, one at a time, by name, and he holds you so no one can pluck
> you away.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** the good shepherd knows you one at a time, calls you by name, leads
  you by his voice rather than driving you, and once you are in his hand no one — not your
  failures, not death — can pluck you out.
- **The turn:** "the sheep are people. You." The metaphor drops and the tired, wandering,
  easily-lost sheep become the listener. That is the hinge.
- **Wound answered:** "heaven is silent." The answer is present tense — "My sheep *hear* my
  voice" — hearing him is normal, and even now he is calling past the flock to the one
  still outside.
- **Whose face carries it:** the SHEEP-PEOPLE — the turn onto human faces, and the outsider
  at the end. The shepherd (Jesus) is shown as shepherd, face via master ref /
  over-the-shoulder, never carrying the emotional beat himself.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Do you think that voice could be for you?" — lands
  on the one thing. No change needed.

## Deviations from the band
Stepped to **11 pictures** (band is 6–9) because this "saying" is an extended shepherd
metaphor that moves through many concrete images — the call, the naming, green pasture,
carrying the lamb, giving his life, the hand, the homecoming, the outsider. Each is a
distinct picture the words paint; collapsing them would leave the words moving on while
the picture stays behind. The turn (the sheep are people) needs its own frame.
