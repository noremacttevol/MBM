## The impact read
- **The one thing:** even the Sinless One went down into the water to fulfil all righteousness — so the gate is surely open and good for everyone — and at that same moment the Godhead stood revealed as three distinct persons.
- **The turn:** two hinges in one scene. First "Suffer it to be so now: for thus it becometh us to fulfil all righteousness" (kv15b) — Jesus insisting on the ordinance. Then the opened heaven, where Son, Spirit, and Father are all present and distinct — the doctrinal payload.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** the necessity and example of baptism, and the distinct persons of the Godhead. Hands off to **Gospel Library → Baptism; Godhead.**
- **Whose face carries it:** John the Baptist — his astonishment ("comest thou to me?") carries the front half. In the baptism and the heaven-opened tableau, do not use a Jesus face-closeup: over-the-shoulder / master ref, and the Father is voice + light from heaven, not a face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "When you come to that same water, will you go down into it?" — lands straight on the one thing.
