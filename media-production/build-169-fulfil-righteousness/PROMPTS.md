Matthew 3:13-17. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills, no
motion clips. → Gospel Library topics: **Baptism; Godhead** (named on the closing card).

down into the water to fulfil all righteousness, so baptism is for everyone; and at that moment
the Godhead stood revealed as three distinct persons — the Son in the water, the Spirit
descending as a dove, the Father's voice from heaven. Reverent, awe-filled, warm.

FACE LAW: Jesus IS shown — the SAME master face in every shot where he appears (s1-s7). Each of
those shots carries the byte-identical JESUS LOCK v3 paragraph and a "REF: jesus-master-ref"

descending — never a human figure. The closing shot (s8) has NO divine figure.

weathered, with wild dark shoulder-length hair and an unkempt dark beard, wearing a rough
camel-hair garment cinched with a wide leather girdle at the waist — plainly a desert prophet;
in earth/animal tones, NEVER cream or off-white.

modern object; no cross, steeple, or insignia; no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no

nudity — Jesus and John modestly and fully robed even in the water; every image is ONE single
unbroken full-frame scene — no panels, no comic strip, no dividing lines, no painted border.
