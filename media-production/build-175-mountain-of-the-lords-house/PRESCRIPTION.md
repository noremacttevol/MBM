## The impact read
- **The one thing:** in the last days the house of the Lord is established above all, and people from every nation freely stream up to it — drawn by invitation, not driven by force.
- **The turn:** the people's own words — "Come ye, and let us go up to the mountain of the LORD" (s2a) — the vision turns from a mountain established to a people *choosing* to climb. That willing turn is the hinge.
- **Doctrine + GL page:** temples in the latter days, gathering all nations. Hands off to **Gospel Library → Temples.**
- **Whose face carries it:** the nations — crowds streaming uphill, then individuals resolving to climb. Isaiah is describing what he was shown, so God is not shown speaking; the picture is the people.
- **The card question / close falls out of:** "The mountain is open to you. Come, and walk in his paths." — lands straight on the one thing.
