[RIDGE LOCK] = the crest of the Mount of Olives: a dusty path over the ridge among
old silver-green olive trees, and beyond and below, across the Kidron valley, the
golden city of Jerusalem — pale walls, crowded rooftops, and the gleaming
white-and-gold temple — in warm hazy late-morning light.

[FOLLOWERS LOCK] = the followers: grown men and a few women in dun, faded brown,
grey and olive travel robes — not one of them in off-white or cream, no second
figure with long dark hair, a full dark beard and a pale robe that could be mistaken
for the man in cream. Every figure: two arms, two hands, two legs, one head, every
limb joined to the correct body.
