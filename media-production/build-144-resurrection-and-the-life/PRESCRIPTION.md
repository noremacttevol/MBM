> Bridge wound: **the distant, abstract God who answers grief with a doctrine instead
> of showing up.** The inherited picture hands a mourner a theory about "someday." The
> video shows him saying "I am" — present tense — to a grieving woman's face, as a
> person, not a proposition.

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he doesn't hand grief a theory about the last day — he stands in
  front of the griever and says *I am* the life, now.
- **The turn:** "I am the resurrection, and the life... Believest thou this?" — Martha
  believed in a far-off resurrection; he moves it from someday into himself, present.
- **Wound answered:** the "God is a distant idea who offers doctrine, not presence"
  wound — he meets Martha personally, in her grief, and then proves it at the tomb.
- **Whose face carries it:** MARTHA the griever — her heavy faith, then her yes. Jesus
  only via master ref / over-the-shoulder onto her face.
- **The card question falls out of:** "Death is not the end of the story. He is the
  life." Lands on the one thing. No change needed.
