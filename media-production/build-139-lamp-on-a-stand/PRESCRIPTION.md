## The impact read
- **The one thing:** your life was meant to be seen — God lit you on purpose and set you where
  the light does good — not to be hidden, small, or ashamed.
- **The turn:** j3 — Matthew 5:16, "Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your
  good works, and glorify your Father." The purpose of the light is named: not applause, but
  people drawn to the God behind it.
- **Wound answered (Bridge):** *the God who wants you small / a religion that hides its light.*
  The inherited picture of a faith you keep private and a God who prefers you diminished. This
  cut answers it: "You were lit on purpose. You were put on the stand on purpose" — your light
  was meant to be seen, not hidden.
- **Whose face carries it:** the everyman listener whose light is meant to shine, and the crowd
  on the hillside. Jesus face-shown via master ref teaching (s1/s4).
- **The card question falls out of:** "You were made to be seen for good. Let it shine." No
  change needed.
