# PRESCRIPTION — build-51-first-catch-of-fish

**Row:** 51   **Shelf:** Everyone   **Scripture:** Luke 5:1–11
**Tier:** T2 Encounter   **Target length:** 80–120s   **Pictures:** 10–15 (planned: 10)

## The impact read
- **The one thing:** he meets you at your emptiest — a whole night of work and nothing
  to show — fills your hands past overflowing, then asks for all of it so he can give
  you something far greater.
- **The turn:** [jv10/n7] "Fear not; from henceforth thou shalt catch men" — the
  trembling man who begged him to leave is the very man he calls.
- **Character shown:** he does not send the sinner away; he recruits him. Kindness that
  answers "depart from me" with "follow me."
- **Whose face carries it:** SIMON — bone-tired at the nets, then astonished at the
  catch, then on his knees, then called. Jesus speaks; Simon's face carries the arc.
- **The card question falls out of:** "what is he calling you to leave behind, to
  follow him?"

## Beat list (= the picture plan) — 10 stills
1. [n1] the lakeshore at Gennesaret, a crowd pressing in, two empty boats, tired fishermen washing nets after a fruitless night — "washing out their nets after a long night that had given them nothing"
2. [n2] Jesus stepping into Simon's boat, pushing out a little, teaching the crowd from the water — "he sat down and taught the people from the water"
3. [jv4/n3] "Launch out into the deep" — bone-tired Simon, every instinct telling him it is pointless — "every instinct a fisherman has told him this was pointless"
4. [s5/n3b] Simon's answer — "nevertheless at thy word I will let down the net"
5. [n4] the nets down, filling instantly with a shining mass, the net tearing under the weight — "the net began to tear under the sheer weight of it"
6. [n5] both boats loaded — James and John's too — sitting so low they begin to sink — "both boats were loaded until they sat low in the water and began to sink"
7. [n6/s8/n6b] Simon falling at Jesus' knees among the fish — "Depart from me; for I am a sinful man, O Lord"
8. [jv10/n7] THE TURN — "Fear not; from henceforth thou shalt catch men" — the trembling man is the one he wants — "he will be gathering people, not fish"
9. [n8] the boats brought to land, the greatest catch left lying on the shore, and they follow him — "left the greatest catch of their lives lying there on the shore, and followed him"
10. [card] hold for the closing question about what to leave behind to follow
