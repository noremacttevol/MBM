# STILL PROMPTS — Story Video #170: "The sacrament, worthily" (1corinthians-11_the-sacrament-worthily)

1 Corinthians 11:23-29. MEMBER shelf verse-video. STILLS-ONLY (Law E) — eight painted stills,
no motion clips. → Gospel Library topic: **Sacrament** (named on the closing card).

CARE FLAGS: handle worthiness GENTLY — milk, never fear. The passage's "unworthily... damnation"
(v27,29) is framed by the narrator only as a tender invitation to examine your own heart and
come sincerely; NO threat, no shame. The note is remembrance + covenant renewal: bread and cup
taken in remembrance, a covenant renewed again and again. Warm, reverent, hopeful.

CHRIST / GOD IS NEVER DEPICTED as a figure — no face, no body, no hands of a divine person. His
presence at the table is shown ONLY as warm radiant light. The emblems themselves — the bread
and the cup — are the visual focus. The people who remember and partake are ordinary believers.
(This keeps the shot blocks free of any divine proper noun, so the face-gate passes.)

THE POINT (Why-Law): in the upper room the sacrament was instituted at a lamplit table (s1);
the bread, broken, given as a remembrance of a body given (s2 — 1 Cor 11:24); the cup, the token
of a new covenant (s3 — 1 Cor 11:25); ordinary believers take it in remembrance (s4); showing
that sacrifice and looking for his return (s5); each one first examining his own heart (s6);
so the covenant is renewed again and again, clean and glad (s7); and a place at the table is set
for you (s8).

PEOPLE LOCK (s4-s8): ordinary first-century believers of mixed age — men, women and children in
undyed DARKER earth-tone wool (dun, faded brown, olive, ochre, russet, grey), faces shown,
reverent and sincere; never in cream or off-white.
THE EMBLEMS (s1-s7): a single simple loaf of rustic bread and a plain undecorated clay or wooden
cup, on a low table — humble, timeless, warmly lit.
THE PRESENCE (s1-s3, s5, s7, s8): warm radiant golden light resting over the table — only light,
never a face, never a figure, never a hand within it.

MUST NEVER SHOW: no figure, face, hand or body of God or the risen Lord anywhere — only warm
light; no crucifix, no cross, no wounds, no blood, no gore; no modern object; no steeple or
church-with-a-spire or insignia of any group; no bright ring or aura around anyone's head; no
baked-in text, letters or numbers; no human dressed in cream or off-white. Every image is ONE
single unbroken full-frame scene — no panels, no comic strip, no dividing lines, no painted
border; the artwork fills the whole frame.

STILL STYLE BLOCK (prepended to every prompt, exactly):
Beautiful hand-painted 2D animation style, reverent and warm, like a classic
illustrated storybook of scripture brought to life. Soft painterly brushstroke
textures, glowing golden light, muted earth tones with warm gold highlights.
First-century Judea. Sacred, hushed tone. Not photorealistic. No text or captions
in the image. Historically modest clothing: rough-woven wool and linen in undyed
earth colors. No modern objects.
