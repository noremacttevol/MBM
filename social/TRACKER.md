## How this board stays honest

- A video appears here ONLY after `refresh-postable.py` byte-verifies it against
  the cut Cameron approved. Files to post always come from `social/exports/`.
- New approvals: rerun the script, add the caption entry in `POST-QUEUE.md`, add
  the row here. Same voice, same laws.
- If a posted video later gets re-cut, the posted copy was approved at post time
  and stays up unless Cameron says otherwise — note it in the row's Story cell.
