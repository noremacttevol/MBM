# SCHEDULE — cadence, launch order, weekly rhythm

## The cadence (simple on purpose)

- **One story per day, every day, all platforms, same story everywhere.**
  Daily posting is the single biggest lever on every one of these platforms —
  a page that posts steadily gets fed to new people; a page that posts in bursts
  doesn't. 41 approved videos = 6 weeks of daily posting, and more approvals are
  landing every week from production.
- **Post time: 7:00 PM Eastern, every day.** Evenings are when people scroll, and a
  fixed time builds a habit — both for viewers and for you. Don't chase "optimal
  hour" articles; consistency beats optimization at this size.
- **Instagram rest days:** videos over 3:00 can't be Reels, so on those days
  Instagram simply doesn't post. It still gets 25 of the 41. Never trim a video to
  fit — only approved cuts exist.
- **Upload the file exactly as it is.** No platform sound, no filters, no auto-caption
  overlay, no "enhance." The silence in these videos is intentional (no-music law),
  and the captions are already burned in. If the app nags you to add trending audio,
  that's a no every time.

## The weekly rhythm (about an hour, once a week)

**Sunday afternoon — schedule the whole week (~45–60 min):**
1. **YouTube:** YouTube Studio → upload the 7 files → paste titles + captions → set
   each to Scheduled, 7:00 PM each day.
2. **Facebook + Instagram together:** Meta Business Suite → Create Reel → schedule
   the week for both platforms in one pass.
3. **TikTok:** tiktok.com on the computer → upload → Schedule (TikTok allows ~10
   days ahead).
4. Tick the boxes in `POST-QUEUE.md` as you schedule, so any session can see where
   the queue stands.

**Daily — 10 to 15 minutes:** open each app once, reply to comments and DMs (the
voice rules are in `GROWTH-PLAYBOOK.md`), and check the message-requests folders.
Replying in the first hours after posting matters more than any hashtag.
