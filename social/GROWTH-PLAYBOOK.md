# GROWTH-PLAYBOOK — everything recommended to spread the app

The situation: a growing library of approved videos, zero audience. This file is the
whole plan for fixing that — what to do at launch, what to do every week, how to
handle people, and what never to do. The tone laws in `README.md` bind every word
posted or replied anywhere.

---

## 1. The two audiences (and what each is for)

**Seekers** — the people the app exists for. The platforms' recommendation feeds
bring the videos to them; nobody has to follow anything first. The caption's open
question invites a comment; the bio link is there when a story lands. This audience
is reached by CONSISTENCY (daily posting) — not by anything clever.

**Latter-day Saint members** — not the target of the milk content, but its best
carriers. A member who loves a reverent KJV story shares it to their own feed, and
their non-member friends see it. Members are reached through Cameron's own network
(section 4), not through the pages themselves. The pages stay milk for everyone.

## 2. Launch sequence (week 0)

1. Create the four accounts per `CHANNEL-PLAN.md` — one sitting, ~25 minutes total.
2. Post Day 1 (The Prodigal Son) on all four; pin it everywhere.
3. Schedule the rest of week 1 per `SCHEDULE.md`.
4. THEN do the personal-network moves in section 4 — after the pages have a pinned
   video and a scheduled week, so a visitor sees a living page, not an empty one.

## 3. Comments and DMs — how to be the person behind the page

This is where the pages either feel like the app or betray it. The rules:

- **Reply like a person, not a brand.** Short, warm, honest. No copy-paste blocks.
- **Answer honestly first, always** (the app's own no-duck rule). Then, if it fits,
  offer more: "the app goes deeper on this one story at a time, if you want it."
- **Never argue doctrine. Not once. Not winnably.** If someone attacks or debates:
  respond with kindness or with the words of Jesus, or don't respond. One reply
  maximum; never a thread war. "That's a fair question — I'd rather talk than
  debate. DM me if you want to." Then stop.
- **Hostile or mocking comments:** leave them unless they're profane at other
  commenters (then platform-hide). Never delete mere disagreement — an honest page
  doesn't scrub critics.
- **If someone asks "who makes these?" / "are you Mormon?":** answer plainly and
  briefly — the app is made by a Latter-day Saint; the videos are word-for-word KJV
  stories; nothing more is hiding behind them. Honest, unashamed, no pivot into a
  pitch. (The pages stay milk; honesty about who we are is not meat.)
- **If someone shares real pain** (grief, doubt, crisis): see them first ("I'm sorry.
  That's heavy."), point gently to the story's hope, and offer the app's Talk-to-a-
  real-person path if they want a human. Never a canned verse dump.
- **Questions about the open question in the caption:** this is the app's mirror
  moment happening in public. Engage with what THEY said, reflect it back — exactly
  like the app's reflect-before-content law.

## 4. The member-sharer moves (Cameron's own network)

1. **Personal Facebook post from Cameron** the day the page goes live — his own
   words, one paragraph: what he built, why, link to the page and milkb4meat.org.
   A person vouching beats any page post. Ask nothing except "watch one."
2. **Family and close friends:** send five people the Prodigal Son video directly
   with one personal sentence. Direct shares are how the first hundred views happen.
3. **Ward / stake:** the `church-launch-kit/` brochures already cover the app for
   members; the pages give members something NEW: a daily story they can share to
   their own feeds. Mention the page in the contexts that already exist (elders
   quorum / Relief Society chats where appropriate) once, humbly, with the link.
   Never spam a chat twice.
4. **The ask to members is always "share a story you love"** — never "promote my
   app." A shared story reaches their friends as a story, which is the whole method.

## 5. What to measure (monthly, 10 minutes — not daily)

Watch these, in this order, and ignore the rest:
1. **Shares** — the only metric that IS the mission (someone carried a story to
   someone else).
2. **Watch-through** (YouTube Studio / TikTok analytics show % watched) — tells you
   which stories hold people; tell the production side which stories to prioritize.
3. **Bio-link taps and app installs** — the pipeline working end to end.
4. **Comments with real substance** — count the conversations, not the likes.

**Follower counts are vanity here.** Shorts/Reels/TikTok distribute to non-followers;
a story can reach 50,000 people off a page with 40 followers. Don't chase, don't
mention, don't celebrate follower milestones on the page.

## 6. What NEVER to do

- Never post an unapproved, complained-about, or working-tree cut. `POST-QUEUE.md`
  and `exports/` are the only source. No exceptions, no "it looks fine."
- Never add platform music, sounds, filters, stickers, or auto-captions. The
  silence and the burned-in captions ARE the product (no-music law).
- Never buy followers, run follow-for-follow, or use engagement bait ("like if you
  love Jesus," "99% will scroll past"). That is manipulation; the laws forbid it,
  and the algorithms punish it anyway.
- Never argue, dunk, or quote-tweet-style dunk on critics or other traditions.
- Never post Church/Restoration/Book of Mormon content on these pages. Milk only.
  (This isn't hiding — it's meeting people where they are; and when asked directly,
  we answer honestly. Section 3.)
- Never let the pages imply they're official Church media.
- Never DM-blast strangers or drop links in other people's comment sections.
- Never pay to boost anything yet (see section 8).

## 7. Keeping the queue alive (for future sessions)

- Production keeps approving new rows. Any session: `python3 social/refresh-postable.py`,
  then append new rows to `POST-QUEUE.md` in the same format and voice, and extend
  the schedule. The queue never runs dry as long as production runs.
- If a posted video's row later gets a complaint/re-cut: the POSTED copy was the
  approved cut at post time — it stays up unless Cameron says otherwise. (A re-cut
  changes the reviewer, not history.)
- Keep `POST-QUEUE.md` checkboxes current — they're the only record of what's live.

## 8. Later, once the base exists (not now)

- **Small Facebook boost** ($10–20) on the single best-performing story, targeted
  broadly — only after several weeks of organic data show which story that is.
- **YouTube playlists** by gospel (all Luke stories, all Matthew 13 parables) once
  ~30 videos are up — free search traffic.
- **Longer-form compilations** (e.g., "Every parable in Matthew 13, KJV") — a
  production question for later; would need Cameron's approval as new cuts.
- **Pinterest / X**: possible fifth and sixth channels; decide after the first four
  have a month of data. The old `Marketing-Launch-Kit/page-art/` X header exists if
  wanted.

## 9. The honest expectation

Weeks 1–2 will likely be quiet — a few hundred views, single-digit comments. That's
normal; recommendation engines test content slowly. The compounding starts when the
library is deep and the page posts daily without flinching. The job is faithfulness,
not virality: post the story, answer the person, repeat. The stories have carried
themselves for two thousand years.
