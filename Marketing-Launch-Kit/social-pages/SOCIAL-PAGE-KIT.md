## Voice guardrails (read before posting anything)

These come straight from the app's own principles. The pages must feel like the app: a sanctuary, not a sales funnel.

- **Lead with the goodness of Jesus, never with a pitch.** The story does the work. Let it.
- **Never bait, shame, or pressure.** No "share or you don't love God," no fear, no guilt. If a caption feels like a hook, rewrite it softer.
- **Ask, don't tell.** End posts with an honest, open question, the way the app does. Invite reflection, not agreement.
- **Be honest about what this is.** Never hide that there's an app behind it once someone asks. Never pretend to be something you're not.
- **Keep a human reachable.** Reply to comments and DMs like a person, gently and without an agenda. A real person is always one message away.
- **Don't argue doctrine in comments.** If someone pushes back, respond with kindness or with the words of Jesus they already accept — never a debate. You can always invite them to talk privately.
- **No Church, no doctrine, no "Book of Mormon" in these posts.** This is milk. The pages meet people where they are. Depth comes later, in private conversation, only when someone is ready.

## Setup steps (about 2 minutes each)

I can't create the accounts for you — that needs your phone number and password — but here's the exact sequence so each takes about two minutes. **You should not need to pay for anything to set these up.**

**Instagram**
1. Sign up / log in at instagram.com or in the app.
2. Convert to a Professional account: Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account → choose "Creator" → category "Personal blog" or "Religious organization."
3. Set name to *Milk Before Meat*, username to `@milkbeforemeat`.
4. Upload `profile_1080.png` as the profile photo.
5. Paste the Instagram bio above.
6. Post the first reel (Woman at the Well) and pin it to your profile.

**Facebook Page**
1. facebook.com → Pages → Create new Page.
2. Page name: *Milk Before Meat*. Category: "Religious Organization" or "Community."
3. Upload `profile_1080.png` (profile) and `cover_facebook_1640x624.png` (cover).
4. Paste the Facebook About text above.
5. Set the username to `@milkbeforemeat` (Page settings → Username).
6. Post the first video and pin it to the top.

**Twitter / X**
1. Sign up / log in at x.com.
2. Set display name *Milk Before Meat*, handle `@milkbeforemeat`.
3. Upload `profile_1080.png` (avatar) and `cover_twitter_1500x500.png` (header).
4. Paste the X bio above.
5. Post the first video and pin it to your profile.

**Optional but recommended:** create a TikTok and a YouTube (for Shorts) with the same name, handle, and art — the vertical videos work as-is on both, and that's where short spiritual content spreads fastest.

## Posting rhythm

Two to four posts a week is plenty to start — consistency beats volume. A good weekly shape: one story video, one short text/quote reflection, and one gentle question or reply-driven post. The two-week starter plan is in `CAPTIONS-AND-CALENDAR.md`.
