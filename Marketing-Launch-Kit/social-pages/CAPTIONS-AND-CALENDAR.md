# Milk Before Meat — Captions & Starter Calendar

Ready-to-paste captions for the four videos, a few text-only posts to fill the gaps, and a two-week plan to get you started. All written in the sanctuary voice: warm, honest, no pressure.

---

## Video captions

### 1. The Woman at the Well
> It was noon — the hour you go to the well when you don't want to be seen.
>
> She came expecting nothing. She left carrying something deeper than what she came for.
>
> Have you ever searched for something and realized later it was deeper than you thought?
>
> #Jesus #faith #hope #GodsLove #Scripture

### 2. The Prodigal Son
> He rehearsed a speech to beg his way back as a servant.
>
> His father never let him finish it. He was already running.
>
> Which part feels closest to you right now — the one who left, the father waiting, or the one who stayed but felt unseen?
>
> #Jesus #grace #hope #faith #TheGospel

### 3. The Woman Who Touched His Cloak
> Twelve years untouched. She didn't ask. She just reached for the edge of His cloak.
>
> Out of a whole crowd pressing in, He stopped — and He felt her. Then He called her "Daughter."
>
> What would it mean to you if someone saw you like that?
>
> #Jesus #hope #faith #GodsLove #peace

### 4. The Good Shepherd
> Ninety-nine were safe. Any reasonable person would have let the one go.
>
> He didn't. He went out into the dark until he found it — and carried it home on his shoulders.
>
> Have you ever felt like the one who wandered off, and wondered if anyone would come?
>
> #Jesus #hope #grace #faith #Scripture

## Text-only / quote posts (for the in-between days)

**A.**
> He didn't wait for her to explain herself. He just called her "Daughter."
> — The woman who touched His cloak, Mark 5

**B.**
> "While he was still a long way off, his father saw him, and ran."
> Not walked. Ran.
> — Luke 15

**C.**
> A question worth sitting with today:
> When was the last time you felt fully known — and still wanted?

**D.**
> Jesus never opened with a survey. He told a story, and let people find themselves inside it.
> That's the whole idea here.

**E.**
> He left the ninety-nine for the one.
> If you've ever wondered whether you're worth the trouble — that's your answer.
