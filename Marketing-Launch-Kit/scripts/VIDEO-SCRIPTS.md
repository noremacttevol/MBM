# Milk Before Meat — Video Scripts (Batch 1)

Four short, vertical (9:16) videos for Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Twitter/X. Each tells one story from the life of Jesus in a few beats, ends on one open question, and offers a quiet invitation — no pressure, no hype, matching the "sanctuary" tone of the app.

**Format:** ~35–40 seconds, vertical 1080×1920, soft ambient music, on-screen text only (no voiceover). Every video ends with the same gentle sign-off: *Milk Before Meat*.

**Posting note:** These are finished MP4s you can post as-is. If you ever want to re-film with your own voice, the beats below double as a shooting script — just read each beat over the matching visual.

## 1. The Woman at the Well
*Source: John 4 · For: seekers, the tired, anyone searching*

| Beat | On-screen text | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| Open | It was noon. The hottest hour. The hour you go to the well when you don't want to be seen. | Empty desert, heat haze, distant well |
| 1 | A woman came alone. She had learned to expect nothing from strangers. | Lone silhouette approaching |
| 2 | A man was sitting there. He asked her for a drink — then offered her water she'd never have to draw again. | Warm light near the well |
| 3 | He knew everything she had done. He told her anyway — not to shame her, but so she'd know she was fully known, and still wanted. | Soft golden light breaking through |
| 4 | She came for water. She left carrying something deeper than what she thought she wanted. | Figure walking toward light |
| Question | Have you ever searched for something, and realized later it was deeper than you thought? | Slow fade, open sky |
| Invite | There's a place to sit with that. — **Milk Before Meat** | Logo / calm close |

## Why these four, and how they're ordered

Each story maps to a different heart the app is built to meet: the seeker (Well), the one who left (Prodigal), the desperate and unseen (Cloak), and the lost or forgotten (Shepherd). Together they cover the emotional range of almost anyone who would ever stop scrolling. None of them mention the Church, doctrine, or the app's deeper purpose — that's by design. They lead with the goodness of Jesus and an honest question, exactly the way the app's onboarding does. The invitation is soft on purpose: "a place to sit with that," never "download this to fix your life."
