[narrator] Nicodemus was a ruler and a respected teacher, a man near the top of his world. And yet, one night, he slipped quietly through the dark streets to ask his questions in private, humble enough to come and learn.

[narrator] He came to the light and listened. And what he heard turned his careful world upside down. It was not a small adjustment or one more rule to keep. It was a whole new beginning — a person must be born all over again.

[scripture] Jesus answered and said unto him,

[jesus] Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.

[scripture] How can a man be born when he is old? can he enter the second time into his mother's womb, and be born?

[narrator] Nicodemus took it literally, and was baffled. He could not picture it. Surely no one could climb back and start his life over from the beginning.

[narrator] But the new birth was never meant to be physical. To be born again is first to be born of water — to go down into the water of baptism and come up new, the old life washed away and left behind.

[narrator] And there was a second half to it, not water only. To be born of the Spirit is to receive the gift of the Holy Ghost — a birth from above, life breathed into the soul by heaven itself.

[scripture] Jesus answered,

[jesus] Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God.

[narrator] Here is the quiet study gem. Notice he did not say this was one good option among many. He said a person cannot enter without it. The gate is the gate — the same for everyone, even a learned and upright ruler like Nicodemus.

[narrator] And that same single gate still stands open before you: born of water, and born of the Spirit. The door is not hidden and it is not narrow with pride — it is simply the way in. When you stand before it, will you go through?

[narrator] To enter the kingdom, a person must be born again — born of water in baptism, and of the Spirit from above. The gate is the gate, the same for everyone. When you stand before it, will you go through?
