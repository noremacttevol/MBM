[narrator] Jesus sat with his disciples on the Mount of Olives, telling them what the long road ahead would hold —

[narrator] wars, rumors, hardship, and patience under strain.

[narrator] Then he turned to the one thing that would outlast all of it:

[narrator] a message carried to every corner of the earth.

[narrator] Not a secret kept for a few.

[narrator] A witness given to all nations — the good news of the kingdom going out until the very end.

[jesus] And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations; and then shall the end come.

[narrator] The task he gave is still the task we carry: tell it, show it, live it — until the promise is fulfilled.

[narrator] The good news was meant for the whole world — and for you. Come, hear it, and carry it on.
