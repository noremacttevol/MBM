[narrator] Jesus told a story about the smallest seed a farmer knew.

[narrator] It was a mustard seed — so tiny it could be lost in the palm of your hand.

[narrator] A man took that one little seed and planted it in his field.

[narrator] Of all the seeds he could have sown, it was just about the smallest of them all.

[jesus] The kingdom of heaven is like to a grain of mustard seed, which a man took, and sowed in his field: which indeed is the least of all seeds:

[jesus] but when it is grown, it is the greatest among herbs, and becometh a tree, so that the birds of the air come and lodge in the branches thereof.

[narrator] But that tiny seed did not stay small. Quietly, slowly, it began to grow.

[narrator] Up out of the ground, higher and higher, until it became the largest plant in the whole garden — a tall, spreading tree.

[narrator] And the wild birds came and built their nests in its broad, sheltering branches.

[narrator] That, Jesus said, is what God's kingdom is like.

[narrator] It almost never begins big. It begins small — a whispered prayer, a single kind act, one quiet change of heart.

[narrator] But God takes that smallest beginning and grows it into something far greater than anyone could have imagined.

[narrator] God does enormous things from the smallest of starts. What small seed is he asking you to plant today?
