[narrator] Jesus taught how to tell what is true — look at what grows from it.

[jesus] Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves.

[narrator] Watch out, he said. Some of them will come to you looking like part of the flock — gentle, harmless, one of your own. Inside, they are wolves.

[narrator] A tree shows what it is by what it bears. You don't guess at a tree by its bark.

[jesus] Ye shall know them by their fruits. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles? Even so every good tree bringeth forth good fruit; but a corrupt tree bringeth forth evil fruit.

[narrator] Nobody picks grapes off a thorn bush. Nobody gathers figs off a thistle. You already know how this works — a good tree gives good fruit, and a bad one gives bad fruit.

[jesus] A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit, neither can a corrupt tree bring forth good fruit.

[narrator] Cannot. Not will not — cannot. The good and the rotten cannot switch places. What is inside comes out in the open, given long enough.

[jesus] Every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire. Wherefore by their fruits ye shall know them.

[narrator] A tree that never gives anything worth eating gets cut down. So watch the fruit. Not the clothes, not the confidence, not the words — the fruit. That is the whole test, and anybody can run it.

[narrator] What grows from a life with him is good fruit — love, kindness, truth. Abide in him, and let it show.
