[narrator] Paul wrote that when God's Spirit lives in a person, a harvest grows — not crops, but character.

[scripture] But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, Meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.

[narrator] First, love. Then joy.

[narrator] Then peace — the kind that doesn't depend on circumstances.

[narrator] Longsuffering next — patience that doesn't quit. Then gentleness, and goodness, and faith that holds.

[narrator] Meekness, and temperance — self-control that masters the storms inside.

[narrator] And no law anywhere forbids any of it. These are the things that can't be overdone.

[narrator] This fruit isn't grown by strain. Ask the Spirit — and let it ripen in you.
