[narrator] On the night before He died, Jesus sat with His disciples and told them they would not be left alone.

[jesus] I will not leave you comfortless: I will come to you.

[narrator] He was hours from the cross, and He was the one doing the comforting — promising that whatever else they lost that week, they would not be left on their own.

[narrator] He promised another Helper would come — the Holy Ghost, sent by the Father in His name.

[narrator] This Helper would do two things: teach them everything,

[narrator] and bring every word Jesus had spoken back to their minds.

[jesus] But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.

[narrator] The promise still stands.

[narrator] The Spirit who taught them then teaches everyone who listens now.

[narrator] You are not left to remember alone. The Comforter is here — let Him teach you.
