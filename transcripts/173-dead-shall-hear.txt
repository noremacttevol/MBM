[narrator] Jesus said a time was coming — and had already begun — when something impossible would happen.

[narrator] The dead would hear a voice. Not a rumor of a voice. His voice.

[jesus] Verily, verily, I say unto you, The hour is coming, and now is,

[jesus] when the dead shall hear the voice of the Son of God:

[jesus] and they that hear shall live.

[narrator] The One who made life is the One who calls it back.

[jesus] Marvel not at this: for the hour is coming, in the which all that are in the graves shall hear his voice, and shall come forth.

[narrator] Not some of the dead. All of them. He said every grave will hear that same voice — and open. Nobody is too far gone, and nobody gets left in the ground.

[narrator] To hear him is to live.

[narrator] He speaks, and death loses its grip. Lean in and listen — and live.
