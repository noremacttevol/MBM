[narrator] Paul gave the early church a short, sharp command about what to believe and what to keep.

[scripture] Prove all things; hold fast that which is good.

[narrator] Test everything, he said. Don't swallow every voice — weigh it, hold it up to the light.

[narrator] And notice what he had just said one breath earlier: despise not prophesyings. The testing was never for shutting God's voice out — it is how you recognize it.

[narrator] And when you find what is genuinely good, cling to it. Don't let it slip.

[narrator] He paired it with a warning — and it's the shortest fence he ever built:

[scripture] Abstain from all appearance of evil.

[narrator] The same word fits now: a faith that checks, then commits — that's steady, not gullible.

[narrator] Weigh it, then hold it. The good He shows you is worth keeping — reach for it.
