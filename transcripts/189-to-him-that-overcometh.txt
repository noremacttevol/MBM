[narrator] To a church that had grown comfortable and half-asleep, Jesus sent a knock at the door — not a storm, just a knock.

[jesus] Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me.

[narrator] He does not break the door. He waits to be invited in.

[narrator] And to the one who opens, who keeps opening, he promises a seat no empire can give.

[jesus] To him that overcometh will I grant to sit with me in my throne, even as I also overcame, and am set down with my Father in his throne.

[narrator] The one who overcomes doesn't earn a throne by being flawless. He shares the one Christ already won.

[narrator] He is knocking right now. Open, and you will eat with him — and reign with him.
