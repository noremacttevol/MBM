[narrator] Parents were bringing their little kids to Jesus, just so he could put his hands on them and bless them.

[scripture] And they brought young children to him, that he should touch them: and his disciples rebuked those that brought them.

[narrator] The disciples tried to wave them off — the Teacher is busy, this is grown-up work, not the place for children.

[narrator] But he saw them do it. And Mark does not soften how he took it.

[scripture] But when Jesus saw it, he was much displeased, and said unto them,

[jesus] Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God.

[narrator] That old word suffer just means let. Let them come. Don't stand in their way. Then he went further.

[jesus] Verily I say unto you, Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of God as a little child, he shall not enter therein.

[narrator] He meant: nobody earns their way into the kingdom of God. You receive it the way a child receives a gift — with open hands.

[scripture] And he took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and blessed them.

[narrator] Then he gathered them up in his arms and blessed them, one at a time, unhurried — like there was nowhere else he needed to be.

[narrator] He had time for the smallest and least important person in the room. That includes you.
