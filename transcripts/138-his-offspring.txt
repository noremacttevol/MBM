[narrator] Paul stood in the middle of Athens, surrounded by statues to every god they could think of —

[narrator] and one altar that simply said, To the unknown God.

[narrator] He told them they were already closer than they knew. The God they'd left unnamed made the whole world, and He is not far from any of us.

[narrator] In Him, Paul said, we live and move and have our being. He even quoted their own poets: we are His children.

[scripture] For in him we live, and move, and have our being; as certain also of your own poets have said, For we are also his offspring.

[narrator] God overlooked the times of ignorance before, Paul said — but now He commands all men everywhere to repent.

[narrator] The unknown God was never unknown to Him. He had been near the whole time.

[narrator] You were made to be His. Reach out — He is not far.
