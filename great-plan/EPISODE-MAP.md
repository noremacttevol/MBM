## MOVEMENT I — BEFORE THE WORLD (Episodes 1–6)

*Where the war starts. Two beings state what they want, and neither one ever
changes his mind again.*

### Episode 1 — The Two Wills ⭐ (build first)
A council before the world. The Father presents a plan for His children —
bodies, agency, a fall, a rescue. One son answers, "Father, thy will be done,
and the glory be thine forever." Another says he will save every soul without
losing one — by force — "wherefore give me thine honor." The whole film in
one room: everything that ever goes wrong on earth traces to the second
speech, and everything that saves us traces to the first.
**The war:** the Father stakes everything on agency; Lucifer demands the
throne in exchange for a rescue that would make rescue meaningless.
**Anchors:** Moses 4:1–4; Abraham 3:24–28.

### Episode 2 — We Were There
Back up: who was standing in that room? Us. Spirit sons and daughters of
glorified Parents, alive before the earth existed — intelligences organized,
taught, and loved long before a body. Abraham is shown the noble and great
ones; Jeremiah is told he was known before he was formed; the morning stars
sang together and all the sons of God shouted for joy — and that shout was
ours, because we heard the plan and wanted it.
**The war:** the Father's opening move is the truth about who we are — His
actual children, with His potential. Every lie the devil ever tells is aimed
at making us forget this one thing.
**Anchors:** Abraham 3:22–23; Jeremiah 1:5; Job 38:4–7; Acts 17:28–29.

### Episode 3 — War in Heaven
The first war ever fought was not fought over territory or treasure. It was
fought over whether God's children would be free. Michael and his hosts
against the rebel; testimony and loyalty against ambition; and a third part
of the hosts of heaven — brothers and sisters we knew — choose the son who
promised them safety without freedom.
**The war:** the only war where every soldier on both sides could see God —
and a third still chose force. Agency is so real it was worth a war in a
perfect place.
**Anchors:** Revelation 12:7–11; D&C 29:36–37; Moses 4:3.

### Episode 4 — Cast Down
Lucifer, son of the morning, falls like lightning. He loses the presence of
the Father, his place, his future — and the one thing he can never get back:
he will never have a body. The children he wanted to rule will all receive
the very thing he forfeited. So his motive from this day forward is written
plainly in scripture: he seeks that all men might be miserable like unto
himself. Then the episode answers the question every honest viewer is
already asking: why would God allow a devil at all? Because God accepted
every spirit who chose freedom — and real freedom means "no" has to be
genuinely possible. Scripture says it straight: "it must needs be that the
devil should tempt the children of men, or they could not be agents unto
themselves." God did not create evil. He refused to counterfeit freedom —
knowing what freedom would cost, with the payment already arranged.
**The war:** the devil thinks his existence proves God failed. It proves
the opposite — agency was real enough to produce him, which is exactly what
makes our growth real enough to matter.
**Anchors:** Isaiah 14:12–15; Luke 10:18; 2 Nephi 2:11, 15–18, 27; D&C 29:39; Revelation 12:12.

### Episode 5 — The Proving Ground
"We will go down... and we will make an earth whereon these may dwell; and we
will prove them herewith." Creation, shown as what it actually was: not an
accident and not a whim — a deliberate, loving construction project. The Son,
under the Father's direction, organizes a world as the school of the second
estate: a place where His children can walk by faith, choose in the dark, and
grow into what their Father is. Two honest notes the script says out loud:
the proving is for OUR becoming, not His information — He already knows His
children; the test is a gym, not a quiz. And on the how of creation — the
process, the timeline — God hasn't told us everything, and we say so. What
He revealed is who did it and why. That's the part that changes your life.
**The war:** the devil calls the earth his kingdom. He's wrong. Every sunrise
is the Father's architecture — a classroom built by Someone who intended His
children to graduate.
**Anchors:** Abraham 3:24–26; Abraham 4; Moses 1:33; John 1:1–3.

### Episode 6 — The Guarantee
The terms of mortality are brutal on paper: a veil of forgetting, real
danger, real death, real sin. Why the veil — why would God hide from His own
children? Because the point is to see what we love when we can't see Him. A
God standing visibly in the room doesn't get chosen — He gets complied with,
and compliance was Lucifer's plan. Faith is the only condition where
becoming is possible. And the plan never had an unfunded risk — before the
first breath was drawn, a Savior was already chosen, "the Lamb slain from
the foundation of the world." We didn't go down uninsured. The Father let us
fall only because the Son had already promised to catch us.
**The war:** the devil bets that pain and silence will prove God doesn't
love us. But the rescue was signed before the danger started — the pain was
never abandonment, and the silence was never absence.
**Anchors:** Revelation 13:8; 1 Peter 1:19–20; Abraham 3:25–26; Alma 34:9; Ether 12:6; 2 Corinthians 5:7.

## MOVEMENT II — THE FIRST WORLD (Episodes 7–13)

*The plan touches down. The devil learns he cannot stop it — so he starts
counterfeiting it.*

### Episode 7 — Two Trees
Eden. Innocence, beauty, and two trees — because a choice with only one
option is not a choice. The serpent arrives with the first lie told on earth,
and it's the same speech from the council wearing a costume. Adam and Eve
face the fork the whole plan was built around: stay innocent and frozen, or
step into a fallen world where children, growth, and godliness become
possible.
**The war:** the devil thinks he is wrecking the plan. He is actually
springing it. He has never once understood how the Father turns his moves.
**Anchors:** Genesis 2–3; Moses 4:5–12; 2 Nephi 2:11, 15–16.

### Episode 8 — Not Damned for Adam ⭐ (build first)
The turning point most of Christianity lost. The Fall brought death and
separation — and it also brought children, experience, and the need for a
Savior who was already standing by. Eve sees it first: "Were it not for our
transgression we never should have had seed... and the joy of our
redemption." And the Father's justice is stated for all time: men will be
punished for their own sins, and not for Adam's transgression. No baby is
born guilty. Little children are alive in Christ. That doctrine, by itself,
clears God's name.
**The war:** the devil's favorite slander is that God condemns the innocent.
The restored gospel answers: He never did. Adam fell that men might be; and
men are, that they might have joy.
**Anchors:** 2 Nephi 2:22–27; Moses 5:10–11; Moses 6:53–54; Articles of Faith 2; Moroni 8:8–12.

### Episode 9 — The First Gospel
Adam and Eve don't wander out of Eden into silence — the gospel is preached
to them immediately. Sacrifice, and an angel who explains it: "This thing is
a similitude of the sacrifice of the Only Begotten of the Father." Faith,
repentance, baptism, the Holy Ghost — the first family on earth had the whole
plan of redemption. And right behind the angel comes Satan with his first
counterfeit ministry: "I am also a son of God... believe it not."
**The war:** from day one the devil's move is never just temptation — it's
imitation. He doesn't fight religion; he manufactures his own.
**Anchors:** Moses 5:4–13; Moses 6:51–66.

### Episode 10 — Master Mahan
Cain doesn't just kill his brother — he covenants with Satan first, and
loves Satan more than God. "Swear unto me..." The first murder on earth is
committed as a religious act of a secret order: kill and get gain. Master
Mahan. The devil now has his own priesthood-shaped thing, his own covenants,
his own dark ordinances — and secret combinations will run under all of
human history from here to the end.
**The war:** God builds covenants that exalt; the devil builds covenants
that devour. Every mafia oath, every conspiracy of blood and power, is
Cain's religion with new stationery.
**Anchors:** Moses 5:18–33; Genesis 4:8–15; Helaman 6:26–30.

### Episode 11 — The God Who Weeps
Enoch preaches to a hardened world and builds the impossible: a people of
one heart and one mind, with no poor among them — so aligned with heaven
that God takes the whole city up. And then the view widens: Satan holds a
great chain, veils the earth in darkness, looks up and laughs. And Enoch
watches the God of heaven look at the same earth — and weep. The most
powerful Being in existence, crying over His children's misery, and telling
Enoch why: these are the workmanship of mine own hands... and I gave them
their agency, and they hate their own blood.
**The war:** the devil laughs at human suffering; the Father weeps over it.
One scene, both faces. There is no clearer portrait of the two wills in all
scripture.
**Anchors:** Moses 7:18–41, 69.

### Episode 12 — The Flood and the Bow
Violence fills the earth until the future itself is in danger — the devil's
endgame is a world so corrupt that no child could ever be raised to choose
God. Noah preaches for years to a world that answers with threats. The flood
is not a tantrum; it is a Father preserving the possibility of the plan —
a remnant, a fresh start, and a covenant sealed with light in the sky.
**The war:** the devil tries to drown the plan in violence; God preserves a
family and hangs His bow in the clouds as a promise to every generation
after.
**Anchors:** Genesis 6–9; Moses 8:20–30; JST Genesis 9:21–25.

### Episode 13 — One Family for All Families
Babel: a forced, proud unity aimed at heaven — scattered. Then the true plan
for unity: God calls one man, Abraham, and cuts a covenant with his family
that is aimed at everybody — "in thee shall all families of the earth be
blessed." Priesthood, posterity, promised land — not favoritism, but a
delivery system: one covenant family carrying the gospel to every nation.
**The war:** the devil unites people by force and pride; God gathers them by
covenant and blessing. The rest of the Old Testament — and the Restoration —
runs on the promise made to this one tent in the desert.
**Anchors:** Genesis 11:1–9; Genesis 12:1–3; Abraham 2:8–11; Galatians 3:8.

## MOVEMENT III — ISRAEL, THE OTHER SHEEP, AND THE SON (Episodes 14–20)

*Jehovah works the covenant, plants a second witness, and then comes down
Himself — and pays the price force would have erased.*

### Episode 14 — Face to Face
Egypt's gods humiliated, the sea split, a slave nation delivered — and then
Sinai, where the Lord speaks to Moses face to face, as a man speaks to his
friend. Moses gets the mission statement of the whole story from God's own
mouth: "This is my work and my glory — to bring to pass the immortality and
eternal life of man." And when Satan shows up demanding worship, Moses —
still glowing from real glory — sees through him: "Who art thou? For behold,
I am a son of God."
**The war:** a man who knows God is a person, and knows himself to be God's
son, cannot be bluffed by the devil. That's why those two truths are the
first things the devil steals in every age.
**Anchors:** Exodus 3, 14, 33:11; Moses 1:1–22, 39; Deuteronomy 34:10.

### Episode 15 — He Kept Sending
Israel forgets. Again. And the astonishing thing is not Israel's forgetting —
it's that God keeps sending. Judges, then prophets: Elijah calling down fire
against Baal's four hundred, Isaiah seeing the Messiah centuries early,
messenger after messenger, "rising up betimes, and sending; because he had
compassion on his people." Mocked, ignored, killed — and He sends the next
one anyway.
**The war:** the devil's play is to make each generation forget the last
one's witness. God's answer is a relay of prophets that never stops — surely
the Lord God will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants
the prophets.
**Anchors:** 1 Kings 18; Isaiah 53; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Amos 3:7.

### Episode 16 — The Other Sheep
Six hundred years before Bethlehem, God does something no one in Jerusalem
knows about: He walks one family out of the city before it burns and plants
them across the ocean. Lehi's family becomes a second covenant nation with
its own prophets and its own record — a second, independent witness being
written on the other side of the world. Jesus will name them later: "Other
sheep I have, which are not of this fold."
**The war:** the devil's long game is to corrupt the record of God's
dealings. So God, quietly, starts a second record the devil doesn't know to
attack — and buries its delivery date twenty-four centuries out.
**Anchors:** 1 Nephi 1–2; 2 Nephi 29:7–14; John 10:16; 3 Nephi 15:21.

### Episode 17 — The Condescension
The Creator of worlds is born in a feed trough to a teenage mother in an
occupied country. Heaven can't contain the announcement — angels over
shepherd fields, a new star, and on the far side of the world a night with
no darkness, exactly as prophesied. The devil answers the birth of the baby
with the only tools he has: Herod's swords in Bethlehem, and in America a
whisper campaign that the signs meant nothing.
**The war:** God's greatest move is to make Himself small enough to be
rejected — condescension, love that walks in the door unarmed. The devil
answers a manger with a massacre, and tells you everything about both sides.
**Anchors:** Luke 2; Matthew 2; 1 Nephi 11:13–33; 3 Nephi 1; Helaman 14:1–8.

### Episode 18 — The Same Old Trade
Forty days fasting in the wilderness, and the devil arrives in person with
three offers — ending with the big one: all the kingdoms of the world, "if
thou wilt fall down and worship me." Listen closely: it's the council speech
again. Glory without Gethsemane, the throne without the cross, the shortcut
in exchange for the honor. The Son refuses him with scripture, then spends
three years doing the opposite of force: healing, feeding, forgiving,
inviting — and letting the rich young ruler walk away.
**The war:** the devil offers Jesus the same deal he offered the council —
and the same deal he offers everyone: skip the suffering, sell the soul.
Jesus answers the way He answered before the world: thy will be done.
**Anchors:** Matthew 4:1–11; Luke 4; Mark 10:17–22; John 6:38.

### Episode 19 — Thy Will Be Done
Gethsemane. The weight of every sin, every grief, every lonely night of
every child of the Father — taken willingly, bleeding at every pore, by the
one Person who never owed any of it. "Not my will, but thine, be done" — the
same sentence He said before the world, now costing everything. Then
betrayal, the mock trial, the cross — "this is your hour, and the power of
darkness" — and the devil throws everything he has at getting Him to quit.
He doesn't. It is finished. And the episode answers the question underneath
it all: why couldn't God just forgive — why a cross? Because justice is
real and mercy cannot rob it. So mercy paid it. Forgiveness was never God
waiving the debt. It was God eating it.
**The war:** Lucifer's plan would have saved our bodies by erasing our souls.
The Father's plan saves the whole person — and here is the bill, and the Son
pays it. This is what "God so loved the world" costs, and He paid it for
people who were busy killing Him.
**Anchors:** Luke 22:39–44, 53; Matthew 26–27; D&C 19:16–19; Mosiah 3:7; Alma 42:13–25; Alma 34:9–16; John 19:30.

### Episode 20 — All Power
The tomb breaks. Death — the devil's oldest, surest weapon — is now a
temporary condition for every human being who ever lived, no faith required,
free of charge, forever. The risen Christ spends forty days with His
apostles, then descends out of heaven in America, where twenty-five hundred
people come one by one and feel the wounds. He organizes His Church in both
hemispheres: apostles, prophets, priesthood keys, ordinances — a kingdom
with authority, not a memory with fans.
**The war:** the devil's checkmate piece is off the board — he can never
make death permanent again. Watch him carefully now. He is about to change
strategies.
**Anchors:** Matthew 28; 1 Corinthians 15:20–22; 3 Nephi 11; Ephesians 2:19–20; 4:11–14.

## MOVEMENT IV — THE GREAT APOSTASY (Episodes 21–25)

*The devil cannot kill the risen Lord. So he takes the Church off the earth
instead — his longest, most successful campaign. This movement stays in the
film.*

### Episode 21 — Kill the Messengers
The new strategy, stated plainly: if you cannot stop the Atonement, stop the
authorized church that applies it. Stephen stoned while he sees the Son of
Man standing at the right hand of God. James by the sword. Peter, Paul, and
one by one the men holding the keys — hunted down across the empire. The
apostles see it coming and say so in writing: after my departing shall
grievous wolves enter in... there shall come a falling away first.
**The war:** the devil learned at Calvary that martyrdom can't stop a
resurrected Lord — but it can remove the men authorized to act in His name,
if no one is left to ordain replacements. Count the apostles as they fall.
That number is the whole tragedy.
**Anchors:** Acts 7:54–60; Acts 12:1–2; Acts 20:29–31; 2 Thessalonians 2:1–3; 2 Timothy 4:6.

### Episode 22 — The Changed Ordinances
With the keys gone, the drift begins — slow, sincere, and fatal. Baptism
altered. Priesthood assumed rather than conferred. Councils voting on the
nature of God until the embodied Father and Son who stood in Eden and
Galilee are replaced, in many minds, by a formless abstraction no child
could recognize as Father. Isaiah saw it seven centuries early: they have
transgressed the laws, changed the ordinance, broken the everlasting
covenant.
**The war:** the devil's masterpiece isn't persecution — it's renovation.
Keep the vocabulary, swap the contents. A church-shaped thing remains, full
of good people, holding forms of godliness — with the authority gone.
**Anchors:** Isaiah 24:5; Amos 8:11 (foreshadow); 2 Timothy 3:5; JS—History 1:19.

### Episode 23 — The Famine of the Word ⭐ (build first)
"Not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but of hearing the words of
the LORD: and they shall wander from sea to sea... and shall not find it."
Revelation declared finished. The heavens declared closed. Plain and
precious things stripped from the book as it passes through uninspired
hands — and whole doctrines collapse in the dark: infants pronounced guilty,
God made unknowable, grace and works at war. Centuries of honest, believing,
praying people — wandering from sea to sea with no prophet to find.
**The war:** this is the devil's ideal world: not atheism — starvation.
Enough religion to argue about, no authority to settle anything, and a God
redefined until asking Him directly seems absurd. Remember this episode when
a boy in 1820 decides to ask anyway.
**Anchors:** Amos 8:11–12; 1 Nephi 13:24–29; Moroni 8; 2 Nephi 28:3–6.

### Episode 24 — Moroni Alone
The same night falls on the other hemisphere. The Nephite nation — visited
by the risen Christ, two centuries of Zion after — chooses its way down into
ruin, and dies in a final battle at Cumorah. One man is left: Moroni. His
father slain, his people gone, alone with the record of a thousand years of
God's dealings, hunted, wandering "whithersoever I can for the safety of
mine own life." And instead of despairing, he finishes the book, seals it,
and buries it in a hillside — addressed to us.
**The war:** the devil now believes he has both hemispheres — churches
without keys in one, and in the other, a dead nation and a book in the
ground. He has made one miscalculation. A buried record isn't a corpse.
It's a seed.
**Anchors:** Mormon 6; Mormon 8:1–7; Moroni 1:1–4; Moroni 10:27.

### Episode 25 — Sparks in the Dark
God never let go — the long centuries are full of His fingerprints. The
book is carried across Europe; translators die to put scripture in common
tongues; a printing press breaks the information monopoly; reformers —
raised up, and scripture says so — pry open space for conscience; and a free
land is prepared, with a constitution God claims He established by wise men
raised up for that very purpose. But watch what none of these good men
claim: none says God sent him with keys. Reformation names the disease. Only
restoration is the cure — and restoration requires heaven to send back the
men who held the authority last.
**The war:** the devil holds a fortress a thousand years and mistakes God's
patience for absence. Every spark — press, Bible, liberty — is the Father
staging the ground for one spring morning in New York.
**Anchors:** 1 Nephi 13:12–19, 37; D&C 101:77–80; 2 Nephi 3.

## MOVEMENT V — THE RESTORATION (Episodes 26–30)

*Heaven takes it all back — by personal visit, in order, with witnesses.*

### Episode 26 — The Grove ⭐ (build first)
Spring 1820. A fourteen-year-old farm boy, caught in a war of words between
churches, reads one verse: "If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God."
He walks into a grove of trees to try the promise. Darkness seizes him
first — an actual power that binds his tongue, because the devil knows
exactly what this one prayer will cost him. Then a pillar of light, brighter
than the sun. Two Persons, whose brightness and glory defy all description,
standing above him in the air. One of them speaks his name, points to the
other, and says: "This is My Beloved Son. Hear Him!"
**The war:** the famine ends. Every apostasy lie dies in about ten seconds —
God is not an abstraction, the heavens are not closed, the Father and the
Son are two, and they know a farm boy by name. The devil fought harder to
stop this prayer than any battle since the war in heaven. He lost.
**Anchors:** James 1:5; JS—History 1:5–20.

### Episode 27 — The Book Rises
An angel by night — Moroni, the same man who buried the record fourteen
centuries earlier, now sent back to unbury it. Four years of preparation,
then golden plates in a hillside, translated by the gift and power of God
by an unschooled young man at a pace scholars still can't explain. The
famine gets its answer in the devil's own trophy case: the second witness,
written for our day, carrying the plain and precious things back — its title
page declaring its whole purpose: the convincing of Jew and Gentile that
JESUS is the CHRIST.
**The war:** the devil corrupted one record over centuries; God had a second
one buried under his feet the entire time. Episode 16's seed, sprouting on
schedule.
**Anchors:** JS—History 1:27–54, 59–60; 2 Nephi 27; Book of Mormon title page; 2 Nephi 29:8.

### Episode 28 — Hands on Heads
Authority doesn't come from wanting it, studying it, or voting on it — it
comes by the laying on of hands by someone who holds it. So heaven sends
the last men who held it. John the Baptist — beheaded eighteen centuries
earlier — restores the Aaronic Priesthood on the banks of the Susquehanna.
Peter, James, and John — the First Presidency of the old world — restore the
Melchizedek Priesthood and the apostleship. The exact thing the devil spent
Movement IV removing is handed back, hand to head, by the exact men he
killed to remove it.
**The war:** the devil's entire apostasy campaign assumed dead men stay
gone. Resurrected messengers are the Father's answer — you cannot
permanently steal keys from a God who can send the keyholders back.
**Anchors:** D&C 13; JS—History 1:68–72; D&C 27:12–13; D&C 128:20; Hebrews 5:4.

### Episode 29 — Elijah Came
April 6, 1830: the Church of Jesus Christ is legally, formally on the earth
again — prophets, apostles, the same organization He built in Galilee. Six
years later, in the Kirtland temple, the messengers come in sequence like
department heads returning keys: Moses, with the keys of the gathering of
Israel; Elias, with the gospel of Abraham; and then — exactly as Malachi
promised in the last words of the Old Testament — Elijah, with the sealing
power that binds families on earth and in heaven, turning the hearts of the
children to their fathers.
**The war:** the devil's version of death rips every family apart forever.
Elijah's keys mean marriage and family can outlast the grave — and the dead
are not out of reach. The devil is now losing territory he thought was
permanently his.
**Anchors:** D&C 20:1; D&C 110; Malachi 4:5–6; 3 Nephi 25:5–6.

### Episode 30 — One Great Whole
The last dispensation isn't one more link in the chain — it's the welding of
every link into one. Adam's gospel, Enoch's Zion, Abraham's covenant,
Moses' gathering, the apostles' keys — everything every dispensation ever
held, gathered into one, in the dispensation of the fulness of times.
Temples multiply across the earth; the work for the dead reaches back
through every century the famine starved; and Daniel's stone, cut out of the
mountain without hands, starts rolling — small, unstoppable, filling the
earth.
**The war:** for the first time in history, the devil's oldest victories are
being reversed retroactively — the apostasy's stolen centuries are being
ministered to, name by name, in the temples. He has never faced this before:
a Church that rescues backward as well as forward.
**Anchors:** Ephesians 1:10; D&C 128:18–22; Daniel 2:44–45; D&C 65:2; 1 Peter 4:6.

## MOVEMENT VI — NOW (Episodes 31–33)

*The same war, your street. And how it ends.*

### Episode 31 — Same Two Speeches, Modern Clothes
The council never really adjourned — both speeches are still playing,
everywhere, in modern dress. The devil's speech today sounds like: control
them for their own good; take the shortcut; the ends justify it; there is no
devil; all is well. The Father's speech still sounds like it did before the
world: no power or influence ought to be maintained except by persuasion,
long-suffering, gentleness, meekness, and love unfeigned. One of these
voices is in your feed, your politics, your habits — and one is still
quietly asking, not forcing, you to come home.
**The war:** every day, every person re-votes the war in heaven. The devil
still offers safety for freedom and glory for souls. The Father still
refuses to force — because He still wants sons and daughters, not puppets.
**Anchors:** Moses 4:1–4 (reprise); D&C 121:34–46; 2 Nephi 28:19–25; Moroni 7:12–17.

### Episode 32 — Kingdoms of Glory
So how does the story end for the seven billion who never heard, the
children who died young, the honest people born in the famine centuries?
Exactly the way you'd hope, if God is who this film says He is. The gospel
is preached to the dead. Every child who dies is saved in the celestial
kingdom — Episode 8's promise, kept. And the final judgment isn't heaven-
or-incinerator: it's kingdoms of glory, where even the least glory defies
description, because the Father is not rescuing a handful and discarding
the family of Adam. Through the Atonement of Christ, all mankind may be
saved.
**The war:** the devil's last slander is that God's heaven is a gated
community for the lucky few. The revealed answer is a Father who loses as
close to nobody as agency allows — the most generous view of God's justice
ever revealed. Vengeance was never the plan. Family was.
**Anchors:** D&C 76; D&C 137:7–10; D&C 138; 1 Corinthians 15:22, 40–42; 1 Peter 4:6; Articles of Faith 3.

### Episode 33 — He Will Finish It
The Son is coming back — not as a baby this time, but as the King. Every
knee shall bow. The devil, who has lost every single exchange in this film
while claiming to be winning, is bound — not negotiated with, bound — and
the earth receives its paradisiacal glory. And then the point of everything:
"When he shall appear, we shall be like him." Bodies glorified. Families
sealed. Agency intact the entire way — He never forced anyone, and He never
gave up on anyone. The Father wanted children who would become like Him,
and He was willing to pay what it cost. And to the last question — if He
wins in the end, why the wait? — His own parable: the wheat and the tares
grow together until the harvest, because ripping out tares early tears up
wheat. Every day the end delays is mercy for someone still deciding. Back
to the room where it started: He told us this would work. He was right.
**The war:** ends. The first speech wins — "Father, thy will be done, and
the glory be thine forever" — and the glory is: us, home.
**Anchors:** Revelation 20:1–3; Philippians 2:10–11; Articles of Faith 10; Matthew 13:24–30; 2 Peter 3:9; 1 John 3:2; Moroni 7:48; Revelation 21:3–4; Romans 8:16–17.
