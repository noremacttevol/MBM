# THE GREAT PLAN — Question Ledger

> **The law (Cameron, 2026-08-31):** the film must answer every question a
> regular person has about whether God's historical plan makes sense and
> shows He is good — inside the stories, entertainingly, and with the
> Humility Law in force: say plainly what we KNOW and what we DON'T KNOW.
>
> This ledger is the audit tool. Every question maps to the episode and
> story that answers it. **Gate rule: no episode ships unless the questions
> assigned to it are actually answered in the cut.** Items marked 🤲 are
> humility items — the script must openly say "God hasn't revealed
> everything about this" and testify only of what He has said.

---

## About God and why the plan exists

1. **"If God is good, why does evil exist at all?"** → **Ep 4** — God did
   not create evil; He refused to counterfeit freedom. Real agency means
   "no" must be possible, and evil is what freedom can say. (D&C 29:39;
   2 Nephi 2:11, 15–16)
2. **"Why would God create Lucifer if He knew he'd rebel?"** → **Ep 3–4** —
   He accepted every spirit who chose freedom, knowing rebellion would come
   from somewhere, and folded it into the plan rather than fake the
   freedom. 🤲 why this particular son — not fully revealed; what IS
   revealed is that God foreknew, and provided the Lamb before the danger
   started. (Rev 13:8)
3. **"Why does God allow the devil to keep tempting us?"** → **Ep 4** —
   "It must needs be that the devil should tempt the children of men, or
   they could not be agents unto themselves." Opposition is the training
   condition for becoming like Jesus. (D&C 29:39; 2 Nephi 2:11)
4. **"Why doesn't God just show Himself and end all doubt?"** → **Ep 6** —
   the veil is the point: a God visibly in the room gets complied with, not
   chosen — and compliance was Lucifer's plan. Faith is the only condition
   where becoming is possible. (Ether 12:6; 2 Cor 5:7)
5. **"Why test us if He already knows the outcome?"** → **Ep 5** — the
   proving is for OUR becoming, not His information. A test can be a gym,
   not a quiz. (Abraham 3:25)
6. **"Why do bad things happen to good people?"** → **Ep 11 + Ep 19** — the
   God who weeps suffers WITH His children, and the best Person who ever
   lived suffered most of all. Pain is the cost of a real world, and He
   paid the largest share Himself. (Moses 7:28–33; D&C 19:16–19)
7. **"Isn't God an egotist demanding worship and glory?"** → **Ep 14** —
   His own definition of His glory: "to bring to pass the immortality and
   eternal life of man." His glory IS our life. Worship is for what it does
   to us, not what it does for Him. (Moses 1:39)
8. **"Where did God come from?"** → **Ep 2** 🤲 — not fully revealed. What
   is revealed: we are His actual children, He has always been our Father,
   and His whole aim is to raise us to what He is. (Acts 17:28–29)
9. **"What about science — evolution, the age of the earth?"** → **Ep 5**
   🤲 — God revealed WHO created and WHY; He has not revealed every HOW or
   how long. The film testifies of the part that changes your life and
   says so about the rest. (Abraham 4)
10. **"Why doesn't God stop all the evil right now?"** → **Ep 33** — the
    wheat and the tares: rip the tares out early and you tear up wheat.
    Every day the end delays is mercy for someone still deciding. (Matthew
    13:24–30; 2 Peter 3:9)

## About the Fall and sin

11. **"Why punish all humanity for Adam's mistake?"** → **Ep 8** — He
    doesn't. Men are punished for their own sins, not Adam's
    transgression. This is the film's flagship proof of God's justice.
    (Articles of Faith 2; Moses 6:54)
12. **"What about original sin — aren't babies born guilty?"** → **Ep 8 +
    Ep 32** — no baby is born guilty; little children are alive in Christ,
    and children who die are saved in the celestial kingdom. (Moroni 8;
    D&C 137:10)
13. **"Why put the forbidden tree there at all?"** → **Ep 7** — a choice
    with one option is not a choice. Two trees or no agency. (2 Nephi
    2:15–16)
14. **"Why commandments — isn't obedience just control?"** → **Ep 18 +
    Ep 31** — God's own stated rule of power: persuasion, long-suffering,
    love unfeigned — no compulsion, ever. Commandments are training for
    becoming, from a Coach who refuses to force. (D&C 121:41–46)

## About Jesus

15. **"Why did Jesus have to die — couldn't God just forgive?"** → **Ep
    19** — justice is real and mercy cannot rob it; so mercy PAID it.
    Forgiveness isn't God waiving the debt — it's God eating it. (Alma
    42:13–25; Alma 34:9–16)
16. **"How can one man's suffering pay for everyone?"** → **Ep 19** — only
    an infinite and eternal Being could make an infinite atonement; that's
    exactly who volunteered. (Alma 34:10–14; Mosiah 3:7)
17. **"Why arrive as a helpless baby in a poor family?"** → **Ep 17** —
    condescension: love that walks in unarmed, small enough to be
    rejected, because force was never on the table. (1 Nephi 11)

## About the hard Old Testament

18. **"The flood — how is drowning the world loving?"** → **Ep 12** — a
    world so saturated in violence that no child could be raised free to
    choose is the death of the plan itself; the flood preserved the
    possibility of goodness, then God hung His bow as a promise. 🤲 the
    full accounting of everyone who died — not revealed; what is revealed
    is that the dead are preached to and no one is discarded (links to
    Ep 32). (Genesis 6:11; 1 Peter 4:6)
19. **"Why did God favor one nation?"** → **Ep 13** — He didn't pick a
    favorite; He hired a delivery service. One family covenanted FOR all
    families of the earth. (Genesis 12:3; Abraham 2:9–11)
20. **"Why is the Old Testament so violent?"** → **Ep 15** 🤲 — records
    passed through human hands in a hard age, and plain and precious
    things were lost; what stands revealed is the pattern: a God of
    compassion who kept rising early and sending prophets. (2 Chronicles
    36:15; 1 Nephi 13:24–29)

## About churches and religion

21. **"Why are there thousands of churches all claiming truth?"** → **Eps
    21–25 + Ep 26** — this is THE question the whole apostasy movement
    answers, and the exact question a 14-year-old took into a grove.
    (JS—History 1:10)
22. **"Isn't organized religion just power and control?"** → **Ep 22 +
    Ep 31** — control is the signature of the counterfeit; the true
    Church's own scripture voids any authority the moment it's used to
    compel. (D&C 121:37, 41)
23. **"Why would God let His church disappear for centuries?"** → **Eps
    21–25** — He didn't idly let it; men with agency killed it, and God
    spent those centuries staging the comeback — book, press, liberty,
    land. (1 Nephi 13; D&C 101:80)
24. **"If the Bible is God's word, why would anything need restoring?"** →
    **Ep 23 + Ep 27** — the Bible is true and beloved AND plain and
    precious things were taken from it; the answer isn't a better Bible,
    it's a second witness. (1 Nephi 13:26–29, 40)
25. **"Why prophets — why won't God just speak to me directly?"** → **Ep
    15 + Ep 26** — He does both: prophets for the world, answers for the
    one. James 1:5 worked for a farm boy; it still works. (Amos 3:7;
    James 1:5)
26. **"Why would God pick a 14-year-old farm boy?"** → **Ep 26** — it's
    His signature move: shepherd boys, fishermen, a manger. God chooses
    the weak things to confound the mighty, so no one can credit the
    marketing. (1 Corinthians 1:27)
27. **"Why ordinances — why would God care about ceremonies?"** → **Ep 9 +
    Ep 28** — every ordinance is a similitude pointing to Christ, and
    authority is what makes a promise from God instead of a gesture from
    men. (Moses 5:7; Hebrews 5:4)

## About the fairness of salvation

28. **"What about the billions who never heard of Christ?"** → **Ep 32** —
    the gospel is preached to the dead; nobody's chance dies with them.
    (D&C 138; 1 Peter 4:6)
29. **"What happens to children who die?"** → **Ep 32** — saved, celestial,
    no asterisk. (D&C 137:10)
30. **"Would a loving God burn people in hell forever?"** → **Ep 32** —
    the revealed end is kingdoms of glory, where even the least defies
    description; judgment is a Father placing children where they can
    still receive, not an incinerator. (D&C 76; 1 Cor 15:40–42)
31. **"So is heaven a gated community for the lucky few?"** → **Ep 32** —
    through Christ, ALL mankind may be saved; God loses as close to nobody
    as agency allows. (Articles of Faith 3)

## About the end and the point

32. **"If God wins in the end, what was the point of all this?"** → **Ep
    33 + the whole film** — He doesn't want something FROM us; He wants
    something FOR us: to be like Him, home, with agency intact the whole
    way. (1 John 3:2; Romans 8:16–17)
33. **"How do I know any of this is true?"** → **Ep 26 + Ep 33** — the
    same way Joseph did: ask. The film never argues anyone into it; it
    tells the story and hands the viewer James 1:5 — the one experiment
    anyone can run. (James 1:5; Moroni 10:4–5)

## Out of scope (on purpose)

Questions about specific Church-history controversies (polygamy, the
priesthood restriction, etc.) are questions about the Church's history,
not about the plan of salvation — this film testifies of the PLAN from the
council to now. Those questions deserve honest answers on other MBM
surfaces if Cameron chooses; wedging them into this story would break its
spine. If a viewer asks, the app's human-connection lane is the answer
path.

## Maintenance rule

When Cameron (or a real viewer) surfaces a question this ledger doesn't
cover, it gets ADDED here and assigned to an episode in the same session —
this list is meant to grow toward "every question someone could ever have."
