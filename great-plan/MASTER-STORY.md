# THE GREAT PLAN — Master Story

> **What this is:** the canonical story document for Cameron's God-vs-the-devil
> film — the entire history of what Latter-day Saints understand God to have
> done for His children, from the council in heaven to now, told as one war.
> This is a **testimony of the restored gospel**, not a careful introduction
> for people who might flinch. Alignment with other views of God can happen;
> it is not the job. The job is to tell what Cameron believes Jesus has made
> clear.
>
> Every episode script is written FROM this document. If a script conflicts
> with this document, this document wins.

---

## The story is one war, not a highlight reel

**The Father's will:** His children get bodies, keep agency, come to Christ,
and become like Him. He will not force them. He will not charge them with
Adam's act. He will still pay everything to save them.

**Lucifer's will:** Destroy agency, take the honor, then — when he cannot
stop the plan — corrupt the record, kill the prophets, change the ordinances,
and take the keys off the earth.

That is the plot from the council to now. Every episode shows one exchange in
that war, and every episode proves the same thing: **God has always loved us
more than an earthly father could, because He always wanted us to become like
Him — and He would not cheat to get us there.**

## Six movements

### I. Before the world
We were His spirit children. He presented His plan. Jehovah yielded to the
Father. Lucifer offered a forced save in exchange for the throne. War in
heaven. A third part cast down. The earth organized as the school for the
second estate.

### II. First dispensations
Eden. The Fall opens the plan instead of ruining it. Adam receives the gospel
immediately. Children are not guilty of Adam's transgression. Cain gives the
devil a murder-religion. Enoch's Zion is taken up. Noah preserves a remnant.
Babel scatters a false unity. Abraham is given a covenant meant for all
nations.

### III. Israel, the other sheep, and the Son
Jehovah delivers Israel and speaks with Moses face to face. Israel forgets;
God sends prophets anyway. Lehi's family becomes a second witness nation. The
Firstborn is born in the flesh. Gethsemane and the cross pay what force would
have erased. The tomb breaks for all. The risen Christ teaches the Nephites.
Apostles hold keys in the old world.

### IV. The Great Apostasy
This stays in the film. The devil cannot kill the risen Lord, so he takes the
Church off the earth. Apostles slain. Keys gone. Ordinances changed. The
embodied Father and Son replaced in many minds by an abstraction. Infants
charged with guilt they never chose. Revelation treated as finished. Plain
things lost from the book. The Nephite nation collapses and Moroni buries the
record. Good people remain. The Church Christ organized does not. That is why
a restoration, not a reformation, is required.

### V. The Restoration
The Father and the Son stand in a grove as two glorified persons. A buried
book rises. John the Baptist, then Peter, James, and John, then Moses, Elias,
and Elijah bring back what death and apostasy removed. The Church is on the
earth again. Temples, gathering, work for the dead. The last dispensation is
the Father gathering into one all that every previous dispensation held.

### VI. Now
Same two speeches, modern clothes. He still will not force. He still will not
damn the innocent for Adam. Through Christ, all mankind may be saved by
obedience to the gospel's laws and ordinances. Kingdoms of glory mean the
Father is not trying to rescue a handful and discard the family of Adam. The
Son will finish what He started.

## The doctrines that must never be compressed

**Not damned for Adam.**
Article of Faith 2 is not a footnote. It is a turning point in Movement II.
The Fall brought death and separation. It also brought children, agency in
mortality, and the need for a Savior. Little children are alive in Christ. We
answer for our own sins. That is one of the clearest proofs in the whole
story that the Father is just and good.

**Apostasy as the devil's long theft.**
After Calvary he changes strategy. If he cannot stop the Atonement, he tries
to stop the authorized church that applies it. That is why keys, ordinances,
living apostles, and the true nature of God are the things that go missing —
and the things heaven has to bring back by sending the last men who held
them.

**The necessity of the devil (Cameron, 2026-08-31).**
The film must show HOW WE KNOW the devil is necessary — not as God's
partner, but as the price of real freedom. God accepted every spirit who
chose His plan; real agency means "no" must be genuinely possible, so He
knew rebellion would come, and He folded it into the plan rather than fake
the freedom. "It must needs be that the devil should tempt the children of
men, or they could not be agents unto themselves" (D&C 29:39); "it must
needs be, that there is an opposition in all things" (2 Nephi 2:11).
Opposition is the condition that makes truly growing like Jesus possible.
God did not create evil — He refused to counterfeit freedom.

## The Humility Law (Cameron, 2026-08-31)

The film answers every question a regular person has about whether God's
plan makes sense and shows He is good — inside the stories, entertainingly —
but it says plainly what we KNOW and what we DON'T KNOW. Where revelation is
silent (the how of creation, the full why behind hard Old Testament
passages, the origins of God), the narrator says so, humbly, and testifies
only of what has been revealed. Confidence about the revealed, honesty about
the unrevealed — that mix is what makes the testimony credible.

The complete question list, and which episode answers each, lives in
[QUESTION-LEDGER.md](./QUESTION-LEDGER.md). **Gate: no episode ships unless
the questions assigned to it are actually answered in the cut. New questions
get added to the ledger and assigned to an episode in the same session they
surface.**
