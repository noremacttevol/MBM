# The Print Kit — what to print and hand out

**Updated July 5, 2026 — the folder is now organized around ink:**

- **`Bishopric-Stack/`** — the complete 16-document set for presenting to the
  bishopric, numbered in the order to use them. ALL ink-light except #14 and #16,
  which contain app screenshots. #1 in this folder is Cameron-only; #2 is the one
  you hand the bishop.
- **`Ink-Heavy (screen or print shop)/`** — the beautiful dark-cover Come-and-See
  brochure lives here now. Show it on a screen, or have a print shop run it —
  do NOT print it at home. Its ink-light twin is "1 - Come-and-See Brochure
  (ink-light).pdf" in this folder and #09 in the Bishopric-Stack.

Everything in this folder is **ready to print** as-is. They're all light on ink
(white paper, a little navy-and-gold), so they won't drain a color cartridge.
Each piece shows your contact info: **(843) 582-7278 · admin@milkb4meat.org · milkb4meat.org**.

Print these on plain Letter paper. If your printer can do double-sided, the
brochures look great that way; if not, single-sided is fine.

## What's here, and who each one is for

**1 — Come-and-See Brochure (everyone).** The main handout. Warm, gentle, no
pressure — for anyone at all: a friend, a neighbor, someone curious. This is the
one to print the most of. (3 pages: a cover, the inside, and a back page.)

**2 — For-Members Outreach Brochure.** For fellow members of the Church. It does
two things at once: shows them how the app feeds their own faith, and gives them
the words to share it with a friend who's searching. Hand this to ward members.
(1 page.)

**3 — Bishop & Church-Leader Brochure.** For when you sit down with your bishop or
another leader. It explains what the app is, what it is *not*, how it honors the
Church's counsel, and the simple thing you're asking. Print a copy to bring to the
meeting. (1 page.)

**4 — How to Get the App (hand-out sheet).** The simple "here's how to put it on
your phone" sheet — iPhone and Android steps, plus the website. Great to leave with
someone after you've told them about it. (1 page.)

**5 — Roadmap (where it's going).** The printable picture of what's done and what's
next. Not for mass handing-out — print a few to show someone who wants to see that
this is a real, growing project. (Light on ink by design.)

## My quick advice on quantities

- **Print a stack of #1 (Come-and-See)** — that's your everyday handout.
- **Print several of #2 (For-Members) and #4 (How to Get the App)** — these two
  travel together well: "here's why, here's how."
- **Print #3 (Bishop)** only when you have a leader meeting coming up — one or two.
- **#5 (Roadmap)** is optional — a few copies for anyone who wants the big picture.
- The bigger items (the full book, the field guide, the launch plan) live in the
  **FOR-CAMERON** folder and are meant for *you* to read on screen — no need to
  print those.
