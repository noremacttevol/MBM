## Page or Group? → **Page.**

A **Page** is the right tool for launch:
- Pages are public — anyone can find it, watch a video, and share it WITHOUT joining anything.
- Reels posted from a Page can reach people who never heard of you. Group posts only reach members.
- You can boost a Page post for a few dollars later if you want. Groups can't do that.
- A Group is for community AFTER you have people. Start the Page tonight; add a Group in a month or two if fans show up and want to talk.

## Setting it up (on your phone, ~10 min)

1. Facebook app → your profile → **Pages** → **Create**.
2. **Page name:** `Milk Before Meat`
3. **Category:** "App Page" (or "Religious Organization" if App Page isn't offered — App Page is better).
4. **Bio / About:** paste the text below.
5. **Profile picture:** the app icon (`mobile/assets/icon.png`).
6. **Cover photo:** any still you love — `media-production/build-01-cloak/assets/` has good ones.
7. Add the **website** field: `https://milk-b4-meat.web.app`
8. Add an **action button** → "Use App" or "Learn More" → point it at the App Store link below.

## House rules (saves you grief later)

- Turn ON profanity filter: Page settings → "Content moderation."
- Decide now: theology debates in comments get one kind reply, then you stop. The page's
  voice is the app's voice — warm, never defensive.
- Post between 6–8 AM or 7–9 PM Eastern for reach.
- Reply to every comment the first week — Facebook rewards pages that talk back.
