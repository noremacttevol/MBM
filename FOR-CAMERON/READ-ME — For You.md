# For You, Cameron — the things you actually open

This folder holds the documents meant for *you* to read or look at when you need to
do your side of the work. None of it is code. None of it is something you have to
print and hand out (the print-and-hand-out stuff lives in the **TO-PRINT** folder).

## What's here

**Roadmap.pdf** — the short, clear picture of what's built, what's in testing, and
what's next. The best place to start if you want to know where things stand.

**Overview & Launch Plan.pdf** — the bigger-picture plan for getting Milk Before Meat
out into the world: the stages, the audiences, the order to do things in.

**Cameron's Field Guide.pdf** — your personal how-to: how to talk about the app, how
to walk someone through it, how to handle the common questions.

**Walkthrough for Testers.pdf** — a screen-by-screen tour of the app, with pictures,
so you can see (and show) exactly what a person experiences.

**The Complete Book.pdf** — the full, deep write-up of the whole vision and how the
app lives it out. For when you want the complete story in one place.

## Two more files worth knowing about (they live at the top level)

- **START-HERE.md** — the always-true "what's real right now" file (what's published,
  what's in testing, what's paid for). If you ever wonder "wait, is that done?", that's
  the file with the answer.
- **CAMERON — START HERE.md** — the master index for the whole folder, in plain English.
