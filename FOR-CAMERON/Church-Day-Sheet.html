  • Someone finds a bug → thank them, write it down, and tell them that's exactly what this phase is for. You are never the one who has to fix it — just carry it home.
  • Hard doctrinal questions about the app → "It never replaces a person. A real human is always one tap away inside it, and it tells people plainly what it is."
