● For the two-way human inbox, the closest equal to Supabase that's still free and no-login is Firebase (Cloud Firestore + Anonymous Auth). It does everything the Supabase plan did —
  device-as-identity (no login screen), live replies pushed into the app in real time, and security rules instead of RLS — on Google's free Spark tier. The port is nearly 1-to-1, so
  almost none of the messaging design changes.

  Two other options, weaker for your case:

  - Ably or Pusher — excellent realtime, but they only move messages; they don't store the thread. You'd still need somewhere to keep history, so you'd end up bolting on a second
  service. More moving parts.
  - A tiny endpoint on your existing proxy (Railway/Render free tier) — you already run a proxy for the AI key, so you could add an /inbox route + simple polling. No new vendor at all,
   but no live push (the app would poll every few seconds) and you maintain the server yourself.
  
  My recommendation: Firebase. It's the only one that keeps the whole no-login, realtime, device-as-identity architecture intact without adding a second service or losing live updates.
   Main tradeoff vs. Supabase: it's NoSQL (documents, not SQL tables), so the schema is shaped a little differently — but for a notes-and-replies inbox that's actually simpler, not
  harder.
