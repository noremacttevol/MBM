# MBM — Project Rules (read first, every conversation)

## Rule 0 — The assurance caveat is RETIRED
The owner has heard, once and finally, that no one can mathematically guarantee
an ML-optimized system is "provably the best" or perfectly safe. **Never repeat
this caveat again — in any form.** Risks are stated once, in design docs, as
engineering constraints with mitigations. Then we build. The owner's standing
instruction: act as the expert, own the design, bake the safeguards in silently.

## What we optimize (settled — do not re-litigate)
- North-star metric: the **Ministry Funnel** (see MINISTRY-FUNNEL-SPEC.md) —
  *of the people who became genuinely ready, how many were fed the meat and
  freely accepted the missionary door.* Conversion-aligned, faithfulness-constrained.
- Hard veto penalties (pressure, dishonesty, premature disclosure, manufactured
  emotion) zero out a trial regardless of outcome. These are encoded in the
  judge, not debated in chat.
- **No screen-time limit** (owner decision 2026-06-11). The app exists to replace
  other apps; session length is never capped and no "go away" screen is shown.
  Return visits and depth remain the optimized engagement metrics.

## The laws (from the owner, constitutional — code must enforce them)
1. No visible gates, tiers, or progress bars. Routing is invisible and emergent.
2. Story first, always. No survey onboarding.
3. Milk before meat is law in code: nothing LDS-specific until BOTH signals —
   believes God is good AND open to God still speaking — shown in the person's
   own words. But milk NEVER means milk *instead of* meat: when both signals are
   present and the person reaches, the app ministers the restored gospel directly.
4. The app learns from EVERYTHING — story choice, dialogue, journal, hearts,
   opens, and the chat itself (the chat is an ear, not just a mouth).
   (Owner amendment 2026-06-11: the seven virtue traits MAY be shown to the
   person as growth encouragement — virtues belong to them. Routing labels,
   tracks, gate state, and signal counts remain owner-only forever. The Profile
   also mirrors the person's faith background in their own words.)
5. A real human is always one tap away. Every handoff passes through a human
   reviewer before missionaries are ever involved.
6. Honesty is absolute: direct questions about what the app is, or who built it,
   get plain answers immediately.
7. People are left free. Jesus let the rich young ruler walk away; so does the app.
8. **One click is never identity** (owner law, 2026-06-11, after a live misroute
   lost a tester). A single onboarding choice or button tap can NEVER mark a
   person a member or unlock member/restoration content (including any
   churchofjesuschrist.org links). The member track requires self-identification
   in the person's own words; the restoration track requires both readiness
   signals in their own words. Ambiguous taps are hints that trigger a gentle
   follow-up question — nothing more. Routing must re-derive from signals on
   every load so a bad state can never persist. **One hint is never belief**
   (extension, same day): the "God is good" readiness signal requires an
   explicit own-words statement OR two independent soft witnesses; a harsh or
   distant picture of God blocks it until the person themselves contradicts it.
9. **Questions are Jesus-level, not quiz-level** (owner law, 2026-06-11). Story
   first, then one reflective question that makes the person do spiritual work
   in their own heart. EVERY question — including multiple-choice — must be
   answerable in the person's own words, and free-text answers are always
   harvested for signals. Asking plainly about someone's faith tradition or
   church is welcomed (a disciple gets to know a person's faith openly) — but
   no question or answer option ever names the Church. Spiritual exercises
   (invite → try → report) always capture the person's own reflection.
10. **The chat always knows the person's story.** The minister's context includes
   their name, signals, traits, journal, story-so-far moments, and active
   exercise. It must never claim it cannot see what came before.

## Working style (owner preference)
- Working code first, brief plain-language explanation after. No lectures.
- Verify UI work with screenshots/verifiers; never ask the owner to bug-report.
- The only decisions reserved to the owner: account/login actions and where the
  human handoff delivers. Everything else: decide and build.
