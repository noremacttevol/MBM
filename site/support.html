Support

We're a small team and we genuinely want to help.

Get in touch

For any question, problem, or feedback about the app, email hello@milkb4meat.org. A real person reads it.

Talk to a real person inside the app

Anywhere in the app you'll find a way to reach a real human. Tap it, write a note, and someone on our team will reply — no account needed.

Your privacy

You can use almost the whole app without an account and without your words ever leaving your phone. The details are on our privacy page.

Deleting your information

Removing the app erases everything stored on your device. If you ever messaged a real person and want that anonymous conversation deleted, email privacy@milkb4meat.org and we'll remove it.
